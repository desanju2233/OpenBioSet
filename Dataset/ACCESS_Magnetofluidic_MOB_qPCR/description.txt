Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Automated Cartridge-Based Cancer Early Screening System (ACCESS) - Droplet Magnetofluidic, Methylation-on-Beads (MOB) Assay with Methylation-Specific qPCR (qMSP) for DNA Methylation Cancer Biomarker Detection

REAGENTS USED:
  - Sample (patient tissue lysate, extracted gDNA, or cultured cell pellet, loaded into the cartridge's sample port)
  - Magnetic Beads (silica superparamagnetic MagBinding beads that bind nucleic acids for magnetic transport between reagent droplets)
  - Conversion Reagent (Lightning Conversion Reagent, used with thermolabile proteinase K for tissue digestion, DNA denaturation, and bisulfite sulfonation/deamination of unmethylated cytosines)
  - Proteinase K (thermolabile proteinase K, digests sample and self-inactivates with heat, preventing qPCR inhibition from spillover)
  - Binding Buffer (M-Binding buffer, mediates DNA-silica bead conjugation)
  - Wash Buffer (200 mM NaCl, 10% w/v PEG 6000, 0.1% Tween 20, used across three wash droplets to remove unbound reagent from the beads while remaining anchored in the cartridge's wash wells)
  - Desulfonation Buffer (L-desulfonation buffer, completes bisulfite conversion by removing sulfonate groups from converted DNA)
  - qPCR Mix (200 nM methylation-specific forward/reverse primers, Platinum Taq polymerase, EvaGreen Plus dye, dNTPs, MgCl2, and custom qPCR buffer, contained in the final droplet where DNA is eluted off the beads and amplified)

STEPS:
1. Sample mixture preparation: The patient sample (lysed tissue, extracted DNA, or cell pellet) is mixed with silica magnetic beads, bisulfite conversion reagent, and thermolabile proteinase K, then loaded into the cartridge's sample port.
2. Sample digestion: The cartridge is inserted into the instrument, and the sample mixture is incubated at 30 C for 45 min in the first thermally isolated well to digest the sample and extract genomic DNA.
3. DNA denaturation: The extracted DNA is incubated at 98 C for 8 min to denature the DNA in preparation for bisulfite conversion.
4. Sulfonation and deamination: The denatured DNA is incubated at 58 C for 60 min, selectively converting unmethylated cytosines to uracils while methylcytosines remain unconverted (the core bisulfite conversion chemistry).
5. DNA-bead binding: M-Binding buffer is added and incubated at room temperature for 10 min, binding the DNA to the silica magnetic beads so it can be magnetically transferred between wells.
6. First wash: The motorized magnet array transfers the DNA-bound beads into a wash droplet to remove unbound sample components.
7. Desulfonation: The washed, DNA-bound beads are transferred into the desulfonation droplet, completing bisulfite conversion of the DNA.
8. Second and third washes: The DNA-bound beads are transferred through two more wash droplets to remove residual desulfonation reagent.
9. Elution into qPCR droplet: The washed beads are transferred into the final qPCR droplet, where the bisulfite-converted DNA is directly eluted off the beads by incubation at 70 C for 10 min; the (now DNA-free) beads are then magnetically transferred out of and upstream from the qPCR droplet to avoid interfering with fluorescence detection.
10. Methylation-specific qPCR: The eluted, bisulfite-converted DNA undergoes 45 cycles of methylation-specific qPCR thermocycling (103 C for 5 s denaturation, 63 C for 15 s annealing/extension), with only methylated-cytosine-derived sequences available for primer amplification.
11. Fluorescence detection and readout: Between each thermocycle, the optical module's motorized fiber sequentially scans each of the four cartridge channels to acquire the EvaGreen Plus fluorescence signal, and upon assay completion the instrument sends the resulting amplification curve data over USB to a laptop for Cq analysis and display.

INTERPRETATION: Successful methylation biomarker detection is confirmed by a qPCR amplification curve (fluorescence vs. cycle number) crossing a quantification threshold to yield a cycle of quantification (Cq) value; a positive (methylated) sample shows amplification (lower Cq) while a true-negative (unmethylated/normal) sample shows no amplification even after 55 cycles in later validation runs. Using this platform, the housekeeping gene ACTB was consistently detected on-cartridge (mean Cq 24.46 from spiked bisulfite-converted DNA; mean Cq 27.32 from raw gDNA), the esophageal cancer methylation biomarkers TAC1 and ZNF132 were detected down to 1% methylated DNA in a nonmethylated DNA background, and both markers were successfully detected in tumor tissue/DNA from ESCC patients while remaining negative in paired normal tissue, supporting ACCESS as a portable, automated diagnostic tool for early esophageal cancer detection in resource-constrained settings.
