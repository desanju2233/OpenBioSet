--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This protocol implements the first fully automated CRISPR-Cas9 gene-editing
assay on a digital microfluidic platform. The ACE platform automates all steps
— cell seeding, culture, lipid-mediated transfection, CRISPR-Cas9 knockout, drug
inhibition, and fluorescence-based viability readout — for lung cancer cells on a
single device.

The specific assay interrogates the MAPK/ERK (RAS-RAF-MEK-ERK) signaling
pathway by knocking out the RAF1 gene in NCI-H1299 lung squamous cell carcinoma
cells using CRISPR-Cas9, combined with dose-response treatment using the RAF
kinase inhibitor Sorafenib Tosylate. The combination of genomic disruption and
enzymatic inhibition results in lower IC50 values compared to inhibitor treatment
alone, confirming successful on-device gene editing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CELL LINE AND REAGENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cell Line       : NCI-H1299 human lung squamous cell carcinoma, dual-labeled
                  stable line (Genecopoeia SL001). Stably expresses eGFP at AAVS1
                  locus and mCherry. Grown in RPMI-1640 + 10% FBS, no antibiotics,
                  37 degrees C, 5% CO2.

Plasmids        :
  - pCRISPR_ctrl  : All-in-one pCRISPR/Cas9 plasmid (~10.5 kb) with sgRNA
                    targeting eGFP (non-genomic control, AAVS1 locus).
  - pCRISPR_RAF1  : All-in-one pCRISPR/Cas9 plasmid (~10.5 kb) with sgRNA
                    RAF1_sg94 targeting the human RAF1 genomic locus.
                    Designed via Benchling; assembled by restriction
                    digestion/ligation into All-in-one_CRISPR/Cas9_LacZ backbone.

Transfection    : Invitrogen Lipofectamine 3000 + P3000 reagent (Thermo Fisher).
                  On-device optimum lipid:media ratio is 1:1 (not the off-chip
                  manufacturer default of 1:10). The 1:1 ratio compensates for
                  the increased surface-area-to-volume ratio at microscale and
                  yields ~65% transfection efficiency vs ~15% at 1:10 (Fig. 2d).

Drug            : Sorafenib Tosylate (Selleckchem). RAF kinase inhibitor targeting
                  the MAPK/ERK pathway upstream at BRAF/RAF1.
                  Six concentrations: 0, 7.5, 15, 30, 60, 120 uM in complete media.

Stains          :
  - Hoechst 33342 (1 uM): nuclear stain; used ONLY at Day-1 transfection-
                           efficiency checkpoint. lambda_ex=350nm, lambda_em=461nm.
  - Calcein-AM violet (5 uM): live-cell esterase viability probe; used ONLY at
                               Day-5 readout. lambda_ex=408nm, lambda_em=450nm.
                               NOTE: Hoechst and Calcein-AM must NOT be used at
                               the same timepoint due to spectral overlap at ~450-
                               461nm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEVICE DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The DMF device consists of two parallel plates separated by a 140 um spacer.
The bottom plate contains chromium-patterned electrodes with dielectric and
hydrophobic layers (DuPont AF) for droplet actuation via electrowetting-on-
dielectric (EWOD) at ~200 VRMS, 10 kHz. Electrode boundaries are interlaced to
improve droplet movement for protein-rich solutions over multi-day culture.

The top plate (ITO-coated) contains six 1.2 mm diameter hydrophilic spots
microfabricated for adherent cell culture. Cells are passively dispensed onto
these spots; a fraction of the moving 690 nL droplet pins to the hydrophilic
site, forming a ~160 nL culture microvessel. Devices are incubated inverted in
a 3D-printed humidified chamber at 37 degrees C.

Reservoirs hold: cell suspension, Lipofectamine, Opti-MEM, plasmid DNA, P3000
reagent, complete media, Hoechst stain, Calcein-AM stain, and Sorafenib Tosylate.
A waste reservoir collects excess liquid from passive dispensing steps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Arms            : 2 (Ctrl sgRNA arm / RAF1_sg94 arm)
Sites per arm   : 6 (one per drug concentration)
Total sites     : 12
Drug doses (uM) : 120, 60, 30, 15, 7.5, 0
Duration        : 5 days (Day 0 seeding through Day 5 viability readout)
Readout         : Calcein-AM violet fluorescence cell count, normalised to
                  vehicle control (0 uM site) for each arm.
Analysis        : Sigmoid dose-response curve fit; IC50 extracted per arm;
                  F-test for significance in linear region (2.5-50 uM, P<0.05).

QC gates        : Two independent QC branches in the CFG:
                  - Ctrl arm: gated on signalCtrl6 (0 uM Ctrl sgRNA vehicle).
                  - RAF1 arm: gated on signalRaf6 (0 uM RAF1_sg94 vehicle).
                  Pass = positive fluorescence signal (dispose viability droplets,
                  data accepted). Fail = zero/negative signal (drain droplets,
                  arm flagged). The two gates are independent; failure of one arm
                  does not invalidate the other.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DAY 0 — CELL SEEDING
  1. Grow NCI-H1299 cells in T-75 flasks, rinse with PBS, trypsinize, centrifuge
     at 1000 x g for 5 min, resuspend in complete media + 0.05% w/v Pluronics
     F-68 to ~1.75e6 cells/mL.
  2. Pipette 2 uL of cell suspension onto the ITO reservoir edge. Apply ~200 VRMS
     at 10 kHz to actively dispense 690 nL unit droplets. Passively dispense onto
     each of the 12 hydrophilic spots to form 160 nL culture microvessels.
  3. Invert device into 3D-printed humidified chamber. Incubate at 37 degrees C,
     5% CO2, 16h overnight to allow H1299 cell adhesion to hydrophilic spots.

DAY 1 — TRANSFECTION (7-STEP ACTUATION SEQUENCE)
  Step 1: Dilute Lipofectamine 3000 in Opti-MEM. Add 2 uL of the dilution to
          a reservoir. Actively dispense 360 nL Lipofectamine-Opti-MEM droplets
          per site (12 total, 6 per arm).
  Step 2: Mix 500 ng/uL of pCRISPR plasmid (ctrl or RAF1) with 1 uL P3000
          reagent in 25 uL Opti-MEM. Add to a separate reservoir. Actively
          dispense 360 nL DNA-P3000-Opti-MEM droplets per site.
          Ctrl arm: pCRISPR_ctrl (eGFP sgRNA).
          RAF1 arm: pCRISPR_RAF1 (RAF1_sg94 sgRNA).
  Step 3: Merge lipid droplet with DNA droplet on 2x2 electrode array. Mix in
          square configuration for 10 min to form lipid-DNA complexes.
  Step 4: Dilute complexes 1:1 by merging with a 690 nL Opti-MEM unit droplet.
          (On-device optimum: 1:1 ratio yields ~65% transfection efficiency.)
  Step 5: Passively dispense 6x160 nL of diluted complexes onto each hydrophilic
          cell culture spot.
  Step 6: Incubate 24h overnight at 37 degrees C, 5% CO2.
  Step 7: Transfection-efficiency checkpoint — dispense 1 uM Hoechst 33342 in
          liquid media (6x160 nL per site). Incubate 30 min. Image with
          fluorescence microscope (UV filter: 250 ms, GFP filter: 500 ms, mCherry
          filter: 1000 ms) using custom 3D-printed microscope holder.
          Analyse with CellProfiler transfection pipeline (Fig. 2a):
            Module 1: Gaussian smooth (sigma=1), Otsu global threshold, detect
                      nuclei 20-100 px, declump by intensity.
            Module 2: Threshold mCherry image (TCF=1.0), binarize.
            Module 3: Overlap — mask nuclei with mCherry-positive regions.
            Module 4: Efficiency (%) = overlapping nuclei / total nuclei x 100.
          Dispose all Hoechst/detection droplets before Day 2.

DAY 2 — MEDIA MAINTENANCE
  1. Remove lipid-DNA complexes and Hoechst stain by passively dispensing fresh
     complete media (6x160 nL per site) to wash. Drain spent droplets to waste.
  2. Replenish each site with fresh complete media (6x160 nL). Maintain cells
     at 37 degrees C, 5% CO2 for 24h.

DAY 3 — SORAFENIB DRUG DOSING
  1. Prepare Sorafenib Tosylate serial dilutions on-device:
     Stock -> 120 uM -> 60 uM -> 30 uM -> 15 uM -> 7.5 uM -> 0 uM (vehicle).
     Each step: actively dispense drug droplet, merge with complete media droplet,
     mix for 30s. Split each concentration into one Ctrl-arm aliquot, one RAF1-arm
     aliquot, and one carry for the next dilution step. The 0 uM step requires no
     carry (terminal step).
  2. Passively dispense one drug aliquot (0.7 uL) onto each cell culture spot:
     Sites 1 and 1: 120 uM (highest dose)
     Sites 2 and 2: 60 uM
     Sites 3 and 3: 30 uM
     Sites 4 and 4: 15 uM
     Sites 5 and 5: 7.5 uM
     Sites 6 and 6: 0 uM (vehicle control, QC baseline)
  3. Incubate 48h at 37 degrees C, 5% CO2 (bridges Day 3 to Day 5 readout).

DAY 5 — CALCEIN-AM VIABILITY ASSAY AND DETECTION
  1. Prepare 5 uM Calcein-AM violet in complete media. Passively dispense 6x unit
     droplets to each of the 12 cell culture spots.
  2. Incubate 30 min at 37 degrees C.
  3. Image using single-module CellProfiler pipeline (Fig. 4 imaging pipeline):
     Gaussian smooth (sigma=1), Otsu global threshold, detect objects 20-100 px
     (TCF=1.25), declump by intensity. Count viable cells per site.
  4. Normalise cell counts at each drug concentration to the vehicle control
     (0 uM site) for the respective arm to obtain % cell survival.
  5. QC gate — Ctrl arm: if signalCtrl6 > 0 (vehicle baseline positive), data
     accepted (dispose viability droplets). If signalCtrl6 <= 0, arm flagged
     bad (drain droplets).
  6. QC gate — RAF1 arm: independent gate on signalRaf6 using same logic.
  7. Dispose all signal droplets. Experiment ends.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMAGING AND ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Microscope      : Olympus IX73 inverted fluorescence microscope with Hamamatsu
                  C1140-42U digital camera, HC ImageLive software.
Filter sets     : UV (250 ms), GFP (500 ms), mCherry (1000 ms).
                  Hoechst: lambda_ex=350nm, lambda_em=461nm.
                  GFP:     lambda_ex=488nm, lambda_em=509nm.
                  mCherry: lambda_ex=585nm, lambda_em=608nm.
                  Calcein-AM violet: lambda_ex=408nm, lambda_em=450nm.

Transfection    : CellProfiler 2.2.0 r9969F42 transfection pipeline.
efficiency      : Efficiency (%) = mCherry-positive nuclei / total nuclei x 100.
                  Corrected against non-transfected negative control.

Knockout        : CellProfiler knockout pipeline.
efficiency      : Knockout (%) = GFP-negative nuclei / total nuclei x 100.
                  GFP threshold: Otsu method (2-class, TCF=0.65).
                  Cells with >90% GFP-positive pixel overlap are not counted as
                  knocked out.

Viability       : Calcein-AM violet fluorescence cell count normalised to 0 uM
                  vehicle control. Sigmoid dose-response curve fit. IC50 extracted.
                  Statistical significance: F-test in linear region (2.5-50 uM).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY RESULTS (from paper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Transfection    : ~74.7% +/- 6.8% efficiency at 48h post-transfection on-device.
efficiency        Comparable to well-plate (no significant difference, P>0.05).

GFP knockout    : ~35% average on-chip, comparable to well-plate ~39% (P>0.05).
efficiency        Consistent across three sgRNA loci (sg_12, sg_497, sg_683).

MAPK/ERK assay  : IC50 for Sorafenib Tosylate:
                    Ctrl sgRNA arm  : 7.54 uM (on-chip), ~9 uM (off-chip)
                    RAF1_sg94 arm   : 13.2 uM (on-chip), ~17 uM (off-chip)
                  ~1.8-fold reduction in IC50 with RAF1 CRISPR targeting vs control
                  (P<0.05, F-test). Confirms successful on-device RAF1 knockout and
                  sensitisation to Sorafenib inhibition.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN BUGS IN ORIGINAL .BS FILE (CORRECTED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fix 1 — Wrong manifest at Day-1 Hoechst checkpoint:
  Original : hoechstTfx* variables dispensed from manifest calceinAM
  Corrected: hoechstTfx* variables dispensed from manifest hoechst
  Reason   : Calcein-AM is a cytoplasmic esterase probe (lambda_em~450nm), not a
             nuclear stain. The CellProfiler transfection pipeline requires Hoechst
             33342 (lambda_em~461nm) to identify nuclei in Module 1. Using
             Calcein-AM would produce invalid nuclear object detection and incorrect
             transfection efficiency measurements.

Fix 2 — Double-use of spent drug dilution variables:
  Original : drugConc120 (and other intermediates) split twice in two separate
             code sections, causing a fluid-ownership violation in the BioScript
             compiler.
  Corrected: Each dilution step immediately produces three named outputs:
             _ctrlAliquot, _raf1Aliquot, _carry. Each variable is split exactly
             once. The carry feeds the next dilution step; the two aliquots are
             delivered to their respective arms.
