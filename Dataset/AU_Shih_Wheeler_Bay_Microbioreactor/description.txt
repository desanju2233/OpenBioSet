PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Integrated Microbioreactor for Culture and Analysis of Bacteria, Algae and Yeast
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/BAY_paper.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1007/s10544-010-9469-3
Journal      : Biomed Microdevices, 2011, Vol. 13, pp. 41-50
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) - EWOD parallel-plate device ("BAY" microbioreactor)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated digital-microfluidic (DMF) microbioreactor capable of
culturing and analyzing three distinct microorganism types -- bacteria, algae and yeast -- in
a shared device architecture, giving rise to the name "BAY" microbioreactor. Cells are grown
in a ~70 uL droplet ("mother drop") confined to a reactor region, kept in suspension by
automated semi-continuous circular-path mixing, and periodically sampled via daughter droplets
that are driven to dedicated absorbance-measurement electrodes before being returned to the
mother drop, preserving culture volume over multi-day growth curves. The platform was validated
by comparing DMF-scale growth curves and doubling times against conventional macroscale
(shake-flask/vented-bottle) culture, and was further demonstrated to support two downstream
on-chip processes: a fluorescent cell-viability assay and genetic transformation.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. Long-term automated culture of bacteria, algae and yeast in a common mother-drop
     reactor architecture, with organism-specific temperature and mixing regimes
  2. Absorbance-based density monitoring (600 nm bacteria/yeast, 660 nm algae) via
     daughter-droplet dispense onto transparent L-shaped electrode windows
  3. On-chip fluorescent viability assay of yeast using Ethidium homodimer-1 (EthD-1),
     with Triton X-100 as a lytic positive control
  4. On-chip genetic transformation of E. coli with a YFP reporter plasmid via
     CaCl2/heat-shock, followed by off-chip plating for colony confirmation

Growth rates measured on-chip were comparable to macroscale culture for all three organisms
(doubling times: E. coli 1.23 +/- 0.43 h vs. 0.79 +/- 0.06 h macroscale; S. cerevisiae
1.88 +/- 0.15 h vs. 1.80 +/- 0.24 h; C. cryptica 42.6 +/- 2.4 h vs. 37.0 +/- 1.2 h),
despite substantial differences between the micro- and macro-scale systems (volume,
actuation, detector geometry).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------
Bottom plate : Chromium electrodes patterned on glass by photolithography/etching, coated
               with Parylene-C dielectric (6.9 um, vapor-deposited) and Teflon-AF hydrophobic
               layer (235 nm, spin-coated, post-baked 160 C).
Top plate    : Unpatterned ITO/glass coated with Teflon-AF (235 nm), serving as the ground
               electrode.
Spacer       : 350 um, formed from five-high stacks of double-sided tape between plates.

Device regions:
  - Reactor region: four 10.5 x 9.5 mm electrodes arranged in a 2x2 array; holds the
    ~70 uL mother drop, actuated in a circular path (four adjacent-electrode movements,
    ~1.5 s actuation) for semi-continuous mixing.
  - Sample region: three rows of eleven 3 x 3 mm electrodes (one row per organism), used
    to drive daughter droplets to the absorbance-measurement electrodes.
  - Reservoir region: three 6 x 6 mm electrodes (one per sample row), used to dispense
    assay/transformation reagents for on-demand merging with sample droplets.
  - L-shaped electrodes: one per sample row, with a 1.5 x 1.5 mm transparent window
    (1/4 of the 3 x 3 mm electrode area -- 1/2 and 1/8 ratios were tested and found
    sub-optimal) positioned on a 9 mm pitch matching a 96-well plate, allowing devices to
    be read directly in a PHERAstar microplate reader.

Actuation: 400-500 Vrms, 1-5 kHz, generated by a function generator/amplifier and switched
           through a network of high-voltage relays under custom LabVIEW/DAQPAD 6507 control.


ORGANISMS
---------
- Escherichia coli DH5alpha (bacteria): LB broth, 37 C, mixed every 2.5 min, absorbance
  (600 nm) measured every 1 h.
- Saccharomyces cerevisiae BY4741, S288C background (yeast): YPD broth, 30 C, mixed every
  2.5 min, absorbance (600 nm) measured every 2 h.
- Cyclotella cryptica CCMP 332 (algae): f/2 medium + biotin/cyanocobalamin (2 nM each),
  14 C under continuous 60 W illumination, mixed every 120 min, absorbance (660 nm)
  measured every 24 h.

Microscale media were supplemented with Pluronic F68 (0.1% w/v bacteria/yeast, 0.02% w/v
algae) to reduce cell/protein adhesion to the device surface; no detrimental effect on
viability or proliferation was observed.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Sterilization and Priming
  Reagent  : 70% ethanol
  Action   : Devices rinsed in 70% ethanol prior to reagent loading.
  Notes    : Off-chip preparatory step; not represented as a droplet operation in the
             BioScript encoding.

Step 2 | Bacteria Culture and Growth Monitoring
  Organism   : E. coli DH5alpha
  Inputs     : E. coli inoculum, LB broth
  Operation  : Inoculate mother drop; semi-continuous circular mixing at 37 C; daughter
               droplets dispensed hourly onto the L-shaped electrode for absorbance
               (600 nm) measurement, then returned to the mother drop.
  QC gate    : bactGrowthSignal > 0 -> culture proceeding normally (dispose);
               bactGrowthSignal <= 0 -> no growth/contamination (drain).

Step 3 | Yeast Culture and Growth Monitoring
  Organism   : S. cerevisiae BY4741
  Inputs     : S. cerevisiae inoculum, YPD broth
  Operation  : Inoculate mother drop; semi-continuous circular mixing at 30 C; daughter
               droplets dispensed every 2 h for absorbance (600 nm) measurement, then
               returned to the mother drop.
  QC gate    : yeastGrowthSignal > 0 -> culture proceeding normally (dispose);
               yeastGrowthSignal <= 0 -> no growth/contamination (drain).

Step 4 | Algae Culture and Growth Monitoring
  Organism   : C. cryptica CCMP 332
  Inputs     : C. cryptica inoculum, f/2 medium
  Operation  : Inoculate mother drop; semi-continuous circular mixing at 14 C under
               continuous illumination; daughter droplets dispensed every 24 h for
               absorbance (660 nm) measurement, then returned to the mother drop.
  QC gate    : algaeGrowthSignal > 0 -> culture proceeding normally (dispose);
               algaeGrowthSignal <= 0 -> no growth/contamination (drain).

Step 5 | Yeast Viability Assay (EthD-1 +/- Triton X-100)
  Organism   : S. cerevisiae BY4741
  Inputs     : S. cerevisiae inoculum, YPD broth, Ethidium homodimer-1 (2 uM),
               Triton X-100 (0.05% v/v)
  Operation  : Grow yeast 4 h at 30 C; dispense and merge dye/surfactant droplets from
               reservoirs; mix along the linear path (~10 actuations); incubate 1 h at
               30 C; read fluorescence.
  Results    : >99% of yeast non-viable following Triton X-100 exposure at this
               concentration, confirming EthD-1 as a functional dead-cell readout.
  QC gate    : viabilitySignal > 0 -> viable population detected (dispose);
               viabilitySignal <= 0 -> non-viable/compromised population (drain).

Step 6 | E. coli Transformation with pTriEx-YFP Plasmid
  Organism   : E. coli (ampicillin-sensitive)
  Inputs     : E. coli inoculum, LB broth, pTriEx-YFP plasmid (200 ng), 0.20 M CaCl2
  Operation  : Grow 1 h at 37 C to early/mid-log phase; chill on ice 5 min; merge with
               plasmid/CaCl2 droplet (~10 actuations); chill on ice 1 h; heat shock at
               42 C for 50 s; cool on ice 1 min; recover 1 h at 37 C with mixing.
  Off-chip   : Droplet spread on ampicillin (100 mg/L) LB agar plate, incubated
               overnight at 37 C to confirm successful transformation.
  QC gate    : transformSignal > 0 -> ampicillin-resistant, YFP-expressing colonies
               observed (dispose); transformSignal <= 0 -> no colonies (drain).


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : au_shih_wheeler_bay_microbioreactor.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 16 basic blocks (BB1-BB16), 5 if/else gates, 20 CFG edges
DAG            : au_shih_wheeler_bay_microbioreactor_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations expressed in seconds (s) or minutes (m) per compiler
                 constraints (h token not supported).
                 1 h  -> 60m   (bacteria absorbance interval; IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
                 2 h  -> 120m  (yeast absorbance interval; IR: 7200.0 SECOND)
                 24 h -> 1440m (algae absorbance interval; IR: 86400.0 SECOND)
                 4 h  -> 240m  (pre-assay yeast growth; IR: 14400.0 SECOND)
Modules        : densityDetection, viabilityDetection, transformationQC
Abstraction note: The paper's daughter-droplet dispense/return-to-mother-drop cycle for
                 absorbance measurement (Section 2.5, Fig. 2b) is modelled as a
                 non-consumptive detect() applied directly to the culture droplet, since
                 the compiler does not reliably support split/re-merge of a single mother
                 droplet without stranding fragments. Repeated circular-mixing/absorbance
                 cycles are represented as one representative growth/measurement cycle per
                 organism (heat at culture temperature for one measurement interval),
                 since BioScript has no loop construct for the multi-day unrolled sequence.
Biological scope note: This .bs file encodes the E. coli / S. cerevisiae / C. cryptica
                 growth-curve arms, the yeast viability assay, and the E. coli YFP
                 transformation arm described in the paper. The algae viability/growth
                 comparison figures (Fig. 3-4, Table 2) are represented via the growth QC
                 gate only; downstream algae-specific assays were not reported in the
                 source paper and are therefore not encoded.
