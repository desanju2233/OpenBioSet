Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics for Aerosol Impaction, Extraction, and Colorimetric Detection of Inorganic Ions (Sulfate, Nitrate, Ammonium)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Ambient/Laboratory-Generated Aerosol Sample (impacted directly onto the digital microfluidic chip surface via an integrated 5-nozzle inertial impactor, 1 L/min air flow)
  - Distilled Water (sub-microliter scanning/extraction droplet, ~0.5 uL)
  - Methylthymol Blue (MTB) - Barium Reagent (sulfate colorimetric assay, competitive complexation, detected at 608 nm)
  - Ammonium Colorimetric Reagent (detected at 365 nm)
  - Nitrate Colorimetric Reagent (acidic reagent, detected at 540 nm)
  - Sulfate/Ammonium/Nitrate Liquid Standards (six concentrations each, dispensed from on-chip reservoirs to build calibration curves)

STEPS:
1. Aerosol Collection: Air is drawn through five inertial-impactor nozzles (0.24 mm diameter, 1 L/min total flow) built into the chip's top plate, depositing particles >100 nm in diameter directly onto the CYTOP-coated actuation electrodes of the bottom plate (collection efficiency >95% for particles >100 nm).
2. Extraction: After the air flow is stopped, a sub-microliter distilled-water droplet is dispensed from an on-chip reservoir in oil, actuated through the oil/air interface, and scanned twice across the five-electrode impaction area to fully dissolve the deposited aerosol into a single concentrated extract droplet.
3. Sulfate Calibration: Six standard sulfate concentrations are sequentially dispensed and each mixed with the MTB-Ba reagent; the resulting droplet is actuated between two horizontal optical fibers and absorbance is measured at 608 nm to build a calibration curve (LOD ~11 ppm).
4. Sulfate Sample Measurement: The aerosol extract droplet is mixed with the MTB-Ba reagent and absorbance is measured at 608 nm; the mixed droplet is then discarded.
5. Ammonium Calibration: Six standard ammonium concentrations are sequentially dispensed and each mixed with the ammonium reagent; absorbance is measured at 365 nm to build a calibration curve (LOD ~0.256 ppm).
6. Ammonium Sample Measurement: The aerosol extract droplet is mixed with the ammonium reagent and absorbance is measured at 365 nm; the mixed droplet is then discarded.
7. Nitrate Calibration (unstable): Six standard nitrate concentrations are sequentially dispensed and each mixed with the acidic nitrate reagent; because a precipitate forms over the first 3-4 minutes, droplets are held ~150 s post-mixing before absorbance is measured at 540 nm (nonzero y-intercept observed, indicating reduced sensitivity).
8. Nitrate Sample Measurement: The aerosol extract droplet is mixed with the nitrate reagent, held 150 s, and absorbance is measured at 540 nm; the mixed droplet is then discarded, and the extract droplet itself is discarded once all three assays are complete.

INTERPRETATION: Successful ion quantification is confirmed by linear calibration curves (R^2 ~0.95-0.98) for sulfate and ammonium comparable to off-chip Tecan plate-reader results, on-chip limits of detection of 11 ppm (sulfate) and 0.256 ppm (ammonium) sufficient for converted air-concentration LODs of 0.275 ug/m3 and 6.5 ng/m3 respectively at 1 L/min for 1 h sampling, negligible cross-ion interference, and an unresolved precipitate-driven artifact that prevents stable nitrate quantification on-chip despite a linear off-chip response.
