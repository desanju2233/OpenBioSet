================================================================================
PROTOCOL: Integrated Droplet-Digital Microfluidic (ID2M) System
================================================================================

SOURCE LINK : https://www.shihmicrolab.ca/_files/ugd/f0a828_699eb4a700494d0c9503599110132dba.pdf
PAPER DOI   : https://doi.org/10.1039/c8lc01170b
JOURNAL     : Lab on a Chip, 2019, Vol. 19, pp. 524-535
PLATFORM    : Integrated droplet-digital microfluidic (ID2M) -- single-plate DMF
              co-planar electrodes fabricated beneath a droplet-in-channel layer
TARGET      : Fluorescein dye (Protocol 1); S. cerevisiae BY4741 wild-type and
              EMS-mutagenized ionic-liquid-tolerant mutants (Protocol 2)
APPLICATION : On-demand droplet generation/mixing/sorting calibration, and yeast
              mutant library n-ary screening for ionic-liquid tolerance

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper describes the ID2M (integrated droplet-digital microfluidic) device,
which merges single-plate digital microfluidics (co-planar ground/driving
electrodes) with droplet-in-channel microfluidics on the same multilayer chip.
The device performs five fluidic operations on the same substrate: droplet
generation (flow-focusing or on-demand T-junction), merging/mixing, trapping
and incubation (up to 24 h), optical detection, and n-ary (4-channel) sorting --
avoiding the droplet-transfer difficulties of separate droplet/digital platforms
used in prior work (e.g. the Mugele group's electrowetting-channel devices,
which were limited to binary sorting).

Two demonstrations are reported:

  (1) ON-CHIP FLUORESCEIN CALIBRATION (Protocol 1):
      A 1 mM fluorescein stock droplet is merged on-demand with diluent
      droplets to build a 2x/4x/8x dilution series. Each droplet is read by
      an optical-fibre fluorescence interface and sorted into one of four
      n-ary channels by threshold intensity. Sorting efficiency for
      positive vs. negative fluorescein droplets was measured at ~96%.
      Calibration curve: R^2 = 0.99 (N = 10); RSD 3.2-10.7% across dilutions.

  (2) YEAST / IONIC-LIQUID (IL) N-ARY SCREENING (Protocol 2):
      Wild-type S. cerevisiae BY4741 and two EMS-mutagenized IL-tolerant
      mutants (selected from a random mutant library on 50/75/100 mM IL
      agar plates) are Poisson-encapsulated as single cells, merged
      on-chip with a 200 mM ionic liquid droplet (final 100 mM IL),
      incubated 24 h at 30 C in on-chip traps, then sorted into 3 channels
      by absorbance (cell density). Mutant growth rates in 100 mM IL:
      ~2.2 and ~2.3 cells/h (mutant #1, #2) vs. ~0 cells/h (wild-type).
      This is reported as the first demonstration of culturing, mixing with
      ionic liquid, and n-ary (non-binary) sorting of microbes on one device.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REAGENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ID                    DESCRIPTION                           QUANTITY / SPEC
  --------------------  ------------------------------------  ----------------------
  FluoresceinStock      Fluorescein (free acid), 95%           1 unit per droplet
                        1 mM in 1 M NaOH buffer, pH 9          (~30 nL, T-junction)
  DilutionBuffer        Diluent droplet (buffer or water)      1 unit per droplet
                        used to build the 2x/4x/8x series      (~30 nL, T-junction)
  IonicLiquidStock      1-Ethyl-3-methylimidazolium acetate    1 unit per droplet
                        >=95% HPLC grade; 200 mM stock         (~35 nL); 100 mM final
                        (final on-chip: 100 mM after 1:1 mix)  on-chip after merge
  WildTypeYeast         S. cerevisiae BY4741 (MATa his3D1      1 unit per droplet
                        leu2D0 met15D0 ura3D0); Poisson-        (~35 nL, flow-focus)
                        encapsulated single cells
  MutantYeast1          EMS-mutagenized IL-tolerant mutant     1 unit per droplet
                        #1 (fastest grower on IL agar)          (~35 nL, flow-focus)
  MutantYeast2          EMS-mutagenized IL-tolerant mutant     1 unit per droplet
                        #2 (second-fastest grower on IL agar)  (~35 nL, flow-focus)

  NOTE: On-demand T-junction droplets are tuned 40-115 nL by oil flow rate
  (0.001-0.06 uL/s) at constant aqueous flow (0.0005 uL/s); droplets used in
  this protocol are ~30-35 nL. Actuation: 200 V_rms at 15 kHz. Oil phase:
  HFE7500 + 0.75% fluorosurfactant; main-channel oil flow rate 0.01 uL/s.
  Cell encapsulation: 2x10^5 cells/mL + 1% BSA, flow-focusing at 0.0008 uL/s
  (cells) : 0.01 uL/s (oil). Device throughput: ~1 Hz.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  fluorescenceDetection : Abstracts the on-chip optical-fibre fluorescence
                          readout (530 nm strip-chart, 100 ms integration,
                          boxcar smoothing width 3, 5 scans, update rate 1)

  absorbanceDetection   : Abstracts the on-chip optical-fibre absorbance
                          readout (boxcar smoothing width 2, 2 scans);
                          used as a proxy for cell density / culture growth

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Multilayer chip   : Single-plate co-planar DMF layer (104 chromium
                       electrodes on glass) + ~7 um SU-8 dielectric layer +
                       110-120 um SU-8 channel layer (300 um wide) + ~0.5 mm
                       PDMS slab (inlets/outlets). Three-layer device combines
                       pressure-driven channel flow with electrostatic droplet
                       actuation on the same substrate.
  Actuation         : 200 V_rms at 15 kHz; oil flow rate in main channel
                       0.01 uL/s; throughput ~1 Hz
  Optical interface : Two bare optical fibres (100 um core, NA 0.22)
                       perpendicular to the main channel, ~200 um standoff;
                       4-channel LED source (470/530/590/627 nm) + Flame
                       spectrometer (Ocean Optics)
  Sorting channels  : Symmetrical T-channel, 4 n-ary outlets (avoids the
                       stagnation zones and asymmetric resistance of Y-channel
                       sorters when expanded past 2 outlets)
  Mixing-area sinks : Multiple sink (side) channels reduce local oil
                       hydrodynamic resistance, allowing droplet actuation
                       from the main channel into the mixing area despite the
                       plug resistance of a droplet already present there.
                       Modelled in COMSOL Multiphysics V5.3 (single-phase
                       laminar Navier-Stokes; oil density 1614 kg/m^3;
                       dynamic viscosity 0.00124 Pa.s; inlet velocity
                       0.0003 m/s).

  NOTE: heat primitive in BioScript is void (no assignment). The 24 h, 30 C
  incubation is modelled as a heat statement acting on fluid variables in
  place; the device holds droplets in passive on-chip traps in a humidified
  chamber (not active closed-loop PID TEC as in Moazami et al. 2019).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL 1: ON-CHIP FLUORESCEIN CALIBRATION / DILUTION SERIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 -- Dispense Undiluted Fluorescein Stock Droplet
  Operations:
    fluorStockCheck = dispense 1 units of FluoresceinStock
  Notes:
    - 1 mM fluorescein (1 M NaOH, pH 9) dispensed on-demand via T-junction
    - Used directly as the highest-concentration calibration reference
    - Not consumed by any dilution step

STEP 2 -- Generate 2x Dilution Droplet (0.5 mM)
  Operations:
    fluorStock2x = dispense 1 units of FluoresceinStock
    bufFor2x     = dispense 1 units of DilutionBuffer
    dil2x        = mix fluorStock2x with bufFor2x for 5s
  Notes:
    - 1:1 mix: (1 unit x 1 mM) / 2 units total = 0.5 mM. Correct.
    - Fresh stock + fresh buffer droplet per condition; avoids reusing an
      already-consumed droplet variable (compiler does not enforce linearity)

STEP 3 -- Generate 4x Dilution Droplet (0.25 mM)
  Operations:
    fluorStock4x = dispense 1 units of FluoresceinStock
    buf4x_a      = dispense 1 units of DilutionBuffer
    buf4x_b      = dispense 1 units of DilutionBuffer
    buf4x_c      = dispense 1 units of DilutionBuffer
    buf4x_ab     = mix buf4x_a with buf4x_b for 5s
    buf4x_abc    = mix buf4x_ab with buf4x_c for 5s
    dil4x        = mix fluorStock4x with buf4x_abc for 5s
  Notes:
    - Target: 1:3 dilution; (1 unit x 1 mM) / 4 units total = 0.25 mM. Correct.
    - Compiler ignores multi-unit volume scalars ("dispense 3 units" defaults
      to 1-unit dispense in IR); three buffer droplets explicitly instantiated
      and chain-merged before final mix with fresh stock droplet
    - Every variable assigned and consumed exactly once; no aliasing in IR

STEP 4 -- Generate 8x Dilution Droplet (0.125 mM)
  Operations:
    fluorStock8x  = dispense 1 units of FluoresceinStock
    buf8x_1 .. buf8x_7 = dispense 1 units of DilutionBuffer  (x7)
    buf8x_12      = mix buf8x_1 with buf8x_2 for 5s
    buf8x_123     = mix buf8x_12 with buf8x_3 for 5s
    buf8x_1234    = mix buf8x_123 with buf8x_4 for 5s
    buf8x_12345   = mix buf8x_1234 with buf8x_5 for 5s
    buf8x_123456  = mix buf8x_12345 with buf8x_6 for 5s
    buf8x_1234567 = mix buf8x_123456 with buf8x_7 for 5s
    dil8x         = mix fluorStock8x with buf8x_1234567 for 5s
  Notes:
    - Target: 1:7 dilution; (1 unit x 1 mM) / 8 units total = 0.125 mM. Correct.
    - Matches the paper's reported maximum 8x dilution of stock analyte given
      the device's minimal-inlet droplet-in-channel constraint
    - Seven buffer droplets explicitly chained; every variable used once

STEP 5 -- Fluorescence Detection of All Four Calibration Droplets
  Operations:
    calSignalStock = detect fluorescenceDetection on fluorStockCheck for 30s
    calSignal2x    = detect fluorescenceDetection on dil2x for 30s
    calSignal4x    = detect fluorescenceDetection on dil4x for 30s
    calSignal8x    = detect fluorescenceDetection on dil8x for 30s
  Notes:
    - Optical-fibre fluorescence readout (530 nm) as each droplet passes
      the detection region prior to n-ary sorting
    - detect consumes its fluid operand in this compiler; distinct variable
      per detect call

STEP 6 -- Calibration QC Gate, N-ary Sort, and Join Cleanup
  Operations:
    if (calSignalStock > 0) {
        dispose fluorStockCheck
        dispose dil2x
        dispose dil4x
        dispose dil8x
    } else {
        drain fluorStockCheck
        drain dil2x
        drain dil4x
        drain dil8x
    }
    dispose calSignalStock
    dispose calSignal2x
    dispose calSignal4x
    dispose calSignal8x
  Notes:
    - QC gate keyed on undiluted stock signal (expected ~1900 a.u. threshold)
    - Pass (BB2): all four droplets exceed their respective sort thresholds
      (~1900/~1350/~950/~700 a.u.); routed to one of the four n-ary sorting
      channels (dispose models successful sort-and-collect)
    - Fail (BB3): below the ~700 a.u. detection floor; drain to waste
    - Both branches join at BB4; Protocol 2 begins after join
    - drain vs dispose: both emit as dispose() in the compiled IR (compiler
      bug); source uses drain on the fail path to preserve biological intent

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL 2: YEAST / IONIC-LIQUID (IL) N-ARY SCREENING ASSAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 7 -- Poisson Single-Cell Encapsulation of Yeast Droplets
  Operations:
    wtDroplet   = dispense 1 units of WildTypeYeast
    mut1Droplet = dispense 1 units of MutantYeast1
    mut2Droplet = dispense 1 units of MutantYeast2
  Notes:
    - Flow-focusing encapsulation (2x10^5 cells/mL + 1% BSA); one droplet
      per cell type: wild-type and the two fastest-growing EMS-mutagenized
      IL-tolerant isolates

STEP 8 -- On-Demand Generation of Ionic-Liquid Droplets
  Operations:
    ilDropWT   = dispense 1 units of IonicLiquidStock
    ilDropMut1 = dispense 1 units of IonicLiquidStock
    ilDropMut2 = dispense 1 units of IonicLiquidStock
  Notes:
    - 200 mM IL stock, on-demand T-junction (~35 nL) droplets
    - One IL droplet per single-cell droplet; 1:1 merging yields
      final on-chip concentration of 100 mM

STEP 9 -- Merge Each Single-Cell Droplet With Its IL Droplet
  Operations:
    wtMix   = mix wtDroplet with ilDropWT for 5s
    mut1Mix = mix mut1Droplet with ilDropMut1 for 5s
    mut2Mix = mix mut2Droplet with ilDropMut2 for 5s
  Notes:
    - Electrode actuation sequence (200 V_rms, 15 kHz) in the mixing area
    - Produces a final 100 mM IL droplet containing a single yeast cell
      for each of the three conditions

STEP 10 -- Trap and Incubate at 30 C for 24 h
  Operations:
    heat wtMix at 30c for 1440m
    heat mut1Mix at 30c for 1440m
    heat mut2Mix at 30c for 1440m
  Notes:
    - Each merged droplet actuated into one of four on-chip incubation traps
    - Incubated 24 h at 30 C in a humidified chamber (1440m = 86400 s)
    - heat is void in BioScript; reaction variables survive intact

STEP 11 -- Post-Incubation Absorbance Detection
  Operations:
    ilSignalWT   = detect absorbanceDetection on wtMix for 30s
    ilSignalMut1 = detect absorbanceDetection on mut1Mix for 30s
    ilSignalMut2 = detect absorbanceDetection on mut2Mix for 30s
  Notes:
    - Optical-fibre absorbance interface measures cell density (OD)
    - Expected OD per paper Fig. 4: wild-type ~0.04-0.07 (no growth;
      indistinguishable from oil background), mutant #1 ~0.6, mutant #2 ~1.0

STEP 12 -- IL-Tolerance QC Gate, N-ary Sort, and Final Cleanup
  Operations:
    if (ilSignalMut2 > 0) {
        dispose wtMix
        dispose mut1Mix
        dispose mut2Mix
    } else {
        drain wtMix
        drain mut1Mix
        drain mut2Mix
    }
    dispose ilSignalWT
    dispose ilSignalMut1
    dispose ilSignalMut2
  Notes:
    - QC gate keyed on mutant #2 signal (expected highest absorbance)
    - Pass (BB5): growth detected; droplets routed to respective n-ary
      sorting channels by absorbance level for downstream analysis
      (e.g. single-cell sequencing per paper suggestion)
    - Fail (BB6): no growth signal above oil background (~0.04-0.07);
      drain all
    - Both branches join at BB7 (terminal); signal droplets disposed

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Compiler    : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based, Python 3)
  Target      : -t ir (Intermediate Representation + Control Flow Graph)
  Command     : python3 main.py -d -i <file.bs> -t ir -o ./output -cfg

  KNOWN COMPILER BEHAVIOURS (relevant to this protocol):

  1. heat is void -- no assignment allowed.
     WRONG : result = heat wtMix at 30c for 1440m
     RIGHT : heat wtMix at 30c for 1440m

  2. detect consumes its fluid operand -- variable is spent after detect.
     Must use a distinct variable per detect call; do not reuse.

  3. Multi-unit volume scalars are silently ignored.
     "dispense 3 units of DilutionBuffer" generates dispense(DilutionBuffer)
     [0] in IR -- a single 1-unit dispense. To accumulate N units, explicitly
     instantiate N separate 1-unit dispense variables and chain with mix.

  4. Droplet linearity is not enforced by the compiler.
     A droplet consumed by mix no longer physically exists; referencing it
     again in a later mix/detect/dispose produces a silently-accepted but
     physically invalid IR. An earlier draft of this script reused consumed
     stock and buffer droplets when building the dilution series serially;
     the corrected version dispenses independent fresh droplets per condition.

  5. drain and dispose are not distinguished in the compiled IR.
     Both are lowered to the same dispose() instruction in false (_f) and
     true (_t) branch blocks alike. Confirmed reproducible across multiple
     compiles of two different versions of this script. This is a compiler
     backend code-generation gap; the .ir file must be manually patched
     (or post-processed with patch_ir_drain.py) before running on hardware.

  6. Split arrays cannot be partially consumed; array zipping does not work
     for multi-unit dispense. Avoided entirely by using only scalar dispense
     and chained mix operations throughout this script.

  7. ASCII-only source files -- ANTLR4 FileStream rejects non-ASCII bytes.
     All comments must use ASCII characters only (no Unicode arrows, degree
     symbols, box-drawing characters, or em-dashes).

  CFG STRUCTURE (this protocol):
    BB1  main          -- entry: dilution-series generation through detect
    BB2  bsbbif_2_t    -- calibration pass: dispose stock + 3 dilution droplets
    BB3  bsbbif_3_f    -- calibration fail: drain stock + 3 dilution droplets
    BB4  bsbbif_4_j    -- join: dispose 4 calibration signals; IL assay begins
    BB5  bsbbif_5_t    -- IL assay pass: dispose all three incubated droplets
    BB6  bsbbif_6_f    -- IL assay fail: drain all three incubated droplets
    BB7  bsbbif_7_j    -- terminal: dispose all 3 signal droplets, NOP

  CFG EDGES:
    BB1 -> BB2 (calSignalStock > 0)
    BB1 -> BB3 (calSignalStock <= 0)
    BB2 -> BB4
    BB3 -> BB4
    BB4 -> BB5 (ilSignalMut2 > 0)
    BB4 -> BB6 (ilSignalMut2 <= 0)
    BB5 -> BB7
    BB6 -> BB7
