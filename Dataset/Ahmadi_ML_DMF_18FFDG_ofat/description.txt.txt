PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Integrating Machine Learning and Digital Microfluidics for Screening Experimental Conditions
Source Link  : https://rscj.silverchair-cdn.com/rscj/content_public/journal/lc/23/1/10.1039_d2lc00764a/2/d2lc00764a.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/d2lc00764a
Journal      : Lab on a Chip, 2023, Vol. 23, pp. 81-91
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) on EWOD electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a new method for assay optimization on a digital microfluidic (DMF) device,
combining one-factor-at-a-time (OFAT) experimental design with machine learning (ML) predictive
modeling to optimize the synthesis yield of [18F]FDG (fluorodeoxyglucose) radiotracers. The method
is demonstrated on a seven-parameter radiosynthesis assay and is generalizable to any biological or
chemical assay on DMF that involves many parameters and levels.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. Base-case [18F]FDG synthesis to establish the reference fluorination efficiency (61.27%)
  2. OFAT variable experiments: seven synthesis runs each changing exactly one parameter,
     compared against the base by one-tailed one-sample t-test (P < 0.05 significance cutoff)
  3. ML modeling: linear regression (Ridge) and neural network (Keras) trained on a 55-experiment
     database to predict optimal conditions and generate a predictive model for synthesis yield
  4. On-chip neutral alumina disc purification of the optimized [18F]FDG product (~93% purity)

The OFAT approach reduced the required experiment count from 3^7 = 2187 (full factorial, no
replicates) to 8 experiments (1 base + 7 variable; 24 with 3 biological replicates). The OFAT
optimal fluorination efficiency was 70.82 +/- 1.54% (n=6). ML modeling further improved the
total crude radiochemical yield to 79.13% (vs 66.64% from OFAT alone), with a synthesis time
of ~19 +/- 2 min.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Substrate    : 2 inch x 3 inch chromium-coated glass slides (standard photolithography,
               Quintel Q-4000 mask aligner, 25400 dpi photomask from CAD/Art Services Inc.).
               AZ1500 positive photoresist; CR-4 chromium etchant; AZ-300 T photoresist stripper;
               MF321 developer.
Silane       : Silane A-174 (gamma-methacryloxypropyl trimethoxysilane) in DI water + 2-propanol
               (50:50:1 v/v), 15 min immersion, rinse, bake 80 C.
Dielectric   : Parylene-C, 7 um thick (SCS Labcoater 2 PDS 2010, Specialty Coating Systems).
Hydrophobic  : Teflon AF 1600 (The Chemours Company), 150 nm; Laurell spin coater (1500 rpm,
               60 s, 300 rpm/s acceleration), bake 165 C for 10 min.
Top plate    : ITO-coated glass (RS = 15-25 ohm, Delta Technologies CG-61IN-S207), cut to
               1 inch x 3 inch; Teflon AF 1600 spin-coated and baked as above.
Inter-plate gap : ~140 um (two strips of double-sided tape, 3M).

Reaction site   : 10 central electrodes; TEC/Peltier module (TE Technology) placed directly
                  below bottom plate; closed-loop temperature control via Arduino Uno,
                  two half-bridge driver chip, and low-resistance N-channel MOSFET.
Purification site: 5 electrodes aligned to 6 mm alumina-PDMS disc sandwiched between plates.
Reagent delivery: Gastight syringe (Hamilton 1002 C) + stepper motor (Arduino Uno) for [18F];
                  manual pipetting into reservoir edges for all other reagents.
Pogo-pin holder : 104 pogopins, 3 mm pitch; delivers 104 individual voltage inputs.
Actuation       : 160 Vrms sine wave at 15 kHz between top and bottom plates.
Software        : Open-source automation software (https://bitbucket.org/shihmicrolab/f_ahmadi_2022);
                  optocoupler switch stack applies high-voltage potentials to electrodes.
DI water        : Resistivity 15 MOhm cm-1.


REAGENTS
--------
Radiosynthesis (all Sigma-Aldrich, Oakville ON, unless noted):
  [18F]fluoride       : No-carrier-added; McConnell Brain Imaging Centre cyclotron (IBA Cyclone
                        18/9, 18 MeV protons on 98% [18O]-enriched H2O). Radioactivity measured
                        by dose calibrator (CRC 55 TR Capintec).
  Mannose triflate    : >=98% (TLC); substrate for [18F] nucleophilic fluorination (SN2).
  Kryptofix K222      : 4,7,13,16,21,24-hexaoxa-1,10-diazabicyclo[8.8.8]hexacosane, 98%.
  Potassium carbonate : K2CO3, 99.9%.
  Acetonitrile        : Anhydrous MeCN, 99.8%.
  Sodium hydroxide    : NaOH, 1N.
  Kryptofix/MeCN mix  : K222 + K2CO3 in anhydrous MeCN; pre-mixed off-chip; loaded as single
                        reservoir on DMF device to re-solvate dried [18F] after evaporation.
  Neutral alumina     : 50-300 um particle size (Waters Corp.); embedded in PDMS for on-chip
                        purification disc (6 mm diameter).
  Sep-Pak QMA         : Sep-Pak Accell Plus QMA Plus Light cartridges (Waters Corp.);
                        used off-chip for [18F] pre-concentration.

Analysis:
  TLC scanner         : MiniGITA star (Raytest USA Inc.); silica gel HLF 250 um plates
                        (Analtech Inc., 10x20 cm scored). Fluorination efficiency quantified
                        by radioactive TLC.
  Dose calibrator     : CRC 55 TR Capintec Inc.; total radioactivity measurement.
  Gamma spectrometer  : In-house; identity and purity of [18F]fluoride confirmed.


OFAT PARAMETERS AND RESULTS
----------------------------
Seven parameters affect [18F]FDG fluorination efficiency on DMF.
Base case yield: 61.27 +/- 3.88% (n=6); taken from Keng et al. 2012 and Chen et al. 2014 as
starting point. OFAT significance cutoff: P < 0.05, one-tailed one-sample t-test.

  ID  Parameter                        Base    Variable   p-value  Significant  ML Coeff  Effect
  P1  Mannose triflate / [18F] ratio   1.0     0.3        0.0297   Yes          +2.43     positive
  P2  Mannose triflate conc (mM)       30.0    20.0       0.0086   Yes          +3.74     positive
  P3  Radiolabeling temperature (C)    85      100        0.0282   Yes          +2.92     positive
  P4  Radiolabeling time (min)         5       7          0.0671   No           +0.04     negligible
  P5  NaOH concentration (M)          1.0     0.10       0.0001   Yes          +4.50     positive (dominant)
  P6  Deprotection temperature (C)    25      50         0.2778   No           +2.30     non-sig by OFAT; coeff discrepancy due to limited range (25-50 C) and small N
  P7  Deprotection time (min)         5       3          0.0041   Yes          -0.89     negative (shorter = better)

Significant parameters: P1, P2, P3, P5, P7 (5 of 7).
Excluded from final ML model: P4 (radiolabeling time), P6 (deprotection temperature).


MACHINE LEARNING MODELING
--------------------------
Database     : 55 experiments; initial conditions from literature (Keng 2012, Chen 2014),
               followed by OFAT-guided and expert-selected conditions.
Split        : 80% training (44 experiments), 20% test (11 experiments).
Normalization: Standardization (subtract mean, divide by SD, eqn (1) in paper).
Oversampling : Rare-condition datapoints (those with at least one infrequent parameter level)
               replicated in training set to balance distribution and reduce model bias
               (method: Borderline-SMOTE variant adapted for experimental data).
Cross-val    : 10-fold cross-validation on training set for all models.

Models:
  (i)  Single-input linear (7 models, one per parameter):
       RMSE train range: 13.89 +/- 1.1 to 15.53 +/- 1.3.
       Does not capture parameter interactions; insufficient for prediction.

  (ii) Multiple-input linear regression (Ridge, scikit-learn):
       All 7 variables as inputs. RMSE train: 11.47 +/- 1.0; RMSE test: 11.71.
       Regularization: L2 (Ridge); optimum regularization factor via GridSearchCV.
       Coefficients (ordered by significance) confirm OFAT significant parameters.

  (iii) Non-linear neural network (Keras):
        Architecture: input (7 neurons) -> hidden dense layer (4 neurons, ReLU, L2 reg) ->
        output (1 node; predicted FDG incorporation yield).
        RMSE train: 11.54 +/- 1.2; RMSE test: 13.26.
        Similar accuracy to linear model (limited by N=55 database size).
        Adam optimizer; learning rate 1; regularization factor 0.5; GridSearchCV tuned.

Best model   : Multiple linear regression (lowest test RMSE 11.71; practical and interpretable).
Final model  : Rebuilt using only the 5 significant parameters and their coefficients (Fig. S4).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | [18F] Delivery and Activation
  Reagents    : [18F]fluoride (no-carrier-added)
  Operation   : Syringe delivery to central reaction electrode; evaporation at 120 C for ~10 min
  Notes       : [18F] loaded at reaction center on bottom plate before ITO top plate is sealed.
                TEC heats 10 central electrodes to 120 C. H2[18O] carrier water removed by
                evaporation, activating [18F] for nucleophilic substitution.
                After evaporation, ITO top plate placed and secured; reagent reservoirs loaded.
                Kryptofix K222 + K2CO3 in anhydrous MeCN dispensed immediately after to
                re-solvate dried [18F] into the reaction droplet (1 min mix).

Step 2 | Mannose Triflate Dispense and Radiolabeling (BASE: P1=1.0, P2=30 mM, P3=85 C, P4=5 min)
  Reagents    : MannoseTriflate (30 mM in anhydrous MeCN)
  Operation   : Dispense unit droplet; merge with activated [18F]; heat at 85 C for 5 min
  Notes       : Base P1=1.0 (equal volume ratio). TEC PID-controls reaction site at 85 C.
                [18F] incorporates into mannose triflate via SN2 nucleophilic fluorination to
                yield the acetyl-protected intermediate.

Step 3 | Deprotection (BASE: P5=1.0 M NaOH, P6=25 C, P7=5 min)
  Reagents    : SodiumHydroxide (1N)
  Operation   : Dispense unit NaOH droplet; merge with labeled intermediate; heat at 25 C for 5 min
  Notes       : Base-catalyzed hydrolysis at room temperature (25 C) removes acetyl protecting
                groups from the fluorinated intermediate to yield deprotected [18F]FDG.
                TEC set to 25 C (room temperature; no active heating).

Step 4 | Base Case Detection
  Module      : fluorinationEfficiencyDetection
  Duration    : 30 s
  Instrument  : Radioactive TLC scanner (MiniGITA star); silica gel HLF 250 um TLC plates
  Expected    : 61.27 +/- 3.88% fluorination efficiency (n=6)
  QC gate     : baseSignal > 0 -> dispose, proceed to OFAT (BB2)
                baseSignal <= 0 -> drain, halt (BB3)

Step 5 | OFAT Variable Experiments (BB4: 7 experiments, V1-V7)
  Each experiment re-runs the full synthesis (Steps 1-3) changing exactly one parameter.
  All other parameters remain at base level.
  Each product detected by fluorinationEfficiencyDetection (30 s); signal and product
  both disposed inline within BB4.

  V1 (P2): Mannose triflate conc 30 -> 20 mM; P3=85 C, P4=5 min, P5=1.0 M, P6=25 C, P7=5 min
           Fluorination efficiency: 46.34% (p=0.0086, significant)

  V2 (P3): Radiolabeling temp 85 -> 100 C; P2=30 mM, P4=5 min, P5=1.0 M, P6=25 C, P7=5 min
           Fluorination efficiency: 74.92% (p=0.0282, significant)
           Device note: 110 C (model optimum) causes droplet movement fidelity loss due to
           evaporation; 100 C is the highest practically achievable radiolabeling temperature.

  V3 (P4): Radiolabeling time 5 -> 7 min; P3=85 C, all others base
           Fluorination efficiency: 54.56% (p=0.0671, non-significant)

  V4 (P5): NaOH conc 1.0 -> 0.10 M; all others base
           Fluorination efficiency: 15.62% (p=0.0001, most significant parameter)
           Device note: 1.4 M NaOH (model optimum) causes poor droplet movement fidelity;
           1.0 M is the practical upper limit for reliable on-chip actuation.

  V5 (P6): Deprotection temp 25 -> 50 C; all others base
           Fluorination efficiency: 66.12% (p=0.2778, non-significant by t-test)
           Note: ML coefficient = 2.30 despite non-significance; discrepancy due to limited
           range studied (25-50 C) and small dataset (N requires >1000 for accurate non-linear model).

  V6 (P7): Deprotection time 5 -> 3 min; all others base
           Fluorination efficiency: 42.79% (p=0.0041, significant, negative effect)
           Note: variable tested at lower level (3 min); negative ML coefficient (-0.89)
           means shorter deprotection coupled with higher conc and temp increases yield.

  V7 (P1): Volume ratio 1.0 -> 0.3 (less mannose triflate relative to [18F]); all others base
           Fluorination efficiency: 51.48% (p=0.0297, significant)

Step 6 | OFAT Optimal Synthesis
  Conditions  : P1=1.0, P2=30 mM, P3=100 C, P4=5 min, P5=1.0 M, P6=50 C, P7=5 min
  Expected    : 70.82 +/- 1.54% fluorination efficiency (n=6)
  QC gate     : ofatSignal > 0 -> proceed to purification (BB5)
                ofatSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB6)

Step 7 | On-Chip Neutral Alumina Disc Purification
  Reagent     : NeutralAlumina_Disc (6 mm PDMS-embedded disc, pre-placed at purification site)
  Operation   : Actuate product droplet to disc; move over adjacent electrodes for 40 min at 25 C
  Removes     : Kryptofix K222, unreacted [18F], F-
  Purity      : ~93.05 +/- 2.46% (6 mm disc, 40 min incubation)
  Detection   : radioactivityDetection module, 30 s (dose calibrator + TLC readout)
  Notes       : Disc fabricated by mixing neutral alumina beads into PDMS pre-curing; placed
                between top and bottom plates at purification site (5 electrodes). After
                incubation, product droplet actuated away from disc and manually pipetted for
                downstream processing (radioactivity measurement, QC, patient dosing).
  QC gate     : purifSignal > 0 -> dispose purified [18F]FDG (BB8)
                purifSignal <= 0 -> drain product (BB9)


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : ahmadi_ml_dmf_18FFDG_ofat.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG            : ahmadi_ml_dmf_18FFDG_ofat_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations in s or m (BioScript supports s and m only; h not supported)
                 [18F] evaporation : 10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
                 Kryptofix mix     :  1m  (IR: 60.0 SECOND)
                 Radiolabeling     :  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND, base and most variants)
                 Radiolabeling V3  :  7m  (IR: 420.0 SECOND)
                 Deprotection      :  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND, base and most variants)
                 Deprotection V6   :  3m  (IR: 180.0 SECOND)
                 Purification      : 40m  (IR: 2400.0 SECOND)
Modules        : fluorinationEfficiencyDetection, radioactivityDetection
Manifests      : F18_Fluoride, MannoseTriflate, Kryptofix_Acetonitrile_Mix,
                 SodiumHydroxide, NeutralAlumina_Disc
Design notes   : Each OFAT variable product (V1-V7) receives a fluorinationEfficiencyDetection
                 call followed by inline dispose of both signal and product within BB4.
                 This ensures the compiler tracks all 7 variable outputs as consumed and
                 represents the t-test comparison intent from the paper.
                 Dead manifests (Kryptofix_K222, PotassiumCarbonate, AnhydrousAcetonitrile)
                 removed; Kryptofix_Acetonitrile_Mix is the single on-chip reservoir abstraction.
                 NeutralAlumina_Disc dispensed via `dispense` and merged with product droplet
                 via `mix` to abstract the on-chip purification actuation (no disc-transfer
                 primitive exists in BioScript).
