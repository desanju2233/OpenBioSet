Source link: http://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/apoptosis.pdf
Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/C2LC20893H

Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic 6-Plex Caspase-3 Apoptosis Assay with Staurosporine Dose-Response (NucView Fluorogenic Readout)

REAGENTS USED:
  - HeLa Cells (human cervical adenocarcinoma cell line, ~2x10^6 cells/mL, ~800 cells per 0.3 uL sub-droplet)
  - Staurosporine (ST; apoptosis-inducing kinase inhibitor, dose series 0, 1.25, 2.5, 5, and 10 uM in high-glucose DMEM + 1% FBS)
  - NucView 488 (cell-permeable fluorogenic caspase-3 substrate, 5 uM in PBS)
  - Ac-DEVD-CHO (cell-permeable caspase-3 inhibitor, 10 uM in PBS; used only for the specificity-control zone)

STEPS:
1. Cell Seeding: Dispense droplets of HeLa cells from reservoirs and passively dispense onto six dry hydrophilic assay-zone sites; store devices inverted in a humidified chamber in an incubator overnight to allow cell adhesion and growth.
2. Staurosporine Dose-Response: Dispense staurosporine droplets at 0, 1.25, 2.5, 5, and 10 uM (with a duplicate 10 uM zone reserved for the inhibitor-control lane) and merge each with its corresponding cell-bearing zone to form 1.3 uL combined droplets; incubate 5 h in the humidified incubator.
2B. Inhibitor Specificity Control (zone 6 only): Dispense Ac-DEVD-CHO and merge with the duplicate 10 uM staurosporine zone; incubate 30 min prior to substrate addition.
3. Substrate Incubation: Dispense NucView 488 and merge with each of the six zones; incubate 45 min to allow caspase-3-mediated substrate cleavage and fluorogenic signal development.
4. Detection: Read fluorescence intensity of each of the six zones (lambda ex/em = 485/520 nm) using a benchtop multiwell plate reader.
5. Endpoint: Compare the inhibitor-treated zone's signal to the matched 10 uM staurosporine-only zone to confirm caspase-3 specificity, then discard the reaction droplets.

INTERPRETATION: Successful induction and detection of apoptosis is confirmed by increasing NucView (caspase-3 substrate) fluorescence intensity as a function of increasing staurosporine concentration across the five dose-response zones, and by significantly reduced fluorescence in the zone co-treated with the Ac-DEVD-CHO caspase-3 inhibitor relative to the matched staurosporine-only zone, confirming that the fluorogenic signal is specifically attributable to caspase-3 activation rather than nonspecific probe cleavage.

A NOTE ON HOW CLOSELY THIS MATCHES THE CITED SOURCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The authored BioScript code is a direct translation of the paper's "Caspase-3 assay" General Procedure (Steps 1-3, plus the optional Step 2B specificity control), covering overnight cell seeding, the five-point staurosporine dose-response, NucView substrate incubation, plate-reader detection, and the Ac-DEVD-CHO inhibitor control shown in Fig. 6. Incubation durations (overnight seeding, 5 h staurosporine exposure, 45 min NucView incubation, 30 min inhibitor pre-incubation) and reagent concentrations (0/1.25/2.5/5/10 uM ST, 5 uM NucView, 10 uM Ac-DEVD-CHO) are taken directly from the Experimental section. Because BioScript's grammar does not support parameterized concentrations or reagent arrays, each staurosporine concentration is modeled as its own named manifest (st_0, st_1_25, st_2_5, st_5, st_10a, st_10b) mapped one-to-one onto the paper's six physical assay zones, rather than a single "staurosporine" reagent varied by amount. Likewise, the paper's device-flipping/"upside-down" incubator storage and the humidified-chamber handling used between steps have no dedicated primitive in BioScript and are folded implicitly into the surrounding heat instructions (duration and nominal temperature only, not orientation or humidity). The paper's Experimental section states Step 3 is "identical to that described for step 2," implying the same 37C humidified-incubator conditions used for the staurosporine exposure; the authored script instead models the Step 2B/Step 3 incubations at 23C (bench-top temperature) as a simplifying assumption, which is flagged as a numeric mismatch below rather than silently corrected. The paper's separate 96-well-plate benchmark comparison, the wash-step cell-loss assay (Fig. 4), and the ITO-vs-polystyrene cell-spreading assay (Fig. 3) are physically distinct sub-experiments used for validation/comparison rather than part of the core screening assay itself, and are intentionally not modeled; only the 6-plex caspase-3 dose-response and specificity-control protocol (Figs. 5 and 6) is captured here.
