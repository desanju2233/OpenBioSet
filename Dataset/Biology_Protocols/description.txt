Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: BioCoder-Formalized Plasmid DNA Extraction Protocol (Alkaline Lysis Miniprep, Excerpt, Figure 3)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Rich Medium (LB, YT, or Terrific medium containing appropriate antibiotic, 2 ml)
  - Bacteria Colony (a single colony of transformed bacteria)
  - Alkaline Lysis Solution I (50 mM Glucose, 25 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10 mM EDTA pH 8.0, 100 ul)
  - Alkaline Lysis Solution II (freshly prepared, 0.2 N NaOH, 1% SDS w/v, 200 ul)
  - Alkaline Lysis Solution III (5 M sodium acetate, glacial acetic acid, 150 ul)

STEPS (as encoded in BioCoder, Figure 3; text output in Figure 4):
1. Preparation of cells: inoculate 2 ml Rich medium with a single colony of transformed bacteria; incubate with shaking for 12 hrs (overnight) at 37 C.
2. Measure out 1.5 ml of culture into a sterile 1.5 ml microcentrifuge tube. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 30 secs at 4 C; gently aspirate out the supernatant and discard it (leave the pellet as dry as possible).
3. Lysis of Cells: add 100 ul of Alkaline Lysis Solution I; resuspend the pellet by vortexing or shaking vigorously.
4. Add 200 ul of freshly prepared Alkaline Lysis Solution II; close the tube tightly and invert 5 times (do not vortex); store the tube on ice.
5. Add 150 ul of Alkaline Lysis Solution III; close the tube tightly and gently mix the contents by inverting until the precipitate stops sticking to the walls of the tube; store the tube on ice for 3-5 mins.
6. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 mins at 4 C and aspirate out the top layer into a clean sterile microfuge tube; discard the bottom layer.
(Steps 7-13, phenol-chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation, are omitted for brevity in the source paper itself.)
