Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Direct Loading of Blood for Plasma Separation and 21-Step Rubella Virus (RV) IgG Immunoassay on a Digital Microfluidic (DMF) Device[cite: 58]

REAGENTS USED:
  - Whole Blood (stabilized with EDTA, introduced via finger-dab onto the plasma separation membrane)[cite: 58]
  - Extraction Buffer (DPBS supplemented with 0.1% v/v Tetronic 90R4)[cite: 58]
  - Virus-Coated Paramagnetic Particles (Abbott Architect RV IgG assay particles, 1.5 x 10^8 mL^-1)[cite: 58]
  - DMF-Compatible Wash Buffer (DPBS supplemented with 0.1% v/v Tetronic 90R4)[cite: 58]
  - Conjugate Solution (HRP-conjugated anti-human IgG)[cite: 58]
  - Luminol Enhancer Solution (SuperSignal ELISA Femto chemiluminescent component)[cite: 58]
  - Stable Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2) Solution (SuperSignal ELISA Femto chemiluminescent component)[cite: 58]

STEPS:
1-4. On-Chip Blood-Plasma Separation: Dispense a five-unit extraction-buffer droplet (~6.5 uL) onto the extraction zone, and dab whole blood (~55 uL) onto the plasma separation membrane (SM) where cells are trapped while filtered plasma wicks through the transport membrane (TM) for ~3 min; drive the extraction droplet through the TM (~30 s) to extract and collect the filtered plasma into a droplet.
5. Particle Dispensing: Dispense a double-unit droplet of virus-coated paramagnetic particles into the assay zone and immobilize using the magnetic lens while moving supernatant to waste.
6. Plasma Capture: Actuate the separated plasma droplet to the assay zone, deliver it to the paramagnetic particles, actively mix for 7 min, immobilize particles, and move supernatant to waste.
7-12. Initial Wash Cycle: Dispense a double-unit droplet of wash buffer, deliver to the particles, actively mix for 30 s, immobilize particles, and move supernatant to waste; repeat this wash step a total of 6 times.
13. Conjugate Addition: Dispense a double-unit droplet of HRP-conjugated anti-human IgG conjugate solution, deliver to the particles, and actively mix for 5 min.
14-17. Post-Conjugate Wash: Repeat the wash cycle (dispense wash buffer, mix for 30 s, immobilize, move supernatant to waste) a total of 4 times.
18. Final Pre-Detection Wash: Dispense a double-unit droplet of wash buffer, mix for 10 s, actuate the droplet to the detection zone, immobilize particles, and move supernatant to waste.
19. Substrate Addition (Luminol): Dispense a single-unit droplet of luminol enhancer solution, deliver to the paramagnetic particles, and actively mix for 10 s.
20. Substrate Addition (H2O2): Dispense a single-unit droplet of stable H2O2 solution, deliver to the droplet containing particles, and actively mix for 80 s to initiate the chemiluminescent reaction.
21. Detection & Readout: Measure the resulting chemiluminescence for 120 s (collecting data at 60 Hz and recording the mean of the final 40 seconds) using the integrated photomultiplier tube (PMT).

INTERPRETATION: Rubella virus (RV) IgG concentration is quantified against a linear calibration curve (0-30 IU mL^-1), yielding an LOD of 1.9 IU mL^-1 and an LOQ of 3.6 IU mL^-1—both well below the World Health Organization diagnostic cut-off of 10 IU mL^-1 for RV immunity—confirming that the integrated platform successfully performs multi-step diagnostic assays directly from unprocessed whole blood without pre-processing.
