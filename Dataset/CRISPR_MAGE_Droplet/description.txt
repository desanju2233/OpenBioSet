Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Droplet-Based Digital Microfluidic CRISPR/Cas9-MAGE (CRMAGE) Recombineering for Automated Bacterial Strain Engineering (galK Disruption and Indigoidine Pathway Optimization in E. coli)

REAGENTS USED:
  - gRNA Plasmid (pMAZ-SK, curable CRISPR/Cas9 guide-RNA plasmid, 100-200 ng per reaction)
  - 70 bp Mutagenic Oligo (single-stranded recombineering oligo, 0.5 uL of 10 pmol/uL stock per reaction)
  - Electrocompetent E. coli Cells (MG1655/MG1616-derived, 50 uL per reaction)
  - Recovery Medium (Lennox LB with 100 ug/mL carbenicillin and 35 ug/mL chloramphenicol, 1 mL per reaction)
  - Kanamycin Selection Reagent (50 ug/mL final concentration, added post-recovery)
  - Anhydrotetracycline (aTet, 200 ng/mL, Cas9 induction/counter-selection reagent)
  - Galactose MacConkey Indicator Medium (1% galactose agar, colorimetric galK screen)
  - IPTG Induction Reagent (isopropyl beta-D-1-thiogalactopyranoside, indigoidine pathway induction)

STEPS:
1. Droplet loading: dispense gRNA plasmid, 70 bp oligo, and electrocompetent cell droplets (nanoliter-scale acoustic-printer droplets) into individually addressable chip wells via a Labcyte Echo 550 acoustic liquid handler.
2. On-chip mixing: actuate electrowetting (AC, 80-100 kHz, 10-100 ms pulse) to merge the plasmid, oligo, and cell droplets within each of the 100 individually addressable reaction chambers.
3. Electroporation: apply an exponential-decay voltage pulse across each chamber's electrode pair (60 V optimal for 20 um electrode gap, 100 V optimal for 100 um gap) to transform the merged droplet.
4. Recovery: incubate the transformed droplet in recovery medium for 2 h at 37 C.
5. Selection: add kanamycin and incubate 3 h at 37 C to select for cells retaining the CRISPR-MAGE plasmids.
6. Induction/counter-selection: add anhydrotetracycline and incubate overnight (~12 h) at 37 C to induce Cas9 expression, which counter-selects against unedited wild-type cells.
7. galK disruption screen: plate a recovered aliquot on 1% galactose MacConkey agar; galK-disrupted (mutant) colonies appear white while wild-type colonies ferment galactose and appear red, giving a colorimetric readout of editing success.
8. Indigoidine pathway optimization: for each of 6 gRNA/oligo target sets (2 targeting the bpsA/sfp promoter, 4 targeting the native glnA promoter), repeat steps 1-6, then add IPTG and incubate at 30 C to induce indigoidine biosynthesis.
9. Detection: quantify GFP-based transformation efficiency (excitation/emission 460 nm/515-535 nm) as a process-validation readout across the 100 parallel reactions, and quantify indigoidine yield per mutant strain by absorbance at 615 nm normalized to 800 nm background; discard analyzed droplets after measurement.

INTERPRETATION: Successful on-chip CRMAGE is confirmed by a galK wild-type-killing (disruption) rate statistically indistinguishable from benchtop CRMAGE (98+-3% on-chip, N=16, vs. 94+-5% benchtop, N=6), by over 80% successful GFP transformation across 100 parallel electroporation reactions, and by measurable increases in indigoidine absorbance for glnA-targeted mutants (e.g., glnA-40) relative to the unmutated control, supporting use of the platform for scalable, automated, combinatorial genetic pathway engineering.
