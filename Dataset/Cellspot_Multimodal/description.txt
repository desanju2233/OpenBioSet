Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Multimodal Digital Microfluidic (Cellspot) Testing Platform for Antibody-Producing Cell Lines - Live/Dead Viability Assay and Fluorescent-Linked Immunosorbent Assay (FLISA) for Secreted IgG Quantification

REAGENTS USED:
  - Cell Sample (CHO DG44-Adalimumab cell suspension / culture supernatant containing secreted IgG)
  - Hoechst 33342 Stain (nuclear stain, labels total cell population)
  - Propidium Iodide (PI) Stain (labels non-viable/dead cells)
  - Protein A Magnetic Beads (Dynabeads Protein A, captures secreted IgG)
  - Wash Buffer
  - Fluorescent Secondary Antibody (Alexa Fluor 488-conjugated, labels captured IgG for FLISA readout)

STEPS:
1-2. Viability staining: Load the cell sample droplet, combine with Hoechst 33342 stain, and incubate to label all cells (total count channel).
3. Combine the Hoechst-stained droplet with propidium iodide stain and incubate at room temperature to label non-viable cells (dead count channel).
4. Detection: Image the stained droplet in the blue (Hoechst, total cells) and red (PI, dead cells) fluorescence channels; calculate cell viability (%) as Hoechst count / (Hoechst count + PI count) x 100.
5-6. FLISA bead capture: Load a fresh cell-culture-supernatant droplet containing secreted IgG, combine with a suspension of Protein A magnetic beads, and incubate to capture IgG onto the beads.
7. Magnetic bead wash: Pellet the beads using an adjacent magnetic plate and wash the bead pellet three times with wash buffer to remove unbound material.
8. Fluorescent labeling: Combine the washed bead pellet with fluorescent (Alexa Fluor 488) secondary antibody and incubate to label captured IgG.
9. Post-label wash: Pellet the beads magnetically and wash twice more with wash buffer to remove unbound secondary antibody.
10. Detection: Image the bead pellet by fluorescence microscopy; quantify integrated fluorescence intensity per bead area and convert to IgG concentration (µg/mL) using a Gompertz-model standard curve fit to a human IgG reference standard.
