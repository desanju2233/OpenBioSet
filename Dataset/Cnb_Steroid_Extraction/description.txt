Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics (DMF) Coupled to HPLC-MS/MS for Quantification of Steroid Hormones in Core Needle Biopsy (CNB) Tissue Samples

REAGENTS USED:
  - Core Needle Biopsy tissue sample (2-8 mg human breast tissue, or rat abdominal fat tissue for method validation)
  - Lysing solvent (dichloromethane:acetone, 80:20, 25 uL droplets)
  - Extraction solvent (mixture B: deuterated internal standards estradiol-d3, androstenedione-d7, testosterone-d3, progesterone-d9, 2 ng/mL each, in methanol, 25 uL droplets)
  - Methanol (for porous polymer monolith disc activation)
  - Porous Polymer Monolith (PPM) disc (C12-functionalized solid-phase extraction matrix)
  - Dansyl chloride derivatization reagent (1:1 mixture of 1 mg/mL dansyl chloride in acetone and 100 mM sodium bicarbonate, pH 10.7)
  - Methanol/DI water (50:50 dilution solvent for HPLC injection)

STEPS:
1. Setup: Position a tissue sample and a PPM disc on a DMF device bottom plate; pipette a 25 uL droplet of lysing solvent (DCM:acetone 80:20) onto the sample and allow it to evaporate (~30 s); assemble the device with a top plate.
2. Extraction: Load 25 uL of extraction solvent (mixture B, containing deuterated internal standards) into a reservoir and drive it onto the tissue sample; actuate the droplet in a clockwise circular motion across the tissue for 5 min, then incubate statically for 10 min.
3. PPM Activation: Dispense a droplet of methanol onto the PPM disc, incubate for 5 min, then drive the remaining methanol to waste.
4. Cleanup (SPE): Drive the sample-extract droplet back and forth across the activated PPM disc for 2 min, then drive it away to a collection reservoir.
5. Repeat: Repeat the extraction (Step 2) and cleanup (Steps 3-4) cycle a total of four times, pooling the four resulting extract droplets; allow the pooled extract to dry at room temperature (~30 min).
6. Derivatization: Reconstitute the dried pooled extract in 25 uL of a 1:1 mixture of 1 mg/mL dansyl chloride in acetone and 100 mM sodium bicarbonate (pH 10.7); incubate in a 60 C water bath for 5 min.
7. Dilution: Mix with 25 uL of 50:50 methanol/DI water and transfer to a vial/96-well plate for analysis.
8. Detection: Analyze by HPLC-MS/MS (electrospray ionization, positive mode, multiple reaction monitoring) using a 22 min gradient run, quantifying estradiol, androstenedione, testosterone, and progesterone from the analyte/internal-standard peak-area ratio against a calibration curve.

INTERPRETATION: Successful steroid hormone quantification is confirmed by linear calibration curves (R^2 = 0.9967-0.9997) for all four hormones with limits of detection/quantification of 3.6/13.2, 1.6/5.4, 5.8/20.2, and 8.5/27.0 fmol for estradiol, androstenedione, testosterone, and progesterone respectively; extraction efficiencies for the DMF method (65.3%, 80.3%, and 89.1% for estradiol, androstenedione, and testosterone) comparable to conventional macroscale extraction; and measured hormone levels in human CNB breast tissue samples that differ significantly by menopausal status and hormone/aromatase-inhibitor therapy, supporting use of the method for personalized dosing and timing of antihormone or hormone-replacement therapies.
