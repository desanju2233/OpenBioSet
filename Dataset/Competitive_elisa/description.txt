Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Competitive ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Reference Antigen (Coated onto microplate wells as a capture target)
  - Blocking Buffer (Blocks non-specific binding sites)
  - Sample (Contains unknown target antigen to compete)
  - Labeled Antibody (Enzyme-conjugated antibody directed against the antigen)
  - Wash Buffer (Clears unbound reagents during processing)
  - Substrate (Generates signal inversely proportional to sample antigen concentration)

STEPS:
1. Coating: Dispense reference antigen and incubate at 4 C overnight (720 min).
2. Blocking: Add blocking buffer, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 60 min.
3. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer.
4. Premixing & Competition: Mix sample and labeled antibody together, then combine with the blocked plate; incubate at 37 C for 60 min to allow competitive binding.
5. Washing: Perform 3 final wash cycles using wash buffer to remove unbound complex components.
6. Substrate Reaction: Add substrate, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 15 min.
7. Detection & Readout: Measure signal (inversely correlated with sample antigen amount); trigger conditional disposal if signal $\le 1$.
