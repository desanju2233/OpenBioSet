Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/ac300305s

Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics Coupled to Nanoelectrospray Ionization Mass Spectrometry (DMF-nESI-MS) for Dried Blood Spot (DBS) Succinylacetone (SA) Quantification

REAGENTS USED:
  - Dried Blood Spot Punch (3.2 mm diameter filter-paper punch bearing dried whole blood, fortified with 13C5-SA internal standard)
  - Methanol (neat MeOH, pre-extraction solvent, 15 uL droplets)
  - SA Extraction/Derivatization/MS Reagent (6.5 mM hydrazine and 15 uM 13C5-SA in 80:20 acetonitrile:DI water with 0.1% v/v formic acid, 15 uL droplets)

STEPS:
1-2. Pre-extraction wash 1: Load a 15 uL methanol droplet, actuate onto the DBS punch, incubate 15 min at room temperature, then actuate the droplet away from the punch to a waste reservoir.
3-4. Pre-extraction wash 2: Repeat the load/incubate/waste-removal cycle a second time.
5-6. Pre-extraction wash 3: Repeat the load/incubate/waste-removal cycle a third time.
7. First Derivatization Step: Load a fresh 15 uL droplet of the hydrazine/13C5-SA/acetonitrile:water solvent, actuate onto the residual (methanol-washed) DBS punch, and incubate 15 min at 37 C on a hot-plate until the droplet evaporates.
8. Second Derivatization Step: Load a second fresh 15 uL droplet of the same hydrazine reagent, deliver it to the residual blood spot, and incubate for 1 min at room temperature (with circular actuation around the punch).
9. Detection: Actuate the derivatized droplet to the inlet of a pulled-glass capillary nanoESI emitter positioned near the mass spectrometer orifice; apply a DC potential to generate a stable nanoelectrospray and acquire MS/MS spectra, quantifying succinylacetone from the m/z 155->137 (SA) product-ion intensity relative to the m/z 160->142 (13C5-SA internal standard) product-ion intensity; then discard the analyzed droplet.
