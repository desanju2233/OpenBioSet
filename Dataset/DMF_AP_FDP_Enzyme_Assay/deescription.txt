Source link: http://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/DMF%20Enzyme%20Assays.pdf
Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/ac702269d

ASSAY TYPE

Digital-microfluidic homogeneous fluorogenic enzyme assays, including substrate quantitation, Michaelis–Menten enzyme kinetics, and competitive enzyme inhibition.

REAGENTS USED
ap_enzyme (Alkaline phosphatase in DEA buffer, 1 mM MgCl₂, 0.1% w/v Pluronic F-127)
fdp_substrate (Fluorescein diphosphate in DEA buffer)
dea_buffer (Diethanolamine buffer, pH 10.1)
inorganic_phosphate (Pi, competitive AP inhibitor)
UNIT NOTE

Base droplet volumes are 70 nL, merging into a 140 nL reaction droplet.

STEPS
Basic Assay: Dispense 1 unit of ap_enzyme and 1 unit of fdp_substrate, mix for 15 seconds, and detect fluorescence for 1 minute.
Substrate Quantitation: Run 5 separate reactions combining 1 unit of ap_enzyme with 1 unit of various fdp_substrate standards, mixing for 15 seconds and reading fluorescence for 1 minute.
Enzyme Kinetics: Dispense 1 unit of ap_enzyme and 1 unit of fdp_substrate, mix for 15 seconds, and monitor fluorescence continuously over 10 consecutive 30-second intervals.
Enzyme Inhibition: Pre-mix fdp_substrate with varying doses of inorganic_phosphate (0, 15, 30, and 45 μM equivalents) for 15 seconds, then merge with ap_enzyme, mix for 15 seconds, and record fluorescence over 5 consecutive 30-second intervals. Repeat this process for both 10 μM and 5 μM FDP baseline concentrations.
Discard all reaction droplets after detection.
