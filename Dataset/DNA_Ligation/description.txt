Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics (DMF) Parallel DNA Ligation for Recombinant DNA Cloning — Water-Oil Core-Shell Droplet Format with Multiple Insert Lengths

REAGENTS USED:
  - Vector DNA (linearized plasmid backbone droplets)
  - Insert DNA, 0.9 kb fragment
  - Insert DNA, 1.7 kb fragment
  - Insert DNA, 1.9 kb fragment
  - DNA Ligase Buffer (containing DNA ligase enzyme)

STEPS:
1. Dispense a vector DNA droplet and an insert DNA droplet (0.9 kb) from their respective reservoirs.
2. Merge and mix the vector and 0.9 kb insert droplets to pool them.
3. Dispense a droplet of DNA ligase buffer and merge/mix it with the pooled vector/insert droplet to initiate ligation.
4. Incubate the ligation reaction droplet at 16 C to allow the DNA ligase reaction to proceed, then discard the droplet.
5. Repeat steps 1-4 in parallel for the 1.7 kb insert: dispense fresh vector and 1.7 kb insert droplets, merge, add ligase buffer, incubate at 16 C, and discard.
6. Repeat steps 1-4 in parallel for the 1.9 kb insert: dispense fresh vector and 1.9 kb insert droplets, merge, add ligase buffer, incubate at 16 C, and discard.
7. Perform a fourth parallel reaction combining both the 0.9 kb and 1.9 kb inserts: dispense droplets of each insert, merge them together, then merge the combined-insert droplet with a fresh vector droplet, add ligase buffer, incubate at 16 C, and discard.

INTERPRETATION: Successful ligation is confirmed off-chip by transforming the resulting recombinant DNA into competent bacteria, culturing the transformed colonies, and confirming the presence of the correct insert fragment length by plasmid extraction and gel electrophoresis, with the four parallel on-chip reactions each yielding the expected vector-insert recombinant product corresponding to its designated insert length or combination.
