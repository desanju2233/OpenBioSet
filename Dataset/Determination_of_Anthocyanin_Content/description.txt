Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Determination of Anthocyanin Content (pH-Differential Method)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Sample Powder (plant tissue powder)
  - Potassium Chloride Buffer (0.025 mol/L, pH 1.0)
  - Sodium Acetate Buffer (0.4 mol/L, pH 4.5)

STEPS:
1. Combine sample powder with 0.025 mol/L potassium chloride buffer (pH 1.0) in a 1:8 ratio of extract to buffer.
2. Measure absorbance of the pH 1.0 mixture at 515 nm and 700 nm against a blank.
3. Combine sample powder with 0.4 mol/L sodium acetate buffer (pH 4.5) in a 1:8 ratio of extract to buffer.
4. Measure absorbance of the pH 4.5 mixture at 515 nm and 700 nm against a blank.

INTERPRETATION: Total monomeric anthocyanin content is calculated from the pH-differential absorbance readings [(A515 - A700)pH1.0 - (A515 - A700)pH4.5], which corrects for degradation products and turbidity; a higher differential absorbance value indicates greater anthocyanin content in the sample.
