Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Determination of Total Flavonoid Content (Aluminium Chloride Colorimetric Method)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Methanolic Extract
  - Distilled Water
  - Sodium Nitrite (5% w/v)
  - Aluminium Chloride (10% w/v)
  - Sodium Hydroxide (1 M)

STEPS:
1. Add 100 uL of extract to 4 mL of distilled water.
2. Add 0.3 mL of 5% sodium nitrite; incubate for 5 min.
3. Add 0.3 mL of 10% aluminium chloride; incubate for 6 min.
4. Add 2 mL of 1 M sodium hydroxide to the mixture.
5. Immediately dilute with 3.3 mL of distilled water and mix thoroughly.
6. Measure absorbance at 510 nm against a blank.

INTERPRETATION: Absorbance at 510 nm reflects the pink-colored aluminium-flavonoid complex formed in the reaction; higher absorbance indicates greater total flavonoid content in the extract, typically expressed as standard-equivalent units (e.g., quercetin equivalents) via a calibration curve.
