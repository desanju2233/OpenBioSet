Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Microfluidics-Enabled Digital Isothermal Cas13a Assay (MEDICA) - Enhanced One-Pot SHERLOCK for Viral HPV Detection

REAGENTS USED:

* LwaCas13a (CRISPR effector nuclease)
* crRNA (Target-specific guide RNA)
* rNTP Mix (Nucleotides for transcription)
* RNase Inhibitor (Murine RNase inhibitor)
* T7 RNA Polymerase (Transcribes target ssRNA from RPA amplicons)
* PolyU Reporter (Quenched fluorescent RNA reporter)
* RPA Lyophilized Pellet (Recombinase polymerase amplification enzymes)
* Forward & Reverse Primers (With T7 promoter overhangs)
* Reaction Buffer (Optimized Tris-HCl buffer with KCl, PEG 8000, Tween 20, and DTT)
* MgOAc (Magnesium acetate initiator to trigger RPA)
* HPV Sample (Extracted viral target DNA/RNA)
* Fluorinated Oil (Novec 7500 with surfactant for water-in-oil emulsion)

STEPS:

1. Master Mix Preparation: Combine reaction buffer, LwaCas13a, crRNA, rNTPs, RNase inhibitor, T7 RNA polymerase, PolyU reporter, primers, and RPA enzymes without MgOAc to prevent premature pre-amplification. Add the HPV sample to the master mix.
2. Initiator Phase Preparation: Prepare a separate aqueous phase containing MgOAc and reaction buffer.
3. Droplet Partitioning: Co-encapsulate the target master mix and the MgOAc phase in fluorinated oil using a flow-focusing droplet generator (~30 µm droplets) to trigger the reaction exclusively upon droplet formation.
4. Isothermal Incubation: Incubate the emulsion droplets at 37 C for 30 min to drive simultaneous RPA amplification, T7 transcription, and Cas13a collateral cleavage.
5. Qualitative & Quantitative Detection: Perform a real-time checkpoint at 10 min for qualitative detection and an end-point absolute quantification readout at 25 min via Poisson distribution counting of positive droplets.

INTERPRETATION: MEDICA eliminates background noise and premature pre-amplification by physically separating the magnesium initiator from the master mix prior to droplet co-encapsulation, achieving background-free absolute digital quantification with a wide dynamic range from 0.3 cp/uL to 1,000,000 cp/uL.
