Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Direct ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Antigen (Target protein sample coated directly onto the microplate)
  - Blocking Buffer (Seals unoccupied binding sites to prevent non-specific adsorption)
  - Labeled Primary Antibody (Enzyme-conjugated primary antibody directed against the target antigen)
  - Wash Buffer (Removes unbound components between incubation steps)
  - Substrate (Chromogenic or fluorogenic substrate for enzymatic signal generation)

STEPS:
1. Coating: Add antigen solution to the plate wells and incubate at 4 C overnight (720 min) to immobilize the target.
2. Blocking: Add blocking buffer, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 60 min to block free binding sites.
3. Washing: Perform 3 successive washes using wash buffer to remove unbound blocking agents.
4. Incubation: Add labeled primary antibody, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min for direct binding to the coated antigen.
5. Washing: Perform 3 successive washes using wash buffer to remove unbound labeled antibody.
6. Substrate Reaction: Add substrate, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 15 min for color/fluorescence development.
7. Detection & Readout: Measure signal intensity via detection; if signal meets the threshold ($\ge 1$), dispose of or process the reaction mixture.
