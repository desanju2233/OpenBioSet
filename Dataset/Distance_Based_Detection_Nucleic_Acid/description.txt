Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics with Distance-Based Detection (DMF-DBD) for Semi-Quantitative Nucleic Acid Diagnostics (RT-LAMP detection of SARS-CoV-2 from saliva)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Saliva Sample (lysed, column-extracted and purified viral RNA)
  - Lysis Buffer
  - RT Mastermix (reverse-transcription reagents, converts viral RNA to cDNA)
  - LAMP Mastermix (isothermal amplification reagents for cDNA)
  - Capto(TM) Adhere Agarose Bead Suspension (amplicon clean-up reagent)
  - Wash Buffer
  - SYBR(TM) Safe Intercalating Dye (fluorescent staining reagent for purified LAMP amplicons)

STEPS:
1. Sample lysis: Combine the saliva sample with lysis buffer and incubate at 95 C for 5 min to release viral RNA.
2. Reverse transcription: Mix the lysate with RT mastermix; incubate 15 min at 42 C then 5 min at 65 C to reverse-transcribe viral RNA into cDNA.
3. RT-LAMP amplification: Mix the cDNA with LAMP mastermix and incubate isothermally at 65 C for 30 min to generate LAMP amplicons.
4. Amplicon clean-up: Mix the LAMP product with a suspension of Capto(TM) Adhere agarose beads; incubate 5 min at 25 C, then wash three times with wash buffer to remove unincorporated primers and reduce background fluorescence.
5. Dye staining: Mix the purified, bead-cleaned amplicons with SYBR(TM) Safe intercalating dye.
6. Distance-based detection: Deliver the stained amplicon mixture to the distance-based detection (DBD) paper substrate; measure the resulting fluorescent signal (travel distance/intensity) to semi-quantitatively determine viral load in the sample, then discard the analyzed droplet.
