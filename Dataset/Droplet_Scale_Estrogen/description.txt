Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Droplet-Scale Estrogen Assays in Breast Tissue, Blood, and Serum (Liquid-Liquid Extraction and ELISA Readout)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Raw Sample (whole blood, serum, or breast tissue homogenate)
  - Lysing Solvent (DCM/acetone, 80:20 v/v)
  - Methanol (polar extraction and reconstitution solvent)
  - Isooctane (nonpolar extracting solvent)
  - ELISA Reagent

STEPS:
1. Sample Loading: Load 1 unit of raw sample into the sample reservoir and let it dry on the surface[cite: 45].
2. Lysis: Dispense lysing solvent, mix with the sample for 60s, heat at 23C for 1m, and dispose of the solvent; repeat this cycle 5 times[cite: 45].
3. Primary Extraction: Dispense methanol to dissolve the dried, lysed steroid residue and mix for 30s[cite: 45]. Deliver a unit droplet into the isooctane reservoir, mix for 20s for liquid-liquid extraction, and dispose of the isooctane[cite: 45].
4. Re-Extraction Pooling: Repeat the methanol extraction and isooctane clean-up cycle 4 times, pooling the extract droplets into the output reservoir[cite: 45].
5. Reconstitution: Dry the pooled extract by heating at 25C for 10m, then reconstitute with a unit of methanol and mix for 30s[cite: 45].
6. Detection: Dispense ELISA reagent, mix with the final extract for 60s, and detect fluorescence/readout for 5m before disposing of the mixture[cite: 45].

INTERPRETATION: Quantifies the concentration of estradiol extracted from biological samples via liquid-liquid extraction followed by an ELISA-based readout.
