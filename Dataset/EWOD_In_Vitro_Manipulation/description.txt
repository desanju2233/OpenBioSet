Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Electrowetting-on-Dielectric (EWOD) Digital Microfluidics for In Vitro Maturation, Fertilization, and Individual On-Chip Culture/Manipulation of Bovine Embryos

REAGENTS USED:
  - Cumulus Oocyte Complexes (COCs), abattoir-derived, aspirated from 4-8 mm follicles
  - TCM-199 maturation medium (bicarbonate-buffered, supplemented with gentamycin and EGF)
  - Gentamycin (50 mg/mL)
  - Epidermal Growth Factor, EGF (20 ng/mL)
  - HEPES-TALP medium (oocyte/gamete wash buffer)
  - IVF-TALP medium (bicarbonate-buffered Tyrode solution for sperm preparation/fertilization)
  - Bovine Serum Albumin, BSA (6 mg/mL in sperm medium; 4 mg/mL in culture medium)
  - Heparin (25 ug/mL, sperm capacitation agent)
  - Frozen-thawed bull spermatozoa (Percoll-gradient selected, 1x10^6 cells/mL)
  - SOFaa-ITS-BSA culture medium (synthetic oviductal fluid with amino acids, insulin, transferrin, selenium, BSA)
  - Tween 80 surfactant (0.01% v/v, added to on-chip droplets to prevent protein fouling of the EWOD surface)
  - Fresh/pre-equilibrated top-up culture medium droplet (2-5 uL, merged on-chip at 96 hpi)

STEPS:
1. In Vitro Maturation (IVM): Wash and culture COCs in groups of 60 per 500 uL modified TCM-199 medium supplemented with gentamycin and EGF; incubate 22 h at 38.5 C in 5% CO2.
2. Sperm preparation: Segregate frozen-thawed bull semen on a 45:90% Percoll gradient; wash the viable-sperm pellet in IVF-TALP and adjust to 1x10^6 cells/mL in IVF-TALP supplemented with BSA and heparin.
3. In Vitro Fertilization (IVF): Wash matured oocytes in HEPES-TALP, then co-incubate with the prepared spermatozoa for 21 h in IVF-TALP at 38.5 C in 5% CO2.
4. Denudation: Vortex the presumed zygotes to remove surrounding cumulus cells.
5. On-chip droplet loading: At 48 hpi, select 2- to 4-cell stage zygotes, wash in SOFaa-ITS-BSA medium supplemented with 0.01% Tween 80, and suspend each embryo individually in a 5 uL droplet of this medium; pipette the droplet between the top (ground) and bottom (driving-electrode) plates of the EWOD chip, surrounded by oil.
6. Droplet actuation off the loading edge: Apply 70-90 V DC to the driving electrode (top electrode grounded) to move the embryo-containing droplet away from the chip edge and the oil/air interface.
7. Cleavage assessment: At 48 hpi, evaluate embryo cleavage by morphological grading under a stereomicroscope.
8. Dynamic on-chip manipulation: Every 24 h, move the embryo-containing droplet to the next electrode pair by manual charging of the corresponding contact pad, keeping the embryo individually tracked on-chip through the 2-cell, 4-cell, 8-cell, and morula stages.
9. On-chip medium supply: At 72 hpi, pipette a 2 uL pre-equilibrated droplet of SOFaa-ITS-BSA-Tween 80 medium onto the chip; at 96 hpi, actuate the embryo-containing droplet toward this on-chip droplet and merge them by contact to refresh the culture medium.
10. Continued culture and blastocyst assessment: Continue on-chip incubation with periodic electrode-pair moves through 192 hpi; assess blastocyst formation by morphological grading under a stereomicroscope; discard/dispose of the droplet after assessment.

INTERPRETATION: Successful on-chip embryo manipulation is indicated by comparable cleavage rates (~48 hpi) and blastocyst rates (~192 hpi) between individually cultured droplet embryos (static Petri-dish droplets and on-chip "squeezed" droplets) and conventional static controls, together with visual confirmation that individual embryos progress through successive cleavage stages (2-cell to morula) while being moved and supplied with fresh medium on the EWOD chip, supporting digital microfluidics as a viable platform for non-invasive, individual embryo handling in assisted reproductive technologies.
