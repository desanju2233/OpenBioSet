Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Droplet-Based Method (ENTRAP) for High-Throughput Conjugal DNA Transfer Between Bacillus subtilis Strains

REAGENTS USED:
  - B. subtilis Donor Strain JAB981 (XPORT donor, engineered with a modified integrative conjugative element (ICE), GFPmut2 reporter, xylose-inducible conjugation machinery)
  - B. subtilis Recipient Strain JAB545 (cured of ICEBs1, chloramphenicol-resistance marker)
  - LB Miller Broth/Agar with selection antibiotics (tetracycline, D-alanine, chloramphenicol)
  - Xylose Inducer (1% final concentration, induces donor conjugation machinery)
  - Phosphate Buffered Saline (PBS) Wash Buffer
  - Tris-Spizizen Salts (TSS) Buffer (resuspension/co-incubation media)
  - Carrier Oil/Surfactant Phase (Novec 7500 with 1.25% v/v PicoSurf)
  - Selection Agar (LB agar + 10 ug/mL tetracycline for transconjugant selection; control plates with D-alanine+tetracycline or no antibiotic)
  - IPTG Induction Reagent (1 mM, induces GFPmut2 reporter expression post-conjugation)

STEPS:
1. Culture donor (JAB981) and recipient (JAB545) strains separately in selective LB media to OD600 0.8-1.2, then subculture into fresh selective media.
2. Grow the donor strain to OD600 0.2, then add xylose to a final concentration of 1% to induce the conjugation machinery (critical step - no conjugation occurs without this).
3. Continue culturing both donor and recipient strains to OD600 0.8-1.0.
4. Pellet both cultures by centrifugation, wash twice with PBS, and resuspend in TSS buffer (or LB + D-alanine) to a final OD600 of 0.6.
5. Combine donor and recipient cells at a 1:1 ratio.
6. Load the cell mixture and the oil/surfactant carrier phase into separate syringes and infuse them into the ENTRAP microfluidic droplet-generator chip (cell suspension at 400 uL/h, carrier phase at 2000 uL/h) to encapsulate donor-recipient pairs in monodisperse droplets.
7. Flow the generated droplets into a downstream micro-vessel chip and co-incubate for at least 1 h at ambient temperature or 37 C to allow conjugation to occur within droplets.
8. Flow droplets out of the micro-vessel chip and plate an aliquot onto tetracycline selection agar (spreading/breaking the droplets with a sterile L-spreader) to select for transconjugants; also plate control aliquots onto D-alanine+tetracycline agar (donors+transconjugants) and non-selective agar (recipients+transconjugants) for conjugation-efficiency calculations.
9. Incubate all plates ~16 h at 37 C, then inspect for transconjugant colonies on the tetracycline selection plates.
10. Pick transconjugant colonies, culture in tetracycline-supplemented media, induce with 1 mM IPTG at OD600 ~0.2, and quantify GFPmut2 expression by flow cytometry or plate-reader fluorescence (normalized to OD600) to confirm successful DNA transfer.

INTERPRETATION: Successful conjugation is confirmed by colony growth specifically on the tetracycline selection plates (indicating transconjugants), a conjugation efficiency calculated as the ratio of transconjugant CFUs to donor CFUs across at least three biological replicates, and elevated GFPmut2 fluorescence (relative to uninduced/negative controls) in IPTG-induced transconjugant cultures, with the droplet-based ENTRAP method yielding higher conjugation efficiency than traditional free-culture co-incubation.
