Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics (DMF) Oil-Free Homogeneous Enzyme Kinetics Assay — Alkaline Phosphatase (AP) Activity and Inorganic-Phosphate Product Inhibition, Monitored via Fluorescein Diphosphate (FDP) Fluorogenic Substrate

REAGENTS USED:
  - Alkaline Phosphatase (AP) enzyme droplets
  - Fluorescein Diphosphate (FDP) fluorogenic substrate droplets
  - Inorganic Phosphate solution (product-inhibition titrant)
  - Distilled Water (dilution/quench droplets)

STEPS:
1. Dispense an AP droplet and an FDP droplet on an oil-free DMF device.
2. Merge and actively mix the AP and FDP droplets to initiate the enzymatic reaction, converting FDP into fluorescent fluorescein.
3. Incubate the merged droplet at room temperature to allow the reaction to proceed.
4. Measure the baseline fluorescence intensity of the fluorescein product to establish uninhibited AP reaction kinetics.
5. Titrate inorganic phosphate into the reaction droplet across a series of points to assess product inhibition: for each point, dispense a fresh phosphate droplet, merge/mix it with the reaction droplet, incubate, and re-measure fluorescence intensity.
6. Repeat the phosphate titration/measurement cycle across multiple points to build a dose-response inhibition curve.
7. Dilute/quench the final reaction droplet with distilled water and discard it.

INTERPRETATION: Successful assay performance is confirmed by AP enzyme kinetic constants (e.g., Km, Vmax) that are consistent with values reported in the literature for conventional macroscale assays, and by a phosphate-concentration-dependent decrease in fluorescence signal consistent with competitive product inhibition — demonstrating that an oil-free DMF platform supports quantitative homogeneous enzyme kinetics studies without partitioning of hydrophobic reagents into a carrier-oil phase.
