Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of Ascorbic Acid Content (2,6-Dichloroindophenol Method)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Apple Fruit Tissue
  - Meta-Phosphoric Acid (1% w/v)
  - 2,6-Dichloroindophenol Reagent

STEPS:
1. Cut apple fruit into small pieces; homogenize 1 g of tissue in 10 mL of 1% meta-phosphoric acid for 1 min.
2. Agitate the mixture slowly at room temperature for 45 min, then vortex.
3. Centrifuge at 1370 x g for 10 min.
4. Filter the supernatant using Whatman No. 1 filter paper.
5. Mix 1 mL of filtrate with 9 mL of 2,6-dichloro-indophenol reagent.
6. Measure absorbance at 515 nm.

INTERPRETATION: Absorbance at 515 nm reflects the reduction of the blue 2,6-dichloroindophenol dye by ascorbic acid (vitamin C) in the extract; a greater decrease in dye absorbance corresponds to higher ascorbic acid content, typically quantified against an ascorbic acid standard calibration curve.
