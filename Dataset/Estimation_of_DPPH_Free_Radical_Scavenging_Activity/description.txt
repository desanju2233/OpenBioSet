Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of DPPH Free Radical Scavenging Activity

REAGENTS USED:
  - Methanolic Extract (various concentrations)
  - DPPH Solution (100 uM 2,2-diphenyl-1-picrylhydrazyl, prepared in methanol)

STEPS:
1. Combine 40 uL of methanolic extract (at a given concentration) with 160 uL of 100 uM DPPH solution (in methanol).
2. Incubate the mixture in the dark at room temperature for 30 min.
3. Measure absorbance at 517 nm.

INTERPRETATION: A decrease in absorbance at 517 nm relative to a DPPH-only control reflects free-radical scavenging by the extract; percent scavenging activity is calculated from the absorbance decrease, with greater scavenging (larger decrease) indicating higher antioxidant capacity of the extract.
