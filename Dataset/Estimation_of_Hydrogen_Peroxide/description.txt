Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2) Content in Plant Leaf Tissue

REAGENTS USED:
  - Leaf Tissue (control and infected samples, crushed in liquid nitrogen)
  - Trichloroacetic Acid (TCA)
  - Potassium Iodide (KI)
  - Potassium Phosphate Buffer (10 mM, pH 6.5)

STEPS:
1. Crush 100 mg of leaf tissue (control or infected) with liquid nitrogen and mix with 1 mL reaction mixture comprising 0.25 mL TCA, 0.5 mL KI, and 0.25 mL potassium phosphate buffer (10 mM, pH 6.5).
2. Vortex and homogenize the reaction for 10 min at 4 C.
3. Centrifuge the homogenate at 12,000 x g for 15 min at 4 C.
4. Incubate the collected supernatant at ~25 C for 20 min.
5. Measure absorbance at 390 nm.

INTERPRETATION: Absorbance at 390 nm reflects the KI-mediated colorimetric detection of H2O2 in the tissue extract; higher absorbance indicates greater H2O2 accumulation, and infected-tissue readings are typically compared against control-tissue readings to assess oxidative stress response.
