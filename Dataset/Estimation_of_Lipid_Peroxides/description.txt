Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of Lipid Peroxides (TBARS) in Plant Leaf Tissue

REAGENTS USED:
  - Leaf Tissue (control and infected samples, ground in liquid nitrogen)
  - Trichloroacetic Acid (TCA), 0.1% (w/v) for extraction
  - TBA Reagent (0.5% thiobarbituric acid in 20% trichloroacetic acid)

STEPS:
1. Grind leaf tissue (control or infected) with liquid nitrogen into a fine powder; homogenize 100 mg of powder with 1 mL of 0.1% (w/v) TCA.
2. Centrifuge the extract at 12,000 x g for 15 min at 4 C; collect the supernatant.
3. Mix 1 mL of 0.5% TBA in 20% TCA with 0.5 mL of the supernatant.
4. Heat the mixture at 95 C for 30 min in a dry bath, then cool at ice-cold temperature.
5. Centrifuge the mixture at 14,000 x g for 15 min at 4 C.
6. Measure absorbance at 532 nm, then measure absorbance at 600 nm to correct for unspecific turbidity; subtract A600 from A532 to obtain corrected absorbance.

INTERPRETATION: The corrected absorbance (A532 - A600) reflects malondialdehyde (MDA)-TBA complex formation and is used as a measure of lipid peroxidation (TBARS); higher corrected absorbance indicates greater oxidative membrane damage, with infected-tissue readings typically compared against control-tissue readings.
