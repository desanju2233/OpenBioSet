Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of Total Phenolic Content (TPC) by the Folin-Ciocalteu Colorimetric Assay

REAGENTS USED:
  - Methanolic Extract (plant tissue extract in methanol)
  - Folin-Ciocalteu Reagent (diluted 1:9 with water)
  - Sodium Carbonate Solution (5% w/v)

STEPS:
1. Combine 100 uL of methanolic extract with 500 uL of diluted Folin-Ciocalteu reagent (1:9 diluted with water); keep the mixture at room temperature (~28-30 C).
2. After 5 min incubation, add 400 uL of 5% sodium carbonate to the mixture.
3. Incubate the mixture for 20 min in the dark.
4. Measure absorbance at 765 nm.

INTERPRETATION: The developed blue color is proportional to the total phenolic content of the extract; absorbance at 765 nm is compared against a gallic-acid (or equivalent standard) calibration curve to express TPC in standard-equivalent units per gram of tissue, with higher absorbance indicating greater phenolic content.
