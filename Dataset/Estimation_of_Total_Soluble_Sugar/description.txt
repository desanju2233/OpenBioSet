Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Estimation of Total Soluble Sugar (Anthrone Method)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Apple Fruit Tissue
  - Methanol (extraction solvent)
  - Deionized Water
  - Anthrone Reagent (0.1 g anthrone dissolved in concentrated sulfuric acid, 0.2% v/v)

STEPS:
1. Crush 3 g of apple fruit tissue into 10 mL of methanol; homogenize for 2 min at room temperature.
2. Centrifuge the homogenate at 10,000 x g for 15 min; collect the supernatant.
3. Add 100 uL of supernatant to 900 uL of deionized water (final volume 1 mL); keep in an ice bath.
4. After 2 min of incubation, add 2 mL of cold anthrone reagent to the mixture.
5. Heat the reaction for 10 min in a boiling water bath.
6. Cool the reaction, then measure absorbance at 620 nm.

INTERPRETATION: Absorbance at 620 nm reflects the anthrone-sulfuric acid colorimetric reaction with soluble carbohydrates; higher absorbance indicates greater total soluble sugar content in the fruit tissue extract, typically compared against a glucose (or equivalent standard) calibration curve.
