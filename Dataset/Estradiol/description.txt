Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic (DMF) Tissue Extraction Coupled to Magnetic Bead-Based Chemiluminescent Immunoassay for Estradiol (E2) Quantification in Core-Needle Biopsy (CNB) Samples

REAGENTS USED:
  - Core Needle Biopsy (CNB) tissue homogenate (~5 mg breast tissue sample)
  - Methanol (neat, extraction solvent, 4.8 uL droplets)
  - Immunoassay Reconstitution Buffer (BSA in DPBS with Tetronic 90R4, 4.8 uL droplet)
  - Anti-E2 Coated Paramagnetic Microparticles (1.6 uL droplet)
  - Wash Buffer (Tris-based DMF wash buffer, 1.6 uL droplets)
  - Estradiol-HRP Conjugate (E2 conjugated to horseradish peroxidase via 6-CMO, 1.6 uL droplet)
  - Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2) substrate solution (0.8 uL droplet)
  - Luminol/Enhancer Solution (0.8 uL droplet)

STEPS:
1. Load the CNB tissue homogenate onto the bottom plate and affix the top plate bearing the hydrophilic anchor.
2-3. Dispense a 4.8 uL methanol extraction-solvent droplet, deliver it to the CNB tissue sample, and extract by moving the droplet in a circular pattern around the tissue for 5 min.
4. Drive the sample-extract droplet away from the tissue to the hydrophilic anchor and evaporate at room temperature for 5 min.
5-6. Repeat steps 2-4 once more (second extraction/evaporation cycle onto the anchor).
7. Redissolve the dried analyte on the anchor: dispense a 4.8 uL droplet of immunoassay reconstitution buffer onto the anchor and actuate for 30 s.
8. Dispense a 1.6 uL droplet of anti-E2 paramagnetic microparticles and engage the magnet to separate the particles from the diluent.
9. Deliver the 4.8 uL reconstituted sample extract to the immobilized particles for resuspension.
10. Actively mix the particles with the sample for 6 min, then engage the magnet and drive the supernatant to waste.
11-13. Wash cycle: dispense a 1.6 uL wash-buffer droplet, deliver it to the particles, resuspend/mix for 10 s, engage the magnet, and drive the supernatant to waste; repeat this cycle a total of 4 times.
14. Dispense a 1.6 uL droplet of E2-HRP conjugate and deliver it to the immobilized particles for resuspension.
15. Actively mix for 2 min.
16-18. Repeat the wash cycle (steps 11-13) a total of 4 times.
19. Dispense a 0.8 uL droplet of H2O2 and deliver it to the immobilized particles.
20. Dispense a 0.8 uL droplet of luminol/enhancer solution and deliver it to the immobilized particles, combining with the H2O2 droplet.
21. Actively mix the combined droplet for 10 min and measure chemiluminescence using the integrated photomultiplier tube (PMT); then discard the droplet.

INTERPRETATION: E2 concentration in each tissue extract is determined against a four-parameter logistic calibration curve built from ten E2 standards (0-20,000 pg/mL), and reported as the amount of analyte detected relative to sample mass. The on-chip immunoassay was validated against HPLC-MS/MS (strong positive correlation, r = 0.996, though with reduced % recovery reflected in a slope <1), correctly distinguished lower endogenous E2 in lactating vs. non-lactating rat breast tissue (p = 0.0454), and was applied to 30 paired human CNB samples from 15 post-menopausal patients before and after one week of aromatase inhibitor therapy (Letrozole plus Estrace hormone-replacement therapy), revealing three distinct patient response groups (decreased, unchanged, and increased breast-tissue E2), supporting the platform's potential use for personalized monitoring and dosing of aromatase inhibitor therapy.
