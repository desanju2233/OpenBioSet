Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics (DMF) Colorimetric Two-Enzyme Glucose Assay with Integrated Absorbance Detection, on an Oil-Filled Two-Plate Device

REAGENTS USED:
  - Glucose Oxidase/Peroxidase Colorimetric Reagent (two-enzyme reaction mixture)
  - Physiological Sample (e.g., whole blood, serum, urine, or saliva)

STEPS:
1. Dispense a droplet of the glucose oxidase/peroxidase colorimetric reagent.
2. Dispense a droplet of the physiological sample to be tested.
3. Merge and mix the reagent and sample droplets to initiate the two-enzyme colorimetric reaction.
4. Incubate the merged reaction droplet at 37 C to allow the coupled glucose oxidase/peroxidase reaction to develop a color change proportional to glucose concentration.
5. Measure the absorbance of the reaction droplet with the integrated LED/photodiode absorbance detector.
6. Discard the analyzed reaction droplet.

INTERPRETATION: Successful glucose quantification is confirmed by a linear absorbance response over the clinically relevant range of approximately 25-300 mg/dl glucose, with glucose values obtained from physiological samples (whole blood, serum, urine, saliva) that agree with reference measurements — except in the case of urine, where endogenous uric acid can interfere with the enzymatic reaction, underscoring the importance of sample pretreatment for clinical diagnostic use.
