Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Hemagglutination Assays for Blood Typing, Donor Compatibility Testing, and Hematocrit Analysis

REAGENTS USED:
  - Whole Blood
  - Reconstitution Solvent
  - Anti-A, Anti-B, Anti-AB, and Anti-D Blood Typing Antibodies (lyophilized)
  - Hematocrit Reagent (chemical agglutination agent)

STEPS:
1. Reconstitute lyophilized antibodies and hematocrit reagents using the reconstitution solvent and mix for 5s.
2. Meter whole-blood unit-droplets for each parallel test channel (A, B, AB, D, and hematocrit).
3. Merge each whole-blood droplet with its respective reconstituted reagent and mix for 10s to form the test droplet.
4. Perform digital aperture absorbance detection (DAAD) imaging readout to score agglutination states for the ABO/RhD blood-typing panel.
5. Apply threshold conditions to selectively dispose of nonagglutinated (negative) droplets, and perform quantitative DAAD scoring for hematocrit.
6. Clear all remaining test droplets from the array.
