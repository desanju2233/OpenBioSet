Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic (DMF) Screening of Hepatotoxicity Using 3D Self-Assembled Hepatic Organoids (HepG2/NIH-3T3 Spheroids)

REAGENTS USED:
  - HepG2/NIH-3T3 Collagen Organoid Suspension (free-floating hepatic organoids self-assembled from HepG2 hepatocyte-like cells and NIH-3T3 fibroblasts in a collagen hydrogel matrix, confined on-chip by SU-8 pillars)
  - Culture Media (standard growth media used for organoid maintenance and as diluent for the on-chip acetaminophen dilution series)
  - Acetaminophen (cytotoxic hepatotoxin, used to generate a dose-response dilution series on-chip)
  - Hoechst 33342 (nuclear stain, labels total cell population)
  - Caspase-3 Activity Reporter / NucView488 (fluorogenic apoptosis marker)
  - Ethidium Homodimer-1 (necrosis marker, labels compromised-membrane/dead cells)

STEPS:
1. Organoid Seeding: Dispense the HepG2/NIH-3T3 collagen organoid suspension and merge with culture media; incubate 60 min at 37 C to allow the organoids to settle and equilibrate on-chip (confined by SU-8 pillars).
2-6. Dose-Response Loop (repeated once per acetaminophen concentration in the on-chip dilution series):
   a. Dispense a fresh acetaminophen droplet and a culture-media diluent droplet; merge to form one dose of the dilution series.
   b. Merge the diluted acetaminophen dose with the seeded organoid droplet; incubate 24 h at 37 C to expose the organoids to the hepatotoxin.
   c. Dispense Hoechst 33342 and merge with the treated organoid droplet to stain total nuclei.
   d. Dispense the caspase-3 activity reporter and merge with the Hoechst-stained droplet; incubate 30 min at 37 C to develop the apoptosis signal.
   e. Dispense ethidium homodimer-1 and merge with the apoptosis-stained droplet; incubate 15 min at 37 C to develop the necrosis signal.
   f. Detect apoptotic-marker fluorescence on the fully stained droplet. If the apoptotic signal is at or above a defined toxicity threshold, discard the droplet (organoid scored as apoptotic/toxic response).
   g. Otherwise, detect necrotic-marker fluorescence on the same droplet to score the necrotic fraction, then discard the droplet.
7. Repeat the dose-response loop across the acetaminophen concentration series to build the apoptotic-versus-necrotic population profile as a function of acetaminophen concentration.
8. End of Assay: Discard the residual seeded-organoid reservoir droplet.

INTERPRETATION: Successful hepatotoxicity screening is confirmed by a dose-dependent shift from an apoptotic-dominant response at intermediate acetaminophen concentrations (peaking near 10 mM) to a necrotic-dominant response at higher concentrations (e.g., 20 mM), consistent with 3D organoid culture recapitulating in vivo-like hepatotoxic responses not observed in comparable 2D (96-well plate) assays, supporting use of the DMF organ-on-a-chip platform for drug/toxicity screening.
