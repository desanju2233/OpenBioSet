Protocol Details
ASSAY TYPE

Digital Microfluidic Platform for Human Plasma Protein Depletion (HSA and Immunoglobulin Depletion using Magnetic Beads)

REAGENTS USED
Protein Sample (human plasma containing target and high-abundance proteins)
Protein A/G Beads (superparamagnetic particles for immunoglobulin capture)
Anti-HSA Beads (superparamagnetic particles coated with anti-human serum albumin antibodies)
Working Buffer
STEPS
Bead Preparation: Dispense 4 units of Protein A/G beads and 4 units of Anti-HSA beads, then mix them together.
Initial Depletion: Dispense 4 units of the protein sample, merge and mix with the mixed bead suspension, and incubate at 25°C for 10 minutes to deplete high-abundance proteins.
Initial Detection: Measure the fluorescence signal of the depleted sample for 30 seconds.
Conditional Secondary Depletion: If the residual signal exceeds the threshold (> 5), perform a secondary depletion cycle by dispensing fresh Protein A/G and Anti-HSA beads, mixing them with the primary depleted sample, incubating at 25°C for 10 minutes, measuring the secondary signal, and disposing of the initial depletion mixture.
