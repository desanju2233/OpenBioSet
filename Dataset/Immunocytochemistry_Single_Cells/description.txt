Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital microfluidic Immunocytochemistry in Single Cells (DISC) for high-time-resolution analysis of PDGFR/PI3K/Akt phosphorylation signalling in adherent fibroblasts and breast cancer cells

REAGENTS USED:
  - Cell Suspension (NIH-3T3 fibroblasts, MCF-7, or MDA-MB-231 cells at 500,000 cells per mL in culture media; passively dispensed to form virtual microwells (VMs) on the patterned top-plate hydrophilic sites)
  - Starving Media (0.5% serum, 0.05% v/v Pluronic F68 in DMEM)
  - Serum-Free DMEM (0.05% v/v Pluronic F68 rinse/wash medium)
  - PDGF-BB Ligand (human recombinant PDGF-BB stimulant in serum-free DMEM, delivered as single-bolus or brief pulse doses)
  - Fixing Solution (4% paraformaldehyde, 0.05% v/v F68 in DPBS)
  - Wash Buffer 1 (0.05% v/v F68 in DPBS, post-fixation rinse)
  - Permeabilization Solution (0.2% v/v Tween-20 in DPBS)
  - Wash Buffer 2 (0.05% v/v Brij 35 in DPBS, post-permeabilization/antibody rinse)
  - Blocking Solution (1% bovine non-fat dried milk, 0.05% v/v Brij 35 in DPBS)
  - Primary Antibody Solution (anti-PDGFR/anti-phospho-PDGFR or anti-Akt/anti-phospho-Akt mouse and rabbit antibodies in blocking solution)
  - Secondary Antibody Solution (Alexa Fluor 647 anti-mouse and Alexa Fluor 555 anti-rabbit conjugates in blocking solution)

STEPS:
1. Seeding: Passively dispense cell suspension droplets onto the top-plate hydrophilic sites to form virtual microwells; incubate 24 h at 37 C to allow cell adherence.
2. Starving: Dispense starving media to the VM; incubate 24 h at 37 C to serum-starve adherent cells.
3. Rinse: Passively dispense six successive serum-free DMEM droplets across the VM to wash out starving media.
4. Stimulation: Deliver PDGF-BB ligand droplet(s) to the VM for a defined stimulation duration (single bolus of 1 s to 120 s, or a brief 3-40 s pulse followed by ligand-free wash-away, depending on experiment).
5. Fixation: Immediately after stimulation, dispense fixing solution to the VM and incubate 10 min at room temperature to arrest the signalling state.
6. Rinse: Passively dispense six successive Wash Buffer 1 droplets to remove residual fixing solution.
7. Permeabilization: Dispense permeabilization solution to the VM and incubate 10 min at room temperature.
8. Rinse: Passively dispense nine successive Wash Buffer 2 droplets to remove permeabilization solution.
9. Blocking: Dispense blocking solution to the VM and incubate 1 h at room temperature.
10. Primary Antibody Staining: Dispense primary antibody solution (target protein + phosphoprotein antibodies) to the VM and incubate overnight at 4 C.
11. Rinse: Passively dispense six successive Wash Buffer 2 droplets to remove unbound primary antibody.
12. Secondary Antibody Staining: Dispense secondary antibody solution to the VM and incubate 2 h at room temperature.
13. Detection: Disassemble the top plate and scan on a microarray/laser-scanning cytometer; analyze images in CellProfiler to compute, for each single cell, the ratio of phosphorylated-protein to total-protein (phosphorylated + non-phosphorylated) fluorescence intensity, normalized to unstimulated control cells on the same device, yielding a per-cell phosphorylation fold-change used to classify normal vs. hyperactive responders.

PULSE-STIMULATION VARIANT (time-resolved wash-away experiments, Fig. 6):
After seeding, starving, and rinsing as above, cells are stimulated with a brief PDGF-BB pulse (as short as 3 s) and then washed with ligand-free medium to terminate ligand exposure. Cells are held for a defined post-stimulation interval (1-5 min, in 1 min increments) before fixation, so that phosphorylation state is captured at successive time points after the pulse. Steps 5-12 (fixation, permeabilization, blocking, primary/secondary antibody staining, and washes) are then performed identically to the standard protocol, followed by detection/classification of Akt activation.

INTERPRETATION: Successful DISC operation is confirmed by dose-dependent, bulk-averaged PDGFR/Akt phosphorylation curves matching conventional (western blot) measurements of the PDGFR/PI3K/Akt pathway, Hill-coefficient/half-maximal-activation values consistent with prior literature, no significant signalling perturbation from droplet-movement shear stress, and single-cell-resolved histograms/scatter plots revealing heterogeneous "hyperactive responder" subpopulations (e.g., in MDA-MB-231 vs. MCF-7 breast cancer cells) and fast pulse-stimulation kinetics (e.g., a 10 s PDGF pulse committing >30% of cells to Akt activation) not resolvable by traditional bulk assays.
