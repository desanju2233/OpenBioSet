Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Indirect ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Antigen (Target protein coated onto the microplate wells)
  - Blocking Buffer (Blocks non-specific protein binding sites)
  - Primary Antibody (Unlabeled specific antibody targeting the coated antigen)
  - Labeled Secondary Antibody (Enzyme-conjugated secondary antibody directed against the primary antibody species)
  - Wash Buffer (Removes unbound reagents between assay steps)
  - Substrate (Reacts with the secondary antibody enzyme conjugate to yield a measurable signal)

STEPS:
1. Coating: Add antigen solution to the wells and incubate at 4 C overnight (720 min).
2. Blocking: Add blocking buffer, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 60 min.
3. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer to clear unbound blockers.
4. Primary Incubation: Add primary antibody, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min.
5. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer to eliminate excess primary antibody.
6. Secondary Incubation: Add labeled secondary antibody, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min for signal amplification.
7. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer to remove unbound secondary antibody.
8. Substrate Reaction: Add substrate, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 15 min.
9. Detection & Readout: Measure detection signal; conditionally dispose of reaction based on threshold ($\ge 1$).
