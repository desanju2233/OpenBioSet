Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Integrated Microfluidic SELEX (Systematic Evolution of Ligands by Exponential Enrichment) for Aptamer Isolation Using Electrophoretic Oligonucleotide Manipulation 

REAGENTS USED:Target-Functionalized Microbeads (IgE-functionalized magnetic or affinity microbeads for positive selection)  Randomized ssDNA Library (initial pool of random oligonucleotide sequences)  Wash Buffer (standard selection wash buffer to remove unbound sequences)  Reverse-Primer-Functionalized Beads (capture beads used in the amplification chamber)  PCR Reagents (master mix and primers for bead-based on-chip amplification)  NaOH Solution (0.2 M sodium hydroxide for chemical single-strand regeneration)  IgG Counter-Target Beads (used in rounds 2 and 3 for negative selection against non-specific binding)  

STEPS:
Affinity selection: Incubate the randomized ssDNA library with IgE-functionalized microbeads in the selection chamber at 25 C for 10 min.  Washing: Perform 10 wash cycles using wash buffer to remove weakly-binding and non-binding oligonucleotides.  Thermal release: Raise the chamber temperature to 57 C for 5 min to thermally release strongly bound, target-specific oligonucleotides into solution.  Electrophoretic transfer: Electrophoretically transfer the released oligonucleotides through a gel-filled interconnection channel into the amplification chamber.  Primer capture: Capture the transferred ssDNA on reverse-primer-functionalized beads within the amplification chamber for 10 min.  On-chip PCR: Perform bead-based PCR amplification in the amplification chamber (initial denaturation at 95 C for 30 s, followed by 20 thermal cycles of 95 C for 15 s, 60 C for 30 s, and 72 C for 30 s, with a final extension at 72 C for 60 s).  Chemical strand separation: Treat the PCR mix with 0.2 M NaOH solution to chemically denature the duplex and release the amplified target-binding ssDNA from its bead-immobilized complementary strand.  Counter-selection: For rounds 2 and 3, perform negative selection by mixing the released ssDNA with IgG counter-target beads, followed by 3 wash cycles to remove non-specific binders.  Inter-round transfer: Electrophoretically transfer the enriched, counter-selected ssDNA pool back to the selection chamber to begin the next SELEX round.  Detection & Readout: Measure fluorescence signal from the eluted band to confirm successful round enrichment; if the signal meets the threshold ($\ge 50$), dispose of the verified pool or proceed.  

INTERPRETATION: Successful integration of affinity selection, thermal elution, electrophoretic migration across gel channels, on-chip bead-based PCR, chemical denaturation, and counter-selection allows automated and streamlined aptamer isolation, significantly reducing manual intervention and processing time compared to traditional benchtop SELEX protocols.
