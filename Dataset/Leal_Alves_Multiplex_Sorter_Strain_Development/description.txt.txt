PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Microfluidic Multiplex Sorter for Strain Development
Source Link  : https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1002/admt.202401209
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1002/admt.202401209
Journal      : Advanced Materials Technologies, 2025, Vol. 10, Article 2401209
Platform     : Droplet-digital microfluidics with multiplex co-planar electrostatic sorting


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a droplet-digital high-throughput screening (HTS) workflow for strain
improvement of Saccharomyces cerevisiae var. diastaticus, a diastatic yeast used in beverage
fermentation for its ability to convert starch and dextrin into fermentable sugars via the
secreted glucoamylase enzyme encoded by the STA1 gene.

The core innovation is a multiplexed electrostatic droplet sorter capable of partitioning a
single genome-shuffled yeast library into two fitness sub-populations simultaneously:
  GT1 (high glucoamylase activity, SC1 channel, 95th fluorescence percentile)
  GT2 (mid glucoamylase activity, SC2 channel, 85th fluorescence percentile)

This avoids the non-uniform sampling bias of binary sorting (which would target only the
top-performing population) and reduces the post-analysis sample size by approximately 14-fold
compared to a binary approach at equivalent 95% confidence. The key finding is that mid-level
sorted strains (IDY1-M23, IDY2-M9) outperform high-level sorted strains in beverage
fermentation, an outcome only discoverable through multiplex screening.

The protocol consists of four sequential phases:
  1. Generation of a diversified genome-shuffled library (>10^7 variants) by self-mating
  2. Single-cell encapsulation in 30 pL droplets, off-chip incubation, and multiplex sorting
  3. Post-HTS colony recovery and maltodextrin growth screening
  4. Beverage fermentation evaluation of selected strains in synthetic media


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i   : Chromium co-planar electrodes patterned on 50 x 75 mm glass slide;
            ground and activated electrodes on same plane (<25 um gap).
Layer ii  : SU-8 5 dielectric, 7 um thick; spin-coated and hard-baked.
Layer iii : PDMS channel layer fabricated by soft lithography;
            channel height 75 um, 100 um width for 30 pL droplet sorting.
            Additional channel heights: 35 um (for 30 pL), 50 um (for 110 pL).

Sorter design:
  - Symmetrical co-planar electrode geometry with three outlets:
      SC1: sorting channel 1 (high-fitness, GT1, SE1 actuation)
      SC2: sorting channel 2 (mid-fitness, GT2, SE2 actuation)
      WC:  central waste channel (non-selected droplets)
  - Two sorting electrodes: SE1 (lateral deflection to SC1) and SE2 (lateral deflection to SC2)
  - No pressure rebalancing or device redesign required for droplet volumes 30-1000 pL
  - Droplet and plug morphologies both sortable (co-planar field intersects both)

Droplet generator: T-junction with 2% fluorosurfactant in HFE7500 oil

Actuation: 15 kHz AC sine wave, 105 Vrms, applied via in-house automation
           software and graphical user interface (Python-based, previously described)
           Software repository: https://bitbucket.org/shihmicrolab/f_ahmadi_2023_uflowcontrol


ORGANISMS
---------
- S. cerevisiae var. diastaticus IDY1: isolated from high-sugar, high-alcohol beverage
  industry setting. More robust and better suited for elevated sugar levels. Grows well
  in all droplet volumes (30, 110, 1000 pL). Peak glucoamylase fluorescence ~3 a.u. at 48 h.
- S. cerevisiae var. diastaticus IDY2: isolated from low-sugar, low-alcohol beverage
  setting. Less adaptable to high sugar; only 30 pL droplets show significant growth.
  Peak glucoamylase fluorescence ~1 a.u. at 48 h.
- IY1: non-diastatic S. cerevisiae (STA1-); negative control; does not secrete glucoamylase.
- IRY1: recombinant S. cerevisiae with heterologous glucoamylase gene; positive control.

Both strains sourced from Lallemand Inc. (Montreal, QC, Canada).


FLUORESCENT SUBSTRATE
---------------------
Substrate : 4-methylumbelliferyl-alpha-D-glucopyranoside (4-MUG-a)
            Validated using commercial Aspergillus niger glucoamylase at pH 7.5 and 11.
            Fluorescent signal distinguishable at neutral pH (pH 7.5) between IDY1/IDY2
            and negative control IY1.
            Previous substrate candidates 4-NPM (absorbance) and 4-NPG rejected:
              4-NPM requires pH >10 (reduces cell viability); absorbance less sensitive.
              4-NPG showed no distinguishable signal for diastatic strains.
Stock     : 4 mM in 0.1 M phosphate buffer pH 7.5
Final     : 2 mM per droplet (1:1 co-encapsulation with cell suspension)
Excitation: 360 nm (ex) / 450 nm (em); measured on CLARIOstar Plus (BMG LABTECH)
BSA       : 10% (w/w) bovine serum albumin added to cell suspension to reduce
            protein adsorption to droplet interface and PDMS walls


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Genome Shuffling Library Generation
  Strains        : IDY1 and IDY2 (independent libraries)
  Method         : Sporulation and self-mating (genome shuffling)
  Sporulation    : Inoculate saturated cultures into sporulation medium (1 g/L yeast
                   extract, 10 g/L KOAc, 0.5 g/L glucose); OD600 start = 0.3
                   Incubate 5 days at 23 C, 150 rpm
  Spore prep     : Ether-zymolyase ascospore technique
  Germination    : ~50 uL YPD 2% glucose, 30 C overnight; then 5 mL 2% YPD, 30 C overnight
  Library size   : >10^7 variants
  Freezer stocks : 25% glycerol (final) added to saturated YPD culture; multiple single-use vials
  Notes          : Non-GMO technique; compliant with agro-food regulatory requirements.
                   IDY2 starts with enhanced growth phenotype in maltodextrin relative to IDY1.

Step 2 | Cell Suspension Preparation
  Preculture     : Overnight YPD broth at 30 C, 200 rpm
  Wash           : Sonicate and wash 3x with PBS by centrifugation
  Resuspend      : 2x YPD broth buffered at pH 7.5
  Cell density   : Adjusted for Poisson lambda=0.3 per droplet size:
                     30 pL  -> 2x10^7 cells/mL
                     110 pL -> 5x10^6 cells/mL
                     1 nL   -> 6x10^5 cells/mL
  BSA            : 10% (w/w) added to cell solution before encapsulation

Step 3 | Single-Cell Encapsulation in 30 pL Droplets
  Generator      : T-junction droplet generator
  Oil            : HFE7500 + 2% PEG fluorosurfactant
  Encapsulation  : 1:1 cell suspension : substrate solution co-flow
  Droplet size   : 30 pL (D = 40 um channel)
  Collection     : PCR tube with 50 uL 2% fluorosurfactant in HFE7500
  Monitoring     : Droplet generation monitored by Fiji (ImageJ) for size verification

Step 4 | Off-Chip Incubation (48 h at 30 C)
  Temperature    : 30 C in the dark
  Duration       : 48 h (= 2880 min = 172800 s)
  Imaging        : Fluorescence + bright-field every 24 h
  Cell growth    : Counted by in-droplet cell counting (Fiji ROI)
  Fluorescence   : CTDF (corrected total droplet fluorescence) quantified per droplet:
                     CTDF = DF/(DA x Mean_BR) - DF_t0/(DA_t0 x Mean_BR_t0)
                     where DF = integrated density, DA = droplet area, BR = background reading
  Optimal volume : 30 pL selected for highest fluorescence and fastest throughput
  Results        : IDY1 peak ~3 a.u. at 48 h (30 pL); IDY2 peak ~1 a.u. at 48 h (30 pL)

Step 5 | Droplet Re-injection and Fluorescence Histogram Gating
  Re-injection   : 3 nL/s droplet flow rate; 300 nL/s spacer oil flow rate
  Tubing         : 1/32" OD, 0.381 mm ID PEEK (re-injection syringe)
  Throughput     : 160 Hz for 30 pL droplets
  Detection      : In-line optical fiber (ex: 470 nm LED, em: 450 nm bandpass filter)
                   Spectrometer: FLAME-S UV-VIS (Ocean Insight)
  Droplets screened: ~720,000 total
  Histogram      : Gaussian fluorescence distribution plotted; gates set as:
                     GT1 (gate-SC1): high fluorescence 95th percentile
                     GT2 (gate-SC2): mid fluorescence 85th percentile

Step 6 | Multiplex Electrostatic Sorting
  Mechanism      : Co-planar electrode actuation laterally deflects droplets into SC1 or SC2.
                   Without actuation: all droplets flow to WC (central waste channel).
  GT1 (SC1)      : SE1 actuated at 15 kHz, 105 Vrms -> high-fitness droplets deflect to SC1
  GT2 (SC2)      : SE2 actuated at 15 kHz, 105 Vrms -> mid-fitness droplets deflect to SC2
  WC (waste)     : Neither electrode actuated; droplet flows undeflected to central channel
  Sorting efficiency: >90.2% true positive; ~3% false negative; no significant difference
                      between SC1 and SC2 channels
  Multiplex efficiency at 30 pL:
    1 mM fluorescein: 100% (SC1), ~3% FN
    0.1 mM fluorescein: 98.6% (SC2), ~3% FN
  Droplets sorted : ~108,000 total (from ~720,000 screened)
  Sorted strains:
    IDY1 GT1 (high): H2, H3
    IDY1 GT2 (mid):  M2, M23
    IDY2 GT1 (high): H13, H23
    IDY2 GT2 (mid):  M9, M22

Step 7 | Colony Recovery and Plating
  Recovery       : Pipette top layer of oil emulsion from pipette tip at SC1 or SC2 outlet
  Aliquot        : 5 uL from sorted population diluted into 50 uL YPD broth
  Plating        : YPD agar plates; incubate 30 C for 48 h
  Colony count   : 24 single colonies picked per gate (GT1 and GT2)
  Glycerol stocks: Remaining emulsion pipetted into YPD broth; grow overnight;
                   add 25% glycerol (final); store at -80 C

Step 8 | Post-HTS Maltodextrin Growth Screening
  Preculture     : Colonies grown in YPD broth, 48 h at 30 C / 300 rpm
  Inoculation    : 5 uL into 1 mL maltodextrin media per well (96-well plate)
  Media          : 2 g/L yeast extract, 4 g/L bacto peptone, 20 g/L maltodextrin
  Incubation     : 30 C, 48 h; OD600 measured at 24 h and 48 h (Tecan Sunrise microplate reader)
  Metric         : Maltodextrin growth fold improvement = Mutant OD600 / Wild type OD600
  Results:
    IDY1 GT1 (high): only H2 showed significant improvement (~2.5x at 48 h)
    IDY1 GT2 (mid):  M2 ~1.4x improvement
    IDY2 GT1 (high): average 2.2x growth improvement vs WT
    IDY2 GT2 (mid):  average 1.7x growth improvement vs WT
  Notes          : IDY2 genome-shuffled populations show enhanced growth baseline vs IDY1
                   in maltodextrin due to starting phenotype advantage.

Step 9 | Enzyme Activity Measurement (parallel to maltodextrin screening)
  Preculture     : 48 h in YPD broth, 30 C, 300 rpm
  Substrate      : 4-MUG-a, 2 mM final concentration
  Protocol       : 20 uL culture + 150 uL YPD broth + 30 uL 4-MUG-a (2 mM);
                   incubate 30 C, 200 rpm; measure fluorescence every 1 h for 15 h
                   (CLARIOstar Plus, ex 360 nm / em 450 nm)
  End-point      : 96-well plate; 50 uL enzyme substrate (2 mM) + 50 uL supernatant;
                   incubate 30 C for 2 h; stop buffer pH 11; read fluorescence
  Metric         : Enzyme activity fold improvement = Mutant Activity (U/mL OD) / WT Activity (U/mL OD)
  Results:
    IDY1 GT1: ~3.7x fold improvement (average) vs WT; only H2 statistically significant (~3x)
    IDY1 GT2: ~1.2x fold improvement (average)
    IDY2 GT1: ~2x fold improvement (7 high strains showed improvement)
    IDY2 GT2: ~2x fold improvement (13 mid strains showed improvement at 24 h and 48 h)

Step 10 | Beverage Fermentation Evaluation
  Preculture     : YPD broth 48 h at 30 C, 300 rpm
  Inoculum       : 100 uL into 3 mL synthetic beverage media
  Synthetic media: 125 g/L maltose, 60 g/L maltodextrin, 1 mL ethanol,
                   1.25 mL 50% glycerol (in water), 3 mL 85% lactic acid,
                   2 g/L yeast extract, 6.4 g/L bactopeptone,
                   pH 5.9, 25 mg/mL chloramphenicol
  Vials          : 6 mL mini vials sealed with rubber caps; needles for gas exchange
  Mixing         : 180 rpm for 5 min; then without agitation
  Temperature    : Phase 1: 20 C for 22 h; Phase 2: 30 C until 72 h endpoint
  Weight loss    : Measured at 0, 18, 24, 42, 48, 66, 72 h (precision scale)
  Fermentation complete: 72 h (final mass loss point)
  Dry biomass    : Pellet cells; dry at 60 C overnight; weigh
  Metrics:
    CO2 released per mg dry biomass (mg CO2/mg yeast)
    Ethanol content normalised to dry biomass (mg ethanol/mL per mg yeast)
    Fermentation rate (mass loss rate h^-1)
  Statistical    : One-way ANOVA at 95% CI, N=3; p-values vs WT:
                   ns: p=0.1234, *: p=0.0332, **: p=0.0021, ***: p=0.0002, ****: p<0.0001
  Key results:
    IDY1-M23 (GT2/mid): highest CO2 mass loss ~7.8 mg CO2/mg biomass at 72 h
                         (vs ~5.01 mg CO2/mg biomass for WT IDY1)
                         Only IDY1 strain to show significant ethanol content improvement
    IDY2-M9  (GT2/mid): mass loss rate ~0.128 h^-1; highest CO2 and ethanol yield for IDY2
    IDY2-H13 (GT1/high): mass loss rate ~0.105 h^-1; second best for IDY2
    Top ethanol producers: IDY1-M23, IDY2-M9 (both mid-level sorted)
  Key finding    : Mid-level sorted strains outperform high-level sorted strains in fermentation.
                   Hypothesised mechanism: high-level strains incur increased metabolic burden
                   from elevated glucoamylase expression (higher ATP demand, amino acid
                   consumption for protein synthesis, diversion from fermentation).
                   Mid-level strains balance enzyme secretion with metabolic load, maintaining
                   fermentation efficiency. This is consistent with metabolic burden literature
                   in heterologous protein production.
