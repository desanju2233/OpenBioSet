Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics Platform for Loop-Mediated Isothermal Amplification Detection (DMF-LAMP) for c-Myc Oncogene Amplification

REAGENTS USED:
  - LAMPMix (LAMP reaction reagents: 0.8 uM each inner primer FIP and BIP, 0.2 uM each outer primer FP and BP, 0.4 mM dNTP mix, 1 M betaine, 4 mM MgCl2, 1x supplied buffer, 8 U Bst DNA polymerase large fragment; 0.14 uL per droplet)
  - DNASample (target c-Myc proto-oncogene DNA resuspended in 1x enzyme buffer at 0.5 ng/uL; 0.14 uL per droplet)

STEPS:
1. Reagent dispensing: Dispense 10 droplets (~0.14 uL each, ~1.40 uL total) of LAMPMix from the left reservoir and transport to the central reaction reservoir via zig-zag electrodes.
2. Sample dispensing: Dispense 1 droplet (~0.14 uL) of DNASample from the right reservoir and transport to the central reaction reservoir.
3. Mixing: Merge and mix the LAMP reagent and DNA sample droplets in the central reaction reservoir (total on-chip reaction volume: 1.54 uL; final DNA concentration: 0.5 ng/uL).
4. First incubation: Incubate the reaction droplet isothermally at 65 C for 15 min (bottom-plate set point; vertical temperature gradient across droplet: 0.3 C; mixing region electrodes kept activated at 20 VRMS, 5 kHz).
5. First amplification check: Assess amplification by fluorescence readout (standing in for gel-electrophoresis band intensity).
6. Second incubation: If no amplification detected, continue isothermal incubation at 65 C for a further 15 min (cumulative 30 min).
7. Second amplification check: Assess amplification again.
8. Third incubation: If still no amplification detected, continue isothermal incubation at 65 C for a further 15 min (cumulative 45 min).
9. Third amplification check: Assess amplification again. If still negative, discard the reaction droplet.
10. Product retrieval: On successful amplification, transport the reaction product droplet to the retrieving reservoir and withdraw from the device via pipette.

INTERPRETATION: Successful c-Myc LAMP amplification is confirmed by the appearance of a characteristic LAMP ladder band pattern on gel electrophoresis (Figure 3e, Lane 7, positive at 45 min cumulative incubation). The on-chip DMF-LAMP product shows higher normalized band fluorescence intensity (139.0 a.u.) than the equivalent bench-top reaction (78.8 a.u.), indicating enhanced amplification efficiency in the miniaturized DMF format. Negative controls show no amplification at any time point. The platform achieves sensitive amplification of 0.5 ng/uL target DNA in 1.54 uL total volume in 45 min.
