Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Automated Electrotransformation of Escherichia coli on a Digital Microfluidic Platform Using Bioactivated Magnetic Beads (bead-based preparation of electrocompetent cells, heat-shock induction, DI-water media exchange, oligonucleotide loading, on-cartridge electroporation, and recovery for a multiplex automated genome engineering, MAGE, cycle)

REAGENTS USED:
  - E. coli Culture (EcNR2 DmutS lambda-Red+ strain, grown to stationary phase then sampled at mid-log phase, OD600 4.5-6.0)
  - Magnetic Beads (Wheat Germ Agglutinin, WGA, lectin-coated streptavidin superparamagnetic beads, 1 um diameter, for cell binding)
  - Blocking Agent (fat-free powdered milk, 2.5 mg/mL, to prevent in-droplet bead-cell clumping)
  - Growth Medium (defined MOPS EZ medium supplemented with biotin, or LB, used for growth, heat shock, and recovery)
  - Wash Buffer (DI water with 0.05% Tween 20, used for repeated media-exchange washes to make cells electrocompetent)
  - Oligo Reagent (90-nucleotide single-stranded oligodeoxynucleotide, e.g. bla-restore ODN, introduced for lambda-Red-mediated allelic replacement)
  - Recovery Medium (growth medium added immediately after the electroporation pulse for cell recovery and outgrowth)

STEPS:
1. Bead binding: mix the mid-log-phase E. coli culture with WGA-lectin-coated magnetic beads and rotate/incubate for 15 minutes to bind cells to beads (approximately one bead per cell).
2. Bead blocking: mix the bead-bound cells with fat-free powdered milk blocking agent for 1 minute to prevent bead-cell aggregation and promote uniform dispersion.
3. Media exchange: pull bound cells out of suspension with a magnet, remove the supernatant, and resuspend the bead-bound cells in growth medium.
4. Heat-shock induction: incubate the resuspended, bead-bound cells at 42 C for 15 minutes to induce expression of the lambda-Red recombination proteins (Exo, Beta, Gam).
5. Electrocompetence washes: for each of 3 wash cycles, magnetically pellet the bound cells, remove the supernatant, and replace it with DI water (with 0.05% Tween 20) without resuspension, to render the cells electrocompetent.
6. Oligo loading: suspend the washed, bead-bound cells in DI water, add the single-stranded oligodeoxynucleotide, and incubate briefly (about 30 seconds) to allow oligo uptake.
7. Temperature control and electroporation: hold the sample at the target pulse temperature (4 C or room temperature, depending on protocol variant) and apply a high-field-strength electroporation pulse (up to 24-26 kV/cm, RC time constant approximately 5-6 ms) to transiently permeabilize the cell membrane and deliver the oligonucleotide into the cells.
8. Recovery: immediately merge the pulsed sample with recovery growth medium and incubate at 30 C for 2.5 hours to allow the cells to recover and express any successfully incorporated genetic changes.
9. Transformation frequency readout: plate (or otherwise assay) the recovered cell sample on selective and non-selective media to determine the transformation frequency as the ratio of colony-forming units on the selective condition to the total colony-forming units.
