Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic (DMF) Platform for Complete Mammalian Cell Culture — Adherent Cell Seeding, Growth, Media Exchange, Subculture, and On-Demand Transfection

REAGENTS USED:
  - Fibronectin (ECM protein, dispensed and dried to form an "adhesion pad" on the Teflon-AF electrode surface)
  - Cell Suspension (CHO-K1, HeLa, NIH-3T3, or INS-1 cells, 20-100 cells seeded per 150 nL droplet)
  - Growth Media (DMEM, F-12/DMEM, or RPMI 1640, supplemented with 10% FBS)
  - Fresh Media (used for daily passive-dispensing media exchange, three source droplets per exchange)
  - Trypsin/EDTA (0.25% vol/vol trypsin, 1 mM EDTA, used to detach cells from the adhesion pad)
  - Harvest Media (1 uL droplet passed over the primary pad to collect detached cells and deliver them to the secondary pad; ~2-3% of cells transferred per pass, four passes per subculture)
  - Transfection Mix (pCAG-GFP plasmid DNA complexed with Dreamfect cationic lipid, diluted in culture media)

STEPS:
1. Adhesion pad preparation: Dispense a droplet of fibronectin onto the electrode and allow it to dry, forming an ECM-coated adhesion pad on the hydrophobic Teflon-AF surface.
2. Cell seeding: Dispense a 150 nL droplet of cell suspension (20-100 cells) onto the primary (1 deg) adhesion pad and mix with growth media.
3. Growth incubation: Incubate the seeded droplet at 37 C (5% CO2 humidified chamber) for 24 h, allowing cells to shift from round to spread morphology.
4. Daily media exchange (passive dispensing): Every 24 h, drive three successive 1 uL source droplets of fresh media across the adhesion pad; each passing droplet sweeps away old media and leaves a fresh ~150 nL droplet behind (<3% carryover after one exchange, background level after three). Repeat this daily exchange/incubation cycle across the culture period.
5. Confluency check: Monitor cell density; once cells reach the second half of exponential growth (high density, e.g. 200-300 cells on the pad), proceed to subculture.
6. Cell detachment: Passively exchange the old media on the primary pad with a 150 nL droplet of trypsin/EDTA (three source-droplet exchanges) and allow cells to dissociate for 3-5 min.
7. Harvest and delivery to secondary pad: Translate a 1 uL droplet of fresh media over the primary pad to collect dissociated cells (blocking further trypsin activity), then drive the droplet to the secondary (2 deg) adhesion pad, where a passive-dispensing step deposits some of the cells. Repeat four times (collect + deliver) so that ~10% of the primary-pad cells are transferred to the secondary pad, then discard the depleted primary-pad droplet.
8. Secondary-pad growth: Incubate the newly seeded secondary pad at 37 C for 72 h, with daily passive-dispensing media exchange as in step 4, before subculturing again onto a subsequent generation.
9. On-demand transfection: Passively exchange the media on a subcultured (2 deg) pad with a 150 nL droplet of transfection mix (pCAG-GFP plasmid + Dreamfect lipid, ~5 ng DNA/~20 nL lipid per droplet) and incubate 3 h at 37 C.
10. Post-transfection recovery: Passively exchange the transfection solution for fresh media (three source droplets) and incubate overnight (24 h) at 37 C to allow GFP expression.
11. Detection: Image/quantify GFP fluorescence on the droplet to determine transfection efficiency, then discard the analyzed droplet.

INTERPRETATION: Successful implementation of complete on-chip mammalian cell culture is confirmed by growth/proliferation rates and morphology in droplets comparable to conventional multiwell-plate culture across multiple cell lines and subculture generations, and by transfection efficiencies (~20% GFP-positive cells) comparable to macro-scale transfection, supporting the DMF platform as an automated, miniaturized alternative to conventional mammalian cell seeding, feeding, passaging, and on-demand genetic transformation.
