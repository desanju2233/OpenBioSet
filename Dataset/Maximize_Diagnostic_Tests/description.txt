PROTOCOL: Digital Microfluidic G6PD Deficiency Assay
Module: fluorescence

MANIFEST:
- whole_blood
- diluent
- g6pd_reagent_1
- g6pd_reagent_2

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. wb = dispense 20 units of whole_blood
2. d1 = dispense 40 units of diluent
3. lysate = mix wb with d1 for 30s
4. repeat 3 times:
   - d2 = dispense 40 units of diluent
   - lysate = mix lysate with d2 for 30s
5. r1 = dispense g6pd_reagent_1
6. rxn1 = mix lysate with r1 for 30s
7. r2 = dispense g6pd_reagent_2
8. g6pd_rxn = mix rxn1 with r2 for 30s
9. heat g6pd_rxn at 37c for 1m (60s)
10. g6pd_signal = detect fluorescence on g6pd_rxn for 10s
11. if (g6pd_signal <= 2):
    - dispose g6pd_rxn
12. repeat 23 times:
    - g6pd_signal = detect fluorescence on g6pd_rxn for 10s
13. dispose g6pd_rxn
