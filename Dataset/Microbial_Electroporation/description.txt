PROTOCOL: Scalable Device for Automated Microbial Electroporation in a Digital Microfluidic Platform
Authors: Andrew C. Madison, Matthew W. Royal, Frederic Vigneault, Liji Chen, Peter B. Griffin, Mark Horowitz, George M. Church, Richard B. Fair
Journal: ACS Synthetic Biology, 2017
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.7b00007
PDF Source: https://pubs.acs.org/asbcd6/article-supplement/683293/pdf/sb7b00007_si_001/

MANIFEST:
- ecnr2_culture
- wash_buffer
- resuspension_buffer
- pgerc_plasmid
- recovery_media
- lb_kan_agar

========================================
PHASE 1: SINGLE PULSE PROTOCOL
========================================
1. cells = dispense 1000 units of ecnr2_culture
2. repeat 2 times:
   - wash = dispense 1000 units of wash_buffer
   - cells = mix cells with wash for 90s
   - dispose wash
3. resusp = dispense 200 units of resuspension_buffer
4. cells = mix cells with resusp for 10s
5. plasmid = dispense 5 units of pgerc_plasmid
6. celldna = mix cells with plasmid for 10s
7. heat celldna at 4c for 15m (900s)
8. heat celldna at 23c for 1s
9. recover = dispense 200 units of recovery_media
10. outgrowth = mix celldna with recover for 60s
11. heat outgrowth at 30c for 60m (3600s)
12. single_pulse_reading = detect fluorescence on outgrowth for 5m (300s)
13. if (single_pulse_reading <= 80):
    - dispose outgrowth
14. plate = dispense 50 units of lb_kan_agar
15. colony = mix outgrowth with plate for 10s
16. dispose colony

========================================
PHASE 2: PULSE TRAIN PROTOCOL
========================================
1. cells2 = dispense 1000 units of ecnr2_culture
2. repeat 2 times:
   - wash2 = dispense 1000 units of wash_buffer
   - cells2 = mix cells2 with wash2 for 90s
   - dispose wash2
3. resusp2 = dispense 200 units of resuspension_buffer
4. cells2 = mix cells2 with resusp2 for 10s
5. plasmid2 = dispense 5 units of pgerc_plasmid
6. celldna2 = mix cells2 with plasmid2 for 10s
7. heat celldna2 at 4c for 15m (900s)
8. repeat 10 times:
   - heat celldna2 at 23c for 1s
9. recover2 = dispense 200 units of recovery_media
10. outgrowth2 = mix celldna2 with recover2 for 60s
11. heat outgrowth2 at 30c for 60m (3600s)
12. train_pulse_reading = detect fluorescence on outgrowth2 for 5m (300s)
13. if (train_pulse_reading <= 80):
    - dispose outgrowth2
14. plate2 = dispense 50 units of lb_kan_agar
15. colony2 = mix outgrowth2 with plate2 for 10s
16. dispose colony2
