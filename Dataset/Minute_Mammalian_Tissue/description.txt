Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jproteome.3c00281

Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidics for Microproteomic Analysis of Minute Mammalian Tissue Samples Enabled by a Photocleavable Surfactant

REAGENTS USED:
  - Tissue Sample (murine brain tissue biopsies, ~3 ug, 2 mm diameter punch)
  - Azo-TEAB Buffer (0.5% w/w 4-hexylphenylazosulfonate in 25 mM aqueous tetraethylammonium bicarbonate)
  - TCEP Reagent (tris(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine, reduction buffer)
  - IAA Reagent (iodoacetamide, alkylation buffer)
  - Digestive Enzyme Mixture (trypsin and Lys-C)
  - Formic Acid / Azo Quench Reagent

STEPS:
1. Tissue Suspension: Dispense the tissue sample and mix with Azo-TEAB buffer to suspend the tissue matrix and extract proteins on-chip.
2. Reduction: Dispense TCEP reagent, mix with the suspended tissue droplet, and incubate at 70 C for 10 min to reduce disulfide bonds.
3. Alkylation: Dispense IAA reagent, mix with the reduced sample droplet, and incubate at 25 C for 30 min in the dark to alkylate free thiols.
4. Digestion: Dispense the digestive enzyme mixture (trypsin/Lys-C), mix with the alkylated sample droplet, and incubate at 37 C for 240 min (4 h) to proteolytically digest proteins into peptides.
5. Quenching: Dispense the formic acid/azo quench reagent, mix with the digested peptide droplet, and incubate at 25 C for 10 min to quench digestion.
6. Detection / Clean-up: Measure peptide fluorescence or absorbance-based readouts to verify sample processing, then discard the analyzed droplet prior to downstream LC-MS/MS proteomic analysis.

INTERPRETATION: Successful microproteomic processing is confirmed by the identification of thousands of proteins (e.g., nearly 2,000 proteins including neural biomarkers and synaptic signaling proteins from minute 3 ug tissue punches) with high peptide sequence coverage and robust droplet actuation enabled by the Azo photocleavable surfactant, demonstrating that Azo-enabled digital microfluidics effectively automates the handling and preparation of size-limited clinical and biological tissue samples for advanced bottom-up proteomics.
