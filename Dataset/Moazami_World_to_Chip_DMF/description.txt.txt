================================================================================
PROTOCOL: World-to-Chip DMF -- Bacterial Transformation and Enzymatic Assay
================================================================================

SOURCE LINK : https://www.shihmicrolab.ca/_files/ugd/f0a828_18be169d68164b85b204ed067c7dc1f5.pdf
PAPER DOI   : http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.8b05754
JOURNAL     : Analytical Chemistry, 2019, Vol. 91, pp. 5159-5168
PLATFORM    : Digital Microfluidic (DMF) -- ITO-well-DMF configuration
TARGET      : E. coli DH5alpha (Protocol 1); Endoglucanase enzyme (Protocol 2)
APPLICATION : Bacterial co-transformation and enzymatic endoglucanase activity assay

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This paper describes two "world-to-chip" interface features integrated on a DMF
platform and demonstrates them with two biological protocols:

  (1) A 3D-printed reagent delivery system: a cylindrical PLA well (400 uL
      capacity) mated to the ITO top plate via a drilled-hole inlet, with a
      threaded screw to deliver fluid to an ancillary reservoir on-chip. The
      ancillary reservoir refills the main reservoir (7 uL target) after every
      dispense, improving droplet dispensing reproducibility:
        - RSD with well    : 11.62% (LB), 16.78% (water), 15.32% (methanol)
        - RSD without well : 19.61% (LB), 49.29% (water), 35.41% (methanol)

  (2) A closed-loop PID thermoelectric cooler (TEC): a 20x40 mm Peltier module
      integrated below the DMF chip with an aluminum heat block, RTD sensor,
      and Arduino Uno microcontroller. PID parameters (Kp=15, Ki=1.2, Kd=0.5)
      modelled in MATLAB/Simulink:
        - Rise to 42 C    : 2.1 C/s
        - Drop to 4 C     : 1.01 C/s
        - Steady-state    : high stability, low fluctuation

  PROTOCOL 1 -- Bacterial Co-Transformation results:
    - No control (open-loop)       : 3.67e3 CFU/ug (single), 3.17e3 (co-)
    - PID control (ITO-DMF)        : 2.03e4 CFU/ug (single), 9.00e3 (co-)
    - PID + well (ITO-well-DMF)    : 1.48e5 CFU/ug (single), 9.27e4 (co-)
    - 7-fold improvement with reagent well over PID alone (p=0.007)
    - Antibiotic selection: kanamycin (50 ug/mL) + chloramphenicol (35 ug/mL)

  PROTOCOL 2 -- Enzymatic Endoglucanase Assay results:
    - Higher temperature (30 C) produces higher MUF fluorescence vs 23 C
    - ITO-well-DMF fluorescence significantly higher than ITO-DMF (p<0.05,
      ANOVA two-way test) across all three substrate concentrations
    - Enzyme: endoglucanase cleaves beta-(1,4) glycosidic bond in MUC
    - Product: 4-methylumbelliferone (MUF); emission 449 nm, excitation 368 nm

REAGENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  --------------------  ------------------------------------  ----------------------
  CompetentCells        E. coli DH5alpha competent cells      1 unit per droplet
                        Thawed on ice 10 min before use
  pFAB4876              Plasmid; GFP reporter; KanR           1 unit; 100 ng/rxn
                        Diluted to 1 ug/uL stock              1 ug/uL stock
  pSB1C3_RFP            Plasmid; RFP (BBa_J04450); CamR       1 unit; 100 ng/rxn

  EndoglucanaseEnzyme   Endoglucanase; cleaves beta-(1,4)     1 unit per assay area

                        Stops reaction; enhances MUF

  Buffer                50 mM sodium-phosphate pH 7.0         1 unit per droplet
                        + 0.05% Pluronics F-68
                        Used for MUC serial dilution
  PluronicF68           0.05% Pluronics F-68 additive         Dissolved in all
                        Prevents biofouling; maintains         assay solutions;
                        droplet actuation on device            not a standalone

  NOTE: All droplet volumes are ~1 uL, dispensed at 160 V_rms, 15 kHz.
  Reservoir target volume: 7 uL (measured by impedance, Vread = 12.9 V).
  ITO-well-DMF: 400 uL bulk reagent per well; ancillary reservoir refills
  main reservoir after every unit droplet dispense.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  transformationQC      : Abstracts off-chip colony count QC step (Kan+Cam agar
                          plate, 37 C overnight incubation); used as on-chip
                          proxy for post-heat-shock cell viability signal

  endoglucanaseDetection: Abstracts CLARIOStar plate reader fluorescence readout
                          (off-chip; device placed on 96-well plate);
                          MUF emission 449 nm, excitation 368 nm;
                          well-scan: 15x15 matrix, 6 mm diameter, focal height
                          4.0 mm, gain 1180; max fluorescence per droplet recorded

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TEMPERATURE CONTROL PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TEC module     : 20x40 mm Peltier (TE technology, Texas, USA)
  Heat block     : 20x40x5 mm aluminum; 1.5 mm drilled hole for RTD sensor
  Controller     : Arduino Uno with H-bridge driver and N-channel MOSFET
  Control type   : Closed-loop PID (negative feedback)
  PID parameters : Kp=15, Ki=1.2, Kd=0.5 (optimised in MATLAB/Simulink)

  Thermal zones used in this protocol:
    42 C  -- heat shock (transformation Phase 1); rise rate 2.1 C/s
     4 C  -- cold recovery (transformation Phase 2); drop rate 1.01 C/s
    30 C  -- enzymatic assay incubation; steady-state maintained by PID

  NOTE: heat primitive in BioScript is void (no assignment). Temperature
  control is modelled as heat statements acting on fluid variables in-place.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL 1: MICROSCALE BACTERIAL CO-TRANSFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 -- Dispense Competent Cells
  Operations:
    cellDroplet = dispense 1 units of CompetentCells
  Notes:
    - E. coli DH5alpha thawed on ice 10 min before use
    - In ITO-well-DMF: 400 uL bulk cells in 3D-printed well
    - Ancillary reservoir refills main reservoir after each dispense

STEP 2 -- Dispense Plasmid DNA Droplets
  Operations:
    dna1Droplet = dispense 1 units of pFAB4876
    dna2Droplet = dispense 1 units of pSB1C3_RFP
  Notes:
    - pFAB4876: kanamycin selection (50 ug/mL); GFP reporter
    - pSB1C3-RFP: chloramphenicol selection (35 ug/mL); RFP insert BBa_J04450
    - Both diluted to 1 ug/uL; 100 ng per plasmid per reaction on-chip
    - Co-transformation: both plasmids present simultaneously

STEP 3 -- Dispense CaCl2 Solution
  Operations:
    cacl2Droplet = dispense 1 units of CaCl2_Stock
  Notes:
    - Stock 150 mM; final concentration on-chip 75 mM (after 1:1 merge)
    - CaCl2 critical for chemical competence and DNA uptake efficiency

STEP 4 -- Merge DNA Plasmids
  Operations:
    dnaMix = mix dna1Droplet with dna2Droplet for 5s
  Notes:
    - Sequential merging required for DMF electrode actuation
    - Paper states "equal volume ratio" merge of all four components

STEP 5 -- Merge Cells with DNA Mix
  Operations:
    cellDnaMix = mix cellDroplet with dnaMix for 5s

STEP 6 -- Add CaCl2 to Complete Transformation Mix
  Operations:
    transformMix = mix cellDnaMix with cacl2Droplet for 10s
  Notes:
    - Final droplet contains cells, pFAB4876, pSB1C3-RFP, CaCl2 at 75 mM
    - All four components merged in equal ~1 uL volume ratio

STEP 7 -- Heat Shock Phase 1 (42 C, 60 s)
  Operations:
    heat transformMix at 42c for 60s
  Notes:
    - Closed-loop PID TEC; rise rate 2.1 C/s
    - Drives transient membrane pore formation for DNA uptake in DH5alpha
    - Temperature fluctuations affect transformation efficiency (refs 37, 38)

STEP 8 -- Heat Shock Phase 2 (4 C, 180 s)
  Operations:
    heat transformMix at 4c for 180s
  Notes:
    - TEC drops to 4 C at 1.01 C/s; stops membrane disruption
    - Stabilises DNA-cell complex; 180 s = 3 min cold recovery
    - heat is void in BioScript; transformMix variable survives intact

STEP 9 -- Post-Transformation QC Detection and Branch
  Operations:
    transformSignal = detect transformationQC on transformMix for 30s
    if (transformSignal > 0) {
        dispose transformMix
    } else {
        drain transformMix
    }
    dispose transformSignal
  Notes:
    - Pass (BB2): transformSignal > 0 -> viable transformed cells detected
    - Fail (BB3): transformSignal <= 0 -> no viable signal; drain
    - Both branches join at BB4; enzymatic assay protocol begins after join
    - Expected (PID + well): ~9.27e4 CFU/ug DNA for co-transformation
    - drain vs dispose: functionally identical in lilott8/BioScript IR
      (both emit as dispose() hardware instruction); source code uses drain
      for fail path to preserve biological intent in dataset documentation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROTOCOL 2: ENZYMATIC ENDOGLUCANASE ASSAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 10 -- Dispense 40 uM MUC Substrate Assay Droplet
  Operations:
    muc40uM_Assay = dispense 1 units of MUC_Substrate
  Notes:
    - Dispensed first and stored untouched in assay area 1
    - Not consumed by any dilution step
    - All solutions contain 0.05% Pluronics F-68 additive

STEP 11 -- Prepare 20 uM MUC Assay Droplet (Direct Dilution)
  Operations:
    muc40_for20   = dispense 1 units of MUC_Substrate
    buffer_for20  = dispense 1 units of Buffer
    muc20uM_Assay = mix muc40_for20 with buffer_for20 for 5s
  Notes:
    - 1:1 mix: (1 unit x 40 uM) / 2 units total = 20 uM. Correct.
    - No split used; avoids compiler split-array stranding bug (lilott8/BioScript
      does not support array indexing; stranded split elements cause hardware
      collision by blocking electrode routing paths)
    - Scalar mix produces single scalar droplet; all downstream ops are 1-to-1

STEP 12 -- Prepare 10 uM MUC Assay Droplet (Explicit Buffer Chaining)
  Operations:
    muc40_for10 = dispense 1 units of MUC_Substrate
    b1          = dispense 1 units of Buffer
    b2          = dispense 1 units of Buffer
    b3          = dispense 1 units of Buffer
    b_mix_2     = mix b1 with b2 for 5s
    b_mix_3     = mix b_mix_2 with b3 for 5s
    muc10uM_Assay = mix muc40_for10 with b_mix_3 for 5s
  Notes:
    - Target: 1:3 dilution for 10 uM; (1 unit x 40 uM) / 4 units = 10 uM. Correct.
    - Compiler ignores multi-unit volume scalars: "dispense 3 units of Buffer"
      defaults silently to a 1-unit dispense in the IR, yielding a second 20 uM
      droplet instead of 10 uM -- a critical concentration error.
    - Fix: three buffer droplets (b1, b2, b3) explicitly instantiated and merged
      into a 3-unit buffer blob (b_mix_3) before final mix with substrate.
    - Every variable assigned and consumed exactly once; no aliasing in IR.

STEP 13 -- Dispense Enzyme Droplets
  Operations:
    enzymeDrop40 = dispense 1 units of EndoglucanaseEnzyme
    enzymeDrop20 = dispense 1 units of EndoglucanaseEnzyme
    enzymeDrop10 = dispense 1 units of EndoglucanaseEnzyme
  Notes:
    - Paper M&M: "a unit droplet of enzyme solution (~1 uL) was dispensed into
      one of three assay areas"
    - Enzyme concentration held constant across all three substrate conditions
      (paper Fig. 5 caption: "enzyme concentration remaining constant")
    - 1 unit per assay area regardless of substrate droplet volume
    - In ITO-well-DMF: enzyme reservoir refilled from 3D well after each dispense

STEP 14 -- Start Reactions
  Operations:
    reaction40uM = mix muc40uM_Assay with enzymeDrop40 for 5s
    reaction20uM = mix muc20uM_Assay with enzymeDrop20 for 5s
    reaction10uM = mix muc10uM_Assay with enzymeDrop10 for 5s
  Notes:
    - Paper M&M: "all three substrate-containing droplets were simultaneously
      mixed with the enzyme droplets in the assay areas"
    - TEC activates at this point for closed-loop 30 C control

STEP 15 -- Incubate at 30 C for 30 min
  Operations:
    heat reaction40uM at 30c for 30m
    heat reaction20uM at 30c for 30m
    heat reaction10uM at 30c for 30m
  Notes:
    - Endoglucanase cleaves beta-(1,4) glycosidic bond in MUC
    - Releases 4-methylumbelliferone (MUF) fluorophore
    - Closed-loop PID TEC maintains 30 C steady-state
    - All three reactions run concurrently in separate assay areas on device
    - heat is void in BioScript; reaction variables survive intact

STEP 16 -- Quench Reactions
  Operations:
    quench40     = dispense 1 units of QuenchingSolution
    quench20     = dispense 1 units of QuenchingSolution
    quench10     = dispense 1 units of QuenchingSolution
    quenched40uM = mix reaction40uM with quench40 for 5s
    quenched20uM = mix reaction20uM with quench20 for 5s
    quenched10uM = mix reaction10uM with quench10 for 5s
  Notes:
    - Paper M&M: "a unit droplet of quenching solution (0.3 M glycine-NaOH,
      pH 11.0) was mixed with each reaction droplet in the assay area"
    - 1 unit quench per assay area; enzyme concentration held constant so
      quench volume is not scaled with substrate volume

STEP 17 -- Fluorescence Detection and Cleanup
  Operations:
    assaySignal40 = detect endoglucanaseDetection on quenched40uM for 30s
    assaySignal20 = detect endoglucanaseDetection on quenched20uM for 30s
    assaySignal10 = detect endoglucanaseDetection on quenched10uM for 30s
    if (assaySignal40 > 0) {
        dispose quenched40uM
        dispose quenched20uM
        dispose quenched10uM
    } else {
        drain quenched40uM
        drain quenched20uM
        drain quenched10uM
    }
    dispose assaySignal40
    dispose assaySignal20
    dispose assaySignal10
  Notes:
    - Off-chip readout: device placed on 96-well plate in CLARIOStar reader
    - MUF emission 449 nm, excitation 368 nm; well-scan 15x15 matrix,
      6 mm diameter, focal height 4.0 mm, gain 1180
    - Maximum fluorescence intensity per droplet recorded
    - QC gate keyed on 40 uM signal (highest expected MUF output)
    - Pass (BB5): assaySignal40 > 0 -> valid fluorescence; dispose all
    - Fail (BB6): assaySignal40 <= 0 -> no signal (enzyme inactive or reagent
      failure); drain all
    - Both branches join at BB7 (terminal); all three signal droplets disposed

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Compiler    : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based, Python 3)
  Target      : -t ir (Intermediate Representation + Control Flow Graph)
  Command     : python3 main.py -d -i <file.bs> -t ir -o ./output -cfg

  KNOWN COMPILER BEHAVIOURS (relevant to this protocol):

  1. heat is void -- no assignment allowed.
     WRONG : result = heat transformMix at 42c for 60s
     RIGHT : heat transformMix at 42c for 60s

  2. detect consumes its fluid operand -- variable is spent after detect.
     Must use a distinct variable per detect call; do not reuse.

  3. Multi-unit volume scalars are silently ignored.
     "dispense 3 units of Buffer" generates dispense(Buffer) [0] in IR --
     a single 1-unit dispense. To accumulate N units of a reagent, explicitly
     instantiate N separate 1-unit dispense variables and chain with mix.

  4. Split arrays cannot be partially consumed.
     The compiler does not support array indexing syntax (e.g. arr[0], arr[1]).
     All elements of a split array must be consumed by a single downstream
     operation (array zip). Partial consumption leaves stranded droplets on the
     electrode grid, blocking routing paths and causing hardware collisions.
     Fix: avoid split for assay droplets; use direct dispense + mix instead.

  5. Array zipping does not work for dispense.
     "dispense N units" does not produce an N-element array. Only split
     produces arrays. Attempting to zip a split array with a multi-unit
     dispense results in only index [0] being mixed; remaining indices strand.

  6. ASCII-only source files -- ANTLR4 FileStream rejects non-ASCII bytes.
     All comments must use ASCII characters only. No Unicode arrows (->),
     box-drawing characters, degree symbols, or em-dashes in source files.

  CFG STRUCTURE (this protocol):
    BB1  main          -- entry: transformation + enzymatic assay through detect
    BB2  bsbbif_2_t    -- transform pass: dispose transformMix
    BB3  bsbbif_3_f    -- transform fail: drain transformMix
    BB4  bsbbif_4_j    -- join: dispose transformSignal; enzymatic assay begins
    BB5  bsbbif_5_t    -- assay pass: dispose all quenched droplets
    BB6  bsbbif_6_f    -- assay fail: drain all quenched droplets
    BB7  bsbbif_7_j    -- terminal: dispose all signal droplets, NOP

  CFG EDGES:
    BB1 -> BB2 (transformSignal > 0)
    BB1 -> BB3 (transformSignal <= 0)
    BB2 -> BB4
    BB3 -> BB4
    BB4 -> BB5 (assaySignal40 > 0)
    BB4 -> BB6 (assaySignal40 <= 0)
    BB5 -> BB7
    BB6 -> BB7
