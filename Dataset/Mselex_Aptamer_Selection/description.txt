Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Microfluidic SELEX (M-SELEX) for Rapid Generation of High-Affinity DNA Aptamers Against Protein Targets of Varying Isoelectric Point (PDGF-BB, Thrombin, Apolipoprotein E3)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Randomized ssDNA Library (100-base library, 60 randomized nucleotides flanked by two 20-base PCR primer sites)
  - Target-Coated Magnetic Beads (M-270 carboxylic acid beads with immobilized target protein: PDGF-BB, thrombin, or ApoE)
  - Selection Buffer (PBS + 1 mM MgCl2 + 0.025% Tween 20 for PDGF-BB/ApoE; 50 mM Tris + 100 mM NaCl + 1 mM MgCl2 + 5 mM KCl + 1 mM CaCl2 + 0.025% Tween 20, pH 7.5, for thrombin)
  - Yeast tRNA Blocking Reagent (blocks target-coated beads before rounds 2 and 3)
  - PCR Master Mix and Primers (unlabeled and biotinylated/fluorescently-labeled primers for pool amplification)
  - Lambda Exonuclease (digests the phosphorylated strand to generate single-stranded DNA)

STEPS:
1. Starting pool: Heat-denature the ssDNA library at 95C for 10 minutes, then snap-cool on ice and bring to room temperature.
2. Incubation with target: Incubate the denatured library with washed, target-coated magnetic beads for 1 hour with gentle rotation (blocked with yeast tRNA in rounds 2 and 3).
3. MMS separation: Load the sample onto the micro-magnetic separation (MMS) device; wash at a combined flow rate of 50 mL/hr with selection buffer (6 minutes in round 1, 20 minutes in rounds 2-3, increasing stringency), then elute the aptamer-bound beads with selection buffer.
4. PCR amplification: Amplify the eluted DNA using a biotinylated reverse primer to generate double-stranded DNA, purify, and quantify by UV spectroscopy.
5. Single-strand separation: Digest the double-stranded PCR product with lambda exonuclease to remove the phosphorylated strand and recover the fluorescently-labeled single-stranded forward strand for the next round.
6. Measure pool binding: Incubate a range of concentrations of the fluorescently-labeled ssDNA pool with target-coated beads for 1 hour, wash four times, elute the bound DNA at 95C for 10 minutes, and measure fluorescence in the supernatant to fit a saturation binding curve and determine bulk pool affinity (Kd).
7. Clone and sequence: For pools showing sufficient enrichment/affinity after three rounds, clone into competent E. coli, sequence individual clones, and rank-order them by relative binding signal to identify the highest-affinity individual aptamer sequences (performed off-chip, not modeled).

INTERPRETATION: Successful selection is confirmed by a bulk pool binding affinity (Kd) that decreases (improves) across the three selection rounds, and by individual cloned aptamer sequences exhibiting nanomolar-to-subnanomolar Kd values with negligible binding to non-target proteins or uncoated beads; the paper further reports an inverse relationship between target protein isoelectric point/net charge and aptamer Kd across the three protein targets and under varying selection-buffer pH conditions.
