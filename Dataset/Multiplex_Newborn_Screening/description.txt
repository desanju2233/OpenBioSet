Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic 5-Plex Fluorometric Enzyme Assay for Newborn Screening of Pompe, Fabry, Hunter, Gaucher, and Hurler Lysosomal Storage Diseases from a Single Dried Blood Spot Punch

REAGENTS USED:
  - DBS Extract (single 3-mm dried-blood-spot punch eluted in 100 uL of 0.1% w/v Tween20 extraction buffer, 30 min on an orbital shaker at ambient temperature; one extract shared across all 5 assays)
  - GAA Reagent (Pompe assay: 4-methylumbelliferyl-alpha-D-glucoside substrate with acarbose inhibitor and 20.0 mmol/L methyl-beta-cyclodextrin)
  - GLA Reagent (Fabry assay: 4-methylumbelliferyl-alpha-D-galactoside substrate with N-acetyl-D-galactosamine inhibitor and 20.0 mmol/L methyl-beta-cyclodextrin)
  - IDS Reagent (Hunter assay: 4-methylumbelliferyl-alpha-L-iduronate-2-sulfate substrate with 20.0 mmol/L methyl-beta-cyclodextrin)
  - GBA Reagent (Gaucher assay: 16 mmol/L 4-methylumbelliferyl-beta-D-glucoside substrate with 1.5% sodium taurocholate, no inhibitor)
  - IDU Reagent (Hurler assay: 2 mmol/L 4-methylumbelliferyl-alpha-L-iduronide substrate with 3.0 mmol/L D-saccharic acid 1,4-lactone inhibitor and 20 mmol/L methyl-beta-cyclodextrin)
  - Extraction Buffer (EXT: 0.1% w/v Tween20 in water)
  - Stop Buffer (STB: 0.2 mol/L NaHCO3, pH 10.0, 0.01% w/v Tween20)
  - Calibrant (4-methylumbelliferone, 4-MU, standard used for a 4-point calibration curve run in duplicate)

STEPS:
1. Four-point fluorescence calibration (in duplicate): dispense triple-sized calibrant droplets from each of 4 calibrant reservoirs and read fluorescence for each, giving 8 calibration readings used to build a linear-regression calibration curve.
2. Substrate/reagent background: for 2 of the 44 sample positions, use extraction buffer alone (no DBS extract) merged with each of the 5 reagents, incubate under the same conditions as sample reactions, and read background fluorescence for each of the 5 assays.
3. For each of the remaining DBS extracts loaded onto the cartridge (up to 42 additional samples): dispense one extract droplet and merge it separately with each of the 5 reagents (GAA, GLA, IDS, GBA, IDU) to form 5 parallel reaction droplets from the single punch.
4. Incubate all 5 reaction droplets per sample at 37 C for 1 h.
5. Stop and read: merge each incubated reaction droplet with its corresponding stop buffer droplet and measure endpoint fluorescence for each of the 5 assays (excitation 370 nm / emission 460 nm), converting readings to enzymatic activity via the calibration curve after subtracting background.
6. Screening interpretation: compare each of the 5 endpoint activities against the normal/affected separation boundary established from presumed-normal and confirmed-affected samples; flag and discard reaction droplets whose activity falls at or below the cutoff as presumptively affected (Pompe, Fabry, Hunter, Gaucher, or Hurler), otherwise report as normal.
