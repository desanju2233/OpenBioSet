Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Tissue-Digital Microfluidic Isolation of Single Cells for -Omics (tDISCO) for Spatially-Resolved Single-Cell Transcriptomic and Proteomic Profiling of Proximal vs. Distal Astrocytes after Ischemic Stroke

REAGENTS USED:
  - Cryosectioned Brain Tissue Biopsy (2 mm diameter punch from a cortical stroke tissue section, mounted on the tDISCO top-plate)
  - Fixation Reagent (paraformaldehyde-based fixative, applied on-chip)
  - DAPI Nuclear Stain (marks all nuclei)
  - Anti-GFAP Antibody (fluorescent astrocyte marker)
  - Anti-NEUN Antibody (fluorescent neuronal marker)
  - Cell Lysis/Receiving Buffer (droplet positioned above the tissue for laser-induced single-cell lysis and lysate collection)

STEPS:
1. Mount a 2 mm cryosectioned cortical tissue biopsy onto the tDISCO top-plate.
2. Fix the mounted tissue biopsy in place on-chip using the fixation reagent.
3. Stain the fixed tissue on-chip with DAPI, anti-GFAP, and anti-NEUN to mark nuclei, astrocytes, and neurons, respectively.
4. Image the stained tissue at low magnification (20X) to map DAPI+/GFAP+ and DAPI+/NEUN+ cells and record their distance from the stroke lesion.
5. Select a single target cell (e.g., a GFAP+ astrocyte at a defined distance from the injury site) and re-image at high magnification (100X) to confirm its identity and position before lysis.
6. Dispense a receiving-buffer droplet and use digital microfluidic actuation to position it directly over the selected single cell.
7. Fire the focused laser at the targeted cell to induce a cavitation bubble that mechanically lyses the cell, releasing its contents into the overlying receiving-buffer droplet.
8. Actuate the single-cell lysate droplet away from the tissue for downstream -omics processing (library preparation for transcriptomics, or digestion for proteomics).
9. Replenish a fresh receiving-buffer droplet over the same tissue section and repeat steps 5-8 to iteratively isolate additional single cells (e.g., a second GFAP+ astrocyte at a different lesion distance, or a DAPI+/NEUN+ neuron) from the same biopsy.
10. Pool/queue the collected single-cell lysate droplets for transcriptomic (RNA-seq) and proteomic (LC-MS/MS) analysis, comparing molecular profiles across cells at varying distances from the stroke lesion to resolve the spatial boundaries of the astrocyte response.
