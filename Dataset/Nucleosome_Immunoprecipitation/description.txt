Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Nucleosome Immunoprecipitation (NuIP) with Fluorescent Detection on Functionalized Magnetic Beads (Digital Microfluidic Device)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Biotinylated Nucleosome Sample (purified human H3K4me2 nucleosomes)
  - Streptavidin-Coated Magnetic Beads
  - Primary Antibody (anti-H3K4me2, raised in rabbit)
  - Secondary Antibody (Alexa Fluor 405 Goat Anti-Rabbit, fluorescent-conjugated)
  - Wash Buffer

STEPS:
1. Capture: Mix the biotinylated H3K4me2 nucleosome sample with streptavidin-coated magnetic beads; incubate for 6 hours with agitation at 4 C.
2. Wash: Wash away unbound nucleosome species from the beads.
3. Detection Complex Preparation: Separately and simultaneously, under the same conditions (6 hours, 4 C, agitation), incubate the primary antibody (anti-H3K4me2) with the Alexa Fluor 405 Goat Anti-Rabbit secondary antibody to form the fluorescent detection complex.
4. Labeling: Add the detection complex to the washed, nucleosome-bound beads and incubate again.
5. Final Wash: Wash away unbound antibody species.
6. Detection: Re-suspend the beads and place a droplet on the digital microfluidic device; electrowet the droplet to the current wires to concentrate the beads magnetically, then image/detect via fluorescence (Alexa Fluor 405: ~395 nm excitation, ~421 nm emission, 420 nm longpass emission filter, CCD camera).

INTERPRETATION: Fluorescence signal on the concentrated magnetic beads confirms successful capture and antibody labeling of the target nucleosome modification (H3K4me2); a nucleosome-containing sample droplet shows clear fluorescence versus a negative control droplet, and fluorescence localizes to the beads (not the supernatant) when tested against an external magnet. In a semiquantitative dose-response series (0-20 uL of stock H3K4me2 nucleosomes), normalized pixel intensity (via ImageJ blue-channel analysis) increases with nucleosome volume, with a linear response observed between 0-10 uL, establishing this on-chip NuIP assay as a confirmatory checkpoint for antibody specificity/sensitivity prior to downstream analyses such as PCR, gel electrophoresis, or sequencing.
