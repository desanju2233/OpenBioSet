Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Isolation of Single Cells for -Omics (DISCO) — Single-Cell Genomics Pipeline

REAGENTS USED:
  - Adherent Cell Suspension (U87 human glioblastoma or B16 murine melanoma cells cultured on a DISCO top-plate, ~0.5x10^6 cells/mL loading density)
  - Wash Buffer (DPBS with 0.05% v/v Pluronic F68)
  - Fixative Solution (HOPE Fixative solution I)
  - Permeabilization Buffer (ice-cold acetone)
  - Immunostaining Reagents (CD47-APC antibody, clone CC2C6; Hoechst 33342 nuclear stain)
  - Collection Buffer (DPBS with 0.05% v/v F68, used as the virtual-microwell collection droplet)
  - Whole Genome Amplification Reagent (Repli-g single cell kit, Qiagen)
  - QC-1 PCR Mix (Taq DNA Polymerase, primers for conserved loci on chromosomes 4, 12, 13, 22)
  - DNA Cleanup Reagent (DNA Clean and Concentrator columns, Zymo Research)
  - QC-2 PCR Mix (AmpFlSTR Identifiler PCR Amplification Kit, STR + Amelogenin loci)
  - Library Prep Reagent (Nextera DNA Flex Library Prep Kit, Illumina)

STEPS:
1. Cell seeding and culture: Adherent cells are loaded onto the DISCO top plate at a density of ~0.5x10^6 cells/mL and incubated for 24 h at 37 C in a humidified atmosphere.
2. Fixation: A fixative droplet is delivered to the virtual microwell and incubated 20 min at 4 C, followed by a wash step with DPBS/F68.
3. Permeabilization: An ice-cold acetone droplet is delivered to the fixed cells and incubated at -20 C for 10 min, followed by a wash step.
4. Immunofluorescent staining: The permeabilized cells are labeled with an anti-CD47-APC antibody and Hoechst 33342 nuclear stain to enable phenotype-based, AI-driven cell selection (CNN-based image segmentation, AUC = 0.991).
5. Cell selection and laser lysis: The user (manually or via the trained CNN) selects a single cell of interest; a focused Q-switched Nd:YAG laser pulse (532 nm, ~0.8 um spot size) lyses the targeted cell, releasing its contents into a collection droplet (virtual microwell). Cells with insufficient/ambiguous phenotype signal are excluded from downstream processing.
6. Whole genome amplification (WGA): The single-cell lysate is combined with the Repli-g multiple displacement amplification (MDA) reagent and incubated isothermally (~30 C) to amplify the genome.
7. Quality control 1 (QC-1): The amplified product is PCR-amplified at four conserved loci (chromosomes 4, 12, 13, 22) via standard thermal cycling (94 C denature / 55 C anneal / 72 C extend) and evaluated by gel electrophoresis; samples with fewer than 3 of 4 expected loci are discarded.
8. DNA cleanup: The QC-1-passing product is purified/concentrated using DNA Clean and Concentrator columns.
9. Quality control 2 (QC-2): The cleaned DNA is PCR-amplified at 15 short tandem repeat (STR) loci plus Amelogenin (94 C denature / 59 C anneal / 72 C extend) and evaluated by microfluidic gel electrophoresis; samples with fewer than 12 of 16 expected loci are discarded.
10. Sequencing library preparation: The QC-2-passing DNA is tagmented and library-prepped using the Nextera DNA Flex kit, then the finished single-cell whole-genome sequencing library is collected off-chip for storage (-20 C/-80 C) and downstream next-generation sequencing (Illumina MiSeq).

INTERPRETATION: Successful single-cell genomic analysis by DISCO is confirmed by high sequencing uniformity (Lorenz curve near-theoretical distribution), minimal amplification bias from MDA, negligible background DNA contamination from non-selected cells (<5% vs. bulk), copy-number-variant (CNV) profiles concordant between single cells and bulk samples (R^2 = 0.843), and strong agreement between AI-selected and manually-selected single-cell genomes (R^2 = 0.894), supporting use of DISCO for deep single-cell -omics analysis of heterogeneous or rare adherent cell populations, including detection of single-nucleotide-level CRISPR-induced variants.
