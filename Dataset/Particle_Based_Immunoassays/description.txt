Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Magnetic Separation Particle-Based Immunoassay (TSH and 17β-estradiol)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Antibody-coated paramagnetic microparticles (suspended in DMF-compatible buffer)
  - Sample containing analyte (e.g., Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH) or 17β-estradiol (E2))
  - Wash buffer
  - Enzyme-conjugated reporter antibody (for TSH) or competitive tracer (for E2)
  - Chemiluminescent substrate

STEPS:
1. Particle and Sample Mixing: Dispense droplets of magnetic microparticles and sample. Merge and mix to allow antibody-antigen binding.
2. Magnetic Separation: Engage external magnet to immobilize magnetic particles, drawing off the supernatant.
3. First Wash: Dispense wash buffer, merge with particles, disengage magnet to resuspend, then re-engage magnet to separate and draw off supernatant. Repeat to ensure greater than 90% unbound reagent removal.
4. Reporter Binding: Dispense reporter antibody droplet, merge with particles, and mix.
5. Second Wash: Engage magnet, separate particles, and draw off supernatant. Wash again with buffer droplets.
6. Detection: Dispense chemiluminescent substrate, merge with the separated particles, and detect the signal.

INTERPRETATION: Target analyte concentration is proportional to the chemiluminescent signal for the noncompetitive TSH assay, and inversely proportional for the competitive 17β-estradiol assay.
