Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Cell-Based Non-Invasive Diagnostic Testing Using the DISCO Platform -- Detergent Direct Capture (DDC) Reconstitution of the ABC Transporter MsbA in Peptidiscs and Malachite Green ATPase Activity Assay

REAGENTS USED:
  - MsbA-Enriched Membrane Extract (E. coli membrane fraction bearing His6-tagged MsbA)
  - DDM Detergent (n-dodecyl-beta-D-maltoside, 1% w/v solubilization solution)
  - Peptidisc Peptide (NSPr amphipathic peptide, molar excess)
  - Dilution Buffer (Buffer A: 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.8, 100 mM NaCl, 10% glycerol)
  - Wash Buffer (Buffer A, used for Ni-NTA resin washing)
  - Elution Buffer (Buffer A + 600 mM Imidazole)
  - ATP Reagent (2 mM ATP in reaction buffer: 50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.8, 100 mM NaCl, 5 mM MgCl2)
  - Malachite Green Reagent (Malachite green solution with 0.05% Triton X-100)

STEPS:
1. Solubilization: Solubilize the MsbA-enriched membrane extract with 1% DDM detergent for 30 min at 4 C to release MsbA from the native membrane.
2. Peptidisc reconstitution: Mix the clarified detergent extract with a molar excess of peptidisc peptide (NSPr) to initiate detergent-direct-capture reconstitution.
3. Dilution/concentration cycles: Rapidly dilute the mixture into fresh dilution buffer and concentrate over a filter; repeat this dilution/concentration cycle 3 times to progressively lower the detergent concentration to a calculated ~0.008% final level.
4. Resin binding and washing: Incubate the reconstituted mixture with Ni-NTA resin for 1 hour at 4 C, then wash the resin with wash buffer to remove unbound material.
5. Elution: Elute the peptidisc-reconstituted MsbA (DDC-MsbA) from the resin using elution buffer containing imidazole.
6. ATPase reaction: Combine the eluted peptidisc-MsbA with ATP reagent and incubate at 37 C for 10 min to allow ATP hydrolysis.
7. Colorimetric detection: Add Malachite green reagent to the reaction, incubate at room temperature for 10 min to allow color development, then measure light absorption at 660 nm to quantify the released inorganic phosphate and calculate the initial rate of ATP hydrolysis, then discard the analyzed reaction mixture.

INTERPRETATION: Successful peptidisc reconstitution and ATPase activity measurement is confirmed when the DDC-MsbA preparation shows comparable purity and yield to other reconstitution methods (SDS-PAGE), a distinct blue-native gel migration pattern reflecting captured endogenous lipid content, and an ATPase rate elevated 2- to 5-fold relative to detergent-solubilized MsbA (DDM-MsbA), consistent with the lipid-mediated increase in both thermal stability and ATPase activity described for peptidisc preparations with higher endogenous lipid content.
