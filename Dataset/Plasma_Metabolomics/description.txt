Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: All-in-One Digital Microfluidic Pipeline for Automated Proteomic Sample Processing -- Plasma Metabolite Extraction and Two-Step Chemical Derivatization for Comprehensive Two-Dimensional Gas Chromatography Time-of-Flight Mass Spectrometry (GCxGC-TOFMS) Biomarker Discovery

REAGENTS USED:
  - Plasma Sample (100 uL room-temperature plasma from EDTA-collected, clotted, and centrifuged whole blood)
  - Extraction Solvent (900 uL methanol:water, 8:1 v/v)
  - Methoxyamine Reagent (20 mg/mL methoxyamine hydrochloride in ACS grade pyridine, 30 uL aliquot)
  - MSTFA + TMCS Reagent (N-methyl-N-(trimethylsilyl)trifluoroacetamide with 1% trimethylchlorosilane, 70 uL aliquot)

STEPS:
1. Extraction: Combine the 100 uL plasma sample with 900 uL methanol:water (8:1 v/v) extraction solvent in a microcentrifuge tube.
2. Cool/homogenize/cool: Cool the mixture on ice for 10 min, homogenize by vortex mixing for 10 min, then cool again on ice for 10 min prior to centrifugation and transfer of the supernatant.
3. Pre-derivatization drying: Dry the transferred extract using a SpeedVac for 40 min immediately prior to the first derivatization step to ensure no residual water is present.
4. First derivatization (methoximation): Add a 30 uL aliquot of methoxyamine hydrochloride/pyridine solution to the dried extract and incubate at 30 C for 90 min with the vial capped.
5. Second derivatization (silylation): Add a 70 uL aliquot of MSTFA + 0.1% TMCS solution to the methoximated extract and incubate at 60 C for 90 min.
6. Detection: Inject the derivatized extract onto the GCxGC-TOFMS instrument (reverse column configuration) and acquire full-scan mass spectra (m/z 33-500) for downstream Fisher-ratio and control-normalized Fisher-ratio biomarker analysis, then discard the analyzed extract.

INTERPRETATION: Successful automated sample processing is confirmed when the resulting chromatographic data reproduce the metabolite/biomarker hit lists (e.g., succinic acid, arachidonic acid, L-methionine, and other patient-vs-control discriminating metabolites) obtained via standard and control-normalized Fisher-ratio analysis, with signal reproducibility and concentration ratios consistent across replicate extraction/derivatization batches, supporting the feasibility of an automated, all-in-one digital microfluidic pipeline for proteomic/metabolomic sample preparation upstream of GCxGC-TOFMS biomarker discovery.
