Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Solid-Phase Extraction (DMF-SPE) Using in Situ Formed Porous Polymer Monolith (PPM) Discs

REAGENTS USED:
  - C12 Casting Solution (butyl acrylate, 1,3-butanediol diacrylate, lauryl acrylate, and 2,2-dimethoxy-2-phenylacetophenone photoinitiator in a 4:1:1 acetonitrile:ethanol:phosphate-buffer porogen; used to form the PPM disc)
  - Activation Solvent (0.1% v/v formic acid in acetonitrile, 5 uL droplets)
  - Equilibration/Wash Solvent (0.5% v/v formic acid in deionized water, 5 uL droplets)
  - Sample (fluorescamine-labeled peptide or fluorescein analyte, 1 uL droplet)
  - Elution Solvent (500 mM sodium borate at pH 9.0, or 0.1% v/v formic acid in acetonitrile, 5 uL droplets)

STEPS:
1. PPM Disc Formation: Dispense a 1 uL droplet of C12 casting solution onto the device, translate it to a central actuation electrode, and cure it by UV irradiation (100 W, 365 nm, 5 min) to form a circular porous polymer monolith disc in situ.
2. Activation: Dispense a 5 uL droplet of activation solvent, actuate it back and forth across the PPM disc five times, incubate 1 min, then actuate the droplet away to a waste reservoir.
3. Equilibration: Dispense a fresh 5 uL droplet of equilibration solvent, actuate it back and forth across the PPM disc five times, incubate 1 min, then actuate the droplet away to waste.
4. Sample Loading: Dispense a 1 uL sample droplet, actuate it back and forth across the PPM disc five times, incubate 2 min (optimized loading time), then actuate the droplet away to waste.
5. Monolith Washing: Dispense a 5 uL droplet of wash solvent, actuate it back and forth across the PPM disc five times, then move it to waste; repeat this wash cycle a second time (2 x 5 uL total).
6. Elution: Dispense a 5 uL droplet of elution solvent, actuate it back and forth across the PPM disc five times, then move it to a collection spot; repeat this elution cycle a second time so that two eluate droplets are pooled (optimized elution volume).
7. Detection: Interrogate the pooled eluate droplet for fluorescence intensity (lambda_ex/lambda_em as appropriate for the analyte) using a plate reader, or alternatively analyze the eluate by nanoESI-MS, to quantify the extracted/recovered analyte relative to a non-extracted control; then discard the analyzed droplet.

INTERPRETATION: Successful DMF-SPE performance is confirmed by an extraction efficiency (93 +/- 14% for a fluorescamine-labeled peptide) comparable to that of commercial C18 ZipTips (92 +/- 5%), successful preparative sample cleanup (recovery of MS signal for angiotensin II after removal of NaCl salt matrix or Pluronic F68 surfactant that otherwise fully suppressed the [M+H]+ signal), and a demonstrated sample-concentration factor of ~9 (12 uL sample eluted into 1 uL), supporting use of the method for on-chip desalting, surfactant removal, and preconcentration ahead of downstream analysis.
