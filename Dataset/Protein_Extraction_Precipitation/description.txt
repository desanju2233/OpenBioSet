Source link: http://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/precipitation.pdf
Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/ac8021554


ASSAY TYPE

Digital microfluidic protein extraction via precipitation, including washing, resolubilization, and fluorescamine-based fluorescence quantification.

REAGENTS USED
bsa_sample (BSA, 50 mg/mL)
tca_precipitant (20% Trichloroacetic acid in DI water)
rinse_solution (chloroform/ACN 70/30 v/v)
resolubilization_buffer (100 mM borate with 1% SDS)
fluorescamine_reagent (5 mg/mL in acetone)
UNIT NOTE

Droplet volumes are normally 140 nL for samples and precipitants, and 315 nL for rinse and resolubilization solutions.

STEPS
Dispense 140 nL droplets of bsa_sample and tca_precipitant.
Merge and mix for 60 seconds on the extraction electrode.
Incubate at 23°C for 5 minutes until the protein is observed to precipitate.
Actuate the supernatant away to the waste reservoir (dispose).
Repeat 3 times to wash the precipitate:
Dispense a 315 nL droplet of rinse_solution.
Heat/incubate at 23°C for 10 seconds.
Drive the rinse solution away to waste (dispose).
Dispense 315 nL of resolubilization_buffer and heat the droplet at 95°C for 5 minutes.
Dispense 13 units of fluorescamine_reagent and mix for 60 seconds.
Detect fluorescence for 2 minutes to quantify the percent recovery of the protein.
Discard the final droplet.
INTERPRETATION

The extraction efficiency is quantitatively evaluated using the fluorescamine assay, which reacts with primary amines at the N-terminus of proteins to produce fluorescence. High fluorescence indicates high protein recovery compared to the control.
