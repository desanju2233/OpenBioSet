Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Electrowetting-Based Digital Microfluidic Protein Droplet Stamping for MALDI Mass Spectrometry (Oil-Encased Droplet Transport, Passive Stamping, and MALDI-TOF Analysis)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Protein Sample (bovine serum albumin (BSA) test solutions at 0.001-10 mg/mL, and the ABI 4700 proteomics analyzer calibration mixture containing des-Arg-Bradykinin, Angiotensin I, Glu-Fibrinopeptide B, ACTH (1-17), ACTH (18-39), and ACTH (7-38) in alpha-cyano-4-hydroxycinnamic acid/acetonitrile/TFA matrix)
  - Silicone Oil (1 cSt filler/encasing medium that immerses droplets to prevent evaporation, minimize non-specific protein adsorption to the Teflon AF surface, and facilitate electrowetting transport)

STEPS:
1. Reservoir loading: Inject the protein sample into the on-chip reservoir, either from a well-plate via syringe-pump pressure (for water/low-viscosity samples) or by pipette (for protein solutions), with the reservoir and droplets immersed in silicone oil.
2. Droplet formation: Generate a unit droplet (~40 nL, electrode pitch L=750 um, spacer thickness H~75 um) from the reservoir using purely internal electrowetting forces, reliable up to 0.01 mg/mL BSA.
3. Transport: Actuate the oil-encased droplet across the electrode array toward the stamping hole; the silicone oil film isolates the droplet from the hydrophobic Teflon AF surface, minimizing irreversible protein adsorption and enabling transport of BSA concentrations up to 10 mg/mL.
4. Passive stamping: Route the droplet to the stamping hole (diameter >> spacer thickness) and vertically stamp it through the polycarbonate top plate onto the target substrate (hydrophobic glass slide with hydrophilic patch, or a standard MALDI target plate), where the larger hole diameter passively forces the droplet out without active pressure, leaving some liquid behind as dead volume.
5. MALDI-MS analysis: Analyze the stamped droplet(s) on a MALDI-TOF mass spectrometer (e.g. ABI 4700 MALDI-MS TOF system) to acquire the mass spectrum and confirm protein identity and relative intensity.
6. Cycle repeat: Repeat droplet formation, transport, and stamping for additional droplets to increase the total amount of protein deposited at the stamping site (e.g. 10 droplets dispensed, 6 stamped, 4 left as dead volume in one reported run).

INTERPRETATION: Successful protein stamping is confirmed by droplet transport being demonstrated for BSA concentrations up to 10 mg/mL (three orders of magnitude higher than prior electrowetting protein-handling reports) and reliable electrowetting-only droplet formation up to 0.01 mg/mL, by passive stamping depositing quantifiable protein amounts onto a target substrate (3.6 fmoles from 0.001 mg/mL and 16 fmoles from 0.01 mg/mL BSA solutions), and by MALDI-TOF mass spectra of the stamped ABI 4700 calibration mixture correctly identifying all constituent proteins (des-Arg-Bradykinin, Angiotensin I, Glu-Fibrinopeptide B, ACTH (1-17), ACTH (18-39)) after on-chip transport and stamping, establishing the feasibility of a compact, fast, moving-part-free electrowetting interface between well-plate sample formats and MALDI-MS target substrates.
