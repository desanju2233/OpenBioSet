Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Droplet-Based Pyrosequencing Using Digital Microfluidics (on-chip PCR amplification, magnetic-bead-based purification, and cyclic dNTP pyrosequencing with real-time luminescence detection)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Genomic DNA (C. parapsilosis genomic DNA purified from culture, PCR template)
  - Primer Mix (ITS1 forward primer 5'-Bio-tccgtaggtgaacctgcgg-3' and ITS2 reverse primer 5'-gctgcgttcttcatcgatgc-3', 0.24 uM each, amplifying a 229 bp fragment)
  - PCR Reagent (20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.4, 1.5 mM MgCl2, 50 mM KCl, 0.2 mM dNTPs, 1 U Platinum Taq polymerase)
  - Magnetic Beads (streptavidin-coated magnetic beads for capture of the biotinylated PCR product)
  - Wash Buffer (used in repeated merge-and-split wash cycles to purify bead-bound ssDNA)
  - Sequencing Primer (hybridizes to the bead-bound single-stranded template for pyrosequencing, e.g. P6/P6-1-style primer)
  - Enzyme Mix (ATP sulfurylase/luciferase/apyrase pyrosequencing enzyme cocktail with adenosine 5' phosphosulfate and luciferin)
  - dNTP Reagents (dATP, dCTP, dGTP, dTTP dispensed sequentially in a defined nucleotide addition order)

STEPS:
1. PCR setup: combine genomic DNA template with the ITS1/ITS2 primer mix and PCR reagent (Tris-HCl/MgCl2/KCl/dNTP/Taq polymerase buffer) to form the amplification reaction droplet.
2. Hot start: incubate the reaction at 95 C for 2 minutes.
3. Amplification: cycle 40 times through 95 C for 15 s (denaturation) and 60 C for 30 s (anneal/extend).
4. Final extension: incubate the reaction at 72 C for 1 minute.
5. Bead capture: merge the amplified, biotinylated PCR product droplet with a streptavidin magnetic bead droplet to capture the product.
6. Magnetic bead washing: repeatedly merge a fresh wash buffer droplet with the bead-bound droplet and split off the wash droplet, cycling to purify the captured single-stranded template (demonstrated over hundreds of wash cycles with no observable bead loss).
7. Primer annealing: merge the washed, bead-bound ssDNA with sequencing primer and incubate at 65 C to hybridize the sequencing primer.
8. Enzyme addition: merge the annealed template droplet with the ATP-sulfurylase/luciferase/apyrase enzyme mix to prepare the pyrosequencing reaction droplet.
9. Cyclic nucleotide dispensing and detection: in a defined dispensing order, sequentially merge each dNTP droplet (dATP, dCTP, dGTP, dTTP) with the reaction droplet and measure the real-time luminescence (photons/sec) generated by each addition; a rising signal indicates nucleotide incorporation into the growing strand, while a flat/background signal indicates a non-incorporating ("bkgd") addition, together revealing the template sequence.
