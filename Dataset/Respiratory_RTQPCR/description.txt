ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic RT-qPCR Platform for Multiplex Respiratory Pathogen Detection

Overview: A multi-target RT-qPCR assay protocol executed on a digital microfluidic platform capable of simultaneously dispensing, merging, and amplifying samples to identify up to 11 pathogens from single specimens.

REAGENTS USED:
  - Extracted RNA/DNA Sample (nasal or throat swab nucleic acid extract, 5 uL)
  - RNase-Free Water (6.25 uL)
  - 2x One Step Q Probe Mix (RT-qPCR master mix component, 12.5 uL)
  - One Step Q Probe Enzyme Mix (reverse transcriptase/polymerase enzyme mix, 1.25 uL)
  - 12 Dried Primer-Probe Sets, pre-stored and dehydrated at individual reaction spots on-chip, one per target: MP, CP, SP, RSVA, ADV, HKU1, 229E, HMPV, N (SARS-CoV-2), ORF1ab (SARS-CoV-2), FLUA, FLUB

STEPS:
1. Master Mix Preparation: Dispense 5 uL RNA/DNA sample, 6.25 uL RNase-free water, 12.5 uL 2x One Step Q Probe Mix, and 1.25 uL One Step Q Probe Enzyme Mix; combine sequentially into a single 25 uL RT-qPCR amplification master mix.
2. Droplet Splitting to Reaction Spots: Transport and split the master mix (2 uL per spot) onto each of the 12 on-chip reaction spots, rehydrating the pre-stored dried primer-probe set at each spot to form 12 independent reaction mixtures (one per pathogen target).
3. Reverse Transcription: Heat each of the 12 reaction mixtures at 50 C for 15 min to reverse-transcribe viral/bacterial RNA into cDNA.
4. Initial Denaturation: Heat each of the 12 reaction mixtures at 95 C for 30 s.
5. Thermal Cycling (Amplification): For each of the 12 reaction mixtures independently, cycle 45 times through 95 C for 10 s (denaturation) then 60 C for 30 s (annealing/extension), with real-time fluorescence acquisition during the anneal/extend step of each cycle.
6. Detection: Acquire the real-time fluorescence signal (delta Rn) from each of the 12 reaction spots over the amplification run; a Ct value is computed per spot from the cycle at which fluorescence crosses ten times the standard deviation above the mean baseline (cycles 3-15), and each spot's reading is compared against its corresponding detection-limit threshold to call the sample positive or negative for that target.
7. Disposal: Dispose of each of the 12 analyzed reaction droplets after the amplification/detection run completes.

INTERPRETATION: Successful multiplex detection is confirmed by pathogen-specific amplification curves crossing threshold within 45 cycles for true positives, no amplification (or amplification only after 45 cycles) for true negatives, and reported diagnostic performance (versus off-chip benchtop RT-qPCR) of TPR 93.33%, TNR 100%, PPV 100%, NPV 99.56%, ACC 99.85% (on the plasmid/clinical validation panel), and a Kappa value of 0.963, supporting use of the DMF RT-qPCR platform for field and point-of-care respiratory pathogen screening in medical resource-limited settings.
