Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic (DMF) Platform for Chemiluminescent Detection of Rubella Virus (RV) IgG and IgM by Indirect ELISA

REAGENTS USED:
  - RV-Coated Paramagnetic Particle Suspension (virus-immobilized magnetic microparticles for antigen capture, 1.8 uL droplets)
  - Patient Sample (serum or plasma, pre-diluted, containing RV IgG and/or RV IgM, 1.8 uL droplets)
  - DMF-Compatible Wash Buffer (1.8 uL droplets, used in 4x wash cycles after each binding step)
  - HRP-Conjugated Goat Anti-Human IgG (detection conjugate for the RV IgG assay, 1.8 uL droplets)
  - HRP-Conjugated Goat Anti-Human IgM (detection conjugate for the RV IgM assay, 1.8 uL droplets)
  - Goat Anti-Human IgG Pretreatment Reagent (blocks endogenous RV IgG/rheumatoid-factor IgM interference before the IgM assay, 0.9 uL droplets)
  - Hydrogen Peroxide, H2O2 (chemiluminescent substrate component, 0.9 uL droplets)
  - Luminol-Enhancer Solution (chemiluminescent substrate component, 0.9 uL droplets)

STEPS:

RV IgG Immunoassay (7-step indirect ELISA):
1. Dispense a droplet of RV-coated paramagnetic particle suspension (1.8 uL) and separate the particles from the storage diluent.
2. Dispense a sample droplet (1.8 uL), deliver it to the particles, mix for 3 min, then magnetically separate particles from the supernatant.
3. Wash the particles in 4 successive 1.8 uL wash-buffer droplets, mixing and then separating from the supernatant each time.
4. Dispense a droplet of HRP-conjugated anti-human IgG (1.8 uL), deliver to the particles, and mix for 2 min.
5. Repeat the 4x wash cycle from step 3.
6. Dispense one droplet each of H2O2 (0.9 uL) and luminol-enhancer solution (0.9 uL), merge them with the particles, and mix for 2 min.
7. Measure the mean chemiluminescent signal over 10 s with the integrated photomultiplier tube (PMT).

RV IgM Immunoassay (identical 7-step assay, preceded by an IgG-blocking pretreatment):
0. Pretreatment: Mix a 1.8 uL sample droplet with a 0.9 uL droplet of goat anti-human IgG pretreatment reagent for 7 min to block endogenous RV IgG and rheumatoid-factor IgM before analysis.
1-7. Repeat the same capture -> 4x wash -> conjugate (HRP-anti-human IgM in place of anti-IgG) -> 4x wash -> substrate -> PMT-detect sequence described above.

INTERPRETATION: Results are reported as a signal-to-cutoff (s/co) ratio, calculated by normalizing the mean chemiluminescent signal of each assay to the mean signal of the corresponding cutoff control (a 10 IU/mL RV IgG calibrator for the IgG assay; the RV IgM cutoff Cal 1 reagent for the IgM assay). An s/co >= 1 is interpreted as positive; an s/co in the gray zone (0.69-0.99 for IgG, 0.75-0.99 for IgM) is interpreted as equivocal; an s/co below the gray zone is interpreted as negative. Diagnostic sensitivity and specificity of 100%/100% for both RV IgG and RV IgM were obtained against a commercial mixed-titer performance panel referenced against an Abbott EIA platform, supporting use of the method as a portable, decentralized alternative to central-laboratory rubella serology for identifying pregnant women at risk of transmitting rubella to their offspring.
