Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic qPCR Cartridge Protocol for Viral Detection (SARS-CoV-2)

REAGENTS USED:
  - cDNA template of the SARS-CoV-2 N gene (SARS-CoV-2 RUO plasmid control, ten-fold serial dilutions from 10,000 to 1 copy/uL)
  - N1 primer-probe mix (U.S. CDC-referenced, IDT)
  - N2 primer-probe mix (U.S. CDC-referenced, IDT)
  - RNase P (RP) primer-probe mix (human internal control gene)
  - TaqMan Fast Advanced Master Mix (2X, Applied Biosystems)
  - Nuclease-free water (diluent, and NTC substitute for template)
  - Positive control template
  - Hexadecane (environmental oil filling the cartridge to prevent droplet evaporation and reduce friction)
  - Tween-20 surfactant (0.1% final concentration in aqueous reagents, reduces surface tension/biofouling)

STEPS:
1. Reaction assembly: On the DMF cartridge, generate a 1.5 uL droplet-scale reaction for each of five parallel tracks/reservoirs (N1, N2, RP, positive control, NTC) by combining 1 uL cDNA template (or nuclease-free water for the NTC), 1.5 uL primer-probe mix, 10 uL TaqMan Fast Advanced Master Mix, and 7.5 uL nuclease-free water (20 uL bulk reaction mix, scaled down on-chip).
2. Enzyme activation: Heat each reaction droplet at 95 C for 2 min (1 cycle) to activate the polymerase.
3. Thermal cycling (40 cycles): For each cycle, denature at 95 C for 1 s, then anneal/extend at 60 C for 20 s, with droplets electrowetting-actuated between the two on-chip temperature zones (~1.75 C/s ramp rate, ~42 min total for 40 cycles).
4. Real-time fluorescence detection: After every thermal cycle, capture the FAM fluorescence intensity of each droplet using the optical module (camera, fluorescence filter cube, LED) to build real-time amplification curves.
5. Data analysis / dispose: Subtract baseline intensity to obtain delta fluorescence intensity (dFI), determine threshold cycle (Ct) values via LinRegPCR, and evaluate qPCR efficiency from the Ct-vs-log(concentration) standard curve; discard analyzed droplets once data collection is complete.

INTERPRETATION: Successful SARS-CoV-2 N gene detection is confirmed by sigmoidal amplification curves resembling standard benchtop qPCR, a limit of detection down to 10 copies/uL (15 copies/droplet) for both N1 and N2 target regions, and standard-curve qPCR efficiencies within the acceptable 90-110% range (92.8% for N1, 101.2% for N2), supporting use of the DMF cartridge as a point-of-care NAAT platform for COVID-19 diagnosis.
