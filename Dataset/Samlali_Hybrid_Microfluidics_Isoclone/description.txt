PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : One Cell, One Drop, One Click: Hybrid Microfluidics for Mammalian Single Cell Isolation
Source Link  : https://www.shihmicrolab.ca/_files/ugd/f0a828_20644bba5a194fc0966d7bba24bbc451.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.202002400
Journal      : Small, 2020, Vol. 16, Article 2002400
Platform     : Hybrid DMF (digital microfluidics + droplet-in-channel microfluidics) on EWOD/electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a hybrid microfluidics method for deterministic single-cell isolation,
encapsulation, on-demand droplet manipulation, and isoclonal recovery for mammalian gene-editing
workflows. The device integrates a bottom digital microfluidic (DMF) layer (chromium co-planar
electrodes on glass + 7 um SU-8 5 dielectric) with a top PDMS channel layer (35 um height,
50-75 um width), enabling individual electrode-addressable control of droplets flowing inside
microfluidic channels -- a configuration referred to as "hybrid" microfluidics.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. Hydrodynamic single-cell trapping in serpentine channel traps (8 um constrictions)
  2. In situ deterministic single-cell encapsulation by phase change (no Poisson statistics)
  3. On-demand droplet operations: forward/reverse release, keep, on-demand generation, and merge
  4. CRISPR-Cas9 gene-editing pipeline: transfection, on-chip fluorescence screening,
     isoclone sorting, capillary recovery, and clonal expansion in a 96-well plate

The complete trap-to-recovery workflow takes approximately 45 minutes, compared to hours
or days required by standard FACS or limited dilution methods.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i   : Chromium electrodes patterned on glass (50 x 75 mm); electrode size ~100 um,
            area 0.06 mm2; co-planar ground and activated electrodes on same plate.
Layer ii  : SU-8 5 dielectric, 7 um thick; spin-coated and hard-baked to 180 C.
Layer iii : PDMS channel layer; channel height 35 um, main channel width 50 um,
            bypass channel width ~50 um; fabricated by soft lithography on SU-8 2035 master.

Device sections:
  - T-junction on-demand droplet generator (2 T-junctions, multiple sub-electrodes)
  - Serpentine single-cell trapping array: 12 traps total, 6 electrode-equipped;
    each trap has 4 co-planar electrodes (2 below main channel, 2 below trap)

Inlets  : I1 (oil + droplets), I2 (cells + priming), I3 (PBS/media for generator),
          I4 (HFE7500 for generator)
Outlets : O1 (waste), O2 (sample recovery / flow reversal), O3 (bridge to encapsulation)

Actuation: 15 kHz AC, 126 Vrms applied via optocoupler switches controlled through
           an in-house Python-based GUI automation framework.


CELL LINES
----------
- MCF-7 (human breast cancer): model cell line for trapping, encapsulation, and viability assays.
  Cultured in DMEM + 10% FBS, no antibiotics, 37 C, 5% CO2.
- NCI-H1299 (human lung squamous cell carcinoma, eGFP+/Luciferase dual-labelled stable line):
  used for gene-editing, isoclone sorting, and expansion.
  Cultured in RPMI-1640 + 10% FBS, no antibiotics, 37 C, 5% CO2.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming
  Reagents : PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free), Pluronics F-127
  Mix      : 2% Pluronics F-127 in PBS
  Duration : 5 min at 0.8-8 nL/s via inlet I1
  Purpose  : Reduces MCF-7 cell adhesion to PDMS channel walls.

Step 2 | Hydrodynamic Single-Cell Trapping
  Cell line      : MCF-7
  Concentration  : 1e5-1e6 cells/mL in PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free), stirred continuously in syringe
  Flow rate      : 1-4 nL/s (optimal); 5 nL/s tested at ~88.3% single-cell efficiency
  Trap design    : 8 um constriction (smaller than MCF-7 cell diameter); positioned at
                   curvature of serpentine channel so Qtrap > Qbypass when trap is empty.
                   Slanted overhang along main channel steers flow into trap.
  Multi-occupancy: >54 consecutive single-cell events confirmed; second cell does not enter
                   occupied trap (increased Rtrap reduces Qtrap).
  Filtering      : Cells passed through 40 um cell strainer before loading to remove clumps.

Step 3 | In Situ Deterministic Single-Cell Encapsulation
  Method         : Phase change from single-phase (aqueous) to two-phase (water-in-oil) flow.
  Oil            : HFE7500 + 2% w/v PEG fluoro-surfactant (Ran Biotechnologies), 4 nL/s
  Electrode      : 15 kHz, 126 Vrms; electric field density ~5x10^6 V/m between main channel
                   and trap; electrostatic force pulls aqueous phase into trap when oil shears.
  Droplet volume : 150.3 +/- 5.6 pL (N=11); monodisperse, no Poisson-based encapsulation.
  Frame sequence : Frame I: trap electrode activated, oil enters main channel.
                   Frame II: trap electrode active, other electrodes grounded; oil cuts aqueous
                             phase at both trap ends; cell remains inside.
                   Frame III: all potentials grounded; oil continues to next trap.

Step 4 | On-Demand Droplet Operations
  Forward release : Actuate electrodes below trap then main channel (15 kHz, 126 Vrms).
                    Flow rate <1 nL/s → >95% release probability.
                    Without actuation: droplet never released at any flow rate.
  Reverse release : Same actuation sequence under reverse oil flow.
                    Hydrodynamic only: >10.92 nL/s needed; with actuation: 100% at any rate.
  Keep            : 4 electrodes activated; field centred at trap opening.
                    Flow rates <45.4 nL/s → >95% keep probability (Ph < Felec).
                    Above 45.4 nL/s: Ph > Felec; droplet escapes.
  On-demand gen.  : T-junction; oil 2-2.5 nL/s, aqueous 0.6 nL/s; 15 kHz 126 Vrms.
                    Droplet volume 250.9 +/- 39 pL.
  Merge           : Incoming droplets merged with trapped droplets on demand; no stream
                    synchronisation required.

Step 5 | Cell Viability Assay
  Cells          : MCF-7 (fresh trap), PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free)
  Actuation      : 15 kHz, 126 Vrms for 30 s total (two top electrodes 10 s each,
                   ground electrode 10 s, two bottom electrodes 10 s)
  Stain          : FDA/PI 1X; 10 uL PI stock (2 ug/mL) + 2.5 uL FDA stock (5 ug/mL)
                   FDA: ex 490 nm / em 526 nm (live cells, green)
                   PI : ex 488 nm / em 617 nm (dead cells, red)
  Incubation     : 10 min on device in dark; imaged at 4x or 10x on inverted fluorescence
                   microscope (Olympus IX73)
  Results        : Control 87.5 +/- 7.2% viability; potentiated 82.5 +/- 8.1% viability
                   (p = 0.6687, unpaired t-test, N=3). No significant difference.
                   ~18% loss attributed to pre-processing handling outside culture environment.

Step 6 | Off-Chip CRISPR-Cas9 Transfection (eGFP KO arm)
  Cell line      : NCI-H1299 (eGFP+/Luciferase stable)
  Plasmid        : pCRISPR_eGFP_314 (Cas9 + mCherry reporter + eGFP sgRNA)
                   eGFP sgRNA sequence: GCAACTACAAGACCCGCGCCG
  Reagent        : Lipofectamine 3000, 5 ug DNA/well
  Format         : 24-well plate; Day 0: subculture; Day 1: transfect; Day 3: harvest
  Incubation     : 48 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 (= 2880 min = 172800 s)
  Results        : ~25.3% transfection efficiency (mCherry+); ~4.95% KO efficiency (GCD assay)
  KO confirm     : Genomic Cleavage Detection assay (GeneArt GCD kit, Thermo Scientific);
                   ~500 bp PCR fragments re-annealed and cleaved; resolved on 2% LAB gel at 220 V.
  Note           : RAF1 KO is an independent parallel experiment (pCRISPR_RAF1_94;
                   RAF1 sgRNA: GCCGCCCGAGAGTCTTAATCG; 13.7% transfection, 8.3% KO efficiency).

Step 7 | On-Chip Isoclone Trapping, Screening, and Encapsulation
  Cells          : Transfected NCI-H1299, resuspended in PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free)
  Trapping rate  : 4 nL/s
  Screening      : Fluorescence microscopy
                   mCherry (ex ~565 nm): positive = successful transfection
                   eGFP   (ex ~460 nm): loss of signal = eGFP knocked out
  Expected hits  : ~1 in 6 traps (~16.7%) contain a mCherry+ isoclone
  Re-trapping    : If trap contains no transfected clone, increase forward flow rate to
                   squeeze cell through trap and load a new cell iteratively.
  Encapsulation  : HFE7500 + 2% fluorosurfactant at 4 nL/s; 15 kHz, 126 Vrms
  Detection      : mCherryGFPDetection module; 30 s fluorescence read
  Sorting        : Positive clone released on-demand toward outlet (forward release)
  Total time     : ~45 min from trapping to release

Step 8 | Isoclone Recovery and Clonal Expansion
  Recovery method:
    1. Positive droplet forward-released on demand to outlet
    2. Loaded into glass capillary pre-filled with HFE7500 2% fluorosurfactant
    3. Capillary tip placed onto 20 uL conditioned media on hydrophobic PTFE membrane
       (0.22 um) on custom 3D-printed holder at 37 C
    4. Capillary flushed with 10 uL FC-40 oil; oil absorbs HFE7500, releasing aqueous droplet
    5. 1 min incubation; isoclone released into conditioned media droplet
    6. Transferred to 96-well plate with 50 uL conditioned media/well at 37 C

  Conditioned media: 50% RPMI-1640 10% FBS 1% pen/strep collected from 1-day 80%-confluent
                     H1299 cells + 50% fresh complete growth media; filter-sterilised; -80 C storage.
  Expansion        : 37 C, 5% CO2; 7 days (= 10080 min = 604800 s)
                     Day 1   : Single adherent clones visible
                     Day 2-3 : mCherry reporter lost (transient transfection, plasmid diluted)
                     Day 5   : Image eGFP-negative clones (460 nm channel)
                     Day 7   : Confirm clonal expansion; media changed with fresh complete media
  Format           : 96-well plate; expand to 50% confluency
