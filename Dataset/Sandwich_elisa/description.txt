Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Sandwich ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Capture Antibody (Immobilized antibody coated onto plate wells to bind target antigen)
  - Blocking Buffer (Minimizes non-specific binding)
  - Sample (Test solution containing target antigen)
  - Detection Antibody (Unlabeled primary antibody specific to a secondary epitope on the antigen)
  - Labeled Secondary Antibody (Enzyme-conjugated secondary antibody directed against the detection antibody)
  - Wash Buffer (Washes away unbound proteins and conjugates)
  - Substrate (Produces a signal upon enzymatic interaction)

STEPS:
1. Capture Coating: Dispense capture antibody and incubate at 4 C overnight (720 min).
2. Blocking: Add blocking buffer, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 60 min.
3. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer.
4. Sample Capture: Add sample, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min to trap target antigen.
5. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer to clear uncaptured sample components.
6. Detection Incubation: Add detection antibody, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min.
7. Washing: Wash 3 times with wash buffer.
8. Secondary Conjugation: Add labeled secondary antibody, mix, and incubate at 37 C for 60 min.
9. Washing: Perform 3 final wash cycles using wash buffer.
10. Substrate Reaction: Add substrate, mix, and incubate at 25 C for 15 min.
11. Detection & Readout: Measure signal; conditionally dispose based on threshold ($\ge 1$).
