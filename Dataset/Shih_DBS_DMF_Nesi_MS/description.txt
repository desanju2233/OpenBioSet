PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Dried Blood Spot Analysis by Digital Microfluidics Coupled to Nanoelectrospray Ionization Mass Spectrometry
Source Link  : https://pubs.acs.org/doi/pdf/10.1021/ac300305s?ref=article_openPDF
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac300305s
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2012, Vol. 84, pp. 3731-3738
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) coupled to nanoelectrospray ionization mass spectrometry (nESI-MS) on EWOD electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated digital microfluidic (DMF) method for the extraction,
derivatization, and nanoelectrospray ionization tandem mass spectrometry (nESI-MS/MS)
quantification of succinylacetone (SA), a biomarker for hepatorenal tyrosinemia (HT,
tyrosinemia type 1), from 3.2 mm dried blood spot (DBS) punches on Whatman 903 filter paper.

The system integrates a standard two-plate DMF device (patterned chromium electrodes on glass
bottom plate + unpatterned ITO-coated glass top plate, both Teflon-AF coated) with a pulled-glass
capillary nanoESI emitter inserted between the two plates. This "sandwich" configuration
immobilizes the emitter without external seals or gaskets, and allows emitters to be exchanged
in seconds without disassembling the device.

A key innovation is an impedance-based automated feedback control system (RBBB Arduino
microcontroller) that detects droplet movement failures and automatically escalates driving
voltage (50 Vrms steps up to 350 Vrms) until movement succeeds -- achieving 100% droplet
movement success across DBS punches versus 60% without feedback.

The protocol is divided into three functional phases:
  1. Sequential methanol pre-extraction (3 cycles) to remove non-SA analytes
  2. SA extraction and hydrazine derivatization (two-step: evaporate then re-dissolve)
  3. On-chip nESI-MS/MS quantification with internal standard normalization

An extended protocol (Fig. 6) additionally enables parallel amino acid (AA) analysis from
the methanol pre-extractate using a second capillary emitter and n-butanol HCl derivatization,
demonstrating compatibility with multi-analyte newborn screening panels.

The DMF method was validated against the gold-standard NSO technique (paired t-test,
p = 0.05, t = 0.8177) with no statistically significant difference at 95% CI, using
75 uL total reagent volume vs 200-310 uL conventionally.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Bottom plate : Chromium electrodes patterned on glass (photolithography + wet etching);
               22 square actuation electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm), 7 reservoir electrodes (5 x 5 mm);
               interelectrode gaps 30 um; contact pads interface with 40-pin connector.
               Coated with 7 um Parylene-C (vapor deposition) + 50 nm Teflon-AF (spin-coated).

Top plate    : Unpatterned ITO-coated glass (Delta Technologies Ltd.);
               37.5 x 25 mm; coated with 50 nm Teflon-AF.

Assembly     : 5 layers double-sided tape spacer (~450 um total gap).
               Gap > DBS punch thickness (~430 um including blood) to allow punch insertion.
               15 uL droplets span approximately two 2.2 x 2.2 mm electrodes.

nESI interface: Pulled-glass capillary emitter (New Objective Inc.); 360 um o.d., 50 um i.d.,
               30 um tip i.d., ~5 cm long; inserted ~2.7 cm between the two plates.
               Emitter tip positioned ~3 mm from LTQ MS orifice.
               Droplet fills capillary (~100 nL) by capillary action on contact.
               DC 1.7-2.2 kV applied to ITO top plate for nanoelectrospray generation.
               Spray stable >200 s, RSD = 7.3%, fluid consumption ~800 nL/min.
               Extended protocol uses two emitters (one per end of device, Fig. 6a).

Fabrication  : University of Toronto ECTI cleanroom facility.
               Photomask printed at Pacific Arts and Design (Markham, ON).


REAGENTS
--------
- MeOH (neat methanol):
    Pre-extraction solvent; 15 uL droplets x 3 cycles = 45 uL total.
    Extracts amino acids and acylcarnitines; SA remains in residual spot.

- HydrazineReagent (SA extraction/derivatization/MS solvent):
    6.5 mM hydrazine + 15 uM 13C5-SA in 80:20 acetonitrile:H2O + 0.1% v/v formic acid.
    15 uL droplets x 2 steps = 30 uL total.
    Derivatizes SA to hydrazone; 13C5-SA serves as internal standard.

- InternalStandard_13C5_SA (3,4,5,6,7-13C-succinylacetone):
    Supplier: Cambridge Isotope Laboratories (Andover, MA).
    Concentration: 15 uM. MS transition: m/z 160 -> 142.
    Co-formulated in HydrazineReagent (modelled separately in BioScript).

- AADerivatizationReagent (extended protocol only):
    3 N HCl in n-butanol; butyl ester derivatization at 75 C.

- AASolvent (extended protocol only):
    Acetonitrile:H2O 4:1 v/v; re-dissolution of dried AA derivatives.

- DBSPunch:
    3.2 mm diameter punch from Whatman 903 filter paper bearing dried blood.
    Toronto samples: male EDTA blood (Biochemed Services) spiked with SA (5-80 uM) and 13C5-SA.
    Ottawa samples: NSO standard technique; stored -20 C; pre-fortified with 13C5-SA before use.
    Note: DBSPunch is a solid-phase persistent object on the DMF bottom plate, not a liquid
    reagent. Declared as a BioScript manifest to allow mix step referencing.


ACTUATION AND FEEDBACK CONTROL
-------------------------------
Waveform     : Sine wave, 15 kHz, 150-350 Vrms
Source       : Function generator (Agilent Technologies) + amplifier
Contact pads : 40-pin connector; automated droplet control via C++ software / Arduino USB

Feedback control system (new, Table 1):
  - Buffered impedance measurement circuit managed by RBBB Arduino (Modern Device)
  - Digital potentiometer (1-10 kOhm) for automatic thresholding per liquid type
  - Actuation pulse: 505 ms (500 ms drive + 5 ms measure)
  - Failure response: step-up 50 Vrms per failed pulse (max 350 Vrms)
  - Relays: 80 AQV259H Panasonic solid-state relays, 2 per output (40 outputs)
  - Control software: custom C++ program via USB to Arduino
  - Result: 100% success rate (avg 4.8 +/- 1.3 pulses) vs 60% success (avg 25.6 +/- 4.8
    pulses) at constant 150 Vrms without feedback. First system with automated
    feedback-controlled variation of actuation potentials.


MS/MS DETECTION
---------------
Instrument   : LTQ linear ion trap mass spectrometer (Thermo Scientific)
Ionization   : Nanoelectrospray (nESI), positive mode

SA quantification (MS/MS):
  Derivatization   : Hydrazine -> SA hydrazone (reaction: Sander et al. Clin. Chem. 2006)
  Parent ion scan  : 25% collision-induced dissociation (CID)
  Scan range       : m/z 100-300
  SA transition    : m/z 155 -> 137 (neutral loss H2O, -18 m/z)
  IS transition    : m/z 160 -> 142 (neutral loss H2O, -18 m/z; 13C5-SA)
  Averaging        : 50 acquisitions per spectrum; >=3 samples per condition
  Calibration      : SA/13C5-SA intensity ratio vs SA concentration (linear regression)
  R^2              : 0.9923
  LOD              : 4.95 uM (2.43 ng)
  Extraction efficiency: 85.2% (CV = 6.85%)
  Spray RSD        : 7.3% (TIC, Fig. 1d)
  HT clinical range: 16-150 uM (well above LOD)
  Tip washing      : 4-5 rinse droplets reduce SA signal to <LOD (Fig. 5)

AA analysis (extended protocol, MS1, Fig. 6b):
  Derivatization   : 3 N HCl in n-butanol, 75 C (butyl esters)
  AAs detected     : VAL, MET, TYR, HIS, GLN, PRO, PHE, ASP
  Mode             : Proof-of-concept, non-quantitative; primary MS (MS1)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | MeOH Pre-Extraction Cycle 1
  Reagents  : MeOH (15 uL), DBSPunch
  Operation : mix (droplet actuated to DBS punch)
  Duration  : 15 min (900 s) at room temperature
  IR op     : preExtract11[0] = mix(meoh11[0], dbsPunch11[0]) @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : First methanol wash; extracts amino acids, acylcarnitines, and small
              molecules. SA not efficiently extracted by MeOH; retained in spot.
  Outcome   : MeOH extractate actuated to waste reservoir (drain).

Step 2 | MeOH Pre-Extraction Cycle 2
  Reagents  : MeOH (15 uL), DBSPunch (persistent)
  Operation : mix
  Duration  : 15 min (900 s) at room temperature
  IR op     : preExtract21[0] = mix(meoh21[0], dbsPunch11[0]) @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : Second MeOH wash; DBSPunch reused (solid-phase persistent object).
  Outcome   : Drain to waste.

Step 3 | MeOH Pre-Extraction Cycle 3
  Reagents  : MeOH (15 uL), DBSPunch (persistent)
  Operation : mix
  Duration  : 15 min (900 s) at room temperature
  IR op     : preExtract31[0] = mix(meoh31[0], dbsPunch11[0]) @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : Third and final MeOH wash. Residual punch now ready for SA extraction.
  Outcome   : Drain to waste.

Step 4 | SA Extraction and Derivatization - Part A (evaporation step)
  Reagents  : HydrazineReagent (15 uL), InternalStandard_13C5_SA, DBSPunch (persistent)
  Operation : mix + heat
  Mix duration  : 15 min (900 s) contact with DBS punch
  Heat          : 37 C for 15 min (900 s) on hot-plate until evaporation
  IR ops    : extractionMix11[0] = mix(hydrazineReagent11[0], is11[0]) @ 5.0SECOND
              saExtraction11[0] = mix(extractionMix11[0], dbsPunch11[0]) @ 900.0SECOND
              heat(saExtraction11[0]) @ 37.0CELSIUS @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : 6.5 mM hydrazine extracts SA from residual DBS and derivatizes it to
              hydrazone. Incubation at 37 C drives evaporation, concentrating product.
              Internal standard co-dispensed for ratio-metric quantification.

Step 5 | SA Extraction and Derivatization - Part B (circular re-dissolution)
  Reagents  : HydrazineReagent (15 uL), InternalStandard_13C5_SA, saExtraction1
  Operation : mix (circular 5-electrode actuation pattern)
  Duration  : 1 min (60 s) at room temperature
  IR ops    : extractionMix21[0] = mix(hydrazineReagent21[0], is21[0]) @ 5.0SECOND
              derivatizedSA1[0] = mix(extractionMix21[0], saExtraction11[0]) @ 60.0SECOND
  Purpose   : Fresh hydrazine droplet re-dissolves and collects evaporated SA hydrazone.
              Circular actuation around 5 adjacent electrodes maximises recovery.

Step 6 | nESI-MS/MS Quantification of Derivatized SA
  Inputs    : derivatizedSA
  Operation : detect (saAnalysis module)
  Duration  : 200 s
  IR op     : saSignal1[0] = detect(saAnalysis, derivatizedSA1[0]) @ 200.0SECOND
  Purpose   : Droplet driven to capillary emitter inlet; fills by capillary action (~100 nL).
              DC 1.7-2.2 kV applied to ITO top plate; stable nESI generated into LTQ.
              MS/MS: 25% CID, neutral loss 18, transitions m/z 155->137 (SA) and 160->142 (IS).
              SA/IS ratio used for calibration curve quantification (R^2 = 0.9923, LOD 4.95 uM).
  Branch    :
    Pass (BB2) : saSignal > 0  -> SA detected above LOD; dispose(derivatizedSA)
    Fail (BB3) : saSignal <= 0 -> signal below LOD or spray failure; drain(derivatizedSA)

Step 2a | AA Extended Protocol - MeOH Extraction and Evaporation (BB4, post-SA join)
  Reagents  : MeOH (15 uL), DBSPunch (second punch / conceptual extractate)
  Operation : mix + heat
  Mix duration  : 5 min (300 s) [shorter than SA protocol]
  Heat          : 25 C for 15 min (900 s) [ambient evaporation to dryness]
  IR ops    : aaExtract1[0] = mix(meoh_aa1[0], dbsPunch21[0]) @ 300.0SECOND
              heat(aaExtract1[0]) @ 25.0CELSIUS @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : Isolates MeOH pre-extractate for AA analysis. In paper this is performed
              concurrently (Fig. 6 setup); linearized in BioScript after SA QC join.

Step 2b | AA Extended Protocol - Butyl Ester Derivatization
  Reagents  : AADerivatizationReagent (15 uL), aaExtract
  Operation : mix + heat
  Mix duration  : 5 s
  Heat          : 75 C for 15 min (900 s) until dryness
  IR ops    : aaDerivMix1[0] = mix(aaDerivReagent1[0], aaExtract1[0]) @ 5.0SECOND
              heat(aaDerivMix1[0]) @ 75.0CELSIUS @ 900.0SECOND
  Purpose   : 3 N HCl in n-butanol derivatizes amino acids to n-butyl esters at 75 C.

Step 2c | AA Extended Protocol - Re-dissolution and nESI-MS Analysis
  Reagents  : AASolvent (15 uL), aaDerivMix
  Operation : mix + detect (aminoAcidAnalysis module)
  Mix duration  : 1 min (60 s)
  Detect duration: 200 s
  IR ops    : aaReadySample1[0] = mix(aaSolventDrop1[0], aaDerivMix1[0]) @ 60.0SECOND
              aaSignal1[0] = detect(aminoAcidAnalysis, aaReadySample1[0]) @ 200.0SECOND
  Purpose   : ACN:H2O re-dissolves dry butyl ester derivatives; droplet actuated to
              second nESI emitter (Fig. 6a, top-left). Primary MS spectrum collected.
              AAs detected: VAL, MET, TYR, HIS, GLN, PRO, PHE, ASP (Fig. 6b).
  Branch    :
    Pass (BB5) : aaSignal > 0  -> AA peaks detected; dispose(aaReadySample)
    Fail (BB6) : aaSignal <= 0 -> no signal; drain(aaReadySample)

                 DBSPunch, InternalStandard_13C5_SA
drain vs dispose note:
                 lilott8/BioScript emits dispose() for both drain and dispose in IR.
                 Semantic intent preserved in .bs source comments only.
DBS abstraction note:
                 DBSPunch is a solid-phase persistent object, not a liquid reagent.
                 Declared as manifest and reused across all mix steps in BB1 (dbsPunch11[0]).
                 Second DBSPunch dispense (dbsPunch21[0]) in BB4 represents the conceptual
                 MeOH extractate isolated for AA analysis (linearization of parallel branch).
AA branch note:
                 In the paper, AA extraction runs concurrently with SA extraction (Fig. 6).
                 BioScript's linear execution model requires sequential encoding; AA branch
                 is placed after the SA QC join (BB4) as the correct approximation.
