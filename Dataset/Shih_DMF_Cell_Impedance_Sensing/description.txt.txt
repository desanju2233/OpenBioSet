PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Microfluidics with Impedance Sensing for Integrated Cell Culture and Analysis
Source Link  : https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0956566312007105
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bios.2012.10.035
Journal      : Biosensors and Bioelectronics, 2013, Vol. 42, pp. 314-320
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) with integrated label-free impedance sensing on EWOD/electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first digital microfluidic system capable of impedance-based sensing
of mammalian cells, integrating cell seeding, long-term culture, and multiplexed label-free analysis
on a single DMF device. The approach repurposes the feedback-control impedance circuit already used
for high-fidelity droplet position sensing (Vsense loop) to simultaneously measure cell density
as a function of impedance -- eliminating the need for fluorescent dyes, enzymatic stripping,
fixatives, or imaging for routine cell population analysis.

Three assays are validated in sequence on the same device platform:
  1. Calibration -- impedance vs. surface density regression for three cell lines
  2. Proliferation -- 4-day growth curve monitoring by periodic impedance measurement
  3. Serum screening -- 5-plex on-chip FCS dilution series with 4-day growth readout

The system achieves approximately 1000-fold reduction in reagent use compared to commercial
multiwell-plate impedance systems (e.g. Applied Biophysics ECIS: 1.5-4 mL per assay vs.
1-10 uL per assay in DMF format). Automated media exchange without intervention enables
uninterrupted long-term culture and analysis.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Bottom plate : 66 electrodes on glass substrate; coated with Parylene-C dielectric + Teflon-AF
               hydrophobic layer. Electrode types: reservoir, driving, active dispensing,
               and cell-sensor (ITO, patterned). Device dimensions: 50 x 100 mm.
Top plate    : ITO-coated glass with Teflon-AF hydrophobic coating; 6 hydrophilic cell-culture
               sites (1 mm diameter, exposed ITO) patterned for cell attachment and virtual
               microwell formation.

Droplet volumes:
  Unit droplet    : ~1 uL (covers area over single driving electrode)
  Virtual microwell: ~0.2 uL (sub-droplet spontaneously formed when unit droplet is translated
                     across hydrophilic ITO culture site; passive dispensing, S1-S3 program)

Reagent exchange (E1-E3 program): three-droplet sequential wash sequence replaces virtual
microwell contents (e.g. cell media -> sucrose buffer) without disturbing the adherent cell
monolayer on the hydrophilic ITO surface.

Feedback control: Vsense loop (Fig S1) repeatedly measures droplet impedance to enable
high-fidelity automated droplet movement (104 movements per full protocol run).
Same hardware repurposed for cell density measurement by detecting Vsense as a function
of cell surface density in low-conductivity sucrose medium.


IMPEDANCE SENSING SYSTEM
-------------------------
Sensing voltage  : 100 Vrms AC pulse, 1 s duration
Sensing frequency: 15 kHz (optimal; selected from 5, 15, 30 kHz characterisation)
Measurement medium: 500 mM aqueous sucrose (10 mM HEPES, 0.05% Pluronic F68, pH 7.4)
Rationale        : Low-conductivity sucrose resolves density-dependent Vsense differences
                   that are indistinguishable in full cell media (p=0.101 in media vs.
                   p=2.36e-7 in sucrose at 15 kHz for NIH-3T3 low vs. high density).
Sucrose exposure : ~10 min per measurement cycle; no impact on proliferation for any cell type.
LOD              : 20-25 cells/mm2 (improvable by thinner insulator, smaller electrode, reduced gap).
Circuit model    : Modified from Morgan et al. 2007; adds capacitor element for DMF insulator
                   and bottom sub-circuit for electrode-adhered cell interface.


CELL LINES
----------
- HeLa (human cervical carcinoma): DMEM + 10% FCS + penicillin (100 IU/mL) +
  streptomycin (100 ug/mL) + 0.05% w/v Pluronic F68. 37 C, 5% CO2.
- NIH-3T3 (mouse embryonic fibroblast): same media as HeLa.
- CHO-K1 (Chinese hamster ovary): 50/50 v/v F-12/DMEM + 10% FCS + penicillin +
  streptomycin + 0.05% w/v Pluronic F68. 37 C, 5% CO2.

All cell suspensions supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68 (w/v) to prevent biofouling
during DMF electrode actuation. Cells detached with 0.25% w/v trypsin + 1 mM EDTA
before each DMF experiment, centrifuged, and resuspended at target density.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Cell Seeding and Culture -- Calibration Assay
  Cell lines     : HeLa, NIH-3T3, CHO-K1
  Densities      : 0.5 x 10^6, 1.0 x 10^6, 2.0 x 10^6 cells/mL (9 conditions total; 3 lines x 3 densities)
  Operation      : Passive dispensing (S1-S3): unit droplet driven over hydrophilic ITO site
                   leaves 0.2 uL virtual microwell containing cell suspension.
                   Abstracted as mix in BioScript (no DMF electrode mixing implied).
  Incubation     : 24 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 (= 1440 min = 86400 s)
  Media          : HeLa/NIH-3T3: complete DMEM; CHO-K1: complete F-12/DMEM (50/50)
  Replicates     : 5 per condition

Step 2 | Sucrose Exchange and Vsense Measurement -- Calibration Assay
  Exchange medium : 500 mM sucrose buffer (E1-E3 three-droplet wash; ~10 min sucrose exposure)
  Sensing         : 100 Vrms, 15 kHz, 1 s pulse; Vsense recorded
  Validation      : ImageJ microscopy cell count: surface density (cells/mm2) and area (mm2)
                    calculated for each virtual microwell; used to generate regression curves.
  Regression      : Vsense vs. surface density linear over >= 1 order of magnitude:
                    HeLa R2=0.9965; NIH-3T3 R2=0.9935; CHO-K1 R2=0.9920
  QC gate         : NIH-3T3 high-density Vsense (nih3t3High_Vsense) used as gate signal.
                    Pass (BB2): dispose all calibration droplets.
                    Fail (BB3): drain all calibration droplets.

Step 3 | Cell Seeding and 4-Day Proliferation Monitoring
  Cell lines      : HeLa, NIH-3T3, CHO-K1
  Seeding density : 0.25 x 10^6 cells/mL (all three lines)
  Operation cycle : For each 24h timepoint (24h, 48h, 72h, 96h):
                    (a) Exchange media with sucrose (E1-E3)
                    (b) Vsense at 15 kHz, 1 s; translate to surface density via calibration curves
                    (c) Exchange sucrose with fresh complete media (E1-E3)
                    (d) Culture 24h at 37 C, 5% CO2
  Replicates      : 5 per cell line
  Results         : Day 1: all lines below LOD (~20-25 cells/mm2).
                    Days 2-4: measurable densities; NIH-3T3 and HeLa proliferate faster
                    than CHO-K1, consistent with standard macroscale culture conditions.
  QC gate         : NIH-3T3 Day 4 Vsense (nih_vsense_d4) used as gate signal.
                    Pass (BB5): dispose Day 4 sucrose droplets.
                    Fail (BB6): drain Day 4 sucrose droplets.

Step 4 | Serum Screening Assay (5-Plex FCS Dilution Series)
  Cell line       : NIH-3T3
  Seeding density : 0.25 x 10^5 cells/mL (10x lower than proliferation assay)
  Initial culture : 6 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 in complete DMEM (= 360 min = 21600 s) for attachment
  FCS concentrations: 0.625%, 1.25%, 2.5%, 5%, 10% (5-plex, 6 virtual microwells on device)
  Dilution method : On-chip serial dilution (D1-D9 program); 20% FCS in serum-free DMEM as
                    concentrated stock; serum-free DMEM as diluent.
                    Dilution chain:
                      10%    FCS: 1 part FCS_Stock (20%) + 1 part diluent
                      5%     FCS: 1 part 10% mix    + 1 part diluent
                      2.5%   FCS: 1 part 5% mix     + 1 part diluent
                      1.25%  FCS: 1 part 2.5% mix   + 1 part diluent
                      0.625% FCS: 1 part 1.25% mix  + 1 part diluent
  Operation cycle : Same as proliferation assay (Steps 3 cycle) except FCS-specific media
                    regenerated on-chip (D1-D9) for each daily refresh instead of plain DMEM.
  Measurement points: 24h, 48h, 72h, 96h
  Replicates      : 5 per concentration
  Results         : 5% and 10% FCS: sigmoidal growth profiles.
                    0.625%-2.5% FCS: flat or declining profiles; poor proliferation.
                    Max surface density ~200 cells/mm2 at 10% FCS on Day 4.
  QC gate         : 10% FCS Day 4 Vsense (vsense_10_d4) used as gate signal (highest serum,
                    highest expected Vsense).
                    Pass (BB8): dispose all 5 concentration Day 4 sucrose droplets.
                    Fail (BB9): drain all 5 concentration Day 4 sucrose droplets.


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : shih_dmf_cell_impedance_sensing.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG file       : shih_dmf_cell_impedance_sensing_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations in s or m only (h token not supported by compiler)
                 1440m = 86400.0 SECOND (24h culture/incubation cycles)
                 360m  = 21600.0 SECOND (6h initial attachment in serum assay)
Modules        : impedanceSensing, cellCountDetection
Virtual microwell abstraction:
                 mix steps labelled "seeding" abstract passive dispensing (S1-S3) event;
                 not DMF electrode mixing. No reagent mixing occurs at these steps.
Sucrose exchange abstraction:
                 mix steps labelled "exchange" abstract E1-E3 three-droplet sequential wash;
                 single mix statement represents the full exchange program.
Serial dilution note:
                 BioScript has no loop or parametric dilution support. All 5 FCS concentration
                 chains are fully unrolled for the initial dilution and each of 3 daily refresh
                 cycles (Days 1, 2, 3), resulting in explicit variable chains per concentration.
QC gate note   : Variable alias assignment (e.g. calibSignal = nih3t3High_Vsense) is not
                 registered in the compiler symbol table and causes UndefinedVariable error
                 at dispose. Detect result variables used directly in if conditions.
drain vs dispose: drain and dispose both lower to dispose() in IR; semantically equivalent
                 at the lilott8/BioScript compiler level.
