PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Versatile Microfluidic Device for Automating Synthetic Biology
Source Link  : https://escholarship.org/content/qt07q6b4f6/qt07q6b4f6.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/acssynbio.5b00062
Journal      : ACS Synthetic Biology, 2015, Vol. 4, pp. 1151-1164
Platform     : Hybrid DMF (digital microfluidics + droplet-in-channel microfluidics) on EWOD/electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a hybrid microfluidic platform for automating the synthetic biology
build-and-test cycle. The device integrates a digital microfluidic (DMF) bottom plate (76
patterned gold/chromium electrodes on glass, 7 um SU-8 dielectric, Aquapel hydrophobic
coating) with a PDMS serpentine incubation channel and an on-chip electroporation region.
The three functional regions -- DNA assembly (DMF), queuing and incubation (channel), and
transformation (electroporation electrodes) -- are integrated on a single 75 x 25 mm chip.

Three DNA assembly methods are implemented and validated:
  1. Golden Gate assembly (25 C, 10 min; T4 DNA ligase; Type IIS BsaI restriction)
  2. Gibson assembly (50 C, 15 min; 2x Gibson master mix)
  3. Yeast/TAR cloning assembly (25 C, 10 min; no additional reagent; in vivo assembly)

For each method, 16 combinatorial plasmid variants (4 Promoter variants x 4 BCD-gfp variants)
are assembled, transferred into the incubation channel, and electroporated into E. coli DH5alpha
(or S. cerevisiae CEN.PK for yeast assembly) on-chip. Each step uses ~0.2 uL dispensed
subdroplets from 1.5 uL reservoir droplets (10x lower total volume than comparable platforms).

Cross-contamination between the 16 sequential reactions is prevented by Pluronic F-127
(0.05% w/v) surfactant additive in HFE 7500 oil -- the first application of this strategy
to on-chip DNA assembly -- eliminating the need for additional wash reservoirs or steps.

The BioScript file encodes the Golden Gate assembly arm (representative primary method;
full 16-plasmid sequencing panel shown in paper Fig 6) for the canonical pProm1_BCD1-gfp
plasmid (~3070 bp). Gibson and Yeast arms would differ only in the ligase manifest and
heat() parameters and should be encoded in separate .bs files.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Dimensions : 75 x 25 mm (standard microscope slide form factor)

DMF bottom plate:
  Substrate     : Gold- and chromium-coated glass (Telic)
  Electrodes    : 76 total; interelectrode gap 20 um
    - 12 reservoir electrodes (3.6 x 3.0 mm); 1.5 uL droplet capacity
    - 12 narrow electrodes (2.5 x 1.0 mm); 100% dispensing fidelity
    - 8 mixing electrodes (1.2 x 1.2 mm)
    - 3 digital-to-channel electrodes (1.75 x 1.75 mm)
    - 41 actuation electrodes (1.2 x 1.2 mm)
  Dielectric    : SU-8-5, 7 um thick (spin-coated)
  Hydrophobic   : Aquapel (0.2 um filtered), 15 min, removed with Kimwipe
  Top plate     : Unpatterned ITO-glass, flush with PDMS side
  Spacer        : SU-8, ~140 um thick; dispensed droplets ~0.2 uL

Incubation and queuing region (PDMS serpentine channel):
  Width         : 1.5 mm
  Capacity      : 16 droplets; >2 h without volume loss
  Temperature   : Region-specific Peltier heater (Arduino RBBB PID control)
                  Heats incubation zone only; reservoirs remain at room temperature
                  (critical: prevents T4 ligase heat inactivation above 50 C)
  Valves        : 4 pneumatic elastomeric microvalves (Parker VSO-EP controller)
  Inlets        : 2 oil inlets (HFE 7500, 5 uL/s) + 1 syringe-vacuum inlet (0.1 uL/s)
  Transfer      : Droplet drawn from DMF surface by syringe-vacuum then driven by oil phase

Electroporation region:
  Electrodes    : 2 bare gold (no dielectric), 2.0 x 0.3 mm, 100 um gap
  Dispensing    : 4 electrodes at cell-inlet/channel intersection
                  (three 1.4 x 0.7 mm + one 4.0 x 0.7 mm)
  Microvalve    : 1 elastomeric valve (valve 5) prevents cross-contamination between
                  successive assembled plasmid droplets
  Detection     : Impedance feedback (Vsense threshold ~0.15 V); oil phase > 10 GOhm;
                  droplet 0.1-10 MOhm; triggers pulse delivery automatically

Automation system:
  Controller    : Arduino RBBB; driving potential ~100-120 V rms at 8 kHz (Agilent function
                  generator); hardware: neMESYS syringe pumps (Cetoni), Parker pneumatic
                  actuators, Peltier heater, function generator
  Dispensing precision : ~1.5% volumetric variation (6 successive droplets)


REAGENTS
--------
Carrier oil       : HFE 7500 (3M Novec); biocompatible, immiscible with aqueous DNA solutions
Surfactant        : 0.05% w/v Pluronic F-127 in all DNA solutions (prevents biofouling;
                    eliminates cross-contamination without wash steps)
DNA fragments     : 30-40 ng/uL each; DpnI digested 37 C 1 h; BsaI digested overnight 37 C
                    (Golden Gate only); gel-purified on 1% agarose
Vector backbone   : pFAB4876-based, p15A origin + kanamycin resistance; ~2100 bp (GG/Gibson)
                    pRS424, 2 micron origin + TRP1 (yeast) + F1 + ampicillin; ~5600 bp (Yeast)
Promoter inserts  : Prom1, Prom2, Prom9, Prom11; ~100 bp; from pFAB4876/4884/4924/4932
BCD-gfp inserts   : BCD1, BCD2, BCD20, BCD21 fused to sfGFP; ~800 bp; from pFAB4876/77/82/83
T4 DNA ligase     : 2.5 U/uL with 0.05% Pluronic F-127 (NEB); for Golden Gate only
Gibson master mix : 2x Gibson Assembly Master Mix (NEB); for Gibson only
EP media (E. coli): 8 mM Na2HPO4, 2 mM KH2PO4, 250 mM sucrose pH 7.4
EP media (yeast)  : 1.0 M sorbitol
E. coli cells     : DH5alpha electrocompetent (NEB C2989K); plated on LB + kan 50 ug/mL
Yeast cells       : CEN.PK (TRP deletion); plated on CSM-agar delta-TRP
Recovery media    : 200 uL LB broth (E. coli); 200 uL deionized water (yeast)
Plasmid source    : JBEI public registry (https://public-registry.jbei.org/login)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Oil Phase Priming
  Reagents    : HFE 7500 + 0.05% w/v Pluronic F-127
  Operation   : 1 mL pipetted onto DMF surface
  Purpose     : Oil surrounds all aqueous droplets throughout protocol; Pluronic F-127
                prevents biofouling and cross-contamination without wash steps.
                First application of this strategy to on-chip DNA assembly.

Step 2 | Dispense Vector Backbone
  Reagent     : VectorBackbone (pFAB4876-based, BsaI + DpnI digested, gel-purified)
  Volume      : 1.5 uL reservoir; ~0.2 uL dispensed subdroplet
  Notes       : ~2100 bp band (Golden Gate). Reservoir refilled via SU-8 capillary
                between successive reactions (16 total per run).

Step 3 | Dispense Promoter1 Insert
  Reagent     : Promoter1 (pFAB4876, ~100 bp; DpnI + BsaI digested, gel-purified)
  Volume      : 1.5 uL reservoir; ~0.2 uL dispensed
  Notes       : Four variants used across 16 reactions: Prom1, Prom2, Prom9, Prom11.
                This .bs file encodes Prom1 as the representative variant.

Step 4 | Dispense BCD1-gfp Insert
  Reagent     : BCD1_gfp (pFAB4876, ~800 bp; DpnI + BsaI digested, gel-purified)
  Volume      : 1.5 uL reservoir; ~0.2 uL dispensed
  Notes       : Four variants: BCD1, BCD2, BCD20, BCD21 (all fused to sfGFP). GFP enables
                colony color screening: green = correct insert, white = self-ligation artifact.

Step 5 | Dispense T4 DNA Ligase
  Reagent     : T4 DNA ligase, 2.5 U/uL + 0.05% w/v Pluronic F-127
  Volume      : 1.5 uL reservoir; ~0.2 uL dispensed
  Notes       : Reservoir maintained at room temperature while incubation zone is Peltier-
                heated. T4 ligase is heat-inactivated above 50 C; region-specific heating
                allows reagent storage on-chip without degradation. Gibson assembly substitutes
                2x Gibson master mix; Yeast assembly requires no additional reagent.

Step 6 | Mix Vector Backbone with Promoter1
  Inputs      : vectorDrop, promoterDrop
  Output      : dnaMix
  Duration    : 5 s
  Actuation   : ~100-120 V rms, 8 kHz; 2-3 circular mixing passes over mixing electrodes

Step 7 | Add BCD1-gfp to DNA Mix
  Inputs      : dnaMix, bcdDrop
  Output      : threePartMix
  Duration    : 5 s
  Volume      : ~0.6 uL (three-component droplet)

Step 8 | Add T4 Ligase to Complete Golden Gate Mix
  Inputs      : threePartMix, ligaseDrop
  Output      : assemblyMix
  Duration    : 10 s
  Total volume: ~0.8 uL
  Notes       : Paper Table 1: 0.8 uL total, 2 DNA fragment insertions, 16 plasmid
                combinations. Droplet circulates 2-3 times in circular fashion on
                digital-to-channel electrodes (1.75 x 1.75 mm) near D2D interface.

Step 9 | Digital-to-Droplet Transfer and Golden Gate Incubation
  Input       : assemblyMix
  Operation   : heat at 25 C for 10 min (600 s)
  Transfer    : Syringe-vacuum at 0.1 uL/s draws droplet from DMF; HFE 7500 at 5 uL/s
                drives droplet into serpentine incubation zone
  Incubation  : 25 C (room temperature), 10 min; BsaI-HF creates 4-bp overhangs;
                T4 ligase joins complementary sticky ends
  Peltier     : Region-specific; incubation zone at 25 C; reservoirs unheated
  Alternatives: Gibson: 50 C, 15 min. Yeast/TAR: 25 C, 10 min (no ligase).

Step 10 | Assembly QC Detection
  Input       : assemblyMix
  Output      : assemblySignal
  Module      : assemblyQC
  Duration    : 30 s
  Notes       : Abstracts GFP colony color screening after off-chip electroporation and
                plating. Expected pass rate: 95% Golden Gate (76/80), 100% Gibson (80/80),
                97.2% Yeast (70/72). QC branch gate decides proceed vs. discard.

  Branch BB2 (assemblySignal > 0)  : dispose(assemblyMix) -- proceed to transformation
  Branch BB3 (assemblySignal <= 0) : drain(assemblyMix)   -- ligation failed; discard

Step 11 | Dispose Assembly QC Signal [BB4 join]
  Operation   : dispose(assemblySignal)
  Notes       : Join block; transformation branch begins.

Step 12 | Dispense E. coli Cells
  Reagent     : CompetentEcoli (DH5alpha, NEB C2989K)
  Output      : cellDroplet
  Notes       : Dispensed from cell inlet via 4 microelectrodes at cell-inlet/channel
                intersection. Elastomeric microvalve (valve 5) closed to prevent
                cross-contamination with other assembled plasmid droplets in channel.

Step 13 | Dispense Incubated Plasmid (Channel Exit)
  Reagent     : AssembledPlasmid
  Output      : incubatedPlasmid
  Notes       : Models the assembled pProm1_BCD1-gfp droplet exiting the serpentine
                incubation channel and entering the electroporation zone, driven by
                HFE 7500 at 5 uL/s (all channel valves closed per Fig S2).
                BioScript ownership note: assemblyMix was consumed by detect(assemblyQC)
                in BB1; incubatedPlasmid is a new fluid object at this CFG location,
                representing the same physical droplet. This avoids the assemblyMix2
                phantom SSA index emitted by the compiler when a variable is re-used
                after detect has consumed it.

Step 14 | Mix Cells with Electroporation Media
  Inputs      : cellDroplet, electroporationMedia
  Output      : cellMix
  Duration    : 5 s
  EP media    : 8 mM Na2HPO4, 2 mM KH2PO4, 250 mM sucrose pH 7.4
  Notes       : Osmotically balanced buffer prevents cell lysis during pulse.

Step 15 | Merge Incubated Plasmid with Cell Mix
  Inputs      : incubatedPlasmid, cellMix
  Output      : preMix
  Duration    : 5 s
  Volume      : ~2 uL (merged droplet positioned over electroporation electrodes)

Step 16 | Electroporation
  Input       : preMix
  Operation   : heat at 37 C for 60 s (models post-pulse recovery temperature)
  Pulse params: 3 x 100 ms DC square pulses at 1800 V/cm (E. coli)
                Impedance feedback triggers pulse when Vsense > 0.15 V
                Oil phase (HFE 7500) > 10 GOhm impedance; prevents short when no droplet
  Efficiency  : 4.58e3 CFU/ng assembled DNA (110 +/- 8 colonies, N=3, microfluidic)
                4.46e3 CFU/ng (137 +/- 16 colonies, N=3, cuvette); not significantly different
  Notes       : Yeast (CEN.PK): 1200 V/cm; 1.90e3 CFU/ng (21 +/- 6 colonies, N=3).
                Higher fields (above optimum) decrease efficiency by killing cells.

Step 17 | Post-Electroporation Recovery
  Inputs      : preMix, LB_Recovery
  Output      : recoveredCells
  Mix         : 5 s
  Recovery    : 200 uL LB broth; 1 h at 37 C (3600 s)
  Notes       : Electroporated droplet driven to outlet by HFE 7500 (all valves closed).
                Plated on LB agar + kanamycin 50 ug/mL. ~100/110 colonies green (GFP+,
                correct insert); ~10 white (self-ligation). Yeast: collected in 200 uL
                deionized water; plated on CSM-agar delta-TRP.

Step 18 | Transformation QC Detection
  Input       : recoveredCells
  Output      : transformSignal
  Module      : transformationQC
  Duration    : 30 s
  Notes       : Abstracts off-chip colony counting (LB agar + kanamycin, 37 C overnight).
                Colony-PCR and Sanger sequencing (QB3284_fwd / QB3810_rev) confirm assembly.
                For yeast: 3 yeast colonies miniprepped; transformed into Turbo E. coli;
                5 green E. coli colonies sequenced per assembled plasmid (Genewiz).

  Branch BB5 (transformSignal > 0)  : dispose(recoveredCells) -- colonies confirmed
  Branch BB6 (transformSignal <= 0) : drain(recoveredCells)   -- no colonies; discard

Step 19 | Final Cleanup [BB7 terminal]
  Operations  : dispose(transformSignal), dispose(oilPhase)
  Notes       : Terminal block. In the actual protocol, Steps 1-19 are iterated 16 times
                in total (one reaction per plasmid combination) using the same electrode
                path, mixing channel, and incubation channel. Reservoir droplets refilled
                via SU-8 capillary channel between iterations.


SEQUENCING RESULTS
------------------
Golden Gate (16 plasmids, 5 colonies each = 80 total):
  Correct clones : 76/80 = 95% (Fig 6, main text)
  Method         : Sanger sequencing (QuintaraBio); primers QB3284_fwd / QB3810_rev
  Analysis       : Excluding first/last bases of read and 20 bp flanking assembly junctions

Gibson (16 plasmids, 5 colonies each = 80 total):
  Correct clones : 80/80 = 100% (Fig S5)

Yeast/TAR (16 plasmids, ~3-5 colonies each = 72 sequenced):
  Correct clones : 70/72 = 97.2% (Fig S6)
  Note           : 2 Sanger reactions failed for 4 plasmids (pProm2_BCD1, pProm9_BCD1,
                   pProm11_BCD1, pProm1_BCD2); sequenced from Turbo E. coli colonies
