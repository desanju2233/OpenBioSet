Source link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4128248/
Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2013.046

Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Droplet-Based Microfluidic Single-Cell Antibody-Secretion Binding Assay with Fluorescence-Activated Droplet Sorting (FADS)

REAGENTS USED:
  - Cell Suspension (mixture of antibody-secreting mouse 9E10 hybridoma cells and non-secreting K562 cells, ~1:10 ratio)
  - Bead/Detection-Antibody Reagent (6 um streptavidin-coated magnetic beads coated with biotinylated anti-mouse-Fc capture antibody, combined with a DyLight 488-labeled anti-mouse-F(ab)2 detection antibody)

STEPS:
1. Co-encapsulate single cells from the mixed cell suspension together with the capture-bead/detection-antibody reagent into 50-pl droplets, using a flow-focusing microfluidic junction.
2. Keep droplets cold (~4 C) during collection and handling to slow cellular metabolism and limit premature antibody secretion.
3. Shift the collected emulsion to 37 C, 5% CO2 for ~15 min to allow antibody secretion; secreted antibody is captured on the bead surface via the capture antibody and recruits the fluorescent detection antibody, concentrating fluorescence signal onto the bead in a wash-free sandwich format.
4. Re-inject the droplets into a sorting device and measure green fluorescence intensity of each droplet as it passes a laser detection point.
5. Sort droplets by fluorescence-activated dielectrophoresis: droplets with fluorescence above the sorting threshold (~1.5 relative fluorescence units, RFU) are diverted into the collection channel; droplets below threshold (background ~0.5 RFU) flow by default into the waste channel.

INTERPRETATION: A clearly distinguishable, bead-localized fluorescence spike indicates a droplet containing an antibody-secreting cell coencapsulated with a capture bead; droplets lacking a secreting cell (or lacking a bead) show diffuse, low fluorescence and are not sorted. In the reported experiment, sorting at the 1.5 RFU threshold produced ~220-fold enrichment of antibody-secreting (9E10) cells relative to non-secreting (K562) cells after a single round of sorting, confirming the assay's ability to isolate rare antibody-producing cells without any washing steps.
