Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Digital Microfluidic Bead Densification and Processing Coupled to Single-Molecule Array (DMF-Simoa) for Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha (TNF-alpha) Quantification

REAGENTS USED:
  - TNF-alpha Sample (solution of TNF-alpha antigen, 100 uL aliquot)
  - Simoa Capture Beads (anti-TNF-alpha antibody-coated superparamagnetic beads, 25 uL suspension containing 15000 functionalized beads)
  - Biotinylated Detection Antibody (anti-TNF-alpha detection antibody, 20 uL aliquot)
  - Wash Buffer (PBS containing 0.01% w/v Tetronic 90R4 surfactant)
  - Streptavidin-beta-Galactosidase (SbG) Reagent (enzyme label conjugate)
  - RGP Substrate (resorufin-beta-D-galactopyranoside fluorogenic substrate)

STEPS:
1. Off-chip pre-incubation: Mix the 100 uL TNF-alpha sample, 25 uL bead suspension (15000 beads), and 20 uL detection antibody together; incubate for 20 min off-chip on a shaker at 800 rpm to form antibody-antigen-bead sandwich immunocomplexes.
2. Stepwise sample loading onto DMF: Dispense the entire pre-incubated sample-bead mixture onto the sample reservoir electrode; sequentially load, split, and remove waste from ~9 uL segments over ~12 closed-loop, capacitance-monitored cycles to fully transfer the sample into the device while retaining beads on the densifying electrode (>98% bead retention).
3. Bead washing: Wash the densified beads with fresh wash buffer over multiple wash cycles (5 repeats) to remove unbound sample components.
4. SbG incubation: Deliver the streptavidin-beta-galactosidase reagent to the densified beads and incubate for 5 min at 25C to label captured immunocomplexes with enzyme.
5. Post-SbG washing: Wash the labeled beads again with fresh wash buffer over multiple wash cycles (5 repeats) to remove excess unbound SbG.
6. Substrate addition and detection: Deliver the RGP (resorufin-beta-D-galactopyranoside) substrate to the washed, labeled beads and incubate for 30 min at 25C to allow enzymatic conversion to fluorescent resorufin; the processed bead suspension is then collected and transferred to a microtiter plate/SR-X imager, where beads are loaded into femtoliter-sized microwells and imaged to determine the average number of enzyme labels per bead (digital single-molecule readout), then the analyzed droplet is discarded.

INTERPRETATION: Successful DMF-Simoa TNF-alpha quantification is confirmed by calibration curves of average enzyme labels per bead vs. TNF-alpha concentration obtained via the DMF-processed workflow that are comparable to those obtained via conventional manual microtiter-plate Simoa processing, with more than 98% bead retention during stepwise sample loading and no significant loss of assay sensitivity, supporting the feasibility of a miniaturized, automated DMF-Simoa platform for ultrasensitive biomarker detection.
