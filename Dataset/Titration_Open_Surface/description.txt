Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: Open-Surface Digital Microfluidic Potentiometric/Conductometric Titration

REAGENTS USED:
  - Analyte solution (sample droplet, created from an on-chip analyte reservoir)
  - Titrant solution (diluent droplet, created from an on-chip titrant reservoir)

STEPS:
1-2. Droplet creation: Create one droplet from the analyte reservoir and one droplet from the titrant reservoir.
3-4. Splitting: Split each of the two droplets into two smaller droplets.
5-6. First merge: Merge one analyte half with one titrant half to form a 50:50 mixture; return the unused titrant half to the reservoir.
7. Mixing: Mix the merged 50:50 droplet by rotating it clockwise then counterclockwise over four grid electrodes.
8. First split-for-detection: Split the mixed 50:50 droplet into two. Send one half to the detection site to record its pH or conductivity reading.
9. Second dilution: Merge the retained half with a fresh analyte droplet to form a 75:25 (analyte:titrant) mixture; mix by rotation and split again.
10. Second split-for-detection: Send one half to the detection site to record its pH or conductivity reading; create and split another fresh analyte droplet.
11. Third dilution: Merge the retained half with the fresh analyte droplet to form an 88:12 (analyte:titrant) mixture.
12. Final detection: Send the resulting droplet to the detection site to record its pH or conductivity reading.

INTERPRETATION: The protocol demonstrates that serial dilution/titration -- normally requiring continuous titrant addition on a benchtop potentiometer or conductometer -- can instead be reprogrammed as a sequence of droplet splitting, merging, and rotational mixing steps on an open-surface digital microfluidic array. Each detection-site reading (pH or conductivity) at the 50:50, 75:25, and 88:12 dilution stages allows the assay to be halted once the target endpoint is reached, illustrating the reprogrammability advantage of open-surface droplet manipulation over fixed-channel or continuous-flow microfluidics.
