Protocol details:

ASSAY TYPE: DNA ENTRAP (DNA ENhanced TRAnsfer Platform) - Droplet Microfluidic XPORT-Mediated Conjugation Between Bacillus subtilis Donor and Recipient Strains

REAGENTS USED:
  - B. subtilis Donor Strain JAB981 (XPORT donor derived from B. subtilis JH642, tetracycline-resistant, xylose-inducible conjugative machinery)
  - B. subtilis Recipient Strain JAB545 (derivative of B. subtilis PY79, chloramphenicol-resistant, comK-interrupted so unable to take up DNA by chemical/natural competence)
  - LB Media supplemented with 100 ug/mL D-alanine and 10 ug/mL tetracycline (donor culture medium)
  - LB Media, no supplementation (recipient culture medium)
  - Xylose Inducer (50% xylose solution, induces conjugative element production in the donor)
  - Phosphate Buffered Saline (PBS) wash buffer
  - Resuspension Media (fresh LB supplemented with 100 ug/mL D-alanine, used to normalize OD600 and set donor:recipient ratios)
  - Carrier Oil/Surfactant Phase (Novec 7500 fluorinated oil with 1.25 wt/wt% Pico-Surf 1 surfactant)
  - Selection Agars: LB agar (recipients + transconjugants), LB + 10 ug/mL tetracycline agar (transconjugants only), LB + 100 ug/mL D-alanine + 10 ug/mL tetracycline agar (donors + transconjugants)
  - IPTG Induction Reagent (1 mM, induces the miniICE-encoded GFPmut2 reporter in transconjugants)

STEPS:
1. Pick single colonies of donor (JAB981) and recipient (JAB545) strains and culture in selective LB media (donor: +D-alanine+tetracycline; recipient: no supplement) until OD600 > 1.2.
2. Sub-culture both strains into fresh media at a starting OD600 = 0.025 and incubate at 37 C, 225 rpm.
3. Grow the donor sub-culture for 1.5 h until OD600 = 0.15, then add 50% xylose (60 uL) to induce conjugative element production.
4. Continue culturing both donor and recipient for another 1.5 h until OD600 = 0.9-1.1.
5. Pellet 2.5 mL of each culture by centrifugation, wash twice with PBS (repeating the pellet/wash cycle), and resuspend in 500 uL sterile PBS.
6. Resuspend both strains in fresh LB media supplemented with 100 ug/mL D-alanine to a final OD600 = 0.6, at the desired donor:recipient ratio (3:1 or 1:1).
7. Inject the donor and recipient cell suspensions into the DNA ENTRAP microfluidic chip via two distinct cell-port inlets at equal flow rates, together with the fluorinated oil/surfactant carrier phase, to generate cell-encapsulated droplets of 60 um diameter (approximately 20-30 cells per droplet).
8. Flow the droplets into the downstream "first-in-first-out" micro-vessel incubation system and co-incubate for the designated period (0.5 h, 1 h, or 2 h) to allow XPORT-mediated conjugation to occur.
9. Remove ~50 uL of total droplet/carrier-oil volume from the incubator at each timepoint and spread 16 uL onto each of three selection agar plates: LB agar (recipients + transconjugants), LB+tetracycline agar (transconjugants only), and LB+D-alanine+tetracycline agar (donors + transconjugants); incubate plates at 37 C for 16 h.
10. Calculate conjugation efficiency (TC/D) by subtracting colony-forming-unit counts between the selective plates to isolate donor, recipient, and transconjugant populations.
11. Confirm successful conjugation by colony PCR amplification and whole-genome sequencing of the miniICE insertion site in transconjugant colonies (primers flanking the trnS-leu2 integration locus).
12. Pick transconjugant colonies, induce with 1 mM IPTG for 1 h, and quantify GFPmut2 reporter expression by flow cytometry to confirm functional miniICE cassette integration, compared against benchtop-generated transconjugants.

INTERPRETATION: Successful in-droplet XPORT-mediated conjugation is confirmed by colony growth specifically on the tetracycline-selective plates (transconjugants), a conjugation efficiency (TC/D ratio) that is 2-fold to over 20-fold higher for droplet-based conjugation compared to the benchtop approach (peak efficiency at the 1 h co-incubation timepoint for both 3:1 and 1:1 donor:recipient ratios), correct miniICE insertion at the trnS-leu2 locus confirmed by sequencing, and IPTG-induced GFPmut2 fluorescence intensities in droplet transconjugants that are statistically indistinguishable from benchtop transconjugants, along with donor and recipient cell viabilities remaining above 98% throughout the 12 h in-droplet incubation window.
