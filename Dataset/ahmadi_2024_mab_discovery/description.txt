PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : An Automated Single-Cell Droplet-Digital Microfluidic Platform for Monoclonal Antibody Discovery
Source Link  : https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1002/smll.202308950
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.202308950
Journal      : Small, 2024, Vol. 20, Article 2308950
Authors      : Fatemeh Ahmadi, Hao Tran, Natasha Letourneau, Samuel R. Little,
               Annie Fortin, Anna N. Moraitis, Steve C. C. Shih
Platform     : Droplet-Digital (D2) microfluidics (digital microfluidics + droplet-in-channel
               microfluidics) on EWOD/electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a Droplet-Digital (D2) microfluidic platform for automated monoclonal
antibody (mAb) discovery. The platform integrates a bottom digital microfluidic (DMF) electrode
layer (chromium co-planar electrodes on glass + 7 um SU-8 dielectric) with three stacked
PDMS-based microchannel devices, enabling individual electrode-addressable control of droplets
flowing inside microfluidic channels -- a configuration combining "droplet-in-flow" techniques
with digital microfluidics for on-demand droplet generation and manipulation.

The protocol is divided into five functional phases:
  1. Target cell encapsulation and single-cell sorting (Devices 2 and 3)
  2. Hybridoma cell staining and hydrodynamic single-cell trapping (Device 1)
  3. In situ deterministic co-encapsulation by phase change and electrode-actuated merge
  4. On-chip incubation, droplet disruption, and PBS/Alexa Fluor 647 wash (Device 1)
  5. Fluorescence-based antibody binding detection and positive hybridoma recovery

The complete workflow from trapping to recovery processes 100 doublets in ~60 min, achieving
co-encapsulation efficiency of 95 +/- 3.2% -- the highest reported without preliminary sorting
of both cell types or microvalve-based capture systems. This surpasses the Poisson-based
nondeterministic co-encapsulation efficiency of ~16%, which is the limit of conventional
flow-focusing droplet microfluidic approaches.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i   : Chromium electrodes patterned on glass; co-planar ground and actuation electrodes.
Layer ii  : SU-8 dielectric, 7 um thick; spin-coated and hard-baked.
Layer iii : Three stacked PDMS-based microchannel devices adhered to the dielectric layer.

Device 1 -- Trapping array:
  100 traps total (50 per serpentine side); channel height 20 um, channel width 50-75 um;
  7 um constriction per trap; 2 actuation electrodes + 1 ground electrode per trap;
  sets of 5 traps bussed together to one potential circuit to reduce electrode density
  and operational complexity.

Device 2 -- Flow-focusing droplet generator:
  Generates ~30 pL monodisperse droplets at ~3 kHz throughput;
  used for target cell encapsulation following Poisson distribution.

Device 3 -- Co-planar binary electrode sorter:
  Selects single-target-cell droplets by CFSE fluorescence (ex 470 nm / em 520 nm);
  PMT photon-counting head triggers electrode actuation to sort droplets above single-cell
  fluorescence threshold and below two-cell threshold.

Inlets  : Inlet 1 (HFE7500 oil, main channel), Inlet 2 (PBS / Alexa647 Ab / target droplets),
          Inlet 3 (hybridoma cell suspension in IMDM)
Outlets : Outlet 1 (sample recovery via reverse oil flow, 4 nL/s)

Actuation: 15 kHz AC; 32 Vrms for encapsulation/merge; 64 Vrms for droplet disruption/wash.
           Electrode actuation controlled through in-house Python-based GUI automation software
           (open-source: https://bitbucket.org/shihmicrolab/f_ahmadi_2023_uflowcontrol).


CELL LINES
----------
- Hybridoma cells (confidential line, provided by NRC Canada):
  Produces monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) that bind to specific membrane-bound antigens on target
  cells. Cultured in IMDM + 10% heat-inactivated FBS + mouse IL-6 (1 ng/mL) + pen/strep;
  37 C, 5% CO2. Passaged every 3 days at 8e6 cells/mL. Loaded at 1e6 cells/mL in IMDM
  (10% FBS + IL-6, no antibiotic). Stained with CellTrace Calcein Red-Orange AM
  (ex 577 nm / em 590 nm) prior to chip loading.

- Target cells (confidential line, provided by NRC Canada):
  Express membrane-bound antigens recognised by the desired antigen-specific mAbs.
  Cultured in DMEM + 10% heat-inactivated FBS + G418 (0.4 mg/mL); 37 C, 5% CO2.
  Passaged every 3 days at 3e6 cells/mL. Loaded at 1e6 cells/mL in IMDM
  (10% FBS + IL-6, no antibiotic). Stained with CellTrace CFSE (ex 470 nm / em 520 nm)
  prior to chip loading for Device 3 sorting.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming
  Reagents  : PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free), Pluronics F-127
  Mix       : 2% Pluronics F-127 in PBS
  Flow rate : 50 nL/s from inlet 3 for 5 min (outlet 1 closed); then reduced to 1 nL/s
              and outlet 1 opened
  Duration  : 5 min (300 s)
  Purpose   : Reduces non-specific hybridoma cell adhesion to PDMS channel walls.

Step 2 | Target Cell Staining and Encapsulation (Device 2)
  Cell line      : Target cells
  Stain          : CellTrace CFSE (5 uM, 1e6 cells/mL in PBS, Ca2+/Mg2+ free)
  Stain time     : 20 min at 37 C; wash with PBS to remove excess dye
  Resuspension   : IMDM (10% FBS + mouse IL-6 1 ng/mL, no antibiotic, 1e6 cells/mL)
  Operation      : Flow-focusing encapsulation (Poisson distribution)
  Oil            : HFE7500 + 2% Ran Fluoro-surfactant
  Droplet size   : ~30 pL monodisperse
  Throughput     : ~3 kHz
  Purpose        : Encapsulate target cells in small droplets for downstream sorting (Device 3).

Step 3 | Single Target-Cell Droplet Sorting (Device 3)
  Detection      : CFSE fluorescence (ex 470 nm / em 520 nm) via PMT photon-counting head
  Sorting gate   : Fluorescence counts above single-cell threshold and below two-cell threshold
  Action (pass)  : Single-target-cell droplet collected into gas-tight glass syringe with HFE7500
  Action (fail)  : Empty or multi-cell droplets sent to waste channel
  Transfer       : Collected droplets bridged to Device 1 inlet 2 via 0.014" OD / 0.006" ID tubing
  Purpose        : Ensure only droplets with exactly one target cell are used for co-encapsulation.

Step 4 | Hybridoma Cell Staining and Hydrodynamic Trapping (Device 1)
  Cell line      : Hybridoma cells
  Stain          : CellTrace Calcein Red-Orange AM (1e6 cells/mL, 20 min at 37 C; PBS wash)
  Resuspension   : IMDM (10% FBS + IL-6, no antibiotic, 1e6 cells/mL)
  Flow rate      : 1-5 nL/s; optimal 1 nL/s
  Trapping eff.  : 98 +/- 3.92% at 1 nL/s (N=5 repeats, n=10 replicates per flow rate)
  Trap design    : 7 um constriction (smaller than hybridoma cell diameter); hydrodynamic
                   confinement by fluidic resistance. Open trap Rtrap < Rbypass; when occupied
                   Rtrap increases, directing flow to next open trap.
  Trap count     : 100 traps (50 per serpentine side per device)
  Occupancy check: singleCellTrapDetection module; fluorescence threshold gates single occupancy
  Purpose        : Isolate single hybridoma cells in addressable traps for deterministic
                   co-encapsulation and individual droplet-level screening.

Step 5 | In Situ Droplet Generation (Device 1)
  Method         : Phase change from single-phase aqueous (IMDM) to two-phase (water-in-oil)
  Oil            : HFE7500 + 2% Ran Fluoro-surfactant, 1 nL/s from inlet 1
  Electrode      : 32 Vrms, 15 kHz; electrodes below traps actuated to shear aqueous phase
  Droplet volume : 40-150 pL (varies with oil flow rate)
  Success rate   : >95% at 1 nL/s
  Frame sequence : Oil enters main channel while trap electrode holds aqueous phase.
                   Oil shears aqueous at both trap ends; cell remains inside.
                   Droplet pinches off; oil continues to next trap.
  Purpose        : Deterministic encapsulation of single hybridoma cell per trap.
                   No Poisson statistics. No preliminary sorting of hybridoma cells required.

Step 6 | Deterministic Co-Encapsulation (Droplet Merge, Device 1)
  Operation      : On-demand electrode-actuated droplet merge
  Re-injection   : Sorted single-target-cell droplets re-injected from inlet 2 at 5 nL/s
  Oil spacer     : HFE7500 from a separate inlet at 1 nL/s (spaces droplets entering traps)
  Electrode      : 32 Vrms, 15 kHz; actuated when target-cell droplet reaches trap proximity
  Efficiency     : 95 +/- 3.2% co-encapsulation at 5 nL/s re-injection flow rate
                   (N=5 repeats, n=10 replicates; highest reported without microvalves)
  Time for 100   : 60 min for 100 two-cell doublets with automated system
  Notes          : Two separate inlets avoid merging of sequential target-cell droplets.
                   Electrode actuation pattern detailed in Figure S6 of paper.
                   Failure mode at >10 nL/s: target droplet coalesces with empty trap droplet.

Step 7 | On-Chip Co-Incubation
  Temperature    : 25 C (room temperature; no external heating element)
  Duration       : 30 min (1800 s)
  Events         : Hybridoma cells secrete mAbs into confined droplet volume.
                   Antigen-specific mAbs diffuse and bind to membrane-bound antigens on
                   co-encapsulated target cell surface.
                   Non-specific mAbs remain free in droplet volume.

Step 8 | Droplet Disruption and PBS / Alexa Fluor 647 Wash (Device 1)
  Wash solution  : PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free) + Alexa Fluor 647-conjugated goat anti-mouse
                   IgG (H+L) secondary antibody; loaded from inlet 2 at 1-5 nL/s
  Electrode      : 64 Vrms, 15 kHz; field intensity 2-4 x 10^5 V/m; electrodes below traps
  Duration       : ~5 min to wash all 50 traps
  Mechanism      : Electrostatic force perpendicular to droplet interface (Figure S7 of paper)
                   disrupts IMDM droplet; non-specific mAbs washed out through 7 um constriction.
                   Hybridoma + target cell retained in trap (antigen-Ab bond maintains target cell;
                   hybridoma remains by hydrodynamic confinement).
  Wash efficiency: 96.8% reduction in normalised fluorescence intensity after 1 wash step
                   (N=15 replicates; Figure 4C of paper)
  Cell retention : 96 +/- 9.6% at 1-5 nL/s (N=5 repeats, n=10 replicates; Figure 4D)
  Re-formation   : PBS/Alexa647 flow reduced to 0.1 nL/s; HFE7500 oil flow increased to
                   5 nL/s to re-form PBS droplet around both cells in situ.
  Advantage      : Individual droplet disruption by electrostatic forces avoids chemical
                   demulsifiers (which compromise cell viability) and acoustic/magnetic methods
                   (which have device material limitations).

Step 9 | Fluorescence Detection and Antibody Binding Screening
  Channel        : Alexa Fluor 647 (ex 627 nm / em 665 nm); 300 ms exposure
  Camera         : Hamamatsu ORCA-Flash 4.0 (Township, NJ, USA)
  Detection      : antibodyBindingDetection module gates on mean fluorescence of cell >
                   mean fluorescence of surrounding droplet (SNR threshold)
  Result (pass)  : Localised red fluorescent signal around target cell membrane =
                   antigen-specific mAb detected; hybridoma is a positive producer
  Result (fail)  : No localised signal = non-specific mAb producer; droplet drained
  SNR            : Significant improvement post-wash for antigen-specific vs non-specific
                   hybridomas (P < 0.05); without washing both types have similar SNR (P > 0.05)
  Selection rate : 94.00 +/- 1.41% at 100:0 ratio (100% desired); 1.60% at 1:50 ratio
                   (platform detects rare-cell populations at biologically relevant concentrations
                   of ~2% antigen-specific B cells from immunised donors)
  Purpose        : Distinguish antigen-specific from non-specific mAb-producing hybridomas
                   at the single-cell level without requiring pre-sorting or bulk assays.

Step 10 | Positive Hybridoma Recovery
  Condition      : antibodySignal > 0 only (antigen-specific mAb detected)
  Recovery steps :
    1. Positive droplet released by reverse oil flow at 4 nL/s from outlet 1
       (inlet 3 briefly opened to remove previously accumulated droplets in that channel)
    2. Droplet collected into glass capillary pre-filled with HFE7500 2% Ran Fluoro-surfactant
    3. Capillary contents expelled onto 10-20 uL IMDM complete medium placed on hydrophobic
       PTFE membrane (0.22 um, Thomas Scientific) secured in custom 3D-printed holder
    4. ~1 mL Fluorinert FC-40 oil added; FC-40 disrupts surfactant-stabilised emulsion
       and PTFE membrane absorbs HFE7500 and surfactant
    5. Aqueous cell droplet released into IMDM complete medium
    6. Merged droplet transferred to well-plate (e.g. 96-well) using pipette for downstream
       antibody characterisation (sequencing, ELISA, binding kinetics, SPR, etc.)
  Downstream     : Antibody characterisation assays (off-chip; not encoded in BioScript).
