PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Quantification of Proteins and mRNA in Single Mammalian Cells
Source Link  : https://www.cell.com/molecular-cell/pdfExtended/S1097-2765(16)00174-X
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2016.02.030
Journal      : Molecular Cell, 2016, Vol. 61, pp. 914-924
Platform     : Digital PLA (proximity ligation assay + droplet digital PCR) combined with
               two-step RT-ddPCR; bench-top Bio-Rad QX100 microfluidic droplet system


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a practical, commercially available workflow for the absolute and
simultaneous quantification of both protein and mRNA copy numbers from the same single
mammalian cell. The method combines proximity ligation assay (PLA) with droplet digital PCR
(ddPCR) to achieve femtomolar sensitivity for protein detection (digital PLA), and integrates
a two-step reverse transcription ddPCR (RT-ddPCR) for absolute mRNA quantification from the
same single-cell lysate.

The protocol is divided into two parallel arms operating on split lysate from one cell:
  1. Digital PLA arm (protein): proximity probes bind target protein -> connector
     oligonucleotide hybridises -> ligation forms dsDNA -> protease digestion ->
     ddPCR in 20,000 droplets -> absolute protein copy number via calibration curve.
  2. RT-ddPCR arm (mRNA): two-step GoScript RT (42 C, 60 min) -> ddPCR (40 cycles,
     60 C annealing, TaqMan Probe 2) -> mRNA copy number corrected for 87.6% RT recovery.

The joint dataset was used to construct and validate a stochastic two-state model of CD147
transcription and translation in single HEK293T cells. Key findings: CD147 mRNA and protein
are poorly correlated at the single-cell level (Spearman R = 0.10); approximately 1,000
proteins are produced per mRNA molecule; transcription occurs in bursts of ~40 mRNAs at
a rate of 3.78 bursts/day.

The entire assay uses commercially available reagents and equipment; no genetic manipulation
of host cells or custom microfluidic device fabrication is required. A single researcher can
process 100 single-cell samples in one day.


ASSAY SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------
Digital PLA:
  Target proteins   : CD147 (endogenous, HEK293T), GFP-p65 (exogenous, RAW264.7), ICAM-1
  LOD (CD147)       : 0.14 fM assay / 16.2 fM sample / 86 proteins per uL ddPCR solution
  LOD (single cell) : 29,300 CD147 molecules per cell (from 2 uL lysate fraction)
  Sensitivity       : 70 proteins per DNA count (CD147); 95 (GFP); 565 (ICAM-1)
  Dynamic range     : 2-3 orders of magnitude (linear)
  Calibration curve : CD147: y = 0.0142x + 12.4, R2 = 0.9997
                      GFP  : y = 0.0094x + 10.6, R2 = 0.9982
                      ICAM-1: y = 0.00177x + 1.30, R2 = 0.9993
  Droplets          : ~20,000 per reaction at ~0.85 nL each (limiting dilution; 0 or 1 dsDNA)
  Concentration eq. : C = (1000/Vd) * ln(1 - P/R)

RT-ddPCR:
  Target            : CD147 mRNA (NM_198589.2)
  Primer/probe      : TaqMan Gene Expression Assay Hs00174305_m1 (Probe 2; FAM/MGB)
                      Exon-exon junction spanning; excludes genomic DNA signal
  PCR cycles        : 40; fluorescence threshold 3200 RFU
  RT recovery rate  : 87.6% (validated against T7 in-vitro transcribed CD147 mRNA standard)
  LOD               : 52.5 copies/cell (87 aM); corrected for RT recovery
  Expected output   : Mean 188.2 +/- 90 CD147 mRNA copies per HEK293T cell


CELL LINES
----------
- HEK293T (human embryonic kidney; Tlr4-GFP stable line, gift from Prof. Kensuke Miyake,
  University of Tokyo): primary cell line for joint CD147 protein and mRNA measurements.
  Media: high-glucose DMEM (Gibco 41965-039) + 10% FCS (Gibco 26010-066) + 100 U/mL
  penicillin G + 100 ug/mL streptomycin (Gibco 15070063).
  Conditions: 37 C, 5% CO2; passage every 3-4 days to 1e5 cells/mL; max 30 passages.

- RAW 264.7 (murine macrophage; GFP-p65 h2b-mCherry dual-fluorescent stable line):
  used for GFP-p65 single-cell protein quantification only.
  Media: DMEM (Sigma D6429) + 8.8% FBS (Sigma F9665) + 0.88X GlutaMAX + 17.7 mM HEPES.
  Conditions: 37 C, 5% CO2; passage every 2-3 days to 1e5 cells/mL; max 15 passages.


REAGENTS
--------
Lysis buffer     : 0.1X TM lysis buffer (10-fold diluted TM Buffer, Biochain) +
                   protease inhibitor cocktail (Biochain) + CRB (Life Technologies) +
                   RNaseOUT (Invitrogen). Compatible with both digital PLA and RT-ddPCR.
                   Alternative for RAW264.7: 1X standard lysis buffer (2X Cell Lysis Reagent,
                   Life Technologies + Protease Inhibitor Cocktail I + Phosphatase Inhibitor
                   Cocktail II + CRB).

Proximity probes : Anti-human CD147 biotinylated polyclonal goat antibody (R&D Systems,
                   BAF972; oligonucleotide-conjugated; 400 pM each stock, 200 pM final).
                   Anti-GFP biotinylated polyclonal goat antibody (Abcam, AB6658).
                   Anti-murine ICAM-1 biotinylated polyclonal goat antibody (R&D Systems,
                   BAF796).

Connector oligo  : Custom DNA oligonucleotide (Microsynth, Switzerland); hybridises to
                   both proximity probe oligonucleotides to template ligation.

Ligation         : T4 DNA ligase + ligation buffer (Life Technologies TaqMan Protein Assay);
                   25-fold dilution of probe-bound sample; 10 min at 37 C.

Protease         : Diluted protease solution (Life Technologies TaqMan Protein Assay);
                   10 min at 37 C; inactivated 15 min at 95 C.

ddPCR reagents   : 2X ddPCR Supermix for Probes (Bio-Rad); 20X Universal PCR Assay
                   (Life Technologies); DG8 cartridges; QX100 Droplet Generator;
                   QX100 Droplet Reader; QuantaSoft v1.3.2.0.

RT reagents      : GoScript Reverse Transcription System (Promega); random primers;
                   dNTPs 0.5 mM each; MgCl2 3.1 mM; RNaseOUT 1 U/uL (Invitrogen).

Protein standards: Human CD147 (R&D Systems, 972-EMN); pure GFP (Abcam, AB84191);
                   murine ICAM-1 (R&D Systems, 796-IC).

Containers       : Low-adsorption 8-well PCR strips (Thermo Fisher, AB-0266);
                   1.5 mL low-adsorption microfuge tubes (Ambion, AM12450).
                   Used for ALL steps to minimise protein loss.


FACS SORTING
------------
Instrument       : MoFlo XDP (Beckman-Coulter); 70 um nozzle; 60 psi sheath fluid
Lasers/filters   : 488 nm blue + 561 nm yellow-green; 529/28, 613/20, 785/60 nm bandpass
Viability dye    : DRAQ7 (BioStatus, DR71000) at 600 nM; stains dead cell nuclei (high signal)
HEK293T gates    : 4 nested gates (SSC-H/FSC-H, SSC-H/SSC-W, FSC-H/FSC-W, DRAQ7/GFP);
                   R4 gate selects single viable Tlr4-GFP-positive cells
RAW264.7 gates   : 3 nested gates (FSC/SSC, DRAQ7/FSC-W, GFP/mCherry);
                   R3 gate selects dual-fluorescent GFP-p65 mCherry-positive macrophages
Sort volumes     : 2 uL (RAW264.7 protein only); 2 uL (HEK293T protein only);
                   3 uL (HEK293T joint protein + mRNA)
Post-sort        : Plates centrifuged briefly; sealed with clear adhesive sheet + Parafilm;
                   stored at -80 C until processing. Software: Summit (Beckman Coulter).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Single-Cell FACS Sort and Lysis
  Cell line      : HEK293T (Tlr4-GFP stable)
  Buffer         : 0.1X TM lysis buffer (3 uL per well)
  Operation      : Single viable cell sorted by FACS (R4 gate) directly into well containing
                   3 uL 0.1X TM lysis buffer; cell contacts buffer and lyses immediately.
  Duration       : 5 min mixing (BioScript abstraction: mix for 5m; IR: 300.0 SECOND)
  Notes          : 0.1X TM buffer confirmed compatible with both PLA and RT-ddPCR (Fig S4G-H).
                   Cell strainer (40 um) used before loading to remove clumps. DRAQ7 excludes
                   dead cells. Tlr4-GFP expression used as sort marker.

Step 2 | Lysate Split: Protein Fraction (2 uL) and mRNA Fraction (1 uL)
  Total lysate   : 3 uL
  Protein arm    : 2 uL (2/3 of lysate) -> digital PLA workflow
  mRNA arm       : 1 uL (1/3 of lysate) -> RT-ddPCR workflow
  Notes          : Physical 1:2 ratio split; no BioScript native split operator; modelled as
                   two independent dispense+mix variables (proteinLysate, mRNALysate).
                   LOD for CD147 protein in the 2 uL fraction: 29,300 molecules/cell.

Step 3 | Proximity Probe Adsorption  [DIGITAL PLA ARM]
  Inputs         : 2 uL protein lysate + 2 uL assay probe solution (200 pM each probe final)
  Temperature    : 37 C
  Duration       : 60 min
                   (BioScript: heat at 37c for 60m; IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  Mechanism      : Both anti-CD147 antibody-oligonucleotide probes bind distinct epitopes on
                   the same CD147 molecule; tethered oligonucleotides come into ~40 nm
                   proximity enabling connector hybridisation in the next step.
  Notes          : Probe specificity confirmed: human CD147 probes show no signal with mouse
                   3T3 bulk lysate (Fig S3A). GFP-negative cell lysate background comparable
                   to PBS buffer (Fig S3C).

Step 4 | Connector Oligonucleotide Hybridisation and Ligation  [DIGITAL PLA ARM]
  Inputs         : Probe-bound sample + 96 uL ligation solution (25-fold dilution)
  Temperature    : 37 C
  Duration       : 10 min
                   (BioScript: heat at 37c for 10m; IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  Mechanism      : Connector oligonucleotide hybridises to both paired probe oligonucleotides;
                   T4 DNA ligase seals nicks; three-oligonucleotide complex becomes a dsDNA
                   molecule encoding one protein detection event.

Step 5 | Protease Digestion and Inactivation  [DIGITAL PLA ARM]
  Inputs         : Ligated sample + 2 uL diluted protease solution
  Digestion      : 37 C for 10 min (IR: 600.0 SECOND); removes antibody-oligo conjugates,
                   leaving only dsDNA amplicons in solution.
  Inactivation   : 95 C for 15 min (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
  Notes          : BioScript: two sequential heat steps (heat at 37c for 10m;
                   heat at 95c for 15m).

Step 6 | ddPCR Droplet Generation and Amplification  [DIGITAL PLA ARM]
  Inputs         : 9 uL PLA solution + 10 uL 2X ddPCR Supermix + 1 uL 20X Universal PCR Assay
  Total volume   : 20 uL; emulsified into ~20,000 droplets (~0.85 nL each)
  Device         : Bio-Rad QX100 Droplet Generator (DG8 cartridges); Veriti thermal cycler
  PCR schedule   :
    Hot-start        : 95 C for 10 min  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
    40 cycles        : 94 C 30 s / 60 C 1 min (total ~35 min; IR: 2100.0 SECOND abstracted)
    Final extension  : 98 C for 10 min  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
    Ramp speed       : 2.5 C/s
  Readout        : QX100 Droplet Reader; QuantaSoft v1.3.2.0 Absolute Quantification setting;
                   threshold set from most concentrated standard (applied to all wells).
  Concentration  : C = (1000/Vd) * ln(1 - P/R); where Vd = droplet volume (nL),
                   P = positive droplets, R = total droplets.
  Protein count  : Converted via daily calibration curve (accounts for day-to-day variation;
                   CV of DNA concentration used as error for each single-cell measurement).
  Notes          : QuantaSoft v1.3.2.0 uses 0.91 nL droplet volume (incorrect; true = 0.85 nL);
                   calibration curve construction corrects for this systematic 10% error.
                   BioScript: three sequential heat steps (95c/10m, 94c/35m, 98c/10m).

Step 7 | Protein ddPCR Detection and QC Gate  [DIGITAL PLA ARM]
  Module         : ddpcrDetection (abstracts QX100 readout + calibration curve conversion)
  Detection      : 30 s
  Pass gate      : proteinSignal > 0 -> CD147 protein detected above LOD -> dispose (BB2)
  Fail gate      : proteinSignal <= 0 -> below LOD -> drain (BB3)
  Join           : BB4 (both branches converge; mRNA arm begins)
  Results        : 138 of 186 sorted HEK293T cells had CD147 protein above LOD.
                   Range: 35,900 +/- 2,900 to 636,000 +/- 36,000 proteins/cell (18-fold).
                   Population mean: ~218,000 CD147 molecules/cell.
                   Poisson CV: 6-8% (fundamental ddPCR error).

Step 8 | RT Primer Annealing  [RT-ddPCR ARM]
  Inputs         : 1 uL mRNA lysate + 1 uL random primers + 2.95 uL nuclease-free water
  Denaturation   : 70 C for 5 min (IR: 300.0 SECOND); denatures RNA secondary structure
  Chilling       : 4 C for 5 min (IR: 300.0 SECOND); prevents re-folding before RT
  Notes          : BioScript: heat at 70c for 5m then heat at 4c for 5m.

Step 9 | Reverse Transcription (cDNA Synthesis)  [RT-ddPCR ARM]
  System         : GoScript Reverse Transcription System (Promega)
  Additives      : RNaseOUT 1 U/uL; dNTPs 0.5 mM each; MgCl2 3.1 mM; 1X GoScript buffer
  Total volume   : 9 uL
  RT schedule    :
    Primer annealing : 25 C for 5 min  (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
    cDNA synthesis   : 42 C for 60 min (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
    RT inactivation  : 70 C for 15 min (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
  Recovery rate  : 87.6% (validated by T7 in-vitro transcribed CD147 mRNA standard;
                   linear fit y = 0.876x, R2 = 0.988; Fig S5C). All single-cell reads
                   divided by 0.876 to obtain true copy numbers.
  Notes          : BioScript: three sequential heat steps (25c/5m, 42c/60m, 70c/15m).

Step 10 | ddPCR Amplification of cDNA  [RT-ddPCR ARM]
  Inputs         : RT product + 10 uL 2X ddPCR Supermix + 1 uL 20X TaqMan Probe 2 assay
  Total volume   : 20 uL; emulsified into ~20,000 droplets
  TaqMan assay   : Hs00174305_m1 (Probe 2); FAM/MGB; exon-exon junction spanning;
                   excludes amplification of genomic CD147 DNA.
  PCR schedule   :
    Hot-start        : 95 C for 10 min  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
    40 cycles        : 94 C 30 s / 60 C 1 min (~35 min; IR: 2100.0 SECOND abstracted)
    Final extension  : 98 C for 10 min  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
    Ramp speed       : 2.5 C/s
  Threshold      : 3200 RFU (separates CD147 amplicon-positive from negative droplets;
                   confirmed in Fig S4C). QuantaSoft Absolute Quantification setting.
  Notes          : BioScript: three sequential heat steps (95c/10m, 94c/35m, 98c/10m).

Step 11 | mRNA RT-ddPCR Detection and QC Gate  [RT-ddPCR ARM]
  Module         : rtddpcrDetection (abstracts QX100 readout + 87.6% RT recovery correction)
  Detection      : 30 s
  Pass gate      : mRNASignal > 0 -> CD147 mRNA detected above LOD -> dispose (BB5)
  Fail gate      : mRNASignal <= 0 -> below LOD -> drain (BB6)
  Terminal       : BB7 (both branches converge; dispose mRNASignal; NOP; experiment ends)
  Results        : Single-cell range: 57 +/- 18 to 2,651 +/- 121 CD147 mRNA copies/cell.
                   Population mean: 188.2 copies/cell; SD: 90 copies/cell.
                   100-cell averages: 106 +/- 2 to 215 +/- 3 copies/cell (narrower range).
                   ~1,000 proteins translated per CD147 mRNA molecule.
                   Spearman R = 0.10 with protein (poor correlation; n=131 cells).
                   mRNA distribution fits gamma pdf (p=0.65; two-state stochastic process).
                   Transcription burst size: 40 +/- 4 mRNAs/burst; rate: 3.78 +/- 0.38/day.


DOWNSTREAM ANALYSIS
-------------------

mRNA-Protein Correlation
  Method         : Spearman rank correlation
  Result         : R = 0.10 (n=131 HEK293T cells; Fig 4B)
  Interpretation : Poor single-cell correlation; bulk analyses (Pearson R = 0.41-0.59) overestimate
                   correlation due to noise averaging. mRNA abundance is a poor surrogate for
                   protein copy number in single mammalian cells.

Distribution Fitting
  Software       : R v3.1.0; fitdist / fitdistcens (fitdistrplus package; Delignette-Muller 2015)
  mRNA           : Gamma pdf fit; p=0.65 (chi-squared); bins of 40 copies/cell.
                   Inconsistent with Poisson (one-step birth-death process).
  Protein        : Gamma pdf fit; p=0.24; bins of 65,000 copies/cell.
  Conclusion     : Both distributions consistent with two-state stochastic gene expression.

Stochastic Two-State Model
  Model          : Promoter stochastically switches between Off and On states.
                   mRNA transcribed only from On state; translated into protein.
                   Six kinetic parameters (c1-c6) + extrinsic noise parameter.
  Inference      : Moment-based MCMC (Zechner et al. 2012); fitted to E[M], E[P],
                   E[M^2], E[P^2] at steady state. Five independent MCMC runs per dataset.
  Validation     : E[M(t)P(t)] (mRNA-protein covariance) computed from model parameters
                   and compared against experimentally measured correlation (not used in fit).
                   4 of 5 experimental repeats: predicted and measured E[M(t)P(t)] agree.
  mRNA half-life : 20.97 hr (from Schwanhausser et al. 2011; mouse fibroblasts)
  Protein half-life: 33.48 hr (from Schwanhausser et al. 2011)
  Parameters     : See Table S1 in paper for posterior MCMC estimates.


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : albayrak_digital_pla_rtddpcr.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges
DAG            : albayrak_digital_pla_rtddpcr_main_dag.dot
               : strict digraph with nodes: main, bsbbif_2_t, bsbbif_3_f, bsbbif_4_j,
                 bsbbif_5_t, bsbbif_6_f, bsbbif_7_j
               : edges: 1->2, 1->3, 2->4, 3->4, 4->5, 4->6, 5->7, 6->7

Duration units : All durations in minutes (m) for compiler compatibility
                 (BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported)
                 Time conversions used:
                   5 min   lysis/split mix    ->  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                   60 min  probe adsorption   -> 60m  (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)  [corrected from 25m]
                   10 min  ligation           -> 10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
                   10 min  protease digest    -> 10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
                   15 min  protease inact.    -> 15m  (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
                   10 min  PCR hot-start      -> 10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
                   35 min  40-cycle PCR block -> 35m  (IR: 2100.0 SECOND; abstracted)
                   10 min  final extension    -> 10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
                   5 min   RT denaturation    ->  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                   5 min   RT chilling (4 C)  ->  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                   5 min   primer annealing   ->  5m  (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                   60 min  cDNA synthesis     -> 60m  (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
                   15 min  RT inactivation    -> 15m  (IR: 900.0 SECOND)

Modules        : ddpcrDetection  (QX100 readout + calibration curve -> protein copy number)
                 rtddpcrDetection (QX100 readout + 87.6% RT recovery correction -> mRNA copies)

Lysate split   : BioScript has no native ratio-split operator. The 1 uL mRNA fraction and
                 2 uL protein fraction are modelled as two independent dispense+mix events
                 from the same manifest, each representing the respective volume fraction.

PCR abstraction: 40 amplification cycles (94 C 30 s / 60 C 1 min) collapsed into a single
                 heat step at 94c for 35m. Hot-start (95c/10m) and final extension (98c/10m)
                 are separate heat instructions. Applied identically to both PLA ddPCR (Step 6)
                 and RT-ddPCR (Step 10) arms.

Compiler fix   : Probe adsorption duration corrected from 25m (1500 SECOND) to 60m
                 (3600 SECOND) following biological review against Albayrak et al. Experimental
                 Procedures: "the mixture was incubated at 37 C for 1 hr". Both the mix and
                 heat instructions for probeBindingRxn updated accordingly.

Biological scope: This .bs file encodes the joint CD147 protein + mRNA quantification
                 workflow from single HEK293T cells. GFP-p65 quantification from RAW264.7
                 macrophages (Fig 3C-D) follows the digital PLA arm only (no RT-ddPCR) and
                 requires a separate .bs file with GFP_p65_Proximity_Probe manifests and
                 a 2 uL sort volume into standard 1X lysis buffer.
