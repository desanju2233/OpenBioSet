PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Sperm-seq wet lab protocol: sperm preparation and droplet-based sequencing library generation
Source Link  : https://www.protocols.io/view/sperm-seq-wet-lab-protocol-sperm-preparation-and-d-j8nlk9q81v5r/v1
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.pex-823/v1
Journal      : Research Square (preprint), posted June 3rd, 2020
Authors      : Avery Davis Bell, Curtis J. Mello, Steven A. McCarroll
Affiliations : Department of Genetics, Harvard Medical School;
               Program in Medical and Population Genetics, Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard
Platform     : Droplet-based single-sperm sequencing (10X Genomics GemCode, Option 1)


OVERVIEW
--------
Sperm-seq is a high-throughput, droplet-based single-sperm sequencing technology capable of
generating thousands of cell-barcoded single-sperm sequencing libraries in a single run. This
protocol encodes the complete wet-lab workflow: sperm preparation (decondensation), fluorescence-
based cell quantification, and 10X Genomics GemCode droplet library generation.

The protocol is divided into three phases:
  1. Sperm preparation (~3.5 h): PBS washes, Buffer SMT freeze-thaw cycling, chromatin
     decondensation in Buffer DB (heparin + BME), and heparinase treatment to remove PCR inhibitor
  2. Sperm quantification (~15 min): SYBR Green I fluorescent staining (1:100 dilution),
     hemocytometer counting, and concentration-based QC gate
  3. Library generation (1-3 days): dilution to target cell number, GemCode master mix assembly,
     droplet generation on chip, GEM barcoding incubation, and library QC

Sequencing output is processed using the Bell et al. Nature 2020 pipeline (31,228 human sperm
genomes; "Insights about variation in meiosis from 31,228 human sperm genomes").


REAGENTS
--------

Buffer SMT (freeze-thaw buffer; adapted from Montag et al. 1992; Samocha-Bone et al. 1998):
  250 mM sucrose (from 1 M sucrose, Sigma S7903)
  5 mM MgCl2 (from 1 M MgCl2, Sigma M8266)
  10 mM Tris pH 7.5 (from 1 M Tris pH 7.5, ThermoFisher Scientific 15567027)
  Example 10 mL recipe: 2500 uL sucrose + 50 uL MgCl2 + 100 uL Tris pH 7.5 + 7350 uL ultrapure water

Buffer DB (salt decondensation buffer; adapted from Montag et al. 1992; Samocha-Bone et al. 1998):
  113 mM KCl (from 1 M KCl, Sigma P5405)
  12.5 mM KH2PO4 (from 1 M KH2PO4, Sigma P9791)
  2.5 mM Na2HPO4 (from 1 M Na2HPO4, Sigma S3264)
  2.5 mM MgCl2 (from 1 M MgCl2, Sigma M8266)
  20 mM Tris pH 7.5 (from 1 M Tris pH 7.5, ThermoFisher Scientific 15567027)
  Freshly added per 100 uL (4 samples): 1.5 uL 10 mM heparin + 2 uL 100 mM BME + 96.5 uL Buffer DB base

Other reagents:
  1X PBS pH 7.4 (Life Technologies 10010023)
  10 mM heparin (Heparin Sodium Salt from Porcine, Sigma H3393; diluted in ultrapure water)
  100 mM beta-mercaptoethanol / BME (diluted from 14.3 M, Sigma M6250; used in fume hood)
  0.1 units/uL Heparinase 1 (Sigma H2519; diluted in ultrapure water; stored at -20 C)
  10X SYBR Green I (diluted from 10000X, ThermoFisher Scientific S7563)

10X GemCode reagents (Option 1):
  GemCode Gel-Bead Kit
  GemCode Chip Kit
  GemCode Library Kit
  Master mix per sample (60 uL total): 32.5 uL GemCode reagent mix + 1.5 uL primer release agent
    + 9.2 uL GemCode polymerase + 16.8 uL ultrapure water
  Beads: vortex full speed 25 s; dilute 1:11 in ultrapure water (>=90 uL per sample)
  Chip loading: 60 uL sample / 85 uL diluted beads / 150 uL droplet generation oil


EQUIPMENT
---------
  Nonstick 1.5 mL microfuge tubes (ThermoFisher Scientific AM12450)
  Fuchs-Rosenthal C-chip hemocytometer (VitaScientific NANC07005)
  10X GemCode controller and accessories (per GemCode User Guide Rev C)
  Centrifuge capable of 400g at 4C
  Fluorescence microscope (green channel for SYBR Green I counting)
  Liquid nitrogen (for freeze-thaw cycles)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Thaw and Transfer Sperm Aliquot
  Input    : SpermAliquot (>=250,000 cells; typically 10 uL)
  Procedure: Thaw at room temperature. Quick spin. Transfer to nonstick 1.5 mL tube.
  Notes    : Nonstick tubes minimise cell loss during subsequent centrifugation steps.

Step 2 | First PBS Wash
  Inputs   : spermRaw, PBS
  Procedure: Spin 400g, 4C, 10 min. Remove supernatant. Resuspend pellet in 10 uL PBS.
  BioScript: heat at 4c for 10m abstracts cold centrifugation (in-place, no variable assignment).

Step 3 | Second PBS Wash
  Inputs   : spermPellet1, PBS
  Procedure: Spin 400g, 4C, 10 min. Remove supernatant.
  BioScript: heat at 4c for 10m.

Step 4 | Resuspend in Buffer SMT
  Inputs   : spermPellet2, BufferSMT
  Volume   : 2.5 uL Buffer SMT
  Purpose  : Osmotic protection of sperm membrane during freeze-thaw cycles.

Step 5 | Freeze-Thaw Cycles x3
  Input    : spermInSMT
  Procedure: Submerge tube in liquid nitrogen 5-10 s. Uncap to de-pressurise.
             Hold in fist until thawed. Repeat for a total of 3 cycles.
  Purpose  : Mechanical disruption of sperm membrane to aid chromatin release.
  BioScript: 3 cycles unrolled as 6 sequential heat calls (no loop constructs):
             heat at 4c for 10s (freeze) / heat at 37c for 30s (thaw) x3.

Step 6 | Prepare Buffer DB with Heparin and BME
  Inputs   : BufferDB_Base, Heparin_10mM, BME_100mM
  Recipe   : 1.5 uL 10 mM heparin + 2 uL 100 mM BME + 96.5 uL Buffer DB base per 100 uL (4 samples)
  Notes    : Prepare fresh immediately before use. BME handled in fume hood.
  BioScript: Two sequential mix steps (heparin into DB base, then BME).

Step 7 | Chromatin Decondensation in Buffer DB
  Inputs   : spermInSMT, bufferDBReady
  Volume   : 22.5 uL Buffer DB per sample
  Incubation: 37C for 45 min
  Purpose  : BME reduces protamine disulfide crosslinks; heparin prevents chromatin aggregation.
  BioScript: heat at 37c for 45m (in-place on spermDecondensing).

Step 8 | Heparinase Treatment
  Inputs   : spermDecondensing, Heparinase (0.1 units/uL)
  Volume   : 5 uL freshly-thawed heparinase per sample
  Thaw     : On ice during last half of 37C incubation (Step 7)
  Incubation: Room temperature, 2 h (120 min)
  Purpose  : Removes heparin to eliminate PCR inhibition in downstream library amplification.
  BioScript: heat at 25c for 120m abstracts RT incubation.
  Dataflow : spermDecondensed is the terminal output of the full prep chain; consumed at Step 11.

Step 9 | SYBR Green I Staining for Cell Counting
  Inputs   : SpermCountAliquot_Decondensed (1 uL sub-sample), SYBRGreenI_10X, PBS
  Dilution : 1:100 (1 uL sperm + 10 uL 10X SYBR Green I + 89 uL PBS)
  Incubation: 5-10 min in dark at room temperature (with or without gentle rotation)
  Morphology: Decondensed sperm appear as large bright fluorescent circles under green light;
              non-decondensed sperm appear as compact dots.
  BioScript abstraction note: BioScript has no split operation. The 1 uL counting sub-sample is
    physically pipetted from the post-heparinase decondensed tube before the bulk stock is used
    for library prep. In BioScript this sub-sample is modelled as a single dispense of
    SpermCountAliquot_Decondensed -- a manifest explicitly named to identify its physical source.
    The bulk stock (spermDecondensed) flows unbroken into Step 11 to preserve end-to-end dataflow.

Step 10 | Hemocytometer Cell Count
  Input    : stainedSperm
  Instrument: Fuchs-Rosenthal C-chip hemocytometer (VitaScientific NANC07005)
  Procedure: Load 20 uL stained sperm into C-chip. Count sperm in 5 big squares under
             green fluorescent light. Concentration (cells/uL) = count in 5 squares x 100.
  Target   : 10,833 cells in 5 uL for GemCode loading.
  Detection: spermCountDetection module; 5 min detect duration.
  QC gate:
    Pass (spermCountSignal > 0): sufficient cells confirmed; dispose stain aliquot; proceed to library.
    Fail (spermCountSignal <= 0): insufficient count (failed decondensation or too few cells); drain.

Step 11 | Dilute Sperm to Target Concentration
  Inputs   : spermDecondensed (full prep chain output), UltrapureWater
  Target   : 10,833 cells in 5 uL ultrapure water
  Notes    : This step is the primary consumption of spermDecondensed; the main dataflow is intact.

Step 12 | GemCode Master Mix Assembly
  Inputs   : GemCodeReagentMix, PrimerReleaseAgent, GemCodePolymerase, UltrapureWater
  Volumes  : 32.5 uL reagent mix + 1.5 uL primer release agent + 9.2 uL polymerase + 16.8 uL water
  Total    : 60 uL per sample
  BioScript: Three sequential mix steps in component addition order.

Step 13 | Combine Sperm with Master Mix
  Inputs   : spermDiluted (5 uL), masterMix (60 uL)
  Total    : 65 uL
  Notes    : Pipette gently with wide-bore tips; decondensed chromatin is fragile.

Step 14 | Prepare GemCode Beads
  Inputs   : GemCodeBeads, UltrapureWater
  Procedure: Vortex beads at full speed for 25 s. Dilute 1:11 in ultrapure water (>=90 uL per sample).
  BioScript: Two sequential mix steps chain to accumulate dilution water.

Step 15 | Load GemCode Chip
  Inputs   : spermMasterMix, beadsDiluted, DropletGenerationOil
  Wells    : Sample 60 uL / Beads 85 uL / Oil 150 uL
  Notes    : GemCode controller partitions mixture into GEMs; single sperm barcoded per GEM.

Step 16 | GEM Incubation and Library Construction
  Input    : chipLoaded
  Incubation: 37C, 180 min (3h GEM incubation; BioScript: heat at 37c for 180m)
  Library  : Adapter ligation and indexing per GemCode User Guide Rev C Steps 5.36-6.2.6
  QC       : libraryQCDetection module (Bioanalyzer/qPCR fragment size and yield); 30 s detect.
  Sequencing: Illumina sequencer per 10X Genomics instructions; slightly increased input
              concentration improves yield.
  Analysis : Bell et al. Nature 2020 pipeline.
  QC gate:
    Pass (libraryQCSignal > 0): valid library; dispose chipLoaded.
    Fail (libraryQCSignal <= 0): library prep failed; drain chipLoaded.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
If sequence data shows uneven genome coverage or unclear bead/ambient DNA boundary:
  - Make fresh Buffer SMT and Buffer DB before Step 1
  - Vary BME: try 1 mM final concentration (reduce BME_100mM from 2 uL to 1 uL per 100 uL DB)
  - Decrease freeze-thaw cycles (try 2 instead of 3)
  - Use wide-bore pipette tips and gentle pipetting at all mixing steps


TIMING
------
  Sperm preparation    : ~3.5 hours
  Sperm quantification : ~15 minutes (more for multiple samples)
  Library preparation  : 1-3 days (depending on kit/approach and break points taken)


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : bell_sperm_seq_library_generation.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges
DAG file       : bell_sperm_seq_library_generation_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations in s or m (BioScript supports s and m only; h not supported)
                 45 min decondensation  -> 45m   (IR: 2700.0 SECOND)
                 120 min heparinase RT  -> 120m  (IR: 7200.0 SECOND)
                 7 min SYBR stain       -> 7m    (IR: 420.0 SECOND)
                 5 min detect count     -> 5m    (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                 180 min GEM incubation -> 180m  (IR: 10800.0 SECOND)
Modules        : spermCountDetection, libraryQCDetection
Compiler notes :
  - heat is in-place throughout; never assigned to a variable
  - 3 freeze-thaw cycles unrolled as 6 sequential heat calls (no loop constructs)
  - drain lowered to dispose in IR; this is known compiler behaviour; .bs source is correct
  - SpermCountAliquot_Decondensed manifest models the 1 uL sub-sample from the decondensed stock;
    spermDecondensed flows unbroken into the library dilution step (Step 11)
