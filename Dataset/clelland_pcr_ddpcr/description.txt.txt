OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol covers two sequential, complementary molecular biology workflows:

  1. Standard PCR: DNA amplification from cultured mammalian cells extracted with QuickExtract,
     followed by gel electrophoresis QC on a Bio-Rad Sub-Cell GT system and fluorescence imaging
     on a LI-COR Odyssey M imager.

  2. Droplet Digital PCR (ddPCR): absolute quantification of target DNA copy number using a
     Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Generator and Droplet Reader, with a two-channel FAM/HEX probe
     design (target gene + RPP30 reference). ddPCR partitions the PCR reaction into ~20,000
     nanodroplets (~0.85 nL each), enabling Poisson-statistics-based absolute counting of
     positive (target-containing) vs. negative (empty) droplets without a standard curve.

An optional gel extraction module (Qiagen MinElute, modified) is included for downstream
sequencing applications requiring a larger 50 uL PCR reaction.

The BioScript encoding covers both workflows in a single file with a linear-sequential
structure: DNA extraction → PCR → gel QC gate (BB1-BB4) → ddPCR → droplet readout gate
(BB4-BB7). A total of 7 basic blocks and 2 if/else QC gates are generated.


PROTOCOL 1: STANDARD PCR
-------------------------

Step 1 | DNA Preparation by QuickExtract (96-well plate)
  Reagents   : PBS, QuickExtract DNA Extraction Solution (Biosearch Technologies, 76081-768)
  Operations :
    1. Wash cells with 100 uL PBS per well; aspirate.
    2. Add 30 uL QuickExtract per well.
    3. Scrape well bottom with pipette tip to detach cells.
    4. Transfer cells to labelled PCR tubes.
    5. Vortex 15 s.
    6. Incubate at 65 C for 6 min.
    7. Vortex 15 s.
    8. Incubate at 98 C for 2 min.
    9. Store at -20 C.
  Output     : PCR-ready DNA template (direct lysis, no column purification required)

Step 2 | Primer Preparation
  Working stock : 10 uM (diluted from 100 uM IDT stock)
  Preparation   :
    - Resuspend IDT lyophilised primers in H2O to 100 uM (e.g. 20 nmol -> add 200 uL H2O).
    - Dilute to 10 uM working stock in new microcentrifuge tube (20 uL + 180 uL H2O).
    - Store at -20 C.
  Design guidelines (PCR primers):
    - Length: 18-30 bases (~20 optimal); GC content: 40-60%.
    - 3' end should be G or C; avoid >3 consecutive G/C repeats.
    - Tm: 50-65 C; primer pairs within 5 C of each other.
    - Product length: 200-1000 bp (up to 2-3 kb if needed).
    - Tools: Primer-BLAST (NCBI), Primer3web, OligoCalc (Properties Calculator).
    - Order from IDT as Custom DNA oligos.

Step 3 | PCR Reaction Setup (20 uL)
  Master Mix for one 20 uL reaction:
    BioMix Red (Meridian Life Sciences, C755F25)     10.0 uL
    DMSO (Sigma Aldrich, D8418)                       0.6 uL
    Forward primer (10 uM)                            1.0 uL
    Reverse primer (10 uM)                            1.0 uL
    RNase/DNase-free H2O                              6.4 uL
    ------------------------------------------------ -------
    Total Master Mix                                 19.0 uL
    + 1 uL DNA template per tube                  -> 20 uL

  Setup: prepare Master Mix for (number of reactions + 1) as extra.
  Load into Bio-Rad Thermal Cycler.

  PCR for sequencing (with gel extraction): use 50 uL reaction; 2.5 uL DNA + 47 uL Master Mix.
    BioMix Red 25 uL | DMSO 1.5 uL | Fwd primer 2.5 uL | Rev primer 2.5 uL | H2O 16 uL.

Step 4 | PCR Thermocycling (Bio-Rad Thermal Cycler)
  Step | Name                 | Temperature | Time
  -----+----------------------+-------------+----------
    1  | Enzyme Activation    | 94 C        | 3 min
    2  | Denaturation         | 94 C        | 30 s
    3  | Annealing            | 50-65 C*    | 30 s
    4  | Primary Extension    | 72 C        | 1 min/kb
    5  | Repeat steps 2-4     | --          | x34
    6  | Secondary Extension  | 72 C        | 5 min
    7  | Hold                 | 4 C         | infinity
  *Annealing temperature: 5-10 C below primer Tm.

  PCR for sequencing cycle (39 amplification cycles, 50 uL reaction):
    Enzyme Activation 94 C 3.5 min -> Denature 94 C 45 s -> Anneal 50-65 C 45 s ->
    Extend 72 C 1 min/kb -> x39 -> Secondary Extension 72 C 5 min -> Hold 4 C.

Step 5 | Gel Electrophoresis
  Buffer preparation:
    1X TAE: add 36 mL 50X TAE Buffer (Thermo Scientific, B49) to 2L bottle; add diH2O to 1800 mL.

  Gel preparation (1.5-2% agarose):
    - For 150 mL gel: weigh agarose (e.g. 2 g for 2% in 100 mL 1X TAE).
    - Heat in microwave until completely dissolved; let cool slightly.
    - Add 15 uL SYBR Safe DNA Gel Stain (Invitrogen, S33102) per 150 mL gel.
    - Pour onto tray; remove bubbles; place 15 or 20-well comb; let solidify.
    - NOTE: clean and rinse beaker after every use; do NOT discard excess gel in sink.

  Running the gel:
    - Place solidified gel in Bio-Rad Sub-Cell GT Cell; wells at negative (black) terminal.
    - Fill with 1X TAE buffer until gel is fully submerged.
    - Load ~12 uL Quick-Load Purple 1 kb Plus DNA Ladder (NEB, N0550S) and PCR products.
      Submerge pipette tip into well before dispensing; press only to first stop to avoid bubbles.
    - Set Bio-Rad PowerPac Power Supply to 100-140 V (Black to Black; Red to Red); START.
    - Run until bands resolved (~40 min typical).

  Gel imaging (LI-COR Odyssey M Imager):
    - Clean imager glass with EtOH and Kimwipe before and after each use.
    - Open LI-COR Acquisition Software.
    - Select: Scan -> Username: Clelland Lab -> Imager: Odyssey M -> Connect.
    - Select: Gel -> Connect -> Draw Scan Area -> Next.
    - Select: 488 / SYBR Safe -> Save -> Focus offset 2.00 mm -> Scan.
    - Wait for scan to finish; export image to desired destination.


PROTOCOL 2: DROPLET DIGITAL PCR (ddPCR)
-----------------------------------------
Instrument: Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Generator + Droplet Reader.
NOTE: Schedule equipment at CAT on iLab before proceeding.

Step 6 | 20X Target Primers/Probe Preparation
  Component                     Volume
  Forward primer (100 uM)       18 uL
  Reverse primer (100 uM)       18 uL
  Target probe (FAM)*            5 uL
  RNase/DNase-free H2O          59 uL
  Total                        100 uL
  *Light sensitive. Store all at -20 C.

  Design guidelines (ddPCR probes):
    - Between primer pair; no overlap with primers.
    - Length: <30 bases; Tm: 3-10 C above primers.
    - More Cs than Gs; no G at 5' end.
    - Product length: 50-250 bp (optimal 80-120 bp).
    - Order from IDT as PrimeTime qPCR Probes (5' FAM/ZEN/3' Iowa Black FQ).

Step 7 | ddPCR Master Mix Setup (22 uL per reaction)
  Component                                          Volume
  2X ddPCR Supermix for Probes (No dUTP, Bio-Rad)   11.0 uL
  20X target primers/probe (FAM)                      1.1 uL
  20X reference primers/probe (HEX/VIC) - RPP30       1.1 uL
  RNase/DNase-free H2O + DNA sample           up to 22.0 uL
  DNA input: up to 350 ng per reaction.

  Reference assay: ddPCR Copy Number Assay RPP30, Human, Homo sapiens
                   (Bio-Rad, dHsaCP1000485; HEX/VIC channel; Ch 2 Reference).
  Setup: thaw all components to room temperature; vortex at full speed 15 s when mixing.
  Prepare Master Mix for (number of reactions + 1).

Step 8 | Droplet Generation (Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Generator)
  1. Insert DG8 Cartridge (Bio-Rad, 1864008) into cartridge holder (Bio-Rad, 1863051).
  2. FIRST: load >20 uL sample into sample wells.
     - Empty wells: add 50:50 mix of Supermix and H2O. All 8 wells must be loaded.
     - No bubbles.
  3. THEN: load 70 uL Droplet Generation Oil for Probes (Bio-Rad, 1863005) into oil wells.
     - Oil is single-use; use within one day of opening.
  4. Apply DG8 Gasket (Bio-Rad, 1863009) on cartridge.
  5. Load cartridge into QX200 Droplet Generator; START (~1 min).

Step 9 | Droplet Transfer and Plate Sealing (within 5 min of generation)
  1. Transfer 40 uL droplets to ddPCR 96-Well Plates (Bio-Rad, 12001925).
     - Pipette SLOWLY: >7 s for aspiration and dispensing to preserve droplet integrity.
  2. Cover plate with foil seal sheet (PCR Plate Heat Seal, pierceable; Bio-Rad, 1814040).
     - Red line facing UP.
  3. Seal plate in PX1 PCR Plate Sealer (Bio-Rad, 1814000) at 180 C for 5 s.

Step 10 | ddPCR Thermocycling (within 40 min of sealing)
  Instrument: Bio-Rad C1000 Touch Thermal Cycler with 96-Deep Well C1000 block (Bio-Rad, 1851197).
  Ramp rate: 2 C/s throughout.

  Step | Name                   | Temperature | Time
  -----+------------------------+-------------+---------
    1  | Enzyme Activation      | 94 C        | 10 min
    2  | Denaturation           | 94 C        | 30 s
    3  | Annealing/Extension    | *50-65 C    | 1 min
    4  | Repeat steps 2-3       | --          | x39
    5  | Enzyme Deactivation    | 98 C        | 10 min
    6  | Hold                   | 4 C         | infinity
  *Optimise annealing temperature using gradient when using a new primers/probe set.

Step 11 | Plate Reading and Data Analysis (Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Reader)
  1. Let plate equilibrate at room temperature for 1-2 min.
     (Plate storable at 4 C for up to 3 days before reading.)
  2. Place plate in Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Reader.
  3. Open QuantaSoft; configure each well:
     - Sample: enter name; Experiment: CNV2; Supermix: ddPCR Supermix for Probes (no dUTP).
     - Target 1 (FAM):     enter name; Type: Ch 1 Unknown.
     - Target 2 (HEX/VIC): enter name (e.g. RPP30); Type: Ch 2 Reference.
     - NOTE: copy and paste configuration for every well in the plate.
  4. Save template; RUN.
  5. Data analysis: set threshold manually (pink line); ensure positive droplets (blue/green)
     are separated from negative droplets (grey). See 2-D and 1-D droplet plots.

BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file  : clelland_pcr_ddpcr.bs
Compiler        : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output file  : clelland_pcr_ddpcr.ir
DAG output file : clelland_pcr_ddpcr_main_dag.dot
Basic blocks    : 7 (BB1-BB7); 2 if/else QC gates; 8 CFG edges

Duration tokens: BioScript supports s (seconds) and m (minutes) only; h token not supported.
  QuickExtract 65 C 6 min        ->  6m   (IR: 360.0 SECOND)
  QuickExtract 98 C 2 min        ->  2m   (IR: 120.0 SECOND)
  PCR enzyme activation 3 min    ->  3m   (IR: 180.0 SECOND)
  PCR final extension 5 min      ->  5m   (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
  Gel electrophoresis run 40 min ->  40m  (IR: 2400.0 SECOND)
  ddPCR enzyme activation 10 min ->  10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  ddPCR enzyme deactivation 10min->  10m  (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  ddPCR QuantaSoft readout 30 min->  30m  (IR: 1800.0 SECOND)

Modules    : gelElectrophoresisDetection, dropletReadoutDetection
Manifests  : Cells, PBS, QuickExtract, BioMixRed, DMSO, ForwardPrimer_10uM,
             ReversePrimer_10uM, RNaseDNaseFreeH2O, AgaroseGel_1pct5to2pct,
             SYBR_Safe_Stain, TAE_Buffer_1X, DNALadder, ForwardPrimer_100uM,
             ReversePrimer_100uM, TargetProbe_FAM, ReferencePrimersProbe_HEX,
             ddPCR_Supermix_NoUTP, GenerationOil, FoilSealSheet

Abstractions:
  - Vortex steps (15 s) abstracted as heat at 25 C for 15 s (no standalone vortex op).
  - PBS wash/aspirate: mix then dispose(pbsWash); disposed variable never re-referenced.
  - PCR thermocycle loop: one representative cycle at 57 C (midpoint of 50-65 C); no loop.
  - ddPCR thermocycle loop: one representative cycle at 57 C; no loop construct.
  - Plate sealing (180 C, 5 s): heat(sealedPlate) after mix with FoilSealSheet.
  - Gel electrophoresis run: heat(gelRunReady) at 25 C for 40 m (voltage at ambient temp).
  - ddPCR DNA input: fresh second dispense/re-extraction block; avoids referencing disposed variable.
  - drain normalises to dispose in IR output per compiler behaviour.

CFG structure:
  BB1  main          -- entry: QuickExtract extraction; PCR master mix; thermocycling;
                        gel electrophoresis; gelElectrophoresisDetection
  BB2  bsbbif_2_t   -- gelSignal > 0:  dispose(gelRunReady)
  BB3  bsbbif_3_f   -- gelSignal <= 0: drain(gelRunReady)
  BB4  bsbbif_4_j   -- join: ddPCR probe prep; ddPCR master mix; droplet generation;
                        plate sealing; thermocycling; dropletReadoutDetection
  BB5  bsbbif_5_t   -- ddpcrSignal > 0:  dispose(sealedPlate)
  BB6  bsbbif_6_f   -- ddpcrSignal <= 0: drain(sealedPlate)
  BB7  bsbbif_7_j   -- terminal: dispose(ddpcrSignal); NOP; experiment ends
