PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Automated Microfluidic System for Dynamic Stimulation and Tracking of Single Cells
Source Link  : https://taylab.uchicago.edu/uploads/9/1/8/0/91804060/2018_anal_chem.pdf
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.8b00312
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2018, XXXX, XXX-XXX (ACS Publications)
Platform     : Large-scale integrated microfluidics (LSIM); two-layer PDMS valve-based chip


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a programmable, valve-controlled microfluidic system designed for
long-term culture, dynamic cytokine stimulation, and continuous single-cell time-lapse imaging
of nonadherent hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells (HSPCs) and adherent murine embryonic
stem cells (muESCs) without displacing or losing track of individual cells.

The system overcomes a key limitation of existing LSIM devices: most rely on direct media
flow over cells, which physically displaces nonadherent cells during each media exchange.
Here, media exchange occurs exclusively by diffusion through 50-um-wide ports connecting
flanking media reservoirs to the cell culture area. Valves alternate reservoir filling
between left and right sides to prevent cross-chamber pressure-driven flow (flow-switching
strategy). No flow crosses the cell culture area at any point during culture or stimulation.

The protocol is divided into two independent experimental arms encoded in sequence:

  Protocol 1: Long-term GMP differentiation assay
    - Murine LysM-GFP granulocyte-macrophage progenitors (GMPs) seeded into 48 chambers
    - 48 h baseline culture in permissive SCF/IL3/IL6 Stem-Span SFEM
    - Switch to 20 ng/mL M-CSF for 48 h; LysM-GFP expression tracked every 30 min
    - In-culture continuous anti-F4/80-APC antibody staining for macrophage commitment
    - Result: 77% of M-CSF colonies vs. 54% of control colonies upregulated LysM-GFP
      within 96 h (p=0.001636, two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test)

  Protocol 2: Dynamic FGF1 stimulation of muESC ERKKTR reporter line
    - Line R1 muESCs expressing ERK kinase translocation reporter (ERKKTR)
    - 2-day self-renewing culture (DMEM + 10% FCS + 1000 U/mL Lif)
    - 16 h starvation (0.5% FCS DMEM without Lif) to synchronise ERK baseline
    - Oscillating FGF1 stimulation: 5 s FGF1 pulse / 32 min incubation / 2 s wash /
      15 min incubation; three complete cycles encoded
    - ERK activity imaged every 3 min (ERKKTR nuclear-to-cytoplasmic C/N ratio)
    - 93% of cells adjacent to reservoir respond within 3 min; all within 10 min


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i  : Flow layer -- PDMS, channel height 25 um; fabricated by photolithography on
           SU-8 3025 master (4-in. silicon wafer); 40 kdpi photomask (Fine Line Imaging).
Layer ii : Control layer -- PDMS, channel height 25 um; AZ-50XT deposited at valve
           positions to produce parabolic valve surfaces for tight closure.
Layer iii: Coverslip bonded to underside of flow layer after O2 plasma treatment.

Fabrication:
  - PDMS ratio: 10:1 polymer:catalyst (RTV 615, Momentive Performance Materials)
  - Cast at 80 C for 45 min on wafer
  - O2 plasma treatment, layer alignment, bonding
  - 2 h thermal bonding at 80 C; additional 12 h cure after full assembly at 80 C

Chip architecture:
  - 48 independently addressable cell culture chambers (1000 um x 350 um x 25 um)
  - 16 independent inputs (left side); 2 outlets / harvesting ports (right side)
  - Four-valve on-chip peristaltic pump for fluid manipulation and cell deposition
  - 2 media reservoirs per chamber; connected via 50-um-wide diffusion ports
  - Valve control: solenoid manifold (1.7-2.5 bar closing; 0.5 bar flow layer)
  - MATLAB GUI for experimental scripting and valve actuation control

Chip validation (bead displacement test):
  - 10 um polystyrene beads (Spherotech) pre-treated with 0.1% Triton-X
  - Bead translocation in culture chamber: <2 um after 15 s reservoir flushing
  - Confirms absence of direct flow in culture area during media exchange
  - 40 kDa rhodamine-dextran (300 uM) used for diffusion rate calibration
    (comparable in size to cytokines; reaches steady state within 10 min)


CELL LINES
----------
- Murine LysM-GFP GMPs (granulocyte-macrophage progenitors):
    Source: C57BL/6J male mice, 12-14 weeks old, Lysozyme M gene locus (LysM-GFP)
    Immunophenotype: lin-/Sca1-/c-Kit+/CD34+/CD16/32+
    Isolation: Bone marrow dissected, pulverized (4 C mortar), 40 um nylon mesh strainer
    Lineage depletion: Biotinylated antibodies (CD3e, CD11b, CD19, CD41, B220, Gr-1,
      TER-119) + Streptavidin Roti-MagBeads (Carl Roth AG) on EasySep magnet
    FACS sorting: Anti-Sca-1, c-Kit, CD34 staining; sorted on FACS Aria (BD Biosciences)
    Media: Stem-Span SFEM + 1% pen/strep + 10% FCS + 100 ng/mL SCF + 10 ng/mL IL3
           + 10 ng/mL IL6 (permissive arm); 20 ng/mL M-CSF (differentiation arm)

- Murine ESCs line R1 (ERKKTR reporter):
    Reporter: ERK kinase translocation reporter (ERKKTR)
    Seeding: ~10 cells per chamber
    Media: DMEM (Gibco) + 10% FCS11 (PAA) + 1000 U/mL Lif (CellGS)
    Starvation: 0.5% FCS DMEM without Lif, 16 h prior to stimulation
    Culture type: Adherent (E-Cadherin surface coating, 150 ng/mL)


ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
---------------------
Microscope   : Nikon Ti-eclipse inverted (Orca Flash IV camera, 10x/NA0.45 objective)
Temperature  : 37 C (feedback-controlled heating unit, Life Imaging Services)

PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Chip Priming
  Reagents  : PBS
  Operation : Dispense PBS; force through all channels under pressure while outlet
              valves remain closed to expel air.
  Purpose   : Fully wets all surfaces before functionalization. Required step before
              any biological use of the chip.

Step 2 | Surface Functionalization
  Reagents  : PBS + Fibronectin (GMPs) or E-Cadherin (muESCs), 150 ng/mL (Merck KGaA)
  Operation : Flow fibronectin through all channels for 3 h at 25 C (or overnight).
              Wash extensively with PBS, then equilibrate with cell culture media.
  Duration  : 180 m (= 3 h) at 25 C; IR: 10800.0 SECOND
  Purpose   : Promotes hematopoietic progenitor retention and muESC adhesion;
              prevents non-specific cell-to-PDMS binding.

Step 3 | Chip Validation -- Bead Displacement Test
  Reagents  : PolystyrenBeads_10um (0.1% Triton-X pre-treated)
              RhodamineDextran_40kDa (300 uM in PBS)
  Operation : Load beads into chambers; flush media reservoirs for 15 s;
              confirm <2 um bead translocation in culture area.
              Separately, introduce single rhodamine-dextran pulse into PBS-filled
              reservoirs and monitor fluorescence for 30 min (diffusion calibration).
  Detection : cellTrackingDetection module, 15 s read
  Purpose   : Quantitatively validates no-flow condition in culture chambers.
              Diffusion calibration confirms cytokine-scale molecule exchange kinetics.

Step 4 | GMP Seeding  [Protocol 1]
  Reagents  : LysM_GFP_GMPs, StemSpanSFEM
  Operation : Connect pressurized microcentrifuge tube to first inlet via 25 cm PEEK
              tubing (65 um ID). Use peristaltic pump to deposit 4-90 cells per chamber
              in a targeted, chamber-by-chamber manner.
  Duration  : 2 s seeding pulse per chamber
  Notes     : Targeted seeding enables precise cell number control. Each of the 48
              chambers can be independently addressed.

Step 5 | Baseline Culture in Permissive SCF/IL3/IL6 Media  [Protocol 1]
  Reagents  : SCF_IL3_IL6_Media (SFEM + 1% pen/strep + 10% FCS + 100 ng/mL SCF
              + 10 ng/mL IL3 + 10 ng/mL IL6)
  Operation : Flow-switching: left reservoir filled (5 s pulse), then right reservoir
              filled (5 s pulse) in alternating sequence every 30 min.
              No flow enters culture area; diffusion equilibrates chambers in ~10 min.
  Duration  : 2880 m (= 48 h) at 37 C; IR: 172800.0 SECOND
  Notes     : Proliferation and cell cycle length comparable to standard Ibidi VI
              channel slides (Figs. S1A, S1B in paper Supporting Information).

Step 6 | M-CSF Differentiation Culture  [Protocol 1]
  Reagents  : MCSF_Media (SFEM + 1% pen/strep + 10% FCS + 20 ng/mL M-CSF)
  Operation : Switch media reservoirs to M-CSF-containing media. Same 30-min
              flow-switching cadence. LysM-GFP fluorescence imaged every 30 min
              (Nikon Ti-eclipse, 10x/NA0.45, ORCA Flash IV).
              Single-cell tracking performed with tTt software.
  Duration  : 2880 m (= 48 h) at 37 C; IR: 172800.0 SECOND
  Detection : lysM_GFP_Detection module, 30 s read
  Results   : 77% of M-CSF-treated colonies upregulate LysM-GFP within 96 h vs.
              54% without M-CSF (p=0.001636, two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov test).
  CFG gate  : differentiationSignal > 0 -> BB2 (dispose); <= 0 -> BB3 (drain)
              Both converge at BB4 (differentiationJoin)

Step 7 | In-Culture Continuous Antibody Staining of Live Cells  [Protocol 1]
  Reagents  : LysM_GFP_GMPs, StemSpanSFEM, SCF_IL3_IL6_Media, AntiF480_APC_Antibody,
              MCSF_Media
  Operation : Fresh GMPs seeded. Baseline culture (2880 m, permissive media).
              Anti-F4/80-APC (clone BM8, eBioscience; 0.2 mg/mL at 1:10,000 dilution)
              added continuously to all M-CSF media during diffusion exchange cycles.
              Automated fluid replacement incorporates antibody in every 30-min cycle.
  Duration  : 2880 m baseline + 2880 m M-CSF+antibody at 37 C; IR: 172800.0 SECOND each
  Detection : f480_MacrophageDetection module, 30 s read
  Notes     : F4/80 is a molecular reporter for macrophage lineage commitment.
              Fluorescence detectable on differentiated cells confirms commitment.
  CFG gate  : f480Signal > 0 -> BB5 (dispose); <= 0 -> BB6 (drain)
              Both converge at BB7 (antibodyJoin)

Step 8 | muESC Seeding and Self-Renewing Culture  [Protocol 2]
  Reagents  : MuESC_ERKKTR, DMEM_SelfRenewing (DMEM + 10% FCS + 1000 U/mL Lif)
  Operation : ~10 R1 muESCs seeded per chamber (E-Cadherin surface coating, 150 ng/mL).
              Diffusion media exchange every 30 min (same flow-switching strategy).
  Duration  : 2880 m (= 2 days) at 37 C; IR: 172800.0 SECOND
  Notes     : Cells cultured to stable self-renewing state before starvation.

Step 9 | Overnight Starvation  [Protocol 2]
  Reagents  : DMEM_Starvation (0.5% FCS DMEM without Lif)
  Operation : Switch to starvation media; same 30-min flow-switching cadence.
  Duration  : 960 m (= 16 h) at 37 C; IR: 57600.0 SECOND
  Purpose   : Synchronises ERK baseline activity across all cells before stimulation.

Step 10 | Dynamic FGF1 Stimulation -- 3 Oscillation Cycles  [Protocol 2]
  Reagents  : FGF1_StarvationMedia (10 ng/mL recombinant mouse FGF1, R&D Systems,
              in 0.5% FCS DMEM without Lif)
              DMEM_Starvation (plain starvation wash media)
  Operation per cycle:
    - 5 s flow pulse: FGF1-containing starvation media into left reservoir
    - 32 min incubation: FGF1 diffuses into culture area at 37 C
    - 2 s flow pulse: plain starvation media washes right reservoir
    - 15 min incubation: FGF1 dilutes from chamber by diffusion at 37 C
  Cycles    : 3 complete ON/OFF cycles encoded (fgf1FeedL_C1/C2/C3 + washFeedR_C1/C2/C3)
  Duration  : 32 m FGF1 incubation (IR: 1920.0 SECOND) + 15 m wash (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
              per cycle; 37 C throughout
  Imaging   : ERK activity imaged every 3 min (ERKKTR C/N ratio)
  Response  : 93% of cells adjacent to reservoir respond within 3 min;
              all cells respond within 10 min of stimulation onset
  Detection : erkktr_ERKDetection module, 3 m read (IR: 180.0 SECOND)
  CFG gate  : erkSignal > 0 -> BB8 (dispose); <= 0 -> BB9 (drain)
              Both converge at BB10 (finalCleanup)

Step 11 | Final Cleanup  [BB10 -- terminal]
  Operation : dispose(erkSignal); dispose(validationSignal); dispose(diffusionCalib); NOP
  Notes     : All outstanding QC signal droplets disposed. Experiment ends.


FLOW-SWITCHING STRATEGY
------------------------
The key innovation enabling nonadherent cell culture is the alternating reservoir fill:

  1. Left reservoir filled:  left-side inlet valves open; right-side valves closed.
     Fresh media or stimulant flows into left reservoir channel only.
  2. Right reservoir filled: right-side inlet valves open; left-side valves closed.
     Mirrors left fill. Completes one exchange cycle.
  3. Seeding valves:         always closed during culture phases.

This sequential approach eliminates pressure differentials that would drive flow across
the culture chamber. Reagents exchange into the culture area by diffusion only (Fick's
Law through 50 um port). Steady state reached within 10 min per reservoir fill cycle.
Oscillation periods of 20 min and 40 min were validated with rhodamine-dextran (Fig. 2B).
Chemical gradients across the chamber last a maximum of 20 min per exchange.

  960m  starvation              -> 57600.0s   (16 h)

  15m   wash incubation (x3)   -> 900.0s     per cycle
  3m    ERK detect              -> 180.0s

Single-assignment constraint:
  BioScript's SimpleSymbolVisitor forbids reusing a variable that has already been
  consumed as a mix operand. Every flow-switch cycle and every media repeat requires
  a freshly dispensed named variable. Variables introduced to satisfy this constraint:
    permissiveMedia2    -- right-reservoir fill, baseline GMP culture
    mcsfMedia2          -- right-reservoir fill, M-CSF culture
    mcsfForAb           -- MCSF_Media dispense for antibody mixing step
    sfemMedia2          -- SFEM for antibody-branch GMP seeding
    permissiveBase2     -- right-reservoir fill, antibody-branch baseline
    mcsfAb2             -- right-reservoir fill, antibody-in-M-CSF culture
    selfRenewMedia2/3   -- left/right reservoir fills, muESC self-renewing culture
    starvationMedia2    -- right-reservoir fill, starvation phase

mix abstraction:
  `mix` in this .bs file represents valve-controlled reservoir fill events followed
  by passive diffusion equilibration -- NOT physical droplet-level mixing. No direct
  fluid flow enters cell culture chambers. The 5 s / 2 s mix durations encode the
  reservoir flushing pulse times; subsequent `heat` statements encode incubation.
