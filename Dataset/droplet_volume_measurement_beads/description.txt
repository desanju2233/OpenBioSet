PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Measuring Droplet Volume in Home-Made Microfluidic Devices
Source Link  : https://www.protocols.io/view/measuring-droplet-volume-in-home-made-microfluidic-3byl46drgo5d/v1
DOI Link     : https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.mj5c4q6
Platform     : Home-made droplet microfluidic device (droplet-in-channel; EWOD/DMF-adjacent)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a bead-based volumetric calibration procedure for determining droplet
volume in home-made microfluidic devices. Monodisperse polystyrene beads suspended at a known
concentration are co-flowed with PBS through the device to generate droplets. The number of
beads per droplet (droplet occupancy) is measured by microscopy over several hundred droplets,
and the droplet volume is calculated from the occupancy and the known bead concentration in
the combined aqueous phase.

This is a device characterisation/calibration protocol, not a cell biology assay. It is
applicable to any home-made droplet microfluidic chip prior to biological experiments, providing
a ground-truth volume estimate without requiring dedicated optical volume sensors.


PRINCIPLE
---------
Beads (10 um carboxylated polystyrene) are resuspended in Drop-seq lysis buffer at
1000 beads/uL and loaded into the bead syringe. PBS is loaded into the cell-channel syringe.
When both syringes co-flow into the microfluidic device at equal rates, the combined aqueous
phase has an effective bead concentration of:

    1000 beads/uL (bead syringe) / 2 (co-flow dilution) = 500 beads/uL in droplet fluid

After generating droplets, beads inside droplets are counted under microscopy to compute:

    Droplet occupancy  = total beads counted / total droplets counted
    Droplet volume     = droplet occupancy / 500 beads/uL   [units: uL per droplet]

A minimum of several hundred droplets should be counted to obtain a statistically sound estimate.


REAGENTS
--------
PolyBeads_LysisBuffer:
  - Monodisperse carboxylated polystyrene beads, ~10 um diameter
  - Standard (non-fluorescent): Bangs Labs catalog PC06N-11355
  - Fluorescent (recommended): Bangs Labs catalog FC06F-10163
  - Suspension buffer: Drop-seq lysis buffer
  - Concentration: 1000 beads/uL
  - Loaded in syringe with magnetic mixer (as per standard barcoded-bead protocol)

PBS:
  - Phosphate-buffered saline (standard, no additives required)
  - Loaded into the syringe intended for cells (no cells present in calibration run)
  - Co-flow with bead syringe; halves bead concentration to 500 beads/uL in droplet fluid


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Prepare Bead Suspension
  Reagent      : PolyBeads_LysisBuffer
  Operation    : dispense (BioScript abstraction for syringe loading)
  Details      : Wash and resuspend 10 um carboxylated polystyrene beads in Drop-seq lysis
                 buffer at 1000 beads/uL. Draw into syringe equipped with a magnetic mixer
                 to prevent bead settling during the run.

Step 2 | Load PBS into Cell-Channel Syringe
  Reagent      : PBS
  Operation    : dispense (BioScript abstraction for syringe loading)
  Details      : Load the syringe intended for cells with regular PBS. No cells are present
                 in this calibration run. The co-flow of bead suspension and PBS at equal
                 volumetric rates gives an effective bead concentration of 500 beads/uL
                 in the combined droplet fluid.

Step 3 | Co-Flow and Droplet Generation
  Inputs       : beadSuspension, pbsFlow
  Operation    : mix (BioScript abstraction for co-flow junction merging)
  Duration     : 30 s (stabilisation period before droplet collection begins)
  Details      : Connect all tubing to the appropriate channels in the microfluidic device.
                 Start both syringe pumps. The bead syringe and PBS syringe co-flow into the
                 aqueous inlet; the carrier oil generates droplets at the T-junction or
                 flow-focus nozzle.
  Note         : mix here models the co-flow merging of two aqueous streams at the chip inlet
                 junction, not a DMF electrode-merging event.

Step 4 | Bead Counting Detection (Droplet Occupancy Measurement)
  Input        : combinedFluid
  Operation    : detect (beadCountDetection module)
  Duration     : 60 s (image acquisition and counting window)
  Details      : Image several hundred droplets under microscopy (fluorescence channel if
                 fluorescent beads; brightfield otherwise). Count total beads inside all
                 imaged droplets and the number of droplets counted. Compute:
                   Droplet occupancy = total_beads / total_droplets
                   Droplet volume    = occupancy / 500 beads/uL
  Minimum      : >= 200 droplets recommended for a statistically sound estimate

  QC Gate:
    Pass (beadSignal > 0): Beads detected in droplets; valid droplet population; volume
                           estimate obtained; dispose combinedFluid.
    Fail (beadSignal <= 0): No beads detected; device failure path (check tubing connections,
                            nozzle, oil flow rate, and bead resuspension quality); drain
                            combinedFluid.


CFG STRUCTURE
-------------
BB1  main         -- entry: dispense beads, dispense PBS, co-flow mix (30 s),
                     detect bead occupancy (60 s)
BB2  bsbbif_2_t   -- beadSignal > 0: valid droplet population; dispose(combinedFluid)
BB3  bsbbif_3_f   -- beadSignal <= 0: device failure; drain(combinedFluid)
BB4  bsbbif_4_j   -- terminal join: dispose(beadSignal); experiment ends

Edges: BB1->BB2, BB1->BB3, BB2->BB4, BB3->BB4
Total blocks: 4 | Total edges: 4 | if/else gates: 1
