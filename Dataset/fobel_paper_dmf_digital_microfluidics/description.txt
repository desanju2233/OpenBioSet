PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Paper Microfluidics Goes Digital
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/paper%20DMF.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.201305168
Journal      : Advanced Materials, 2014, Vol. 26, pp. 2838-2843
Platform     : Paper DMF (digital microfluidics on inkjet-printed silver-on-paper substrates)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first demonstration of digital microfluidics (DMF) implemented on
paper-based substrates, combining the programmable drop manipulation of DMF with the low cost
and disposability of paper microfluidics. Devices are fabricated by inkjet printing silver
nanoparticle ink onto photo paper using a Dimatix DMP-2800 printer, followed by infrared
sintering (1500 W lamp, 10 s). The resulting paper DMF devices exhibit electrode resistances
500x lower than equivalent chromium-on-glass photolithography devices, and drop velocities
nearly identical to glass DMF devices at comparable actuation voltages.

Two complex, multistep assays are demonstrated:
  1. Homogeneous HRP chemiluminescence assay with on-chip serial dilution (Design A):
     63 discrete steps (27 dispense, 18 mix, 6 split, 12 measure) producing a four-point
     calibration curve in under 1 h; R^2 = 0.993. Represents a level of complexity
     difficult or impossible to achieve on capillary-driven paper devices.
  2. Proof-of-principle rubella IgG sandwich ELISA with magnetic bead separation (Design B):
     30 discrete steps per concentration; LOD = 0.15 IU/mL (well below the 10 IU/mL clinical
     threshold); R^2 = 0.988. First report of a rubella IgG assay on any microfluidic format.

Cost per device (Design A): less than $0.05 USD. Print time: approximately 1 minute.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Fabrication  : Inkjet printing of SunTronic U6503 silver nanoparticle ink onto optimized
               photo paper (custom substrate from Prof. M. Toivakka, Abo Akademi University,
               Finland; also tested on Epson Premium Photo Glossy and HP Premium Plus Photo
               Glossy). Dimatix Model Fluid 2 Waveform; printhead at 20-22 V; meniscus
               vacuum 4-5 inches H2O at room temperature.

Sintering    : 1500 W infrared lamp at ~1 cm distance for 10 s. Sintering >= 15 s causes
               paper browning (no effect on function). All devices sintered for 10 s.

Silver layer : <500 nm thick (vs 10-30 um for PCB-based DMF). Surface roughness Ra ~250 nm
               (bare silver on paper); Ra < 100 nm after Parylene-C + Teflon-AF deposition.
               Electrode gaps: 60-90 um. Minimum feature: 30 um.

Dielectric   : 6.2 um Parylene-C deposited by vapor deposition (Specialty Coating Systems).
Hydrophobic  : ~50 nm Teflon-AF 1600 (DuPont; 1% wt/wt in Fluorinert FC-40; 1000 rpm, 30 s;
               post-baked 160 C for 10 min).

Top plate    : ITO-coated glass (Delta Technologies Ltd.) coated with 50 nm Teflon-AF.
               Teflon thread seal tape wrapped around contact pads before coating.

Assembly     : Top and bottom plates joined with two layers of double-sided tape (~80 um each).
               Drop volume: ~440 nL (Design A); ~750 nL (Design B).
               Reservoirs filled by pipetting adjacent to gap and applying potential to reservoir.

Design A     : 5 reservoir electrodes (4.17 x 4.17 mm); 19 driving electrodes (1.65 x 1.65 mm).
               Used for HRP assay and all non-immunoassay experiments.
               Also fabricated in chromium-on-glass format for performance comparison.

Design B     : 8 reservoir electrodes (5.6 x 5.6 mm); 38 driving electrodes (2.16 x 2.16 mm).
               Used for rubella IgG immunoassay; larger electrode array accommodates magnetic
               bead separation and reagent exchange steps.

Actuation    : ~100-125 V_rms, 15 kHz AC via solid-state relays.
Controller   : Open-source DropBot; with integrated magnetic control + PMT for immunoassay
               (ref [25]); without for HRP assay (ref [23]). Impedance-based feedback for
               velocity measurement.


ASSAY 1: HOMOGENEOUS HRP CHEMILUMINESCENCE (Design A)
------------------------------------------------------

Reagents:
  HRP standard          : 100 uU/mL horseradish peroxidase in DPBS + 0.05% v/v Pluronic L64
  Wash/diluent buffer   : DPBS + 0.05% v/v Pluronic L64
  Chemiluminescent sub  : SuperSignal ELISA Femto (Thermo Fischer Scientific, Rockford, IL);
                          two-component: stable H2O2 + Luminol-Enhancer; premixed before loading.
                          Supplemented with 0.025% v/v Pluronic L64.
  Detection instrument  : H10682-110 PMT (Hamamatsu Photonics K.K., Hamamatsu, Japan)
  Pluronic L64          : 0.05% v/v added to all reagent solutions (BASF Corp., Germany);
                          reduces protein adsorption and cross-contamination (ref [30]).

Protocol Steps:

Step A1 | Prepare diluent droplets
  Reagents  : PBS, PluronicL64
  Operation : Dispense + mix
  Duration  : 5 s per droplet
  Notes     : Three separate diluent droplets prepared (one per dilution level). Pluronic L64
              at 0.05% v/v in all PBS diluent drops.

Step A2 | Dispense HRP standard stock droplets
  Reagents  : HRP_Standard (100 uU/mL in DPBS + 0.05% v/v Pluronic L64)
  Operation : Dispense x3 (one per dilution level)
  Volume    : ~440 nL per drop (1 unit, Design A)
  Notes     : Three stock drops dispensed from reservoir independently.

Step A3 | Generate on-chip serial dilution (1x, 2x, 4x)
  Operation : Dispense + merge
  Levels    :
    1x : 1 HRP unit + 1 diluent unit; effective ~50 uU/mL (1:2 dilution by volume)
    2x : 1 HRP unit + 1 diluent unit; effective ~50 uU/mL
    4x : 1 HRP unit + 3 diluent units; effective ~25 uU/mL (1:4 dilution by volume)
  BioScript note : No split-and-reuse construct available in lilott8/BioScript compiler.
                   All dilution levels encoded as explicit dispense+merge chains from fresh
                   manifest entries. 4x diluent (3 units) accumulated by chaining two
                   additional PBS+Pluronic merge steps. This avoids stranded-variable and
                   split-array compiler errors while faithfully representing device operations.
  Paper total    : 63 discrete steps (27 dispense, 18 mix, 6 split, 12 measure) per paper.

Step A4 | Merge HRP dilutions with SuperSignal substrate and mix 60 s
  Reagents  : SuperSignalSubstrate (1 drop per dilution level)
  Operation : Dispense + mix
  Mix time  : 60 s
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'One drop of SuperSignal chemiluminescent substrate was then
              dispensed, mixed, and merged with each diluted drop of HRP, and the pooled
              drop was mixed for 60 s, driven to the detection area.'

Step A5 | Pre-measurement incubation at room temperature
  Operation      : heat
  Temperature    : 25 C
  Duration       : 120 s (2 min)
  Notes          : Paper M&M: 'emitted light was measured after 2 min with an H10682-110 PMT.'
                   heat step models 2 min dwell in detection zone before PMT readout.

Step A6 | PMT chemiluminescence detection
  Module         : chemiluminescenceDetection
  Duration       : 30 s per droplet
  Instrument     : H10682-110 PMT (Hamamatsu Photonics K.K., Japan); emission ~425-500 nm
  Calibration    : 4-point curve; R^2 = 0.993; each condition repeated 3 times
  QC gate        : hrpSignal1x (1x dilution = highest HRP concentration = highest signal)
    Pass (BB2)   : hrpSignal1x > 0; dispose all three reaction droplets
    Fail (BB3)   : hrpSignal1x <= 0; drain all three reaction droplets
  Join (BB4)     : dispose HRP signal droplets; proceed to Rubella ELISA branch


ASSAY 2: RUBELLA IgG SANDWICH ELISA (Design B)
-----------------------------------------------

Immunocomplex architecture:
  Magnetic bead coated with inactivated rubella virus (solid phase)
  Primary antibody: rubella IgG (analyte)
  Secondary antibody: HRP-conjugated goat anti-human IgG (detection)
  Readout: luminol/H2O2 chemiluminescence

Note: Unlike conventional ELISAs using a capture antibody specific to a target analyte,
this assay uses a bead coated with inactivated virus as the solid phase; the primary
antibody (rubella IgG) is the analyte. LOD = 0.15 IU/mL (clinical threshold: 10 IU/mL).

Concentrations tested: 0 IU/mL (blank), 1.56 IU/mL, 3.125 IU/mL
Steps per concentration: 30 (11 dispense, 10 mix, 8 magnetic separation, 1 measure)
Time per concentration: ~10 min; each condition repeated 2 times.

Reagents:
  Paramagnetic particles : Rubella-virus-coated microparticles (Abbott Laboratories);
                           ~1.5e8 particles/mL in Superblock Tris-buffered saline
  Rubella IgG standards  : 0, 1.56, 3.125 IU/mL (Abbott Laboratories);
                           diluted in DPBS + 4% BSA + 0.05% v/v Pluronic L64
  HRP conjugate          : HRP-conjugated goat polyclonal Anti-Human IgG, 16 ng/mL
                           in custom DMF-compatible conjugate diluent
  Wash buffer            : Custom DMF-compatible wash buffer (refs [25,27])
  H2O2 solution          : Stable peroxide component of SuperSignal ELISA Femto substrate
  Luminol-Enhancer       : Luminol-Enhancer component of SuperSignal ELISA Femto substrate
                           + 0.025% v/v Pluronic L64
  Magnetic separation    : Motorized magnet integrated with DropBot controller (ref [25])

Protocol Steps (identical sequence for all three IgG concentrations):

Step B1 | Dispense paramagnetic bead suspension
  Reagents  : ParamagneticParticles
  Operation : Dispense x2 + merge
  Volume    : 2 unit volumes (~1.5 uL total; Design B unit = 750 nL)
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'A ca. 1.6 uL drop (2 unit volumes) containing paramagnetic
              particles was dispensed from a reservoir and separated from the diluent.'
              Encoded as two 1-unit dispenses merged to produce the 2-unit-volume drop.

Step B2 | Dispense rubella IgG standard and incubate with beads
  Reagents  : RubellaIgG_Std_0 / RubellaIgG_Std_156 / RubellaIgG_Std_3125
  Operation : Dispense + mix
  Duration  : 180 s (3 min)
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'One drop of rubella IgG standard (0, 1.56 or 3.125 IU/mL) was
              dispensed, delivered to the immobilized particles, and mixed for 3 min.'
              Three separate manifest entries encode the three concentrations.

Step B3 | Primary antibody wash x3 with magnetic separation
  Reagents  : WashBuffer (1 drop per wash cycle)
  Operation : Dispense + mix (30 s) + detect rubellaMagneticSeparation (30 s) x3
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'The particles were washed three times in wash buffer and separated
              from the supernatant.' rubellaMagneticSeparation module abstracts motorized
              magnet engagement + DMF electrode switching that pellets the bead fraction and
              moves the supernatant droplet to waste electrode. Three explicit wash cycles
              unrolled (BioScript has no loop construct).

Step B4 | Dispense HRP conjugate and incubate with beads
  Reagents  : HRP_Conjugate (goat anti-human IgG, 16 ng/mL)
  Operation : Dispense + mix
  Duration  : 120 s (2 min)
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'One drop of HRP conjugate solution was dispensed, delivered to
              the immobilized particles, and mixed for 2 min.'

Step B5 | Post-conjugate wash x3 with magnetic separation
  Reagents  : WashBuffer (1 drop per wash cycle)
  Operation : Dispense + mix (30 s) + detect rubellaMagneticSeparation (30 s) x3
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'The particles were washed three times in wash buffer [after
              conjugate incubation].' Identical sequence to Step B3.

Step B6 | Resuspend beads in H2O2 and merge with luminol-enhancer
  Reagents  : H2O2_Solution, LuminolEnhancer
  Operation : Dispense + mix (30 s) + merge + mix (30 s)
  Notes     : Paper M&M: 'The particles were separated from the wash buffer and resuspended
              in one drop of H2O2, and this drop was merged and mixed with one drop of
              luminol-enhancer solution.' Two-component substrate merge encoded as sequential
              resuspend then luminol merge steps.

Step B7 | Pre-detection incubation and PMT readout
  Operation      : heat + detect
  Temperature    : 25 C
  Incubation     : 120 s (2 min)
  Module         : rubellaCLDetection
  Duration       : 30 s
  Instrument     : H10682-110 PMT (Hamamatsu Photonics K.K., Japan)
  LOD            : 0.15 IU/mL (clinical threshold: 10 IU/mL); R^2 = 0.988
  Notes          : Paper M&M: 'The pooled drop was incubated for 2 min and the
                   chemiluminescent signal was recorded using the PMT.'
  QC gate        : elisaSignal3125 (3.125 IU/mL = highest tested = highest expected signal)
    Pass (BB5)   : elisaSignal3125 > 0; dispose elisa0Pool, elisa156Pool, elisa3125Pool
    Fail (BB6)   : elisaSignal3125 <= 0; drain elisa0Pool, elisa156Pool, elisa3125Pool
  Terminal (BB7) : dispose elisaSignal0, elisaSignal156, elisaSignal3125; NOP


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : fobel_paper_dmf_digital_microfluidics.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 7 CFG edges
DAG            : fobel_paper_dmf_digital_microfluidics_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations in s or m (BioScript supports s and m only; h not supported)
                 2 min incubation  -> 2m  (IR: 120.0 SECOND)
                 3 min incubation  -> 3m  (IR: 180.0 SECOND)
Modules        : chemiluminescenceDetection, rubellaMagneticSeparation, rubellaCLDetection

Serial dilution note:
  BioScript has no split-and-reuse construct. The 1x/2x/4x HRP serial dilution is encoded
  as explicit dispense+merge chains from fresh manifest entries to avoid split-array and
  stranded-variable compiler errors. The 4x diluent (3 units) is accumulated by chaining
  two additional PBS+Pluronic merge steps.

Magnetic wash note:
  The rubellaMagneticSeparation detect variables (sep*, csep*) produced during wash cycles
  are intermediate separation signals; they are not used as QC gate inputs and are not
  explicitly disposed. The lilott8/BioScript compiler accepts unconsumed detect intermediates
  without error. If future compiler versions flag these, add dispose statements for each
  sep* variable after the final wash in each concentration arm.

drain normalization:
  Both if/else gate false branches use drain in .bs source. The compiler normalizes drain
  to dispose in the IR output -- expected behavior consistent with all prior encoded protocols.

Manifest entries (13):
  PBS, HRP_Standard, SuperSignalSubstrate, WashBuffer, ParamagneticParticles,
  RubellaIgG_Std_0, RubellaIgG_Std_156, RubellaIgG_Std_3125, HRP_Conjugate,
  H2O2_Solution, LuminolEnhancer, PluronicL64

  Note: Three separate manifest entries encode the three IgG concentrations because
  BioScript dispense must reference a named manifest entry; concentration cannot be
  parameterized at runtime.
