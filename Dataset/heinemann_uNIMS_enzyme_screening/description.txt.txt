PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : On-chip Integration of Droplet Microfluidics and Nanostructure-Initiator
               Mass Spectrometry for Enzyme Screening
Source Link  : https://ipo.lbl.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2017/05/2016-049_pub.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/c6lc01182a
Journal      : Lab on a Chip, 2016 (Royal Society of Chemistry)
Authors      : Joshua Heinemann, Kai Deng, Steve C. C. Shih, Jian Gao, Paul D. Adams,
               Anup K. Singh, Trent R. Northen
Platform     : uNIMS (digital droplet microfluidics + NIMS array, EWOD-actuated)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the uNIMS platform -- a first-of-its-kind integration of droplet
microfluidics with nanostructure-initiator mass spectrometry (NIMS) for label-free,
time-resolved enzyme kinetics screening at the 150 nL droplet scale. The system couples
a digital microfluidics (DMF) chip (electrowetting-on-dielectric, EWOD) with a patterned
silicon NIMS array (640 pads, 5x5 cm wafer), enabling programmable droplet deposition
onto discrete NIMS elements for subsequent mass spectrometry (MS) imaging.

The protocol is divided into two functional parts:
  PART 1: Dextromethorphan (DXM) standard validation -- confirms droplet actuation
          onto NIMS pads and analyte sorption at m/z 272.
  PART 2: CelE-CBM3a enzyme kinetics -- two independent pH conditions:
    2A: pH 6.0 (phosphate buffer, 50 mM) -- parallel droplet loading, 5 time points
        (10, 20, 30, 40, 50 min), G4 substrate degraded to G3, G2, G1.
    2B: pH 5.0 (acetate buffer, 100 mM) -- continuous series loading, 3 time points
        (10, 20, 40 min), similar product distribution to pH 6 demonstrating CelE
        robustness to process conditions.

Key advantages over conventional NIMS screening:
  - 150 nL droplets vs 20 uL dead volume from 384-well acoustic printing (~133x reagent
    reduction)
  - Programmable time resolution for kinetics (droplets deposited and removed at defined
    intervals via EWOD)
  - Fluorous probe and products remain adsorbed on NIMS surface after droplet removal,
    enabling off-line MS imaging
  - Scalable: demonstrated 20 assays per NIMS array; protocol modification enables 620
    individual assays (1 pad per enzyme); theoretical maximum >100,000 pads per 5 cm2
    at 50 um grid resolution


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
The uNIMS stack consists of five layers held together in a 3D-printed chassis:

Layer i   : 3D-printed PLA top cover (Ultimaker 2, 215 C, Blender.org design)
Layer ii  : PDMS mounting gasket for PEEK tubing interfacing (upper gasket)
Layer iii : DMF chip -- glass substrate; 128 chrome electrodes (5 cm L x 500 um W x
            375 um H); 5 um SU-8-5 dielectric; PDMS fluidics channel (500 um W x 225 um H);
            31 digital arrayers for droplet actuation over NIMS pads;
            1 T-junction for pressure-driven droplet generation (~150 nL droplets)
Layer iv  : NIMS array chip -- 5 x 5 cm silicon wafer; 640 patterned NIMS pads
            (photopatternable PDMS protective gasket, laser-cut wafer, ICP-RIE etching)
Layer v   : 3D-printed PETG bottom piece with Dropbot PCB adapter (pogo pins)

Seal      : Reversible PDMS compression seal between DMF chip (layer iii) and NIMS array
            (layer iv) -- allows NIMS array removal and mounting in mass spectrometer
            after experiment, without disturbing adsorbed analytes.

DMF chip fabrication:
  - Photolithography on chrome Telic wafers (AZ1500 positive resist, OAI Series 200 aligner,
    16 mW/cm2, 15 s exposure; MF-321 developer ~1 min)
  - Chrome etch: CR-4 chromium etchant, 5 min agitation
  - Post-bake: 120 C, 10 min
  - Plasma treatment: 20% O2, 20 W RF, 5 min
  - Dielectric: 5 um SU-8-5 (Microchem protocol)
  - Channel layer: SU-8-2075 master mold (140 um height); PDMS (20:1, cured 100 C 1 h);
    sealed against glass slide with 100 uL Picoglide, rinsed with Novec HFE-7500

NIMS array fabrication:
  - Photopatternable PDMS (10:1 ratio, 3% benzophenone, degassed by centrifugation)
  - Spin-coat on silicon wafer: 2500 rpm, 30 s
  - UV exposure: <365 nm, 10 min; photomask ~100 um above substrate
  - Post-exposure bake: 120 C, 2.5 min (vacuum oven)
  - Development: toluene, 10 s; rinse IPA and H2O
  - ICP-RIE etching (PlasmaLab 100, Oxford Instrument):
      Gas: SF6/O2 at 30/20 ratio; pressure: 6 mTorr; temperature: -80 C (cryogenic)
      Etching chamber power: 5 W; plasma generator power: 1000 W
  - Result: silicon nanostructures for NIMS-based laser desorption/ionisation

Dropbot control:
  - Hold voltage: 20 V (maintains droplet position during flow halt)
  - Actuation: 90 V, 10 kHz, 250 ms bursts (moves droplets in/out of NIMS pad pockets)
  - Impedance detection: used to detect actuation failure
  - Software: Dropbot + microdrop GUI; monitoring and validation visual (limitation noted)


REAGENTS
--------

Carrier oil      : Novec HFE-7500 (3M, US); 100 uL Picoglide (Dolomite Microfluidics, UK)
                   pumped through channel for 30 min for surface preparation.
                   Flow rates: 0.05 uL/s reaction cocktail, 0.2 uL/s HFE-7500 oil.

Enzyme           : CelE-CBM3a (CelE); chimeric endoglucanase; GH family 5 (GH5) domain
                   from Clostridium thermocellum (Cthe_0797) fused to carbohydrate binding
                   module CBM3a. Deconstructs plant hemicellulose via beta-1,4 glycosidic
                   bond cleavage. Concentration: 240 ug/mL. (Ref: Deng et al., ACS Chem.
                   Biol. 2014, 9(7), 1470-1479)

Substrate probe  : 1,4-beta-D-cellotetraose-probe (G4). Fluorous-tagged (perfluorocarbon)
                   for NIMS surface affinity. MW 1515.48; formula C55H78F17N5O24.
                   Detected at m/z 1516.43 (H+). Volume: 1 uL per reaction.

Degradation      :
products           G3 (cellotriose-probe)  -- MW 1353.42; C49H68F17N5O19; m/z 1354.37 (H+)
                   G2 (cellobiose-probe)   -- MW 1191.37; C43H58F17N5O14; m/z 1192.32 (H+)
                   G1 (glucose-probe)      -- MW 1029.32; C37H48F17N5O9;  m/z 1030.28 (H+)

Buffers          : pH 6: Phosphate buffer, 50 mM, pH 6.0 (4 uL per 6 uL reaction)
                   pH 5: Acetate buffer, 100 mM, pH 5.0 (4 uL per 6 uL reaction)

Validation std   : Dextromethorphan (DXM); 1 mg/mL in H2O; detected at m/z 272 (H+);
                   matrix-free ionisation; no signal on non-NIMS surrounding structures.

MALDI matrix     : Universal MALDI matrix; 20 mg/mL in MeOH; applied to NIMS array after
                   removal from uNIMS system prior to MS imaging.

General reagents : SU-8-5, SU-8-2075, SU-8 Developer (Microchem, Newton, MA); gold and
                   chromium-coated glass slides (Telic, Valencia, CA); ITO-coated glass slides
                   and silicon wafers (Delta Technologies, Stillwater, MN); Aquapel (TCP Global,
                   San Diego, CA); MF-321 developer (Rohm and Haas); CR-4 chromium etchant
                   (OM Group); AZ-300 T photoresist stripper (AZ Electronic Materials);
                   Sylgard 184 PDMS (Dow Corning); benzophenone and mixed xylenes (Sigma);
                   TPS (Trichloro(1H,1H,2H-perfluorooctyl)silane) for master mold silanisation.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

-- PART 1: DXM STANDARD VALIDATION --

Step 1 | Device Surface Preparation and Oil Phase Loading
  Reagent  : Novec HFE-7500 (with 100 uL Picoglide surfactant)
  Operation: Dispense oilPhase; pump through PDMS channel for 30 min
  Purpose  : Coats channel surfaces; stabilises droplets at T-junction; enables EWOD
             actuation; primes reversible PDMS seal interface with NIMS array.

Step 2 | Dextromethorphan Standard Preparation
  Reagent  : DXM, 1 mg/mL in H2O; 5 uL plug prefilled into syringe tubing
  Operation: Dispense dxmDroplet (1 unit = ~150 nL droplet abstraction)
  Purpose  : Validates microfluidic droplet actuation and NIMS pad analyte sorption before
             enzymatic assay. DXM detected at m/z 272 (H+); matrix-free ionisation.

Step 3 | DXM Droplet Generation at T-Junction
  Inputs   : dxmDroplet + oilPhase
  Operation: mix for 5 s (abstracts T-junction droplet generation)
  Flow rate: 0.05 uL/s reaction cocktail; 0.2 uL/s HFE-7500 oil
  Result   : ~150 nL DXM droplets in HFE-7500 oil train; synchronised with electrode
             actuation for uniform spacing along central fluidics chamber.

Step 4 | DXM Droplet Loading and Incubation on NIMS Pads
  Input    : dxmDropletInOil
  Operation: heat at 25 C for 10 m (600 s; room temperature)
  Actuation: Flow halted; 20 V electrode hold. 31 arrayers actuate droplets at 90 V,
             10 kHz, 250 ms bursts into NIMS pad pockets.
  Incubate : DXM sorbs onto NIMS surface during contact. Fluorous-tagged substrates
             confirmed to remain adsorbed in microfluidic environment (consistent with
             traditional NIMzyme execution).
  Eject    : Droplets ejected from pocket after incubation; evacuated via central chamber.

Step 5 | MALDI Matrix Coating
  Inputs   : dxmDropletInOil + UniversalMALDI_Matrix
  Operation: mix for 1 m (60 s; abstracts matrix co-crystallisation)
  Procedure: NIMS array physically removed from uNIMS system after experiment completion.
             Universal MALDI matrix (20 mg/mL MeOH) applied to array surface.
             Array mounted in ABSciex 5800 MALDI-TOF for MS imaging.

Step 6 | MS Imaging QC -- DXM Deposition Detection (Branch Gate 1)
  Input    : dxmNimsArray
  Module   : nimsImagingDetection
  Duration : 30 s
  MS scan  : ABSciex 5800 MALDI-TOF; positive mode; laser intensity 4150; focus m/z 1200;
             50 um laser step; m/z 272 ion map; OpenMSI spot set analysis.
  Branch:
    Pass (depositionSignal > 0)  --> BB2: dispose(dxmNimsArray); proceed to pH 6 kinetics
    Fail (depositionSignal <= 0) --> BB3: drain(dxmNimsArray); no sorption (surfactant
                                         suppression or actuation failure)
  Join: BB4 (validationJoin) -- dispose(depositionSignal); pH 6 kinetics branch begins.


-- PART 2A: CelE KINETICS AT pH 6.0 (PHOSPHATE BUFFER) --

Step 7 | Reaction Cocktail Preparation (pH 6)
  Buffer   : Phosphate buffer, 50 mM, pH 6.0 (4 uL)
  Substrate: G4 substrate-probe (1 uL)
  Enzyme   : CelE-CBM3a, 240 ug/mL (1 uL)
  Total    : 6 uL; premixed off-chip in 0.5 mL eppendorf tube
  Operation: 4 buffer units dispensed individually, chained via sequential mix in BioScript
             (ph6_buf_12, ph6_buf_34, ph6_bufBulk); substrate and enzyme then merged.
             [Compiler constraint: explicit sequential merging required; no multi-unit
             dispense; same pattern as b1/b2/b3 in Moazami et al. reference.]
  Loading  : 5 uL plug drawn into syringe tubing and injected onto uNIMS.

Step 8 | Reaction Droplet Generation at T-Junction (pH 6)
  Inputs   : ph6_reactionCocktail + HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix for 5 s
  Flow rate: 0.05 uL/s cocktail; 0.2 uL/s HFE-7500
  Loading  : Parallel mode (Fig. 4A) -- flow halted after chamber fills; all 31 arrayer
             electrodes actuated simultaneously; droplets loaded in parallel onto all
             corresponding NIMS pads.

Step 9 | Sequential Incubation on NIMS Pads -- 5 Time Points (pH 6)
  Input    : ph6_dropletsInOil
  Operation: 5 sequential heat blocks at 25 C (room temperature)
  Time pts : 10 min (600 s) -- cumulative: 10 min
             10 min (600 s) -- cumulative: 20 min
             10 min (600 s) -- cumulative: 30 min
             10 min (600 s) -- cumulative: 40 min
             10 min (600 s) -- cumulative: 50 min
  Pads     : 6 pads per time point; 31 total pads used
  Ejection : At each 10 min interval, 6 droplets synchronously ejected from NIMS pocket
             into central chamber and evacuated. Fresh droplets actuated for next interval.
  Products : G4 (m/z 1516.43) degraded to G3 (m/z 1354.37), G2 (m/z 1192.32),
             G1 (m/z 1030.28) over 50 min. Probe and products remain adsorbed on NIMS
             surface after droplet removal.

Step 10 | MALDI Matrix Coating and MS Imaging (pH 6) (Branch Gate 2)
  Inputs   : ph6_dropletsInOil + UniversalMALDI_Matrix
  Mix      : 1 m (60 s); array coated after removal from uNIMS system
  Detect   : nimsImagingDetection; 30 s; same MS parameters as Step 6
  Analysis : OpenMSI spot set analysis; fractional conversion:
             [G1] / ([G4] + [G3] + [G2] + [G1]) for each time point pad.
  Note     : MS imaging required (not simple spot analysis) because HFE-7500 surfactant
             adheres to NIMS surface and suppresses ionisation; surfactant causes
             heterogeneous ions at high concentration, reducing sensitivity.
             NIMS surface acts as capture substrate for substrates and products.
  Branch:
    Pass (ph6Signal > 0)  --> BB5: dispose(ph6_nimsArray); G3/G2/G1 detected; proceed to pH 5
    Fail (ph6Signal <= 0) --> BB6: drain(ph6_nimsArray); no product signal
  Join: BB7 (pH6Join) -- dispose(ph6Signal); pH 5 kinetics branch begins.


-- PART 2B: CelE KINETICS AT pH 5.0 (ACETATE BUFFER) --

Step 11 | Reaction Cocktail Preparation (pH 5)
  Buffer   : Acetate buffer, 100 mM, pH 5.0 (4 uL)
  Substrate: G4 substrate-probe (1 uL)
  Enzyme   : CelE-CBM3a, 240 ug/mL (1 uL)
  Total    : 6 uL; premixed off-chip in 0.5 mL eppendorf tube
  Operation: Identical chaining pattern to pH 6 (ph5_buf_12, ph5_buf_34, ph5_bufBulk);
             acetate buffer replaces phosphate buffer.

Step 12 | Reaction Droplet Generation at T-Junction (pH 5)
  Inputs   : ph5_reactionCocktail + HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix for 5 s
  Flow rate: 0.05 uL/s cocktail; 0.2 uL/s HFE-7500
  Loading  : Continuous series mode (Fig. 4B) -- droplets removed directly from flow without
             halting; successive pads occupied and cleared continuously.

Step 13 | Sequential Incubation on NIMS Pads -- 3 Time Points (pH 5)
  Input    : ph5_dropletsInOil
  Operation: 3 sequential heat blocks at 25 C (room temperature)
  Time pts : 10 min (600 s)  -- cumulative: 10 min
             10 min (600 s)  -- cumulative: 20 min
             20 min (1200 s) -- cumulative: 40 min
  Note     : Three time points vs five for pH 6; final heat block is 20 m (600 s gap
             between 20 min and 40 min time points). CelE produces similar product
             distributions at pH 5 and pH 6, demonstrating enzyme robustness to process
             conditions (reaction profile consistent with Deng, Front. Bioeng. Biotechnol. 2015).

Step 14 | MALDI Matrix Coating and MS Imaging (pH 5) (Branch Gate 3 + Terminal)
  Inputs   : ph5_dropletsInOil + UniversalMALDI_Matrix
  Mix      : 1 m (60 s); NIMS array removed and matrix-coated as before
  Detect   : nimsImagingDetection; 30 s; same MS parameters
  Branch:
    Pass (ph5Signal > 0)  --> BB8: dispose(ph5_nimsArray); kinetics confirmed
    Fail (ph5Signal <= 0) --> BB9: drain(ph5_nimsArray); no product signal
  Terminal : BB10 -- dispose(ph5Signal); NOP. Experiment ends.
             NIMS array placed in ABSciex 5800 MALDI-TOF for final MS imaging session.


QUANTIFICATION
--------------
Method   : Fractional conversion (internal normalisation)
Formula  : [Gx] / ([G4] + [G3] + [G2] + [G1]) for each product Gx at each time point
Rationale: Internal normalisation eliminates sensitivity to droplet volume variation
           (device maintains +/-10% SD in droplet size; turbulent mixing at t-junction
           causes initially larger droplets). Less sensitive to concentration measurement
           errors than direct quantification approaches.
Software : OpenMSI spot set analysis tools (Rubel et al., Anal. Chem. 2013, 85(21),
           10354-10361)


MASS SPECTROMETRY
-----------------
Instrument  : ABSciex 5800 MALDI-TOF
Mode        : Positive ionisation
Laser int.  : 4150
Focus m/z   : 1200
Laser step  : 50 um
Matrix      : Universal MALDI matrix, 20 mg/mL in MeOH
Analysis    : OpenMSI spot set analysis
