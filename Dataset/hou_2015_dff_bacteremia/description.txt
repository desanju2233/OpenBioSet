PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Direct Detection and Drug-Resistance Profiling of Bacteremias Using Inertial Microfluidics
Source Link  : https://pubs.rsc.org/lc/article/15/10/2297/1246161/Direct-detection-and-drug-resistance-profiling-of
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/c5lc00311c
Journal      : Lab on a Chip, 2015, Vol. 15, pp. 2297-2307
Platform     : Inertial microfluidics (Dean Flow Fractionation) + NanoString hybridization-based RNA detection


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a method for rapid isolation of bacteria from whole blood using a PDMS
spiral microchannel device operating on the principle of Dean Flow Fractionation (DFF), coupled
to hybridization-based RNA detection (NanoString nCounter) for direct pathogen identification
and antibiotic susceptibility determination without bacterial culture or enzymatic amplification.

The protocol encodes two sequential diagnostic workflows:

  Workflow 1 | Species identification via rRNA detection:
    Bacteria (~100 cfu/mL) isolated from 3x-diluted whole blood by DFF; rRNA probes targeting
    species-variable regions of 16S and 23S rRNA identify E. coli, K. pneumoniae, and
    P. aeruginosa from concentrated bacterial eluate. Sensitivity approaches clinically relevant
    uncultured bacteremia loads without PCR or enzymatic amplification.

  Workflow 2 | Antibiotic susceptibility via mRNA detection:
    E. coli clinical isolates at ~10^5 cfu/mL (direct spike or 7.5 h BACTEC pre-enrichment
    from 1 cfu/mL) processed by DFF; recovered bacteria exposed to ciprofloxacin (2.5 mcg/mL,
    37 C, 30 min); mRNA transcriptional response profiled by NanoString. Transcriptional
    Susceptibility Score discriminates susceptible (score ~0) from resistant (score ~1) strains.

Total workflow time: ~8 hours including DFF separation, RNA hybridization, and ciprofloxacin
exposure. This represents a >6-fold acceleration over standard culture-based methods (48-72 h).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Material  : Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS) cast from silanized silicon wafer master; bonded
            irreversibly to glass slide by air plasma (Harrick Plasma Cleaner).
Channel   : Spiral geometry; 500 um (w) x 80 um (h); total length ~10 cm.
            Height fixed at 80 um so that blood cells (ap/h > 0.07) experience inertial
            lift (F_L) while bacteria (<1 um, ap/h << 0.07) are governed by Dean drag (F_D) only.

Inlets:
  Outer inlet (75 um wide)  : 3x diluted whole blood sample (~15% hematocrit)
  Inner inlet (425 um wide) : PBS + 0.1% BSA sheath fluid at ~10x sample flow rate

Outlets:
  Bacterial outlet (200 um) : Bacteria and platelets (Dean-migrated to outer wall, DC1 complete)
  Waste outlet              : RBCs and leukocytes (inertially focused at inner wall)

Separation principle:
  Dean drag (F_D) migrates all particles toward the inner wall. RBCs (~6-8 um) and leukocytes
  (~7-12 um) satisfy ap/h > 0.07 and are arrested at the inner wall by inertial lift forces (F_L).
  Bacteria (<1 um) and platelets (~2-3 um) are unaffected by F_L; they complete one full Dean
  Cycle (DC1), recirculating from inner to outer wall, and exit at the bacterial outlet.
  Separation is label-free, species-independent, and based solely on physical size.

Equipment:
  Syringe pumps : PHD 2000 (Harvard Apparatus) x2 (separate pumps for sample and sheath)
  Microscope    : Olympus IX71 inverted phase contrast + Hamamatsu CCD camera
  High-speed camera : Phantom v9 (Vision Research) for blood cell imaging
  Flow cytometer : BD Accuri C6 (BD Biosciences) for separation efficiency quantification
  Centrifuge    : 16,000 x g for bacterial eluate pelleting


BACTERIAL STRAINS AND CULTURE
------------------------------
- E. coli (clinical isolate)       : LB broth, mid-log phase
- Klebsiella pneumoniae            : LB broth, mid-log phase
- Pseudomonas aeruginosa           : LB broth, mid-log phase
- Staphylococcus aureus            : LB broth, mid-log phase
- Enterococcus faecalis            : Todd-Hewitt Broth (THB), mid-log phase

All organisms cultured to mid-log phase; diluted to target concentration based on OD measurements.
Concentration verified by plating serial dilutions for cfu counting. Bacteria spiked into whole
blood immediately before loading onto the DFF device.

Blood culture route (mRNA workflow only):
  BACTEC blood culture bottle inoculated at 1 cfu/mL of E. coli; incubated at 37 C for 7.5 hours
  to reach >10^5 cfu/mL (verified by plating). Cultured blood diluted 1:5 (v/v) with culture
  media before loading.


REAGENTS
--------
Sample buffer       : 1x PBS + 0.1% BSA (Miltenyi Biotec); prevents non-specific adsorption
Sheath fluid        : 1x PBS + 0.1% BSA
Lysis buffer        : RLT (Qiagen) + beta-mercaptoethanol (4x excess); 5 min RT incubation
Ciprofloxacin stock : 2.5 mcg/mL working concentration (fluoroquinolone; inhibits DNA gyrase)
Blood anticoagulant : EDTA (fresh human whole blood; Research Blood Components, Brighton MA)
Blood culture bottle: BACTEC (identical to clinical microbiology labs)
Immunofluorescence  : CD41a-FITC 1:50 (platelets); CD45-APC 1:100 (leukocytes); BD Biosciences
RBC lysis buffer    : eBioscience (for leukocyte imaging experiments only)
E. coli bioparticles: Alexa Fluor 488 E. coli K-12 BioParticles conjugates (Invitrogen);
                      used for device characterization and separation efficiency measurement


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Blood Sample Preparation
  Reagents    : WholeBlood, PBS_BSA_Buffer, EcoliClinicalIsolate
  Operation   : Dilute whole blood 1:3 (v/v) with PBS + 0.1% BSA to reach ~15% hematocrit.
                Spike bacteria at target concentration immediately before loading.
  rRNA workflow bacteria concentration  : ~100 cfu/mL (3 mL blood processed)
  mRNA workflow bacteria concentration  : ~10^5 cfu/mL (direct spike or blood culture route)
  Blood culture route                   : BACTEC bottle, 1 cfu/mL, 37 C, 7.5 h (450 min)
  Notes       : BSA at 0.1% prevents non-specific adsorption to tubing and PDMS walls.
                Bacteria spiked into diluted blood immediately before device loading.

Step 2 | Device Priming
  Reagents    : SheathFluid (PBS + 0.1% BSA)
  Operation   : Sheath fluid loaded into 60 mL syringe; pumped through inner inlet (425 um)
                at ~10x the sample flow rate to form tight sample stream at outer wall.
  Purpose     : Pins sample stream near outer wall; removes air bubbles; conditions channel walls.

Step 3 | Dean Flow Fractionation Separation
  Reagents    : Diluted spiked blood (outer inlet), SheathFluid (inner inlet)
  Operation   : mix (continuous-flow spiral microchannel separation; abstracted from DMF mixing)
  Total flow rate             : 1700 uL/min
  Sample flow rate            : ~150 uL/min
  Processing time             : ~20 min per mL whole blood (at 45% starting hematocrit)
  Channel height              : 80 um (governs inertial focusing threshold ap/h > 0.07)
  Dean Cycle                  : DC1 (bacteria complete full recirculation to outer wall)
  Bacterial recovery          : >65% across 10^2 to 10^4 cfu/mL
  Platelet recovery at outlet : ~40% (larger size slows Dean migration)
  Leukocyte removal           : Focused inertially at inner wall; exits waste outlet
  Notes       : Separation is label-free and species-independent. Bacteria are recovered
                in PBS+BSA sheath fluid, removing cellular inhibitors of downstream assays.

Step 4 | Bacterial Eluate Concentration
  Operation   : Centrifuge eluate at 16,000 x g; remove supernatant; resuspend pellet in
                50 uL 1x PBS. Modelled as heat at 25 C for 5 min in BioScript.
  Purpose     : Concentrates bacteria ~20-fold for sufficient input to RNA detection assay.

Step 5 | Bacterial Recovery QC
  Module      : bacterialRecoveryQC (abstracts cfu enumeration on LB agar)
  Duration    : 30 s detection
  Expected    : >65% recovery
  Branch      :
    Pass (recoverySignal > 0)  -> BB2: dispose bacterialEluate; proceed to lysis
    Fail (recoverySignal <= 0) -> BB3: drain bacterialEluate; abort

Step 6 | Bacterial Lysis for RNA Extraction
  BB          : BB4 (recovery join)
  Reagents    : bacterialPellet (resuspended in 50 uL PBS), RLT_Lysis_Buffer + BME
  Operation   : Vigorous vortex with 4x excess RLT+BME; 5 min at room temperature (25 C).
  Storage     : Use immediately or store at -80 C.
  Notes       : RLT denatures proteins and inactivates RNases. No RNA purification step;
                crude lysate used directly as NanoString input.

Step 7 | NanoString rRNA Hybridization for Species Identification
  BB          : BB4 (continued)
  Target RNA  : 16S and 23S ribosomal RNA (rRNA)
  Advantage   : rRNA present at >1000-fold excess over most abundant mRNA; enables detection
                of ~100 bacteria/mL without enzymatic amplification.
  Probeset    : 13 discriminatory probes total
                  3 probes specific to E. coli (Ec)
                  1 probe specific to K. pneumoniae (Kp)
                  5 probes specific to P. aeruginosa (PsA)
                  4 probes shared between Ec and Kp (not PsA)
                Probes designed to species-variable regions of 16S/23S by ClustalOmega MSA.
  Hybridization : 65 C for 2 h (shortened from standard protocol with minimal signal loss)
  Readout     : NanoString PrepStation + nCounter Digital Analyzer; nSolver software normalization
  Organism Score: Geometric mean of species-specific probe counts / sum of all geometric means
  Sensitivity : ~100 cfu/mL from 3 mL processed whole blood
  Module      : rRNASpeciesDetection
  Duration    : 30 s detection
  Note        : Unprocessed whole blood gives uninterpretable hybridization due to blood
                product interference. DFF pre-processing is required for valid rRNA signal.
  Branch      :
    Pass (rRNASignal > 0)  -> BB5: dispose bacterialLysate; proceed to mRNA workflow
    Fail (rRNASignal <= 0) -> BB6: drain bacterialLysate

Step 8 | Susceptibility Sample Preparation and Blood Culture Enrichment
  BB          : BB7 (rRNA join)
  Reagents    : EcoliClinicalIsolate_Susceptible, EcoliClinicalIsolate_Resistant,
                WholeBlood, PBS_BSA_Buffer
  Route A (direct spike): Spike at 10^5 cfu/mL into 1 mL whole blood; process immediately.
  Route B (blood culture): BACTEC bottle at 1 cfu/mL; incubate 37 C for 7.5 h (450 min,
                27000 s) to reach >10^5 cfu/mL; dilute 1:5 with culture media before DFF.
  Notes       : Both susceptible and resistant isolates processed identically. Classification
                resolved downstream by Transcriptional Susceptibility Score.
                mRNA detection requires ~10^5 cfu/mL; blood culture bridging step needed
                for typical bacteremia loads (~10-100 cfu/mL).

Step 9 | DFF Separation for Susceptibility Workflow
  Operation   : Same spiral device, same flow conditions (1700 uL/min, 20 min/mL).
  Inputs      : spikedBloodS, spikedBloodR, SheathFluid
  Post-separation: Concentrate eluate by centrifugation at 16,000 x g; resuspend in 50 uL PBS.
                   Modelled as heat at 25 C for 5 min.
  Notes       : Both susceptible and resistant arms processed in parallel.

Step 10 | Ciprofloxacin Exposure
  Reagents    : eluateS, eluateR, Ciprofloxacin_Stock
  Concentration: 2.5 mcg/mL ciprofloxacin
  Temperature : 37 C
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s)
  Mechanism   : Susceptible organisms upregulate 6 stress-response mRNA transcripts in
                response to DNA gyrase inhibition. Resistant organisms do not show equivalent
                induction at this concentration.
  Notes       : 30 min sufficient for detectable transcriptional differential without
                significant bacterial growth. Susceptible and resistant isolates exposed
                identically; classification is by transcriptional outcome, not treatment.

Step 11 | Lysis and NanoString mRNA Hybridization for Susceptibility Determination
  BB          : BB7 (continued)
  Reagents    : ciproExposure_S, ciproExposure_R, RLT_Lysis_Buffer
  Lysis       : 4x RLT+BME, vortex, 5 min at 25 C (modelled as mix + heat at 25 C for 5 min)
  Target RNA  : mRNA (6 ciprofloxacin-responsive transcripts + 3 non-responsive controls)
  Normalization: Responsive transcripts normalized to geometric mean of control transcript
                 expression from same lysate and same hybridization assay. Controls for
                 cell number variation between samples.
  Transcriptional Susceptibility Score:
    Derivation set of clinical E. coli strains (susceptible and resistant) defines score
    anchors: 0 = centroid of susceptible strains; 1 = centroid of resistant strains.
    Unknown samples projected onto vector between centroids in transcriptional space.
    Threshold: dashed line = 4 SD from susceptible derivation cohort mean.
  Hybridization : 65 C for 2 h
  Sensitivity   : ~10^5 cfu/mL (mRNA less abundant than rRNA; >1 order of magnitude better
                  than VITEK-2 threshold of >10^6 cfu/mL)
  Module      : mRNASusceptibilityDetection
  Duration    : 30 s detection
  Branch      :
    Pass (susceptibilitySignal_S <= 0) -> BB8: dispose lysate_S and lysate_R
                                          Interpretation: susceptible organism
    Fail (susceptibilitySignal_S > 0)  -> BB9: drain lysate_S and lysate_R
                                          Interpretation: resistant organism

Step 12 | Final Cleanup
  BB          : BB10 (terminal)
  Operation   : dispose(susceptibilitySignal_S); dispose(susceptibilitySignal_R); NOP
  Experiment ends.


PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
-------------------
Total workflow time       : ~8 h (vs 48-72 h for standard culture-based methods)
Bacterial recovery        : >65% (vs ~30% by low-speed centrifugation)
rRNA sensitivity          : ~100 cfu/mL (from 3 mL blood; no amplification)
mRNA sensitivity          : ~10^5 cfu/mL (>1 order of magnitude better than VITEK-2)
Species identified        : E. coli, K. pneumoniae, P. aeruginosa (simultaneous or sequential)
Mixed infection detection : All 3 pairwise combinations of pathogens detected at 1000 cfu/mL
Susceptibility metric     : Transcriptional Susceptibility Score 0 (susceptible) to 1 (resistant)
Clinical threshold (VITEK-2): >10^6 cfu/mL; this method operates at >10^5 cfu/mL
Clinical threshold (BACTEC): ~10^8 cfu/mL for positive culture detection

Abstraction notes:
  - `mix` steps abstract continuous-flow spiral DFF separation events, not DMF electrode mixing.
    This is consistent with the samlali reference file's treatment of hydrodynamic trapping events.
  - Blood dilution (1:3) encoded as two explicit dispense + chained mix steps to avoid split
    arrays and compiler errors (no array indexing used).
  - Susceptibility gate uses signal <= 0 for susceptible (score near 0) and > 0 for resistant
    (score near 1), mapping the paper's Transcriptional Susceptibility Score to BioScript's
    integer comparison semantics.
  - Both susceptible and resistant isolate arms encoded as scalar variables; processed in
    sequence within BB7 join block.
