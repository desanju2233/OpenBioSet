PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Microfluidics Approach Towards High-Throughput Pathogen Removal from Blood Using Margination
Source Link  : https://pubs.aip.org/aip/bmf/article/6/2/024115/923894
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4710992
Journal      : Biomicrofluidics, 2012, Vol. 6, Article 024115
Platform     : Pressure-driven continuous-flow PDMS microfluidic device; cascaded straight margination channels with bifurcation outlets


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a label-free, high-throughput microfluidic blood filtration technique for
non-specific removal of blood-borne pathogens and inflammatory cellular components from whole blood,
inspired by the in vivo microcirculatory phenomenon of leukocyte margination. The device exploits
the Fahreaus effect: as whole blood flows through narrow 20x20 um microchannels, deformable red
blood cells (RBCs) migrate axially to the channel centre under Poiseuille flow, forming an RBC-rich
core and a cell-free layer adjacent to the channel walls. Mechanical collisions between migrating
RBCs and other cellular components (bacteria, yeast, platelets, and leukocytes) drive their radial
displacement into the cell-free wall region -- a phenomenon termed margination.

At the downstream bifurcation (1:8:1 ratio), marginated cells are skimmed off via narrow side
channels (waste) while RBC-enriched, pathogen-depleted blood exits the large centre outlet. Two
margination channels are cascaded in series: the bacteria-depleted blood from stage 1 enters stage 2
with higher hematocrit, amplifying Fahreaus effect and RBC-bacteria hydrodynamic interactions, and
achieving overall bacterial removal efficiency greater than 80% in a single pass.

The technique requires no sample preparation, no magnetic labelling, and no sheath flow. Whole blood
can be drawn directly from patients and pumped into the device, making it suitable for a blood
dialysis circuit concept for adjunctive sepsis treatment.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Fabrication : PDMS (Sylgard 184, Dow Corning); standard soft lithography; silicon master patterned
              by deep reactive ion etching (DRIE); PDMS 10:1 prepolymer:curing agent, cured 70 C
              for 2 h; bonded to glass slides by air plasma (Harrick Plasma Cleaner); left 2 h at
              70 C to complete bonding; 1.5 mm holes punched for inlets and outlets.
Silanization: Trichloro(1H,1H,2H,2H-perfluorooctyl)silane 1 h (silicon wafer and PDMS master).

Single-channel device:
  - First margination channel : 20 um (W) x 20 um (H) x 5 mm (L); aspect ratio = 1
  - Second margination channel: 20 um (W) x 20 um (H) x 1 mm (L); aspect ratio = 1
  - Bifurcations in series    : 1:8:1 (side:centre:side outlet ratio)
  - Expanded bifurcation      : channel expands to 200 um width to enhance cell-free layer
  - Side outlet width         : 20 um (skims leukocytes 8-15 um; flow ratio 1:4 side:centre)
  - Throughput                : 1 mL/h per channel at 15 uL/min

Parallel 6-channel device:
  - 6 cascaded-design channels in parallel with additional upstream filter region
  - Filter region removes clogs and debris before margination channels
  - Throughput: 6 mL/h (100 uL/min total); performance equivalent to single-channel device

Flow driver  : Syringe pump (NE-1000, New Era Pump Systems Inc.)
Imaging      : Olympus IX71 inverted phase contrast microscope + Hamamatsu C4742-80-12AG CCD;
               IPLAB software (video acquisition); ImageJ (analysis)
Confocal     : LSM 510 meta laser scanning confocal (Carl Zeiss) on Axiovert 100 M Inverted;
               488 nm excitation; 10x objective + 2.5x digital zoom; 1 um z-interval; 512x512 px


SEPARATION PRINCIPLE
--------------------
Aspect ratio : Low aspect ratio (height/width <= 1; 20x20 um square channels) gives isotropic
               shear distribution across the channel cross-section, closely resembling circular
               blood vessels. RBC axial migration distances are equal in all directions, forming
               a well-defined RBC core and enabling efficient margination to all four sidewalls.
               High aspect ratio channels (>1) produce blunt z-direction velocity profiles and
               uneven margination, trapping bacteria at the mid-plane.

Flow rate    : Bacteria margination efficiency increases with flow rate and plateaus above
               10 uL/min. At high flow rates (Re ~4-5), RBCs experience higher shear and
               inertia, accelerating axial migration and RBC-bacteria interactions. Inertial
               focusing is negligible in whole blood at Re << 50.

Cascaded design: Removing plasma and marginated bacteria at stage 1 bifurcation increases
               hematocrit of blood entering stage 2 (~10% increase). Higher hematocrit enhances
               platelet and bacteria diffusivity at the RBC core periphery and increases frequency
               of RBC-bacteria collisions, driving further margination in stage 2.

Yeast vs bacteria: S. cerevisiae (~5 um ovoid; elastic modulus ~1e8 Pa) is larger and stiffer
               than E. coli (~2x0.5 um rod; ~1e7 Pa). Larger size drives stronger margination
               towards all four channel corners, yielding higher removal (~90%) than bacteria (~80%).
               Rod-shaped bacteria also undergo additional tumbling/rotational motion that enhances
               lateral drift across streamlines.


CELL LINES AND PATHOGENS
-------------------------
E. coli      : Strain HB101 K-12; GFP-expressing (arabinose-inducible); cultured overnight at
               37 C in LB Agar + ampicillin 100 ug/mL + arabinose 0.1%; harvested and
               resuspended in 1x PBS before use. Spiked at 1e6-1e7 CFU/mL.
               For device characterisation: Alexa Fluor 488 E. coli K-12 BioParticles
               conjugates (Invitrogen) used for FACS analysis.

S. cerevisiae: Haploid W303-1B strain; YFP-expressing; gift from Dr. Jacquin Niles, MIT
               Department of Biological Engineering. Spiked at 1e6-1e7 per mL.
               Morphology (~5-7 um ovoid) representative of pathogenic Candida albicans.

Human blood  : Whole blood (Research Blood Components, Brighton MA).
               Device characterisation: washed 3x with PBS + 0.5% BSA; resuspended at 45%
               hematocrit in PBS + 0.5% BSA + 3% w/v Dextran-40.
               Whole-blood pathogen removal experiments: fresh unwashed blood used directly.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | RBC Sample Preparation (Device Characterisation)
  Reagents    : Whole blood, PBS + 0.5% v/v BSA (wash buffer),
                PBS + 0.5% v/v BSA + 3% w/v Dextran-40 (sample buffer)
  Operation   : Wash RBCs 3x with wash buffer (centrifuge 300 x g, 5 min each wash).
                Resuspend at 45% hematocrit in sample buffer.
  BioScript   : Centrifugation abstracted as heat at 25c (no centrifuge primitive).
                Each wash cycle: mix 5m + heat at 25c for 5m.
  Notes       : Dextran-40 prevents RBC sedimentation during syringe pump operation.
                For pathogen removal experiments using fresh whole blood, this step is skipped.

Step 2 | Antibody Staining for Cellular Analysis
  Reagents    : Fresh whole blood (unwashed), CD41a-FITC (1:50, BD Biosciences),
                CD45-APC (1:100, BD Biosciences)
  Operation   : Incubate blood with antibodies at 4 C for 40 min without prior washing.
  Purpose     : CD41a-FITC labels platelets; CD45-APC labels leukocytes.
                Staining in fresh blood preserves native cell populations for FACS analysis.

Step 3 | Microbial Spiking
  Reagents    : GFP-E. coli (resuspended in 1x PBS), YFP-S. cerevisiae
  Operation   : Add each microbe to antibody-stained blood at 1e6-1e7 per mL; vortex 30 s.
  Notes       : Both microbe types spiked simultaneously for combined whole-blood analysis.
                Separate experiments performed for E. coli vs S. cerevisiae to quantify
                removal efficiency by CFU counting and haemocytometer independently.

Step 4 | Stage 1 Margination (First Channel, 5 mm)
  Input       : Spiked whole blood loaded at inlet via syringe pump
  Flow rate   : 15 uL/min (Re ~4-5; wall shear rate ~6e4 s-1)
  Operation   : Continuous laminar flow through 20x20 um channel (5 mm). RBCs migrate
                axially to form RBC-rich core; bacteria, yeast, platelets, and leukocytes
                marginate to cell-free wall region via RBC mechanical collisions.
  Bifurcation : 1:8:1; channel expands to 200 um at bifurcation to enhance cell-free layer.
                Marginated cells collected in narrow side channels (20 um; drain/waste).
                RBC-enriched, pathogen-depleted blood exits large centre outlet (ratio 8).
  Stage 1 result: ~50% bacterial removal; hematocrit increases ~10% in centre outlet.
  BioScript   : mix abstracts laminar co-flow through margination channel;
                rbcSuspension supplies RBC hydrodynamic environment.

Step 5 | Stage 2 Margination (Cascaded Channel, 1 mm)
  Input       : Stage-1 filtered blood (higher hematocrit, reduced bacteria)
  Flow rate   : 15 uL/min (continuous from stage 1)
  Operation   : Bacteria-depleted blood undergoes second round of margination. Higher
                post-stage-1 hematocrit amplifies Fahreaus effect and RBC-bacteria
                collision frequency; additional bacteria driven to channel periphery.
  Bifurcation : 1:8:1; second waste stream drained; final filtered blood collected.
  Stage 2 result: cumulative >80% bacterial removal, >80% platelet and leukocyte depletion,
                ~90% S. cerevisiae removal, ~30% increase in RBC concentration at centre outlet.
  Haemolysis  : Negligible (optical absorption 414 nm); RBC transit time < 0.01 s in
                high-shear margination zone.

Step 6 | FACS Analysis of Filtered Blood
  Instrument  : BD LSR II flow cytometer (BD Biosciences)
  Gating      : FSC/SSC + fluorescence: CD41a-FITC (platelets), CD45-APC (leukocytes),
                GFP (E. coli), YFP (S. cerevisiae)
  Normalisation: Concentration at centre outlet / inlet sample concentration (%)
  QC gate     : cellularComponentDetection module; 30 s detect
                Pass (separationSignal > 0): >80% removal confirmed; dispose filtered blood
                Fail (separationSignal <= 0): insufficient removal; drain; re-optimise

Step 7 | Bacterial CFU Quantification
  Operation   : Plate 10 uL aliquots from inlet and centre outlet on LB agar.
                Incubate 24 h at 37 C. Count colony-forming units (CFUs).
  Removal efficiency = 1 - (CFU_outlet / CFU_inlet)
  Yeast count : Haemocytometer (independent of FACS/CFU pipeline).
  BioScript   : 24 h incubation = 1440 min (compiler duration constraint; IR: 86400.0 SECOND)
  QC gate     : bacterialColonyCount module; 30 s detect
                Pass (bacteriaSignal > 0): >80% bacterial removal confirmed; dispose
                Fail (bacteriaSignal <= 0): insufficient removal; drain; flag for re-run

Step 8 | Parallel Device Validation (6-Channel Scale-Up)
  Device      : 6 cascaded-design channels in parallel + upstream filter region
  Flow rate   : 100 uL/min total (6 ml/h)
  Result      : Similar platelet, leukocyte, and bacteria filtration performance to single channel
  Note        : Further parallelisation (100+ channels) or device stacking could approach
                conventional dialysis throughput (~1-2 L/h)


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : hou_margination_pathogen_removal_blood.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges
DAG            : hou_margination_pathogen_removal_blood_main_dag.dot

Duration conversions (s/m only; h not supported):
  40 min antibody staining -> 40m  (IR: 2400.0 SECOND)
  24 h agar incubation     -> 1440m (IR: 86400.0 SECOND)
  5 min wash cycles        -> 5m   (IR: 300.0 SECOND)

Platform note  : This is a pressure-driven continuous-flow device, not a DMF platform.
                 `mix` operations abstract laminar co-flow through the margination channel
                 (NOT electrode-driven droplet merging). `rbcSuspension` models the RBC
                 hydrodynamic environment required for margination physics.
                 `drain` and `dispose` both normalise to dispose() in IR output (expected
                 compiler behaviour); physical distinction is preserved in .bs source comments.
                 `heat at 25c` abstracts centrifugation and room-temperature operations.
                 `heat at 4c` abstracts cold/on-ice incubation steps.
                 `heat at 37c` abstracts bacterial culture incubation.

Modules        : bacterialMargination, yeastMargination,
                 cellularComponentDetection, bacterialColonyCount

IR audit note  : BB2 (bsbbif_2_t) true-branch jump to BB4 is implicit in IR (no explicit
                 jump opcode); BB3 (bsbbif_3_f) carries explicit jump -- standard compiler
                 behaviour, does not affect CFG correctness. BB5 and BB6 both dispose
                 platesMix (pass and fail paths identical for physical waste handling;
                 bacteriaSignal differentiates device validation status only).
