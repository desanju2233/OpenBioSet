PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Combinatorial Synthesis of Peptidomimetics Using Digital Microfluidics
Source Link  : https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1556/JFC-D-12-00012.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1556/JFC-D-12-00012
Journal      : J. Flow Chem., 2012, Vol. 2, No. 3, pp. 103-107
Authors      : Mais J. Jebrail, Naila Assem, Jared M. Mudrik, Michael D.M. Dryden,
               Kaixiang Lin, Andrei K. Yudin, Aaron R. Wheeler
Affiliation  : Department of Chemistry, University of Toronto,
               Toronto ON M5S-3H6, Canada
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) -- two-plate electrowetting-on-dielectric
               (EWOD) device with chromium bottom electrodes and ITO top plate


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first reported use of digital microfluidics (DMF) for
combinatorial chemical synthesis. The method performs thioacid-aziridine ligation of
peptidomimetics and their subsequent desulfurization entirely on-chip, with an integrated
magnetic separation step to remove Raney Nickel (RaNi) catalyst from reaction products.
The approach represents a greater than 1000-fold reduction in reagent consumption compared
to macroscale bench synthesis (microlitres vs millilitres) and requires no stir bars,
rotary evaporators, or traditional gloveboxes.

The protocol is divided into two functional parts:
  PART 1 (Steps 1-4, 16 total steps): Combinatorial thioacid-aziridine ligation
    producing 4 peptidomimetic products (A1-B1, A1-B2, A2-B1, A2-B2) under N2
    atmosphere at room temperature for 1 hour in methanol.
  PART 2 (Steps 5-8, 16 total steps): On-chip desulfurization using Raney Nickel
    catalyst at room temperature for 15 minutes, followed by magnetic immobilisation
    of RaNi and solvent evaporation to isolate 4 desulfurized analogues.

Total processing steps: 32 steps producing 8 products on a single DMF device.
Characterisation: ESI-MS (all 8 products, triplicate) and 1H NMR (desulfurized A1-B1,
compared to macroscale synthesis; spectra nearly identical).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration : Two-plate DMF device
Bottom plate  : Patterned chromium electrodes on glass
                - 88 square actuation electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm,
                  140 um peak-to-peak sinusoid interdigitated borders)
                - 10 reservoir electrodes (5 x 5 mm each)
Top plate     : Contiguous indium tin oxide (ITO) electrode
Spacer        : Two layers of double-sided tape, total thickness 180 um
Coating       : Hydrophobic insulator on both plates

Droplet volumes:
  Unit droplet   : ~900 nL
  Reservoir      : ~4.5 uL

Actuation:
  Voltage        : 70-100 V_rms
  Frequency      : 18 kHz (sine wave)
  Generator      : Agilent function generator + amplifier
  Mechanism      : Driving potential applied between top ITO electrode and
                   sequential bottom electrodes; droplets pinched off from
                   reservoirs and actuated across electrode array

Inter-electrode gaps : 40 um
Reagent capacity     : 6 reagents per device


REAGENTS
--------
Starting materials (all at 0.1 M in HPLC-grade MeOH):
  A1 : Boc-Ala-SH  -- Boc-protected Ala thioacid peptide
  A2 : Boc-Val-SH  -- Boc-protected Val thioacid peptide
  B1 : Az-Ala-An   -- NH aziridine-terminated Ala-Anilide amino acid
  B2 : Az-Pro-An   -- NH aziridine-terminated Pro-Anilide amino acid
       (B1 and B2 synthesised from commercially available amphoteric
        aziridine aldehydes; ref Hili & Yudin, JACS 2006)

Catalyst:
  RaNi : Raney Nickel (~5 mg in MeOH per 4.5-uL reservoir droplet)
         Ni-Al alloy grains; ferromagnetic (enables magnetic recovery)

Solvent:
  MeOH : HPLC-grade methanol (reaction solvent and re-dissolution solvent)
         MeOH-d4 (Cambridge Isotope Laboratories) used for NMR analysis

Other:
  Formic acid : 0.1% in MeOH (MS sample preparation)


PRODUCTS
--------
Peptidomimetics (thioacid-aziridine ligation products):
  A1-B1 : Boc-Ala-Cys-rAla-Anilide   m/z 425
  A1-B2 : Boc-Ala-Cys-rPro-Anilide   m/z 451
  A2-B1 : Boc-Val-Cys-rAla-Anilide   m/z 452
  A2-B2 : Boc-Val-Cys-rPro-Anilide   m/z 479

Desulfurized analogues (RaNi-catalysed desulfurization products):
  desulf A1-B1 : m/z 393
  desulf A1-B2 : m/z 419
  desulf A2-B1 : m/z 421
  desulf A2-B2 : m/z 447


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

PART 1 -- COMBINATORIAL LIGATION

Step 1 | Dispense Starting Material Droplets
  Reagents  : Boc_Ala_SH, Boc_Val_SH, Az_Ala_An, Az_Pro_An (all 0.1 M in MeOH)
  Operation : Dispense 2 unit droplets (~900 nL each) per reagent = 8 droplets total
  Actuation : 70-100 V_rms, 18 kHz; reservoir electrode activated to pinch off
              unit droplet onto actuation electrode array
  Notes     : Each device reservoir holds ~4.5 uL; ancillary reservoirs connected
              to main via electrode actuation sequence

Step 2 | Merge Reagent Pairs (Combinatorial Pairing)
  Operation : Mix each A droplet with the corresponding B droplet (4 pairs)
              A1 + B1 -> a1b1_merge
              A1 + B2 -> a1b2_merge
              A2 + B1 -> a2b1_merge
              A2 + B2 -> a2b2_merge
  Duration  : 5 s per merge
  Actuation : 70-100 V_rms, 18 kHz; sequential electrode actuation drives droplets
              to shared electrode where coalescence occurs

Step 3 | Circular Mixing and N2 Atmosphere Ligation Incubation
  Operation : Circular mixing (60 s) then N2 incubation (1 h, RT)
  Mixing    : Droplets actuated in circular motion on 4 electrodes for 60 s
  Incubation: 1 h at room temperature (25 C) under N2 atmosphere
  Atmosphere: Miniaturised Petri-dish glovebox (150 mm o.d. x 15 mm h polystyrene
              Petri dish); purged with N2 at ~50 mL/min for 2 min; sealed with
              parafilm; inlet and outlet tubes fitted in lid (UV-cured epoxy)
  Chemistry : Thioacid-aziridine ligation; installs peptide bond isostere with
              thiol sidechain between peptide fragments
  Products  : A1-B1 (m/z 425), A1-B2 (m/z 451), A2-B1 (m/z 452), A2-B2 (m/z 479)
  BioScript : heat at 25c for 60m (RT incubation; no glovebox primitive)

Step 4 | Solvent Evaporation -- Ligation Product Isolation
  Operation : Remove top plate; allow MeOH to evaporate at RT for 15 min
  Duration  : 15 min at RT (25 C)
  Result    : Dried peptidomimetic solids remain on bottom plate electrode surface
  BioScript : heat at 25c for 15m (no evaporation primitive)

--- LIGATION QC GATE ---
  Detection : ESI-MS on A1-B1 (sentinel product; expected m/z 425)
  Module    : msDetection; 30 s detection
  MS params : Agilent 1200 series quadrupole; positive ion mode; 50 V; 100 C
              capillary; 0.5 mL/min; 2.3 mM in MeOH + 0.1% formic acid;
              average of 67 acquisitions; verified in triplicate
  Pass (BB2): ligationSignal > 0 -> dispose all 4 ligation products; proceed to PART 2
  Fail (BB3): ligationSignal <= 0 -> drain all 4 ligation products

PART 2 -- DESULFURIZATION

Step 5 | Re-dissolve Peptidomimetic Products in MeOH
  Operation : Dispense 900-nL MeOH droplet; drive to each dried product spot
  Duration  : 5 s mixing per product
  Procedure : Top plate replaced; MeOH droplet dispensed from reservoir and driven
              by electrode actuation to re-solubilise each dried peptidomimetic solid
  Notes     : 900-nL droplets sufficient; volumes up to hundreds of uL possible
              depending on compound solubility (ref Au et al., Biomed. Microdevices 2011)

Step 6 | Dispense and Merge RaNi Catalyst with Product Droplets
  Catalyst  : RaneyNickel (~5 mg Ni-Al alloy in MeOH, 4.5-uL reservoir droplet)
  Operation : Dispense 1 RaNi droplet per product; merge with each dissolved product
  Duration  : 5 s per merge
  Pairs     : a1b1_dissolved + rani_a1b1 -> desulf_a1b1_raw
              a1b2_dissolved + rani_a1b2 -> desulf_a1b2_raw
              a2b1_dissolved + rani_a2b1 -> desulf_a2b1_raw
              a2b2_dissolved + rani_a2b2 -> desulf_a2b2_raw
  Chemistry : Raney Nickel catalyses desulfurization: removes -SH from cysteine-
              like sulfa backbone; yields reduced amide bond (confirmed by NMR
              peaks at 4.05 and 4.29 ppm for reduced amide CH protons)

Step 7 | Circular Mixing and RT Desulfurization Incubation
  Operation : Circular mixing (60 s) then RT incubation (15 min)
  Mixing    : Droplets actuated in circular motion on electrodes for 60 s
  Incubation: 15 min at room temperature (25 C)
  BioScript : heat at 25c for 15m
  Products  : desulf A1-B1 (m/z 393), desulf A1-B2 (m/z 419),
              desulf A2-B1 (m/z 421), desulf A2-B2 (m/z 447)

Step 8 | Magnetic RaNi Immobilisation and Product Isolation
  Magnet    : Neodymium Iron Boron (NdFeB) magnet (Main Electronics, BC)
  Procedure :
    1. NdFeB magnet placed beneath device; RaNi grains (ferromagnetic) immobilised
       to device bottom plate surface by magnetic force
    2. Supernatant (desulfurized product in MeOH, now RaNi-free) driven away from
       immobilised RaNi by electrode actuation
    3. Top plate removed; MeOH allowed to evaporate at RT for 30 min
    4. Desulfurized product remains as dried solid; collected by pipette after
       dissolving in appropriate solvent for off-chip analysis
  Duration  : 30 min evaporation at RT (25 C)
  BioScript : drain (magnetic separation) + heat at 25c for 30m (evaporation)
              NOTE: lilott8/BioScript maps drain to dispose() in IR; magnetic-
              separation semantic is preserved in source comments only
  NMR check : Desulf A1-B1; 1H NMR (CD3OD, 400 MHz):
              delta 4.05 and 4.29 ppm (reduced amide bond CH protons)
              delta 1.44 ppm (Boc protecting group)
              delta 7.60-7.17 ppm (phenyl group)
              Nearly identical to macroscale bench synthesis spectrum

--- DESULFURIZATION QC GATE ---
  Detection : ESI-MS and NMR on desulf-A1-B1 (sentinel product; expected m/z 393)
  Module    : nmrDetection; 30 s detection
  Pass (BB5): desulfSignal > 0 -> dispose all 4 desulfurized product droplets
  Fail (BB6): desulfSignal <= 0 -> drain all 4 desulfurized product droplets

--- TERMINAL (BB7) ---
  dispose desulfSignal; NOP
  Experiment ends. 8 products synthesised in 32 steps on a single DMF device.

  Instrument        : Agilent 1200 series quadrupole MS (Santa Clara, CA)
  Ionisation        : Electrospray (ESI), positive ion mode

  Capillary temp    : 100 C
  Flow rate         : 0.5 mL/min
  Sample prep       : ~2.3 mM in MeOH + 0.1% formic acid, 70 uL aliquot
  Acquisitions      : Average of 67 per spectrum
  Replicates        : Triplicate for all 8 products

                      torch, positioned in 3-mm Norell S400 NMR tube
  Concentration     : ~1.2 mM in 80-uL MeOH-d4
  Pulse width       : 90 degrees (7.3 us)
  Spectral width    : 16 ppm
  Recycle delay     : 1 s
  Steady states     : 1
  Scans             : 512
  Temperature       : 25 C
  Replicates        : Triplicate

BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES

IR output       : jebrail_combinatorial_peptidomimetics_dmf.ir
DAG output      : jebrail_combinatorial_peptidomimetics_dmf_main_dag.dot
Duration units  : s (seconds) and m (minutes) only; h token not supported
Duration conversions:
  1h ligation incubation   -> 60m   (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  15 min ligation evap.    -> 15m   (IR:  900.0 SECOND)
  15 min desulf incubation -> 15m   (IR:  900.0 SECOND)
  30 min desulf evaporation -> 30m  (IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
Modules         : msDetection, nmrDetection
Manifests       : Boc_Ala_SH, Boc_Val_SH, Az_Ala_An, Az_Pro_An, RaneyNickel, MeOH

BioScript abstractions:
  Glovebox N2 atmosphere  : heat at 25c for 60m (no glovebox primitive)
  Circular mixing         : mix x with x for 60s (self-mix; models circular
                            electrode actuation on 4 electrodes)
  Solvent evaporation     : heat at 25c for 15m or 30m (no evaporation primitive)
  Magnetic separation     : drain (collapses to dispose() in IR; semantic
                            preserved in source comments only)

Known compiler behaviours:
  drain -> dispose()  : lilott8/BioScript maps drain to the same IR opcode as
                        dispose. Magnetic-separation and waste-routing semantics
                        are source-level distinctions only.
  self-mix accepted   : mix x with x for Ns compiles without error; used to model
                        on-device circular electrode actuation mixing.

CFG structure (7 basic blocks, 8 edges):
  BB1  main          entry   Dispense, merge, mix, ligate, evaporate; MS detect A1-B1
  BB2  bsbbif_2_t    pass    ligationSignal > 0: dispose 4 ligation products
  BB3  bsbbif_3_f    fail    ligationSignal <= 0: drain 4 ligation products
  BB4  bsbbif_4_j    join    dispose ligationSignal; re-dissolve; RaNi; desulfurize;
                             magnetic sep; evaporate; NMR detect desulf-A1-B1
  BB5  bsbbif_5_t    pass    desulfSignal > 0: dispose 4 desulfurized products
  BB6  bsbbif_6_f    fail    desulfSignal <= 0: drain 4 desulfurized products
  BB7  bsbbif_7_j    term    dispose desulfSignal; NOP

Edges: BB1->BB2, BB1->BB3, BB2->BB4, BB3->BB4,
       BB4->BB5, BB4->BB6, BB5->BB7, BB6->BB7
