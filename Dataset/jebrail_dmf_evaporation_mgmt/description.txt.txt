PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Solvent Replenishment Solution for Managing Evaporation of Biochemical Reactions in Air-Matrix Digital Microfluidics Devices
Source Link  : https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1225865
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/c4lc00703d
Journal      : Lab on a Chip, 2015 (Sandia National Laboratories, SAND2015-0066J)
Platform     : Air-matrix digital microfluidics (DMF) on six-layer printed circuit board (PCB) with EWOD electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a just-in-time solvent replenishment strategy for managing droplet evaporation
in an air-matrix digital microfluidics (DMF) device, enabling reliable execution of biochemical
reactions requiring high temperatures (up to 95 C) and extended incubation times (up to 1 h or more)
without the use of an oil matrix, humidified chamber, or off-chip heating modules.

The core innovation is periodic merging of pre-heated solvent droplets (nuclease-free water, 0.5 uL
each) with the reaction droplet to restore volume lost to evaporation. Replenishing droplets are
delivered via a PEEK tube through a drilled through-hole (0.8 mm ID) in the PCB bottom plate,
controlled by a stepper-motor-driven syringe pump. Each replenishing droplet is transported to the
edge of the thermal zone and pre-heated for 5 s before merging, minimising the temperature perturbation
to the reaction droplet (<=1 C change on merging). Volume is replenished when the reaction droplet
loses 15-20% of its target volume (monitored visually in the current device; future devices could use
automated concentration or volume sensing with feedback control).

Three representative biochemical reactions from a gene expression analysis workflow are demonstrated:

  1. RNA Fragmentation
     Human PBMC total RNA fragmented at 94 C for 3 min on the DMF device. Six 0.5 uL replenishing
     water droplets added during incubation. Reaction terminated at 4 C with NEBNext stop solution.
     DMF product size distribution (40-1000 nt, peak ~150 nt) indistinguishable from conventional
     benchscale fragmentation.

  2. First-Strand cDNA Synthesis (Peregrine Method)
     Fragmented PBMC RNA converted to first-strand cDNA via the Peregrine template-switching approach
     using SMARTScribe RT. Protocol involves sequential incubation steps at 65 C (2 min denaturation),
     4 C (snap-cool), 25 C (3 min primer annealing), 42 C (1 min RT activation), 42 C (60 min
     extension with primer PP_TS), and 70 C (5 min termination). Thirteen 0.5 uL replenishing water
     droplets added in total (9 during the 42 C 60-min incubation; 4 during 70 C termination).
     qPCR Ct values and ds-cDNA yields comparable to conventional benchscale (p=0.14, N=3).

  3. PCR (M13mp18 template, 200 bp amplicon)
     PCR amplification of a 200 bp region (positions 4905-5104) of bacteriophage M13mp18 ssDNA
     using Hot Start Taq (doubled concentration) and thermal cycling between three zones:
     95 C 45 s initial denaturation; 33 cycles of (95 C 20 s, 50 C 30 s, 68 C 45 s); 68 C 5 min
     final extension. One 0.5 uL water replenishment added per cycle (33 total) after the 95 C
     denaturation step (~45 min total experiment time). Correct 200 bp product confirmed by gel
     electrophoresis; slightly reduced yield vs conventional PCR.

The protocol is encoded as a single BioScript file with three sequential protocol arms each terminated
by a QC detection gate, yielding a 10-block CFG.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Substrate     : Six-layer PCB; copper electrodes (43 um) plated with nickel (185 um) and gold (3.6 um);
                fabricated by World International PCB (Orange, CA) via photolithography and etching.
Dielectric    : Soldermask, ~15 um thick.
Hydrophobic   : 50 nm Teflon-AF (1% wt/wt in Fluorinert FC-40; 1500 rpm 30 s; baked 100 C 5 h);
                applied to both top and bottom plates.
Top plate     : Glass substrate coated with unpatterned ITO (Delta Technologies) + 50 nm Teflon-AF.
Bottom plate  : 100 actuation electrodes (1.6 x 1.6 mm each); 6 reservoir electrodes (3.5 x 5.5 mm);
                1 waste reservoir (5.2 x 5.5 mm); 80 um inter-electrode gaps.
Gap spacing   : 185 um (RNA fragmentation and cDNA synthesis); 800 um (PCR).

Thermal zones:
  - 4 independently controlled TEC thermal zones; each spanning 6 actuation electrodes.
  - Isolated by 1.0 x 7.0 mm voids machined into PCB substrate.
  - TEC: Laird Technologies OT08-08-F0-0303; RTD: US Sensor PPG102A1.
  - Attached with thermally conductive epoxy to gold-plated pads on PCB back side.
  - Thermally coupled to front-side zone electrodes via drilled through-holes filled with
    conductive epoxy.
  - Temperature offset between RTD (back side) and droplet: 3.0 C (+/- 0.5 C); incorporated
    into zone temperature programming as a compensation factor.
  - Controller: custom PICAXE 14M2 board; PWM + polarity control for heating and cooling.
  - Rapid temperature transitions achieved by shuttling reaction droplet between zones.

Through-hole interface:
  - 0.8 mm ID through-hole drilled in PCB bottom plate.
  - 360/150 OD/ID PEEK tube (IDEX) inserted from back side; immobilised with custom fittings.
  - Driven by stepper motor with computer-controlled electronic interface (Allmotion) via ASCII commands.
  - Delivers replenishing solvent droplets from external syringe pump reservoir onto device surface.

Actuation     : 80-100 Vrms, 18 kHz; function generator (Trek) + amplifier (Digi-Key).
Imaging       : Olympus MVX10 microscope; QIClick digital camera (Qimaging); 35x26 mm2 FOV;
                Streampix5 software (Norpix).
Thermal imaging: ThermoProTM TP8 IR camera (Guide Infrared); 10 s equilibration before capture.


REAGENTS
--------

Shared:
  NucleaseFreeWater  : Nuclease-free distilled water (molecular biology grade); replenishing solvent
                       in all three protocols. Dispensed from external syringe pump via through-hole.
  PluronicF127       : 0.1% w/v in cDNA master mix (Sigma Chemical).

RNA Fragmentation:
  RNA_PBMC           : Human PBMC total RNA, 380 ng/uL; extracted with RNAzol + Direct-zol kit
                       (Zymo Research); quantified on Qubit 2.0; stored at -80 C.
                       Source: 5-10e6 cells centrifuged 1000 rpm 4 C 5 min; 1 mL RNAzol +
                       400 uL water; 15 min RT; 16000 rpm 4 C 15 min; aqueous phase 1:1 EtOH;
                       Direct-zol purification; eluted 10 uL nuclease-free water.
  FragmentationBuffer: 10X NEBNext RNA fragmentation buffer (New England Biolabs).
  StopSolution       : NEBNext stop solution (New England Biolabs).

cDNA Synthesis (Peregrine Method):
  RNA_PBMC           : Pre-fragmented human PBMC total RNA, 100 ng per 0.5 uL reaction droplet.
  PrimerPP_RT        : Primer PP_RT, 25 mM.
  MasterMixCDNA      : 45% SMARTScribe 5X First-Strand Buffer (Clontech) + 5.5% 20 mM DTT +
                       22% 10 mM dNTP mix + 5.5% RiboGuard RNase inhibitor (Epicentre) +
                       22% SMARTScribe Reverse Transcriptase (Clontech) + 0.1% w/v Pluronic F-127.
  PrimerPP_TS        : Primer PP_TS, 12 mM.
  Purification       : AMPure XP beads (Beckman Coulter Genomics), 1.8X volumes, eluted 10-20 uL.
  qPCR primers       : PP_P1 (5'-CAGGACGCTGTTCCGTTCTATGGG-3') and PP_P2 (5'-CAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGATCT-3').

PCR:
  PCRMasterMix       : Hot Start Taq 2X Master Mix (NEB) + 0.025 U/uL additional Hot Start Taq (NEB)
                       to double Taq concentration.
  PCRPrimers         : Forward TGTTAATACTGACCGCCTCAC + Reverse CAGTAATAAAAGGGACATTCTGGC;
                       target M13mp18 positions 4905-5104 (200 bp amplicon; GenBank X02513.1);
                       equimolar mix, 4 uM per primer in 4X stock; 2 uM final concentration.
  M13mp18_Template   : Bacteriophage M13mp18 ssDNA (NEB), 250 pg/uL in nuclease-free water.
  Reservoir volumes  : 4 uL master mix; 2 uL primers; 2 uL template.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

=== PROTOCOL 1: RNA Fragmentation ===

Step 1 | Dispense reagents
  Inputs   : 3 x 0.5 uL RNA_PBMC (380 ng/uL); 1 x 0.5 uL 10X NEBNext fragmentation buffer
  Operation: dispense from respective reservoirs (reservoir volume 3.6 uL each)

Step 2 | Merge into reaction droplet
  Operation: sequential mix; rna1 + rna2 (10 s), + rna3 (10 s), + fragBuffer (10 s)
  Result   : 2 uL reaction droplet; transported to thermal zone

Step 3 | Thermal fragmentation
  Operation: heat at 94 C for 180 s
  Notes    : Without replenishment, droplet dries completely in <2 min at 94 C

Step 4 | Evaporation replenishment (6 water droplets)
  Operation: 6 x (dispense 0.5 uL NucleaseFreeWater + mix 5 s)
  Notes    : Each droplet pre-heated at zone edge 5 s before merge; volume maintained <=20% variation;
             temperature perturbation <=1 C per replenishment

Step 5 | Termination
  Operation: heat at 4 C for 30 s (droplet shuttled to cold zone); then dispense 0.5 uL StopSolution +
             mix 5 s
  Notes    : Stop solution terminates fragmentation reaction

Step 6 | QC detection
  Module   : rnaFragmentationDetection
  Duration : 30 s
  Expected : Fragments 40-1000 nt, peak ~150 nt; no 18S (1868 nt) or 28S (5025 nt) rRNA peaks
  Off-chip : Zymo RNA Clean + Concentrator-5 purification; Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer RNA Nano 6000 Chip
  Gate     : rnaSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB2); rnaSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB3)
  Join     : BB4

=== PROTOCOL 2: First-Strand cDNA Synthesis (Peregrine Method) ===

Step 7 | Dispense and merge RNA + primer PP_RT (Step A)
  Inputs   : 0.5 uL fragmented RNA_PBMC (100 ng); 0.5 uL PrimerPP_RT (25 mM)
  Operation: dispense; mix 10 s -> 1 uL droplet
  Notes    : Begins Peregrine template-switching cDNA synthesis

Step 8 | Denaturation and snap-cool (Step A cont.)
  Operation: heat 65 C 120 s (denaturation); heat 4 C 30 s (snap-cool)
  Notes    : Droplet shuttled between thermal zones for rapid transition

Step 9 | Merge master mix and incubate (Step B)
  Inputs   : 3 x 0.5 uL MasterMixCDNA
  Operation: dispense mm1, mm2, mm3; mix mm1+mm2 (5 s); mix + mm3 (5 s); mix into cdnaRxn (10 s)
  Then     : heat 25 C 180 s (primer annealing); heat 42 C 60 s (RT activation)

Step 10 | Merge primer PP_TS and extend (Step C)
  Input    : 0.5 uL PrimerPP_TS (12 mM)
  Operation: dispense + mix 5 s; heat 42 C 3600 s
  Replenishment: 9 x (dispense 0.5 uL NucleaseFreeWater + mix 5 s) during incubation

Step 11 | Termination (Step D)
  Operation: heat 70 C 300 s
  Replenishment: 4 x (dispense 0.5 uL NucleaseFreeWater + mix 5 s) during incubation
  Notes    : Inactivates SMARTScribe RT; 13 replenishments total across cDNA synthesis protocol

Step 12 | QC detection
  Module   : cdnaSynthesisDetection
  Duration : 30 s
  Expected : Ct comparable to conventional benchscale (p=0.14, N=3); equivalent ds-cDNA yield and size
  Off-chip : AMPure XP bead purification; qPCR Ct assay (SsoFast EvaGreen, CFX96, Bio-Rad);
             Bioanalyzer High Sensitivity DNA Assay Chip
  Gate     : cdnaSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB5); cdnaSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB6)
  Join     : BB7

=== PROTOCOL 3: PCR (M13mp18, 200 bp amplicon) ===

Step 13 | Dispense and merge PCR reagents
  Inputs   : 0.5 uL PCRMasterMix; 0.5 uL PCRPrimers; 0.5 uL M13mp18_Template
  Operation: dispense; mix master mix + primers (5 s); mix + template (5 s) -> 1.5 uL reaction droplet

Step 14 | Initial denaturation
  Operation: heat 95 C 45 s
  Purpose  : Hot Start Taq polymerase activation

Step 15 | 33 PCR cycles with per-cycle replenishment
  Cycle profile per cycle:
    Denaturation : 95 C, 20 s
    Replenishment: dispense 0.5 uL NucleaseFreeWater + mix 5 s (after denaturation, before annealing)
    Annealing    : 50 C, 30 s
    Extension    : 68 C, 45 s
  Total cycles     : 33
  Total water drops: 33 (one per cycle, added after denaturation step as in paper M&M)
  Note             : BioScript has no loop construct; all 33 cycles fully unrolled in source

Step 16 | Final extension
  Operation: heat 68 C 300 s
  Purpose  : Completion of partially extended amplicons

Step 17 | QC detection
  Module   : pcrProductDetection
  Duration : 30 s
  Expected : Single band at 200 bp; slightly reduced yield vs conventional PCR acceptable
  Off-chip : 2% agarose gel electrophoresis (E-Gel system, Life Technologies)
  Gate     : pcrSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB8); pcrSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB9)
  Join     : BB10 (terminal; dispose pcrSignal)


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file    : jebrail_dmf_evaporation_mgmt.bs
Compiler          : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output         : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG               : jebrail_dmf_evaporation_mgmt_main_dag.dot

Duration encoding : All durations use s (seconds) or m (minutes); h token unsupported by compiler.
                    3 min  -> 180s   (RNA fragmentation at 94 C)
                    2 min  -> 120s   (cDNA denaturation at 65 C)
                    1 h    -> 3600s  (cDNA extension at 42 C)
                    5 min  -> 300s   (cDNA termination at 70 C; PCR final extension)

Evaporation replenishment abstraction:
  Each just-in-time replenishment event is encoded as:
    wN = dispense 1 units of NucleaseFreeWater
    rxnVar = mix rxnVar with wN for 5s
  Variable reassignment (rxnVar = mix rxnVar with wN) correctly tracks fluid ownership through
  each sequential merge. The 5 s mix duration abstracts: (1) dispensing from syringe pump via
  through-hole; (2) transport to thermal zone edge; (3) pre-heating; (4) merge with reaction droplet.
  Total replenishment events: 6 (RNA) + 13 (cDNA) + 33 (PCR) = 52 events across the full file.

PCR cycle unrolling:
  BioScript has no loop primitive. All 33 cycles are fully unrolled as sequential heat/dispense/mix
  blocks. This yields a linear CFG for the PCR section rather than a cyclic graph, which correctly
  captures the sequential electrode actuation and thermal zone shuttle schedule.

heat statement semantics:
  `heat` is void/in-place in BioScript; the fluid variable is NOT consumed and persists after the
  statement. All downstream operations correctly reuse the same variable post-heat.

dispose/drain pattern:
  True branches (QC pass) use `dispose`; else branches (QC fail) use `drain` throughout.
  Known compiler behaviour: `drain` is lowered to `dispose()` in IR output due to visitStore
  routing in the compiler. The .bs source is correct; only IR lowering is affected.

Known compiler limitations (consistent with all other dataset entries):
  1. drain -> dispose lowering in IR (bsbbif_3_f, bsbbif_6_f, bsbbif_9_f): expected behaviour.
  2. True-branch blocks (bsbbif_2_t, bsbbif_5_t, bsbbif_8_t) emit no unconditional jump in IR
     text; DOT edges are correct. Known missing-jump-in-true-branch limitation.

Modules declared:
  rnaFragmentationDetection  : Agilent 2100 Bioanalyzer RNA Nano 6000 Chip readout
  cdnaSynthesisDetection     : qPCR Ct assay + Bioanalyzer High Sensitivity DNA Assay Chip
  pcrProductDetection        : Gel electrophoresis (2% agarose E-Gel), 200 bp band
