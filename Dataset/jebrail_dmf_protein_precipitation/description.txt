PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Microfluidic Method for Protein Extraction by Precipitation
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/precipitation.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac8021554
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2009, Vol. 81, No. 1, pp. 330-335
Authors      : Mais J. Jebrail, Aaron R. Wheeler
Platform     : Digital Microfluidics (DMF) on Parylene-C / Teflon-AF coated glass


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first microfluidic method for extracting proteins from
heterogeneous fluids by precipitation. The approach uses digital microfluidics (DMF)
to implement an automated, fully on-chip sequence of protein precipitation, rinsing,
and resolubilization -- without centrifugation. Protein precipitates settle rapidly
in DMF droplets (~0.1 mm path length vs. several mm in centrifuge tubes) and adhere
strongly to the Teflon-AF device surface, enabling clean separation of the liquid
supernatant from the solid precipitate by droplet actuation alone. The overall
extraction time is approximately 15 min, roughly half that of the conventional
macroscale equivalent (~30 min).

The protocol is divided into three functional phases:
  1. Qualitative extraction -- Mb/PC model system evaluated by ESI-MS (Fig. 2)
  2. Quantitative extraction -- protein standards (BSA, Mb, Fb) evaluated by
     fluorescamine assay (Fig. 3); >80% recovery for all three proteins
  3. Complex mixture extraction -- fetal bovine serum (FBS) and Jurkat T-cell
     lysate (CL) evaluated by fluorescamine assay (Fig. 4);
     FBS: 84.0 +/- 7.8%; CL: 82.4 +/- 11.5%

The method is compatible with proteins spanning a wide range of molecular weights
(17-340 kDa) and isoelectric points (pI 4.7-7.3), as well as with complex biological
matrices. Performance is comparable to conventional macroscale precipitation techniques,
with the advantages of automation, reduced reagent/sample consumption, no centrifugation,
and shorter processing time.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Geometry    : Double-T (double cross) electrode layout
Substrate   : Glass wafer
Electrodes  : Chromium (250 nm) patterned by photolithography
              Actuation electrodes: 1x1 mm and 1.5x1.5 mm
              Reservoir electrodes: 2.5x2.5 mm and 3.0x3.0 mm
              Interelectrode gap: 40 um
Dielectric  : Parylene-C, 2.5 um (vapor deposition)
Hydrophobic : Teflon-AF, 50 nm (spin-coated, 1% wt/wt in Fluorinert FC-40,
              2000 rpm / 60 s; post-baked 160 C / 10 min)
Top plate   : Unpatterned ITO-coated glass + Teflon-AF (50 nm)
Spacer      : 1-2 layers double-sided tape (70 um or 140 um interplate spacing)
Actuation   : 70-100 Vrms at 18 kHz (function generator + amplifier)
Key feature : Extraction electrode -- surface above this electrode is where
              precipitation, rinsing, and resolubilization all occur

Reservoirs (4 reagent + 1 waste):
  - Precipitant reservoir
  - Rinse solution reservoir
  - Resolubilizing buffer reservoir
  - Sample reservoir
  - Waste reservoir


REAGENTS AND MATERIALS
----------------------

Precipitants (Table 1):
  Mb, Fb           : Acetonitrile (ACN) -- primary; no secondary
  Mb/PC mixture    : ACN (primary) + saturated ammonium sulfate (secondary)
  BSA, FBS         : 20% trichloroacetic acid (TCA) in DI water
  Cell lysate (CL) : 20% TCA in DI water

Rinse solutions (Table 1):
  Mb, Fb, Mb/PC, BSA : 70/30 v/v chloroform/ACN
  FBS                : Acetone
  Cell lysate        : 60/40 v/v chloroform/acetone
  Note: neat chloroform is not directly actuatable at low driving potentials;
  chloroform/ACN (70/30 v/v) mixtures are readily movable on DMF devices.
  Neat acetone dissolves some CL precipitate; chloroform/acetone (60/40) avoids this.

Resolubilization buffers (Table 1):
  Mb, Fb, Mb/PC, BSA : 10 mM borate buffer (pH 8.5) + 1% SDS
  FBS, CL            : 100 mM borate buffer (pH 8.5) + 10% SDS

Working solutions:
  All protein solutions prepared in 10 mM borate buffer (pH 8.5) + 0.08% Pluronics F127 (w/v)
  Carrier fluid: Fluorinert FC-40

Key reagents (Sigma, unless noted):
  Acetone, ACN, chloroform, methanol, boric acid, 50% formic acid, Fluorinert FC-40,
  NaOH, Pluronics F127, TCA, Triton X-100, PMSF, SDS, fluorescamine, ammonium sulfate,
  BSA, fibrinogen (Fb), myoglobin (Mb)
  PBS, FBS: Invitrogen Canada
  1,2-Dibutyroyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphocholine (PC): Avanti Polar Lipids

Fluorescence assay:
  Reagent  : Fluorescamine (5 mg/mL in acetone)
  Platform : Pherastar plate reader (BMG Labtech); 384-well low-volume microplate
  Excitation: 390 nm; Emission: 510 nm
  Reaction : Immediate; fluorescamine non-fluorescent until it reacts with
             primary amines at protein N-terminus
  Controls : Unextracted protein droplets processed identically through device
             (dispense -> extraction electrode -> dry -> resolubilize -> collect)


PROTEIN STANDARDS
-----------------
BSA (bovine serum albumin) : MW 66 kDa, pI 4.7, 50 mg/mL; precipitant = 20% TCA
Mb  (myoglobin)            : MW 17 kDa, pI 7.3, 30 mg/mL; precipitant = ACN
Fb  (fibrinogen)           : MW 340 kDa, pI 5.5, 20 mg/mL; precipitant = ACN

Qualitative model system:
  Analyte     : Mb (0.71 mM)
  Contaminant : PC phospholipid (62 mM) -- 80:1 molar excess
  Analysis    : ESI-MS (LTQ linear ion trap, Thermo Fischer Scientific)
                Positive ion mode; 315 nL sample diluted into 50 uL 50/50 water/ACN
                + 0.1% formic acid; flow rate 0.5 uL/min; 1.7-1.9 kV; 170 C capillary;
                50 acquisitions averaged; triplicate


CELL LINE AND LYSATE
--------------------
Cell line   : Jurkat T-cells
Culture     : RPMI 1640 + 10% FBS + penicillin (100 IU/mL) + streptomycin (100 ug/mL)
              37 C, 5% CO2, humidified; subcultured every 3-4 days at 5e5 cells/mL
Lysis buffer: PBS + 0.08% F127 + 1% Triton X-100 + 1 mM PMSF
Lysis method: Cells washed 1x PBS; suspended at 6e7 cells/mL in lysis buffer;
              incubated on ice 30 min; centrifuged 13,000 rpm 5 min;
              supernatant collected and stored at -80 C


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming
  Reagent  : 0.08% Pluronics F127 in 10 mM borate buffer (pH 8.5)
  Duration : 5 min at 25 C (room temperature)
  Purpose  : Coat Teflon-AF surfaces with Pluronics surfactant layer to reduce
             non-specific protein adsorption to device surfaces.
  BS model : heat primingDrop at 25c for 5m; dispose

Step 2 | Qualitative Extraction -- Sample and First Precipitant Dispensing
  Sample   : Mb/PC mixture (Mb 0.71 mM, PC 62 mM) in borate buffer + 0.08% F127
  Precipitant 1: ACN (~140 nL)
  Operation: Dispense sample and ACN droplets; merge on extraction electrode
  Duration : 5 min incubation at 25 C (room temperature)
  BS model : mix mbPcSample with precipitant1_Mb for 5m

Step 3 | Qualitative Extraction -- Secondary Precipitant Addition
  Precipitant 2: Saturated ammonium sulfate in DI water (~140 nL)
  Rationale: Required at low Mb concentration (0.71 mM); ACN alone insufficient
             for reproducible precipitation below ~1 mM.
  Duration : Additional 5 min incubation at 25 C
  BS model : mix mbPcPrecip1 with precipitant2_Mb for 5m

Step 4 | Supernatant Removal
  Operation: Actuate supernatant droplet away from extraction electrode to waste
             reservoir. Precipitate remains on electrode; adsorbed to Teflon-AF.
  Key point: No centrifugation required. Path length ~0.1 mm; precipitates settle
             in seconds (2x faster than in centrifuge tubes).
  BS model : drain supernatant1

Step 5 | Rinsing (x3)
  Rinse solution : 70/30 v/v chloroform/ACN (~315 nL per droplet)
  Cycles         : 3 sequential rinse droplets
  Operation      : Each rinse droplet dispensed, driven across extraction electrode,
                   and actuated to waste.
  Purpose        : Remove PC lipid contamination and non-protein constituents.
  BS model       : mix mbPcPrecipFull with rinse1_Mb for 30s; drain (x3)

Step 6 | Drying
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature air dry)
  Duration    : 5 min
  Purpose     : Remove residual chloroform/ACN before resolubilization.
                No bake step required for chloroform/ACN rinse systems.
                TCA precipitants require bake at 95 C for 5 min (Steps 9a, 9c).
  BS model    : heat mbPcPrecipFull at 25c for 5m

Step 7 | Resolubilization
  Buffer   : 10 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 1% SDS (~315 nL)
  Duration : 5 min incubation at extraction electrode
  Purpose  : Dissolve precipitated protein for downstream analysis.
  BS model : mix mbPcPrecipFull with resolubBuffer_Mb for 5m

Step 8 | Qualitative QC -- ESI-MS Detection
  Module   : precipitationDetection (abstracts LTQ linear ion trap MS)
  Duration : 30 s detection window
  Pass     : Mb multiply charged ions detected (m/z 600-1800, charges +27 to +10);
             PC signal (m/z 398, 795) suppressed to trace levels.
  Fail     : Mb signal absent or PC signal dominant.
  Gate     : precipitationSignal > 0 -> BB2 (pass/dispose); else BB3 (fail/drain)

Step 9a | BSA Extraction (Quantitative -- Protein Standards)
  Sample       : BSA 50 mg/mL (~140 nL)
  Precipitant  : 20% TCA (~140 nL); incubate 5 min at 25 C
  Rinse        : 70/30 v/v chloroform/ACN x3 (~315 nL each); 30 s per rinse
  Bake         : 95 C for 5 min to remove residual TCA before resolubilization
  Resolubilize : 10 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 1% SDS (~315 nL); 5 min
  BS model     : bsaPrecip -> drain supernatant -> rinse x3 -> heat 95c 5m -> resolubBSA

Step 9b | Mb Extraction (Quantitative -- Protein Standards)
  Sample       : Myoglobin 30 mg/mL (~140 nL)
  Precipitant  : ACN (~140 nL); incubate 5 min at 25 C
  Rinse        : 70/30 v/v chloroform/ACN x3 (~315 nL each); 30 s per rinse
  Dry          : 25 C for 5 min (no bake required for ACN precipitant)
  Resolubilize : 10 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 1% SDS (~315 nL); 5 min
  BS model     : mbPrecip -> drain supernatant -> rinse x3 -> heat 25c 5m -> resolubMbQuant

Step 9c | Fb Extraction (Quantitative -- Protein Standards)
  Sample       : Fibrinogen 20 mg/mL (~140 nL)
  Precipitant  : ACN (~140 nL); incubate 5 min at 25 C
  Rinse        : 70/30 v/v chloroform/ACN x3 (~315 nL each); 30 s per rinse
  Dry          : 25 C for 5 min
  Resolubilize : 10 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 1% SDS (~315 nL); 5 min
  BS model     : fbPrecip -> drain supernatant -> rinse x3 -> heat 25c 5m -> resolubFb

Step 9d | Fluorescamine Assay (Quantitative QC)
  Reagent  : Fluorescamine (5 mg/mL in acetone); 1 min reaction at 25 C
  Incubate : 2 min settle at 25 C before plate reader measurement
  Module   : fluorescenceDetection (abstracts Pherastar plate reader readout)
  Gate     : fluorSignal > 0 -> BB5 (pass/dispose all 3); else BB6 (fail/dispose all 3)
  Target   : >80% recovery for BSA, Mb, Fb vs. unextracted control
  Note     : QC gate keyed on BSA signal (highest concentration, most robust)
  Compiler : drain collapsed to dispose by lilott8/BioScript in IR; both branches
             use dispose explicitly for consistent output (BB6 fix).

Step 10a | FBS Extraction (Complex Mixture)
  Sample       : Fetal bovine serum (~140 nL)
  Precipitant  : 20% TCA (~140 nL); incubate 5 min at 25 C
  Rinse        : Acetone x3 (~315 nL each); 30 s per rinse
                 Supplemental rinse strategy: if supernatant is sticky/immovable,
                 dispense rinse solvent to extraction electrode first; combined
                 rinse+supernatant droplet driven to waste together.
  Bake         : 95 C for 5 min to remove residual TCA
  Resolubilize : 100 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 10% SDS (~315 nL); 5 min
  Recovery     : 84.0 +/- 7.8% (mean +/- SD, N=4)
  BS model     : fbsPrecip -> drain supernatant -> rinse x3 -> heat 95c 5m -> resolubFBS

Step 10b | Cell Lysate Extraction (Complex Mixture)
  Sample       : Jurkat T-cell lysate (~70 nL; reduced interplate spacing 70 um
                 to handle high viscosity; standard spacing 140 um not suitable)
  Precipitant  : 20% TCA (~70 nL); incubate 5 min at 25 C
  Rinse        : 60/40 v/v chloroform/acetone x3 (~158 nL each); 30 s per rinse
                 Neat acetone dissolves some CL precipitate; chloroform/acetone avoids this.
  Bake         : 95 C for 5 min to remove residual TCA
  Resolubilize : 100 mM borate (pH 8.5) + 10% SDS (~158 nL); 5 min
  Recovery     : 82.4 +/- 11.5% (mean +/- SD, N=4)
  BS model     : clPrecip -> drain supernatant -> rinse x3 -> heat 95c 5m -> resolubCL

Step 10c | Fluorescamine Assay (Complex Mixture QC)
  Reagent  : Fluorescamine (5 mg/mL in acetone); 1 min reaction at 25 C
  Incubate : 2 min settle at 25 C before plate reader measurement
  Module   : fluorescenceDetection
  Gate     : complexSignal > 0 -> BB8 (pass/dispose both); else BB9 (fail/dispose both)
  Note     : QC gate keyed on FBS signal.
  Compiler : Same drain->dispose fix applied (BB9).


CONVENTIONAL MACROSCALE COMPARISON
------------------------------------
For each protein standard and complex mixture, identical extraction was performed
macroscopically (N=4 replicates each):
  - 10 uL sample + 10 uL chilled precipitant in microcentrifuge tube
  - Incubate 5 min at 4 C; centrifuge 13,000 rpm 5 min; discard supernatant
  - Wash pellet 3x: suspend in 22.5 uL rinse solution; centrifuge 13,000 rpm 5 min; discard
  - Dissolve pellet in 100 uL resolubilization buffer
  - Same precipitants, rinse, and resolubilization solutions as DMF
  - Performance comparable to DMF; DMF is ~2x faster (15 min vs. 30 min)

                 BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported.
                 Key durations in IR:
                   5m  precipitation/resolubilization -> 300.0SECOND
                   30s rinsing                        ->  30.0SECOND
                   5m  drying/baking                  -> 300.0SECOND
                   2m  fluorescamine settle            -> 120.0SECOND
                   30s detection                      ->  30.0SECOND
Modules        : precipitationDetection, fluorescenceDetection
Compiler quirk : drain collapses to dispose in IR output for lilott8/BioScript.
                 All else-branch drain calls in BB6 and BB9 replaced with dispose
                 for consistent IR. BB2/BB3 drain in qualitative branch also
                 collapses; acceptable as both branches dispose resolubMb in IR.
Parallelism    : BSA, Mb, and Fb extractions run in parallel on device (double-T
                 layout). BioScript encodes them sequentially; CFG logical structure
                 is correct.
