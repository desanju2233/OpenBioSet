PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Synchronized Synthesis of Peptide-Based Macrocycles by Digital Microfluidics
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/macrocycle_synthesis.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201001604
Journal      : Angewandte Chemie International Edition, 2010, Vol. 49, pp. 8625-8629
Platform     : Two-plate digital microfluidics (DMF) on EWOD/electrode array
Authors      : Mais J. Jebrail, Alphonsus H. C. Ng, Vishal Rai, Ryan Hili,
               Andrei K. Yudin, Aaron R. Wheeler
Affiliation  : Department of Chemistry and Institute of Biomaterials and Biomedical
               Engineering, University of Toronto, Toronto, ON M5S 3H6, Canada


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first two-plate digital microfluidic platform for parallel
multi-component, multi-step chemical synthesis. It implements synchronized on-chip
synthesis of five peptide-based macrocycles (PMs) from three components -- amino acids,
amphoteric aziridine aldehydes, and tert-butyl isocyanide (tBuNC) -- in a one-step
Ugi-type macrocyclization reaction. Products contain aziridine sites that are primed for
late-stage nucleophilic modification.

Downstream processing converts the PMs into macrocyclic peptide thioesters (ring-opened
products, ROPs) by reacting with thiobenzoic acid (PhCOSH). As a further demonstration,
a nine-membered macrocycle is synthesized from a Pro-Leu dipeptide substrate -- a ring
size associated with considerable synthetic difficulty in conventional cyclic peptide
synthesis.

The platform is fully automated, hardware-free (no pumps, tubing, or valves), compatible
with a wide range of organic solvents and corrosive chemicals, and supports critical
steps -- solvent removal and re-dissolution of solids -- that are problematic in
microchannel-based systems. Five products are formed in 30 total processing steps
(6 steps per parallel reaction), verified by ESI-MS and 1H-NMR against macroscale
reference syntheses.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration : Two-plate DMF
Top plate     : Transparent ITO (indium tin oxide) glass; removable for product isolation
Bottom plate  : Electrode array coated with Teflon-based hydrophobic insulator
Reservoirs    : 10 reagent supply reservoirs
Electrodes    : 88 actuation electrodes dedicated to dispensing, merging, and mixing
Droplet volume: 900 nL per unit droplet
Actuation     : Electrical potentials applied to electrode array (EWOD principle);
                discrete nL-to-uL droplets controlled in parallel with no cross-contamination
Surface       : Chemically inert Teflon; compatible with TFE, organic solvents, corrosive
                chemicals -- diversifies reagent scope beyond PDMS-based microchannel systems

Key advantages over microchannel platforms:
  - No pumps, tubing, valves, or 3D channel networks required
  - Individual reagent addressing with no complex microvalve networks
  - Supports solvent removal by evaporation and re-dissolution of solid products
  - Easy off-chip product access (remove top plate; recover by pipette)
  - Scalable: proposed future systems capable of tens to hundreds of simultaneous products


REAGENTS
--------
Amino acids (five tracks):
  Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro -- 0.1 M in deionized water; 90 nmol per droplet (900 nL)

Aziridine aldehyde:
  Amphoteric aziridine aldehyde -- 0.05 M in TFE; 45 nmol per droplet (900 nL)
  Role: electrophilic macrocyclization partner; aziridine ring in product is site for
  late-stage modification by nucleophilic ring-opening

tert-Butyl isocyanide (tBuNC):
  0.1 M in TFE; 90 nmol per droplet (900 nL)
  Role: isocyanide component of Ugi-type three-component cyclization

Thiobenzoic acid (PhCOSH):
  0.1 M in TFE; 90 nmol per droplet (900 nL)
  Role: nucleophile for aziridine ring-opening; yields macrocyclic peptide thioesters
  (ROPs); thioesters are precursors to native chemical ligation (S-to-N acyl transfer)

Trifluoroethanol (TFE):
  Re-dissolution solvent; 4 x 900 nL droplets (~3.5 uL) driven to each dried PM spot
  by electrode actuation before ring-opening

Pro-Leu dipeptide (nine-membered macrocycle synthesis):
  0.1 M in TFE; 90 nmol per droplet (900 nL)

Off-chip analysis solvents:
  MS  : 100 uL MeOH + 0.1% formic acid (ESI-MS)
  NMR : 250 uL CD3OD (1H-NMR)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Phase 1 | Peptide Macrocycle (PM) Synthesis -- Five Parallel Tracks

Step 1 | Amino Acid + Aziridine Aldehyde Merge
  Tracks    : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro (5 simultaneous reactions)
  Inputs    : AminoAcid_X (900 nL, 0.1 M, 90 nmol) + AziridineAldehyde (900 nL, 0.05 M, 45 nmol)
  Operation : Dispense from respective reservoirs; electrode-actuated merging and mixing
  Duration  : 20 s (circular actuation on 4 electrodes)
  Purpose   : Imine formation between amino acid and aziridine aldehyde

Step 2 | tBuNC Addition and Macrocyclization
  Tracks    : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Inputs    : AA+AzAld merged droplet + tBuNC (900 nL, 0.1 M, 90 nmol)
  Operation : Dispense tBuNC; electrode-actuated merging and mixing
  Duration  : 20 s (circular actuation on 4 electrodes)
  Purpose   : Completes three-component Ugi-type macrocyclization

Step 3 | Macrocyclization Incubation
  Tracks      : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature)
  Duration    : 1 h (60 min; IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  Enclosure   : Parafilm-sealed Petri dish to minimize evaporation
  Kinetics    : ~70% depletion of initial reagent within 1 h by DMF;
                ~90% by macroscale; anticipated improvement with continuous rapid mixing

Step 4 | PM Isolation by TFE Evaporation
  Tracks      : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Method      : ITO top plate removed; TFE evaporated at RT (25 C) for 15 min
  Result      : Solid PM precipitates on electrode; accessible by pipette after re-dissolution
                in 100 uL MeOH/0.1% formic acid (MS) or 250 uL CD3OD (NMR)

Phase 1 QC Gate | PM Verification (BB1 -> BB2/BB3)
  Representative track : Met
  Detection module     : macrocycleQC (abstracts off-chip ESI-MS + 1H-NMR)
  Detection duration   : 30 s
  MS verification      : m/z 342 (PM-Met; Fig 4b); other PMs in Supporting Information Figs S1-S4
  NMR verification     : 1H-NMR in CD3OD; chemical shifts correlated with macroscale product
                         (Supporting Information Fig S6)
  Pass (BB2)           : macrocycleSignal > 0 -> dispose(rxnMixMet); proceed to Phase 2
  Fail (BB3)           : macrocycleSignal <= 0 -> drain(rxnMixMet)
  Join (BB4)           : dispose(macrocycleSignal); Phase 2 begins


Phase 2 | Aziridine Ring-Opening (ROP) -- Five Parallel Tracks

Step 5 | PM Re-Dissolution in TFE
  Tracks    : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Inputs    : 4 x TFE droplets (4 x 900 nL = ~3.5 uL) per dried PM spot
  Operation : Four sequential TFE dispenses chained via electrode-actuated mixing;
              combined droplet driven to dried solid spot by electrode actuation
  Purpose   : Re-dissolve solid PM for ring-opening reaction
  Notes     : Volume of TFE is not constrained (unlike microchannel platforms);
              flexibility is a key DMF advantage highlighted in Fig 3 frames 1-2

Step 6 | PhCOSH Merge and Ring-Opening Incubation
  Tracks      : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Inputs      : pmSolnX (~3.5 uL) + PhCOSH (900 nL, 0.1 M, 90 nmol)
  Operation   : Dispense PhCOSH; electrode-actuated merging and mixing
  Mixing      : 20 s (circular actuation on 4 electrodes)
  Incubation  : 1 h at 25 C (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); parafilm-sealed Petri dish
  Purpose     : Nucleophilic opening of aziridine by PhCOSH yields macrocyclic
                peptide thioester at defined position

Step 7 | ROP Isolation by TFE Evaporation
  Tracks      : Val, Met, Ile, Thr, Pro
  Method      : TFE evaporated at RT (25 C) for 15 min (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
  Result      : Precipitated macrocyclic peptide thioesters (ROPs) at defined electrode
                positions; spatially resolved on-chip

Phase 2 QC Gate | ROP Verification (BB4 -> BB5/BB6)
  Representative track : Met
  Detection module     : ringOpeningQC (abstracts off-chip ESI-MS + 1H-NMR)
  Detection duration   : 30 s
  MS verification      : m/z 150 (unreacted Met), 342 (PM-Met), 480 (ROP-Met; Fig 4c)
  NMR verification     : 1H-NMR in CD3OD; chemical shifts correlated with macroscale
                         (Supporting Information Fig S7)
  Pass (BB5)           : ringOpeningSignal > 0 -> dispose all 5 ROP droplets
  Fail (BB6)           : ringOpeningSignal <= 0 -> drain all 5 ROP droplets
  Terminal (BB7)       : dispose(ringOpeningSignal); 9-mer sub-protocol follows


Supplementary Protocol | Nine-Membered Macrocycle Synthesis (Fig 5, paper)

Step 8 | Pro-Leu Dipeptide + AziridineAldehyde + tBuNC Merge
  Substrate   : Pro-Leu dipeptide (0.1 M in TFE, 900 nL, 90 nmol)
  Inputs      : Pro-Leu dipeptide + AziridineAldehyde (900 nL, 0.05 M) + tBuNC (900 nL, 0.1 M)
  Operation   : Dispense all three components; sequential electrode-actuated merging and mixing
  Mixing      : 20 s per merge (circular actuation on 4 electrodes)

Step 9 | Nine-Membered Macrocycle Incubation
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature)
  Duration    : 3 h (180 min; IR: 10800.0 SECOND)
  Mixing      : Every 30 min (6 x 20 s circular actuation events over 3 h)
  BioScript   : Encoded as single heat 180m; periodic mixing is a DMF controller
                firmware operation not representable as a BioScript droplet-level operation
  Notes       : Longer reaction time vs standard PMs due to reduced reactivity of dipeptide
                substrate; nine-membered ring size previously inaccessible by direct
                linear tripeptide cyclization (Failli et al., Can. J. Chem. 1979, 57, 3257)

Step 10 | Nine-Membered Macrocycle Isolation
  Method      : TFE evaporated at 25 C for 15 min (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
  Product     : Compound 8; ESI-MS m/z 421 (Fig 5b, paper)
  Significance: Demonstrates reaction selectivity and scope extension to larger ring sizes
                via dipeptide substrates; Pro-Leu macrocycle contains thioester for
                anticipated native chemical ligation applications

BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : jebrail_macrocycle_dmf.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges
DAG file       : jebrail_macrocycle_dmf_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations converted to minutes (m) or seconds (s);
                 BioScript supports only s and m tokens (h token not supported)
                 1h incubation  -> 60m   (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
                 15min evap     -> 15m   (IR: 900.0 SECOND)
                 3h 9-mer incub -> 180m  (IR: 10800.0 SECOND)
Modules        : macrocycleQC, ringOpeningQC

Compiler constraints and resolutions:
  1. Case-sensitive variables
       Bug  : aaMixMet = mix aamet with azAldMet (lowercase 'aamet' vs declared 'aaMet')
       Error: UndefinedVariable: main.aamet is not defined
       Fix  : corrected to mix aaMet with azAldMet

  2. Self-mix rejected by symbol visitor
       Bug  : rxnMix9mer = mix rxnMix9mer with rxnMix9mer (double-consume of same variable)
       Error: would raise UndefinedVariable or double-consume on next compiler pass
       Fix  : replaced 6 alternating heat(30m)+mix(self) cycles with single heat 180m;
              periodic electrode actuations are firmware-level DMF operations, not
              droplet-level BioScript operations

  3. No parallelism primitive
       Issue: Paper synthesizes 5 PMs simultaneously on-chip
       Resolution: 5 PM tracks and 5 ROP tracks encoded sequentially; concurrent on
                   physical device; documented in comments

  4. drain() normalised to dispose() in IR
       Observation: BioScript compiler emits dispose() opcode for both dispose and drain
                    keywords; distinction is only meaningful at physical execution layer;
                    not a bug in the IR

  5. Multi-unit TFE re-dissolution
       Issue: Compiler does not support multi-unit scalar dispense into solid spot
       Resolution: 4 TFE dispenses chained via sequential mix steps to accumulate ~3.5 uL
                   (pattern from Moazami reference file, lines 222-229)

  6. Pro-Leu dipeptide manifest
       Issue: Pro-Leu dipeptide is a distinct reagent not declared in the manifest block
       Resolution: Mapped to AminoAcid_Pro with prominent comment; separate manifest
                   ProLeu_Dipeptide recommended in a full implementation

  7. Nine-membered macrocycle as post-terminal sub-protocol
       Design: 9-mer synthesis is an independent demonstration (Fig 5), not downstream
               of the five-PM synthesis; runs unconditionally after BB7 terminal node;
               does not introduce additional CFG branches
