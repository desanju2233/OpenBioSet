PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : High-Content Quantification of Single-Cell Immune Dynamics
Source Link  : https://www.cell.com/cell-reports/fulltext/S2211-1247(16)30289-3#
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2016.03.033
Journal      : Cell Reports, Vol. 15, pp. 411-422, April 12, 2016
Authors      : Michael Junkin, Alicia J. Kaestli, Zhang Cheng, Christian Jordi,
               Cem Albayrak, Alexander Hoffmann, Savas Tay
Platform     : Two-layer PDMS microfluidic chip (continuous-flow, valve-controlled)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated microfluidic system for high-content quantification
of single-cell immune dynamics in isolated RAW 264.7 mouse macrophages. The platform
delivers precisely defined dynamic inflammatory inputs (LPS, single 2 hr pulse at
500 ng/mL) to 40 individually addressable 1.35 nL PDMS cell chambers while
simultaneously and non-destructively measuring two dynamic immune readouts from the
same cells: (1) TNF cytokine secretion via on-chip bead-based fluorescent sandwich
immunoassay at 2 hr resolution across 7 time windows (0-14 hr), and (2) NF-kB (RelA)
transcription factor nuclear localization via time-lapse epifluorescence microscopy
every ~6 min throughout the 20 hr run.

Key finding: TNF secretion dynamics are highly heterogeneous and surprisingly
uncorrelated with NF-kB dynamics (Pearson r = -0.06), despite NF-kB being the
master transcription factor controlling TNF production. Computational modeling of the
LPS/TLR4 pathway identifies TRIF-mediated post-transcriptional regulation as the key
determinant of noisy and uncorrelated TNF secretion at the single-cell level.

The BioScript encoding captures the single 2 hr pulse LPS stimulation condition
(Fig 4B left panel), which mimics brief pathogen exposure, with all 7 bead time-point
assay windows and both NF-kB and TNF QC gates.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Design       : Melin & Quake two-layer PDMS microfluidic chip
Layers       : Flow layer (cell chambers, binding chambers, bead storage chambers,
               peristaltic pump channels) + Control layer (pneumatically actuated
               PDMS membrane valves for all fluid routing)
Cell chambers: 40 isolated 1.35 nL chambers, individually addressable via binary
               multiplexer; entirely isolated from one another to prevent
               contamination from neighbouring cells
Trap type    : Weir-type trap (slits without bypass) for non-adherent cells;
               cells retained during washing, stimulation, and medium harvesting
Inlets       : 16 reagent inlets controlled by automated membrane valves
Pumping      : On-chip peristaltic pump; input signal switching time < 5 s
Bead storage : 7 storage chambers per column (one per TNF time point); 4 um PDMS
               slits retain beads during loading and transfer
Binding chamber : One per column; beads moved here for medium-bead incubation
Microscope   : Nikon Eclipse Ti (inverted epifluorescence); automated stage
Enclosure    : Life Imaging Services atmospheric enclosure;
               37 C, 5% CO2, saturating humidity maintained throughout
Control SW   : Custom MATLAB GUI (Gomez-Sjoberg et al. 2007; Kellogg et al. 2014)
Imaging res. : ~6 min per full chip scan; 6 channels (GFP, brightfield + others)
Culture dur. : Up to several days without loss of viability (Fig S4)


CELL LINE
---------
Line         : RAW 264.7 (mouse macrophage)
Reporter     : RelA-GFP fusion protein (nuclear localization reports NF-kB activation)
Culture      : DMEM (Sigma D6429) + 10% FBS (Sigma F9665) + 1% GlutaMax
               (Life Technologies 35050-038) + 2% HEPES (Life Technologies 15630-056)
               Non-tissue culture treated T75 flasks; passaged every 3 days with versene
Off-chip prep: Detach with versene (Life Technologies 15040-033); filter through 20 um
               (CellTrics 04-004-2325); resuspend at 1e5-3.5e5 cells/mL in CO2-
               equilibrated medium
Source       : Ian Fraser Lab, NIH, USA


REAGENTS
--------
Surface coating  : Pluronic F-127 (Life Technologies P6867), 2 mg/mL in DI water;
                   prevents protein adsorption and non-specific cell/bead adhesion
Stimulant        : LPS (Sigma L4524), 500 ng/mL in culture medium;
                   single 2 hr pulse; switchover time < 5 s
Wash buffer      : PBS-Tween (0.563 mg/mL); used between sandwich assay steps
TNF capture beads: Spherotech SVP-100-4 (10.3 um streptavidin-functionalized);
                   biotinylated anti-TNF Ab (United States Biological T9160-14)
                   coupled in PBS pH 5.5; one bead per storage chamber per time point
TNF calibrant    : Life Technologies PMC3014; aliquoted in PBS + 1 mg/mL BSA;
                   dissolved in macrophage medium for on-chip calibrations
Detection Ab     : genwaybio GWB-489500 at 3.19 ug/mL; 30 min with mixing
Secondary Ab     : United States Biological I1903-12H (fluorescent) at 3.3 ug/mL;
                   30 min with mixing
Multiplexed kits : BD Biosciences 552364, 560232, 558345 (IL-6, IL-10, IL-12,
                   MCP-1, IFN-gamma multiplexed detection; PE-tagged secondary Abs)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Chip Surface Preparation
  Reagents   : Pluronic F-127 (2 mg/mL in DI water), PBS
  BS var     : devicePrimed
  Operation  : mix (abstracts automated MATLAB control script rinse sequence)
  Duration   : 720m (12 hr overnight; IR: 43200.0 SECOND)
  Purpose    : Coat all internal surfaces except cell chambers with Pluronic F-127
               to reduce protein adsorption and prevent non-specific cell and bead
               adhesion to PDMS walls; rinse extensively with PBS before loading

Step 2 | Cell Loading
  Reagents   : RAW 264.7 macrophages, devicePrimed
  BS var     : cellsLoaded
  Operation  : mix (abstracts flow-through weir trapping via binary multiplexer)
  Duration   : 10m (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  Cells/mL   : 1e5-3.5e5 (continuous stirring in syringe during loading)
  Load time  : < 10 min for all 40 chambers
  Notes      : Weir-type trap (slits without bypass) retains non-adherent macrophages;
               individual chambers entirely isolated from one another

Step 3 | Post-Loading Equilibration
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, cellsLoaded
  BS var     : cellsEq
  Operation  : mix + heat at 37c
  Duration   : 10m mix + 120m heat (IR: 600.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)
  Notes      : Fresh medium pumped intermittently via peristaltic pump; cells
               equilibrate inside sealed chambers; 37 C / 5% CO2 / saturating
               humidity maintained by atmospheric enclosure

Step 4 | Baseline TNF Bead Capture (t = 0-2 hr, pre-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleT0, bead1Bound, bead1Washed
  Operation  : mix (medium sample, 120m) + mix (bead binding, 90m) + mix (rinse, 5m)
  Duration   : 120m sample + 90m bind + 5m rinse
               IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 SECOND
  Notes      : Pre-stimulus baseline; peristaltic pump transfers medium surrounding
               cells to binding chamber; bead moved from storage, mixed circularly
               for 90 min to reach steady-state capture (Fig 2A); rinsed with
               PBS-Tween to remove interfering molecules; returned to storage.
               bead1Washed is held alive through the entire protocol as TNF QC
               gate target in BB5/BB6

Step 5 | LPS Stimulation -- 2 hr Pulse
  Reagents   : LPS_500ngmL (500 ng/mL), bead1Washed (cell-thread carrier)
  BS var     : cellsStim
  Operation  : mix + heat at 37c
  Duration   : 120m LPS + 120m heat (IR: 7200.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)
  Notes      : LPS injected via 16-inlet binary multiplexer; switching time < 5 s;
               single 2 hr pulse condition (Fig 4B left panel) mimics brief pathogen
               exposure; bead1Washed carries cell-thread state into LPS step as
               the ownership chain carrier from STEP 4

Step 6 | LPS Washout
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, cellsStim
  BS var     : cellsWashed
  Operation  : mix
  Duration   : 10m (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  Notes      : Multiplexer switches back to medium inlet; LPS-free medium flows
               through chamber; switchover time < 5 s

Step 7 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 1 (0-2 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP1, bead2Bound, bead2Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m) + heat (120m) at 37c
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m + 120m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)

Step 8 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 2 (2-4 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP2, bead3Bound, bead3Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m) + heat (120m) at 37c
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m + 120m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)

Step 9 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 3 (4-6 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP3, bead4Bound, bead4Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m) + heat (120m) at 37c
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m + 120m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)

Step 10 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 4 (6-8 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP4, bead5Bound, bead5Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m) + heat (120m) at 37c
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m + 120m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)

Step 11 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 5 (8-10 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP5, bead6Bound, bead6Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m) + heat (120m) at 37c
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m + 120m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 + 7200.0 SECOND)

Step 12 | TNF Bead Capture -- Time Point 6 (10-12 hr post-LPS)
  Reagents   : CultureMedium, TNF_CaptureBeads, PBSTween
  BS vars    : sampleTP6, bead7Bound, bead7Washed
  Operation  : mix (120m) + mix (90m) + mix (5m)
  Duration   : 120m + 90m + 5m (IR: 7200.0 + 5400.0 + 300.0 SECOND)
  Notes      : Final medium sampling window; bead7Washed carries cell-thread
               terminal state into sandwich assay completion step

Step 13 | Sandwich Assay Completion -- Detection Antibodies
  Reagents   : TNF_DetectionAntibody (3.19 ug/mL), SecondaryFluorescentAntibody (3.3 ug/mL)
  BS vars    : detStep, sandwichDone
  Operation  : mix (30m) + mix (30m)
  Duration   : 30m + 30m (IR: 1800.0 + 1800.0 SECOND)
  Catalogs   : Detection Ab: genwaybio GWB-489500; Secondary Ab: USB I1903-12H
  Notes      : Applied over all 7 stored beads simultaneously after all time-point
               captures complete; beads imaged in storage chambers; fluorescence
               intensity correlated to on-chip calibration curve (R2 = 0.9984, Fig 2B)

Step 14 | NF-kB (RelA) Localization -- Aggregate QC Readout
  BS var     : nfkbSignal
  Module     : nfkbLocalizationDetection
  Input      : sandwichDone
  Duration   : 30s (IR: 30.0 SECOND)
  Threshold  : > 2 SD above pre-stimulus baseline = NF-kB+
  NF-kB+ pct : ~93.6% of cells (n = 46) following 2 hr LPS pulse (Fig 6A)
  Pearson r  : -0.06 (NF-kB amplitude vs. TNF amplitude; quantitatively uncorrelated)
  Notes      : Continuous time-lapse imaging throughout 20 hr run; MATLAB image
               analysis computes nuclear/cytoplasmic RelA-GFP ratio per frame;
               nfkbLocalizationDetection abstracts the binary nuclear-peak
               determination; consumes sandwichDone
  Branch     : nfkbSignal > 0  -> BB2 (bsbbif_2_t): dispose(sandwichDone)  [NF-kB+]
               nfkbSignal <= 0 -> BB3 (bsbbif_3_f): drain(sandwichDone)    [NF-kB-]
               Both -> BB4 (bsbbif_4_j): join; dispose(nfkbSignal)

Step 15 | TNF Secretion Fluorescence Readout
  BS var     : tnfSignal
  Module     : tnfSecretionDetection
  Input      : bead1Washed (baseline bead; held alive since STEP 4)
  Duration   : 30s (IR: 30.0 SECOND)
  LOD        : ~60,000 TNF molecules
  Calibration: R2 = 0.9984 (on-chip; Fig 2B)
  TNF+ pct   : ~93.5% of cells produce detectable TNF
  Mean total : ~4.9e6 TNF molecules over 14 hr; log-normal distribution (Fig 5C)
  Notes      : Bead fluorescence imaged in storage chambers; tnfSecretionDetection
               returns > 0 if any time-point bead exceeds LOD; bead1Washed used
               as QC gate key (consistent with moazami assaySignal40 pattern)
  Branch     : tnfSignal > 0  -> BB5 (bsbbif_5_t): dispose(bead1Washed)   [TNF+]
               tnfSignal <= 0 -> BB6 (bsbbif_6_f): drain(bead1Washed)     [TNF-]
               Both -> BB7 (bsbbif_7_j): terminal; dispose(tnfSignal); NOP


SINGLE-CELL RESPONSE HETEROGENEITY
------------------------------------
NF-kB subpopulations (n = 42, 2 hr LPS pulse):
  1 Peak     : n = 7   (single NF-kB peak during stimulation)
  Late Rise  : n = 9   (single peak followed by later secondary rise)
  Multi Peak : n = 23  (single peak followed by sustained oscillations)
  Minimal    : n = 3   (no or minimal NF-kB response)

TNF secretion subpopulations (n = 46, 2 hr LPS pulse):
  1 Peak     : n = 21  (single temporally limited TNF pulse, within 2 hr)
  Extended   : n = 8   (TNF release for extended period, within 4 hr)
  Other      : n = 14  (multi-peak or atypical temporal profiles)
  No secr.   : n = 3   (no detectable TNF above LOD in any 2 hr window)

NF-kB / TNF cross-correlation (Fig 6A, qualitative):
  NF-kB+ / TNF+ : 87.1%   NF-kB+ / TNF- : 6.5%
  NF-kB- / TNF+ : 6.5%    NF-kB- / TNF- : 0%
  Quantitative Pearson r (amplitude) = -0.06 (no correlation)
  TRIF activity vs. TNF amplitude Pearson r = 0.88 (strong correlation, model)

  720m  (overnight rinse)            -> IR: 43200.0 SECOND

   90m  (bead binding)               -> IR:  5400.0 SECOND

Known compiler behaviors applied:
  - drain in .bs source correctly lowered to dispose() in IR (known compiler bug;
    .bs source is semantically correct and preserved as-is)
  - True-branch blocks (bsbbif_2_t, bsbbif_5_t) emit no unconditional jump
    instruction in IR text; DOT graph resolves branch edges correctly
  - h duration token unsupported; all long durations converted to m

Cell-thread design pattern:
  mix is a consuming operation on both operands in BioScript. The cell state is
  modelled as a linear fluid variable re-produced at each pump cycle by mixing
  with the incoming medium or bead-wash product, maintaining a single ownership
  chain across 14 sequential steps without double-consumption.

bead1Washed design:
  The baseline bead variable (bead1Washed, produced in STEP 4) is intentionally
  held alive through the entire main block to serve as the TNF QC gate target in
  BB5/BB6, after the NF-kB gate has resolved at BB4. This mirrors the
  assaySignal40 pattern used in the moazami_world_to_chip_dmf reference file.
