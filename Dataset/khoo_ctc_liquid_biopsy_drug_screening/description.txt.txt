PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Liquid Biopsy and Therapeutic Response: Circulating Tumor Cell Cultures for Evaluation of Anticancer Treatment
Source Link  : https://www.science.org/doi/pdf/10.1126/sciadv.1600274
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1600274
Journal      : Science Advances, 2016, Vol. 2, Article e1600274
Platform     : Integrated three-layer PDMS microfluidic device (continuous-flow); tree-like gradient generator + channel barrier + tapered microwell array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an integrated microfluidic approach for short-term primary circulating tumor cell
(CTC) culture from patient whole blood and in situ anticancer drug screening. Blood samples from breast
cancer patients are collected via liquid biopsy, lysed to remove red blood cells, and the resulting
nucleated cell fraction (WBCs + CTCs) is seeded directly into a custom device incorporating arrays of
tapered elliptical microwells. No pre-enrichment of CTCs is required before culture.

Cells are cultured under hypoxic conditions (1% O2, mimicking the in vivo tumor microenvironment) for
11 days, during which CTC-containing clusters form in the tapered microwells. Culture positivity is
assessed on Day 11 by bright-field imaging. Positive samples (clusters in >=50% of microwells) proceed
to in situ drug screening: a tree-like serpentine gradient generator simultaneously delivers eight
different doxorubicin concentrations across eight parallel culture channels. After 72 hours of drug
exposure, cell viability is assessed by LIVE/DEAD fluorescence staining (calcein-AM / EtBr / CD45-APC),
and IC50 values are extracted by 4-parameter logistic curve fitting.

The complete procedure from blood collection to IC50 readout takes 14 days. Rapid IC50 feedback
enables immediate clinical intervention upon detection of drug resistance or tolerance.
Clinically validated with 73 blood samples from 55 breast cancer patients.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
The integrated device consists of three bonded PDMS layers assembled by oxygen plasma treatment:

Layer 1 (top)    : Tree-like serpentine gradient generator. Mixes two inlet flows (0% vehicle +
                   100% drug) to produce 8 output concentrations simultaneously at 100 uL/min.
                   Concentration gradient confirmed stable over 24 h (P < 0.05) and independent
                   of input dye concentration across 5 flow rates (25-200 uL/min).
                   Fabricated by standard photolithography on SU-8 2050 / SU-8 2000.5 silicon wafer.

Layer 2 (middle) : Channel barrier. Aluminum mold (Whits Technologies, Singapore). Defines 8
                   parallel culture channels; prevents cross-contamination between concentration zones.

Layer 3 (bottom) : Custom multimicrowell array. Each of the 8 channels contains ~1000 tapered
                   elliptical microwells (250 x 150 um footprint, 150 um depth, 4.7 mm pitch).
                   Fabricated by diffuser back-side lithography: laser direct writing on soda-lime
                   optical mask blank (Heidelberg DWL 66fs, Coherent I326C Ar laser), Cr etching,
                   SU-8 2100 resist (300 um thick, double spin-coat at 1500 rpm), UV exposure
                   through opal diffusing glass (Edmund Optics) at 12 mW/cm2, 365 nm, 9 s.
                   Dome-shaped SU-8 pillars (height 150 um) serve as PDMS casting template.

Microwell geometry: Tapered (dome-bottom) elliptical wells preferred over cylindrical wells because
   the inclined walls allow cells to settle to the center, forming a single dense cluster per well
   rather than multiple small aggregates. Tapered design retains 88 +/- 20% cell viability vs
   31.5 +/- 3% for cylindrical microwells.

Device integration: Assembled device placed in 150-mm dish filled with thin PBS film to limit
   evaporation; incubated in humidified chamber. Syringe pump (NE-1000, New Era Pump Systems)
   connected via BD Luer-Lok syringes for all infusion/withdrawal operations.


CELL SOURCE AND CLINICAL SAMPLES
---------------------------------
- Primary circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from breast cancer patients (n = 73 blood samples,
  55 patients; early-stage, newly diagnosed, locally advanced, or refractory metastatic).
- Study approved by DSRB (references 2012/00105, 2012/00979, 2010/00270, 2010/00691).
  All patients provided informed consent.
- Control validation: MCF-7 (HTB-22TM, ATCC) human breast adenocarcinoma cell line used
  to characterise and validate assay conditions before clinical sample evaluation.
  MCF-7 maintained in high-glucose DMEM (Invitrogen) + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep;
  37 C, 5% CO2, 80% confluence; subcultured twice weekly; 0.01% trypsin / 5.3 mM EDTA.
- Presence of cancer cells in clinical samples validated by fluorescence in situ hybridization
  (FISH) for breast cancer-associated markers TOP2A (Abbott) and CCND1 (Empire Genomics).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Blood Collection and RBC Lysis
  Reagents    : Whole blood (EDTA vacutainer, Becton-Dickinson), RBC lysis buffer (Life Technologies)
  Volume      : Equiv. 10 mL whole blood per evaluation run (1.25 mL per channel)
  Procedure   : Blood samples stored in EDTA vacutainer; lysed within 10 h of withdrawal.
                RBC lysis buffer mixed with whole blood for 3-5 min at room temperature (25 C)
                to lyse erythrocytes while retaining nucleated cells (WBCs + CTCs).
  BioScript   : lysateMix = mix bloodSample with lysisBuffer for 5m
                heat lysateMix at 25c for 5m

Step 2 | PBS Wash of Nucleated Cell Fraction
  Reagents    : Sterile PBS
  Procedure   : Lysed sample washed once with sterile PBS at room temperature to remove
                residual lysis buffer and cellular debris. Nucleated cell pellet retained.
  BioScript   : washedCells = mix lysateMix with pbsWash for 2m

Step 3 | Cell Seeding into Microfluidic Device (Day 0)
  Reagents    : Supplemented DMEM (DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep)
  Procedure   : Nucleated cells resuspended in 1.6 mL supplemented DMEM; 200 uL added to
                each of 8 channels via syringe pump at 100 uL/min (equiv. 1.25 mL blood/channel).
                Samples diluted and evenly mixed before addition to minimise cell number variation
                between microwells and channels. Device kept in humidified chamber.
  BioScript   : seededCulture = mix washedCells with seedingMedia for 5m
                (BioScript `mix` abstracts the syringe-pump seeding flow-through event)

Step 4 | Day 0-3 Culture (Incubation Cycle 1)
  Conditions  : 37 C, 5% CO2, hypoxia 1% O2 (clinical samples); humidified chamber
  Duration    : 3 days = 4320 min = 259200 s
  BioScript   : heat seededCulture at 37c for 4320m

Step 5 | Media Exchange #1 (Day 3)
  Reagents    : Supplemented DMEM (fresh)
  Procedure   : 150 uL medium withdrawn from each channel via syringe pump (100 uL/min,
                infusion/withdrawal mode, BD Luer-Lok syringe). 150 uL fresh supplemented
                DMEM introduced per channel. Closed system incubated at 37 C / 5% CO2.
  BioScript   : culture_d3 = mix seededCulture with freshMedia1 for 2m

Step 6 | Day 3-6 Culture (Incubation Cycle 2)
  Conditions  : 37 C, 5% CO2, hypoxia 1% O2; humidified chamber
  Duration    : 3 days = 4320 min = 259200 s
  BioScript   : heat culture_d3 at 37c for 4320m

Step 7 | Media Exchange #2 (Day 6)
  Reagents    : Supplemented DMEM (fresh)
  Procedure   : Same as Step 5.
  BioScript   : culture_d6 = mix culture_d3 with freshMedia2 for 2m

Step 8 | Day 6-9 Culture (Incubation Cycle 3)
  Conditions  : 37 C, 5% CO2, hypoxia 1% O2; humidified chamber
  Duration    : 3 days = 4320 min = 259200 s
  BioScript   : heat culture_d6 at 37c for 4320m

Step 9 | Media Exchange #3 (Day 9)
  Reagents    : Supplemented DMEM (fresh)
  Procedure   : Same as Step 5.
  BioScript   : culture_d9 = mix culture_d6 with freshMedia3 for 2m

Step 10 | Day 9-11 Culture (Incubation Cycle 4)
  Conditions  : 37 C, 5% CO2, hypoxia 1% O2; humidified chamber
  Duration    : 2 days = 2880 min = 172800 s
  BioScript   : heat culture_d9 at 37c for 2880m

Step 11 | Day 11 - Culture Positivity Assessment
  Method      : Bright-field imaging at 10x magnification (Olympus inverted confocal microscope).
                Gray-value plot profiles obtained per microwell across maximum diameter (ImageJ).
                Combined scatterplots of gray values normalised to highest count per microwell.
                Microwells with dense cell clusters show gray values consistently <0.5 within
                microwell region. Microwells with debris or sparse monolayers show high gray values.
  Threshold   : Clusters in >=50% of microwells (~500 clusters from 1.25 mL blood) = POSITIVE.
                Debris/sparse monolayers only = NEGATIVE.
  Note        : Cultures should not have RBC contamination (incomplete RBC lysis impairs cluster
                boundary detection by image processing software).
  Branch      : clusterSignal > 0  -> Positive; proceed to drug infusion (BB2 / bsbbif_2_t)
                clusterSignal <= 0 -> Negative; drain culture, experiment ends (BB3 / bsbbif_3_f)
  Efficiency  : Pretreatment: 59.3% positive (n=27); Posttreatment: 19.6% positive (n=46);
                Overall: 34.2% positive (n=73)
  BioScript   : clusterSignal = detect clusterDetection on culture_d9 for 30s

[POSITIVE CULTURE PATH ONLY -- Steps 12-16]

Step 12 | Pre-Drug Medium Priming Flush (Day 11)
  Reagents    : Supplemented DMEM (fresh)
  Procedure   : 150 uL medium withdrawn per channel before drug loading.
                Device briefly primed with fresh DMEM at 100 uL/min prior to drug-containing
                medium introduction to clear existing medium from channels.
  BioScript   : primedCulture = mix culture_d9 with primingFlush for 2m

Step 13 | Doxorubicin Gradient Preparation and Infusion (Day 11)
  Reagents    : DoxorubicinStock (1 mM in DMSO; 1 uL into 1 mL DMEM -> ~1 uM working stock,
                ~0.1% DMSO final), SupplementedDMEM (vehicle control, 0% drug)
  Gradient    : Tree-like gradient generator mixes 0% vehicle and 100% drug inlets at 100 uL/min
                to produce 8 concentrations simultaneously:
                  Channel 0:  0.00 uM  (vehicle control; viability normalisation reference)
                  Channel 1: ~0.14 uM  (~14% of stock)
                  Channel 2: ~0.29 uM  (~29%)
                  Channel 3: ~0.43 uM  (~43%)
                  Channel 4: ~0.57 uM  (~57%)
                  Channel 5: ~0.71 uM  (~71%)
                  Channel 6: ~0.86 uM  (~86%)
                  Channel 7: ~1.00 uM  (100% of working stock)
  Procedure   : Drug loading carried out carefully at 100 uL/min (inward infusion) to avoid
                drug influx upon reinsertion of tubings. Evaporation limited by humidified chamber;
                gradient concentration remains stable over 72 h exposure period.
  BioScript   : 8 dispense calls (1x SupplementedDMEM + 7x DoxorubicinStock) followed by
                binary-tree mix operations representing gradient channel assignments.
                (BioScript abstraction: no native gradient-generator primitive available)

Step 14 | 72-Hour Drug Exposure Incubation (Day 11-14)
  Conditions  : 37 C, 5% CO2, hypoxia 1% O2; humidified chamber
  Duration    : 72 h = 4320 min = 259200 s
  Notes       : Gradient concentration remains constant over time. Vehicle control channel
                (culture_ch0, 0 uM doxorubicin) and drug-treated channels (drug_all)
                incubated simultaneously; treated as separate BioScript fluid variables.
  BioScript   : heat culture_ch0 at 37c for 4320m
                heat drug_all at 37c for 4320m

Step 15 | Day 14 - LIVE/DEAD In Situ Staining
  Reagents    : Calcein-AM (2 uM; Life Technologies), EtBr (ethidium bromide; red),
                CD45-allophycocyanin (APC; 1:100; Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific)
  Stain roles : Calcein-AM (green, ex 490 nm / em 526 nm) -> viable cells
                EtBr (red, ex 488 nm / em 617 nm) -> dead/damaged cells
                CD45-APC (red) -> leukocyte marker; gates out WBCs; only CD45- cells (green)
                counted for clinical sample viability rate determination
  Procedure   : Staining cocktail incubated in situ for 45 min at room temperature in dark.
                Samples flushed gently with PBS. Imaged with Olympus inverted confocal
                microscope (emission filters ET460/50m and ET535/50m).
  Notes       : Two separate stain dispenses (stainCtrl for vehicle control channel;
                stainDrug for drug-treated channels) required per BioScript ownership rules.
  BioScript   : stainCtrl = dispense 1 units of CalceinAM_EtBr_CD45APC_Stain
                stainDrug = dispense 1 units of CalceinAM_EtBr_CD45APC_Stain
                stained_ctrl = mix culture_ch0 with stainCtrl for 45m
                stained_drug = mix drug_all with stainDrug for 45m

Step 16 | Day 14 - Fluorescence Detection and IC50 Readout
  Module      : ctcViabilityDetection
  Procedure   :
    1. Z-stack images acquired from 25 microwells per channel at 37 C using confocal microscope.
       Stack slices: 15 um; compiled to max-intensity merged image.
       Microwells at consistent distance from assay inlet selected for evaluation.
    2. Images preprocessed by cropping and thresholding (signals 8-150 um);
       merged images compared to rule out repeated signal counts.
    3. CD45- gating: only green-fluorescent (calcein-AM+, CD45-) cells counted per microwell
       for clinical samples (rules out WBC signal).
    4. Viability % per channel normalised to vehicle control channel (channel 0, lowest drug).
    5. 4-parameter logistic equation curve fitting (Microsoft Excel).
    6. IC50 extracted as drug concentration at 50% normalised cell death (y axis).
  Branch      : viabilitySignal > 0  -> valid readout; dispose stained droplets (BB5)
                viabilitySignal <= 0 -> staining/imaging failure; drain all (BB6)
  BioScript   : viabilitySignal = detect ctcViabilityDetection on stained_ctrl for 45m


KEY RESULTS
-----------
MCF-7 cell line IC50  : 0.78 +/- 0.02 uM doxorubicin (4-parameter logistic fit; n=3 triplicate
                         cultures). Slightly higher than monolayer culture values (~0.5 uM)
                         due to heightened cell density in multilayer clusters reducing drug penetration.

Clinical IC50 values (breast cancer patients):
  Sample CTB039  | Pretreatment  | IC50 = 0.85 uM
  Sample CES021  | Posttreatment | IC50 > 1 uM  (drug resistance suspected)
  Sample CES053  | Posttreatment | IC50 = 0.34 uM
  Sample P2B28   | Posttreatment | IC50 > 1 uM  (drug resistance suspected)
  Sample P2B29   | Pretreatment  | IC50 = 0.94 uM
  Sample P2B29   | Posttreatment | IC50 = 0.86 uM (slight IC50 reduction; possible drug sensitivity
                                                    increase after doxorubicin + sunitinib cycle)

Cluster formation and drug response: Percentage of microwells with clusters decreased with
   increasing doxorubicin concentration (average correlation coefficient -0.71), confirming
   that cluster formation correlates inversely with drug sensitivity (consistent with prior work).
   Macrophage-like cell counts per microwell also correlated negatively with cluster formation
   and may serve as an additional prognostic indicator.

Known compiler behaviours observed in IR:
  - `drain` in .bs source lowered to `dispose()` in IR (known bug; .bs semantics preserved)
  - True-branch blocks (bsbbif_N_t) do not emit unconditional jump in IR text;
    DOT edge bsbbif_5_t -> bsbbif_7_j resolves correctly in DAG
  - `heat` is void/in-place; fluid variable not consumed by heat instructions
  - PHI nodes emitted at bsbbif_4_j (cluster join) for all variables assigned in pass branch only

BioScript abstraction notes:
  - Gradient generator: 8-channel simultaneous concentration gradient abstracted as
    8 discrete DoxorubicinStock dispenses followed by binary-tree mix operations.
    No native gradient-generator primitive exists in BioScript.
  - Hypoxia: 1% O2 chamber condition not expressible in BioScript; documented in comments;
    encoded as heat at 37 C.
  - Media exchange: each cycle modelled as dispense SupplementedDMEM + mix with culture
    variable; fluid ownership re-assigned at each exchange per single-use variable rule.
  - Staining: two separate dispense calls (stainCtrl, stainDrug) to satisfy single-use
    ownership constraint; a single stainReagent variable used in two mix calls would
    constitute a double-consume violation.
  - viabilitySignal scoping: assigned and disposed entirely within the cluster-pass branch (BB2);
    never referenced on the cluster-fail path (BB3); clusterSignal assigned unconditionally
    before its if/else and disposed unconditionally at the join (BB4).
