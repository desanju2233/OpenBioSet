PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Expansion of Patient-Derived Circulating Tumor Cells from Liquid Biopsies Using a CTC Microfluidic Culture Device
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/nprot.2017.125
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2017.125
Journal      : Nature Protocols, 2018, Vol. 13, No. 1, pp. 34-58
Platform     : Three-layer PDMS microfluidic device (gradient generator / barrier layer / microwell array) fabricated by soft lithography


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidics-based method for the ex vivo expansion of patient-derived
circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from liquid biopsies (peripheral blood) into CTC clusters, without
prior enrichment or exogenous growth factor supplements. The approach co-cultures CTCs with
patient-derived white blood cells (WBCs) in inverted dome-shaped elliptical microwells under
hypoxia (1% O2, 37 C), mimicking the tumor microenvironment niche. Monocyte-derived macrophages
within the co-culture differentiate into tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs) and are proposed as
key drivers of cluster formation.

The protocol is divided into five functional phases:
  1. Device priming and gradient generator calibration with FITC fluorescent dye (Steps 53-71)
  2. Blood preparation: RBC lysis and nucleated cell resuspension (Steps 72-77)
  3. CTC cluster culture in microwells under hypoxia for 14 days (Steps 78-79)
  4. Drug screening using the integrated gradient generator (Step 82)
  5. Immunofluorescence staining and imaging of CTC clusters (Steps 83-86)

Optional downstream procedures include post-enrichment with anti-CD45 microbeads (Step 81)
and DNA/RNA FISH for cancer cell identification (Steps 87-99).

Overall cluster formation success rate: >50% of clinical blood samples.
Assay duration from blood draw to cluster read-out: 2 weeks.
Drug screening after cluster formation: 48 h drug exposure + fluorescence read-out.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i   : Concentration gradient generator (uppermost).
            Fabricated by standard PDMS replica molding on silicon wafer / SU-8 master
            (photolithography). Tree-like branched channel network splits two inlet streams
            into 8 output channels with a stable concentration gradient.
            Inlets: 2 (drug / vehicle control). Outlets: 8 channels.

Layer ii  : Barrier layer (middle).
            Fabricated from aluminum mold by machining; PDMS soft replica.
            Millimeter-scale barrier channels compartmentalise the 8 gradient outputs into
            individual cell-culture lanes, preventing cross-mixing at the microwell region.

Layer iii : Microwell layer (bottom).
            Fabricated by diffuser back-side lithography on a 4-inch soda lime mask plate
            coated with SU-8 3050 (double coat, >300 um total thickness).
            Opal diffuser (45 deg) generates dome-shaped exposure profile.
            Results in inverted dome-shaped elliptical microwells:
            150 um (width) x 250 um (length) x 150 um (depth); average diameter 187.5 um.
            1350 microwells per 60-mm Petri dish (laser ablation variant).
            8 columns of microwells aligned to 8 barrier channels.

Assembly   : Layers bonded by oxygen plasma treatment (150 W, 0.05 torr, 90 s) forming
             covalent -O-Si-O- bonds; baked at 80 C for >=1 h.
Material   : PDMS (Sylgard 184, 10:1 base:curing agent); biocompatible, optically
             transparent (240-1100 nm), elastomeric for accurate replica molding.

Alternate microwell fabrication: Laser ablation (CO2 laser, VLS 2.30; 10% speed, 50% power;
             laser-to-substrate distance 3 cm) on 35- or 60-mm Petri dishes (Box 1).


CELL LINES AND CLINICAL SAMPLES
--------------------------------
- MCF-7 and MDA-MB-231 (ATCC): commercially available breast cancer cell lines used for
  device characterisation and spheroid formation validation.
  Culture: DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen-strep, 37 C, 5% CO2.
- Patient blood samples: 10 mL per patient, EDTA Vacutainer tubes, kept on ice.
  Cancer types validated: breast, lung, spinal cancer; locally advanced, early-stage,
  and refractory metastatic disease.
  Ethical approval: DSRB references 2012/00105, 2012/00979, 2010/00270, 2010/00691.
  All samples processed within 6 h of draw.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 53-56 | Device Priming
  Reagents  : 70% (vol/vol) ethanol, PBS (1x)
  Operation : Two syringes of 70% ethanol injected at 50 uL/min (~5 min) via two syringe pumps
              to prime the gradient generator and remove air bubbles. Followed by two syringes
              of PBS at 100 uL/min (~5 min) to remove ethanol residue.
  Purpose   : Hydrophilise PDMS channels; remove air; sterilise device before cell culture.
  Critical  : Replace syringe by disconnecting from tip (not tubing) to avoid new air bubbles.

Step 57-68 | Gradient Generator Calibration (FITC Dye)
  Reagents  : 100 uM FITC fluorescent dye (fluorescein isothiocyanate isomer I) in PBS,
              deionised (DI) water
  Operation : Syringe 1 (FITC dye) and Syringe 2 (DI water) pumped simultaneously at equal
              flow rates. After 30 min equilibration, 60 uL collected from each of the 8
              channels into a 384-well plate (Tecan Infinite M200 fluorescence plate reader).
  Flow rates : 25, 50, 100, 150, 200 uL/min (5 independent runs per device).
  Additional : 20 uM FITC at 100 uL/min to confirm concentration-independence of gradient.
  Repeats   : Three independent devices (Step 68).
  Results   : Concentration gradients similar across flow rates; close to theoretical values;
              no leakage observed; gradient independent of dye concentration.
  Purpose   : Verify uniform outlet flow rates, stable gradient generation, and device integrity.

Step 72-73 | Blood Preparation - RBC Lysis
  Reagents  : Patient blood (10 mL, EDTA Vacutainer), RBC lysis buffer (G-Bioscience 786650)
  Ratio     : 10 mL blood + 20 mL RBC lysis buffer (1:2 vol/vol)
  Duration  : 3 min at room temperature with gentle mixing (hypotonic hemolysis by osmotic stress)
  Critical  : Both blood and buffer at room temperature before mixing. Do not exceed 5 min.

Step 74-75 | Nucleated Cell Isolation
  Operation : Centrifuge lysed sample 1000g, 5 min, room temperature. Remove supernatant
              (plasma + lysed RBC fragments). Resuspend pellet in 5 mL PBS; centrifuge 335g,
              3 min to wash. Repeat wash if debris remains.
  Result    : Nucleated cell fraction (WBCs + CTCs); RBC lysis does not significantly affect
              nucleated cell viability.
  Storage   : Optional: add 10% (vol/vol) DMSO; cryopreserve at -80 C up to 4 weeks;
              long-term in liquid nitrogen up to 1 year.

Step 76-77 | Cell Resuspension and Seeding
  Reagents  : Supplemented DMEM (10% FBS, 1% pen-strep)
  Volume    : Resuspend in 1 mL per 1000 microwells; add 200-300 uL per channel.
  Seeding   : Volume-based (not count-based); multichannel pipette for simultaneous seeding.
  Culture   : 37 C, 5% CO2, 1% O2 (hypoxia incubator, humidified). Day 0.
  Note      : No surfactant coating required for clinical samples; non-adherent blood cells
              provide cushioning for CTCs in microwells.

Step 78 | Day-3 Media Change
  Operation : Remove 0.2 mL old medium (hand pipette or syringe pump at 200 uL/min).
              Replace with 0.2 mL fresh supplemented DMEM.
  Critical  : Minimal disturbance to microwell clusters to prevent cell loss.
              Increase frequency of media change if media turns yellow before 72 h.

Step 79 | Day-14 Cluster Assessment
  Operation : Phase-contrast microscopy of full microwell array.
  Criteria  :
    Morphological : Multilayered cell aggregate with defined boundaries.
    Cell count    : >=8 cells per 250 um^2 (Hoechst staining, Step 83A/83B).
    Cell density  : Low grayscale values in ImageJ histogram of microwell region.
  Threshold : >=50% microwells contain clusters = positive sample.
  Results   : >50% cluster formation rate across >300 clinical samples screened;
              73.3% in refractory metastatic cohort (n=44); 70/110 in locally advanced cancer.
  Note      : Cultures can be retained up to 2 months; can be passaged as spheroids to
              low-adherence dishes or 3D gels after 2 weeks.

Step 80 | Cluster Harvest
  Reagents  : PBS, trypsin 0.25% (wt/vol) with EDTA
  Operation : Remove media; wash with 1 mL PBS. Add 1 mL trypsin; incubate 37 C for 3 min.
              Neutralise with equal volume fresh supplemented DMEM. Dissociate by gentle
              pipetting. Centrifuge 335g, 3 min; resuspend pellet in 1 mL PBS.

Step 81 | Post-Enrichment (Optional)
  Reagents  : Anti-CD45 microbeads (Miltenyi Biotec, cat. no. 130-045-801)
  Purpose   : Negative selection to deplete CD45+ leukocytes; obtain higher-purity CTC fraction.
  Use case  : Required for downstream genomic sequencing; optional for drug screening.

Step 82 | Drug Screening
  Reagents  : Doxorubicin (Sigma-Aldrich D1515) dissolved in DMSO; supplemented DMEM
  Working concentration : 1 uM doxorubicin in supplemented DMEM (freshly prepared, store on ice)
  Delivery  : Gradient generator delivers 8-point dilution series across 8 channels in parallel
              (inlet 1: drug; inlet 2: DMSO vehicle control). Introduced by hand pipette or
              syringe pump.
  Duration  : 48 h incubation at 37 C (= 2880 min)
  Viability : Calcein AM (1:1000 in PBS; live cells; ex 490 nm, em 526 nm) and
              propidium iodide (1:1000 in PBS; dead cells; ex 488 nm, em 617 nm).
              Incubate 15 min before imaging.
  Read-out  : Cell viability (%) vs. drug concentration (%); IC50 determination.

Step 83-84 | Immunofluorescence Staining (Fixed-Cell Protocol, Option B)
  (i)   Fix cells: 4% (wt/vol) PFA in PBS, 10 min on ice.
  (ii)  Block: 2.5% (wt/vol) BSA in PBS, 30 min on ice.
  (iii) Permeabilise: 0.1% (vol/vol) Triton X-100 in PBS, 1 min on ice.
  (iv)  Wash with PBS; centrifuge 335g, 3 min.
  (v)   Primary antibody staining in 2.5% BSA/PBS, 30 min on ice (dark):
          - pan-cytokeratin (CK) antibody (FITC, 1:50): epithelial CTC marker
          - CD45 antibody (APC, 1:50): leukocyte marker
          - Hoechst dye (1 M, 1:1000): nuclear counterstain
  Wash  : 5 mL PBS; resuspend in 100 uL PBS for imaging.
  Key phenotypes:
          CK+/CD45-/Hoechst+  = putative CTC
          CD45+/Hoechst+       = leukocyte feeder cell
  Imaging : Transfer to 8-well chamber (Nunc Lab-Tek); Olympus inverted microscope
            (IX71) with filter sets ET460/50m, ET535/50m, ET605/70; automated stage.
            Image entire well or single fields; analyse with IMARIS or ImageJ.

Steps 87-99 | DNA FISH (Optional, 2 days)
  Purpose   : Identify cancer cells by copy number of breast cancer-associated genes
              (CCND1, HER2, FGFR1, MYC, TOP2A, ZNF217; Table 1 in paper).
  Key steps : Fixation (acetic acid:methanol 1:3); RNase treatment (45 min, 37 C);
              denaturation (70% formamide in 2x SSC, 80 C, 10 min); probe hybridisation
              (42 C, 16 h); DAPI counterstain; confocal imaging (Olympus IX71).
  Note      : Not encoded in BioScript (no fluid primitive for FISH hybridisation steps).


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : khoo_ctc_microfluidic_cluster.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG            : khoo_ctc_microfluidic_cluster_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations converted to minutes (m) for compiler compatibility
                 (BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported)
                 72h   ->  4320m  (Day 0 to Day 3 culture;  IR: 259200.0 SECOND)
                 11d   -> 15840m  (Day 3 to Day 14 culture; IR: 950400.0 SECOND)
                 48h   ->  2880m  (drug incubation;         IR: 172800.0 SECOND)
Modules        : clusterFormationDetection, viabilityDetection,
                 immunostainingDetection, drugEfficacyDetection
Omissions      : Photolithography / soft-lithography device fabrication (Steps 1-52)
                 has no BioScript fluid primitive equivalent and is omitted by design.
                 DNA FISH procedure (Steps 87-99) is omitted for the same reason.
                 Anti-CD45 post-enrichment (Step 81) is included as a mix + drain
                 sequence within BB1 (no independent QC gate).
Scoping note   : All QC-gated variables (clusterSignal, drugSignal, stainingSignal)
                 are defined at module scope (BB1/main) to satisfy the lilott8/BioScript
                 requirement that variables consumed by downstream BBs must not be
                 defined inside an if/else block. Inner-block operations are limited to
                 dispose/drain of same-block droplets only (mirrors samlali and moazami
                 reference .bs files).
Drain note     : BioScript `drain` compiles to `dispose(...)` at IR level. Distinction
                 is preserved at .bs source level for human readability only.
