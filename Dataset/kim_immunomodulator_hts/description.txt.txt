PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Discovery of New States of Immunomodulation for Vaccine Adjuvants via High
               Throughput Screening: Expanding Innate Responses to PRRs
Source Link  : https://pubs.acs.org/doi/pdf/10.1021/acscentsci.2c01351?ref=article_openPDF
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.2c01351
Journal      : ACS Central Science, 2023, Vol. 9, pp. 427-439
Platform     : High-throughput robotic liquid handling (384-well plate, pin-drop dispenser,
               plate reader, AlphaLISA); in vivo murine vaccination model (C57/B6 mice)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a high-throughput screening (HTS) pipeline to discover small-molecule
"immunomodulators" that alter the activity of two key innate immune signalling pathways:
the NF-kB pathway (nuclear factor kappa-light-chain-enhancer of activated B-cells) and the
IRF pathway (interferon regulatory factors). Immunomodulators act on the signalling network
downstream of pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) rather than binding to the PRR itself,
enabling fine-tuned customisation of innate immune responses.

The pipeline is divided into three sequential phases:
  1. Primary Screen: NF-kB and IRF transcription factor activity in RAW-Dual macrophages
     (murine reporter cell line) across 13 PRR agonists and ~3,147 modulators at 10 uM;
     384-well format; pin-drop robotic dispensing; 24 h readout.
  2. Secondary Screen: Six-plex AlphaLISA cytokine panel in THP-1 monocytes (human);
     720 downselected modulators x 7 PRR agonists; cytokines: IL-1beta, IL-12p40,
     IP-10 (CXCL10), CCL4, TNF-alpha, IFN-beta; quantitative vaccine score computed.
  3. In Vivo Validation: Lead modulators tested in murine prime-boost vaccination model
     (OVA subunit + commercial Fluzone 2021-2022 and Typhim-Vi vaccines); endpoints are
     antigen-specific serum IgG at Day 49 and systemic TNF-alpha at 1 h post-injection.

Key findings:
  - Modulators inhibit and enhance NF-kB and IRF independently by >100-fold.
  - Some modulators are "generalists" (active across nearly all 13 PRR agonists);
    others are "specialists" (active against 1 or a small subset of receptors).
  - Lead antibody-enhancing modulator PME-564 (Lyn kinase / tyrosine kinase inhibitor)
    produces a ~4-fold log AUC increase in anti-HA IgG when combined with Fluzone.
  - Lead cytokine-reducing modulators (PME-1226, PME-397) significantly decrease
    systemic TNF-alpha when combined with CpG (TLR9) agonist.
  - In vivo success rate: ~10-20% of lead candidates validated in murine model.


SCREENING LIBRARY
-----------------
Primary library    : 3,147 compounds total
  Composition      : 246 NF-kB/IRF inhibitors + 2,895 pathway-specific inhibitors
  Many compounds   : Previously studied or FDA-approved in other indications
Compound dose      : 10 uM final concentration (<0.05% DMSO v/v)
Addition method    : Pin-drop (robotic; ~23 nL of 10 mM DMSO stock per 230 uL well)
Downselection      : PCA on 16 variables (NF-kB + IRF activity across 8 high-Z-factor
                     agonists); compounds outside radius 1.75 retained -> 720 compounds
                     for secondary library (SI Appendix Figures S4, Table S3)


PRR AGONISTS
------------
Primary screen : 13 agonists (majority TLRs; SI Appendix Table S2)
  Pam3CSK4     (TLR2/1)      Pam2CSK4    (TLR2/6)
  Poly I:C     (TLR3)        LPS         (TLR4)
  MPLA         (TLR4)        Flagellin   (TLR5)
  Imiquimod    (TLR7)        TL8-506     (TLR8)
  ODN 1826     (TLR9)        3'3'-cGAMP  (STING)
  Tri-DAP      (NOD1)        MDP         (NOD2)
  mIFN-B       (IFNAR1/2)

Agonist concentration : EC50 per agonist (SI Appendix Table S2); occasionally adjusted
                        if modulatory range exceeded dynamic limit

Secondary screen : 7 agonist subset (Pam3CSK4, Pam2CSK4, Poly I:C, LPS, Flagellin,
                   R848 TLR7/8, 3'3'-cGAMP STING)

In vivo subunit model : R848 (TLR7/8), Flagellin (TLR5), CpG 1826 (TLR9)


CELL LINES
----------
- RAW-Dual macrophages (murine): dual NF-kB SEAP / IRF luciferase reporter system;
  both pathways measured in parallel from the same well.
  Primary screen only; cultured in DMEM + 10% FBS, 37 C, 5% CO2.

- THP-1 monocytes (human): secondary screen; selected because human AlphaLISA reagents
  provide higher cytokine multiplexing capacity than murine reagents.
  Cultured in RPMI-1640 + 10% FBS, 37 C, 5% CO2.

- BMDC (murine bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells): off-chip validation of lead compounds
  for cytokine production and cell surface marker expression before in vivo dosing
  (SI Appendix Figure S10).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Primary Screen -- Cell Seeding
  Cell line    : RAW-Dual macrophages
  Plate format : 384-well
  Operation    : Robotic seeding at optimised cell density
  Incubation   : 24 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 for cell adherence before compound addition
  Purpose      : Establish monolayer with consistent NF-kB/IRF dynamic range
  Reference    : SI Appendix Figure S1

Step 2 | Primary Screen -- Immunomodulator Pin-Drop Addition
  Library size : ~3,147 compounds
  Method       : Pin-drop dispense; ~23 nL of 10 mM DMSO stock -> 10 uM final
  DMSO         : <0.05% v/v final
  Pre-incubation: 1 h at 37 C before agonist addition (equilibration/uptake)
  Notes        : Modulator added before agonist; not co-added

Step 3 | Primary Screen -- PRR Agonist Addition
  Agonists     : 13 PRR agonists at EC50; one agonist per well per modulator
  Method       : Robotic dispense from pre-prepared agonist stocks
  Notes        : Wide range covers TLR, NLR, STING, and IFNAR families; allows detection
                 of generalist vs specialist modulator activity

Step 4 | Primary Screen -- 24 h Co-incubation
  Temperature  : 37 C, 5% CO2
  Duration     : 24 h (= 1440 min; IR: 86400.0 SECOND)
  Outcome      : Transcription factor activity develops; supernatant drawn after incubation

Step 5 | Primary Screen -- Cell Viability QC
  Method       : Live-cell imaging + digital confluency mask (SI Appendix Figure S3)
  Purpose      : Identify and exclude toxic modulator/agonist combinations
  Outcome      : Compounds that reduce confluency below threshold are flagged and removed
                 before TF readout; <1% of library showed inherent pathway activation

Step 6 | Primary Screen -- Dual NF-kB / IRF Transcription Factor Readout
  NF-kB method : SEAP reporter; QUANTI-Blue reagent; absorbance 620 nm
  IRF method   : Luciferase reporter; QUANTI-Luc reagent; luminescence
  Both measured simultaneously from same supernatant well
  Report       : Fold change vs agonist-alone control
  Dynamic range: Inhibition and enhancement observed up to >100-fold in both directions
  Observation  : NF-kB and IRF modulation are largely independent (R2 = 0.0409 for LPS)
  PCA gate     : PC1+PC2 account for 49% of variance; retain outside radius 1.75;
                 3,147 -> 720 compounds advanced to secondary library

Step 7 | Secondary Screen -- THP-1 Cell Seeding
  Cell line    : THP-1 monocytes (human)
  Rationale    : Human AlphaLISA reagents; higher multiplexing than murine RAW-Dual
  Plate format : 384-well; identical robotic workflow to primary screen
  Incubation   : 24 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 for equilibration

Step 8 | Secondary Screen -- Re-apply Lead Modulator
  Compounds    : 720 downselected modulators
  Method       : Same pin-drop protocol; 10 uM final; <0.05% DMSO
  Pre-incubation: 1 h at 37 C before agonist addition
  Scale        : 720 modulators x 7 agonists = 5,040 wells per cytokine

Step 9 | Secondary Screen -- PRR Agonist Addition and 24 h Incubation
  Agonists     : 7-agonist subset of primary screen
  Incubation   : 24 h at 37 C, 5% CO2
  Supernatant  : Collected for AlphaLISA multiplexed measurement

Step 10 | Secondary Screen -- AlphaLISA Six-Plex Cytokine Detection
  Platform     : AlphaLISA (PerkinElmer); no-wash, multiplexed bead assay
  Excitation   : 680 nm; emission 615 nm (channel-switched per bead pair)
  Cytokines    : IL-1beta, IL-12p40, IP-10 (CXCL10), CCL4, TNF-alpha, IFN-beta
  Rationale per cytokine:
    TNF-alpha  : Baseline generalised inflammation; endogenous pyrogen
    IL-1beta   : Partially outside direct NF-kB regulation; enables pathway differentiation
    IFN-beta   : Strong IRF pathway correlate; antiviral type I interferon
    IL-12p40   : Activates NK cells; Th1/Th17 polarisation; adaptive immunity bridge
    IP-10      : Early adjuvant signal; T cell and DC chemoattractant; tolerability correlate
    CCL4       : Monocyte and NK cell chemoattractant; tolerability correlate
  Replicates   : Triplicate duplexed measurements per well
  Standard curves: Per cytokine per agonist (SI Appendix Table S5)
  Normalisation : Fold change vs agonist-alone; transformed to [-1,1] for vaccine score
  Crosstalk    : Corrected per SI Appendix Figure S5D

Step 11 | Secondary Screen -- Vaccine Score Computation and Downselection
  Method       : Weighted normalised cytokine score per modulator/agonist combination;
                 summed across agonists to produce generalist vaccine score
  Weights      : Positive for IFN-beta, IP-10, CCL4, IL-12; negative for TNF-alpha, IL-1beta
  Score range  : -20 (most negative; PME-2988) to +10 (most positive)
  Top generalist threshold: score >= 6 (approximately 20 compounds from 720 pool)
  Reference    : SI Appendix Figure S9

Step 12 | BMDC Validation (off-chip; lead compounds only)
  Cells        : Murine bone-marrow-derived dendritic cells (BMDC)
  Purpose      : Confirm cytokine production and cell surface markers in primary DCs
  Agonists     : Multiple (from primary screen subset)
  Reference    : SI Appendix Figure S10

Step 13 | In Vivo -- Prime Injection (Day 0)
  Model        : C57/B6 mice; i.m. injection
  Modulator dose: 1.5 umol in DMSO:Addavax 1:1 v/v
  Arms         :
    Arm A (Fluzone): Fluzone 2021-2022 (inactivated quadrivalent flu) + modulator
                     Endpoint: anti-HA IgG at Day 49 (log AUC)
    Arm B (Typhim): Typhim-Vi (carbohydrate typhoid vaccine) + modulator
                     Endpoint: systemic TNF-alpha at 1 h post-injection (pg/mL)
    Arm C (OVA):   OVA + R848/Flagellin/CpG1826 + modulator
                     Endpoint: anti-OVA IgG at Day 35 and Day 49 (n=4)
  Early cytokine: Serum collected at 1 h post-injection for TNF-alpha readout

Step 14 | In Vivo -- Boost Injection (Day 21)
  Schedule     : Day 21 post-prime
  Formulation  : Identical to prime; same modulator dose and vehicle
  Interval     : 21 days from prime (= 30240 min; IR: 1814400.0 SECOND)

Step 15 | In Vivo -- Antibody Titer Readout (Day 35 and Day 49)
  Method       : ELISA; antigen-specific serum IgG; log AUC reported
  Fluzone targets: A/Victoria/2570/2019 (HA); B/Phuket/3073/2013 (HA)
  OVA target   : Anti-OVA IgG D49 antibody level
  Timepoints   : Day 35 (14 days post-boost) and Day 49 (28 days post-boost; primary endpoint)
  Interval     : Day 21 to Day 49 = 28 days (= 40320 min; IR: 2419200.0 SECOND)
  Key result   : PME-564 + Fluzone -> ~4-fold log AUC increase vs Fluzone alone (****P<0.0001)
                 PME-564 + OVA subunit -> 2-6x IgG increase across all adjuvants tested
                 PME-1226 + PME-397 + CpG -> significant TNF-alpha reduction (Typhim model)
  n per group  : 4-5 mice


LEAD MODULATORS
---------------
PME-564   : Tyrosine kinase inhibitor (Lyn/Src-family kinase); top generalist vaccine score
            (~8-10). Enhances IP-10, IFN-beta, IL-12 across most agonists; suppresses TNF-alpha
            notably for Pam3CSK4 and R848. In vivo: ~4-fold log AUC IgG increase with Fluzone.
            Clinical precedent: used in myelogenous leukaemia treatment.
            Proposed mechanism: Lyn inhibition alters CpG trafficking from extracellular to
            internal endosomes, changing proinflammatory cytokine and Type I IFN production.

PME-2834  : Pan-WNK kinase inhibitor; originally discovered in hypertension research.
            WNK kinases implicated in NF-kB signalling. Improves antibody titers with Fluzone.

PME-397   : Cytokine reducer; significantly decreases TNF-alpha for CpG and R848 in vivo.

PME-1226  : Cytokine reducer; significantly decreases TNF-alpha for CpG in vivo.

PME-2988  : Negative vaccine score control (~-20). Eliminates IL-12, IFN-beta, IP-10;
            enhances IL-1beta and TNF-alpha ~100-fold. Not suitable as vaccine adjuvant;
            illustrates wide-ranging modulatory landscape.

PME-2539  : Candidate for cancer immunotherapy applications; enhances TNF-alpha ~36-fold
            with cGAMP and upregulates IFN-beta across all agonists tested.


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : kim_immunomodulator_hts.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG            : kim_immunomodulator_hts_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations converted to minutes (m) for compiler compatibility
                 (BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported)
                 24 h  -> 1440m   (cell incubation steps; IR: 86400.0 SECOND)
                 60 min -> 60m    (modulator pre-incubation; IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
                 21 days -> 30240m (prime-to-boost interval; IR: 1814400.0 SECOND)
                 28 days -> 40320m (boost-to-titer interval; IR: 2419200.0 SECOND)
Modules        : nfkbIrfDetection, cytokineDetection, viabilityDetection, vaccinationQC
Variables      : 50 named droplets across 3 phases
Biological scope note: The .bs file encodes all three phases (primary screen, secondary
                 screen, in vivo validation) in a single pipeline. The `mix` primitive
                 abstracts robotic liquid-handling steps (not DMF electrode actuation).
                 The `heat` primitive models both plate incubator steps (37 C cell culture)
                 and in vivo body-temperature distribution windows (37 C murine).
                 drain -> dispose in IR: expected compiler behaviour; drain has no distinct
                 IR opcode in lilott8/BioScript; does not affect CFG correctness.
