PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Microfluidic Cell Retention Device for Perfusion of Mammalian Suspension Culture
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-06949-8
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-06949-8
Journal      : Scientific Reports, 2017, Vol. 7, Article 6703
Platform     : Inertial microfluidic spiral channel (PDMS) coupled to stirred perfusion bioreactor


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a novel membrane-less cell retention device based on inertial sorting
for continuous perfusion culture of suspended mammalian CHO cells. The spiral microchannel
with trapezoidal cross-section focuses CHO cells (17.7 +/- 2.5 um diameter) near the inner
wall of the channel using a combination of shear gradient-induced and wall-induced lift forces
(F_L proportional to a^4) and Dean drag forces (F_D proportional to a) generated by
counter-rotating Dean vortices in the curved channel geometry. Cells are retained through the
inner outlet (retentate, recycled to bioreactor), while the cell-limited harvest stream exits
through the outer outlet (permeate, collected as IgG1-containing product).

The device eliminates membrane fouling, product retention, and filter replacement contamination
risks associated with conventional tangential flow filtration (TFF) and alternating tangential
flow filtration (ATF) hollow-fiber membrane systems. As a passive operation requiring no active
field (no electric field, no acoustic wave), it is simple, low-maintenance, and biocompatible.
IgG1 recovery exceeds 99% because the smallest channel dimension (~100 um) is far larger than
any antibody molecule, allowing free passage of product into the harvest stream.

Three sequential perfusion cultures of CHO-DG44 cells (IgG1 against CD40 ligand) were run
for 18-25 days each in a 350 mL lab-scale bioreactor at >20 million cells/mL, demonstrating
cell viability >97% throughout and a total IgG1 yield of 263 mg per run.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Material     : Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS, Sylgard 184, Dow Corning); platinum-cured silicone.
               Channel dimensions (~100 um) within limit of injection-moulding (polystyrene).
Fabrication  : Standard soft lithography. Molds designed in Rhinoceros (McNeel North America);
               aluminum molds fabricated by micromachining (Whits Technologies, Singapore).
               PDMS poured into molds; cured at 150 C for 15 min on hotplate. Bonded to flat
               glass slide (260230, Ted Pella) or thin PDMS layer (<500 um) by oxygen plasma
               treatment (Harrick Plasma). Cured at 95 C overnight. Silicone tubings inserted
               (Masterflex, Cole-Parmer). Clamped with binder clips for robust operation.

Single spiral channel:
  Width          : 600 um
  Inner depth    : 80 um
  Outer depth    : 130 um
  Loops          : 8
  Volume         : 28 uL
  Footprint      : 26 x 26 mm
  Outlet width   : 300 um (single channel)

Parallel configuration (used for perfusion cultures):
  Channels per layer : 4 spiral channels combined in single layer
  Inner outlet width : 520 um (enlarged for parallel configuration)
  Perfusion rate     : ~700 mL/day (4-spiral single layer at 1 mL/min input)

Modified high-density channel (Fig. 2d, for 30-40e6 cells/mL):
  Width          : 1000 um
  Inner depth    : 260 um
  Outer depth    : 80 um
  Flow rate      : 4 mL/min input; R_OO/R_IO = 1:0.1
  Retention      : 84% at 43.6e6 cells/mL

Fluidic resistance ratio (R_OO/R_IO):
  Set by attaching small-diameter (510 um ID) tubing (EW-06420-02, Cole-Parmer) of
  adjusted length to outer outlet. Values tested: 4.5, 2.7, 1.5.
  Higher R_OO/R_IO moves streamline boundary toward outer outlet, increasing cell retention.
  Perfusion culture: R_OO/R_IO = 4.0 (inner outlet:outer outlet = 1:4).

Inlets  : 1 (cell culture input from bioreactor via peristaltic pump)
Outlets : 2 -- inner outlet (retentate, cells recycled to bioreactor)
               outer outlet (permeate, harvest: cell-free IgG1-containing stream)

Large-scale stacked device estimate:
  For 1000 L/day perfusion: ~600 channels needed (1000 um x 260 um x 80 um, 4 mL/min/channel).
  Stacked device overall dimensions: 100 mm x 80 mm x 300 mm; single layer 2 mm thick.
  Uniform flow distribution demonstrated in 10-layer simulation (Supplementary Fig. S6).
  Single channel failure reduces stacked device retention by only 1%.


CELL LINE AND BIOREACTOR
-------------------------
Cell line    : CHO-DG44 producing human IgG1 against CD40 ligand
               Gifted from Biogen Idec, MA, USA.
               Diameter: 17.7 +/- 2.5 um (mean +/- s.d., n=19,346).

Culture medium: CD OptiCHO (12681011, CD OptiCHO TM, Thermo Fisher Scientific)
               + 50 uM L-methionine sulfoximine (M5379, Sigma-Aldrich).
               Suspension cultures in autoclaved spinner flask (4500-500, PYREX ProCulture,
               Corning) on magnetic stirrer at 45 RPM.

Bioreactor   : Spinner flask (350 mL working volume) customised for pH/DO control.
               pH setpoint: 7.0; DO setpoint: 40% air saturation.
               Controllers: BioFlo/CelliGen 115 (New Brunswick Scientific, USA);
                            BIOSTAT A (Sartorious, USA).
               Working volume controlled by bioreactor weight via customised software.

Inoculation  : 0.3-0.4e6 cells/mL; batch mode for 4 days before perfusion.

Peristaltic pumps : 07522-20 (Masterflex, USA); 120U/DV (Watson Marlow, USA).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming and Sterilisation
  Reagents     : PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free), Pluronics F-127
  Mix          : 2% Pluronics F-127 in PBS
  Duration     : 5 min
  Sterilisation: 10% bleach (min 10 min) then 70% ethanol (min 10 min) via peristaltic pump
                 prior to priming.
  Setup        : Pre-sterilised device, tubings, and bottles connected in biosafety cabinet.
                 400 mL pre-warmed sterile medium transferred into flask.
                 Setup moved into standard CO2 incubator (37 C) after 4-day batch phase.
  Purpose      : Reduces cell adhesion to PDMS surfaces; equilibrates fluidic adaptors.

Step 2 | Batch Growth Phase (Days 0-4, no perfusion)
  Cell line    : CHO-DG44
  Concentration: 0.3-0.4 e6 cells/mL in 350 mL CD OptiCHO + 50 uM MSX
  Operation    : Batch culture (no perfusion); spinner flask at 45 RPM, 37 C, 5% CO2.
  Duration     : 4 days (5760 min; BioScript token: 5760m -> IR: 345600.0 SECOND)
  Notes        : Cells grown in batch mode to establish viable exponential culture before
                 perfusion initiation. Specific growth rate 0.03 h^-1 (same as batch).

Step 3 | Perfusion Initiation and Continuous Cell Retention (Days 4-18/25)
  Input flow rate  : 1 mL/min per single spiral chip (< 1.5 mL/min optimal)
  Perfusion rate   : 2 VVD (vessel volumes per day; 700 mL/day with 4-spiral layer)
  Operation        : Fresh medium supplied continuously at 2 VVD via second peristaltic pump.
                     CHO cell culture pumped continuously through 4-spiral device at 1 mL/min.
                     Cells focused to inner wall by inertial forces (F_L and F_D); retained
                     via inner outlet and recycled to bioreactor.
                     IgG1-containing harvest collected from outer outlet into harvest bottle.
                     Harvest bottle replaced daily.
  Duration         : 18-25 days (min run: 25920 min; BioScript token: 25920m -> IR: 1555200.0 SECOND)
  Cells recycled   : 98.4 +/- 1.1% of total cell number (mean +/- s.d., n=15)
  Temperature      : 37 C / 5% CO2 (modelled as `heat ... at 37c` in BioScript)
  Working volume   : Maintained at ~350 mL by customised weight-control software.

Step 4 | Cell Retention Efficiency Measurement (Daily QC)
  Formula      : R(%) = (X_I - X_OO) / X_I x 100
                 X_I  = cell concentration in bioreactor (inner outlet)
                 X_OO = cell concentration in harvest stream (outer outlet)
  Instrument   : BioProfile CDV Analyzer (Nova Biomedical, USA)
  Aliquots     : 3 per outlet per daily measurement
  Max deviation: 3.8 +/- 2.9% (mean +/- s.d., n=64) for >1e6 cells/mL
  Results:
    At 4.8e6 cells/mL (1 mL/min)  : 99.6 +/- 0.3% (mean +/- s.d., n=9)
    At 15.4e6 cells/mL             : 99.2% (0.1% range, n=3)
    At 22.2e6 cells/mL             : 92.2% (0.8% range, n=3)
    At 20-23e6 cells/mL (perfusion): 82 +/- 3% (mean +/- s.d., n=10)
    Above 26.5e6 cells/mL          : <83% (band broadening; cell-to-cell interaction)
  Detection module (BioScript): cellRetentionDetection
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB2): retentionSignal > 0 -> R > 80% confirmed; dispose bioreactor sample
    Fail (BB3): retentionSignal <= 0 -> retention failure; drain bioreactor sample
    Join (BB4): dispose retentionSignal; proceed to IgG1 branch

Step 5 | IgG1 Product Recovery Measurement (Daily QC)
  Formula      : AR(%) = X_OO / X_I x 100
                 X_I  = IgG1 concentration in bioreactor (feed)
                 X_OO = IgG1 concentration in harvest stream (permeate)
  Instrument   : HPLC affinity chromatography
                 Column: 2-1001-00 (Applied Biosystems, USA)
                 System: 1100 Series (Agilent, USA)
                 Equilibration: 50 mM phosphate + 150 mM NaCl, pH 7.0
                 Elution: 100 mM phosphate + 0.4% (v/v) phosphoric acid (85 wt%), pH 2.5
                 Reference: IgG1-Kappa from human myeloma plasma (I5154, Sigma-Aldrich)
  Results:
    Days 1-2              : ~80% (adsorption to tubing and fluidic adaptors)
    From Day 3 onward     : ~100%
    Peak bioreactor conc. : ~40 ug/mL after Day 9
    Total harvest volume  : 8.16 L per 18-day run
    Total IgG1 yield      : 263 mg per 18-day run
    Production rate       : 26.8 mg/day from Day 9
  Notes        : No membrane = no product retention or concentration polarisation.
                 IgG1 passes freely through ~100 um channel minimum dimension.
  Detection module (BioScript): igG1Quantification
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB5): igG1Signal > 0 -> IgG1 confirmed in harvest; dispose harvest sample
    Fail (BB6): igG1Signal <= 0 -> product loss; drain harvest sample
    Join (BB7): dispose igG1Signal; proceed to viability branch

Step 6 | Cell Viability Assessment (Daily QC)
  Method       : Trypan blue dye exclusion
  Imaging      : 40 images from 40 different fields of view per measurement.
                 Cell diameter by pixel distance. Concentration by cell counting.
                 Calibrants: 6,000 cells -> 5e6 cells/mL; 12,000 cells -> 10e6 cells/mL.
  Instrument   : BioProfile FLEX and CDV Analyzers (Nova Biomedical, USA)
  Stain time   : 2 min (BioScript: 2m -> IR: 120.0 SECOND)
  Results:
    Biocompatibility run : >145 h continuous processing; 98% (1.6% range) avg retention (n=3)
    Viability decrease   : No noticeable decrease observed across 3 independent runs
    Specific growth rate : 0.03 h^-1 (same as batch culture; Supplementary Table S2)
    During perfusion     : Viability maintained >97% throughout
    After Day 10         : 99 +/- 1% (mean +/- s.d., n=9)
  Notes        : Shear stress-related gene not upregulated in CHO cells by spiral sorting
                 (consistent with prior literature: Warkiani et al., Sci. Rep. 2015).
                 No membrane permeability or surface protein changes expected.
  Detection module (BioScript): cellViabilityDetection
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB8): viabilitySignal > 0 -> viability >97% confirmed; dispose stained sample
    Fail (BB9): viabilitySignal <= 0 -> viability drop; drain stained sample
    Join (BB10): dispose viabilitySignal; NOP (terminal)


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : kwon_microfluidic_cell_retention_perfusion.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG            : kwon_microfluidic_cell_retention_perfusion_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations converted to minutes (m) for compiler compatibility.
                 BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported.
                 4 days  -> 5760m   (batch phase;     IR: 345600.0 SECOND)
                 18 days -> 25920m  (perfusion phase; IR: 1555200.0 SECOND)
                 5 min   -> 5m      (channel priming; IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                 2 min   -> 2m      (trypan staining; IR: 120.0 SECOND)
Modules        : cellRetentionDetection, igG1Quantification, cellViabilityDetection
Drain vs Dispose: BioScript compiler normalises `drain` to `dispose` at IR level;
                 distinction is semantic (fail-path waste routing) and does not affect CFG.
Mix abstraction: `mix` steps involving CHO_DG44_Cells model peristaltic pump recirculation
                 through the spiral channel at 1 mL/min; not DMF electrode mixing.
Heat abstraction: `heat ... at 37c` models CO2 incubator dwell and pump operation; not
                 a TEC or hotplate step.
Biological scope: Protocol encodes the full single-run QC workflow (one 18-day perfusion
                 culture). Three sequential runs (18, 25, 25 days) were performed in the
                 paper; the .bs file represents the representative 18-day run (Run 1).
IR verification : All 10 BBs confirmed in .ir output; all 12 CFG edges confirmed in .dot
                 output; all duration conversions verified; no compiler errors or warnings.
