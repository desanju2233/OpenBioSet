PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Attractive Design: An Elution Solvent Optimization Platform for
               Magnetic-Bead-based Fractionation Using Digital Microfluidics and
               Design of Experiments
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/attractive_design.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac504697r
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2015, Vol. 87, pp. 3902-3910
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) with C18-functionalized magnetic beads on EWOD electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a digital microfluidic (DMF) method for solid phase extraction (SPE)
using C18-functionalized magnetic beads manipulated within discrete droplets on an electrode
array. The approach replaces static porous polymer monolith (PPM) sorbents used in prior
DMF-SPE work with reconfigurable C18 magnetic beads, enabling on-the-fly tuning of sorbent
amount and removal of beads after extraction to maintain device reconfigurability.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. Six-step C18 magnetic bead SPE (dispense, activate, wash, load, wash, elute)
  2. Loading capacity and equilibrium characterisation (fluorescein model analyte)
  3. Automated on-chip DOE-guided elution solvent optimisation for sitamaquine and angiotensin I
  4. Two-fraction sample fractionation and dried blood spot (DBS) extraction and cleanup

Total extraction time per sample is ~12 min. Throughput is 15 samples/h with 3 parallel
extractions on the current device implementation.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Bottom plate : Glass slide precoated with chromium (200 nm) and photoresist (AZ1500, 530 nm).
               15 x 4 array of driving electrodes (2 x 2 mm each); 8 reservoir electrodes
               (16 x 6 mm each). Patterned by photolithography and wet etching at the Toronto
               Nanofabrication Centre, University of Toronto.
               Dielectric: Parylene C, 7 um, vapor deposited (Specialty Coating Systems).
               Hydrophobic coating: Teflon AF, 50 nm, spin-coated (1000 rpm, 30 s),
               post-baked at 160 C for 10 min.

Top plate    : ITO-coated glass (Delta Technologies Ltd., Stillwater, MN).
               Hydrophobic coating: Teflon AF, 50 nm, spin-coated.

Assembly     : Top and bottom plates sandwiched with three layers of double-sided tape (3M,
               London, Ontario) as spacers; total gap 270 um.

Actuation    : 100-120 V_rms, 10 kHz, applied via automated control system with integrated
               moveable magnetic lens. Successive electrode actuation drives droplets.

Magnetic lens: Motorised vertical step-motor mounted beneath the DMF device.
               Engaged (close to device): immobilises C18 beads on device surface.
               Disengaged (moved away): beads freely suspended in droplet for active mixing.
               Originally developed for immunoassay workflows; repurposed here for SPE.


REAGENTS
--------
C18 Magnetic beads : SiMAG-Octadecyl; 1 um diameter; C18 aliphatic functionality.
                     Chemicell GmbH, Berlin, Germany.
                     Suspension: 5 mg/mL (~9.0e9 beads/mL) in aqueous.
                     Standard bed: 10 uL -> ~9e7 beads, ~9e-5 cm3.
                     Bed size tunable: 6 uL (~5.4e7 beads) to 12 uL (~1.08e8 beads).

Solvents           : HPLC-grade acetonitrile and methanol (Fisher Scientific, Ottawa, Ontario).
                     Deionized (DI) water. 0.1% TFA aqueous (loading capacity experiments).

Sitamaquine        : Antimalarial drug; small-molecule model pharmaceutical analyte.
                     1 ug/mL aqueous working concentration. Provided by GSK, Stevenage, UK.
                     SMILES: CCN(CC)CCCCCCNc1cc(cc2c1nccc2C)OC
                     Molar volume: 332.3 +/- 3.0 cm3; Molar refractivity: 108.7 +/- 0.3 cm3.

Angiotensin I      : Peptide model analyte. 50 ug/mL aqueous working concentration.

Internal standards : 2H10-sitamaquine (500 ng/mL in acetonitrile; from GSK, Stevenage, UK).
                     Bradykinin (10 ug/mL in 1:1 ACN:H2O with 0.1% formic acid).

Fluorescein        : Model analyte for loading capacity; 0.1% TFA aqueous.
                     Concentrations: 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100, 10000, 100000 ug/mL.

DBS reagents       : Human whole blood (BioChemed Services, Winchester, VA) spiked with
                     sitamaquine at 5 ug/mL. Spotted (15 uL) onto PerkinElmer 226 Sample
                     Collection Cards (Greenville, SC). 2 mm biopsy punch for extraction.
                     Methanol (30 uL, 5 min) used for off-chip DBS extraction.

Antifouling note   : Pluronic F-127, commonly used in DMF to reduce nonspecific adsorption,
                     was found to be INCOMPATIBLE with this protocol. Pluronics caused
                     micellar encapsulation of hydrophobic analytes and/or coating of C18
                     bead surfaces, significantly reducing extraction performance.
                     Pluronics are NOT used anywhere in this protocol.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Dispense C18 Magnetic Bead Bed
  Reagent    : C18 magnetic bead suspension, 5 mg/mL in water
  Volume     : 10 uL (~9e7 beads, ~9e-5 cm3 sorbent volume)
  Duration   : ~10 s
  Operation  : Bead suspension pipetted onto DMF device; magnetic lens engaged to form bed.
  Notes      : Sorbent volume tunable on-the-fly -- a key advantage over static PPM systems.
               Larger beds (12 uL) prevent saturation at high analyte concentrations (Fig. 2B).

Step 2 | Activate C18 Stationary Phase
  Reagent    : Acetonitrile (10 uL droplet)
  Duration   : ~30 s
  Operation  : (i) Droplet dispensed and driven to bead bed; beads engulfed.
               (ii) Magnetic lens disengaged; beads mixed via DMF actuation.
               (iii) Magnetic lens engaged; beads immobilised; supernatant driven away.
  Notes      : Solvent activation extends collapsed C18 chains into conformation capable of
               reversed-phase interaction with hydrophobic analytes (paper Fig. 1B).

Step 3 | Wash Beads with Water
  Reagent    : Deionized water (10 uL droplet)
  Duration   : ~30 s
  Operation  : Same three-operation sequence as Step 2.
  Notes      : Equilibrates activated C18 beads to aqueous phase before sample loading.

Step 4 | Load Sample and Incubate
  Reagent    : Analyte sample (10 uL droplet)
  Duration   : 5 min (300 s)
  Operation  : Droplet driven to beads; beads engulfed and actively mixed for 5 min;
               supernatant driven away. Mixing ensured by continuous DMF electrode actuation.
  Notes      : Analyte adsorbs onto C18 stationary phase during incubation.
               Loading equilibrium log K ~6 for sitamaquine (within typical SPE range).

Step 5 | Wash (x2)
  Reagent    : Deionized water (2 x 10 uL droplets)
  Duration   : ~60 s total (2 x 30 s)
  Operation  : Two sequential wash cycles using the three-operation sequence.
  Notes      : Removes unadsorbed matrix components while retaining adsorbed analyte on beads.

Step 6 | Elute
  Reagent    : Optimised elution solvent (10 uL, composed on-chip from unit droplets)
  Duration   : ~5 min (300 s)
  Operation  : Droplet driven to beads; beads engulfed and mixed for 5 min; eluate collected.
  Notes      : Solvent composition determined by DOE (see below). Unit droplets (2.5 uL) of
               neat water, methanol, and acetonitrile are dispensed from reservoirs and
               combined on-chip in the desired proportions to form the elution solvent.


LOADING CAPACITY CHARACTERISATION
----------------------------------
Model analyte : Fluorescein in 0.1% TFA (aqueous); concentrations 0.01-100000 ug/mL.
Measurement   : Fluorescence intensity of supernatant (post-extraction droplet) relative to
                stock solution. PheraStar plate reader (BMG Labtech, Durham, NC);
                ex 480 nm, em 520 nm. Triplicate experiments.

Results (Fig. 2A):
  - ~10% of sample remained unbound for 1-1000 ug/mL (no saturation).
  - At 10 mg/mL and above: saturation detected; >80% of sample remained unbound.

Sitamaquine equilibrium:
  - 1.45% unretained post-extraction at 1 ug/mL (by ESI-MS/MS).
  - Mobile phase volume: 10 uL; stationary phase volume: 6.03e-7 mL
    (shell model: uniform C18 thickness = octadecyl alkane chain length, 2.14 nm).
  - Equilibrium log K ~6; consistent with commonly reported SPE solute-sorbent equilibria.


DOE ELUTION SOLVENT OPTIMISATION
----------------------------------
Design software : JMP (SAS, Cary, NC); simplex-centroid design.
Solvents        : Acetonitrile (ACN), Methanol (MeOH), Deionized Water (H2O).
Combinations    : 7 points: neat ACN, neat MeOH, neat H2O, 1:1 ACN:MeOH, 1:1 ACN:H2O,
                  1:1 MeOH:H2O, 1:1:1 ACN:MeOH:H2O.

On-chip mixing  : Unit droplets (2.5 uL each) dispensed from reservoir electrodes containing
                  neat solvents, then combined and mixed in desired proportions.
                  DMF can automatically generate any composition from the ternary space.

Model           : Three-component special cubic (eq 2 in paper):
                  y_hat = sum(bi*xi) + sum(bij*xi*xj) + sum(bijk*xi*xj*xk)
                  where xi = solvent fraction; bi = linear, bij = binary, bijk = ternary coefficients.
                  Response surface plots generated for each analyte (Fig. 4A/B).

Solubility prediction: Abraham general solvation model (web calculator) used to predict
                  sitamaquine solubilities: MeOH > ACN > H2O; consistent with DOE results.

Results:
  Sitamaquine (1 ug/mL):
    - Optimal elution solvent: 100% Methanol (extraction efficiency ~45-55%; Fig. 4A).
    - General trend: increasing efficiency with increasing organic fraction.
    - MeOH outperforms ACN, consistent with Abraham solvation model prediction.

  Angiotensin I (50 ug/mL):
    - Optimal elution solvent: 1:1 ACN:H2O (extraction efficiency ~45-50%; Fig. 4B).
    - Consistent with common practice for reversed-phase peptide separations by HPLC.

Comparison with ZipTip (Fig. 5):
  - DMF C18 bead extraction comparable to C18 ZipTip (EMD Millipore) for both analytes.
  - Sitamaquine eluted in neat methanol; angiotensin I eluted in 1:1 ACN:H2O.


SAMPLE FRACTIONATION
---------------------
Mixture    : Sitamaquine (1 ug/mL) + angiotensin I (50 ug/mL), co-loaded on C18 beads.
Strategy   : Sequential elution with two solvents of differing polarity:

Elution 1 (Fraction 1) -- Neat methanol:
  Sitamaquine recovery : ~90-95%
  Angiotensin I carry-over : ~5-10%
  Result: Sitamaquine-enriched fraction.

Elution 2 (Fraction 2) -- 1:1 ACN:H2O:
  Angiotensin I recovery : ~85-90%
  Sitamaquine carry-over : ~10-15%
  Result: Angiotensin I-enriched fraction.

Coelution : <10% for both fractions (Fig. 6B).
Detection : ESI-MS/MS (344->271 m/z) for sitamaquine; ESI-MS (433 m/z, [M+3H]3+) for
            angiotensin I. IS: 2H10-sitamaquine (500 ng/mL) + bradykinin (531 m/z, [M+2H]2+).


DRIED BLOOD SPOT EXTRACTION AND CLEANUP
-----------------------------------------
Purpose    : Proof-of-principle for real-world complex matrix sample cleanup.
             Reduce ionisation suppression of sitamaquine in DBS extracts.

DBS preparation:
  - 15 uL human whole blood (BioChemed Services) spiked with sitamaquine (5 ug/mL).
  - Spotted onto PerkinElmer 226 Sample Collection Cards; allowed to dry.
  - 2 mm biopsy punch extracted into methanol (30 uL, 5 min).

On-chip procedure:
  1. Unit droplet (2.5 uL) of DBS extract split off for cleanup.
  2. Combined with water (22.5 uL) on-chip; total 25 uL at diluted aqueous matrix.
     (Aqueous dilution required for C18 reversed-phase compatibility.)
  3. C18 bead SPE cleanup following six-step protocol (elution: neat methanol, 10 uL).
  4. Off-chip: eluate diluted to 100 uL in 1:1 ACN:H2O + 0.5% formic acid.

Detection:
  - API 4000 Triple Quadrupole MS/MS (AB Sciex, Concord, Ontario).
  - Positive ion mode; direct infusion 2 uL/min; spray voltage 5 kV.
  - SRM transition: 344->271 m/z (sitamaquine); 12 scans averaged.

Results (Fig. 7):
  - Raw DBS extract: little signal for sitamaquine at 271 m/z (ionisation suppression).
  - Post C18 bead cleanup: sitamaquine signal (271 m/z) strongly enhanced.
  - Cleanup reduces matrix interferents (lipids, haem, proteins) that suppress ESI ionisation.

BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
--------------------------
BioScript file : lafreniere_attractive_design_dmf_spe.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 13 basic blocks (BB1-BB13), 4 if/else gates, 16 CFG edges
DAG            : lafreniere_attractive_design_dmf_spe_main_dag.dot
Duration units : All durations in s or m only (compiler supports s and m tokens only)
                 5 min incubations -> 5m (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                 30 s washes -> 30s (IR: 30.0 SECOND)

Modules        : massSpecDetection, fluorescenceDetection, doeElutionModel

Compiler constraints and workarounds:
  DOE loop     : No loop/array-index semantics in lilott8/BioScript. Seven simplex-centroid
                 points are represented by 3 explicitly encoded runs covering analytically
                 critical conditions (100% MeOH, 1:1 ACN:H2O, 1:1:1 ternary). The
                 doeElutionModel detection module abstracts the special cubic model fitting
                 over all 7 conditions per eq 2 of the paper.
  Elution vol. : Unit droplets are 2.5 uL. Four unit droplets chained via `mix` accumulate
                 10 uL for each elution step; binary-tree mixing avoids long serial chains.
  DBS diluent  : 22.5 uL of water (9 unit droplets) accumulated via 8-way binary tree +
                 final single droplet merge.
  No split     : `split` operator avoided entirely; all dilutions use direct dispense + mix.
  Pluronics    : NOT included in manifests or any dispense operations (incompatible with C18).

Pluronics note : Unlike the Samlali et al. protocol which uses Pluronics F-127 for PDMS
                 antifouling, this protocol explicitly excludes Pluronics due to incompatibility
                 with C18 magnetic beads (micellar encapsulation of hydrophobic analytes).
