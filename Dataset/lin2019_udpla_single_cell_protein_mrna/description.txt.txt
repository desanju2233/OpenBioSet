PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Ultra-sensitive Digital Quantification of Proteins and mRNA in Single Cells
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-11531-z
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11531-z
Journal      : Nature Communications, 2019, Vol. 10, Article 3544
Platform     : Automated microfluidic digital proximity ligation assay (u-dPLA); two-layer
               push-up PDMS device; ddPCR and one-step RT-ddPCR readout (Bio-Rad QX200)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated microfluidic system for ultrasensitive, simultaneous
quantification of protein copy numbers and mRNA in individual mammalian cells, called
microfluidic digital proximity ligation assay (u-dPLA). The device traps and lyses single
cells in 7-nL nanoliter chambers, dramatically reducing the dilution factor relative to
regular well-plate dPLA. All assay steps are executed automatically with nanoliter precision
using Matlab-controlled pneumatic valve actuation, reducing pipetting errors to <0.1% versus
~10% for manual pipetting.

Key innovations over regular dPLA:
  - Up to 55-fold improvement in limit of detection (LOD)
  - 25-fold improvement in detection efficiency
  - 100% of single-cell sample used in ddPCR readout (vs 9% in regular dPLA)
  - Linear dynamic range of 3-4 orders of magnitude
  - Integrated live-cell time-lapse microscopy and chemical stimulation in the same device
  - Simultaneous protein and mRNA quantification via duplex one-step RT-ddPCR

The protocol is divided into three functional assay branches:
  1. Single-cell protein quantification via u-dPLA + ddPCR (FAM channel)
  2. Simultaneous protein/mRNA quantification via duplex u-dPLA + RT-ddPCR (FAM + VIC)
  3. HSV-1 viral infection heterogeneity: live-cell ICP4-YFP imaging + viral protein
     quantification by u-dPLA in the same single cells


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Two-layer push-up PDMS device bonded to glass slide (127.76 mm x 85.48 mm x 1 mm).
Chip capacity: 144 parallel single-cell dPLA assay units.

Fluidic layer (thick, top):
  - AZ40XT photoresist 18 um (rounded channels for valve sealing)
  - SU-8 22 um (medium feature channels)
  - SU-8 70 um two-layer (large reagent channels)

Control layer (thin, bottom):
  - SU-8 22 um; microfluidic push-up valves (Unger et al. Science 2000)
  - Pneumatically actuated via Matlab-controlled solenoid valves

Chamber architecture per assay unit (Chambers I-V):
  Chamber I   : Reagent input and wash channel; reagents introduced via dead-end filling
  Chamber II  : Single-cell trap (50 um constriction); cell imaged here and then lysed
  Chamber III : 7-nL lysis chamber; cell contents lysed and retained here; probe mixing zone
  Chamber IV  : Probe-lysate diffusion mixing extension; valve between III and IV opens
                during 1-h incubation to allow mixing by diffusion
  Chamber V   : Ligation reaction chamber; PLA products generated here
  Output port : 1930 um inner diameter biopsy punch; 9 uL PLA products retrieved per unit

Valve system:
  - Microfluidic valves separate each chamber to prevent contamination between steps
  - Dead-end filling delivers precise reagent volumes to empty chambers
  - Wash channels (MilliQ water) flush channels between reagent steps
  - Chip automation via Matlab program controlling valve open/close sequences


CELL LINES
----------
- HEK293T (human embryonic kidney): model cell for CD147/TNFR1 protein quantification
  and mRNA-protein correlation studies. TNFR1 not detected; consistent with no TNF-a
  response in NF-kB stimulation experiment.
  Cultured in DMEM (Cat. No. 11965-092, Life Technologies) + 10% FBS (F9665, Sigma-Aldrich).

- H1299 (human lung epithelial, NSCLC): primary cell line for TNFR1 protein/mRNA
  co-quantification and Spearman mRNA-protein correlation analysis.
  Cultured in RPMI-1640 (Cat. No. 11835-030, Life Technologies) + 10% FBS.

- A549 (human lung epithelial, adenocarcinoma): used for HSV-1 infection heterogeneity
  assay combined with live-cell ICP4-YFP imaging and viral protein u-dPLA.
  Cultured in DMEM (Cat. No. 86012804, Sigma-Aldrich) + 10% FBS.

All cells dissociated with TrypLE (Cat. No. 12605028, Life Technologies) 5 min at 37C,
resuspended in sterile PBS, filtered through 40 um cell strainer, diluted to 2x10^5
cells/mL for on-chip loading at 5 psi input pressure.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | BSA Surface Blocking
  Reagents    : 10% BSA in PBS (Cat. No. 37525, Thermo Fisher)
  Duration    : 1 h (60 min)
  Temperature : 25C (room temperature)
  Purpose     : Coat PDMS chip surfaces to minimise non-specific adsorption of PLA probes
                and cell-lysate proteins to channel walls.
  Post-block  : Wash with MilliQ water; ventilate with compressed air before cell loading.

Step 2 | On-Chip Calibration Curve
  Reagents    : Purified protein standard (TNFR1, CD147, or CSTB), PBS, PLA Probes,
                Ligation Mix, MilliQ water
  Method      : Serial dilution of pure protein standards loaded into assay chambers via
                dead-end filling from lowest to highest concentration. 8 min wash between
                each concentration via Channel I.
  PLA probes  : 500 pM stock diluted to 2 nM in probe binding buffer (0.1% BSA +
                0.05% Tween-20 + 100 nM goat IgG + 0.1 g/L sheared salmon sperm DNA in PBS).
                Channel I flushed 2 min; Channel II flushed 15 min.
  Probe incub : 37C, 60 min (diffusion mixing)
  Ligation    : On ice 30 min; 37C 10 min; 65C 5 min (heat inactivation of T4 ligase)
  Retrieval   : 9 uL per unit, collected for ddPCR
  Purpose     : Calibration converts ddPCR amplicon counts per uL to absolute protein
                copy numbers. Run in parallel with single-cell experiments on the same chip.
  LOD calc    : 3 SD above background reading or y-intercept of fitted linear curve
                (whichever is higher).

Step 3 | Single-Cell Loading and Hydrodynamic Trapping
  Reagents    : SingleCell suspension in PBS, PBS
  Operation   : Pressure-driven flow-through trapping in Chamber II (50 um constriction)
  Loading     : 5 psi input pressure; 2x10^5 cells/mL
  Occupancy   : ~90% average single-cell occupancy per chip run
  Verification: Cell loading confirmed visually under microscope before proceeding
  Note        : Chip immediately moved to ice after loading confirmed.
                BioScript mix abstraction represents pressure-driven hydrodynamic trapping;
                no DMF electrode actuation is involved.

Step 4 | Single-Cell Lysis
  Reagents    : Lysis buffer (0.3x TM buffer + protease inhibitor in cell resuspension buffer)
  Operation   : Lysis buffer introduced from Chamber I to push and lyse cell from
                Chamber II into Chamber III (7 nL)
  Duration    : 10 min on ice (0C)
  Verification: Lysis confirmed visually under microscope
  Key feature : 100% of 7-nL single-cell lysate retained in Chamber III; zero sample loss.
                Regular dPLA loses 91% of sample (only 9% reaches ddPCR readout).
                This 11-fold increase in effective sample use is the primary driver of the
                55-fold LOD improvement.

Step 5 | PLA Probe Incubation with Cell Lysate
  Reagents    : PLA Probes (2 nM working concentration)
  Chambers    : III and IV (valve between open during incubation)
  Duration    : 60 min at 37C; diffusion-based mixing
  Mechanism   : Each PLA probe pair consists of two oligonucleotide-tagged antibodies
                recognising two distinct epitopes on the target protein. When both probes
                bind the same protein molecule, their oligonucleotides are brought within
                ~3-5 nm, enabling connector-mediated ligation.
  Probes used :
    TNFR1 : Biotinylated BAF225 (R&D Systems), streptavidin-biotin conjugation
    CD147 : Biotinylated BAF972 (R&D Systems), streptavidin-biotin conjugation
    CSTB  : Commercially available (Cat. No. 4405465, Applied Biosystems)
    HSV-1 : Anti-HSV-1 polyclonal ab9533 (Abcam), in-house DBCO-NHS/azide click chemistry;
             detects ~80 HSV-1 viral protein species encoded by HSV-1 genome

Step 6 | Ligation Reaction
  Reagents    : Ligation Mix (89.1 uL MilliQ water + 10 uL 10x ligase buffer +
                0.4 uL 1/500-diluted T4 ligase (EL0011, Thermo Scientific) +
                0.5 uL 1 uM connector oligo TTTCGACACGACACGATTTAGGTC per 100 uL)
  Operation   : Ligation mix pushes probe-lysate mix from Chambers III/IV into Chamber V
  On ice      : 30 min for diffusion mixing
  Ligation    : 37C, 10 min; T4 DNA ligase seals nick in connector-bridged probe pair
  Inactivation: 65C, 5 min; heat inactivation of T4 ligase
  Product     : Circular dsDNA PLA product; each molecule = 1 detected protein

Step 7 | PLA Product Retrieval and ddPCR (Protein Quantification Gate)
  Reagents    : MilliQ water (flush), ddPCR Supermix for Probes (Bio-Rad Cat. No. 1863024),
                Universal PCR Assay (Cat. No. 4405501, Applied Biosystems),
                Droplet Generation Oil (Cat. No. 1863005, Bio-Rad)
  Retrieval   : 9 uL PLA products flushed from Chamber V via water through 1930 um port
  ddPCR setup : 9 uL products + 10 uL 2x Supermix + 1 uL 20x PCR Assay = 20 uL total;
                loaded into DG8 cartridge with 65 uL Droplet Generation Oil (QX200, Bio-Rad)
  PCR program : 95C 10 min; 40x (94C 30s / 60C 1 min); 98C 10 min; ramp 1.5C/s
  Readout     : FAM channel; positive droplet count = absolute protein copy number
  ddPCR primers:
    Forward   : GCATCGTCTCGTGGGCTC
    Reverse   : TTGTACGCATGTGGACTTTAGACACGACACGA
  Detection   : ddPCRProteinDetection module; 30 s
  QC gate     :
    Pass (BB2): proteinSignal > 0 -> above LOD; dispose ddPCR droplets
    Fail (BB3): proteinSignal <= 0 -> below LOD; drain
  Results (selected):
    CD147 mean: HEK293T 197,785 (N=266); H1299 327,609 (N=96); A549 179,513 (N=98)
    TNFR1 mean: H1299 14,807 (N=88, range 6,310-33,541); A549 13,017 (N=84)
    TNFR1 in HEK293T: all below LOD (2,277 molecules); consistent with no NF-kB response
    Cells above TNFR1 LOD: 86% (149/174); first single-cell TNFR1 counts reported

Step 8 | Duplex Protein/mRNA Quantification (Same Single Cell)
  Reagents    : RNaseOUT (Cat. No. 10777019, Invitrogen; 20x dilution in all reagent mixes),
                One-step RT-ddPCR Supermix (5 uL), Reverse Transcriptase (2 uL),
                DTT 300 mM (Cat. No. 1864021, Bio-Rad; 1 uL),
                Universal PCR Assay 20x (1 uL),
                TNFR1 TaqMan Probe 20x (Cat. No. Hs01042313_m1, Applied Biosystems; 1 uL),
                Nuclease-free water (1 uL); Total: 20 uL
  Method      : PLA product retrieval (9 uL) includes protected mRNA; RNaseOUT added to all
                reagent mixes throughout chip steps to protect mRNA integrity
  Duplex      : FAM channel -> protein (PLA ligation products, same as Step 7)
                VIC channel -> mRNA (RT-ddPCR amplicons from TNFR1 mRNA)
  RT program  : 42C 1 h; 95C 10 min; 40x (95C 30s / 60C 1 min); 98C 10 min; ramp 2C/s
  Crosstalk   : VIC/FAM crosstalk negligible (Supplementary Fig. 5a/b)
  Detection   : rtDdPCRmRNADetection module (VIC channel); 30 s
  QC gate     :
    Pass (BB5): mrnaSignal > 0 -> mRNA detected; dispose duplex droplets
    Fail (BB6): mrnaSignal <= 0 -> mRNA below detection; drain
  Results     :
    Spearman rho (TNFR1 protein vs mRNA, H1299): 0.45 (N=38 single cells)
    Previous estimates: ~0.1 (Taniguchi 2010, Albayrak 2016, Darmanis 2016)
    Finding: Including lowly-expressing cells (previously below dPLA LOD) increases
    mRNA-protein correlation significantly. Spearman coefficient rises as low-protein
    cells are included in the calculation (Fig. 3e). High-sensitivity quantification is
    essential for accurate mRNA-protein correlation estimates.

Step 9 | HSV-1 Viral Infection Heterogeneity Assay
  Reagents    : HSV1_InfectedCell (A549 + HSV-1 ICP4-YFP, MOI 2, overnight), ControlCell
                (A549, no virus), PBS, LysisBuffer, PLAProbes (HSV-1), LigationMix,
                MilliQWater, ddPCR_Supermix, UniversalPCRAssay, DropletGenerationOil
  Cell prep   : HSV-1 stock prepared by Vero cell infection (MOI 0.01), harvested Day 3
                by three freeze-thaw cycles; stock concentration by plaque assay.
                A549 infected with 25 uL virus in 2 mL fresh medium overnight (MOI 2).
  Live imaging:
    Reporter  : ICP4-YFP; ICP4 expressed immediately upon viral genome nuclear entry,
                prior to genome replication (DeLuca et al. 1985; 1987).
                Nuclear YFP signal = infection proceeded to nuclear entry (YFP-positive).
    Modality  : Time-lapse fluorescence imaging on chip before lysis (liveCellImagingDetection)
    Calculation: Matlab imfindcircles locates cells; ImageJ Rolling Ball Background
                 Subtraction; total fluorescence = sum of all YFP pixel values within cell
  Lysis       : All cells lysed simultaneously with 0.3x TM buffer, on ice, 10 min
  HSV-1 PLA   : Polyclonal anti-HSV-1 ab9533 (Abcam) conjugated via DBCO-NHS click chemistry;
                detects ~80 viral protein species; both immediate-early, early, and late proteins
  Probe incub : 37C, 60 min
  Ligation    : On ice 30 min; 37C 10 min; 65C 5 min
  ddPCR       : Same protocol as Step 7; FAM channel; viral protein copy number per cell
  Detection   : viralProteinDetection module; 30 s
  QC gate     :
    Pass (BB8): infectionSignal > 0 -> ICP4-YFP+ (nuclear entry confirmed); dispose
    Fail (BB9): infectionSignal <= 0 -> uninfected or control (no YFP); drain
  Results     :
    N infected (YFP+) cells: 27; N control cells: 19
    Group difference p-value: 5.356e-11 (two-sided Welch's t test)
    Spearman rho (ICP4-YFP fluorescence vs total viral protein count): 0.48
    Interpretation: Not all YFP-positive cells progress past immediate-early protein
    synthesis stage; partial correlation (rho=0.48) indicates a sub-population undergoes
    abortive infection. Single-cell viral protein counts span ~5-fold range across
    infected cells, consistent with prior reports of substantial single-cell heterogeneity
    in viral progeny release (Heldt 2015, Russell 2018).
