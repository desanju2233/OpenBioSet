PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Digital Microfluidic Platform for Human Plasma Protein Depletion
Source Link  : https://collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/OKQ/TC-OKQ-12209.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac5022198
Journal      : M.Sc. Thesis, Queen's University, Kingston, Ontario, Canada, May 2014
               Supervisors: Dr. Richard D. Oleschuk & Dr. Aaron A. Wheeler
Platform     : Two-plate digital microfluidics (DMF) on glass chip with automated
               motor-controlled magnetic separation and custom Microdrop software


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated DMF-based method for depleting high-abundance
human plasma proteins -- human serum albumin (HSA) and immunoglobulin G (IgG) --
which together constitute approximately 80% of total plasma protein content and severely
mask low-abundance analytes in proteomic studies. The approach uses paramagnetic beads
functionalized with Protein A/G (for IgG capture) and anti-HSA (for HSA capture),
actuated and mixed on a two-plate glass DMF device under an automated controller system.

The protocol depletes IgG and HSA simultaneously with up to 98% efficiency in 10 minutes
across four samples processed in parallel. Depleted samples show an approximately 3-fold
increase in MALDI-MS signal-to-noise ratio and are compatible with downstream LC-MS/MS
proteomic analysis. Successful tryptic in-solution digestion of cytochrome C from depleted
samples yielded 89% sequence coverage (11 peptides, MALDI score 225).

The protocol consists of two encoded phases:
  1. Core on-chip protein depletion: eight-step bead-based immunocapture workflow
  2. Fluorescence kinetics characterisation: FITC-IgG and FITC-HSA depletion efficiency
     probed at 30-second intervals over 10 minutes total using a PHERAstar plate reader


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Format     : Two-plate glass DMF chip
Fabrication: University of Toronto Nanofabrication Centre (TNFC) clean room
Bottom plate: Patterned electrode array on glass
Top plate   : ITO-coated glass (ground plate)
Coatings    : Parylene-C dielectric (vapor deposition) + Teflon-AF hydrophobic layer (spin-coat)

Actuation   : 100-120 V_rms, 10 kHz sine wave
Droplet vol : 4 uL unit droplets; four droplets merged per depletion run
Incubation  : Figure-8 pattern across 4 electrodes for continuous droplet/bead mixing

Magnetic separation:
  Motor-controlled magnetic lens assembly (~600 uN force; minimum threshold 470 uN)
  Magnet position automated via custom Microdrop software + Pogo-pin interface
  Microcapture USB camera for real-time video monitoring

Controller system: National Instruments PXI chassis (MIP), custom Microdrop software
                   NI PXI-4130 (DC power), NI PXI-5422 (AC function generator)


REAGENTS
--------
Plasma proteins:
  HSA          : 0.5 mg/mL in PBS (66,437 Da; lyophilized, Life Technologies)
  IgG          : 2.0 mg/mL in PBS (150,000 Da; diluted from 4.7 mg/mL stock)
  FITC-IgG     : 0.5 mg/mL (GenScript USA Inc., Piscataway, NJ) -- kinetics only
  FITC-HSA     : 1.0 mg/mL (Abcam, Cambridge, MA) -- kinetics only
  Hemopexin    : 0.1 mg/mL in PBS (57,000 Da; low-abundance marker)

Paramagnetic beads (Millipore, Billerica, MA; 10 um diameter):
  Protein A/G Beads : PureProteome Protein A/G Mix Magnetic Beads (LSKMAGAG02)
                      Diluted 1:4 from stock in PBS; captures IgG
  Anti-HSA Beads    : PureProteome Albumin Magnetic Beads (LSKMAGL02)
                      Diluted 1:2 from stock in PBS; captures HSA

Buffer      : Phosphate-buffered saline (PBS)
Surfactant  : Pluronic F-68 at 0.05% in PBS
              Selection rationale: Pluronic F-127 and L64 inhibited Protein A/G bead
              binding (Chapter 3 screening). Pluronics L92, F68, and F88 did not interfere
              with binding; F-68 produced optimal droplet movement on DMF chip.

Waste removal: KimWipes (Kimberly-Clark) wicking from device surface


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Reagent Preparation and Device Loading
  Reagents  : ProteinAG_Beads, AntiHSA_Beads, PlasmaProteinSample, PBS_Buffer, PluronicF68
  Operation : Off-chip bead dilution; pipette-load into device reservoirs
  Notes     : Bead types combined into single mixed-bead suspension on-chip (5s mix).
              Protein sample (IgG + HSA + hemopexin + 0.05% F-68 in PBS) prepared fresh.
              4 uL droplets. Four samples processed simultaneously in parallel.

Step 2 | Bead Actuation onto Device Array
  Reagents  : mixedBeads, PBS_Buffer
  Operation : DMF electrode actuation (100-120 V_rms, 10 kHz)
  Notes     : Mixed bead droplet moved from reservoir into active array. Beads remain
              suspended during electrode-to-electrode transfer.

Step 3 | First Magnet Engagement -- Bead Immobilization
  Operation : heat at 25c for 30s (BioScript abstraction for magnet dwell)
  Duration  : 30 s
  Notes     : Motor-controlled magnet (~600 uN) lowered below chip. Paramagnetic beads
              pelleted onto device surface. heat at 25c is a BioScript-level abstraction
              for the immobilization dwell; no actual thermal step occurs.

Step 4 | Supernatant Removal and PBS Wash
  Reagents  : PBS_Buffer
  Operation : mix beadDroplet + washBuffer for 5s; heat at 25c for 30s
  Notes     : Bead storage buffer actuated to waste and wicked off with KimWipes. PBS
              wash droplet added; magnet re-engaged (30s) to re-pellet beads before
              sample merging.

Step 5 | Protein Sample Merge with Bead Pellet
  Reagents  : beadWash1, samplePlusMix
  Operation : mix for 10s
  Notes     : Sample droplet actuated over immobilized beads; magnet disengaged to
              resuspend beads into protein sample. Merged droplet contains IgG, HSA,
              hemopexin, 0.05% F-68, Protein A/G beads, and anti-HSA beads.

Step 6 | Active Incubation -- Figure-8 Mixing
  Operation : heat at 37c for 10m
  Temperature: 37 C
  Duration  : 10 min (600 s)
  Pattern   : Figure-8 across 4 electrodes; 100-120 V_rms, 10 kHz
  Results   : IgG depletion efficiency up to 98%; HSA depletion efficiency up to 98%
              (FITC-labeled fluorescence assay, N=3-4 per condition)
  Notes     : Continuous movement maximizes bead-protein contact. All four samples
              run concurrently on the same device.

Step 7 | Bead Re-Immobilization and Supernatant Separation
  Operation : heat at 25c for 30s
  Duration  : 30 s
  Notes     : Magnet re-engaged; beads (now carrying captured IgG and HSA) pelleted.
              Depleted supernatant (containing retained hemopexin) actuated to
              collection reservoir by DMF electrode switching.

Step 8 | Depleted Sample Collection and Depletion QC
  Reagents  : PBS_Buffer
  Operation : mix for 5s; detect depletionQC for 30s
  Detection : depletionQC module (30 s)
  Downstream: MALDI-MS or LC-MS/MS analysis (top plate removed; droplet pipetted off-chip)
  Branch    :
    Pass (depletionSignal > 0) : dispose depletedSample  [BB2]
    Fail (depletionSignal <= 0): drain depletedSample     [BB3]

Step 9 | Fluorescence Kinetics Characterisation (FITC-IgG; BB4 join)
  Reagents  : FITC_IgG (0.5 mg/mL), ProteinAG_Beads, PBS_Buffer
  Operation : mix + 30s heat (immobilization) x N + detect fluorescenceDetection for 30s
  Instrument: PHERAstar microplate reader (BMG Labtech, Ortenberg, Germany)
              Mode: well scanning (COSTAR 96-well geometry); 3 flashes/point; gain 100
              Excitation 485 nm / Emission 520 nm
  Protocol  : Steps 1-5 applied to FITC-IgG; incubate 30 s; extract supernatant; measure
              fluorescence; re-suspend beads; repeat to 10 min total (20 x 30s intervals)
              Replicates: N = 3 per time point
  HSA variant: FITC-HSA (0.5 mg/mL, gain 125) with anti-HSA beads; identical procedure
  BioScript note: Loop absent in compiler; 20 x 30s intervals abstracted as one
              representative 30s heat + detect step. Full iterative kinetics run off-chip.
  Branch    :
    Pass (fluorescenceSignal > 0) : dispose kineticsSupern  [BB5]
    Fail (fluorescenceSignal <= 0): drain kineticsSupern     [BB6]
  Terminal  : dispose fluorescenceSignal; NOP              [BB7]


MALDI-MS ANALYSIS
-----------------
Instrument    : PerSeptive Biosystems Voyager DE Pro MALDI-TOF (AB Sciex, Framingham, MA)
m/z range     : 20,000 - 200,000
Shots         : 250 per spectrum; laser power adjusted for S/N
Data processing: Baseline correction, resolution 100, smoothing (Voyager Data Explorer)
Calibrant     : BSA (66.5 kDa, Mann Research Laboratories, Port Saint Lucie, FL)

Matrix:
  Protein depletion : Sinapinic acid (SA); 10 mg SA in 1 mL 50% ACN / 0.1% TFA
  Tryptic digests   : alpha-CHCA; 10 mg in 1 mL 50:50 ACN:H2O / 0.1% TFA
  Calibrant (digest): Synthetic 4-peptide mix (Angiotensin I, Glu-fibrinogen, Renin, ACTH)

Sample preparation:
  Acidification : 2% TFA added to protein solution (3:1 protein:TFA)
  Spotting      : 0.5 uL matrix pre-spotted and dried; 1:1 matrix:acidified protein overlay
  Target        : Stainless steel 100-spot MALDI target plate; air-dried in dark

ZipTip cleanup (Millipore, Etobicoke, ON):
  C4  : Intact protein samples
  C18 : Tryptic digests
  Protocol: Wet in 50% ACN/0.1% TFA (x5); equilibrate in 0.1% TFA (x5);
            bind 20 cycles; wash 0.1% TFA (x3); elute in SA or alpha-CHCA matrix

Protein mixture tested: 2 mg/mL IgG + 0.5 mg/mL HSA + 0.1 mg/mL hemopexin
Depletion rounds : 0 (pre-depletion control), 1, 2 (two sequential bead aliquots)
Replicates       : 4 per condition
S/N improvement  : Approximately 3-fold after depletion


LC-MS/MS PROTEOMIC ANALYSIS
----------------------------
Model protein : Cytochrome C (tryptic in-solution digest)
Trypsin       : Sequencing grade modified (Promega, Madison, WI)
Digest        : 12 h at 37 C; quenched with 5% formic acid
Results       : 89% sequence coverage, 11 peptides matched, MALDI score 225
