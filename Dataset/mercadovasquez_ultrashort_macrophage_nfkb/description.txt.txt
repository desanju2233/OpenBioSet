PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Accurate Processing of Ultra-Short Immune Signals by Single Macrophages
Source Link  : https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.10.26.684377v1.full.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.26.684377
Journal      : bioRxiv preprint, posted October 26, 2025
Platform     : Multilayer PDMS microfluidic live-cell stimulation device with pneumatic valve control


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol investigates the temporal limits of immune signal detection by individual primary
macrophages. Primary bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) from mVenus-RelA (RelA^V/V)
C57BL/6J transgenic mice -- which express an endogenously tagged fluorescent NF-kB p65/RelA
reporter -- are isolated, differentiated, and seeded into multilayer PDMS microfluidic culture
chambers. The cells are then exposed to ultra-short (1-second) chemical pulses of five NF-kB
ligands covering cytokine and pathogen-derived signaling modalities: TNF alpha, Lipid A,
Pam2CSK4, R848 (Resiquimod), and CpG ODN 1668. NF-kB nuclear translocation dynamics of
the mVenus-RelA reporter are tracked by time-lapse epifluorescence microscopy over 6 hours
at single-cell resolution.

Key findings encoded in this protocol:
  - Single macrophages detect and respond to 1-second ligand pulses with high specificity
  - Short-pulse channel capacity (CC_max = 0.82) is comparable to persistent stimulation
    (CC_max = 0.92), demonstrating robust signal discrimination at the 1-second timescale
  - Three macrophage subpopulations emerge: early responders, late responders, non-responders
  - Pam2CSK4 produces the strongest response under 1-second pulse; Lipid A is most potent
    only under persistent stimulation -- stimulus duration alters the ranking of ligand potency
  - Macrophages employ two sensing modes: sensitivity to cytokine duration (TNF) vs. pathogen
    amplitude/dose (Pam2CSK4, R848)
  - Mathematical modeling identifies Hill coefficient (receptor cooperativity) as the primary
    determinant of the three NF-kB activation modes


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Microfluidic chip: Multilayer PDMS fabricated from silicon wafer master molds (10:1 PDMS
monomer:catalyst, Momentive). Control layer and fluid layer spin-coated separately, bonded
by oxygen plasma, overnight-baked. Control and flow inlets punched; glass-bonded.

Flow control:
  - Pneumatic valves electronically controlled via custom MATLAB GUI
  - PEEK tubing (VICI) at 4 psi input pressure
  - Pulse timing CV = 0.06 (6% variability across all analyzed cells)
  - Pulse as short as 1 second reliably delivered to live cells

Environmental control:
  - In-situ microbiome box maintains 37 C, humidity, and CO2 throughout imaging
  - Cells exhibit no photobleaching or phototoxicity under 6-hour imaging protocol


MOUSE MODEL
-----------
Strain     : mVenus-RelA (RelA^V/V) C57BL/6J
Reporter   : mVenus fluorescently tagged p65/RelA NF-kB subunit (endogenous knock-in)
Age/Sex    : 6-12 weeks; male and female

REAGENTS
--------
m-CSF                 : Murine m-CSF (Peprotech); reconstituted in ultrapure water;
                        aliquoted and stored at -80 C; 20 ng/mL in complete RPMI
Cell culture media    : Complete phenol red-free RPMI (Gibco)
Fibronectin           : 0.2 mg/mL fibronectin solution (Sigma-Aldrich) in PBS
Nuclear stain         : Hoechst 33342 (Invitrogen), 1 uM in phenol red-free RPMI
Ligands (all stored at -80 C; thawed on ice and diluted in complete RPMI before use):
  TNF alpha           : Murine TNFalpha (R&D Systems),     10 ng/mL
  Lipid A             : Ultrapure Lipid A from E. coli O111:B4 (Invivogen), 10 ng/mL
  Pam2CSK4           : PAM2CSK4 (Invivogen),               3 ng/mL
  R848 Resiquimod     : R848 Resiquimod (Invivogen),       300 ng/mL
  CpG ODN 1668        : ODN 1668 (Invivogen),              300 nM


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | BMDM Isolation and Differentiation
  Tissue source  : Tibias and femurs of 6-12-week-old male and female RelA^V/V C57BL/6J mice
  Isolation      : Bone marrow cells flushed and resuspended in complete RPMI supplemented
                   with 20 ng/mL murine m-CSF (Peprotech)
  Differentiation: 7-9 days at 37 C / 5% CO2 (encoded as 9 days = 12960 min = 777600 s)
  Harvest        : BMDMs harvested on days 7-9; loaded into microfluidic culture chips
  Reference      : Wang et al., Cell Syst 2025 (PMID protocol source)

Step 2 | Microfluidic Device Fibronectin Priming
  Reagents       : 0.2 mg/mL fibronectin (Sigma-Aldrich) in PBS
  Duration       : 5 min (encoded as 300 s)
  Purpose        : Pre-incubation of PDMS culture chambers to promote BMDM adhesion
  Notes          : mix abstracts the channel-flow fibronectin coating step

Step 3 | BMDM Seeding and Equilibration
  Density        : 10^6 cells/mL in complete phenol red-free RPMI
  Duration       : 4-6 h undisturbed (encoded as 6 h = 360 min = 21600 s at 37 C)
  Purpose        : Cells attach and equilibrate in microfluidic chambers before stimulation

Step 4 | Nuclear Tracking Stain
  Reagent        : 1 uM Hoechst 33342 (Invitrogen) in phenol red-free RPMI
  Duration       : ~5 min (per producer instructions)
  Purpose        : Enables nuclear segmentation (395 nm channel) and single-cell tracking
                   throughout the 6-hour time-lapse imaging run

Step 5a | TNF Alpha 1-Second Pulse Stimulation
  Ligand         : Murine TNFalpha (R&D Systems), 10 ng/mL
  Pulse duration : 1 second via pneumatic valve at 4 psi (PEEK tubing, VICI)
  Receptor       : TNFR1 (CD120a); cytokine intercellular signaling pathway
  Key result     : Weakest response under 1-second pulse; non-monotonic dose-duration
                   relationship; 100-second pulse doubles NF-kB amplitude vs 1-second pulse;
                   macrophages respond to cytokine duration, not amplitude

Step 5b | Lipid A 1-Second Pulse Stimulation
  Ligand         : Ultrapure Lipid A from E. coli O111:B4 (Invivogen), 10 ng/mL
  Pulse duration : 1 second via pneumatic valve at 4 psi
  Receptor       : TLR4; extracellular bacterial PAMP sensing
  Key result     : Largest late responder fraction (~30%); longest time to peak (>50 min);
                   most potent ligand under persistent stimulation but not under 1-second pulse;
                   reversal of potency ranking demonstrates duration-dependent ligand identity

Step 5c | Pam2CSK4 1-Second Pulse Stimulation
  Ligand         : PAM2CSK4 (Invivogen), 3 ng/mL
  Pulse duration : 1 second via pneumatic valve at 4 psi
  Receptor       : TLR2/TLR6 heterodimer; bacterial lipopeptide sensing via MyD88/TIRAP
  Key result     : Strongest response under 1-second pulse (highest early AUC, amplitude,
                   speed, duration); lowest late responder fraction (~3%); CC_max = 0.82;
                   Pam-vs-control MI with features = 0.87; used as QC reference condition

Step 5d | R848 (Resiquimod) 1-Second Pulse Stimulation
  Ligand         : R848 Resiquimod (Invivogen), 300 ng/mL
  Pulse duration : 1 second via pneumatic valve at 4 psi
  Receptor       : TLR7 (endosomal); viral RNA mimic sensing via MyD88
  Key result     : All-or-nothing activation dynamic (similar to fibroblast/cancer cells);
                   largest channel capacity under dose-duration tradeoff; lower dose (longer
                   pulse) elicits minimal to null response; strong dose (short pulse) gives
                   robust activation

Step 5e | CpG ODN 1668 1-Second Pulse Stimulation
  Ligand         : ODN 1668 CpG (Invivogen), 300 nM
  Pulse duration : 1 second via pneumatic valve at 4 psi
  Receptor       : TLR9 (endosomal); foreign/pathogenic DNA sensing
  Key result     : CpG and TNF induce highest oscillatory frequencies under short pulses;
                   pairwise mutual information CpG vs TNF = 0 at 1-second pulse (cells
                   cannot distinguish these two ligands at 1-second duration)

Step 6 | Time-Lapse NF-kB Live-Cell Imaging and QC Detection
  Microscope     : Nikon Ti2 epifluorescence; 20x magnification
  Camera         : Hamamatsu ORCA-Flash4.0 V2
  Channels       : mVenus-RelA (508 nm, NF-kB nuclear translocation); Hoechst (395 nm, nuclei)
  Frame interval : 6 minutes
  Total duration : 6 hours = 360 min = 21600 s (encoded as heat 37 C + detect 360 min)
  Cells per cond : >200 individual cells analyzed per stimulation condition
  Activation threshold: 2 standard deviations above mean nuclear/cytoplasmic ratio of
                   unstimulated cells; first passage time defines activation time
  Analysis software: Custom MATLAB 2024a; nuclear segmentation via Hoechst images;
                   nuclear/cytoplasmic mVenus-RelA ratio per cell per frame; dead cells,
                   dividing cells, and out-of-focus cells removed (>99% tracking accuracy)
  QC module      : nfkbTranslocationDetection; positive signal (nfkbSignal > 0) confirms
                   NF-kB nuclear translocation above threshold in at least one cell

  Branch pass    : nfkbSignal > 0 -> dispose(imagingPool_all)   [BB2]
  Branch fail    : nfkbSignal <= 0 -> drain(imagingPool_all)    [BB3]
  Terminal       : dispose(nfkbSignal); experiment ends          [BB4]


SINGLE-CELL SUBPOPULATION ANALYSIS
-----------------------------------
Method         : k-means clustering (MATLAB; 10 random centroid replicates; Euclidean distance)
                 applied to activation time and amplitude from all NF-kB traces
Subpopulations :
  Early Responders (ER)  -- fast response < 25 min, high amplitude
  Late Responders (LR)   -- response onset > 20 min, low amplitude
  Non-Responders (NR)    -- never exceed activation threshold
Late responder fractions:
  Pam2CSK4    ~3%  (lowest; predominantly early responders)
  Lipid A     ~30% (highest; cells activate up to 1 hour post-stimulation)
  TNF         intermediate; non-responder fraction dominates at low doses
  R848        switch from early responders to non-responders as dose decreases
  CpG         intermediate; broader activation time range than TNF or R848
Paracrine check: No correlation between late responder activation time and distance to
                 nearest early responder (r^2 ~ 0); late response is cell-intrinsic


INFORMATION THEORY ANALYSIS
----------------------------
Mutual Information (MI): Quantifies NF-kB trace distinguishability between two ligands
  (1 bit = perfect distinguishability; 0 = indistinguishable)
Channel Capacity (CC_max, 1-second pulse): 0.82 bits at ~21 min
  Comparison to persistent stimulation CC_max: 0.92 bits
  Conclusion: short-pulse specificity nearly equivalent to persistent stimulation
Pairwise MI highlights:
  Highest: Pam2CSK4 vs TNF
  Lowest:  CpG vs TNF (MI = 0; indistinguishable at 1-second)
  Pam2CSK4 vs control (with dynamic features): MI = 0.87 (near-perfect distinguishability)
Most informative dynamic features: Early AUC, amplitude at t_max, activation speed, duration


MATHEMATICAL MODEL SUMMARY
---------------------------
Type         : Minimal ODE model; ligand-receptor interaction + NF-kB signal transduction
               downstream IKK module; Hill function summarizes upstream IKK interactions
Receptor pathways fit: TNFR (TNFalpha), TLR2 (Pam2CSK4), TLR7 (R848)
Three activation modes predicted:
  m1: Monotonic increase  -- NF-kB amplitude increases with pulse duration (high activation rate)
  m2: Non-monotonic       -- intermediate; peak varies non-monotonically with duration
  m3: Monotonic decrease  -- NF-kB amplitude decreases with pulse duration (high-dose filter)
Key predictor: Hill coefficient n (cooperativity); highest MRMR importance score
  TNFR: n = 1.21 (low cooperativity; border between m1 and m2)
  TLR2: n = 1.83 (moderate cooperativity; m2/m3 boundary)
  TLR7: n = 10   (very high cooperativity; operates as high-dose filter; m3)
Noise sources:
  Extrinsic: receptor expression variability (Gamma-distributed)
    TNFR: Gamma(4.32, 1035); TLR2: Gamma(2.23, 4914); TLR7: Gamma(4.80, 4404)
  Intrinsic: stochastic kinetic rate fluctuations
    TNF -> kc variability; Pam -> Hill coefficient n variability; R848 -> K variability

Scope note:
  Five ligand stimulation conditions run in parallel across separate microfluidic chambers
  in the physical experiment. BioScript sequential encoding abstracts this parallelism.
  The imagingPool_all merge chain (5 sequential mix steps) abstracts the single Nikon Ti2
  field-of-view imaging all five stimulation conditions during the 6-hour time-lapse run.
  The QC detect gate uses Pam2CSK4 as the implicit reference (strongest responder arm).
