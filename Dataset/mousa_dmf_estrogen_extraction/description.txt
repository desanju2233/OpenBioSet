PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Droplet-Scale Estrogen Assays in Breast Tissue, Blood, and Serum
Source Link  : https://www.science.org/doi/abs/10.1126/scitranslmed.3000105
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3000105
Journal      : Science Translational Medicine, 2009, Vol. 1, Issue 1, Article 1ra2
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF / EWOD) on single-plate open-top ITO electrode array
Analyte      : Estradiol (17-beta-estradiol)
Sample types : Whole blood, serum, breast tissue homogenate, estradiol standard solutions
Sample volume: 1 uL per assay (1000-4000x smaller than conventional biopsy methods)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a miniaturised, automated digital microfluidic method for extraction
and quantification of estradiol (the most biologically active form of estrogen) from 1-uL
clinical samples, including whole blood, serum, and breast tissue homogenate from
postmenopausal breast cancer patients.

Estrogen is a key hormone in human reproductive physiology and plays an important role in the
pathogenesis of breast cancer. Local breast tissue estrogen concentrations are not routinely
measured because conventional methods require invasive biopsies of hundreds to thousands of mg
of tissue, followed by 5-6 h of laboratory processing (lysis, homogenisation, extraction,
purification, resolubilisation). The DMF method described here reduces this to 10-20 min on a
1-uL sample, while achieving recoveries of 86-119% (ELISA, CV 7-10%).

The method uses digital microfluidics (EWOD) to automate a complete liquid-liquid extraction
workflow: (1) cell lysis with DCM/acetone, (2) steroid dissolution in methanol, and (3)
liquid-liquid partitioning in isooctane to remove interfering compounds (porphyrin m/z 715,
DNA helicase m/z 677, tyrosine m/z 178), which suppress estradiol ionisation in mass
spectrometry. Detection is performed by ELISA (absorbance 450 nm) and LC-MS/MS with selected
reaction monitoring (SRM, m/z 271/145; retention time ~3.2 min).

Breast tissue application: replicate analyses of 1-uL samples from postmenopausal breast cancer
patients yielded 522 pg estradiol/mg tissue (CV 1%), confirming suitability for quantitative
analysis from fine-needle aspiration-scale biopsies.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Type         : Single-plate open-top DMF (EWOD)
Electrodes   : ITO (indium tin oxide), 1.5-mm pitch
Substrate    : Glass coated with Teflon-AF dielectric
Configuration: Open-top during sample drying; top plate affixed before solvent loading

Reservoirs:
  - Sample reservoir (blood, serum, tissue homogenate, or standard solution)
  - Lysing solvent reservoir (DCM/acetone 80:20 v/v)
  - Polar extraction solvent reservoir (methanol, HPLC grade)
  - Non-polar extraction solvent reservoir (isooctane, 99.8%)
  - Collection reservoir (pooled extract output)

Extraction zone:
  A photoresist "wall" defines the liquid-liquid extraction zone, separating the isooctane
  pool from the collection reservoir. The DMF device drives the methanol droplet in circular
  paths within the isooctane pool (~20 s per cycle, movie S1 in paper) before separating and
  directing the aqueous extract toward the collection reservoir. Phase separation at the wall
  is straightforward compared to microchannel-based liquid-liquid extraction, where separating
  one liquid phase from another after contact is inherently difficult.

Droplet volumes:
  Unit droplet (methanol extract): 200-400 nL
  Lysis wave option 1: 9 x 1.1 uL DCM/acetone (9.9 uL total)
  Lysis wave option 2: 5 x 2.2 uL DCM/acetone (11.0 uL total)
  Methanol extraction option 1: 9 x 1.1 uL methanol (9.9 uL total)
  Methanol extraction option 2: 5 x 2.2 uL methanol (11.0 uL total)


REAGENTS
--------

Lysing solvent       : DCM/acetone 80:20 (v/v)
                       Dichloromethane (Sigma); acetone HPLC grade
                       Function: lyse cells; dissolve lipid/protein matrix to free steroids

Polar extraction     : Methanol, HPLC grade (Fisher Scientific)
solvent                Function: dissolve free steroids from dried lysate into polar phase

Non-polar extraction : Isooctane (2,2,4-trimethylpentane), 99.8% (Sigma)
solvent                Function: partition interferents from polar estradiol-containing phase;
                       DCM/acetone and methanol also used for initial extraction (Supp. fig S1)

Reconstitution       : Methanol/DCM 2:1 (v/v), 30 uL aliquot
solvent                Evaporated; residue reconstituted in medium for desired analysis

ELISA diluent        : Estrogen-free serum (ALPCO Diagnostics) + methanol 4:1 (v/v), 50 uL
                       Used to resuspend dried extract before ELISA plate reader readout

Estradiol standard   : 17-beta-estradiol (Steraloids Inc.)
                       2 mg/mL in methanol for LC-MS/MS; serial dilutions for ELISA
                       (40, 1200, 3000, 6000 pg/mL final concentrations)

HPLC-grade water     : Sigma


CLINICAL SAMPLES
----------------

Breast tissue:
  Source         : Apparently normal tissue adjacent to breast cancer tumors
  Patients       : Two postmenopausal breast cancer patients
  Collection     : Surgical resection
  Storage        : -80 C
  Processing     : Manual homogenisation in 1 mL DCM; 1-uL aliquot taken, dried, extracted by
                   DMF Method 1; resuspended in 100 uL methanol/water 80:20 (v/v);
                   10 uL injected onto LC-MS/MS

Blood and serum:
  Source         : Healthy female volunteer
  Timepoints     : Five different reproductive cycles, midluteal phase
  Storage        : -20 C
  MS samples     : 5-uL aliquot dried, extracted by DMF Method 1, reconstituted in 50 uL
                   methanol/formic acid 0.1% (v/v); injected by nanoelectrospray

Ethics: Mount Sinai Hospital and Ontario Tumor Bank Research Ethics Boards


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Sample Loading and Drying
  Sample    : Whole blood (representative; serum or tissue homogenate also applicable)
  Volume    : 1 uL
  Operation : Dispense 1-uL aliquot into sample reservoir; allow to dry at room temperature
  Duration  : ~60 s (1 min per reservoir volume, room temperature, 25 C)
  Notes     : Standard solutions used immediately without drying; blood, serum, and tissue
              homogenate samples dried on electrode surface before top plate is affixed and
              solvents (DCM/acetone and isooctane) are loaded into reservoirs.

Step 2-4 | Lysis -- DCM/Acetone Wave (3 representative cycles)
  Solvent   : DCM/acetone 80:20 (v/v)
  Operation : Each cycle: dispense 1 reservoir volume of DCM/acetone; drive dropwise by DMF
              to the dried sample; incubate at room temperature until dry (~1 min/cycle)
  Duration  : 60 s per cycle; 3 cycles encoded (paper: 9 x 1.1 uL or 5 x 2.2 uL)
  Temperature: 25 C (room temperature)
  Purpose   : Cell lysis and dissolution of lipid/protein matrix to free steroid analytes
  BioScript : Each cycle modelled as dispense + mix; mix abstracts electrode-driven droplet
              translation and merge onto dried sample plus incubation-to-dryness event

Step 5-7 | Methanol Dissolution and Liquid-Liquid Extraction in Isooctane (3 cycles)
  Reagents  : Methanol (polar), Isooctane (non-polar)
  Cycle description:
    (a) Dispense 1 reservoir volume of methanol; drive by DMF to dried lysate to dissolve steroids
    (b) Dispense 1 unit droplet (200-400 nL) of dissolved sample; drive to isooctane reservoir
    (c) Circulate droplet within isooctane pool for ~20 s (electrode-driven; see movie S1, paper)
    (d) Drive droplet out of isooctane toward collection reservoir
    Repeat until sample reservoir exhausted of methanol
  Dissolution duration: 10 s per cycle (DMF mixing)
  Circulation duration: 20 s per cycle at 25 C (isooctane pool; modelled as heat at 25c for 20s)
  Purpose   : Isooctane partitions away interferents (porphyrin m/z 715, DNA helicase m/z 677,
              tyrosine m/z 178) that suppress estradiol ionisation in MS; estradiol remains in
              polar methanol phase and is collected.
  Paper note: Phase separation at photoresist wall is straightforward; contrast with microchannel
              LLE where separating phases after contact is inherently limited.

Step 8 | Pooling, Reconstitution, and Drying
  Operation : Pool all extract droplets in collection reservoir; mix with estrogen-free serum
              diluent (4:1 serum:methanol, 50 uL) for ELISA reconstitution; dry at 25 C
  Duration  : 5 s (pooling mix) + 5 min (300 s) drying at room temperature
  Storage   : Devices stored at -20 C after extract collected and dried
  Pre-analysis: 30 uL methanol/DCM (2:1 v/v) added to dried extract; dispensed to centrifuge
                tube; solvent evaporated; residue reconstituted in analysis-specific medium

Step 9 | ELISA Extraction QC Detection
  Module    : estradiolExtractionQC
  Target    : pooledExtract
  Duration  : 30 s
  Method    : Estradiol-specific ELISA (ALPCO Diagnostics); absorbance at 450 nm;
              mQuant microplate spectrophotometer (Bio-Tek Instruments); min. 3 replicates
  Results   : Recovery 86-119%; CV 7-10% (Fig. 4, paper)
              Breast tissue: 522 pg estradiol/mg tissue; CV 1% (replicate 1-uL samples)
  QC gate   : extractionSignal > 0 -> dispose (pass, proceed to MS branch)
              extractionSignal <= 0 -> drain (fail, extraction unsuccessful)

Step 10 | MS Confirmation Detection
  Module    : estradiolMSDetection
  Target    : estradiolStd (fresh estradiol standard as MS reference droplet)
  Duration  : 192 s (~3.2 min; SRM retention time plus instrument equilibration)
  Methods   :
    (i) LTQ direct MS (Thermo Scientific): nanoelectrospray, negative mode, 250 C, 0.5 uL/min
        Estradiol fragment m/z 183; absent in whole samples, dominant in DMF-extracted samples
    (ii) QTRAP LC-MS/MS SRM (Applied Biosystems): m/z 271/145; retention time ~3.2 min
        Column: Thermo Gold C18 2.2 um, 2.1 mm ID x 50 mm (microbore)
        Mobile phase: MeOH/H2O 80:20 (v/v), isocratic; 300 uL/min; 10 uL injection
        Spray: 4200 V; collision energy: 60 V; nebulising: 400 C
  QC gate   : msSignal > 0 -> dispose (confirmed); msSignal <= 0 -> drain (not confirmed)

  - True-branch blocks (bsbbif_2_t, bsbbif_5_t) emit no unconditional jump in IR;
    DOT edges are correct

Abstraction notes:
  - Lysis: paper uses 9x or 5x cycles; 3 explicit cycles encoded (no loop primitive in BioScript)
  - Methanol extraction: same; 3 cycles encoded; each cycle models dissolution + LLE + circulation
  - LLE circulation: heat at 25c for 20s abstracts the ~20 s electrode-driven circulation
    of the methanol droplet within the isooctane pool (movie S1 in paper)
  - Isooctane: modelled as a manifest; mix(dissolvedSample, isooctane) abstracts
    electrode-driven translation into the isooctane pool
  - heat is void/in-place throughout; all dispensed fluid variables consumed exactly once
