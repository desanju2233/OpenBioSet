PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Microfluidic Magnetic Separation for Particle-Based Immunoassays
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/estrogen%20and%20TSH%20immunoassay.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac3020627
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2012, Vol. 84, pp. 8805-8812
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF), air-matrix (oil-free), EWOD electrode array with movable permanent magnet


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first particle-based immunoassay implemented on digital microfluidics
(DMF) without the aid of an oil carrier fluid (i.e., droplets surrounded by air). The air-matrix
format enabled a novel on-chip magnetic particle separation and resuspension method -- the
"supernatant separation" method -- capable of removing greater than 90% of unbound reagents in
a single step. Using this approach, two immunoassays were developed:

  1. Competitive immunoassay for 17beta-estradiol (E2), a small-molecule steroid (272.38 Da).
     This is the first competitive immunoassay implemented on any DMF platform.
  2. Noncompetitive (sandwich) immunoassay for Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH), the most
     commonly performed immunoassay in clinical laboratories.

Compared to conventional well-plate ELISA:
  - 100-fold reduction in reagent volume (11.2 uL vs. 1.2 mL total per assay)
  - 10-fold reduction in analysis time (6 min sample incubation vs. 60 min)
  - Analytical performance retained at a level suitable for clinical screening

The technique has potential for use in fast, low-waste, inexpensive instruments for quantitative
analysis of proteins and small molecules in low sample volumes.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Actuation electrodes : 96
Reservoirs           : 10
Assay regions        : 4 (independent assays per device)
Detection region     : 1 (shared; electrodes rinsed with H2O2 between assays)
Unit droplet volume  : ~800 nL
Droplet environment  : Air (no oil carrier fluid; Pluronic L64 enables oil-free actuation)

Magnet system:
  - Neodymium permanent magnet, N48 grade, 5/8 in diameter x 1/4 in thick, 15.3 lb pull force
  - Positioned in Stripwell microplate manifold (selected wells removed as guides) below device
  - Controlled manually via metal clip; horizontal movement adjusts magnetic force on particles
  - Estimated magnetic force on particle pellet: ~470 uN
  - Minimum particle density for supernatant separation: ~3.0 x 10^8 particles/mL

Detection instrument:
  - Pherastar plate reader (BMG Labtech, Cary, NC)
  - Module: LUM (chemiluminescence); Gain: 3600; Focal height: 8.6 mm
  - Device affixed with tape to empty 96-well Stripwell plate; each droplet aligned to a well
  - Endpoint: 1 min (chemiluminescence signal saturates at 1 min)


IMMUNOASSAY MODES
-----------------
Competitive (E2):
  Exogenous E2-HRP conjugate (labeled antigen) competes with unlabeled E2 in sample for
  binding sites on anti-E2 capture antibodies immobilised on paramagnetic particle surfaces.
  Signal (chemiluminescence, RLU) is INVERSELY proportional to E2 concentration.
  Calibration: 10-1000 pg/mL. Curve fit: 4-parameter logistic equation.

Noncompetitive / Sandwich (TSH):
  Anti-TSH-HRP detection antibody and anti-beta-TSH capture antibody bind TSH at different
  epitopes, forming an antibody-antigen-antibody sandwich complex on the particle surface.
  Signal (chemiluminescence, RLU) is DIRECTLY proportional to TSH concentration.
  Calibration: 0.4-20 uIU/mL. Curve fit: linear equation.


REAGENTS
--------
Fouling mitigation:
  Pluronic L64 (BASF Corp., Germany), donated by Brenntag Canada, at 0.05% (v/v) in all
  solutions. Enables oil-free droplet actuation. Not found to interfere with specific binding
  in well-plate or DMF immunoassays. Custom DMF-compatible particle diluent, conjugate diluent,
  and wash buffer formulated with L64 as sole surfactant (ARCHITECT native surfactants
  cross-reacted with added L64 and prevented reliable droplet movement).

TSH-specific blocking:
  TSH paramagnetic particles blocked with nonfat dried milk prior to use. Required because
  initial TSH immunoassay experiments showed high background noise (nonspecific adsorption of
  anti-TSH-HRP on particle surfaces) preventing differentiation between 0 and 0.4 uIU/mL.
  Blocking significantly reduced background without affecting droplet actuation.

E2 conjugate optimisation:
  E2-HRP (E2 antigen labeled with HRP) at 1 ug/mL optimal concentration. At higher
  concentrations the dynamic range collapses: 50 pg/mL and 500 pg/mL produce similar signals.

Substrates:
  H2O2 (oxidising co-substrate for HRP) + luminol/enhancer solution. Mixed on-chip immediately
  before measurement. Two H2O2 pre-rinse cycles remove residual wash buffer before substrate
  delivery, preventing interference with the chemiluminescent reaction.

ELISA kits:
  Calbiotech (Spring Valley, CA) E2 and TSH kits for well-plate comparison experiments and
  wash-test TMB assays.


WASHING METHODS
---------------

Serial dilution (prior art; NOT used in this protocol):
  Particles immobilised by magnet. Droplet diluted by merging with wash buffer droplet.
  DMF splits pooled droplet; excess removed. Repeated until sufficient dilution.
  Requires >=6 washes (1:1 wash/initial ratio) for 98% unbound reagent removal.
  Drawbacks: slow dilution over many cycles; magnet immobilises particles throughout,
  trapping unbound material between particles.

Supernatant separation (THIS PROTOCOL):
  Magnet focuses particles into pellet on device surface. Supernatant droplet actuated
  away to waste with magnet in place. Magnet removed. Fresh droplet passed over pellet
  and shuttled in circular motion across 4 electrodes to resuspend.
  Achieves 98% unbound reagent removal within 2 wash cycles.
  Requirements:
    (1) Sufficient magnetic force: pellet force (~470 uN) must exceed interfacial force
        pulling particles into droplet. Satisfied for pellets of >=3.0x10^8 particles/mL.
    (2) Adjustable magnetic force: magnet moved horizontally for resuspension.
    (3) Stable particle suspension: ~5% residual liquid retained; particles resuspended
        immediately to prevent aggregation or drying.
  Not compatible with oil-immersed DMF devices.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Particle Separation from Diluent
  Operation  : Dispense 1 unit (800 nL) antibody-coated paramagnetic particle suspension.
               Pluronic L64 pre-mixed to 0.05% (v/v).
  Magnet     : Positioned to immobilise particles as pellet; diluent supernatant actuated to waste.
  Notes      : ~5% residual liquid retained by hydrophilic particles in pellet.
               Performed identically for both E2 and TSH assays (different particles dispensed).

Step 2 | Sample Incubation (3 unit droplets, 6 min)
  Operation  : Three 800 nL sample droplets (2.4 uL total) sequentially dispensed and
               delivered to particle pellet for resuspension and incubation.
  Duration   : 6 min (sufficient to saturate analyte binding; droplets show <5% volume loss
               at >=30 min, so evaporation is not a concern at 6 min).
  E2 (competitive)      : Unlabeled E2 competes for anti-E2 binding sites on particle surface.
  TSH (noncompetitive)  : TSH antigen captured by anti-beta-TSH antibody on particle surface.
  Notes      : 3 unit droplets chosen to improve signal over 1 droplet while remaining
               easily manipulated by DMF (volumes much larger than 1 unit are less reliable).

Step 3 | Post-Sample Wash x4 (Supernatant Separation)
  Operation  : 4 supernatant separation wash cycles with 800 nL wash buffer each.
               Each cycle: magnet immobilises pellet; supernatant to waste; fresh wash buffer
               reconstitutes particles via 4-electrode circular motion.
  Purpose    : Removes unbound sample matrix, excess analyte, and nonspecifically bound proteins.

Step 4 | Conjugate Incubation (2 min)
  Operation  : 1 unit (800 nL) conjugate droplet delivered to washed particles for
               resuspension and incubation.
  Duration   : 2 min
  E2         : E2-HRP (1 ug/mL) competes with sample E2 for remaining anti-E2 binding sites.
  TSH        : Anti-TSH-HRP detection antibody binds TSH at a second epitope, completing
               the antibody-antigen-antibody sandwich complex on the particle.

Step 5 | Post-Conjugate Wash x4 (Supernatant Separation)
  Operation  : 4 supernatant separation wash cycles (same as Step 3).
  Purpose    : Removes unbound HRP-labeled conjugate.

Step 6 | H2O2 Pre-Rinse x2 (Supernatant Separation)
  Operation  : 2 supernatant separation wash cycles with 800 nL H2O2 each.
  Purpose    : Removes residual wash buffer prior to substrate delivery. Prevents dilution
               or quenching of the H2O2/luminol chemiluminescent reaction.
               Replaces manufacturer-recommended H2O2/luminol pre-mix, which caused
               high inter-assay variability.

Step 7 | H2O2 Substrate Incubation (2 min)
  Operation  : Particles delivered to shared detection region. 1 unit (800 nL) H2O2 droplet
               reconstitutes particle pellet and incubates for 2 min.
  Purpose    : H2O2 serves as the oxidising co-substrate for HRP enzyme activity.
  Notes      : Detection region pre-rinsed with H2O2 between successive assays.

Step 8 | Chemiluminescence Activation and Detection
  Operation  : 1 unit (800 nL) luminol/enhancer droplet merged on-chip with the
               H2O2/particle droplet. Incubated 1 min (signal saturates at 1 min endpoint).
  Detection  : Pherastar plate reader, LUM module, gain=3600, focal height=8.6 mm.
               Device taped to empty 96-well Stripwell plate, detection zone per-well aligned.
  E2 signal  : Inversely proportional to E2 concentration (competitive assay).
  TSH signal : Directly proportional to TSH concentration (noncompetitive assay).
  QC gate    : Signal > 0 -> dispose assay droplet (pass). Signal <= 0 -> drain (fail).


PERFORMANCE
-----------

E2 Competitive Immunoassay:
  Calibration range : 10-1000 pg/mL
  Dynamic range     : ~2 orders of magnitude
  LOD (absolute)    : 50 fg         (well-plate ELISA: 152 fg)
  LOD (conc.)       : 21 pg/mL      (well-plate ELISA: 6.1 pg/mL)
  CV range          : 5-14%         (well-plate ELISA: 4-12%)
  Sample volume     : 2.4 uL        (well-plate ELISA: 25 uL)
  Incubation time   : 6 min         (well-plate ELISA: 60 min)
  Clinical use      : Ovarian cancer screening (E2 > 20 pg/mL, age-dependent)
                      E2 serum ranges: males 10-50 pg/mL; premenopausal 30-400 pg/mL;
                      postmenopausal <30 pg/mL

TSH Noncompetitive Immunoassay:
  Calibration range : 0.4-20 uIU/mL
  Dynamic range     : ~2 orders of magnitude
  LOD (absolute)    : 2.0 nIU       (well-plate ELISA: 3.0 nIU)
  LOD (conc.)       : 0.83 uIU/mL   (well-plate ELISA: 0.06 uIU/mL)
  CV range          : 9-21%         (well-plate ELISA: 2-13%)
  Sample volume     : 2.4 uL        (well-plate ELISA: 50 uL)
  Incubation time   : 6 min         (well-plate ELISA: 60 min)
  Clinical use      : Diagnosis of hypothyroidism (TSH > 3.0 uIU/mL);
                      normal serum range: 0.4-3.0 uIU/mL

Reagent consumption comparison (DMF vs. well-plate ELISA):
  Sample       : 2.4 uL vs. 50 uL    (E2) / 25 uL (E2) well-plate
  Conjugate    : 0.80 uL vs. 100 uL
  Wash buffer  : 6.4 uL vs. 900 uL
  Substrate    : 1.6 uL vs. 150 uL
  TOTAL        : 11.2 uL vs. ~1.2 mL  -->  100-fold reduction

Time comparison:
  DMF sample incubation    : 6 min
  ARCHITECT analyzer       : 18 min
  Well-plate ELISA         : 60 min
  --> 10-fold faster vs. ELISA; mechanism: smaller volume packs same particle number
      into smaller space, increasing surface-area-to-volume ratio and reaction kinetics

                 Luminol_Enhancer, PluronicL64
CFG structure  :
  BB1 (main)     E2 full protocol (S1-S8) + detect + conditional branch
  BB2 (if_2_t)   e2Signal > 0: dispose(e2_detectionMix)
  BB3 (if_3_f)   e2Signal <= 0: drain(e2_detectionMix); jump BB4
  BB4 (if_4_j)   Join; TSH full protocol (S1-S8) + detect + conditional branch
  BB5 (if_5_t)   tshSignal > 0: dispose(tsh_detectionMix)
  BB6 (if_6_f)   tshSignal <= 0: drain(tsh_detectionMix); jump BB7
  BB7 (if_7_j)   Terminal: dispose(tshSignal); NOP
Compiler note  : Both pass (BB2/BB5) and fail (BB3/BB6) branches emit dispose() in the IR;
                 the compiler resolves drain() to dispose() for both conditional paths.
                 Quantitative signal interpretation (calibration curve fitting) occurs off-chip.
