PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Microfluidic Immunocytochemistry in Single Cells (DISC)
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms8513
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms8513
Journal      : Nature Communications, 2015, Vol. 6, Article 7513
Platform     : Digital Microfluidics (DMF) -- two-plate electrowetting-on-dielectric (EWOD)


OVERVIEW
--------
DISC is a DMF-based method that automates all steps required to analyse signalling events in
adherent cells in situ: cell culture, serum starvation, sub-second ligand stimulation, chemical
fixation, and full immunocytochemistry (ICC) for quantitative single-cell phosphorylation profiling.

The platform overcomes two key limitations of existing approaches:
  1. Flow-based microfluidics require high flow rates for fast stimulus switching, producing
     shear stresses (>1 dyn/cm2) that activate unwanted mechanosensitive signalling pathways.
     DISC operates at ~0.5 dyn/cm2 with no moving parts.
  2. Conventional bulk methods (western blot, mass spectrometry) require cell disaggregation
     and cannot resolve single-cell heterogeneity or achieve time resolution below minutes.
     DISC achieves ~1 s reagent exchange and probes individual adherent cells in situ.

The protocol is demonstrated on the well-characterised PDGFR/PI3K/Akt signalling pathway
in NIH-3T3 fibroblasts and extended to breast cancer cell heterogeneity (MCF-7 and MDA-MB-231).
Key finding: a 10 s pulse of PDGF-BB (3.125 ng/mL) is sufficient to commit >32% of cells to
Akt activation -- kinetics far faster than any previously reported method could resolve.

The complete protocol encodes two sequential ICC arms:
  Arm 1 (PDGFR arm): 2 min stimulation; probes PDGFR Y857 / Y1021 phosphorylation.
  Arm 2 (Akt arm):   5 min stimulation; probes Akt S473 phosphorylation.
Both arms run the identical 11-step ICC pipeline with arm-specific antibody panels.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration: Two parallel plates separated by a ~180 um double-sided tape spacer.

Bottom plate:
  Material    : Chromium electrodes on glass (200 x 300 x 1.1 mm, Telic Co.)
  Electrodes  : 80 actuation electrodes, 2.2 x 2.2 mm each; interdigitated borders
                (140 um sinusoidal pattern); inter-electrode gap 30-80 um.
                Reservoir electrodes for reagent loading and waste removal.
  Dielectric  : Parylene C, ~7 um (vapour deposition with A174 silane adhesion primer).
  Hydrophobic : Teflon-AF, ~200 nm (spin-coat 1% w/w in FC-40, 2000 rpm, 60 s; 165 C bake).

Top plate:
  Material    : ITO-coated glass (Delta Technologies Ltd, 300 x 100 mm).
  VM sites    : 8 circular hydrophilic ITO spots (1.75 mm diameter, 4.4 mm pitch) created by
                Teflon-AF lift-off using S1811 photoresist patterning.
  Function    : Cells adhere to the ITO spots; Teflon-AF surrounds spots to confine droplets.

Virtual Microwells (VMs):
  Volume      : ~400 nL per VM (2.4 mm2 footprint).
  Formation   : Passive dispensing -- when a source droplet (~900 nL unit droplet or larger)
                is swept across a vacant ITO spot, a sub-droplet spontaneously forms by
                hydrophobic-hydrophilic interaction (Supplementary Fig. 1).
  Capacity    : 150-300 cells per VM at 500,000 cells/mL seeding density.
  Exchange    : One passive dispense displaces ~93% of VM contents.
                Two successive exchanges displace >99% (Fig. 2b-d).
  Exchange time: ~1 s between successive droplet deliveries (Fig. 2a).

Actuation:
  Waveform    : Sine wave, 80-100 V_rms, 10 kHz.
  Interface   : 90-pin Pogo pin connector.
  Control     : DropBot open-source DMF automation system (http://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/dropbot).
  Orientation : Standard (top plate up) for droplet manipulation; inverted (top plate down)
                for all incubation steps so cells settle onto ITO spots by gravity.


CELL LINES
----------
- NIH-3T3 (mouse embryonic fibroblasts):
  Primary model for PDGFR/PI3K/Akt pathway validation (Figs. 3, 5, 6).
  Cultured in DMEM + 10% calf serum (CS) + 100 U/mL pen G + 100 ug/mL strep, 37 C, 5% CO2.
  Seeded at 500,000 cells/mL; yields ~150-300 cells per VM.

- MCF-7 (human breast cancer):
  Used for Akt single-cell heterogeneity experiments, 0-5 min stimulation
  (Supplementary Figs. 9-11). Cultured in DMEM + 10% FBS + antibiotics.

- MDA-MB-231 (human breast cancer, triple-negative):
  Used for hyperactive-responder single-cell morphology analysis (Fig. 4).
  Higher levels of Akt activation vs MCF-7 after 5 min (Supplementary Fig. 9a).
  Cultured in DMEM + 10% FBS + antibiotics.


PROTOCOL STEPS -- 11-STEP ICC PIPELINE
---------------------------------------
All reagents contain 0.05% v/v Pluronic F-68 (on-chip) to prevent biofouling.
All incubation steps performed at room temperature (25 C) unless otherwise noted.
Device inverted (top plate down) for all incubation steps.

Step 0 | Cell Seeding and Adhesion
  Reagent  : CultureMedia (DMEM + 10% serum + 0.05% F-68 + antibiotics)
  Cells    : NIH-3T3 at 500,000 cells/mL
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL passively dispensed per VM spot
  Duration : 24 h at 37 C in humidified chamber (= 1440 min = 86400 s)
  Notes    : Device inverted after seeding. Cells settle by gravity; adhere to ITO spots.
             10^5-10^6 cells/mL confirmed sufficient for analysis (Supplementary Fig. 4).

Step 1 | Serum Starvation
  Reagent  : StarvedMedia (DMEM + 0.5% serum + 0.05% F-68 + antibiotics)
  Volume   : 6 x 900 nL = 5.4 uL
  Duration : 24 h at 37 C (= 1440 min = 86400 s)
  Notes    : Reduces basal PDGFR and Akt phosphorylation to unstimulated baseline.

Step 2 | Serum-Free Rinse
  Reagent  : SerumFreeMedia (DMEM + 0.05% F-68 + antibiotics)
  Volume   : 6 x 900 nL = 5.4 uL
  Duration : Immediate (passive dispense; ~1 s per exchange)
  Notes    : Two successive exchanges remove >99% of starving media (Fig. 2b-d).

Step 3 | PDGF-BB Ligand Stimulation
  Reagent  : PDGF-BB in SerumFreeMedia (Sigma-Aldrich human recombinant)
  Volume   : 2 x 900 nL = 1.8 uL
  Duration : 2 min for PDGFR arm; 5 min for Akt arm (encoded separately in BioScript)
  Dose     : 0-200 ng/mL (dose-response); 50 ng/mL (time-series); 3.125 ng/mL (pulse)
  Shear    : ~0.5 dyn/cm2 -- below 1 dyn/cm2 threshold for mechanosensitive signalling.
             Validated: 75 continuous back-and-forth movements, P=0.7188, no Akt effect.
  Variants :
    Single bolus  : Continuous PDGF-BB; fix at t = 30-120 s (Fig. 5a)
    Double bolus  : PDGF-BB at t=0 s + second PDGF-BB or vehicle at t=90 s (Fig. 5b)
    Pulse washaway: PDGF-BB for 3-40 s, then SerumFreeMedia wash, then fix at 2-5 min (Fig. 6a/b/c)

Step 4 | Fixation
  Reagent  : 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) + 0.05% F-68 in DPBS
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL
  Duration : 10 min (= 600 s)
  Notes    : Applied within ~1 s of stimulation termination. Arrests signalling at exact
             time point. In time-series experiments, fixation staggered across VMs so
             all VMs are fixed simultaneously.

Step 5 | Post-Fixation Wash
  Reagent  : WashBuffer1 (0.05% v/v F-68 in DPBS)
  Volume   : 6 x 900 nL = 5.4 uL

Step 6 | Permeabilisation
  Reagent  : 0.2% v/v Tween-20 in DPBS
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL
  Duration : 10 min (= 600 s)
  Notes    : Required for intracellular antibody access; essential for pAkt S473 detection.

Step 7 | Post-Permeabilisation Wash
  Reagent  : WashBuffer2 (0.05% v/v Brij 35 in DPBS)
  Volume   : 9 x 900 nL = 8.1 uL
  Notes    : Extended wash volume to fully remove Tween-20 before blocking.

Step 8 | Blocking
  Reagent  : 1% bovine non-fat dried milk + 0.05% Brij 35 in DPBS
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL
  Duration : 60 min (= 3600 s)

Step 9 | Primary Antibody Incubation
  PDGFR arm:
    pan-PDGFR-b : Mouse mAb 1:50, CST sc-19995             [total protein; Cy5 channel]
    p-PDGFR Y857: Rabbit pAb 1:200, SCB sc-12907-R         [phospho; Cy3 channel]
    p-PDGFR Y1021: Rabbit pAb 1:200, SCB sc-12909-R        [phospho; Cy3 channel]
  Akt arm:
    pan-Akt     : Mouse mAb 1:50, CST 2920S                 [total protein; Cy5 channel]
    p-Akt S473  : Rabbit mAb 1:200, CST 4060P               [phospho; Cy3 channel]
  Diluent  : Blocking solution (BS); pre-mixed off-chip before DMF reservoir loading
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL
  Duration : Overnight at 4 C (= 720 min = 43200 s)

Step 10 | Post-Primary Wash
  Reagent  : WashBuffer2 (0.05% Brij 35 in DPBS)
  Volume   : 6 x 900 nL = 5.4 uL

Step 11 | Secondary Antibody Incubation
  Anti-Mouse IgG Alexa Fluor 647 Fab2, 1:500, CST 4410S   [total protein; Cy5 channel]
  Anti-Rabbit IgG Alexa Fluor 555 Fab2, 1:500, CST 4413S  [phospho-protein; Cy3 channel]
  Diluent  : Blocking solution (BS); pre-mixed off-chip
  Volume   : 3 x 900 nL = 2.7 uL
  Duration : 2 h at room temperature (25 C) (= 120 min = 7200 s)
  Post     : Top plate removed; rinsed 30 s in 0.05% Tween-20/DPBS; rinsed 30 s in DI water;
             air-dried in dark.


DETECTION AND ANALYSIS
-----------------------
Scanner      : Axon GenePix 4000B microarray scanner.
               Settings: 5 um resolution, 0 mm focus, 532 nm and 635 nm lasers at 100% power,
               PMT 500 V (adjusted per experiment to avoid saturation).
Analysis     : CellProfiler 2.0 r11710 (open-source, http://www.cellprofiler.org/).
               Custom pipeline: image crop, cell identification (Otsu Global thresholding,
               two-class, 0.7 correction factor, 15-100 um diameter), shape and neighbour
               analysis, dual-channel fluorescence measurement.
Quantification: For each cell, fold change = (Cy3 phospho-channel intensity) / (Cy5 total-
               protein intensity), normalised to the no-stimulation control on the same device.
               Data exported for GraphPad Prism (bulk plots) and JMP 10 (single-cell analysis).

PDGFR results (Fig. 3b):
  Dose-response: phosphorylation onset at 3.125 ng/mL; plateau at 50 ng/mL.
  Hill coefficient (Y857): 1.73 +/- 0.33; EC50: 15.89 +/- 1.86 ng/mL.
  Hill coefficient (Y1021): 2.13 +/- 0.44; EC50: 18.33 +/- 1.94 ng/mL.
  Maximal phosphorylation at: Y857 = 75 s; Y1021 = 60 s after stimulation (Fig. 5a).

Akt results (Fig. 3c, Fig. 6):
  Downstream amplification: at 3.125 ng/mL PDGF, ~22% PDGFR phosphorylation drives >70% Akt.
  Pulse commitment (Fig. 6b): 10 s pulse commits >32% of cells to Akt activation.
                               3 s pulse insufficient (indistinguishable from control).
  Noise filtering (Fig. 6c): 3 s pulse induces transient Akt (20% active at 3-4 min)
                              deactivated by 5 min -- enzymatic phosphorylation/dephosphorylation.

Cell heterogeneity (Fig. 4):
  Hyperactive responders defined as: p-Akt fold change > Q3 + 1.5 * IQR.
  MDA-MB-231 peak hyperactivity: 10.2% of cells at 5 min stimulation.
  Hyperactive cell morphology: higher eccentricity (elongated), lower form factor (less spherical),
  more and closer neighbours vs normal cells. Effect attributed to paracrine signalling.
  Cell position in VM: no effect on Akt activation (Supplementary Fig. 15).

Issues resolved:
  1. Unicode error (UnicodeDecodeError at position 2598):
     Two UTF-8 degree symbols (0xC2 0xB0) in comment lines 9 and 11 of the ICC protocol
     summary replaced with ASCII hyphen. ANTLR4 FileStream requires strict 7-bit ASCII input.

  2. SSA version-2 undefined variable conflict in BB7_j:
     secondaryStained and aktSecStained were consumed (disposed/drained) in the BB2/BB3 and
     BB5/BB6 gate branches. Re-referencing them in BB7 caused the compiler to emit
     secondaryStained2[0] and aktSecStained2[0] -- undefined SSA names.
     Fix: Replaced mix(secondaryStained, aktSecStained) with a fresh dispense of
     WashBuffer2 as qcCarrier. In the physical experiment, the cellViabilityDetection gate
     is purely computational (CellProfiler on acquired images); the fluid operand is a
     BioScript structural requirement, not a wet-lab step.

BioScript abstractions:
  Passive dispensing    : Modelled as dispense N units + mix. The compiler treats N as a
                          volume scalar; no array elements are generated -- correct because
                          each VM receives the full merged volume.
  Concurrent VMs        : The physical device has 8 VMs; BioScript has no native spatial
                          parallelism. The PDGFR and Akt arms are encoded as sequential BB
                          blocks. For multi-VM or multi-condition runs, replicate the
                          instruction block.
  Antibody pre-mixing   : Primary and secondary antibodies are pre-mixed off-chip in
                          BlockingSolution before reservoir loading. Encoded as successive
                          dispense + mix pairs before VM application.
  Microarray scan       : Off-chip post-processing step; abstracted as detect
                          phosphorylationDetection on the stained VM droplet.
  CellProfiler gate     : Purely computational in the wet-lab; abstracted as detect
                          cellViabilityDetection on qcCarrier (fresh WashBuffer2 droplet).

CFG structure:
  BB1  main          : entry -- cell seeding through secondary antibody and PDGFR detection
  BB2  bsbbif_2_t   : PDGFR pass -- stimulusSignal > 0 -- dispose(secondaryStained)
  BB3  bsbbif_3_f   : PDGFR fail -- stimulusSignal <= 0 -- drain(secondaryStained)
  BB4  bsbbif_4_j   : join -- dispose stimulusSignal; begin Akt ICC arm
  BB5  bsbbif_5_t   : Akt pass -- aktSignal > 0 -- dispose(aktSecStained)
  BB6  bsbbif_6_f   : Akt fail -- aktSignal <= 0 -- drain(aktSecStained)
