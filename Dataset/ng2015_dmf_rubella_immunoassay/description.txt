PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Digital Microfluidic Platform for the Detection of Rubella Infection and Immunity: A Proof of Concept
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/ClinChemV61I2.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1373/clinchem.2014.232181
Journal      : Clinical Chemistry, 2015, Vol. 61, No. 2, pp. 420-429
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF), two-plate, air-filled, with motorised PMT and magnetic lens assembly


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a shoebox-sized DMF immunoassay platform for the detection of rubella
virus (RV)-specific IgG and IgM antibodies from patient serum, plasma, or whole blood. The
system is motivated by the need for decentralised diagnostics in resource-limited settings to
prevent congenital rubella syndrome (CRS): 100,000 infants are born with CRS each year, and
rural-to-laboratory transport for standard centralised testing is often cost-prohibitive.

Two protocols are encoded in sequence on the same DMF device and .bs file:
  1. RV IgG immunoassay (7-step indirect ELISA): detects immunity or past/current infection.
     Clinical cutoff: 10 IU/mL. LOD: 0.03 IU/mL; LOQ: 0.1 IU/mL.
     Run time: ~25 min per quadruplicate set. Intraassay CVs: 0.3-6.5%.
  2. RV IgM immunoassay (7-step indirect ELISA + on-chip IgG blocking pretreatment):
     the critical test for diagnosing active rubella infection and identifying pregnancies
     at risk for CRS. Run time: ~35 min per quadruplicate set. Intraassay CVs: 1.5-13.4%.

Both assays use rubella-virus-coated paramagnetic microparticles for analyte capture,
HRP-conjugated detection antibodies for signal transduction, and SuperSignal ELISA Femto
chemiluminescent substrate (H2O2 + luminol-enhancer) for readout. Signal is detected by an
integrated motorised PMT (photomultiplier tube); readout is in RLU (relative light units)
over a 10 s integration window.

Diagnostic performance was evaluated against 25 plasma/serum samples from the SeraCare
PTR201 commercial mixed-titer performance panel, using Abbott EIA as the reference standard.
Both assays achieved 100% sensitivity and 100% specificity (AUC = 1.00 for each).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration  : Two-plate DMF (top plate ground + bottom plate electrode array); air-filled gap.
Gap            : ~140 um between top and bottom plates.
Instrument     : ~7 x 9 x 12 inches (shoebox-sized); computer-controlled via DropBot.
Bottom plate   : 80 actuation electrodes (2.54 x 2.54 mm each); reservoir electrodes for
                 sample/reagent storage and waste removal; chromium electrode layer + insulator
                 + Teflon-AF hydrophobic coating on glass substrate.
Top plate      : ITO ground electrode + Teflon-AF hydrophobic coating on glass.
Unit droplet   : ~900 nL (electrode area x 140 um gap).
Actuation      : 80-100 V_rms, 10 kHz sine wave; Pogo pin interface (90 pins);
                 controlled by DropBot (open-source DMF automation system).

Magnetic lens  : Motorised neodymium magnet assembly.
                 Active position: ~150 um below device; immobilisation force: ~600 uN.
                 Inactive position: 3.8 cm below device (allows droplet resuspension).
                 Minimum release threshold: ~500 uN (exceeds droplet surface tension).
                 Function: concentrates particles into pellet in the separation zone;
                 deactivation allows a fresh droplet to be mixed over the pellet to
                 resuspend particles for subsequent reagent incubation steps.

PMT detector   : Motorised; positioned a few hundred um above device droplet centre when active;
                 5 cm above when inactive (LED + webcam used for droplet monitoring).
                 PMT protection: 2 position sensors, shutter, light-tight enclosure grooves.
                 Integration: 10 s mean signal; output in RLU.
                 Advantage: no excitation source, filters, or lenses required.

Fabrication    : University of Toronto Nanofabrication Centre cleanroom.


REAGENTS
--------
Particles        : RV-coated paramagnetic microparticles (Abbott Architect rubella kit);
                   pre-washed 2x in Superblock, resuspended at ~10x stock (1.5e8 particles/mL).
Wash buffer      : Custom DMF-compatible formulation + 0.05% Pluronic L64.
Conjugate diluent: Custom DMF-compatible formulation + 0.05% Pluronic L64.
HRP conjugate    : (Protocol 1) HRP-conjugated goat polyclonal antihuman IgG, 21 ng/mL.
                   (Protocol 2) HRP-conjugated goat antihuman IgM, 23 ng/mL.
Substrate        : SuperSignal ELISA Femto (Thermo Fischer Scientific):
                   stable peroxide (H2O2) + luminol-enhancer; 900 nL each.
Pretreatment     : Goat antihuman IgG (Abbott Architect IgM pretreatment reagent, 3x dilution
                   in conjugate diluent); 900 nL per IgM assay.
Sample diluent   : Dulbecco PBS + 4% BSA; all samples pre-diluted 10x off-chip before loading.
Pluronic additive: 0.05% Pluronic L64 added to all on-chip reagents; prevents protein
                   adsorption and cross-contamination in the air-filled DMF device.
Calibrators      : RV IgG: 0, 5, 10, 15, 75, 250 IU/mL (Abbott Architect).
                   RV IgM cutoff: Cal 1 (Abbott Architect); RF IgM content 27.0 IU/mL.
Performance panel: SeraCare PTR201-01-25 (25 plasma/serum samples; collected 1994-1995).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

PROTOCOL 1 | RV IgG IMMUNOASSAY (7 steps, ~25 min per quadruplicate)

Step 1 | Particle dispense and magnetic separation
  Reagents : VirusCoatedParticles
  Volume   : 1.8 uL (2 units); 1.5e8 particles/mL in Superblock
  Operation: Dispense from reservoir; magnetic lens activated (~150 um below device, ~600 uN);
             particles pellet in separation zone; Superblock diluent moved to waste.
  Duration : ~5 s
  Notes    : Particle pellet retained on-chip; resuspended in next step.

Step 2 | Sample incubation
  Reagents : SampleIgG (pre-diluted 10x in DPBS/4% BSA off-chip)
  Volume   : 1.8 uL (2 units)
  Operation: Dispense; deliver to particle pellet; mix in circular motion 3 min.
             RV IgG from sample binds RV-coated particle surface.
             Magnetic lens re-activated; supernatant disposed.
  Duration : 3 min (180 s; IR: 180.0 SECOND)

Step 3 | First wash cycle x4
  Reagents : WashBuffer
  Volume   : 4 x 1.8 uL (4 x 2 units)
  Operation: 4 sequential wash steps. Each: dispense fresh buffer droplet; deliver to pellet;
             mix; magnetic lens activated; supernatant disposed.
             Removes unbound sample antibody and non-specific proteins.
  Duration : 30 s per wash (4 x 30 s; IR: 30.0 SECOND each)

Step 4 | HRP conjugate incubation
  Reagents : HRPConjugateIgG (21 ng/mL HRP-conjugated goat polyclonal antihuman IgG)
  Volume   : 1.8 uL (2 units)
  Operation: Dispense; deliver to particle pellet; mix 2 min.
             HRP-anti-IgG binds RV IgG captured on particles (sandwich immunocomplex).
             Magnetic lens re-activated; unbound conjugate disposed.
  Duration : 2 min (120 s; IR: 120.0 SECOND)

Step 5 | Second wash cycle x4
  Reagents : WashBuffer
  Volume   : 4 x 1.8 uL (4 x 2 units)
  Operation: Identical to Step 3. Removes excess unbound HRP conjugate before substrate.
  Duration : 30 s per wash (4 x 30 s)

Step 6 | Substrate addition, reaction, and chemiluminescent detection
  Reagents : H2O2 (stable peroxide), LuminolEnhancer (SuperSignal ELISA Femto)
  Volume   : 900 nL each (1 unit each)
  Operation: Dispense H2O2 and luminol-enhancer as separate 1-unit droplets; merge (5 s mix);
             deliver substrate mix to particle pellet; mix 2 min. HRP catalyses luminol
             oxidation in H2O2; photons collected by motorised PMT over 10 s.
             Signal reported as RLU; normalised to cutoff calibrator (s/co).
  Substrate mix duration : 5 s
  Reaction duration      : 2 min (120 s)
  Detection duration     : 10 s
  Detection module       : rvIgGDetection
  Interpretation:
    s/co >= 1.0    -> positive  (immune to rubella)
    s/co 0.69-0.99 -> equivocal (gray zone)
    s/co < 0.69    -> negative  (susceptible to rubella)

Step 7 | Diagnostic gate (BB2 / BB3)
  Branch pass (BB2): igGSignal > 0 -> dispose(igGReaction); patient IgG-positive.
  Branch fail (BB3): igGSignal <= 0 -> drain(igGReaction); patient IgG-negative/equivocal.
  Join (BB4): dispose(igGSignal); Protocol 2 begins.


PROTOCOL 2 | RV IgM IMMUNOASSAY + ON-CHIP IgG BLOCKING (7 steps + pretreatment, ~35 min)

Step 8 | On-chip sample pretreatment (IgG blocking)
  Reagents : SampleIgM (pre-diluted 10x in DPBS/4% BSA off-chip),
             PretreatmentReagent (goat antihuman IgG, 3x dilution in conjugate diluent)
  Volumes  : 1.8 uL sample (2 units) + 900 nL pretreatment (1 unit)
  Operation: Dispense both; merge on-chip; mix 7 min.
  Duration : 7 min (420 s; IR: 420.0 SECOND)
  Purpose  : Exogenous antihuman IgG blocks two interference sources in the IgM assay:
               (i)  Endogenous RV IgG competing for particle binding sites
                    -> prevents false negatives (Fig. 3A panel 2).
               (ii) RV IgG / RF IgM immunocomplexes binding non-specifically to particles
                    -> prevents false positives from rheumatoid factor (Fig. 3A panel 3).
  Evidence : Pretreatment significantly suppresses nonspecific signal in IgG+IgM samples
             (P=0.0003); pure IgG (P=0.5) and pure IgM (P=0.6) samples unaffected (Fig. 3B).
             RF IgM in RV IgG calibrators: 1.4-1.85 IU/mL (low).
             RF IgM in RV IgM Cal 1: 27.0 IU/mL (high; pretreatment critical for cutoff use).

Step 9 | Particle dispense and magnetic separation
  Reagents : VirusCoatedParticles
  Volume   : 1.8 uL (2 units); identical stock as Protocol 1.
  Operation: Identical to Protocol 1 Step 1.
  Duration : ~5 s

Step 10 | Pretreated sample incubation
  Reagents : igMPretreatedSample (from Step 8), igMParticles (from Step 9)
  Volume   : (combined pretreated sample droplet delivered to particle pellet)
  Operation: Mix 3 min. RV IgM from pretreated sample binds RV-coated particles;
             IgG/RF IgM competition blocked. Magnetic lens re-activated; supernatant disposed.
  Duration : 3 min (180 s)

Step 11 | First wash cycle x4
  Reagents : WashBuffer
  Volume   : 4 x 1.8 uL (4 x 2 units)
  Operation: Identical to Protocol 1 Step 3. Removes unbound antibody and pretreatment components.
  Duration : 30 s per wash (4 x 30 s)

Step 12 | HRP conjugate incubation
  Reagents : HRPConjugateIgM (23 ng/mL HRP-conjugated goat antihuman IgM)
  Volume   : 1.8 uL (2 units)
  Operation: Dispense; deliver to particle pellet; mix 2 min.
             IgM-specific conjugate; distinguishes this step from Protocol 1 Step 4.
             HRP-anti-IgM binds selectively to RV IgM on particles.
             Magnetic lens re-activated; unbound conjugate disposed.
  Duration : 2 min (120 s)

Step 13 | Second wash cycle x4
  Reagents : WashBuffer
  Volume   : 4 x 1.8 uL (4 x 2 units)
  Operation: Identical to Step 11. Removes excess HRP-anti-IgM before substrate.
  Duration : 30 s per wash (4 x 30 s)

Step 14 | Substrate addition, reaction, and chemiluminescent detection
  Reagents : H2O2 (stable peroxide), LuminolEnhancer (SuperSignal ELISA Femto)
  Volume   : 900 nL each (1 unit each)
  Operation: Identical to Protocol 1 Step 6.
             Cutoff defined by Cal 1 (s/co = 1); s/co = mean signal / mean Cal 1 signal.
  Substrate mix duration : 5 s
  Reaction duration      : 2 min (120 s)
  Detection duration     : 10 s
  Detection module       : rvIgMDetection
  Interpretation:
    s/co >= 1.0    -> positive  (active rubella infection; CRS risk if pregnant)
    s/co 0.75-0.99 -> equivocal (gray zone; retest required per WHO guidance)
    s/co < 0.75    -> negative  (no evidence of acute rubella infection)

Step 15 | Diagnostic gate (BB5 / BB6)
  Branch pass (BB5): igMSignal > 0 -> dispose(igMReaction); active rubella infection confirmed.
  Branch fail (BB6): igMSignal <= 0 -> drain(igMReaction); IgM-negative/equivocal.
  Terminal (BB7): dispose(igMSignal); NOP. Experiment ends.

EQUIVOCAL NOTE: Equivocal results (s/co in gray zone) are interpreted as positive for
sensitivity/specificity calculations per WHO guidance. In clinical practice, all positive or
equivocal patients are retested to confirm final diagnosis. 2 of 25 panel samples produced
positive-equivocal discrepancies between DMF and Abbott EIA at s/co values near the cutoff;
attributed to assay variability (CV ~15%) and 20-year sample age. Final diagnostic accuracy
remained 100% sensitivity and 100% specificity for both assays.


ON-CHIP DILUTION (SUPPLEMENTARY SUB-PROTOCOL)
----------------------------------------------
For patient samples exceeding the linear range (>250 IU/mL) or for whole blood compatibility:

Method   : Serial 2x on-chip dilution.
  Step 1 : Dispense 1 sample droplet (1.8 uL) + 1 diluent droplet (1.8 uL); merge and mix.
  Step 2 : Split pooled droplet into 2 equal sub-droplets.
  Step 3 : Store one sub-droplet; repeat Steps 1-3 with the other.
  4 iterations yield: 2x, 4x, 8x, 16x diluted droplets (125, 62.5, 31.3, 15.6 IU/mL
  from a 250 IU/mL sample).

Blood matrix: Interassay CVs marginally higher for blood samples (10-15%) vs standard (6-9%).
  At 1/4 and 1/8 blood dilutions: signal significantly different from calibrator (P < 0.05).
  At 1/16 and 1/32: indistinguishable from calibrator (P > 0.05).
  Conclusion: 1/16 or 1/32 blood dilution removes matrix interference without reducing
              analyte below LOQ.

BioScript note: Not encoded in this .bs file. BioScript has no loop/repeat construct;
  serial dilution steps would need to be hardcoded per iteration in a separate .bs file.
