OVERVIEW
--------
This BioScript file encodes two protocols from the PhD thesis of Alphonsus H.C. Ng (University
of Toronto, 2015), which describes the development and validation of digital microfluidic (DMF)
methods for heterogeneous immunoassays. DMF electrostatically manipulates discrete droplets
(pL to uL) on a hydrophobic electrode array without the use of enclosed microchannels or
oil carrier fluid -- a key innovation of this work.

Protocol 1 (Chapter 2) introduces the first oil-free DMF particle-based immunoassay, using a
novel "supernatant separation" washing technique and demonstrating both non-competitive
(thyroid-stimulating hormone, TSH) and competitive (17beta-estradiol, E2) immunoassay modes.
Chapter 3 extends this with a fully automated platform (90 Pogo pin interface, integrated PMT,
magnetic lens) and DOE optimization. Chapter 4 applies the platform to infectious disease
diagnosis (rubella virus IgG and IgM), demonstrating 100% diagnostic sensitivity and specificity.

Protocol 2 (Chapter 5) introduces DISC (Digital Microfluidic Immunocytochemistry in Single-
Cells), which automates all sample preparation steps for in-situ adherent cell immunoassays with
<4 s temporal resolution. Using virtual microwells formed by passive dispensing over hydrophilic
lift-off spots, DISC enables single-cell phosphoprotein quantitation by laser scanning cytometry.

The BioScript encodes representative arms of both protocols and two QC gates per protocol,
yielding 13 basic blocks (BB1-BB13) and 4 if/else gates in the compiled IR/CFG.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration : Two-plate EWOD; patterned chromium bottom-plate + unpatterned ITO top-plate
Spacer        : Two pieces of Scotch double-sided tape (3M); total 180 um gap
Bottom plate  : Chromium electrodes (200 nm) on glass (2 in x 3 in x 1.1 mm, Telic Co.)
                Dielectric: ~7 um Parylene C (vapor deposition)
                Hydrophobic coating: ~200 nm Teflon-AF (1% w/w in FC-40, 2000 rpm, 60 s; post-baked 165 C, 10 min)
                Fabrication: photolithography + CR-4 wet etching at ECTI/TNFC cleanroom, U of T
Top plate     : Unpatterned ITO-coated glass; ~200 nm Teflon-AF
                Chapter 5 modification: hydrophilic lift-off spots (1.75 mm diameter, 4.4 mm pitch)
                by S1811 photoresist fluorocarbon lift-off on ITO (UV expose, develop, Teflon-AF
                spin-coat, acetone liftoff ~5-10 s, exposing ITO hydrophilic pads)

Chapter 2 device:
  - 116 actuation electrodes (2.25 x 2.25 mm ea.); 10 reservoir electrodes (4.5 x 4.5 mm ea.)
  - Interdigitated electrode borders (140 um peak-to-peak sinusoids); inter-electrode gaps 30-80 um
  - Electrode pitch 2.25 mm aligns to 96/384/1536 well-plate format
  - Unit droplet: ~800 nL; reservoir droplet: ~3.5 uL; 4 assay regions + 1 detection region

Chapter 3/4 device:
  - 80 actuation electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm Ch3 / 2.54 x 2.54 mm Ch4)
  - 8 sample + 4 waste reservoirs (Ch3); unit droplet ~800 nL (Ch3) / ~900 nL (Ch4)

Chapter 5 (DISC) device:
  - 80 actuation electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm); unit droplet ~900 nL
  - Top plate: 8 lift-off spots (1.75 mm diameter, 4.4 mm pitch); virtual microwell ~400 nL

Actuation:
  - Chapter 2: ~150 Vrms, 10 kHz (function generator + TREK amplifier)
  - Chapters 3-5: 80-100 Vrms, 10 kHz sine wave (DropBot + Arduino HV switching; 90 Pogo pin interface)
  - Droplet mixing: circular shuttling across 4 electrodes (unit droplets) or more (larger volumes)

Magnet system:
  - Chapter 2: NdFeB disc (5/8 in dia x 1/4 in, N48, 15.3 lb pull; mu_r=1.05, Br=1.38 T)
    Positioned 1.40 mm below device; manual movement via stripwell manifold + metal clip
    Minimum density for separation: ~3.0e8 particles/mL; estimated force ~470 uN
  - Chapter 3/4: Motorized magnetic lens; >600 uN; up to 8 simultaneous separations;
    deactivated by positioning 3.8 cm below device for resuspension

Detection:
  - Chapter 2: PHERAstar well-plate reader (BMG Labtech); LUM module; gain 3600; focal height 8.6 mm
  - Chapter 3/4: Integrated motorized PMT (CT2 counter timer); LOD 4.88 uU/mL HRP; CV 0.56-9.3%
  - Chapter 5: Axon GenePix 4000B microarray scanner; 532 nm + 635 nm; 5 um resolution;
    PMT 500 V; CellProfiler (Otsu Global, 15-100 um objects, phospho/total protein ratio per cell)


PROTOCOL 1: DMF MAGNETIC PARTICLE IMMUNOASSAY (Chapter 2)
----------------------------------------------------------
Analytes       : Thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH, non-competitive sandwich immunoassay)
                 17beta-estradiol (E2, competitive immunoassay)
Anti-fouling   : Pluronic L64 (BASF Corp.), 0.05% v/v in all on-chip reagents
                 Enables air-surrounded droplet movement; prevents protein adsorption

Reagents:
  TSH particles  : Mouse monoclonal Anti-beta TSH coated paramagnetic particles
                   (Abbott ARCHITECT kit 7K62); pre-processed: washed 2x DI water,
                   blocked with 3% NFDM/0.01% Tween-20 buffer pH 7.8 (1 h), washed 2x DI water,
                   resuspended at ~3.0e8 particles/mL in DMF-compatible particle diluent (pH 10:
                   Tris-base 6.1 g/L, NaCl 5.8 g/L, Sucrose 136 g/L, BSA 1% w/v,
                   Thimerosal 0.05% w/v, L64 0.05% v/v)
  E2 particles   : Rabbit monoclonal Anti-E2 coated particles (Abbott ARCHITECT kit 7K72);
                   same prep as TSH but no NFDM blocking step
  TSH conjugate  : Anti-TSH-HRP (goat polyclonal, Fitzgerald Industries), 2 ug/mL in conjugate
                   diluent (pH 7.4: Tris-Base 1.9 g/L, Tris-HCl 13.2 g/L, NaCl 17.5 g/L,
                   BSA 1% w/v, cold fish gelatin 0.1% w/v, Thimerosal 0.05% w/v, L64 0.05% v/v)
  E2 conjugate   : E2-HRP conjugated via 6-CMO (BiosPacific), 1 ug/mL in conjugate diluent
                   (optimal conc. determined empirically; higher conc. loses dynamic range)
  Substrate      : SuperSignal ELISA Femto (Thermo Fisher Scientific): H2O2 + luminol/enhancer
  Wash buffer    : pH 7.7; Tris-base 0.35 g/L, Tris-HCl 1.10 g/L, NaCl 8.367 g/L, L64 0.05% v/v

Supernatant Separation Washing (key innovation vs prior serial dilution method):
  Mechanism      : Magnet positions pellet on device surface; supernatant droplet actuated to waste;
                   magnet removed; fresh wash buffer droplet passed over pellet; circularly mixed
                   (4 electrodes) to resuspend particles
  Efficiency     : >98% unbound reagent removal by wash 2 (benchmark)
                   Serial dilution requires 6+ washes for same benchmark; 10-18 washes previously
  Minimum density: ~3.0e8 particles/mL for successful separation
  Magnetic force : ~470 uN (COMSOL FEA; pellet 7.75 mm from magnet bottom-edge origin)
  Residual liquid: ~5% of origin droplet retained by hydrophilic particles; resuspend immediately

Eight-step On-Chip Immunoassay Protocol:
  Step 1 | Particle dispense and separation
    Volume     : 800 nL unit droplet from reservoir
    Operation  : Supernatant separation (magnet on; diluent actuated to waste; magnet off)
  
  Step 2 | Sample incubation
    Volume     : 3 unit droplets (3 x 800 nL = 2.4 uL)
    Duration   : 6 min at room temperature with continuous circular mixing
    Notes      : 3 droplets chosen for optimal signal vs droplet reliability; evaporation <5%
                 over 30 min (measured by capacitance); TSH: analyte binds Anti-beta TSH particles;
                 E2: analyte competes with E2-HRP for Anti-E2 binding sites
  
  Step 3 | Wash x4 in wash buffer
    Volume     : 4 x 800 nL; supernatant separation each time
  
  Step 4 | Conjugate incubation
    Volume     : 800 nL
    Duration   : 2 min
    TSH        : Anti-TSH-HRP binds TSH captured on particles (non-competitive)
    E2         : E2-HRP binds Anti-E2 sites not occupied by analyte (competitive)
  
  Step 5 | Wash x4 in wash buffer + wash x2 in H2O2
    Notes      : 2x H2O2 wash developed in place of pre-mixing H2O2 + luminol off-chip;
                 pre-mixing caused high inter-assay variability
  
  Step 6 | H2O2 incubation
    Volume     : 800 nL; duration: 2 min
    Notes      : Particles moved to detection region (electrode array aligned to 96-well plate)
  
  Step 7 | Luminol/enhancer merge
    Volume     : 800 nL; on-chip mix (immediately before detection)
    Notes      : Activates HRP-catalyzed chemiluminescence
  
  Step 8 | Chemiluminescence detection
    Duration   : 1 min (signal saturates at 1 min; stable endpoint)
    Settings   : PHERAstar LUM module; focal height 8.6 mm; gain 3600;
                 device taped to 96-well Stripwell plate

Performance vs well-plate ELISA:
  TSH LOD (absolute)   : 2.0 nIU (DMF) vs 3.0 nIU (well-plate)
  TSH LOD (conc.)      : 0.83 uIU/mL (DMF) vs 0.06 uIU/mL (well-plate)
  E2 LOD (absolute)    : 50 fg (DMF) vs 152 fg (well-plate)
  E2 LOD (conc.)       : 21 pg/mL (DMF) vs 6.1 pg/mL (well-plate)
  TSH CV               : 9-21% (DMF) vs 2-13% (well-plate)
  E2 CV                : 5-14% (DMF) vs 4-12% (well-plate)
  Sample volume        : 2.4 uL (DMF) vs 50 uL (well-plate)
  Incubation time      : 6 min (DMF) vs 60 min (well-plate)
  Reagent consumption  : ~100-fold reduction (11.2 uL DMF vs 1.2 mL well-plate total)
  Clinical utility     : TSH detects hypothyroidism (>3.0 uIU/mL); E2 screens ovarian cancer (>20 pg/mL)

Optimized Platform (Chapter 3):
  Hardware     : 90 Pogo pin interface + DropBot automation; integrated motorized PMT;
                 magnetic lens (up to 8 simultaneous separations; precise motor positioning)
  DOE analysis : 3-level full factorial; factors: analyte conc., sample incubation time, volume
                 Optimal: 5 sample droplets (4.0 uL), 3 min incubation
  TSH LOD      : 0.15 uIU/mL (screens hyperthyroidism, TSH < 0.3 uIU/mL)
  TSH CV       : 6-10%; measurement procedure: wash buffer removed post-wash; particles in
                 luminol-H2O2 mixed 4 min; stable signal window ~1 min for sequential reading

Rubella Virus Immunoassay (Chapter 4):
  Analytes     : RV IgG (indirect ELISA, 7 steps) and RV IgM (+ on-chip IgG blocking pretreatment)
  Particles    : Rubella virus-coated paramagnetic particles (Abbott ARCHITECT); 1.5e8/mL in Superblock
  IgG protocol : 7 steps; 4 parallel assays; ~25 min per run
                 Step i: particles dispense; Step ii: sample 1.8 uL, mix 3 min; Step iii: 4x wash;
                 Step iv: HRP-anti-human IgG conjugate (21 ng/mL), mix 2 min; Step v: 4x wash;
                 Step vi: H2O2 (900 nL) + luminol-enhancer (900 nL), mix 2 min; Step vii: PMT 10 s
  IgG LOD/LOQ  : 0.03 IU/mL / 0.1 IU/mL (clinical cutoff: 10 IU/mL)
  IgM protocol : Same as IgG but + pretreatment (sample 1.8 uL + anti-human IgG reagent 900 nL, mix 7 min)
                 and HRP-anti-human IgM conjugate; ~35 min per run
  On-chip dil. : 4 iterations merge-mix-split; generates 2x/4x/8x/16x dilutions; compatible with whole blood
  Panel results: 25 serum/plasma samples (SeraCare PTR201); IgG: 100% sensitivity, 100% specificity
                 IgM: 100% sensitivity / 100% specificity (equivocal omitted); two equivocal mismatches
                 near cutoff (s/co = 0.9 and 1.0); attributed to assay variability + 20-year-old specimens


PROTOCOL 2: DISC (Chapter 5)
-----------------------------
Full name      : DISC -- Digital Microfluidic Immunocytochemistry in Single-Cells
Cell line      : NIH 3T3 (murine fibroblast, primary); MCF-7 and MDA-MB-231 for cancer time series
Signaling      : PDGF-BB -> PDGFR auto-phosphorylation (Y857, Y1021) -> PI3K -> PIP3 -> Akt -> p-Akt S473
Seeding density: 500,000 cells/mL -> ~150-300 cells per virtual microwell
Anti-fouling   : 0.05% Pluronic F68 in all culture media, fixing, and wash solutions

Key capabilities:
  Temporal resolution : ~4 s between stimulation and fixation (passive dispense switch)
  Reagent exchange    : 1 exchange removes ~93% of prior solution; 2 exchanges >99%
  Cell reduction      : 150-300 cells/VM vs 15,000 cells/well (flow cytometry): 60-150x fewer cells
  Volume reduction    : 400 nL per VM vs ~200 uL per well: ~500x less volume
  SBR                 : GAPDH signal >=5x background (optimization data, Fig 5.2D)

Passive dispensing mechanism:
  Source droplet translated across vacant lift-off spot (1.75 mm hydrophilic ITO on Teflon-AF top plate);
  hydrophobic-hydrophilic contrast captures a ~400 nL virtual microwell sub-droplet spontaneously.
  Subsequent dispenses displace and replace microwell contents.

Eleven-step DISC protocol (representative arm: Akt phosphorylation at 50 ng/mL PDGF-BB):

  Step 1 | Cell seeding
    Volume   : 3 x 900 nL passive dispenses (2.7 uL) per lift-off spot; VM = ~400 nL
    Incubate : 37 C, 5% CO2, 24 h in humidified chamber; device inverted for cell settling

  Step 2 | Serum starvation
    Reagent  : Starving media (0.5% CS/FBS + 0.05% F68 + pen/strep in DMEM)
    Volume   : 3 x 900 nL; Incubate 37 C, 5% CO2, 24 h
    Purpose  : Synchronizes basal receptor/Akt activity

  Step 3 | Serum-free rinse
    Reagent  : Serum-free DMEM + 0.05% F68; Volume: 3 x 900 nL

  Step 4 | PDGF-BB stimulation
    Reagent  : PDGF-BB 50 ng/mL in serum-free DMEM; Volume: 2 x 900 nL (1.8 uL)
    Duration : 2 min (PDGFR) / 5 min (Akt); ~4 s reagent switch time to fixation
    Dose-response range: 0-200 ng/mL; EC50 ~12.5 ng/mL; Hill n=1.73 (Y857), 2.13 (Y1021)
    Notes    : In time series, Step 4 staggered so Step 5 performed simultaneously for all VMs
               to prevent cross-exposure to paraformaldehyde fumes

  Step 5 | Cell fixation
    Reagent  : 4% paraformaldehyde + 0.05% F68 in DPBS; Volume: 3 x 900 nL (2.7 uL)
    Incubate : 10 min, room temperature

  Step 6 | WB1 rinse
    Reagent  : WB1 (0.05% F68 in DPBS); Volume: 3 x 900 nL (5.4 uL total)

  Step 7 | Permeabilization
    Reagent  : 0.2% Tween-20 in DPBS; Volume: 3 x 900 nL (2.7 uL)
    Incubate : 10 min, room temperature

  Step 8 | WB2 rinse
    Reagent  : WB2 (0.05% Brij 35 in DPBS); Volume: 4 x 900 nL (~8.1 uL)

  Step 9 | Blocking
    Reagent  : 1% bovine NFDM + 0.05% Brij 35 in DPBS; Volume: 3 x 900 nL (2.7 uL)
    Incubate : 1 h, room temperature

  Step 10 | Primary antibody staining
    Reagents : pan Akt (Mouse mAb, 1:50, CST) + p-Akt S473 (Rabbit mAb, 1:200, CST) in BS
               OR PDGFR-beta (Mouse mAb, 1:50, CST) + p-PDGFR Y857/Y1021 (Rabbit pAb, 1:200, SCB) in BS
    Volume   : 3 x 900 nL (2.7 uL); Incubate: overnight (~12 h) at 4 C

  Step 11 | WB2 rinse (unbound primary antibody removal)
    Reagent  : WB2 (0.05% Brij 35 in DPBS); Volume: 3 x 900 nL (5.4 uL)

  Step 12 | Secondary antibody staining
    Reagents : Anti-Mouse IgG AF647 Fab2 1:500 + Anti-Rabbit IgG AF555 Fab2 1:500 in BS (CST)
    Volume   : 3 x 900 nL (2.7 uL); Incubate: 2 h, room temperature

  Step 13 | Off-chip detection (laser scanning cytometry)
    Post-process : Remove top plate; rinse in 10x DPBS/0.5% Tween-20 (30 s); rinse DI water (30 s); air dry
    Scanner      : Axon GenePix 4000B; 532 nm + 635 nm; 5 um resolution; 0 um focus; PMT 500 V
    Analysis     : CellProfiler; Otsu Global thresholding; 15-100 um objects; per-cell p-protein/total ratio
    Normalization: Per-cell ratio normalized to serum-free negative control mean on same device

Key results (DISC):
  PDGFR Y857 max phosphorylation  : 75 s after 50 ng/mL PDGF-BB
  PDGFR Y1021 max phosphorylation : 60 s after 50 ng/mL PDGF-BB
  Akt activation at 3.125 ng/mL   : ~71% despite only ~22% receptor activation (amplification)
  Ligand removal (1 exchange)      : ~93% ligand removed; receptor dephosphorylation confirmed
  Cancer cells                     : MCF-7 and MDA-MB-231 differ significantly in Akt hyper-responder
                                     fraction; may correlate with invasion phenotype
