PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Computer Vision Reveals Hidden Variables Underlying NF-kB Activation in Single Cells
Source Link  : https://www.science.org/doi/epdf/10.1126/sciadv.abg4135
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abg4135
Journal      : Science Advances, 2021, Vol. 7, Article eabg4135
Platform     : High-throughput microfluidic live-cell imaging (PDMS chip) + image-based SVM machine learning


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidic live-cell imaging approach to uncover hidden molecular
variables controlling heterogeneous NF-kB (nuclear factor kappa B) activation in single
mammalian cells. The core experiment uses automated PDMS microfluidic chambers to culture
and stimulate NIH 3T3 mouse fibroblasts expressing dual fluorescent reporters (p65-dsRed
for NF-kB dynamics, H2B-GFP as a nuclear marker), combined with a custom image-based support
vector machine (SVM) pipeline that predicts which individual cells will activate NF-kB from
their prestimulation images alone.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. Microfluidic device preparation: fibronectin coating and cell seeding
  2. Live-cell imaging: 1.5 h unperturbed prestimulation imaging followed by TNF-alpha
     stimulation (0.1 ng/mL) and 6 h post-stimulation tracking of p65 nuclear translocation
  3. Image-based machine learning: SVM classifier trained on 236 hand-crafted features
     per cell; predicts activation with 79.4% accuracy from prestimulation images alone
  4. Fixed-cell immunofluorescence: validates the IkB-alpha/NF-kB ratio as the primary
     molecular determinant of heterogeneous single-cell activation probability

Key finding: cell-to-cell variability in NF-kB activation is largely explained by a
preexisting difference in the IkB/NF-kB ratio. Cells with a low IkB/NF-kB ratio show
minute "leaky" nuclear p65 localization at rest and are significantly more sensitive to
TNF stimulation. Activated cells show on average a 5-fold higher leaky nuclear p65 level
before stimulation than non-responding cells (P = 1.35e-13). Resting cells dynamically
transition between molecular states but maintain their overall activation probability
(autocorrelation r = 0.78 at 35 min before stimulation, n = 1109 cells).


DEVICE AND IMAGING SETUP
-------------------------
Device material  : PDMS microfluidic chambers
Surface coating  : Fibronectin (FC010-10MG), overnight at room temperature
Cell density     : ~20,000 cells/cm2; cells taken at 100% confluence via 0.25% Trypsin-EDTA
                   (25200-056) for 5 min and cultured 5 h before stimulation
Culture          : 37 C, 5% CO2; standard cell culture conditions maintained during imaging
                   using an on-stage incubation chamber
Microscope       : Nikon Eclipse Ti2
Camera           : Hamamatsu ORCA-Flash4.0 V3 Digital CMOS (C13440)
Magnification    : 20x
Imaging interval : 5 min per field of view
Stimulus delivery: TNF-alpha vials pressurized at 5 psi; connected to chip via PEEK microbore
                   tubing (VICI)
Image analysis   : Custom MATLAB scripts; NF-kB quantified as mean nuclear fluorescence
                   intensity after background correction; peak analysis via MATLAB smooth +
                   z-score normalization + peak detection


CELL LINE
---------
Parental         : p65(-/-) knockout NIH 3T3 mouse fibroblasts
Reporters        :
  p65-dsRed      : NF-kB transcription factor p65 fused to dsRed, expressed under native
                   p65 promotor; minimum fluorescence clone selected to represent endogenous
                   NF-kB pathway dynamics
  H2B-GFP        : Histone 2B fused to GFP, expressed from ubiquitin promotor; serves as
                   nuclear marker for segmentation and cell tracking

In activated cells, cytoplasmic p65-dsRed rapidly translocates to the nucleus in a digital
(switch-like) fashion within ~40 min of TNF stimulation. Non-responding cells show no
translocation and no NF-kB target gene expression.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Fibronectin Chamber Coating
  Reagent  : Fibronectin (FC010-10MG)
  Duration : Overnight (~720 min) at room temperature
  Purpose  : Promotes cell adhesion to PDMS chamber surface prior to loading

Step 2 | PBS Wash of Coated Chamber
  Reagent  : PBS
  Duration : 1 min
  Purpose  : Removes unbound fibronectin before cell seeding

Step 3 | Cell Seeding and Pre-stimulation Culture
  Cell line: NIH 3T3 p65-dsRed / H2B-GFP
  Density  : ~20,000 cells/cm2
  Process  : Cells harvested at 100% confluence with 0.25% Trypsin-EDTA (25200-056) for
             5 min, loaded into coated chamber
  Culture  : 5 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 (= 300 min) before stimulation; allows cell adhesion and
             equilibration to endogenous reporter expression levels

Step 4 | Prestimulation Live-Cell Imaging (t = -90 min to t = 0)
  Duration  : 1.5 h = 90 min; image every 5 min (18 time points)
  Channels  : p65-dsRed (red), H2B-GFP (green), phase contrast
  Output    : 236 hand-crafted features per cell per time point, including:
                - Mean nuclear p65-dsRed fluorescence (top predictor, r = 0.62)
                - SD of nuclear p65-dsRed
                - Nuclear phase entropy
                - Nuclear pixel correlation (information measure)
                - Otsu dimension
                - SFTA (Segmentation-based Fractal Texture Analysis)
                - Major axis length
                - H2B-GFP channel morphology features
  Purpose   : Provides the unperturbed cell image input for the SVM activation classifier.
              Cells are not chemically stimulated during this window.
  Note      : mix step in BioScript abstracts the repeated imaging acquisition loop;
              no reagent mixing occurs at this step.

Step 5 | TNF-alpha Stimulation (t = 0)
  Reagent          : TNF-alpha (PMC3014_3671982503)
  Diluent          : FluoroBrite DMEM (A1896701) + 2x GlutaMAX (35050061) +
                     penicillin/streptomycin (15140-122) + FBS (16140071)
  Concentration    : 0.1 ng/mL (main dataset, n = 2113 cells)
                     Multi-dose: 0.005, 0.05, 0.5, 5 ng/mL (n = 3456 cells combined)
  Delivery         : Pressurized vial at 5 psi; PEEK microbore tubing (VICI); chip inlet
  Time zero        : Stimulation marks t = 0 for all single-cell NF-kB trace analyses

Step 6 | Post-Stimulation Live-Cell Imaging (t = 0 to t = +360 min)
  Duration      : 6 h = 360 min at 37 C, 5% CO2; image every 5 min (72 time points)
  Readout       : Nuclear NF-kB (p65-dsRed) tracked per cell over 6 h
  Activation    : Responding cells show complete cytoplasm-to-nucleus translocation
                  (digital / switch-like behavior); non-responding cells show no change
  Labeling      : Binary threshold on (nuclear p65_peak - nuclear p65_(t=0)) > 500
                  (MATLAB: smooth + z-score normalization, peak detection; Fig. S4A)
  Datasets      : Main: 0.1 ng/mL TNF, n = 2113 cells (independent validation set)
                  Multi-dose: 0.005-5 ng/mL, n = 3456 cells (SVM training set)

Step 7 | NF-kB Activation Detection (QC Gate)
  Module   : nfkbActivationDetection
  Duration : 30 s (image analysis classification)
  Gate     :
    Pass (activationSignal > 0)  -> activated cell; proceed to fixed-cell IF (Step 8-13)
    Fail (activationSignal <= 0) -> non-responding cell; drain

Step 8 | PBS Rinse (pass branch only)
  Reagent  : PBS
  Duration : 1 min
  Purpose  : Removes culture medium before fixation; matches Materials and Methods protocol

Step 9 | Paraformaldehyde Fixation (pass branch only)
  Reagent     : 4% PFA in PBS (ParaformaldehydeFixative)
  Duration    : 10 min at room temperature (25 C)
  Imaging note: Cell image taken at t = -5 min (immediately preceding fixation) used to
                align fixed-cell IF data with live imaging UMAP space (Fig. 5A-B)

Step 10 | Blocking and Permeabilization (pass branch only)
  Buffer   : 10% BSA + 0.5% Triton X-100 in PBS (BlockingPermeabilizationBuffer)
  Duration : 1 h (60 min) at room temperature
  Purpose  : Blocks non-specific antibody binding; permeabilizes cell membranes for
             intracellular IkB-alpha antibody access

Step 11 | Primary Antibody - Anti-IkB-alpha (pass branch only)
  Antibody : Rabbit polyclonal anti-IkB-alpha (ab7217)
  Buffer   : 2% BSA + 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS
  Duration : 1 h (60 min) at 37 C
  Target   : IkB-alpha, the main inhibitor of NF-kB; binds p65 and retains it in cytoplasm
             in unstimulated cells; the IkB/NF-kB ratio predetermines activation probability
             (simulation: rho = -0.256, P = 2.7e-11; Fig. 5C)

Step 12 | PBS Wash After Primary Antibody (pass branch only)
  Reagent  : PBS
  Duration : 1 min
  Purpose  : Removes unbound primary antibody before secondary incubation

Step 13 | Secondary Antibody - Alexa Fluor 647 (pass branch only)
  Antibody : Goat polyclonal anti-rabbit IgG H&L Alexa Fluor 647 (G378361)
  Buffer   : 2% BSA + 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS
  Duration : 1 h (60 min) at 37 C
  Read     : AF647 signal (ex ~650 nm / em ~668 nm) quantifies IkB-alpha in nuclear and
             cytoplasmic compartments for IkB/NF-kB ratio analysis
  Validation: Activated (high-scoring) fixed cells show significantly lower IkB/NF-kB ratio,
              higher nuclear NF-kB, and larger cellular area than inactive cells (Fig. 5D)

Step 14 | Immunofluorescence Staining QC Detection
  Module   : immunofluorescenceDetection
  Duration : 30 s
  Readout  : IkB/NF-kB ratio per cell (nuclear + cytoplasmic segmentation of AF647 + p65-dsRed)
  Gate     :
    Pass (ifStainingSignal > 0)  -> IkB/NF-kB ratio quantified; dispose stained sample
    Fail (ifStainingSignal <= 0) -> staining failure; dispose


MACHINE LEARNING PIPELINE
--------------------------
Model            : Support Vector Machine (SVM), cubic kernel
Features         : 236 hand-crafted features per cell (nuclear segmentation, texture,
                   morphology); extracted with custom MATLAB scripts (table S2)
Training set     : Multi-dose dataset: [0.005, 0.05, 0.5, 5] ng/mL TNF, n = 3456 cells
                   10-fold cross-validation, 10% dropout
Validation set   : Independent single-dose dataset: 0.1 ng/mL TNF, n = 2113 cells
Accuracy         : 73% (cross-validation, cubic kernel); 79.4% (independent validation set)
Top predictor    : Mean nuclear p65-dsRed fluorescence prestimulation (r = 0.62, P < 1e-100)
Other predictors : SD nuclear p65, nuclear phase entropy, pixel correlation, information
                   measure, Otsu dimension, SFTA, major axis length
Alternate models : CNN and decision tree outperform SVM in accuracy but lack interpretable
                   feature attribution; SVM chosen for biological insight
Code             : https://github.com/parthivapatel/ML_cellim.git
Dataset DOI      : 10.5281/zenodo.5361956


KEY FINDINGS
------------
1. Prestimulation p65 nuclear leakiness is the primary predictor of single-cell NF-kB
   activation (r = 0.62 with activation probability; Fig. 2C).
2. The IkB/NF-kB ratio is the molecular driver of p65 leakiness and activation probability;
   significant inverse correlation (rho = -0.256, P = 2.7e-11) confirmed by IF (Fig. 5C).
3. Activated cells show on average 5-fold higher leaky nuclear p65 before stimulation than
   non-responding cells (P = 1.35e-13; Fig. 4E). Leakiness represents ~12% of total fluorescence.
4. Unstimulated cells dynamically transition between molecular states but maintain stable
   activation probability (autocorrelation r = 0.78 at 35 min before stimulation, n = 1109).
5. 73.71% of state transitions remain within or adjacent to the same activation-probability
   cluster; TNF sensitivity is stable across the observed prestimulation time window.
6. Counterintuitively, cells with high initial IkB require a smaller TNF dose to achieve
   NF-kB activation, because probability depends on the IkB/NF-kB ratio, not absolute IkB.
