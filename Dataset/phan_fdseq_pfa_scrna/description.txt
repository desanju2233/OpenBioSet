PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Droplet-based single-cell RNA sequencing of paraformaldehyde-fixed cells (FD-seq)
Source Link  : https://www.protocols.io/view/droplet-based-single-cell-rna-sequencing-of-parafo-n2bvj94k5lk5/v1
Paper DOI    : https://dx.doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.pex-1604/v1
Journal      : Research Square (preprint), posted October 1, 2021
Authors      : Hoang Van Phan, Michiel van Gent, Nir Drayman, Anindita Basu,
               Michaela U. Gack, Savas Tay
Affiliation  : The University of Chicago; Cleveland Clinic
Platform     : Drop-seq microfluidic chip (Macosko et al., Cell 2015)
               Droplet Generation Microfluidic Chip (commercial or custom PDMS)


OVERVIEW
--------
FD-seq (Fixed Droplet RNA Sequencing) is a method for droplet-based single-cell RNA
sequencing (scRNA-seq) of paraformaldehyde (PFA)-fixed, stained, and sorted cells.
Standard high-throughput scRNA-seq methods such as Drop-seq are not compatible with
PFA-fixed cells due to RNA crosslinking. FD-seq resolves this by performing PFA
crosslink reversal inside intact droplets (56 C, 60 min) using heat and Proteinase K
before droplet breaking, thereby releasing intact mRNA for reverse transcription.

FD-seq is designed to work with existing Drop-seq experimental setups and does not
require additional specialized processing equipment. It enables scRNA-seq of cells that
require fixation prior to sequencing, e.g., for biosafety, intracellular staining,
or compatibility with FACS sorting workflows.

The protocol is divided into ten sequential phases:
  1.  Cell fixation (4% PFA, 15 min, RT)
  2.  Cell permeabilization (0.1% Triton X-100, 15 min, RT)
  3.  Pre-droplet preparation (0.01% BSA washes; lysis buffer + bead preparation)
  4.  Droplet generation (Drop-seq chip; co-encapsulation of cells and barcoded beads)
  5.  In-droplet crosslink reversal (56 C, 60 min; Proteinase K + heat)
  6.  Droplet breaking (Perfluorooctan-1-ol + 6X SSC)
  7.  Reverse transcription (Maxima H- RT; 25 C 30 min + 42 C 90 min)
  8.  Exonuclease I digestion (37 C, 45 min; removes unextended primers)
  9.  Whole transcriptome amplification (WTA PCR; Kapa HiFi; 14-cycle program)
  10. Tagmentation and library preparation (Nextera XT; NextSeq paired-end sequencing)


DEVICE AND PLATFORM
-------------------
Chip         : Drop-seq Droplet Generation Microfluidic Chip
               Commercial chip (Macosko 2015) or in-house PDMS fabrication.
               Hydrophobic treatment with Aquapel (cat 47100) required before use;
               chip rested overnight at RT after treatment.
Syringe pumps: Harvard Apparatus Pump 33 DDS and/or KD Scientific model 100.
Inlets       : Cell syringe (3 ml/h), Bead syringe (3 ml/h), Oil syringe (12 ml/h)
Oil          : Bio-Rad droplet generation oil (cat 1864006)
Droplets     : Nanoliter-scale water-in-oil emulsion; single cell + single bead
               co-encapsulated per droplet (stochastic, Poisson-limited).
Beads        : Drop-seq beads (ChemGenes Corp, cat Macosko-2011-10(V+));
               resuspended in TE-TW at 4 C after manufacturer wash.
               Loaded at 120,000 beads/ml in lysis buffer + 50 mM DTT + 800 U/ml Proteinase K.
Cells        : Loaded at 100,000 cells/ml in 0.01% BSA; filtered through cell strainer.
Collection   : ~20 min per sample into 50 ml centrifuge tube.


REAGENTS
--------
Fixation and permeabilization:
  - 4% PFA in PBS (Santa Cruz Biotechnology, cat sc-281692)
  - Triton X-100, molecular biology grade (0.1% in wash buffer)

Wash buffer (prepare fresh):
  - PBS + 1% BSA (molecular biology grade) + 40 U/ml RNase inhibitor (NEB, cat N0447S)

0.01% BSA: molecular biology grade BSA in PBS; prepare at least 10 ml per sample

Lysis buffer (store at 4 C):
  - 22 ml nuclease-free water + 15 ml 20% Ficoll (Sigma F5415) + 10 ml 1M Tris pH 7.5
    + 2 ml 0.5M EDTA + 1 ml 10% Sarkosyl (Teknova S3377)
  - Add 50 ul 1M DTT + 50 ul 800 U/ml Proteinase K (NEB) per 1 ml before use
  - Filter with 40 um cell strainer before use; store on ice

Additional buffers (store at RT unless noted):
  - TE-SDS : 46.9 ml NFW + 500 ul 1M Tris pH 8.0 + 100 ul 0.5M EDTA + 2.5 ml 10% SDS
  - TE-TW  : 49.4 ml NFW + 500 ul 1M Tris pH 8.0 + 100 ul 0.5M EDTA + 5 ul 100% Tween-20
  - 10 mM Tris pH 8.0 : 49.5 ml NFW + 500 ul 1M Tris pH 8.0
  - 6X SSC : 350 ml NFW + 150 ml 20X SSC

Droplet breaking reagent:
  - Perfluorooctan-1-ol (Synquest, cat 2101-3-20)

RT mix (200 ul total):
  - 80 ul nuclease-free water
  - 40 ul 5X Maxima H- RT buffer
  - 40 ul 20% Ficoll
  - 20 ul 10 mM dNTPs (NEB)
  - 5 ul RNase inhibitor (NEB, cat M0314L)
  - 5 ul 100 uM TSO_RNAhybrid primer
  - 10 ul Maxima H- reverse transcriptase (Thermo Fisher, cat EP0753)

Exo I mix (200 ul total):
  - 170 ul nuclease-free water + 20 ul 10X Exo I buffer + 10 ul Exo I (NEB, M0293S)

WTA PCR (50 ul per well):
  - 24.6 ul water + beads (5,000 beads/well)
  - 0.4 ul 100 uM TSO_PCR primer
  - 25 ul Kapa HiFi Readymix (Roche, cat 7958935001)

Tagmentation kit:
  - Nextera XT DNA Library Prep Kit (Illumina, cat FC-131-1024)
  - P5-TSO primer + Nextera_N7XX index primer (custom; see primer sequences)
  - Ampure XP beads (Beckman Coulter, cat A63880); 0.6X bead ratio

Primers:
  - TSO_RNAhybrid  : AAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGTGAATrGrGrG  (reverse transcription)
  - TSO_PCR        : AAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGT              (WTA amplification)
  - P5-TSO         : AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACACGCCTGTCCGCGGAAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGT*A*C
  - Nextera_N7XX   : CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[i7]GTCTCGTGGGCTCGG
  - Custom Read 1  : GCCTGTCCGCGGAAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGTAC  (NGS)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Phase 1 | Cell Fixation
  Reagents    : PBS, 1% BSA wash buffer, 4% PFA in PBS
  Step 1      : Wash cells once with 1% BSA in PBS (400g, 5 min)
  Step 2      : Fix with 4% PFA in PBS at RT for 15 min
  Step 3      : Spin (400g, 5 min); discard PFA; wash once with wash buffer
  Storage     : Cells can be stored in wash buffer at 4 C overnight before proceeding
  BS variable : washedFixed (post-fixation, post-wash state)

Phase 2 | Cell Permeabilization
  Reagents    : 0.1% Triton X-100 in wash buffer, wash buffer
  Step 1      : Add 500 ul 0.1% Triton X-100 to cell pellet; incubate RT for 15 min
  Step 2      : Add 1 ml wash buffer; spin (400g, 5 min); discard supernatant
  Step 3      : Wash once more with 1 ml wash buffer
  Optional    : Blocking and intracellular staining can follow; reagents diluted in wash buffer
  BS variable : washed4 (post-permeabilization, post-wash state)

Phase 3 | Pre-Droplet Preparation
  Reagents    : 0.01% BSA, lysis buffer + DTT + Proteinase K, Drop-seq beads
  Cell prep   : Wash PFA-fixed cells 3x with 0.01% BSA (2 ml each); resuspend at
                100,000 cells/ml in 0.01% BSA; filter with appropriate cell strainer
  Bead prep   : Count beads with Fuchs-Rosenthal hemocytometer (20 ul beads + 20 ul 20% Ficoll)
                Resuspend beads at 120,000 beads/ml in lysis buffer (with DTT + ProtK); store on ice
  BS variable : cellsReady, beadsReady
  Compiler note: 3x 0.01% BSA washes unrolled explicitly (no loop construct in BioScript)

Phase 4 | Droplet Generation
  Chip        : Drop-seq Droplet Generation Microfluidic Chip (Aquapel-treated, rested overnight)
  Syringe setup:
    Cell syringe (BD 3 ml Luer-Lok): cells in 0.01% BSA; flow rate 3 ml/h
    Bead syringe (BD 3 ml Luer-Lok + magnetic disc VP 772DP-N42-5-2): beads in lysis buffer;
                  flow rate 3 ml/h; magnetic stirrer (VP710D2) to prevent bead settling
    Oil syringe (BD 10 ml Luer-Lok): Bio-Rad oil; flow rate 12 ml/h
  Start order : Cell pump first, then bead pump, then oil pump
  Collection  : ~20 min per sample into 50 ml centrifuge tube
  QC          : Inspect chip junction under microscope; stable generation = blur at junction
  BS variable : emulsion
  Compiler note: Syringe pump flow and chip geometry are not BioScript primitives;
                 cell/bead co-encapsulation modelled as sequential mix operations

Phase 5 | In-Droplet Crosslink Reversal
  Reagents    : Heat block
  Step 1      : Incubate 50 ml tube with droplets at 56 C for 60 min (heat block)
                KEY STEP: Proteinase K + heat reverses PFA crosslinks inside intact droplets
  Step 2      : Remove from heat block; leave at RT for 10 min
  Step 3      : Place on ice for 5 min
  QC check    : Pipette ~15 ul oil + 5 ul droplets onto Fuchs-Rosenthal hemocytometer;
                verify most droplets contain at most 1 bead
  BS variable : emulsion (in-place heat; heat is void in BioScript)
  Duration    : 56 C for 60m; 25 C for 10m; 4 C for 5m

Phase 6 | Droplet Breaking
  Reagents    : 6X SSC, Perfluorooctan-1-ol (in chemical hood)
  Step 1      : Remove as much oil as possible from 50 ml tube without disturbing droplets
  Step 2      : Add 30 ml 6X SSC + 1 ml Perfluorooctan-1-ol (chemical hood)
  Step 3      : Seal tube; shake vertically 4 times; spin at 1000g for 1 min
  Step 4      : Transfer ~30 ml supernatant (above oil interface) to new 50 ml tube
  Step 5      : Add 30 ml 6X SSC to original tube; wait 2-3 s; transfer 30 ml supernatant
                to second new 50 ml tube
  Step 6      : Spin both new tubes at 1000g for 3 min; pool beads into 1.5 ml DNA LoBind tube
  Step 7      : Wash beads: 2x with 1 ml 6X SSC, 1x with 300 ul 5X Maxima H- RT buffer
                (1000g, 1 min each); discard supernatant
  BS variable : beadWash2 (post-break, post-wash bead fraction)

Phase 7 | Reverse Transcription
  Reagents    : RT mix (200 ul; see Reagents section)
  Step 1      : Add 200 ul RT mix to beads; incubate RT for 30 min with rotation
                (primer annealing and RT initiation)
  Step 2      : Transfer to 42 C for 90 min with rotation (cDNA synthesis)
  Step 3      : Spin beads; discard supernatant
  Washes      : 1x TE-SDS (1 ml), 2x TE-TW (1 ml each), 1x 10 mM Tris pH 8.0
                (all at 1000g, 1 min; modelled as BSA_Wash_Buffer in BioScript)
  BS variable : rtWashed4
  Duration    : RT mix 30m; heat 25c for 30m; heat 42c for 90m
  Compiler note: 90 min = 90m (no h token); 4 wash steps unrolled explicitly

Phase 8 | Exonuclease I Digestion
  Reagents    : Exo I mix (200 ul; see Reagents section)
  Step 1      : Add 200 ul Exo I mix to beads; incubate 37 C for 45 min with rotation
                (removes unextended TSO_RNAhybrid primers from bead surface)
  Step 2      : Spin beads; discard supernatant
  Washes      : 1x TE-SDS, 2x TE-TW (1000g, 1 min each)
  Storage     : If not proceeding immediately: store beads in 1 ml TE-TW at 4 C;
                resuspend in nuclease-free water when ready for WTA
  BS variable : exoWashed3
  Duration    : heat 37c for 45m

Phase 9 | Whole Transcriptome Amplification (WTA)
  Reagents    : Kapa HiFi Readymix, TSO_PCR primer, 96-well plate, Ampure XP beads
  Step 1      : Count beads; resuspend in nuclease-free water at max 5,000 beads per 24.6 ul
  Step 2      : Distribute into 96-well plate (5,000 beads in 24.6 ul per well)
  Step 3      : Set up 50 ul PCR reaction: 24.6 ul beads + water, 0.4 ul 100 uM TSO_PCR,
                25 ul Kapa HiFi Readymix
  Thermocycler program:
    95 C  3 min   (initial denaturation)
    4x  [98 C 20s, 65 C 45s, 72 C 3 min]  (low-cycle amplification)
    10x [98 C 20s, 67 C 20s, 72 C 3 min]  (high-cycle amplification)
    72 C  5 min   (final extension)
    4 C   hold
  Ampure XP purification (0.6X bead ratio):
    Add 10 ul NFW per well; pool 50 ul from each well; add Ampure beads (30 ul equiv per well)
    Incubate 5 min RT; magnetic rack 5 min; wash 4x with 1 ml 80% ethanol
    Dry 5 min; elute in 10 ul NFW per well; recover supernatant
    Store purified WTA at -20 C
  Expected result: Broad band, peak 600-700 bp (TapeStation; Figure 1 in paper)
  BS variable : wtaReaction
  Compiler note: No PCR primitive or loop in BioScript; thermocycler phases encoded as
                 4 sequential heat steps (aggregate cycle durations):
                 95c/3m, 65c/12m (4 cycles x 3 min), 67c/10m (10 cycles x 1 min), 72c/5m

Phase 9 QC Gate | barcodeIntegrityDetection
  Module      : barcodeIntegrityDetection
  Abstracts   : Ampure XP 0.6X cleanup + Qubit/TapeStation quantification of WTA products
  Pass (BB2)  : barcodeSignal > 0 -> valid WTA library (600-700 bp peak); proceed to tagmentation
  Fail (BB3)  : barcodeSignal <= 0 -> amplification or bead failure; drain wtaReaction
  Join (BB4)  : dispose barcodeSignal; fall into tagmentation

Phase 10 | Tagmentation and Library Preparation
  Kit         : Nextera XT DNA Library Prep Kit (Illumina, cat FC-131-1024)
  Input       : 450 pg purified WTA product per reaction (2 parallel reactions)
  Quantify    : Qubit quantification of WTA products before tagmentation
  Step 1      : Tagmentation: Tagment DNA mix (10 ul) + WTA product (5 ul, diluted to 5 ul NFW)
                + Amplicon Tagment mix (5 ul); 55 C for exactly 5 min
  Step 2      : Neutralise: add 5 ul NT buffer; mix; RT for 5 min; place on ice
  Step 3      : PCR amplification:
                add 8 ul NFW + 1 ul 10 uM P5-TSO + 1 ul 10 uM Nextera_N7XX + 15 ul Nextera PCR MM
                Thermocycler: 95 C 30s; 12x [95 C 10s, 55 C 30s, 72 C 30s]; 72 C 5 min; 4 C hold
  Ampure XP purification (0.6X): same protocol as WTA cleanup; store library at -20 C
  Expected result: Broad band, peak ~400 bp (Fragment Analyzer; Figure 2 in paper)
  BS variable : tagReaction1, tagReaction2 (two parallel reactions from same WTA pool)
  Compiler note: Tagmentation PCR encoded as 3 heat steps:
                 95c/30s (init), 55c/6m (12 cycles x 30s), 72c/5m (final extension)

Phase 10 QC Gate | wqaQCDetection
  Module      : wqaQCDetection
  Abstracts   : Ampure XP 0.6X cleanup + BioAnalyzer/Fragment Analyzer quantification
                + NextSeq library loading QC
  Pass (BB5)  : librarySignal > 0 -> valid library (~400 bp peak); dispose tagReaction1, tagReaction2
  Fail (BB6)  : librarySignal <= 0 -> tagmentation failure; drain tagReaction1, tagReaction2
  Terminal (BB7): dispose librarySignal; NOP

Next Generation Sequencing
  Platform    : Illumina NextSeq (>=75 cycles)
  Library QC  : BioAnalyzer quantification; denature per manufacturer guidelines
  Read mode   : Paired-end, single index
  Read lengths: Read 1 = 20 bp (cell barcode + UMI), Read 2 = 60 bp (cDNA), Index 1 = 8 bp
  Custom primer: Custom Read 1 primer (GCCTGTCCGCGGAAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGTAC)
  Throughput  : One 20-min droplet collection run fully sequenced with high output kit


TIME ESTIMATES
--------------
  Sample fixation, permeabilization, and staining : ~2 hours
  Sample preparation and droplet generation        : ~1.5 hours
  Crosslink reversal (in-droplet)                  : ~1 hour 15 minutes
  Reverse transcription                            : ~2.5 hours
  Exonuclease I digestion                          : ~1 hour
  Whole transcriptome amplification                : ~3 hours
  Sample purification (Ampure XP, WTA)             : ~0.5 hours
  Tagmentation                                     : ~2 hours
  Sample purification (Ampure XP, library)         : ~0.5 hours
  Next generation sequencing                       : ~12 hours
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  Total (excluding sequencing)                     : ~14.25 hours
