PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Extracellular Vesicle microRNA Quantification from Plasma Using an Integrated Microfluidic Device
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-019-0435-1
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-019-0435-1
Journal      : Communications Biology, 2019, Vol. 2, Article 189
Platform     : Integrated microfluidics (SAW lysing chip + IEM concentration/sensing chip)
Authors      : Zeinab Ramshani*, Chenguang Zhang*, Katherine Richards, Lulu Chen, Geyang Xu,
               Bangyan L. Stiles, Reginald Hill, Satyajyoti Senapati, David B. Go, Hsueh-Chia Chang
               (* equal contribution)
Institutions : University of Notre Dame; University of Southern California; Jinan University


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a PCR-free integrated microfluidic platform for absolute quantification
of free-floating and extracellular vesicle (EV)-contained microRNAs (miRNAs) directly from
untreated human plasma or serum. The platform integrates two chips in series:

  Chip 1 (SAW lysing chip): Surface acoustic waves (SAWs) generated by interdigitated electrodes
    on a LiNbO3 piezoelectric substrate mechanically lyse EVs under continuous flow, releasing
    their miRNA cargo without chemical reagents.

  Chip 2 (Concentration/sensing chip): Two sets of ion-exchange membranes (IEMs) — a cation-
    exchange membrane (CEM) for electrokinetic concentration and an anion-exchange membrane (AEM)
    functionalized with a target-specific oligoprobe as the sensor — detect and quantify the target
    miRNA via current-voltage curve (CVC) shift in the over-limiting current region.

The two chips are connected by a 500 um diameter, 10 cm Tygon tube enabling uninterrupted sample
transfer with no analyte loss between stages. By conducting two sequential 30-minute measurements
— one with the SAW device OFF (free-floating miRNA only) and one with it ON (total miRNA: free-
floating + EV-released) — the EV-miRNA fraction is directly computed from the difference in
voltage shift (Delta-V) between the two runs.

Target biomarker: miRNA hsa-mir-21-5p (miR-21; sequence: TAGCTTATCAGACTGATGTTGA), associated
with pancreatic, lung, colorectal cancer, and hepatocellular carcinoma.

Key advantages over conventional RT-qPCR:
  - No EV extraction, RNA purification, reverse transcription, or amplification required
  - Assay time: 30 min per measurement (vs. 13 h for RT-qPCR)
  - Sample volume: 20 uL per measurement (vs. 1 mL for RT-qPCR)
  - Quantification uncertainty: <10% (absolute quantification)
  - Detection limit: 1 pM


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Chip 1 — SAW Lysing Chip
  Substrate  : 128 deg YX lithium niobate (LiNbO3), piezoelectric
  Electrodes : 20 pairs of interdigitated aluminum electrodes (200 nm thick; 20 nm Ti adhesion)
               Width-controlled single-phase unidirectional IDT; resonant frequency: 28 MHz
  Channel    : Polycarbonate, 15 x 2 x 0.3 mm (l/w/t); attached with double-sided Kapton tape
               Channel oriented parallel to IDTs; SAW propagates perpendicular to channel
  Operation  : SAW generated at 28 MHz, 1 W (Agilent 33250A + E&I 325LA RF amplifier)
               Acoustic pressure shears EV membranes; ~1 min exposure at 250 uL/h achieves
               ~100% EV lysis (50% more efficient than TRIzol chemical lysis)

Chip 2 — Concentration/Sensing Chip
  Material   : Polycarbonate (three-layer), 60 x 2 x 0.3 mm (l/w/t)
  CEM (x2)  : Cation-exchange membranes (Mega a.s., Czech Republic)
               Implanted at bottom of chip bridging main channel and electrode reservoirs
               200 V DC applied via Keithley 2400 SourceMeter through platinum electrodes
               Reservoir buffer: 10x TAE (400 mM Tris-acetate + 10 mM EDTA)
               Creates depletion zone concentrating negatively charged miRNA up to 1000-fold
  AEM sensor : Anion-exchange membrane (Mega a.s., Czech Republic)
               Polystyrene-divinylbenzene / quaternary ammonium / polyethylene / polyamide support
               Framed by resin mold; positioned in orifice upstream of CEM in main channel
               Functionalized with miR-21 complementary ssDNA oligoprobe via amine-coupling
               Disposable: replaced after each single use to maintain repeatability
  Measurement: Gamry 500 potentiostat; 0-2.5 V CVC sweep; Ag-AgCl reference electrodes;
               platinum measurement electrodes; channel and reservoirs in 0.1x PBS during sweep

Interconnect
  Tygon tube, 500 um diameter, 10 cm length; connects SAW chip outlet to IEM chip inlet


REAGENTS
--------
  PBS 1x          : Injection buffer and driver fluid (Fischer Scientific)
  PBS 4x          : Post-hybridisation wash buffer (removes non-hybridised species from AEM)
  PBS 0.1x        : CVC measurement buffer (low ionic strength maximises Delta-V signal)
  TAE 10x         : Electrode reservoir buffer (400 mM Tris-acetate + 10 mM EDTA)
  miR-21 probe    : ssDNA oligoprobe TCAACATCAGTCTGATAAGCTA (amine-coupled; IDT)
  Plasma samples  : Untreated human plasma (EDTA; Zen-Bio Inc.; 3 healthy donors, 3 runs each)
                    Mouse plasma (Pten-deletion liver cancer model; 15-month-old male mice)
                    Clinical serum (de-identified human liver cancer; USC tissue bank; IRB-approved)

Negative controls:
  NC1: Bare (non-functionalized) AEM exposed to plasma -> no voltage shift
  NC2: 1:1 plasma dilution in 1x PBS -> voltage shift 0.65 V -> 0.6 V (as expected)
  NC3: Non-target miR-196a spike (1 uM; seq: TAGGTAGTTTCATGTTGTTGGG) -> 0.1 V shift (below limit)
  NC4: Man-made probe (GATCGCAGCCAAATGACGTGAC; no known miR complement) -> 0.08 V (below limit)
  NC5: RNAse-treated plasma (free RNA digested) -> no miR-21 detected (confirms signal specificity)
  NC6: Protease-added SAW-lysed plasma (proteins eliminated) -> same shift as untreated (no fouling)


IEM SENSOR CALIBRATION
-----------------------
  Target            : Synthetic miR-21 (IDT) in 1x PBS
  Concentration range: 1 pM to 1 nM
  Replicates        : 3 per concentration point (95% confidence error bars)
  Relationship      : Log-linear over 3 decades of concentration
  Calibration eq.   : C (nM) = exp((Delta-V - 0.76145) / 0.06614)
  Expression level  : ExpressionLevel = exp((Delta-V_target - Delta-V_reference) / b)
                      where b = 0.06614 (calibration fitting parameter)
  Validation        : Synthetic miR-21 at known concentration tested on both integrated device
                      and RT-qPCR; calculated average expression levels nearly identical


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | IEM Sensor Functionalization and Baseline CVC
  Inputs     : PBS 1x, IEM AEM Sensor (oligoprobe pre-functionalized)
  Operation  : Mix (5 s) + detect iemSensorDetection (30 s)
  Purpose    : Acquire baseline CVC for miR-21 oligoprobe in 1x PBS (no sample); used as
               Delta-V = 0 reference for all subsequent measurements.
  Probe seq  : TCAACATCAGTCTGATAAGCTA (amine-coupled to AEM; standard functionalization process)

Step 2 | Measurement 1 - Free-Floating miR-21 Quantification (SAW OFF)
  Inputs     : Plasma Sample (~20 uL untreated), PBS 1x (60 uL driver fluid)
  Operation  : Mix (channel flow; SAW device OFF; 2 s abstraction)
  Flow rate  : 250 uL/h (sample + driver)
  SAW status : OFF; IDT powered down; EVs remain intact
  Inlet      : Sample followed immediately by 60 uL PBS driver fluid
  Notes      : Plasma used as-is; no pre-treatment. Only free-floating miR-21 in plasma
               reaches the AEM sensor. ~1 min transit through SAW channel at 250 uL/h.

Step 3 | IEM Concentration - Measurement 1
  Inputs     : Channel flow 1, IEM CEM Concentrator
  Operation  : Mix (1200 s) + heat at 25 C for 1200 s (IEM electrokinetic abstraction)
  Voltage    : 200 V DC (Keithley 2400 SourceMeter)
  Duration   : 20 min (= 1200 s)
  Temperature: 25 C (ambient; no thermal step; heat models non-thermal IEM operation)
  Effect     : Depletion zone formed upstream of AEM sensor; negatively charged free-floating
               miR-21 concentrates up to 1000-fold directly beneath sensor surface.
               Hybridisation with oligoprobe occurs concurrently during 20-min window.

Step 4 | Post-Hybridisation Wash and Buffer Exchange - Measurement 1
  Wash       : PBS 4x (2 s mix); removes non-hybridised species from AEM surface
  CVC buffer : PBS 0.1x (2 s mix); fills channel and reservoirs for CVC sweep
  Notes      : Low ionic strength of 0.1x PBS maximises over-limiting current Delta-V signal.

Step 5 | CVC Sweep - Free-Floating miR-21 Delta-V Measurement
  Inputs     : readyForCVC1
  Operation  : Detect miR21FreefloatingDetection (30 s)
  Instrument : Gamry 500 potentiostat; 0-2.5 V sweep; Ag-AgCl reference electrodes
  Expected   : Delta-V ~0.1 V for free-floating miR-21 in healthy human plasma (Fig. 3)
  Branch pass (freefloatSignal > 0)  : dispose readyForCVC1 -> proceed to BB4
  Branch fail (freefloatSignal <= 0) : drain readyForCVC1 -> proceed to BB4

Step 6 | Measurement 2 - Total miR-21 Quantification (SAW ON; Free-Floating + EV-miRNA)
  Inputs     : SAW Actuator, Plasma Sample (~20 uL fresh aliquot), PBS 1x (60 uL driver)
  Operation  : Mix (SAW channel flow + acoustic lysis; 60 s) + detect sawLysingDetection (10 s)
  SAW params : 28 MHz, 1 W; Agilent 33250A + E&I 325LA
  Flow rate  : 250 uL/h; ~1 min channel residence time -> ~100% EV lysis
  Notes      : SAWs refract perpendicularly into polycarbonate microchannel and shear EV
               membranes via acoustic pressure. Released EV-miRNA and free-floating miRNA
               both enter the concentration/sensing chip. SAW lysis is 50% more efficient
               than TRIzol and 46% more efficient than Qiagen chemical kit.
               sawLysingDetection module confirms lysis before IEM chip processing.
               Fresh plasma aliquot used; disposable AEM sensor replaced before this run.

Step 7 | IEM Concentration - Measurement 2
  Inputs     : Channel flow 2, IEM CEM Concentrator (fresh)
  Operation  : Mix (1200 s) + heat at 25 C for 1200 s
  Voltage    : 200 V DC for 20 min
  Notes      : Total miRNA pool (free-floating + EV-released) concentrates and hybridises
               with fresh oligoprobe on replaced disposable AEM sensor.

Step 8 | Post-Hybridisation Wash and Buffer Exchange - Measurement 2
  Wash       : PBS 4x (2 s); CVC buffer: PBS 0.1x (2 s)
  Notes      : Identical procedure to Step 4.

Step 9 | CVC Sweep - Total miR-21 Delta-V Measurement
  Inputs     : readyForCVC2
  Operation  : Detect miR21TotalDetection (30 s)
  Instrument : Gamry 500 potentiostat; 0-2.5 V sweep; Ag-AgCl reference electrodes
  Expected   : Delta-V ~0.65-0.7 V for total miR-21 in healthy human plasma (Fig. 3)
  EV-miRNA   : Computed as difference between Measurement 2 and Measurement 1 Delta-V values,
               converted to concentration via: C (nM) = exp((Delta-V - 0.76145) / 0.06614)
               Expression level: exp((Delta-V_total - Delta-V_freefloat) / 0.06614)
  Branch pass (totalSignal > 0)  : dispose readyForCVC2 + dispose lysisSignal -> proceed to BB7
  Branch fail (totalSignal <= 0) : drain readyForCVC2 + drain lysisSignal -> proceed to BB7

Step 10 | Final Cleanup (Terminal)
  Operations : dispose totalSignal; dispose baselineMix; NOP
  Notes      : Off-chip: EV-miR-21 expression level computed and compared to reference.
               Device terminated; experiment ends.


KEY RESULTS
-----------
  Healthy human plasma  : EV-miR-21 ~2 orders of magnitude higher than free-floating miR-21
  Liver cancer mice     : Total plasma miR-21 ~1000x elevated vs healthy mice
                          (tissue upregulation only ~3x, highlighting EV amplification effect)
  Clinical liver cancer : miR-21 ~13x overexpressed in serum vs healthy subjects; no group overlap
  Platform reproducibility: Consistent results across 3 donors x 3 runs x 3 fabricated devices
