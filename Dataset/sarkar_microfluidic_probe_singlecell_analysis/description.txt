PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Microfluidic Probe for Single-Cell Analysis in Adherent Tissue Culture
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms4421
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms4421
Journal      : Nature Communications, 2014, Vol. 5, Article 3421
Platform     : Three-layer PDMS microfluidic probe with hydrodynamic confinement lysis,
               pneumatic valve-controlled nanolitre reaction chambers, and fluorescence readout


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidic probe that selectively lyses single adherent cells
from standard tissue culture using hydrodynamic confinement of a detergent-containing lysis
buffer, captures the resulting single-cell lysate, mixes it with assay reagents in nanolitre-
scale reaction chambers, and performs high-sensitivity fluorescence-based enzyme activity
assays. The platform is demonstrated by measuring Akt kinase activity and GAPDH housekeeping
enzyme activity simultaneously from single HepG2 hepatocellular carcinoma cells in adherent
culture, revealing biological heterogeneity in Akt signalling that correlates with distinct
cell morphology subgroups -- not visible in bulk-level assays.

Hydrodynamic confinement is achieved by simultaneous outflow (Qout, central channel,
5 nL/min lysis buffer) and inflow (Qin, outer channels, 50 nL/min total) at the probe tip,
creating a confined lysis zone of tunable radius R (R/L set by Qin/Qout ratio, confirmed
by COMSOL Multiphysics simulation). The zone is set slightly smaller than the target cell
diameter (~15 um) so that only the targeted cell is lysed; neighbouring cells are unaffected
even in ~80% confluent cultures. The lysate is captured back into the device within ~20 s
and mixed with assay buffers at a 1:4 ratio (set by passive flow resistance ratio) before
pneumatic valves trap the assay mixture in 20 nL reaction chambers at 90 s post-lysis.

The integration of lysis, mixing, and assay in a single chip limits lysate dilution and
maintains sensitivity sufficient to measure enzyme activity from less than half a cell's
worth of total protein. The Akt assay LOD on the device is 0.32 pg active Akt -- approximately
1600x more sensitive than a standard plate reader (LOD 0.51 ng). The device can assay up to
four activities from a single cell (two to four chambers per lysate), and up to four cells
in parallel (one per chamber set), with sequential lysis per cell.

This is, to the authors' knowledge, the first use of hydrodynamic confinement to reclaim
cellular material from adherent culture for biochemical assay.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Material     : Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS, RTV615); three-layer monolithic construction.

Layer 1 (base)     : Bare glass slide; forms base of device except at probe tip region.
Layer 2 (membrane) : ~30 um thin PDMS; forms deformable base of tip (enables close approach
                     to adherent cells on tissue culture surface); contains pneumatic valve
                     lines V1-V4 (15 um SU-8 mould).
Layer 3 (flow)     : ~5 mm thick PDMS; contains all channels, reaction chambers, and
                     flow/control connection ports. Two channel heights:
                       5 um: tip channels + assay buffer mixing regions (tuned for 1:4 mixing
                             by flow resistance ratio)
                      30 um: reaction chambers (maximise fluorescence signal collection)

Fabrication:
  Mask design        : AutoCAD 2000; photoplot film or chrome mask.
  Flow layer 30 um   : AZ4620 photoresist on 6 inch silicon wafer.
  Flow layer 5 um    : SU-8 photoresist on 6 inch silicon wafer.
  Membrane layer     : SU-8 photoresist (15 um valve lines).
  Silanisation       : Heptadecafluoro-1,1,2,2-tetra-hydrodecyl trimethoxysilane;
                       dessicator 1 min vent + 1 h vacuum before casting.
  Membrane PDMS      : RTV615 20:1; spin-coated at 1600 rpm.
  Flow layer PDMS    : RTV615 5:1; cast from Si mould, then plastic replica mould used.
  Partial cure       : 60 min at 65 C; aligned under stereo microscope; baked overnight
                       at 65 C for monolithic bond.
  Final assembly     : Plasma bonded to glass slide with tip protruding over straight edge;
                       baked 95 C 15 min; degassed >= 10 min before use.
  Tip shaping        : Cut with sharp microtome blade to pointed tip with flat front.

Probe tip:
  Channels             : 3 (1 central outflow, 2 outer inflows)
  Channel width (w)    : 100 um
  Channel separation (p): 50 um
  Characteristic length (L = w + 2p): 200 um
  Lysis buffer         : Qout = 5 nL/min (central channel)
  Lysate inflow        : Qin = 50 nL/min total (25 nL/min per outer channel)
  Qin/Qout ratio       : 10 (sets lysis zone R slightly smaller than cell, ~15 um)
  Zone confinement     : COMSOL simulated; detergent diffusivity 6e-11 m2/s; 1% v/v input;
                         R measured as distance from tip where detergent falls to 0.1%

Reaction chambers (AB1-AB4):
  Count            : 4 (AB1-AB4)
  Volume per chamber: ~20 nL
  Assay mix ratio  : lysate:assay buffer = 1:4 (passive flow resistance ratio)
  Travel time      : 90 s from tip to chamber
  Repeatability    : within 10% CV (mixing ratio and travel time)

Pneumatic valves (V1-V4):
  Actuation        : compressed air 30 psi via custom solenoid valve manifold
  Timing           : actuated at 90 s post-lysis (measured travel time)
  Function         : trap assay mixture in selected chamber; optionally split lysate
                     to multiple chambers for multiplexed assays from one cell

Multiplexed chamber assignment (this protocol):
  AB1  : Akt kinase Sox sensor assay
  AB2  : GAPDH NADH formation assay (loading control)
  AB3/4: available for additional activities or parallel cells


CELL LINES
----------
HepG2 (ATCC HB-8065):
  Application    : All enzyme activity assays (Akt, GAPDH, multiplexed); primary cell line.
  Culture medium : Eagle's MEM + 10% FBS (Hyclone, Fisher Scientific)
                   + 1% penicillin/streptomycin (Invitrogen).
  Conditions     : 37 C, 5% CO2; tissue culture treated polystyrene.

MCF-7 (human breast cancer):
  Application    : Single-cell lysis characterisation only (Fig. 3a/b of paper).
  Dye            : Cell Tracker Green (ex 490 nm / em 526 nm).
  Notes          : Used to confirm lysis of target cell and unaffected neighbouring cells;
                   not used for enzyme activity assays. Not encoded in this .bs file.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming with De-ionised Water
  Reagents     : De-ionised water
  Operation    : Syringe-pump driven flow through device channels
  Duration     : 5 min
  Syringes     : Gas-tight glass syringes 50 uL (lysis buffer and inflow source ports)
  Tubing       : Fused silica OD 360 um / ID 100 um
  Pump         : Harvard Apparatus PHD 2000 syringe pump
  Purpose      : Remove air bubbles; equilibrate channels before BSA coating.

Step 2 | BSA Coating to Prevent Non-Specific Protein Binding
  Reagents     : 1% BSA in 0.5x PBS; de-ionised water (wash)
  Flow rate    : 50 nL/min
  Duration     : >= 60 min (1 h) coating; then DI water wash
  Temperature  : 25 C (ambient)
  Purpose      : PDMS non-specifically adsorbs proteins; BSA blocking is critical to
                 preserve enzyme activity from picomolar single-cell lysate in 20 nL chambers.

Step 3 | UV Photobleaching of Observation Area
  Duration     : >= 30 min
  Wavelength   : ex 377 nm (UV)
  Purpose      : Reduce background autofluorescence in reaction chambers before assay.
                 Done immediately before loading assay buffers.

Step 4 | Load Assay Buffers and Lysis Buffer
  Akt assay buffer (AB1, 20 uL):
    50 mM Tris-HCl pH 7.3, 10 mM MgCl2, 1 mM EGTA, 2 mM DTT,
    4 mM PKC inhibitor peptide, 10 mM Sox substrate peptide,
    1 mM ATP (low metals grade, Calbiochem), 0.4 mM PKItide,
    5 uM GF109203X (EMD)
  GAPDH assay buffer (AB2, 20 uL):
    90 mM sodium pyrophosphate pH 7.1, 20 mM NaH2PO4, 3 mM DTT,
    4 mM NAD+, 10 mM glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate
  Lysis buffer (syringe at lysis port):
    50 mM beta-glycerophosphate, 10 mM sodium pyrophosphate, 30 mM NaF,
    50 mM Tris pH 7.4, 1% Triton X-100, 15 mM NaCl, 2 mM EGTA,
    100 uM sodium orthovanadate, 1 mM DTT,
    1X protease inhibitor cocktail (Merck/EMD 539134),
    1X phosphatase inhibitor cocktail (Sigma P2850),
    20 ug/mL Alexa 488-labelled BSA (lysis zone visualisation)
  Duration     : ~2 min (passive loading)
  Timing       : Immediately before experiment; lysis buffer loaded last.

Step 5 | HepG2 Cell Plating and Attachment
  Seeding density : 5,000-20,000 cells/cm2
  Medium          : Eagle's MEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep
  Substrate       : Standard tissue culture treated polystyrene
  Duration        : 4 h minimum (range 4-16 h; BioScript: 240m -> IR: 14400.0 SECOND)
  Temperature     : 37 C, 5% CO2
  Notes           : Cells fully attached before serum starvation. No effect of seeding
                    density on insulin activation time course (15,000-100,000 cells/cm2).

Step 6 | Serum Starvation
  Medium       : Serum-free Eagle's MEM (no FBS)
  Duration     : 12 h minimum (range 12-24 h; BioScript: 720m -> IR: 43200.0 SECOND)
  Temperature  : 37 C, 5% CO2
  Purpose      : Synchronise Akt signalling baseline; sensitise Akt pathway to insulin.
  Notes        : For bulk assay validation: 16-24 h starvation.

Step 7 | Cell Labelling with Cell Tracker Orange Dye
  Dye              : Cell Tracker Orange (Invitrogen), 2.5 uM in serum-free medium
  Labelling        : 15 min minimum (range 15-45 min) at 37 C, 5% CO2
                     BioScript: 15m -> IR: 900.0 SECOND
  Wash             : PBS (Ca2+/Mg2+ free)
  Recovery         : Serum-free medium, 20 min minimum (range 20-45 min) at 37 C, 5% CO2
                     BioScript: 20m -> IR: 1200.0 SECOND
  Imaging filter   : Rhodamine (Semrock, ex 540 nm / em 570 nm)
  Purpose          : Cytosolic fluorescent label; drop in target cell fluorescence within
                     ~20 s confirms lysis and lysate capture. Neighbouring cells unaffected
                     (fluorescence constant throughout lysis event; Fig. 3b of paper).

Step 8 | Insulin Stimulation to Activate Akt
  Reagent          : Insulin, 500 ng/mL final concentration in serum-free medium
  Method           : Spiked into medium on plate (medium NOT removed during time-course lysis;
                     cells lysed one by one under continuous stimulation conditions)
  Duration         : 10 min minimum tested (range 10-16.5 min; BioScript: 10m -> IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  Temperature      : 37 C
  Serum-starved control: No insulin; no medium change
  Notes            : Plate brought to microscope and lysis started immediately after
                     desired stimulation time point.

Step 9 | Single-Cell Lysis by Hydrodynamic Confinement
  Flow rates       : Qout = 5 nL/min (lysis buffer, central channel)
                     Qin = 50 nL/min total (25 nL/min per outer channel)
  Lysis zone R     : ~15 um (slightly smaller than HepG2 cell; Qin/Qout = 10)
  Lysis time       : ~20 s (fluorescence drops and lysate captured within 20 s)
  Microscope       : Olympus IX71
  Positioning      : Micromanipulator
  Monitoring       : Rhodamine filter (ex 540 nm / em 570 nm) for Cell Tracker Orange
  Detection module : lysisDetection (BioScript)
  Detection duration: 20 s (BioScript: 20s -> IR: 20.0 SECOND)
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB2): lysisSignal > 0 -> Cell Tracker Orange drop confirmed in target cell,
                neighbouring cells unaffected; dispose lysis zone, proceed to assay
    Fail (BB3): lysisSignal <= 0 -> lysis failure (no drop, or neighbour affected);
                drain lysis zone; retry on next target cell
    Join (BB4): dispose lysisSignal; lysate transport and assay branches begin

Step 10 | Lysate Transport, Valve Actuation, and Akt Kinase Assay (Chamber AB1)
  Mixing           : Lysate + Akt assay buffer at 1:4 ratio by passive flow resistance
                     in 5 um channels (BioScript: mix for 90s -> IR: 90.0 SECOND)
  Valve actuation  : V1/V2 closed at 90 s post-lysis (compressed air 30 psi, solenoid manifold)
  Assay temperature: 30 C (Tokai Hit heating chamber on automated motorised stage)
  Assay duration   : >= 20 min (BioScript: heat + detect 20m -> IR: 1200.0 SECOND each)
  Microscope stage : Prior Scientific H110 motorised; Olympus IX71
  Imaging          : DAPI filter (Semrock, ex 377 nm / em 447 nm); every 30 s;
                     Uniblitz shutter; 200 ms exposure; >= 20 min total
  Readout          : Initial slope of Sox sensor fluorescence vs time (linear region);
                     activity corrected for bleaching by subtracting lysis buffer control mean
  LOD              : 0.32 pg recombinant active Akt (device) vs 0.51 ng (plate reader M2e)
  Single-cell equiv: ~1 ng total protein per HepG2 cell (400,000 cells -> 360 ug total protein)
  Detection module : aktKinaseDetection (BioScript)
  Results:
    Insulin-stimulated 'high Akt' cells: significant Akt increase (p=3.2e-4, n=12)
    Serum-starved / 'low Akt' cells: no Akt response (p=0.35); GAPDH also unaffected (p=0.50)
    Bulk CV: within 7% for Akt lysate measurements
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB5): aktSignal > 0 -> Akt activity above lysis buffer baseline; 'high Akt'
                phenotype confirmed; dispose Akt assay mixture
    Fail (BB6): aktSignal <= 0 -> no Akt above baseline; 'low Akt' phenotype (putatively
                senescent morphology: larger cell area p=0.037, larger min Feret p=0.0003);
                drain Akt assay mixture
    Join (BB7): dispose aktSignal; GAPDH branch begins

Step 11 | GAPDH Housekeeping Enzyme Assay (Chamber AB2) -- Multiplexed Loading Control
  Mixing           : Lysate + GAPDH assay buffer at 1:4 ratio (same resistance ratio)
                     (BioScript: mix for 90s -> IR: 90.0 SECOND)
  Valve actuation  : V3/V4 closed at 90 s post-lysis
  Assay temperature: 30 C
  Assay duration   : >= 20 min (BioScript: heat + detect 20m -> IR: 1200.0 SECOND each)
  Readout          : Initial slope of NADH fluorescence vs time (NAD+ to NADH conversion)
  Imaging          : DAPI filter (same as Akt; automated stage images AB1 and AB2 simultaneously)
  LOD              : Lower than Akt (higher GAPDH expression levels relative to Akt)
  Bulk CV          : Within 18% for GAPDH lysate measurements
  Detection module : gapdhActivityDetection (BioScript)
  Normalisation    : Akt/GAPDH ratio per cell shows same response pattern as raw Akt
                     (Supplementary Fig. 9); GAPDH is valid single-cell loading control
  Notes            : AB1 and AB2 imaged simultaneously on automated motorised stage.
                     Encoded sequentially in BioScript (compiler does not support concurrent
                     parallel detect statements).
  QC gate:
    Pass (BB8): gapdhSignal > 0 -> GAPDH detected; loading control confirmed;
                dispose GAPDH assay mixture
    Fail (BB9): gapdhSignal <= 0 -> GAPDH not detected (lysate loss or chamber failure);
                drain GAPDH assay mixture
    Join (BB10): dispose gapdhSignal; NOP (terminal); single-cell assay cycle complete


BIOLOGICAL FINDINGS
-------------------
Two subpopulations of HepG2 cells identified under insulin stimulation:

'High Akt' cells:
  Akt activity     : Significantly increased upon insulin stimulation (p=3.2e-4, n=12)
  GAPDH activity   : Unaffected (p=0.98; confirms specificity of Akt response)
  Morphology       : Smaller cell area; smaller minimum Feret diameter (caliper width)

'Low Akt' cells:
  Akt activity     : Not increased upon stimulation (p=0.35)
  GAPDH activity   : Also unaffected (p=0.50)
  Morphology       : Significantly larger cell area (p=0.037) and minimum Feret diameter
                     (p=0.0003); consistent with wider, flatter senescent morphology
  Putative identity: Cells in senescence (elevated SA-beta-gal activity confirmed under
                     sparse plating conditions; lower SMP-30 expression promotes lower Akt
                     in HepG2; consistent with literature on Akt in cardiac fibroblast
                     senescence and oncogene-induced senescence)

Key advantage: Morphological phenotype of 'low Akt' cells not observable in flow cytometry.
This probe allows selection of phenotypically distinct cells and direct signalling measurement
from the same individual cell in its adherent context -- connecting intracellular state to
phenotype in a way not achievable with existing suspension-based single-cell methods.

                 creating lysis zone, (d) passive resistance-ratio mixing of lysate with
                 assay buffer in 5 um channels before valve closure. Not DMF electrode mixing.
Heat abstraction: heat models both 37 C CO2 incubator dwell (cell culture steps) and 30 C
                 heating chamber dwell (assay monitoring steps). Temperature specified per
                 step in BioScript heat statement.
lysisBuffer reuse: In BB4 (Akt mix) and BB7 (GAPDH mix), lysisBuffer1[0] carried from BB1
                 through join nodes; models same physical lysis buffer reservoir feeding
                 both AB1 and AB2 mixing channels in parallel -- consistent with device.
Biological scope: This .bs file encodes the multiplexed Akt + GAPDH arm (Fig. 5, n=12).
                 Single-activity arms (Akt only: Fig. 4a; GAPDH only: Fig. 4b; MK2:
                 Supplementary Fig. 7) differ only in lysate routing to one chamber;
                 each would require a separate .bs file with one assay branch instead of two.
