PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Automated Microfluidic Platform for Dynamic and Combinatorial Drug Screening
               of Tumor Organoids
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-19058-4
DOI          : https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19058-4
Journal      : Nature Communications (2020), Volume 11, Article 5271
Authors      : Brooke Schuster, Michael Junkin, Sara Saheb Kashaf, et al. (Savas Tay lab)
Platform     : Two-part PDMS microfluidic device: 200-well 3D organoid culture chamber array
               + solenoid-valve multiplexer control device; automated via MATLAB GUI


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated high-throughput microfluidic platform for culturing
patient-derived pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) organoids in Matrigel and performing
individual, combinatorial, and temporal/sequential drug screens. The system supports up to
20 independent fluidic conditions and 10 patient samples in a single experiment across 200
individual culture wells.

The BioScript file encodes three experimental arms:
  (A) Single-agent drug screens: 7 FDA-approved chemotherapies at 72-h continuous exposure,
      with a 48-h media replenishment step (Patient 1).
  (B) Temporal/sequential combination drug regimens: 5 standard-of-care PDAC regimens
      delivered in clinically-mirrored pulse sequences (Patient 1).
  (C) Multi-patient parallel comparison: Gemcitabine + 5-FU temporal arm run simultaneously
      for Patients 1, 2, and 3 (mirrors Fig. 5 heatmap experiment).

All 14 treatment wells converge at exactly 4320 minutes (72 hours) for the apoptosisQC
detect gate, ensuring parallel biological comparison at an identical timestamp.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Chamber layer  : PDMS 200-well array; average well depth 610 um (significantly deeper than
                 typical microfluidic devices of 100-200 um); glass slide base.
Channel layer  : PDMS fluidic channels, 455 um height; reversibly clamped onto chamber layer
                 via polycarbonate plate and six screws; valve-less design allows Matrigel
                 loading by manual pipetting without risk of channel clogging.
Well array     : 200 wells divided into 20 subsets of 10; each subset receives one fluidic
                 condition from the multiplexer.
Multiplexer    : Valve-based reusable control device; up to 30 solutions pre-loaded;
                 solenoid valves controlled by custom MATLAB GUI (CSV/tab-delimited input);
                 supply pressure 5 psi; valve closing pressure 35-40 psi.
Microscope     : Nikon Eclipse Ti inverted microscope; automated translational stage;
                 ORCA-Flash 4.0 CMOS camera (Hamamatsu); 10x objective; NIS-Elements software;
                 imaging every 2-4 h; Life Imaging Service GmbH environmental enclosure
                 (37 C / 5% CO2 / 100% RH).


CELL LINES AND CULTURE
-----------------------
Cell type      : Patient-derived pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) organoids
                 (Patients 1, 2, and 3; IRB12-1108 and IRB13-1149; UCM 2014-2017)
Cell prep      : Tumor samples digested and dissociated; single cells embedded in 70%
                 GFR Matrigel (Corning 356231) / 30% complete organoid media; passed through
                 40 um cell strainer before loading; passaged via mechanical dissociation +
                 TrypLE Express (Fisher Scientific 12605-010).
Complete media : Intesticult (Stemcell Technologies 6005) supplemented with A83-01 (0.5 uM),
                 FGF10 (100 ng/mL), Gastrin I (10 nM), N-acetyl-L-cysteine (10 mM),
                 Nicotinamide (10 mM), B27 (1x), Primocin (1 mg/mL), Y-27632 (10.5 uM).
Growth phase   : 14+ days on platform; organoids grow from single cells to ~500 um glandular
                 structures; automated media exchange every 38 h; continuous time-lapse imaging.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Organoid Culture Establishment (Section 1, BB1)
  Operation  : dispense + mix (abstracts manual pipetting of cells in Matrigel/media mixture
               into 610-um wells, followed by Matrigel gelation and channel layer clamping)
  Arms       : 14 independent wells (one per treatment arm; each with fresh dispensed culture)
  Loading    : 70% GFR Matrigel + 30% complete organoid media + dissociated single cells
  Notes      : Each arm uses independently dispensed cells, Matrigel, and media. No fluid
               variable is shared between arms.

Step 2 | 14-Day Growth Phase (Section 2, BB1)
  Operation  : heat at 37 C for 20160m (14 days = 1209600 s in IR)
  Conditions : 37 C / 5% CO2 / 100% RH (Life Imaging Service GmbH environmental chamber)
  Duration   : 14 days minimum (until organoids reach ~500 um diameter and are visibly mature)
  Feeding    : Automated media exchange every 38 h via multiplexer
  Imaging    : Phase contrast + fluorescence, every 2-4 h, MATLAB auto-segmentation
  Notes      : heat is void/in-place in BioScript (no LHS assignment); same instruction
               applied to all 14 culture wells.

Step 3 | Single-Agent Drug Screen, Patient 1, 72-h Arm (Section 3, BB1)
  Drugs      : Gemcitabine 100 nM, Paclitaxel 10 nM, 5-FU 100 nM, Docetaxel 10 nM,
               CPT-11 100 nM, Oxaliplatin 100 nM, Cisplatin 100 nM
  Markers    : Caspase 3/7 5 uM (Essen Bioscience 4400, apoptosis);
               Propidium iodide 1.5 uM (Thermo Fisher P3566, cell death)
  Phase 1    : Drug exposure 0-48 h (2880m)
  Refresh    : Fresh complete media + drug dispensed and mixed at 48-h mark
  Phase 2    : Continued drug exposure 48-72 h (1440m)
  Total      : 2880m + 1440m = 4320m = 72 h
  QC arm     : Gemcitabine arm used for apoptosisQC detect gate
  Fix A      : Split into two phases to correctly place the 48-h replenishment boundary
               (original 4320m single mix delayed replenishment to 72h+10s)

Step 4 | Temporal FOLFIRINOX, Patient 1 (Section 4, BB1)
  Clinical   : Standard IV FOLFIRINOX for PDAC; mirrors real patient treatment sequence
  Sequence   : CPT-11 100 nM (120m) -> Oxaliplatin 100 nM (120m) ->
               High-dose 5-FU 1 uM (30m) -> Low-dose 5-FU 100 nM (4050m)
  Total      : 120+120+30+4050 = 4320m = 72 h
  Fix B      : Final 5-FU extended to 4050m; paper states "low dose 5-FU continued until
               the end of the 72-h experiment" (not 46h/2760m from clinical text block)

Step 5 | Temporal FOLFIRI, Patient 1 (Section 5, BB1)
  Sequence   : CPT-11 100 nM (240m) -> High-dose 5-FU 1 uM (30m) ->
               Low-dose 5-FU 100 nM (4050m)
  Total      : 240+30+4050 = 4320m = 72 h
  Fix B      : Final 5-FU extended from 2880m to 4050m

Step 6 | Temporal FOLFOX, Patient 1 (Section 6, BB1)
  Sequence   : Oxaliplatin 100 nM (240m) -> High-dose 5-FU 1 uM (30m) ->
               Low-dose 5-FU 100 nM (4050m)
  Total      : 240+30+4050 = 4320m = 72 h
  Fix B      : Final 5-FU extended from 2880m to 4050m

Step 7 | Temporal Gemcitabine + 5-FU, Patient 1 (Section 7, BB1)
  Cycles     : 2 cycles, unrolled (no loop constructs in BioScript)
  Cycle 1    : Gemcitabine 100 nM (240m) -> Low-dose 5-FU 100 nM (2880m)
  Cycle 2    : Gemcitabine 100 nM (240m) -> Low-dose 5-FU 100 nM (960m) [truncated]
  Total      : 240+2880+240+960 = 4320m = 72 h
  Fix C      : Cycle 2 final 5-FU truncated from 2880m to 960m per Fig 4f visual
               truncation at the 72-h assay read boundary

Step 8 | Temporal Gemcitabine + Paclitaxel, Patient 1 (Section 8, BB1)
  Cycles     : 2 cycles, unrolled
  Cycle 1    : Gemcitabine 100 nM (240m) -> Paclitaxel 10 nM (240m) -> Media (1440m)
  Cycle 2    : Gemcitabine 100 nM (240m) -> Paclitaxel 10 nM (240m) -> Media (1920m)
  Total      : 240+240+1440+240+240+1920 = 4320m = 72 h
  Clinical   : Most effective regimen for Patient 1 (Fig 4g); retrospectively consistent
               with clinical outcome (Table 2: gemcitabine+paclitaxel administered last)
  Fix C      : Final media step padded from 1440m to 1920m to reach 72-h boundary
  Note       : Table 1 schedule encoded here; Fig 4g shows a different schedule
               (Cycle1: 2h Gem + 2h Pac + 44h Media; Cycle2: 2h Gem + 4h Pac + 18h Media).
               Table 1 selected as primary encoding per reviewer guidance.

Step 9 | Multi-Patient Parallel Gem+5FU Temporal (Section 9, BB1)
  Patients   : Patient 2 and Patient 3 (Patient 1 encoded in Section 7)
  Mirrors    : Fig. 5 high-throughput simultaneous 3-patient experiment
  Structure  : Identical to Section 7 (same temporal cycle, same 960m truncation)
  QC gate    : Patient 2 arm (p2Finished) used for multiPatQC detect;
               P2 was most sensitive (p=7E-07 vs control, Fig 5b)
  Fix C      : Same 960m truncation applied to p2Finished and p3Finished


QC GATES AND CFG
-----------------

Primary QC gate (apoptosisQC):
  Detect     : apoptosisDetection on gemFinished, 30s
  BB2 (pass) : apoptosisQC > 0 -> dispose all 12 P1 arm droplets
  BB3 (fail) : apoptosisQC <= 0 -> drain all 12 P1 arm droplets
  BB4 (join) : dispose apoptosisQC signal

Secondary QC gate (multiPatQC):
  Detect     : apoptosisDetection on p2Finished, 30s
  BB5 (pass) : multiPatQC > 0 -> dispose p2Finished, p3Finished
  BB6 (fail) : multiPatQC <= 0 -> drain p2Finished, p3Finished
  BB7 (term) : dispose multiPatQC; device disassembled for organoid harvest
               (channel layer removed; wells exposed for sequencing, expansion, or grafting)

CFG edges    : BB1->BB2, BB1->BB3, BB2->BB4, BB3->BB4,
               BB4->BB5, BB4->BB6, BB5->BB7, BB6->BB7
DAG shape    : Two sequential diamond gates converging at terminal BB7

FIXES APPLIED (iterative review corrections)
--------------------------------------------

FIX A (Section 3) -- 48-hour replenishment bug:
  Issue  : Original code ran a single 4320m mix then appended replenishment after 72h+10s,
           violating the paper's "48-h mark replenishment" specification.
  Fix    : Each single-agent arm split into:
           phase1 (2880m) + dispense fresh media+drug + phase2 (1440m) = 4320m total.

FIX B (Sections 4, 5, 6) -- Duration deficits in temporal FOLFIRINOX/FOLFIRI/FOLFOX:
  Issue  : Final 5-FU step encoded as 2880m (48h) or 2760m (46h) from Table 1 text,
           leaving each arm 21.5h short of the 72-h endpoint; wells would sit idle.
  Fix    : Final low-dose 5-FU step extended to 4050m in all three regimens.
           Paper explicitly states: "low dose 5-FU continued until the end of the 72-h
           experiment." 120+120+30+4050 = 4320m; 240+30+4050 = 4320m.

FIX C (Sections 7, 8, 9) -- Gem+5FU overshoot and Gem+Pac undershoot:
  Issue  : Gem+5FU two-cycle unroll totalled 6240m (104h), overshooting by 32h.
           Gem+Pac two-cycle unroll totalled 3840m (64h), undershooting by 8h.
           Reviewer first suggested 1200m truncation for Gem+5FU (giving 4560m, incorrect).
           Corrected to 960m (240+2880+240+960=4320m).
  Fix    : Gem+5FU cycle 2 final 5-FU -> 960m; same applied to P2 and P3 in Section 9.
           Gem+Pac cycle 2 final media -> 1920m (240+240+1440+240+240+1920=4320m).

FIX UNICODE -- ANTLR4 UnicodeDecodeError:
  Issue  : UTF-8 checkmark (U+2713, 0xe2 0x9c 0x93) and left arrow (U+2190) in comments
           caused UnicodeDecodeError at byte position 14172 when ANTLR4 FileStream
           attempted ASCII decode.
  Fix    : Decoded file as UTF-8, replaced all non-ASCII chars with ASCII equivalents
           (U+2713 -> "OK", U+2190 -> "<--"), re-encoded as strict ASCII.
