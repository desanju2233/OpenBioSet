PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Microfluidic Device for Measuring Cell Migration Towards Substrate-Bound and Soluble Chemokine Gradients
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/srep36440
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/srep36440
Journal      : Scientific Reports, 2016, Vol. 6, Article 36440
Platform     : Two-layer PDMS microfluidic device with LAPAP photo-patterning and flow-free diffusion gradient generation


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidic approach for simultaneously generating surface-immobilized
haptotactic chemokine gradients and diffusion-based soluble chemotactic gradients within the same
migration chamber, enabling quantitative single-cell tracking of dendritic cell (DC) migration in
combined and competing chemokine gradient fields.

The device integrates three functional capabilities:
  1. LAPAP (Laser-Assisted Protein Adsorption by Photobleaching) photo-patterning of
     arbitrarily shaped, graded surface-immobilized chemokine patterns (CCL21 24-98 bio)
     with ~0.7 um lateral resolution and ~100:1 dynamic range.
  2. Flow-free diffusion-based soluble chemokine gradients (CCL19) via source-sink
     flow-switching, independently controllable in steepness, mean concentration, and duration.
  3. Precise positioning of immune cells on haptotactic gradients, with simultaneous
     superimposition of opposing or co-directional soluble gradients.

The protocol is divided into three experimental arms:
  ARM 1. Haptotaxis arm: DC migration on immobilized CCL21 gradient (no soluble gradient)
  ARM 2. Chemotaxis arm: DC migration in soluble CCL19 diffusion gradient (no immobilized chemokine)
  ARM 3. Competing gradients arm: DC migration simultaneously exposed to opposing immobilized
         CCL21 (haptotactic) and soluble CCL19 (chemotactic) gradients

Key finding: haptotactic CCL21 gradients dominate at low soluble CCL19 (~0-1 ug/mL), while
increasing CCL19 concentrations (~0-1.2 ug/mL) override the immobilized CCL21 signal and
redirect DC migration along the soluble gradient.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Layer i   : PDMS flow layer; 6 migration chambers (l=1100 um, w=200 um, h_max=28 um).
            Each chamber has one side port at the midpoint of the long axis and two ports at
            the short ends connected to source and sink support channels.
Layer ii  : PDMS control layer; independently controllable PDMS membrane valves on all ports.
            Optimal closing pressure determined per chip by incrementing control channel
            pressure in 0.5 bar steps.
Layer iii : Glass substrate; coated with human plasma fibronectin (250 ug/mL, Millipore)
            for beta2-integrin-mediated DC adhesion and 2D migration.

Inlets  : 9 reagent inlets, 1 cell loading inlet
Outlets : 3 waste outlets

Support channels connect reagent inlets with migration chambers. Flow lines pressurised
at 0.2-0.4 bar. All ports equipped with independently controllable PDMS membrane valves.


CELL TYPE
---------
Bone marrow-derived dendritic cells (DCs) from C57BL/6J mice (C57BL6 strain).
Cultured: GM-CSF hybridoma cell line supernatant (granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating
factor) in non-adhesive petri dish. Bone marrow extracted from femur and tibia; 4500 rpm
spin distally capped bones. 2 x 10^6 cells/10 mL R10 medium per dish.
  - Day 0: seed bone marrow
  - Day 4: add 10 mL R10 + 2 mL GM-CSF supernatant
  - Day 7: replace 10 mL with fresh R10 + 2 mL GM-CSF supernatant
  - Day 8-10: harvest; mature overnight with LPS (200 ng/mL)
Cell culture medium (R10): RPMI 1640 + 10% fetal calf serum + L-Glucose +
Penicillin/Streptomycin (all from Life Sciences, Austria).


REAGENTS
--------
Buffer               : PBS (phosphate-buffered saline)
Surface coating      : Human plasma fibronectin, 250 ug/mL (Millipore, Austria)
LAPAP anchor         : Biotin-4-fluorescein (B4F), 150 ug/mL (Sigma Aldrich, US)
Streptavidin adapter : Streptavidin-Cy3 (SA-Cy3), 10 ug/mL in PBS + 3% BSA (Sigma Aldrich, US)
Immobilized chemokine: CCL21 24-98 bio (biotinylated truncated murine CCL21, aa 24-98;
                       custom synthesized by ALMAC, Craigavon, UK); 250 ng/mL in PBS;
                       stock 25 ug/mL in PBS; stored at -20 C.
                       Truncated to remove basic C-terminal extension (aa 99-134) which causes
                       unspecific binding via heparan sulfate. Max surface density: 157+/-33
                       molecules/um2 (quantified by fluorescence intensity standard curve).
Soluble chemokine    : Murine CCL19 (R&D Systems, USA); 2.5 ug/mL in R10; stock 25 ug/mL in PBS.
Gradient tracer      : FITC-dextran 10 kDa, 200 ug/mL (Sigma Aldrich, US);
                       hydrodynamic radius 2.3 nm (comparable to CCL19: 1.7+/-0.4 nm,
                       CCL21: 1.9+/-0.4 nm).
Cell culture medium  : R10 (RPMI 1640 + 10% FCS + L-Glucose + Pen/Strep)
DC maturation        : LPS, 200 ng/mL


LAPAP SYSTEM
------------
Full name            : Laser-Assisted Protein Adsorption by Photobleaching
Laser                : 355 nm pulsed UV, steerable; pulse frequency 1 kHz
Objective            : Zeiss LD Plan Neo 20x/0.4 (long working distance)
Scanning             : High-speed galvanometric mirror pair; spot spacing ~half focus diameter
                       for continuous pattern; total light dose split into multiple pulses per
                       spot to average pulse-to-pulse power variability
Lateral resolution   : ~0.7 um
Dynamic range        : ~100:1
Pattern specification: Image file; pixel intensity values determine local light dose (bleaching dose)
Calibration          : Compensates for off-center numerical aperture drop and geometric
                       distortions from scan mirrors into objective back aperture
Pattern types        : Uniform patch (haptokinesis control), linear gradient (haptotaxis)
Reference            : Behrndt et al., Science 338, 257 (2012) for hardware description


GRADIENT GENERATION
-------------------
Soluble method       : Flow-free diffusion; source-sink flow-switching strategy
                       (Frank & Tay, Lab Chip 13, 1273, 2013;
                        Mehling et al., Lab Chip 15, 1276, 2015)
Source channel       : Sequentially refilled with R10 + CCL19 (2.5 ug/mL) + FITC-dextran
                       10 kDa (200 ug/mL) via short-end port
Sink channel         : Sequentially refilled with fresh R10 via opposite short-end port
Gradient build time  : ~2.5 h to reach peak CCL19 ~1.8 ug/mL (~190 nM)
CCR7/CCL19 Kd       : 10-100 nM (concentrations in gradient cover dissociation constant range
                       and are sufficient for G-protein loading and downstream signalling)
CCL19 regimes tested :
  CCL19_low    : 0-1.2 ug/mL -> chemokinesis (increased speed, undirected)
  CCL19_medium : 0-1.3 ug/mL -> directed chemotaxis (significantly increased directionality)
  CCL19_high   : 0-1.7 ug/mL -> highly directional chemotaxis (further augmented directionality)
Gradient monitoring  : FITC-dextran fluorescence; spatially averaged over centered
                       longitudinal section of chamber (Fig. 1d in paper)


IMAGING AND ANALYSIS
--------------------
Microscope     : Nikon Ti automated inverted microscope
Objectives     : 10x/NA 0.3 Air Plan Fluor Ph1; 20x/NA 0.5 Air Plan Fluor Ph1 (Nikon, Japan)
Camera         : Digital EMCCD (Hamamatsu C9100-02)
Incubator      : Stage-top; 37 C, 5% CO2, 90% humidity
Tracking area  : 300 x 200 um (corresponds to size of chemokine pattern)
Image software : NIS-AR (Nikon, Japan)
Cell tracking  : Fiji (Schindelin et al., Nat Meth 9, 676, 2012) + Manual Tracking plugin
                 (Cordelieres 2005); manual tracking
Data analysis  : Matlab 2013 (MathWorks Inc., US)


STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
--------------------
Directionality metric : <cos(Theta)>; Theta = angle between gradient direction and current
                        cell direction. Determined every 3 min (cells move ~1 cell diameter).
                        Events without any motion excluded.
Bootstrapping         : 200 resamplings with original sample size
Error bars            : Standard deviation of <cos(Theta)> from bootstrapping


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

ARM 1: HAPTOTAXIS (BB1 -> BB2/BB3 -> BB4)
------------------------------------------

Step 1 | Chip Priming with PBS
  BS variable : chipPrime
  Inputs      : PBS, PBS
  Operation   : mix
  Duration    : 5 min (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
  Pressure    : Flow lines at 0.2-0.4 bar
  Purpose     : Fill entire chip with PBS; determine optimal valve closing pressure
                per chip (0.5 bar increments in control channels).

Step 2 | Fibronectin Coating
  BS variable : fibronectinCoat -> coatedChamber -> chamberReady
  Inputs      : Fibronectin (250 ug/mL)
  Operation   : mix (coat) + mix (PBS flush 1 min) + mix (R10 flush 10 min)
  Duration    : 60 min coat (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); 1 min PBS flush (IR: 60.0 SECOND);
                10 min R10 flush (IR: 600.0 SECOND)
  Temperature : Room temperature
  Purpose     : Fibronectin acts as beta2-integrin ligand enabling DC adhesion and 2D
                migration on PDMS surface. Fibronectin in flow channels flushed with PBS
                after coating; chambers flushed with R10 for 10 min before use.

Step 3 | LAPAP - B4F Loading and PBS Wash
  BS variable : b4fLoaded -> b4fCoated
  Inputs      : PBS (wash, 10 s), B4F 150 ug/mL
  Operation   : mix (PBS wash) + mix (B4F loading 1 min)
  Duration    : 10 s PBS wash + 1 min B4F load (IR: 60.0 SECOND for b4fCoated)
  Purpose     : Chambers washed to remove unbound fibronectin. B4F loaded into assembled
                closed channels via reagent inlets; coats glass surface as LAPAP anchor.

Step 4 | LAPAP - UV Laser Gradient Writing
  BS variable : lapapPattern -> patternWashed
  Inputs      : PBS (used as placeholder in BS for laser event)
  Operation   : mix (abstracts laser scanning event); mix (PBS wash 10 s)
  Duration    : 20 min laser writing (IR: 1200.0 SECOND); 10 s PBS wash (IR: 10.0 SECOND)
  Laser       : 355 nm pulsed UV; Zeiss LD Plan Neo 20x/0.4; galvanometric mirrors;
                1 kHz pulse frequency; ~0.7 um lateral resolution; ~100:1 dynamic range
  Pattern     : Uniform patch (haptokinesis control) or linear gradient (haptotaxis);
                pixel intensity image specifies local light dose; spot spacing ~half focus
                diameter; light dose split into multiple pulses per spot.
  Purpose     : Covalent surface-deposition of B4F in arbitrary graded intensity patterns.
                Unbound B4F washed out with PBS (10 s) after writing.

Step 5 | Streptavidin-Cy3 Incubation
  BS variable : saCy3Bound -> saCy3Washed
  Inputs      : StreptavidinCy3 (10 ug/mL in PBS + 3% BSA)
  Operation   : mix (20 min incubation) + mix (PBS wash 10 s)
  Duration    : 20 min (IR: 1200.0 SECOND); 10 s PBS wash (IR: 10.0 SECOND)
  Temperature : Room temperature
  Purpose     : SA-Cy3 binds to photobleached B4F anchors in laser pattern. SA-Cy3
                fluorescence allows visualization and quantification of printed gradient.
                SA-Cy3 intensity correlates closely with anti-CCL21 antibody staining.

Step 6 | CCL21 24-98 bio Immobilization
  BS variable : ccl21Immobilized -> ccl21Washed
  Inputs      : CCL21_24_98_bio (250 ng/mL in PBS)
  Operation   : mix (30 min incubation at 37 C) + mix (PBS wash 10 s)
  Duration    : 30 min at 37 C (IR: 1800.0 SECOND); 10 s PBS wash (IR: 10.0 SECOND)
  Surface density : max 157 +/- 33 molecules/um2 (0.24 ug/mL equivalent unit volume)
  Supporting channels: kept under constant PBS flow (0.2 bar) to prevent non-specific
                adsorption of CCL21 outside cell culture chambers.
  Purpose     : Biotin tag on CCL21 C-terminus binds streptavidin anchors from LAPAP
                pattern. Truncated aa 24-98 avoids heparan-sulfate unspecific binding.

Step 7 | DC Loading into Migration Chambers
  BS variable : dcLoaded -> dcAdhered
  Inputs      : DendriticCells (10 x 10^6 cells/mL in R10), CellCultureMedium_R10
  Operation   : mix (1 min loading) + mix (30 min adhesion recovery)
  Duration    : 1 min loading (IR: 60.0 SECOND); 30 min recovery (IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Loading port: Short-end ports (produces distribution of cells along chamber length)
  Purpose     : DCs adhere loosely to fibronectin-coated PDMS. Round morphology with
                constant protrusions under no-chemokine conditions (undirected migration).

Step 8 | Haptotaxis Detection
  BS variable    : haptotaxisSignal
  Detection module: haptotaxisDetection
  Operation      : detect on dcAdhered
  Duration       : 100 min (IR: 6000.0 SECOND)
  Expected results:
    No chemokine     : Undirected migration; isotropic rose plot; round cell morphology
    CCL21 patch      : Haptokinesis; increased migration speed; no directed migration;
                       tighter adhesion to substrate; isotropic rose plot
    CCL21 gradient   : Haptotaxis; directed migration toward high [CCL21]; rose plot
                       asymmetric toward gradient direction
  Branch:
    PASS (BB2): haptotaxisSignal > 0 -> dispose(dcAdhered); fall through to BB4
    FAIL (BB3): haptotaxisSignal <= 0 -> drain(dcAdhered); jump to BB4


ARM 2: CHEMOTAXIS (BB4 -> BB5/BB6 -> BB7)
------------------------------------------

Step 9 | DC Loading for Chemotaxis Arm
  BS variable : dcLoaded2
  Inputs      : DendriticCells, CellCultureMedium_R10
  Operation   : mix
  Duration    : 30 min adhesion (IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Notes       : Fresh DCs loaded into fibronectin-coated chambers without CCL21 patterning.

Step 10 | CCL19 Soluble Gradient Generation
  BS variable : ccl19FitcMix -> gradientSetup -> ccl19Gradient
  Inputs      : CCL19 (2.5 ug/mL), FITCDextran_10kDa (200 ug/mL), CellCultureMedium_R10
  Operation   : mix (source preparation 5 s) + mix (gradient setup 5 s) +
                mix (gradient build 150 min)
  Duration    : 150 min (IR: 9000.0 SECOND)
  Peak CCL19  : ~1.8 ug/mL (~190 nM) after 2.5 h
  CCL19 regimes:
    CCL19_low    (0-1.2 ug/mL) : chemokinesis; cells faster but undirected
    CCL19_medium (0-1.3 ug/mL) : significantly increased directionality toward high [CCL19]
    CCL19_high   (0-1.7 ug/mL) : further augmented directionality
  Control        : R10 medium from both ends -> undirected migration (Fig. 4a)
  Notes       : FITC-dextran traces gradient profile. CCL19 concentrations cover CCR7/CCL19
                Kd (10-100 nM) and trigger G-protein loading and downstream CCR7 signalling.

Step 11 | Chemotaxis Detection
  BS variable     : chemotaxisSignal
  Detection module: chemotaxisDetection
  Operation       : detect on ccl19Gradient
  Duration        : 150 min (IR: 9000.0 SECOND)
  Analysis        : Directionality <cos(Theta)> vs. chamber position (= local CCL19
                    concentration) and time; Fig. 4e in paper
  Branch:
    PASS (BB5): chemotaxisSignal > 0 -> dispose(ccl19Gradient); fall through to BB7
    FAIL (BB6): chemotaxisSignal <= 0 -> drain(ccl19Gradient); jump to BB7


ARM 3: COMPETING GRADIENTS (BB7 -> BB8/BB9 -> BB10)
-----------------------------------------------------

Step 12 | LAPAP Re-Patterning and DC Loading (Competing Arm)
  BS variables  : b4fLoad2 -> lapapWrite2 -> saCoat2 -> ccl21Coat2 -> dcOnCCL21 -> dcRecovered
  Inputs        : PBS, B4F_BiotinFluorescein, StreptavidinCy3, CCL21_24_98_bio,
                  DendriticCells, CellCultureMedium_R10
  Operation     : Sequential mix steps (full LAPAP patterning protocol repeated)
  Durations     : B4F load 1 min (IR: 60.0 SECOND); LAPAP write 20 min (IR: 1200.0 SECOND);
                  SA-Cy3 20 min (IR: 1200.0 SECOND); CCL21 immobilization 30 min
                  (IR: 1800.0 SECOND); DC loading 1 min (IR: 60.0 SECOND);
                  DC recovery 30 min (IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  CCL21 region  : Lower and middle area of migration chamber; max ~160 molecules/um2
                  (= 0.24 ug/mL equivalent unit volume; Supplemental Figure S6)
  Notes         : DCs positioned to include both CCL21-gradient regions. 30 min recovery
                  before CCL19 gradient generation (Supplemental Movie S3).

Step 13 | Opposing CCL19 Soluble Gradient Superimposition
  BS variables  : conditionAGrad (CCL19_low/CCL21); conditionBGrad (CCL19_medium/CCL21)
  Inputs        : CCL19, FITCDextran_10kDa, CellCultureMedium_R10 (x2 sets)
  Operation     : mix (x2 conditions run in parallel in separate CCL21-gradient regions)
  Duration      : 100 min per condition (IR: 6000.0 SECOND each)
  Gradient direction: Opposing -- CCL19 source at one short end; CCL21 gradient in
                  lower/middle area pointing in opposite direction
  Conditions:
    Condition A (CCL19_low/CCL21):
      CCL19 range : 0-1.0 ug/mL
      Expected    : DC migration toward haptotactic CCL21 gradient at all time points
                    (haptotaxis dominant; Fig. 5a); CCL21 signal not overridden
    Condition B (CCL19_medium/CCL21):
      CCL19 range : 0-1.2 ug/mL
      Expected    : DC migration switches toward chemotactic CCL19 after gradient build-up
                    (chemotaxis dominant, overrides haptotaxis; Fig. 5b)
  Imaging       : SA-Cy3 (CCL21, red) and FITC-dextran (CCL19, green) channels imaged
                  simultaneously to confirm gradient orientation and overlap (Fig. 5a/b)
  Time windows  : Directionality tracked across 0-33 min, 33-66 min, 66-100 min (Fig. 5c)

Step 14 | Competing Gradient Detection and Directionality Resolution
  BS variable     : competingSignal
  Detection module: competingGradientDetection
  Operation       : detect on conditionAGrad
  Duration        : 100 min (IR: 6000.0 SECOND)
  Key result      : Low CCL19 (~0-1 ug/mL) -> haptotaxis dominant;
                    medium CCL19 (~0-1.2 ug/mL) -> chemotaxis dominant;
                    ~1 ug/mL threshold for chemotaxis to override immobilized CCL21 signal.
  Interpretation  : Soluble CCL19 can override immobilized CCL21 haptotactic signal above
                    a concentration threshold. Mechanism may relate to comparable CCR7
                    binding affinities of CCL19 and CCL21, or differential activation of
                    CCR7 binding sites (single amino acid mutation studies show CCL19 and
                    CCL21 can differentially affect receptor activation; Ott et al., 2004).
  Branch:
    PASS (BB8): competingSignal > 0 -> dispose(conditionAGrad, conditionBGrad)
    FAIL (BB9): competingSignal <= 0 -> drain(conditionAGrad, conditionBGrad); jump BB10

                         fall-through to BB4 implicitly. Correct CFG behaviour for this
                         compiler version. Same pattern observed in reference .bs files.
