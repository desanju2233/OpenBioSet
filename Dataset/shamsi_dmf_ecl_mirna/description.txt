PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Electrochemiluminescence on Digital Microfluidics for microRNA Analysis
Source Link  : https://opensiuc.lib.siu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?referer=&httpsredir=1&article=1004&context=chem_pubs
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.bios.2015.10.036
Journal      : Biosensors and Bioelectronics, 2015, PII: S0956-5663(15)30500-5
Platform     : DMF (digital microfluidics) with integrated ECL detection on ITO-coated top-plate (EWOD/DMFB)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first integration of electrochemiluminescence (ECL) detection with
digital microfluidics (DMF), applied to a magnetic particle-based oligonucleotide hybridization
assay for microRNA analysis. The DMF-ECL system uses sample volumes of ~1.8 uL and targets
microRNA-143 (miRNA-143), a molecule down-regulated in some aggressive cancers, via its
synthetic deoxyribose analogue ds-143.

Magnetic particles pre-coated with single-stranded probe DNA (ss-143) capture complementary
target sequences from solution. The intercalating luminophore [Ru(Phen)3]Cl2 binds double-
stranded DNA formed on the bead surface after hybridization. Tripropylamine (TPA) is then
added as co-reactant. The final bead suspension is actuated to ITO working electrodes on the
DMF top-plate, where 1.5 V DC excites ECL emission detected by a photomultiplier tube (PMT).
ECL signal is proportional to hybridized target; the system achieves an absolute limit of
detection of 1.5 femtomoles (CLOD = 1.1 nM) and discriminates single-nucleotide mismatches
with high specificity. The assay also detects miRNA-143 expression differences between
MCF-7 (less aggressive) and MDA-MB-231 (more aggressive) breast cancer cell lysates.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Configuration : Two-plate DMF (top + bottom plates)

Top plate:
  - ITO-coated glass substrate; remainder of ITO serves as DMF ground electrode
  - 4 x bare (transparent) ITO circular working electrodes (WE), diameter 800 um
  - 2 x Ag/Au/ITO square counter/pseudoreference electrodes (CE/RE), 560 x 560 um
  - Teflon-AF hydrophobic coating globally applied; apertures formed above each WE and CE/RE
  - WEs connected to contact pads via patterned ITO wires
  - Ag/Au/ITO CE/RE formed using gold adhesion layer for mechanical stability

Bottom plate:
  - Glass substrate
  - Chromium array of DMF driving electrodes
  - Parylene-C dielectric layer
  - Teflon-AF hydrophobic layer

Assembly:
  - Top and bottom plates biased by AC voltages (open-source DropBot controller) for droplet
    actuation (move, dispense, mix, split)
  - DC voltages applied between ITO WE and Ag/Au/ITO CE/RE via commercial potentiostat for
    electrochemistry and ECL measurements
  - Permanent magnet positioned below bottom plate for Dynabead immobilization
  - PMT suspended above top-plate to collect ECL photons

Droplet volume : ~1.8 uL per unit droplet


PRE-ASSAY STEPS (off-chip, not encoded in BioScript)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Probe immobilization: biotinylated ss-143 (21-mer, deoxyribose analogue of miRNA-143)
   immobilized on Dynabeads M-280 Streptavidin via avidin-biotin chemistry.
   Details in supplementary information of source paper.

2. ITO electrode cleaning: cathodic electrochemical cleaning of ITO WEs by cyclic voltammetry
   (10 cycles, -1 V to 0 V, 100 mV/s) in 20 uL PBS (1x) positioned on DMF top-plates using
   ITO WE and Ag/Au/ITO CE/RE. Removes adsorbed organics and reduces oxide layer.


REAGENTS
--------
MagneticParticles : Dynabeads M-280 Streptavidin (Invitrogen Dynal AS, Oslo, Norway)
                    10 mg/mL, ~6-7e8 particles/mL, diameter 2.8 um, in PBS with 0.1% BSA
                    and 0.02% NaN3. Pre-coated with biotinylated ss-143 probe DNA off-chip.
                    Supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68 v/v prior to DMF use.

TargetSequence    : ds-143 (synthetic deoxyribose analogue of miRNA-143) in hybridization
                    buffer at test concentration (0, 10, 100, or 1000 nM).
                    Variants: 143-m1 (single nucleotide mismatch), 143-m2 (double nucleotide
                    mismatch), nc-145 (fully non-complementary, deoxyribose analogue of
                    miRNA-145, used as negative control).
                    For cell lysate assays: MCF-7 or MDA-MB-231 lysate diluted 1:10 in sample
                    dilution buffer (pH 8.0: 7.7 mM Tris-base, 12.2 mM Tris-HCl, 200 mM NaCl,
                    5 mM MgCl2, 4% BSA w/v, 0.05% Pluronic L64 w/v).
                    Supplier: Bio Basic Canada Inc. (Markham, ON); HPLC-purified; stock 50 uM
                    in DNase/RNase-free ultrapure water (Invitrogen); working dilutions in
                    respective buffer. All supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68.

HybridizationBuffer: 20 mM Tris-HCl, 0.2 M NaCl, 5 mM MgCl2, pH 8.0.
                    Supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68.

RuPhen3Cl2        : 200 uM tris(phenanthroline)ruthenium(II) chloride ([Ru(Phen)3]Cl2) in
                    PBS (1x), pH 7.5. Supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68.
                    Supplier: Sigma Chemical (Oakville, ON, Canada).
                    ECL luminophore: each Ru(Phen)3(2+) ion intercalates into 4 base pairs of
                    dsDNA double helix; does not bind ssDNA or RNA. Signal tracks dsDNA amount.

PBS               : Phosphate buffered saline (1x), pH 7.5. Supplemented with 0.05% Pluronic F68.

TPA               : 20 mM tripropylamine in PBS (1x), pH 7.5. Supplemented with 0.05%
                    Pluronic F68. ECL co-reactant: oxidized at ITO WE simultaneously with
                    Ru(Phen)3(2+); TPA(+) radical reduces luminophore to excited state,
                    which emits photons (~610 nm) on return to ground state.

PluronicF68       : 0.05% v/v additive in all above solutions prior to DMF use. Reduces
                    droplet fouling of Teflon-AF hydrophobic surface.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Magnetic Particle Dispense and Initial Separation
  Inputs     : MagneticParticles (3 unit droplets, ~1.8 uL each)
  Operations : dispense x3 -> mix x2 (merge to ~5.4 uL) -> heat/magnet 30s -> dispose
  BioScript  : beads1, beads2, beads3 -> beadsMerge1 -> beadsMerged -> [magnet] -> dispose
  IR timing  : mix @ 5.0 SECOND; heat @ 25.0 CELSIUS @ 30.0 SECOND
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "three unit droplets containing magnetic particles were
               dispensed from their reservoir and merged, and the particles were separated
               from the supernatant." Permanent magnet immobilizes Dynabeads; storage buffer
               supernatant actuated away. heat at 25c for 30s abstracts magnet hold time.
               For cell lysate assays: 5 unit droplets used instead of 3.

Step 2 | Target Hybridization (15 min)
  Inputs     : TargetSequence (2 unit droplets), MagneticParticles (1 unit, pellet model)
  Operations : dispense x2 -> mix (merge) -> dispense pellet -> mix 15m -> heat/magnet 30s
               -> dispose supernatant
  BioScript  : target1, target2 -> targetMerged; pelletForHybrid -> hybridDroplet -> [magnet]
               -> dispose
  IR timing  : mix @ 900.0 SECOND (15 min); heat @ 25.0 CELSIUS @ 30.0 SECOND
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "two unit droplets of target sequence solution were
               dispensed, merged, and delivered to the immobilized particles for resuspension
               and incubation for 15 min." ds-143 hybridizes to ss-143 probe on bead surface.
               pelletForHybrid re-dispense models bead pellet resuspended by incoming droplet
               (magnet removed; electrode actuation drives resuspension).

Step 3 | Hybridization Buffer Wash x4
  Inputs     : HybridizationBuffer (1 unit droplet per cycle), MagneticParticles (1 unit per cycle)
  Operations : Per cycle: dispense pellet + dispense buf -> mix 1m -> heat/magnet 30s ->
               dispose; repeated 4 times explicitly
  BioScript  : pelletW1-4, washBuf1-4, washMix1-4 (4 independent wash loops)
  IR timing  : mix @ 60.0 SECOND; heat @ 25.0 CELSIUS @ 30.0 SECOND (x4)
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "particles were washed four times in series by
               re-suspending and mixing with one unit droplet of hybridization buffer
               followed by magnetic separation from the supernatant." Removes unbound
               and non-specifically adsorbed target sequences.

Step 4 | Ru(Phen)3Cl2 Intercalation Labeling (2 min)
  Inputs     : RuPhen3Cl2 (2 unit droplets), MagneticParticles (1 unit, pellet model)
  Operations : dispense x2 -> mix (merge 5s) -> dispense pellet -> mix 2m ->
               heat/magnet 30s -> dispose supernatant
  BioScript  : ruDrop1, ruDrop2 -> ruMerged; pelletForRu -> ruLabelMix -> [magnet] -> dispose
  IR timing  : mix @ 5.0 SECOND (merge); mix @ 120.0 SECOND (incubation);
               heat @ 25.0 CELSIUS @ 30.0 SECOND
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "two unit droplets of [Ru(Phen)3]Cl2 solution were
               dispensed, merged, and delivered to the particles, where they were re-suspended
               and mixed for 2 min." ECL signal is proportional to dsDNA intercalation level
               (marker for hybridized target amount).

Step 5 | PBS Wash x2
  Inputs     : PBS (1 unit droplet per cycle), MagneticParticles (1 unit per cycle)
  Operations : Per cycle: dispense pellet + dispense PBS -> mix 1m -> heat/magnet 30s ->
               dispose; repeated 2 times
  BioScript  : pelletPW1-2, pbsWash1-2, pbsMix1-2
  IR timing  : mix @ 60.0 SECOND; heat @ 25.0 CELSIUS @ 30.0 SECOND (x2)
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "particles were washed two times with 2 droplets of PBS
               as in step 3." Removes excess unbound Ru(Phen)3(2+) before TPA addition.

Step 6 | TPA Co-reactant Addition (1 min)
  Inputs     : TPA (2 unit droplets), MagneticParticles (1 unit, pellet model)
  Operations : dispense x2 -> mix (merge 5s) -> dispense pellet -> mix 1m
  BioScript  : tpaDrop1, tpaDrop2 -> tpaMerged; pelletForTPA -> finalDroplet
  IR timing  : mix @ 5.0 SECOND (merge); mix @ 60.0 SECOND (incubation)
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "two droplets of TPA solution were dispensed, merged, and
               delivered to the particles, where they were re-suspended and mixed for 1 min."
               TPA and Ru(Phen)3(2+) are oxidized simultaneously at the ITO WE; TPA(+)
               radical reduces luminophore to excited state (~610 nm emission).

Step 7 | ECL Measurement
  Inputs     : finalDroplet (bead suspension in TPA + Ru(Phen)3(2+))
  Operation  : detect eclDetection on finalDroplet for 45s
  BioScript  : eclSignal = detect eclDetection on finalDroplet for 45s
  IR timing  : detect @ 45.0 SECOND
  Applied voltage : 1.5 V DC (ITO WE vs Ag/Au/ITO CE/RE)
  Duration   : 45 s total; maximum ECL counts recorded within first 10 s (CV ~10%)
  LOD        : CLOD = 1.1 nM; absolute LOD = 1.5 femtomoles
  Notes      : Paper M&M Sec 2.3: "the final droplet containing TPA and Ru(Phen)3(2+)-labeled
               DNA modified particles was actuated to the ECL electrodes on the top-plate for
               ECL measurements." 1.5 V chosen from cyclic voltammogram plateau (1.4-1.5 V).
               ECL signal decays over time due to irreversible luminophore decomposition;
               maximum within 10 s gives reproducible signal. Two parallel assays run
               simultaneously; total assay time ~40 min.


ECL QC GATE (if/else branch)
-----------------------------
  Condition  : eclSignal > 0
  Pass (BB2) : eclSignal > 0 -> hybridization detected (ECL above blank); dispose finalDroplet
  Fail (BB3) : eclSignal <= 0 -> no signal (below LOD or reagent failure); drain finalDroplet
  Cleanup    : dispose eclSignal (BB4, terminal)
  IR note    : drain in .bs false-branch lowered to dispose() in IR -- known compiler bug;
               .bs source is correct as written.


SELECTIVITY RESULTS
-------------------
  ds-143 vs 143-m1 (single mismatch) : p = 0.002 (distinguishable)
  ds-143 vs 143-m2 (double mismatch) : p = 0.001 (distinguishable)
  143-m1 vs 143-m2                   : p = 0.005 (significant difference)
  ds-143 vs nc-145 (non-complementary): p = 0.2  (indistinguishable from blank, as expected)
  Mechanism: mismatch (1) decreases hybridization efficiency (steric advantage for surface-
  bound probes) and (2) introduces structural distortion reducing Ru(Phen)3(2+) intercalation.


CELL LYSATE RESULTS
-------------------
  MDA-MB-231 ECL counts : 41,000 +/- 6,500 (CV 16%)
  MCF-7 ECL counts      : 95,000 +/- 37,100 (CV 39%)
  Background ECL counts : 17,000 +/- 8,000
  MDA-MB-231 vs MCF-7   : p = 0.03
  Conclusion: MDA-MB-231 (more aggressive, higher metastatic propensity) shows significantly
  lower ECL signal than MCF-7, consistent with down-regulation of miRNA-143 in aggressive
  breast cancer phenotypes.

Bead pellet abstraction:
  After each magnetic separation (dispose of supernatant), the bead pellet physically remains
  on-device. BioScript has no 'pellet' primitive; fresh dispense from MagneticParticles
  at each subsequent step models the retained pellet being resuspended by the incoming reagent
  droplet. This is the established pellet re-dispense pattern for this compiler.
