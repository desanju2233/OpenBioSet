PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Single-Cell Chemoproteomics Identifies Metastatic Activity Signatures in Breast Cancer
Source Link  : https://www.science.org/doi/epdf/10.1126/sciadv.adp2622
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adp2622
Journal      : Science Advances, 2024, Vol. 10, Article eadp2622
Authors      : Kavya Smitha Pillai, Olivia Laxton, Gang Li, Jing Lin, Olga Karginova,
               Rita Nanda, Olufunmilayo I. Olopade, Savaş Tay, Raymond E. Moellering
Platform     : Single-Cell Activity-Dependent Proximity Ligation (scADPL) coupled to
               valve-based push-up PDMS microfluidic chip with five-chamber assay units


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated chemoproteomic microfluidic platform, scADPL, for
multiplexed endogenous protein activity measurements at single-cell resolution. Unlike
mRNA or bulk protein abundance methods, scADPL directly reports on the active subpopulation
of the proteome, capturing posttranslational regulation invisible to genomic or transcriptomic
approaches.

The platform integrates two components:
  1. Activity-based chemical probes (Cat-Bio for cathepsins; FP-Bio for serine hydrolases)
     that covalently label the active sites of target enzyme families in live cells.
  2. Activity-Dependent Proximity Ligation (ADPL) chemistry that converts probe-protein
     labelling events into amplifiable, barcoded oligonucleotide amplicons, quantified
     by qPCR or ddPCR.

These are coupled to a valve-based PDMS microfluidic chip that automates:
  (i)  Single-cell isolation and brightfield/fluorescence imaging
  (ii) Nanoliter-volume manipulation to achieve optimal proteome concentrations for ADPL
  (iii) Dead volume minimisation for maximum sensitivity and reproducibility

The protocol is organised into four experimental phases:
  PHASE 1 : Single-plex validation of CTSL activity (ddPCR) and NCEH1 target specificity
            (qPCR + JW480 inhibitor) in SKOV3 single cells
  PHASE 2 : Multiplexed Ag-6 panel (NCEH1, MGLL, CTSL, FAAH, CTSB, uPA) profiling in
            SKOV3 single cells with reproducibility and bulk sADPL concordance validation
  PHASE 3 : Heterogeneity profiling across cell lines (HEK293T vs SKOV3;
            aggressive SKOV3 vs non-aggressive OVCAR3 ovarian cancer)
  PHASE 4 : Patient-derived organoid (PDO) profiling from TNBC primary and metastatic
            tumours (between-patient) and ER+ pleural effusion / lymph node (within-patient)

Key quantitative outcomes:
  - sADPL detection threshold: 10 pg whole proteome; single HeLa cell ~2 ng total protein
  - Average scADPL CTSL signal: 54-fold above vehicle-only control (ddPCR)
  - Average scADPL NCEH1 signal: ~64-fold above vehicle-only control (qPCR)
  - JW480 (selective NCEH1 inhibitor, 2 uM): >90% NCEH1 signal inhibition;
    no significant effect on remaining five Ag-6 targets
  - Multiplexed Ag-6 reproducibility (day 1 vs day 2): Pearson r = 0.9981
  - scADPL vs bulk sADPL correlation: Pearson r = 0.9288
  - PCA of SKOV3 vs OVCAR3 scADPL profiles: PC1 = 84.67%, PC2 = 8.37%


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Type       : Valve-based push-up PDMS microfluidic chip
Material   : Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS), 10:1 base:curing agent
Bonding    : Air/oxygen plasma-assisted; PDMS-to-PDMS and PDMS-to-glass
Glass slide: 127.76 x 85.48 x 1 mm

Photoresist layer heights (SU-8 3025 / AZ40XT):
  - 18 mm AZ40XT : dome valve layer (rounded after overnight reflow)
  - 22 mm SU-8-3025 : first channel feature layer (spun at 1372g)
  - 70 mm SU-8-3025 : thick fluidic features (two coats; first at 1372g, second at 448g)

Chip configuration (push-up):
  - Fluidic layer (~72 g PDMS): thick layer; peeled from fluidic mold and bonded on top
    of control layer. Treated with oxygen plasma 18 s (860 mtorr; Harrick Plasma).
  - Control layer (11 g PDMS): thin layer; spun at 448g for 1 min onto control mold;
    bonded to clean glass slide.
  - Assembly incubated at 80 C overnight after bonding.

Punch sizes:
  - Retrieval outlets: 1930 um inner diameter (CR0950765N13R4, Syneoco)
  - All other inlets/outlets: 710 um inner diameter (CR0350255N20R4, Syneoco)

Five chamber zones per assay unit:
  I   - Input fluid artery
  II  - Single-cell trapping chamber
  III - Cell lysis chamber
  IV  - Proximity barcoding element incubation (whole proteome)
  V   - Proximity barcode ligation

Lithography tool: Heidelberg MLA 150 Direct Write Lithographer
                  (Heidelberg Instruments Mikrotechnik GmbH, Germany)
Wafer           : 4-inch photoresist-coated silicon


CELL LINES
----------
SKOV3       : Human ovarian cancer (high-aggressiveness reference line)
              DMEM-F12 + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep; 37 C, 5% CO2
OVCAR3      : Human ovarian cancer (low-aggressiveness)
              DMEM-F12 + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep; 37 C, 5% CO2
HEK293T     : Human embryonic kidney (non-cancer control; tissue mismatch)
              RPMI 1640 + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep; 37 C, 5% CO2
MCF10A      : Non-cancerous breast epithelium (TNBC continuum baseline)
MDA-MB-231  : Aggressive breast cancer (post-EMT); derived from pleural effusion
BM1         : Formed metastatic breast lesion (kindly provided by Dr. Marsha Rosner,
              University of Chicago); RPMI 1640 + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep
HCC70       : Normal breast cancer cell line; RPMI 1640 + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep
PDOs        : Patient-derived organoids from:
              - 6 TNBC patients (primary and liver/lung/skin metastases)
              - 1 ER+ patient (pleural effusion L, pleural effusion R, lymph node met)
              IRB protocol 16352A; all samples deidentified; informed consent obtained.
              PDOs grown in BC PDO media (ref. 49); tested for mycoplasma before use.

All lines tested negative for mycoplasma contamination.
All lines except BM1 procured from ATCC; BM1 not STR profiled.


CHEMICAL PROBES AND DETECTION CHEMISTRY
-----------------------------------------

Activity Probes (applied off-chip to live cells before loading):
  Cat-Bio (cathepsin family-wide):
    Concentration  : 5 uM
    Incubation     : 2 h at 37 C in complete media
    Targets        : CTSL (cathepsin L), CTSB (cathepsin B)
    Chemistry      : Biotin-tagged covalent cysteine protease probe

  FP-Bio (serine hydrolase family-wide):
    Concentration  : 2 uM
    Incubation     : 30 min at 37 C in complete media
    Targets        : NCEH1, MGLL, FAAH, uPA
    Chemistry      : Fluorophosphonate biotin; labels active-site serine covalently

ADPL Detection Reagents (added on-chip at Chamber IV):
  Streptavidin-oligo conjugate (SA-oligo) : 4000 pM; binds biotin tag on probe-protein complex
  Antibody-oligo conjugate (ab-oligo)     : 200 pM per target; six antibodies for Ag-6 panel

  Antibodies used:
    NCEH1 : NCEH1 polyclonal antibody (in-house; refs 33, 36)
    CTSB  : Abcam ab58802
    FAAH  : Abcam ab54615
    MGLL  : Abcam no. 24701
    uPA   : Abcam no. 24121
    CTSL  : R&D Systems AF952-SP
    RPE   : Abcam no. 34721 (spike-in control antibody)

Proximity Ligation (Chamber V):
  Splint oligonucleotide : 100 nM; bridges SA-oligo and ab-oligo when both bound to
                           the same probe-protein complex
  Ampligase              : 0.05 U (Lucigen E0001-5D3); seals nick in ligation buffer
  Ligation buffer        : 0.3 mM NAD+, 10 mM DTT, 20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.3,
                           50 mM KCl, 1.5 mM MgCl2
  Ligation               : 40 C for 45 min (2700 s)

Lysis Buffer (MCP):
  25 mM Tris pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA, 1 mM EGTA,
  0.5% Triton X-100, 0.5% sodium deoxycholate
  Selected after optimisation against Pierce, CST, and TM buffers (fig. S2)

Spike-In Control:
  R-phycoerythrin (RPE): Thermo Fisher Scientific P801
  Added at lysis step; used for inter-cell normalisation
  Outliers: cells with RPE values >2 SD from mean excluded from analysis


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

PHASE 1 | Single-Plex Validation

Step 1 | Device Priming
  Reagents    : PBS, Pluronics F-127
  Operation   : mix 5 min (300 s)
  Purpose     : 2% Pluronics F-127 in PBS; passivates PDMS channel surfaces,
                reduces non-specific cell adhesion before loading

Step 2 | Off-Chip Probe Labelling (SKOV3, single-plex)
  Cat-Bio arm : SKOV3 + Cat-Bio 5 uM; mix 5 s; heat 37 C, 120 min (7200 s)
  FP-Bio arm  : SKOV3 + FP-Bio 2 uM; mix 5 s; heat 37 C, 30 min (1800 s)
  Wash        : PBS wash after labelling; resuspend 50,000-100,000 cells/mL in PBS

Step 3 | On-Chip Single-Cell Trapping (Chamber II)
  Operation   : mix 2 s (abstracts pressure-driven hydrodynamic single-cell capture)
  Notes       : One cell captured per assay chamber; RPE spike-in dispensed at this stage

Step 4 | On-Chip Cell Lysis (Chamber III)
  Operation   : MCP lysis buffer added; mix 60 s; RPE spike added; mix 5 s
  Notes       : Nanoliter dead volume minimised vs bulk sADPL for maximum sensitivity

Step 5 | On-Chip Proximity Barcoding (Chamber IV)
  Operation   : SA-oligo (4000 pM) + ab-oligo (200 pM) premixed 5 s; added to lysate;
                mix 120 s; heat 25 C, 120 min (7200 s)
  Notes       : Both recognition elements bind same probe-protein complex in proximity

Step 6 | On-Chip Proximity Ligation (Chamber V)
  Operation   : Splint oligo added; mix 2 s; ligation buffer added; mix 2 s;
                heat 40 C, 45 min (2700 s)
  Product     : Ligated barcoded amplicons

Step 7 | Off-Chip Amplicon Retrieval and Preamplification
  Flush       : 9 uL water flush off-chip
  Preamplify  : 11 uL reaction (2.5x buffer, 100 nM primers, 200 uM dNTPs,
                9 U Phusion high-fidelity DNA polymerase; NEB M0503L); 18 cycles
  IR abstraction: mix 18m (1080.0 SECOND in IR; cycle count not supported by compiler)

Step 8 | CTSL Detection by ddPCR
  Module      : ddPCRQuantification
  Duration    : 30 s detection window
  Expected    : 54-fold above vehicle-only control (Cat-Bio vs DMSO; Fig. 1B; n=2)
  QC gate     : ctslSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB2); ctslSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB3)

Step 9 | NCEH1 Specificity Validation: JW480 Pre-Treatment
  Cells       : SKOV3; JW480 2 uM pre-treatment 30 min at 37 C (1800 s)
  Probe       : FP-Bio 2 uM, 30 min at 37 C (1800 s)
  On-chip     : Full scADPL workflow (trap -> lyse -> barcode -> ligate -> preamplify)
  Module      : qPCRQuantification (selected over ddPCR from this step forward;
                higher SNR; amenable to multiplexing)
  Expected    : >90% NCEH1-specific signal suppression vs uninhibited control;
                no significant effect on other five Ag-6 targets (Fig. 1C, Fig. 2B)
  QC gate     : nceh1Signal > 0 -> dispose (BB5); nceh1Signal <= 0 -> drain (BB6)


PHASE 2 | Multiplexed Ag-6 Panel (SKOV3, n=8 single cells per condition)

Step 10 | Dual-Probe Pulse Labelling
  Cells       : SKOV3; FP-Bio 2 uM 30 min at 37 C; then Cat-Bio 5 uM added,
                additional 90 min at 37 C (both probes present for final 30 min overlap)
  IR          : labelledFP_Ag6 heat 1800 s; labelledDual heat 5400 s at 37 C

Step 11 | On-Chip Multiplexed scADPL
  Same chamber-by-chamber workflow as Steps 3-7 above.
  Six orthogonal Taqman barcode channels in forward primer sequences enable
  simultaneous target-specific qPCR quantification from the same single-cell lysate.
  All six measurements per isolated cell (n=8 cells; Fig. 2A).

Step 12 | Six-Channel qPCR Quantification
  Instrument  : CFX384 Real-Time System (Bio-Rad); 384-well plates
  Mix         : Luna Universal Probe qPCR Master Mix; 0.9 uM primers, 0.45 uM Taqman probe
  Module      : qPCRQuantification; 30 s detection window
  Expected fold-changes vs no-probe DMSO:
    NCEH1 : >20-fold   MGLL : >20-fold   CTSL : >20-fold
    FAAH  : detectable  CTSB : detectable  uPA  : detectable
  QC gate : ag6Signal > 0 -> dispose (BB8); ag6Signal <= 0 -> drain (BB9)


PHASE 3 | Cell-Line Heterogeneity Profiling

Step 13 | Dual-Probe Labelling of HEK293T and OVCAR3
  HEK293T and OVCAR3 each labelled identically to Step 10.
  Processed on separate chip runs; n > 10 single cells per target per cell line.

Step 14 | On-Chip scADPL for HEK293T and OVCAR3
  Full scADPL workflow (Steps 3-7) for each cell line independently.

Step 15 | qPCR + Fluorescence Imaging Readout; PCA Analysis
  Module      : fluorescenceImaging (abstracts PCA + heatmap analysis; 30 s)
  Normalisation:
    Fig. 3A   : Activity normalised to HEK293T reference population
    Fig. 3B   : Activity normalised to OVCAR3 reference population
    Both      : RPE internal control per cell
  Results:
    SKOV3 vs HEK293T : All six Ag-6 targets significantly higher in SKOV3
                       (p <= 0.0001 to 0.03; Fig. 3A)
    SKOV3 vs OVCAR3  : Five of six targets significant; NCEH1 highest fold-change (Fig. 3B)
    mRNA vs activity : FAAH and CTSB show discordant mRNA vs protein activity profiles
                       (Fig. 3C; Human Protein Atlas mRNA data)
    PCA              : SKOV3 vs OVCAR3 resolved; PC1 = 84.67%, PC2 = 8.37% (Fig. 3F)
    Heatmaps         : Per-cell Z-score activity profiles reveal intra-population
                       heterogeneity across all six Ag-6 targets (Fig. 3D/E)
  QC gate : heteroSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB11); heteroSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB12)


PHASE 4 | Patient-Derived Organoid Profiling

Step 16 | PDO Preparation and Anti-CD45-FITC Immune Cell Staining
  Protocol    : IRB 16352A; fresh core biopsy or pleural effusion specimens
                enzymatically digested (1 h for biopsy) or centrifuged (450g, 10 min
                for pleural effusion); Matrigel 24-well culture to 50-100 mm;
                Dispase 37 C 20 min -> TrypLE 37 C 5-10 min -> 2% FBS stop ->
                40 mm strainer -> 450g 5 min -> 500 uL BC PDO media on ice
  Staining    : FITC-conjugated anti-CD45 antibody; 30 min on ice (4 C, 1800 s)
  Purpose     : CD45+ immune cells identified by on-chip FITC imaging and excluded
                from scADPL analysis; only CD45-FITC-negative tumour cells processed
                (fig. S6 in paper)

Step 17 | Dual-Probe Labelling of PDO Single-Cell Suspension
  Same as Step 10: FP-Bio 30 min at 37 C (1800 s);
  Cat-Bio added, 90 min at 37 C (5400 s)

Step 18 | On-Chip Trapping with Tumour-Cell Imaging Gate
  Trap        : mix 2 s (hydrodynamic capture)
  Imaging QC  : detect fluorescenceImaging 10 s; confirms FITC-negative (tumour) cell
                in chamber before proceeding to lysis

Step 19 | On-Chip scADPL for PDO Tumour Single Cells
  Full scADPL workflow: lyse 60 s -> RPE spike 5 s -> barcode 7200 s at 25 C ->
  ligate 2700 s at 40 C -> preamplify 1080 s

Step 20 | Ag-6 qPCR on PDO Single Cells
  Module      : qPCRQuantification; 30 s
  Normalisation: Z-scores calculated within each target, normalised to:
    TNBC      : PDO1 (primary tumour reference)
    ER+       : Lymph node metastasis reference
    Both      : RPE spike-in per cell
  Results:
    TNBC PDOs : Higher Ag-6 in all metastatic vs primary tumours;
                highest activity in liver metastases (PDO3, PDO4; Fig. 4C)
    ER+ PDOs  : Highest Ag-6 in pleural effusion cells, concordant with MDA-MB-231
                (pleural-derived cell line); left pleural (early stage) shows greater
                intertarget heterogeneity and elevated CTSL; right pleural (later stage)
                shows general Ag-6 increase, decreased variability; lymph node shows
                general decrease in Ag-6 activity (Fig. 4D/E)
    scADPL vs bulk sADPL: concordant in ER+ PDO samples (Fig. 4D)
    General model: Ag-6 activity peaks in cells undergoing EMT or early metastasis;
                   slightly decreases in established metastatic lesions (Fig. 4F)
  QC gate : pdoSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB14, pdoWithQPCR + pdoTumourSignal);
            pdoSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB15)
  Terminal  : dispose(pdoSignal) -> BB16 (NOP; experiment ends)

Compiler notes:
  - drain vs dispose in IR: BioScript compiler emits both as identical sink operations
    in the IR; semantic distinction (waste channel vs. safe disposal) applies only at
    the physical execution layer
  - Preamplification abstraction: 18m encodes the 18-cycle PCR step as time; the
    BioScript compiler does not support cycle count as an operation parameter
  - Single-cell trap abstraction: `mix @ 2.0 SECOND` in IR represents pressure-driven
    hydrodynamic single-cell capture in Chamber II, not DMF electrode-mediated mixing
  - MCF7_Cells and MDA_MB_231_Cells are declared in manifest for biological completeness
    (referenced in paper but not directly dispensed in this .bs; TNBC cell-line activity
    reported via bulk sADPL in paper Fig. 4B, not encoded as separate scADPL phases here)
  - preamplifiedFPBio variable compiled in IR (Phase 1 FP-Bio arm) but its downstream
    qPCR step is encoded within the NCEH1/JW480 QC branch (BB4); the uninhibited FP-Bio
    signal serves as the reference for the >90% inhibition comparison off-chip
