PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : High-throughput co-culture system for analysis of spatiotemporal cell-cell signaling
Source Link  : https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0956566323000313
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bios.2023.115089
Journal      : Biosensors and Bioelectronics, 2023, Article 115089
Authors      : Minjun Son, Andrew G. Wang, Emma Kenna, Savas Tay
Platform     : Two-layer PDMS microfluidics with pneumatic valve control; 192-chamber high-throughput array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an automated high-throughput microfluidic co-culture system for
spatiotemporal study of cell-cell signaling. The device enables: (i) chemical stimulation
of sentinel macrophages to initiate cytokine secretion, (ii) formation of diffusion-mediated
cytokine gradients over a receiving fibroblast population, (iii) live single-cell NF-kB
translocation imaging across 192 chambers, (iv) spatially resolved cell retrieval from
near/mid/far regions of the receiving chamber, and (v) downstream gene expression
quantification by RT-qPCR or RNA sequencing.

Two experimental arms are encoded in a single .bs file:
  ARM 1 (main, BB1-BB10): LPS-stimulated RAW264.7 macrophages as cytokine source in
                           pre-chamber; paracrine signaling to RelA-DsRed NIH3T3 fibroblasts
                           in receiving chamber.
  ARM 2 (supplemental, BB10-BB16): Fixed-dose 30 ng/mL TNF-a loaded directly into
                                    pre-chamber; no macrophages; controlled distance-dependent
                                    gene expression model. Continues as sequential continuation
                                    from BB10; compiler assigns BB10-BB16.

The protocol reproduces the key experimental conditions of Sections 3.2-3.5 of the paper,
covering macrophage-to-fibroblast paracrine signaling, distance-dependent NF-kB dynamics,
spatially resolved gene expression (Tnfaip3/A20, Casp4, Ccl5/RANTES, GAPDH), and
spatial distribution of sentinel cell density effects on population-level response.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Fabrication : Two-layer PDMS soft lithography; AutoCAD design; bonded to
              127.8 x 85.5 x 1 mm glass substrate (Marienfeld); oxygen plasma (Harrick PDC-001).

Layer 1 (fluid) : Thin PDMS ~50 um height; spun at 2300 RPM from ~10 g mixture on
                  chlorotrimethylsilane-treated fluid mold; contains cell chambers and channels.

Layer 2 (control): Thick PDMS slab; pneumatic valve features; aligned to fluid layer
                   using custom stereomicroscope with XYZ translation stage.

Array format  : 192 chambers in 8x24 arrangement; 96 independently controllable row-column pairs.
                Each unit: small pre-chamber (source) + large receiving chamber.

Receiving chamber: Partitioned into 3 regions (near / mid / far) by pneumatic partitioning valves.
                   Forest of pillars between chamber bottom and sink channel.
                   Cell retrieval side channels (1 per region) lead to dedicated outlets.

Sink channel  : Flushed by gentle peristaltic pumping; provides shear-free cytokine removal;
                mimics capillary clearance of secreted cytokines (Kumar et al., 2019).

Valves        : 88 solenoid-controlled inputs (Festo 197334, Tygon tubes Cole-Parmer ND-100-80).
Control GUI   : Custom Matlab GUI (Gomez-Sjoberg et al., 2010); pre-written scripts for automation.
Imaging       : Nikon Ti2 epifluorescence microscope; temperature/CO2/humidity-controlled enclosure.


CELL LINES
----------
- RelA-/- RelA-DsRed NIH3T3 mouse embryonic fibroblasts (3T3s):
  Receiving chamber cells. NF-kB translocation tracked via nuclear DsRed signal.
  Media: DMEM high glucose (Gibco) + 10% FBS (Omega Scientific) + 2 mM GlutaMAX (Gibco)
         + 1% penicillin/streptomycin (Lonza).
  Loading concentration: 5e5 cells/mL; target ~70% confluency (200-400 cells/chamber).

- RelA-GFP RAW264.7 macrophage-like cells:
  Pre-chamber sentinel cells. NF-kB tracked via GFP.
  Stimulated with 50 ng/mL LPS (InvivoGen, tlrl-3pelps) for 10 min in pre-chamber.

- Primary murine adult fibroblasts (RelA-YFP knock-in mouse):
  Used in cell-patterning experiments (Section 3.5, Fig. 5).

- Bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMMΦ, RelA-YFP knock-in mouse):
  Used in cell-patterning experiments (Section 3.5, Fig. 5).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Priming and Chamber Coating (Fibronectin)
  Reagents    : Fibronectin (Sigma-Aldrich FC010), PBS, DMEM high glucose
  Operation   : Fibronectin loaded through inlet channels into receiving chambers
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature)
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s in IR)
  Wash        : Fresh DMEM flowed through chambers to remove unbound fibronectin
  Purpose     : Promotes fibroblast attachment to glass substrate

Step 2 | RelA-DsRed 3T3 Fibroblast Loading into Receiving Chambers
  Cell line       : RelA-/- RelA-DsRed NIH3T3
  Concentration   : 5e5 cells/mL in complete DMEM
  Target loading  : ~70% confluency, 200-400 cells per chamber
  Operation       : Valve-directed flow through cell loading channel (abstracted as mix in BioScript)
  Adhesion        : 37 C / 5% CO2 for 4-6 h (300 min nominal; 18000 s in IR)
  Notes           : Cells spread evenly before macrophage loading; adhesion verified visually

Step 3 | Hoechst 33342 Nuclear Staining (Optional)
  Reagent         : 1 uM Hoechst 33342
  Incubation      : 10 min at 37 C (600 s in IR)
  Purpose         : Enables nucleus tracking during live-cell epifluorescence imaging
  Notes           : Applied selectively; performed before macrophage loading

Step 4 | RelA-GFP RAW264.7 Macrophage Loading into Pre-Chamber
  Cell line       : RelA-GFP RAW264.7
  Cell number     : 10-100 per pre-chamber; controlled-density experiments use 60 +/- 15
  Suspension      : DMEM high glucose
  Operation       : Flow through source channel; separator valve CLOSED during loading
  Settling        : 30 min at 37 C (1800 s in IR)
  Notes           : Pre-chamber and receiving chamber remain separated by closed separator valve

Step 5 | LPS Stimulation of Macrophages and Wash
  LPS             : 50 ng/mL (InvivoGen tlrl-3pelps)
  Stimulus duration: 10 min (600 s in IR); separator valve CLOSED
  Wash            : Fresh DMEM flowed through pre-chamber to remove LPS before separator opens
  Purpose         : Activates canonical NF-kB via TLR4; triggers TNF-a and cytokine secretion
  Notes           : LPS washed out to prevent direct fibroblast activation by LPS

Step 6 | Separator Valve Opening and Cytokine Gradient Formation
  Operation       : Separator valve opened; peristaltic sink pumping initiated simultaneously
  Tracer (QC)     : Cyanine 5 (Cy5) used to characterise gradient formation kinetics
  Gradient duration: 400 min (24000 s in IR); near region rises by 30 min; full equilibration ~400 min
  Sink pumping    : Gentle peristaltic; removes cytokines at far end; maintains gradient
  Detection       : gradientDetection module; 10 s
  QC gate (BB1->BB2/BB3): gradientSignal > 0 -> dispose(gradientChamber) [BB2];
                           gradientSignal <= 0 -> mix waste + drain [BB3]

Step 7 | Live-Cell NF-kB Translocation Imaging
  Duration        : 200 min (12000 s in IR) post separator opening
  Temperature     : 37 C / 5% CO2 / humidity controlled
  Quantification  : Nucleus/cytosol RelA-DsRed ratio per 3T3 (Kudo et al., 2018)
  Detection       : nfkbTranslocationDetection module; 30 s
  Notes (BB4)     : sinkFlow and gradientJoin are two distinct FreshMedium dispenses;
                    mixed to form imagingChamber (FIX4)
  Findings        : Signaling range correlates with macrophage number; buffered at ~60 macrophages;
                    near cells: rapid strong NF-kB then rapid decay;
                    far cells: slower, lower amplitude, more heterogeneous oscillatory response
  QC gate (BB4->BB5/BB6): nfkbSignal > 0 -> dispose(imagingChamber) [BB5];
                           nfkbSignal <= 0 -> mix waste + drain [BB6]

Step 8 | Spatially Resolved Cell Retrieval (Near / Mid / Far)
  TrypLE flow     : ~10 s (Gibco TrypLE)
  TrypLE incubation: ~60 s (partitioning valves closed before full detachment)
  Total detachment: 70 s (70 s mix duration in BioScript)
  Partitioning    : Pneumatic partitioning valves closed to isolate near / mid / far regions
  PBS retrieval   : Rapidly flowed through cell retrieval side channels to outlet
  Collection      : ~100 cells per region collected as ~2 uL droplet at outlet
  Lysis           : Deposited into 10 uL ice-cold lysis buffer (0.1% Triton-X-100 + RNase inhibitor, Takara)
  Storage         : Biological intent: -80 C long-term cryostorage
                    IR output (v2): heat @ 4.0CELSIUS @ 57600.0SECOND (FIX1; see compiler notes)
  Timepoints      : 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h post separator opening; triplicate at each
  Detection       : geneExpressionDetection module on nearLysate; 30 s
  QC gate (BB7->BB8/BB9): geneExprSignal > 0 -> dispose lysates [BB8];
                           geneExprSignal <= 0 -> mix waste per region + drain [BB9]

Step 9 | Downstream qPCR Gene Expression Quantification
  Pipeline        : SmartSeq2 reverse transcription + pre-amplification (Picelli et al., 2014)
  Genes           : Tnfaip3 (A20), Casp4, Ccl5 (RANTES), GAPDH (control)
  Replicates      : Triplicate per timepoint per region
  Normalisation   : Each gene level normalised to maximum across all samples
  Simulation      : NF-kB dynamics input to ODE; degradation rate varied 20-fold (Fig. 4C);
                    degradation rate explains distance-dependent patterns


SUPPLEMENTAL ARM: Fixed-Dose TNF-a Stimulation (Section 3.4, Fig. 4)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
CFG blocks      : BB10 (entry/join from main arm) -> BB11/BB12 -> BB13 -> BB14/BB15 -> BB16 (terminal)
Rationale       : Macrophage variability (Fig. 3C) prevents reproducible NF-kB profiles across
                  samples; fixed TNF-a eliminates this variability
TNF-a dose      : 30 ng/mL (R&D Systems, aa80-235); loaded directly into pre-chamber; no macrophages
Imaging window  : 8 h (480 min; 28800 s in IR)
Retrieval       : Identical TrypLE / PBS / lysis protocol as main arm; heat at 4c for 960m (FIX1)
Timepoints      : 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 h post separator opening; triplicate
Key finding     : Tnfaip3 peaks 60 min (near) vs 120 min (mid/far);
                  Ccl5 and Casp4 peak 4-6 h with similar magnitude at all positions;
                  degradation rate determines distance sensitivity of NF-kB target gene expression


CELL PATTERNING ARM: Spatial Distribution of Sentinel Cells (Section 3.5, Fig. 5)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cell lines      : Primary murine adult fibroblasts (RelA-YFP) + BMMΦ (RelA-YFP knock-in mouse)
LPS dose        : 3 ng/mL; sub-threshold for direct fibroblast activation
Patterning      : Macrophages loaded at near (0-0.5 mm) or mid (1-1.5 mm) position via side channels
Finding         : Near-position loading -> strong local fibroblast activation, weak at far;
                  Mid-position loading -> more uniform population-level response, weaker absolute
                  activation; spatial distribution of sentinel cells determines population response


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file   : son2023_coculture_spatiotemporal_v2.bs
Compiler         : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output        : son2023_coculture_spatiotemporal_v2.ir
DOT output       : son2023_coculture_spatiotemporal_v2_main_dag.dot
CFG structure    : 16 basic blocks (BB1-BB16); 4 if/else QC gates
                   Main arm:         BB1-BB10  (3 gates: gradient, NF-kB, gene expression)
                   Supplemental arm: BB10-BB16 (1 gate: imagingChamber QC + 1 gate: lysate QC)
                   Total CFG edges:  20
Modules          : gradientDetection, nfkbTranslocationDetection, geneExpressionDetection,
                   viabilityDetection
Duration units   : BioScript supports s and m only; all durations converted accordingly
                   30m  -> 1800 s   (fibronectin coating, macrophage settling)
                   300m -> 18000 s  (fibroblast adhesion)
                   10m  -> 600 s    (LPS stimulation, Hoechst staining)
                   400m -> 24000 s  (gradient formation)
                   200m -> 12000 s  (NF-kB imaging, main arm)
                   480m -> 28800 s  (imaging window, TNF-a arm)
                   960m -> 57600 s  (lysate staging; biological intent: -80 C cryostorage)

  FIX2 | False branches emitted dispose() instead of drain()

    v2 IR : bsbbif_3_f: gradientWaste1[0] = mix(gradientChamber1[0], washBuffer21[0]);
                         dispose(gradientWaste1[0])
    fix   : Explicit waste variable in false branch; semantic drain intent preserved in .bs source

  FIX3 | Dead variable lysisBufferStock dispensed but never consumed
    v1 IR : lysisBufferStock1[0] = dispense(LysisBuffer)  [orphaned, never used in mix]
    v2 IR : variable removed entirely
    fix   : Removed standalone dispense; per-region lysis variables (lysisNear, lysisMid,
            lysisFar) are dispensed inline where consumed

  FIX4 | Self-mix in imagingChamber (same variable both operands)
    v1 IR : imagingChamber1[0] = mix(freshMediumFlow1[0], freshMediumFlow1[0])
    v2 IR : imagingChamber1[0] = mix(sinkFlow1[0], gradientJoin1[0])
    fix   : Two distinct FreshMedium dispenses (sinkFlow, gradientJoin) used as operands

REMAINING LIMITATION:
  drain() has no dedicated opcode in lilott8/BioScript IR; all drain() calls compile to
  dispose() on the waste variable. The waste variable pattern (FIX2) preserves semantic
  intent in .bs source even though the IR output is structurally identical to dispose().
