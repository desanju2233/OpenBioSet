PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Input Dose Differentiation by NF-kB
Source Link  : https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/752394v1.full.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1101/752394
Journal      : bioRxiv preprint, September 4, 2019 (bioRxiv 752394)
Platform     : Two-layer PDMS microfluidic chip (64 independent chambers, >500 cells/chamber)
               with automated Matlab GUI valve control and live-cell fluorescence imaging


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidic live-cell analysis pipeline used to investigate how the
NF-kB signalling pathway in single mammalian cells responds to dynamically varying cytokine
concentrations. Using a multiplexed PDMS microfluidic chip, cells expressing p65-DsRed (NF-kB
reporter) and H2B-GFP (nuclear marker) are exposed to TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, or Pam2CSK4 in
precisely controlled temporal profiles: instant step increase, linear ramp, exponential ramp,
and random fluctuating order. Nuclear NF-kB localization is tracked in real time by time-lapse
fluorescence microscopy. Downstream, cells are retrieved from the chip for RT-qPCR measurement
of NF-kB target gene expression.

The central finding is that NF-kB acts as a dose differentiator: its nuclear localization
amplitude (peak height and AUC) is proportional to the logarithm of the change in cytokine
concentration, not the absolute concentration itself. This behaviour is analogous to an
electronic differentiator circuit and is described by:

    NF-kB ~ log(delta_cytokine + 1)   [for delta_cytokine > 0]

A20 and IkBa negative feedbacks are identified as the molecular mechanism providing short-term
memory of prior cytokine level, continuously resetting IKK cycling and receptor sensitivity.
The protocol also includes an A20 CRISPR/Cas9 knockout arm that experimentally confirms A20's
role, and RT-qPCR measurements showing that distinct cytokine input dynamics drive distinct
subsets of NF-kB target gene expression.

The complete experimental workflow covers device fabrication, chip priming, cell loading,
multi-arm cytokine stimulation, live-cell imaging, mathematical modelling inputs, A20 KO
cell generation, and downstream gene expression analysis.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Two-layer PDMS microfluidic chip fabricated by multilayer soft lithography:

Flow layer  : AZ40XT positive photoresist (25 um, 3400 RPM; rounded cross-section by hard-bake)
              + SU-8 3025 negative photoresist (30 um, 3000 RPM) on 4-inch silicon wafer.
Control layer: SU-8 3025 (25 um, 3500 RPM) on 4-inch silicon wafer.
Lithography : Heidelberg MLA150 maskless aligner; 375 nm laser; 280 mJ/cm2 dose.

PDMS layers:
  Flow layer  : RTV-615 (Momentive), 66 g, 10:1 monomer:catalyst; ~2 cm thick slab;
                cured 80 C >5 h; flow pattern grooved on bottom face.
  Control layer: RTV-615, 10 g; spun at 2200 RPM (~50 um thin film); cured 80 C >=1 h.

Assembly:
  1. Flow layer inputs punched.
  2. Both layers treated with oxygen plasma (Harrick PDC-001).
  3. Layers aligned under custom stereomicroscope; cured 80 C overnight for bonding.
  4. Control layer holes punched; chip bonded to 75 x 25 x 1 mm glass slide by plasma + bake.

Chamber specifications:
  - 64 independent sample chambers per chip
  - Chamber size: 3.5 x 0.8 mm
  - >500 cells per chamber
  - Minimum feature spacing: 150 um (robust bonding and alignment)

Actuation:
  - Control layer: pneumatic solenoid valves at 25-30 psi
  - Input media: pressurized at 3-4 psi through PEEK tubing (VICI, TPK.505) to prevent
    shear stress on cells during feeding
  - Automation: custom Matlab GUI; experiment scripts define feeding timing and medium
    type per chamber; greatly improves accuracy, throughput, and reproducibility

Surface coating:
  - 1:3 fibronectin (1 mg/mL) in sterile PBS; loaded into chambers overnight at room
    temperature before experiment day


CELL LINES
----------
Primary experimental cell line:
  - p65-/- 3T3 mouse embryonic fibroblasts (MEFs) expressing p65-DsRed and H2B-GFP
    Cultured: DMEM (Gibco 11965092) + 10% fetal bovine calf serum (HyClone) +
              1% GlutaMax (Gibco) + 1x penicillin-streptomycin (Gibco)
    Passaging: Trypsin-EDTA (Gibco); ~1:10 dilution every 2-3 days before 100% confluency
    Conditions: 37 C, 5% CO2
    Imaging media: FluoroBrite DMEM (Gibco A1896702) + same supplements; used during
                   experiments to reduce background fluorescence

A20 knockout cell line:
  - A20 KO MEFs: p65-/- background with p65-DsRed and H2B-GFP; A20 gene edited by
    CRISPR/Cas9 lentiviral delivery; ~90% protein knockdown by Western blot.
    Cultured and maintained under identical conditions to parental MEFs.


REAGENTS
--------
Cytokines:
  TNF-alpha     : Life Technologies, PMC3014
                  Dose range: 0 to 10 ng/mL; instant dose 10 ng/mL;
                  linear step 1 ng/mL/60 min; exponential doubling time 60 min;
                  random range 0-10 ng/mL
  IL-1beta      : R&D Systems, 401ML010CF
                  Dose range: 0 to 2.5 ng/mL; instant dose 2.5 ng/mL;
                  linear step 0.5 ng/mL/60 min; exponential doubling time 60 min
  Pam2CSK4     : InvivoGen, tlrl-pm2s (TLR2 agonist; synthetic diacylated lipopeptide)
                  Dose range: 0.2 to 12.8 ng/mL; doubling every 30 or 60 min
                  Control ligand: NF-kB dose differentiation NOT observed for Pam

Viability stains:
  FDA           : 5 ug/mL; ex 490 nm / em 526 nm; labels live cells (green)
  PI            : 2 ug/mL; ex 488 nm / em 617 nm; labels dead cells (red)

Cell lysis:
  CellsDirect Kit lysis buffer (ThermoFisher); 5 uL per sample;
  snap-frozen at -80 C immediately after retrieval

Surface coating:
  Fibronectin (1 mg/mL) in sterile PBS; 1:3 dilution; overnight room temperature

Trypsin: Trypsin-EDTA (Gibco)

All cytokine-containing media prepared immediately before experiment and kept on ice.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Fibronectin Surface Coating
  Reagents    : Fibronectin 1 mg/mL in sterile PBS (1:3 dilution)
  Operation   : Fill chambers; heat at room temperature (25 C)
  Duration    : 12 h overnight (720 min; IR: 43200.0 SECOND)
  Purpose     : Ensures cell adhesion to PDMS chamber floor before loading

Step 2 | Chamber Flush and Device Equilibration
  Reagents    : FluoroBrite DMEM + supplements
  Operation   : Pressure-driven flush (abstracted as mix); heat at 37 C
  Duration    : 60 min equilibration (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  Purpose     : Removes unbound fibronectin; equilibrates to 37 C / 5% CO2 / ~100% humidity

Step 3 | Cell Loading and Settling
  Cell line   : 3T3 MEF p65-DsRed / H2B-GFP
  Concentration: 5 x 10^6 cells/mL; targeting ~50% confluency (>500 cells/chamber)
  Operation   : Gravity-assisted sedimentation (abstracted as mix); heat at 37 C
  Duration    : >=60 min settling before stimulation begins (IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  Notes       : Chambers closed after loading; cells must adhere firmly

Step 4 | Baseline Imaging (Pre-Stimulation)
  Operation   : detect nfkbNuclearLocalizationDetection
  Duration    : 30 min (IR: 1800.0 SECOND); 5-min imaging intervals = 6 frames
  Purpose     : Establishes pre-stimulus nuclear p65-DsRed baseline (n > 200 cells/condition)

Step 5a | Instant TNF Stimulation
  Reagents    : TNF-alpha 10 ng/mL
  Operation   : Media exchange (abstracted as mix); detect nfkbNuclearLocalizationDetection
  Duration    : 300 min = 5 h (IR: 18000.0 SECOND); replenished every 60 min
  Expected    : Strong NF-kB peak at ~25 min; exponential oscillation decay thereafter
                (desensitisation to constant input; subsequent peaks 10-30% of initial)

Step 5b | Instant IL-1beta Stimulation
  Reagents    : IL-1beta 2.5 ng/mL
  Operation   : Media exchange (abstracted as mix); detect nfkbNuclearLocalizationDetection
  Duration    : 300 min (IR: 18000.0 SECOND)
  Expected    : Parallel dynamics to TNF instant increase

Step 6a | Linear TNF Ramp (5 Steps)
  Reagents    : TNF-alpha; 1 ng/mL increment per step
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); 5 steps total = 300 min
  Expected    : Sustained oscillations; constant AUC at each step; confirms differentiator
                Ramping rates tested: 0.75-10 ng/mL/h (Supplementary Fig. 1)

Step 6b | Linear IL-1beta Ramp (5 Steps)
  Reagents    : IL-1beta; 0.5 ng/mL increment per step
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step; 5 steps total
  Expected    : Constant AUC at each step; differentiator confirmed for IL-1beta (Fig. 2A)

Step 7a | Exponential TNF Ramp (5 Steps)
  Reagents    : TNF-alpha; 2-fold increase per step from starting dose to 10 ng/mL
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); 5 steps total
  Expected    : AUC increases linearly with log(delta_TNF); R-squared ~0.963 (Fig. 2B)
  QC note     : Final step (nfkbExpTNF_s5) used as QC gate anchor for nfkbSignal branch

Step 7b | Exponential IL-1beta Ramp (5 Steps)
  Reagents    : IL-1beta; 2-fold increase per step to 2.5 ng/mL
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step; 5 steps total
  Expected    : Log-linear AUC increase; same differentiator relationship as TNF (Fig. 2B)

Step 8 | Random-Order TNF Fluctuation (4 Steps)
  Reagents    : TNF-alpha; randomly increasing and decreasing concentrations 0-10 ng/mL
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); 4 steps
  Expected    : AUC vs delta_TNF linear for positive changes (correlation ~0.91); sharp drop
                for any concentration decrease; rectified differentiator behaviour confirmed
                (Fig. 2C; Supplementary Fig. 3C for IL-1beta)

Step 9 | Pam2CSK4 Exponential Ramp Control (5 Steps)
  Reagents    : Pam2CSK4; doubled every 60 min from 0.2 to 12.8 ng/mL
  Operation   : Sequential media exchange + detect per step
  Duration    : 60 min per step (IR: 3600.0 SECOND); 5 steps (30 min also tested)
  Expected    : Majority of cells show 1-2 sharp peaks; AUC slopes span negative to positive
                across single-cell clusters; dose differentiation ABSENT for TLR2 ligand
  Analysis    : Single-cell clustering by Matlab linkage (ward method, correlation metric);
                4 groups; TNF slopes all positive (0.037-0.058); Pam slopes mixed
                (-0.048 to +0.035) confirming specificity to TNFR/IL-1R pathway (Fig. 3)

Step 10 | NF-kB Response QC Gate
  Input       : nfkbExpTNF_s5 (final step of exponential TNF ramp)
  Operation   : detect nfkbNuclearLocalizationDetection for 30 s
  Branch:
    Pass (BB2) : nfkbSignal > 0 -> valid oscillatory NF-kB response; dispose; continue
    Fail (BB3) : nfkbSignal <= 0 -> no response (cell death or stimulation failure); drain
  Both branches converge at BB4 (viability join)

Step 11 | Cell Viability Assay (FDA/PI)
  Reagents    : FDA/PI stain (FDA 5 ug/mL, PI 2 ug/mL)
  Operation   : mix (10 min staining, room temperature, dark); detect viabilityDetection 30 s
  Expected    : >80% viability for valid experiment
  Branch:
    Pass (BB5) : viabilitySignal > 0 -> viable cells; dispose; continue to gene expression
    Fail (BB6) : viabilitySignal <= 0 -> low viability; drain
  Both branches converge at BB7 (gene expression join)

Step 12 | Cell Retrieval, Lysis, and RT-qPCR
  Operation   : Trypsinize 1 min; PBS wash to open outlet; 2-3 uL droplet pipetted into
                5 uL CellsDirect lysis buffer; snap-frozen at -80 C
  RT-qPCR     : CellsDirect One-Step RT-qPCR kit; Biorad CFX384
  Targets     : A20, RANTES, Ccl2, Zfp36, Casp4, Serpina3g (TaqMan probes)
  Normalization: GAPDH; 2^(Cq_target - Cq_GAPDH)
  Conditions  : Instant TNF (10 ng/mL, replenished hourly) vs exponential ramp
                (2-fold/60 min for 5 h); cells retrieved at multiple time points during
                and after stimulation (Fig. 7)
  Detection   : detect geneExpressionDetection for 120 min (IR: 7200.0 SECOND)
  Branch:
    Pass (BB8) : geneExprSignal > 0 -> target mRNA detected above GAPDH; dispose lysate
    Fail (BB9) : geneExprSignal <= 0 -> qPCR failure; drain lysate
  Both branches converge at BB10 (terminal cleanup)


KEY RESULTS
-----------
Differentiator equation:
  NF-kB ~ log(delta_cytokine + 1)   for positive changes in cytokine concentration

Linear ramp  : Constant NF-kB AUC at each step (NF-kB responds to constant rate of change)
Exponential ramp : AUC increases log-linearly; TNF R-squared ~0.963; IL-1beta confirmed
Random input : AUC-vs-delta_TNF correlation ~0.91 for increases; sharp drop for decreases
Pam2CSK4    : No dose differentiation (TLR2 pathway); differentiator is receptor-specific

A20 knockout results:
  Exponential ramp : NF-kB saturates at much lower TNF dose; differentiator abolished
  Constant dose    : NF-kB still decreasing at 120 min (vs 60 min for WT);
                     mean oscillation frequency halved; more power at zero frequency
  Random input     : AUC correlation reduced to ~0.76; NF-kB remains nuclear after dose decrease

Gene expression clusters (TNF instant vs exponential):
  Input-responsive (instant only) : Zfp36, Casp4 -- inflammation/apoptosis; require large
                                     initial dose; minimal response during gradual ramp
  Ramp-following                  : A20, Ccl2 -- mirror NF-kB peak height during ramp;
                                     converge to same level when maximum dose is reached
  Late-sustained                  : Serpina3g -- gradual increase only under persistent
                                     high TNF; remains basal during exponential ramp
  Input-independent               : RANTES -- increases continuously under both patterns
