PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Spatiotemporal NF-kB Dynamics Encodes the Position, Amplitude, and Duration of Local Immune Inputs
Source Link  : https://www.science.org/doi/full/10.1126/sciadv.abn6240
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abn6240
Journal      : Science Advances, 2022, Vol. 8, Article eabn6240
Authors      : Minjun Son, Tino Frank, Thomas Holst-Hansen, Andrew G. Wang, Michael Junkin,
               Sara S. Kashaf, Ala Trusina, Savas Tay
Platform     : Custom two-chamber PDMS diffusion microfluidic chip with pneumatic membrane valve


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a microfluidic coculture platform designed to quantify spatiotemporal
NF-kB signaling dynamics in a model one-way immune communication channel: a signal-sending
macrophage (or soluble TNF source) to signal-receiving NIH 3T3 fibroblasts. The device consists
of two PDMS chambers separated by a pneumatically actuated membrane valve. Cytokine (TNF) is
loaded in the source chamber and released into a receiving chamber filled with fibroblasts by
opening the valve. A peristaltic sink channel continuously perfuses fresh medium at the far end
to simulate capillary clearance, establishing a stable diffusion gradient along the 2.5 mm
chamber axis.

Single-cell NF-kB nuclear translocation is tracked in p65-DsRed-expressing fibroblasts by
time-lapse fluorescence microscopy at 6 min intervals. After the imaging window, cells are
retrieved from three spatially resolved regions (near, mid, far from the TNF source) and
profiled by one-step RT-qPCR for early (A20/Tnfaip3) and late (Casp4, RANTES/Ccl5) NF-kB
target genes.

The BioScript file encodes the primary TNF diffusion experiment (100 ng/mL, 60 min release,
Fig. 3B primary arm) with downstream spatially resolved gene expression measurement (Fig. 6).
The macrophage coculture arm (Fig. 5) is documented in the manifest but not separately encoded.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Fabrication   : Standard soft lithography. PDMS (Momentive RTV-615, 10:1 polymer:catalyst)
                cast on SU-8 masters. Valve regions: positive photoresist MicroChemicals AZ40XT
                (20 um). Channel and control layers: negative photoresist SU-8 3025 (30 or 25 um).
                All photoresists exposed at 375 nm (Heidelberg MLA150 maskless aligner).
                PDMS layers cured overnight at 80 C. Oxygen plasma treatment (Harrick PDC-001,
                30 s) used to bond PDMS slab to clean glass slide.

Chambers      :
  Source chamber    : Cytokine (TNF) or single macrophage loaded here with valve closed.
  Diffusion chamber : Fibroblast receiving chamber, 2.5 mm along diffusion axis, coated
                      with 1:4 fibronectin (1 mg/mL in PBS) overnight before cell seeding.
  Sink channel      : Bottom channel; continuously washed with fresh medium at 0.33 Hz
                      (20 cycles/min) via on-chip peristaltic pumping to deplete cytokine
                      and simulate capillary clearance (bypass refreshed every 1-2 min).

Valve         : Pneumatic PDMS membrane separator; Festo 197334 solenoid valve; controlled by
                custom MATLAB interface (modified from Gomez-Sjoberg et al. Anal. Chem. 2007).
                Reagents delivered via Tygon ND-100-80 tubing + PEEK VICI TPK.505 tubing.

Microscope    : Nikon Eclipse Ti2; stage-top incubator box (Life Imaging Service, Basel);
                maintained at 37 C, 5% CO2, >98% humidity throughout all experiments.


CELL LINES
----------
- p65-/- NIH 3T3 mouse embryonic fibroblasts (signal-receiving population):
    Reporters : p65-DsRed (NF-kB nuclear translocation marker)
                H2B-GFP (nucleus position and boundary marker)
    Medium    : Fluorobrite DMEM (Gibco A1896701) + 10% FBS (Omega Scientific FB11)
                + 1% GlutaMax (Gibco) + 1x penicillin-streptomycin (Gibco)
    Passaging : Trypsin-EDTA (Gibco); 1:10 resuspension before 100% confluency

- p65-/- RAW 264.7 macrophages (signal-sending population; macrophage coculture arm only):
    Reporter  : p65-GFP
    Medium    : DMEM + 10% certified FBS (Gibco A3160401) + 1% GlutaMax + HEPES (Gibco 15630106)
    Passaging : Scraped and resuspended 1:10 before 100% confluency
    Stimulation: LPS 10 ng/mL (InvivoGen tlrl-3pelps) for 10 min; washed with fresh medium


PROTOCOL STEPS (BioScript-encoded TNF diffusion arm)
------------------------------------------------------

Step 1 | Fibronectin Surface Coating of Diffusion Chamber
  Reagents    : Fibronectin (1 mg/mL) diluted 1:4 in PBS
  Operation   : Applied to PDMS diffusion chamber surface overnight at room temperature
  Duration    : 720m (overnight; = 43200 s in IR)
  Purpose     : Promotes fibroblast adhesion and uniform monolayer in diffusion chamber
  Wash        : Channels washed with fresh DMEM before fibroblast seeding
  Paper M&M   : "surface treated with 1:4 fibronectin (1 mg/ml) in PBS solution overnight
                 and washed with fresh medium"

Step 2 | NIH 3T3 Fibroblast Seeding and Attachment
  Cell line   : p65-/- NIH 3T3 (p65-DsRed + H2B-GFP)
  Confluency  : ~80% target seeding density
  Attachment  : 5 h at 37 C, 5% CO2 (= 300m = 18000 s in IR); 1-2 medium feedings during window
  Side channel clearance: Non-adherent cells in side channels flushed out with trypsin
  Note        : `mix` in BioScript abstracts channel flow-through seeding, not DMF electrode
                actuation; no electrode-mediated mixing occurs in this device

Step 3 | TNF Loading into Source Chamber
  Reagent     : TNF (R&D Systems 410-MT); diluted to 100 ng/mL in Fluorobrite DMEM
  Operation   : Loaded into source chamber with pneumatic separator valve in closed position
  Conditions tested in paper: 10, 30, 100 ng/mL (Fig. 3A); this file encodes 100 ng/mL

Step 4 | Valve Opening - TNF Diffusion Initiation
  Operation   : MATLAB-controlled solenoid valve opened; TNF diffuses passively along 2.5 mm axis
  Release duration: 60 min (= 3600 s in IR); valve then closed to stop TNF source input
  Sink perfusion : 0.33 Hz peristaltic pumping (20 cycles/min); bypass washed every 1-2 min
  Diffusion profile (Fig. 1E, fig. S4):
    Near (0-0.4 mm) : Fast TNF rise, high peak concentration, ~0 min NF-kB delay
    Mid  (0.5-0.9 mm): Intermediate TNF kinetics, moderate delay
    Far  (1.0-1.5 mm): Slow TNF rise, low peak, ~25 min NF-kB activation delay;
                        extended NF-kB activation duration (Fig. 1I blue interval)
  Other durations tested: 15 min and 30 min (Fig. 3B)

Step 5 | NF-kB Fluorescence Imaging Window
  Duration    : 200 min total post-valve-open (= 12000 s in IR)
  Interval    : Fluorescence images every 6 min (automated acquisition, NIS Elements)
  Camera      : Hamamatsu ORCA-Flash4.0 V2; p65-DsRed: 555 nm excitation, 0.3 s / 100% LED;
                H2B-GFP: 470 nm excitation, 40 ms / 100% LED; Nikon S plan Fluor ELWD 20x
  Stitching   : 4 FOVs per chamber stitched by custom MATLAB (detectSURFFeatures +
                estimateGeometricTransform)
  Analysis    : p65-DsRed nuclear intensity tracked per cell; basal level (pre-valve) used as
                reference; lowess smoothing applied; threshold = 2 SDs above mean basal per region
  Observations (100 ng/mL, 60 min):
    - Wave-like NF-kB propagation across entire 2.5 mm chamber (Fig. 1H, Movie S2)
    - Sharply defined activation zone: >75% to <25% activation within 0.4 mm (Fig. 1J)
    - 2-3 NF-kB oscillations within activation zone at 100 ng/mL
  QC criterion: >75% of near-region cells (0-0.4 mm) activate above 2 SD threshold

Step 6 | NF-kB Activation QC Gate
  Detection   : nfkbActivationDetection module; 30 s detect on imagingWindow
  Pass (BB2)  : nfkbSignal > 0 -> wave propagation confirmed; dispose(imagingWindow)
  Fail (BB3)  : nfkbSignal <= 0 -> device failure or TNF degradation; drain(imagingWindow)
  Join (BB4)  : dispose(nfkbSignal); proceed to gene expression arm

Step 7 | Continued Incubation to 8 h Time Point
  Duration    : 280m additional (= 16800 s in IR); 200m imaging elapsed; total 480m = 8 h
  Temperature : 37 C, 5% CO2
  Purpose     : Allow late NF-kB target gene expression to accumulate (Casp4, RANTES peak ~4-8 h)
  Timepoints  : Cells retrieved at 0, 2, 4, and 8 h post-TNF release (Fig. 6C)

Step 8 | Spatially Resolved Cell Retrieval
  Method      : Chip outlet PDMS cut with razor; purge medium carries detached cells onto
                glass slide as ~2 uL droplet; pipetted into 10 uL ice-cold lysis buffer
  Valve actuation: Separating valves divide chamber into three spatial zones:
    Near : 0-0.4 mm from TNF source
    Mid  : 0.5-0.9 mm from TNF source
    Far  : 1.0-1.5 mm from TNF source
  Detachment  : TrypLE Express (Gibco); 1-2 min per region (= 2m = 120 s in IR)
  Lysis buffer: 10 uL per region; 0.1% Triton X-100 + RNase inhibitor (Takara); ice-cold
  Storage     : -80 C until qPCR processing
  BioScript note: Three separate PBS + TrypLE + lysis buffer dispenses (one per region) used;
                  continuedIncub staged through chambNear/Mid/Far via distinct PBS dispenses
                  to comply with compiler single-consumption constraint

Step 9 | One-Step RT-qPCR Gene Expression Measurement
  Kit         : CellDirect One-Step RT-qPCR (Thermo Fisher Scientific)
  Targets     : Tnfaip3 (A20) -- early gene; Casp4 -- late gene; Ccl5 (RANTES) -- late gene
  Regions     : Near, Mid, Far (9 total reactions: 3 probes x 3 regions)
  Normalization: GAPDH; DeltaCT method; fold change = 2^(DeltaDeltaCT)
  RT step     : 50 C for 15 min (= 900 s in IR)
  PCR step    : 95 C for 42 min (2 min initial denaturation + 40 cycles; = 2520 s in IR)
  Expected spatial pattern (Fig. 6C):
    A20 (early) : Strongly induced near source at 2 h; drops sharply at mid/far by 8 h
    Casp4 (late): Upregulated across all three regions at 4-8 h (distance-insensitive)
    RANTES (late): Upregulated across all three regions at 4-8 h (distance-insensitive)
  BioScript note: Probes chained sequentially into each lysate to comply with compiler
                  single-consumption constraint: rxnNearA -> rxnNearAC -> rxnNearACR;
                  same chain applied to Mid and Far. Terminal ACR variable carries all
                  three probes and proceeds through RT/PCR heat steps and detect.

Step 10 | Gene Expression QC Gate
  Detection   : geneExpressionDetection module; 30 s detect on rxnNearACR
  QC criterion: A20 near-region signal must exceed 3 SDs above unstimulated baseline at 2 h
  Pass (BB5)  : geneExprSignal > 0 -> dispose(rxnNearACR, rxnMidACR, rxnFarACR)
  Fail (BB6)  : geneExprSignal <= 0 -> drain(rxnNearACR, rxnMidACR, rxnFarACR)
  Terminal (BB7): dispose(geneExprSignal); NOP


KEY FINDINGS
------------
Wave propagation  : TNF diffusion + intrinsic NF-kB network oscillations produce wave-like
                    NF-kB activity propagation; not paracrine amplification by fibroblasts
                    (fibroblasts do not secrete NF-kB-activating signals, fig. S5).

Activation zone   : Sharply defined for all source doses; transition from >75% to <25%
                    cell activation within 0.4 mm (<15% of chamber length) (Fig. 1J).

Dose controls range: Signaling range increases linearly with log(TNF dose);
                    y = 0.89*log(x) - 1.80 mm (Fig. 3C). Dose does not affect oscillation count.

Duration controls oscillations: Number of NF-kB oscillations correlates with stimulus
                    duration, not dose; y = 0.023*x + 1.40 oscillations per minute (Fig. 3C).
                    Duration does not affect signaling range.

Multidimensional decoding: First NF-kB peak height encodes dose; ratio of second to first
                    peak height encodes distance; oscillation count encodes duration -- all
                    decodable simultaneously from single-cell dynamics (Figs. 4D-E).

Single-cell accuracy: Binary decision tree cross-validation yields 0.69-0.94 accuracy
                    distinguishing dose and distance at single-cell level (Fig. 4F).

Gene expression zones: Early genes (A20) restricted near TNF source; late genes (Casp4,
                    RANTES) expressed broadly across all distances. IRF-family target genes
                    (IRF-1, -2, -7, -8, -9) show longer-range expression than NF-kB-family
                    target genes due to lower TNF activation threshold (Figs. 6-7).

Macrophage variability: Single macrophage secretion variability directly determines NF-kB
                    signaling range (0-2 mm) and oscillation count (0 to >3) across 35
                    independent coculture replicates (Fig. 5C).


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : son_spatiotemporal_nfkb_microfluidics.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 7 basic blocks (BB1-BB7), 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges
DAG file       : son_spatiotemporal_nfkb_microfluidics_main_dag.dot
Duration units : s and m only (h token not supported by compiler)

Duration conversions:
  Fibronectin overnight     : 720m  -> 43200.0 SECOND
  Fibroblast attachment 5h  : 300m  -> 18000.0 SECOND
  TNF diffusion 60 min      : 60m   -> 3600.0 SECOND  (mix + heat)
  NF-kB imaging 200 min     : 200m  -> 12000.0 SECOND
  Continued incubation 280m : 280m  -> 16800.0 SECOND
  RT step 15 min            : 15m   -> 900.0 SECOND
  PCR step 42 min           : 42m   -> 2520.0 SECOND

Compiler constraints resolved:
  1. Bare manifest names in mix: Fixed by adding explicit dispense assignments
     (e.g., freshMedium1 = dispense 1 units of FreshMedium) before every mix operand.
  2. Consumed-variable reuse (continuedIncub): Staged into three independent copy variables
     (chambNear, chambMid, chambFar) via three separate PBS dispenses before TrypLE mixing.
  3. Consumed-variable reuse (lysates): Probes chained sequentially into each lysate
     (rxnNearA -> rxnNearAC -> rxnNearACR) so each variable is consumed exactly once.

drain normalization: BioScript `drain` statements compile to `dispose` in IR output.
                     This is expected compiler behavior, not a bug.

Dead manifest entries: LPS_Stimulant and RAW264_7_Macrophage are declared in the manifest
                       for biological documentation of the macrophage coculture arm (Fig. 5)
                       but are not dispensed in this file. No compile error is raised.
                       Remove for cleanliness if desired.
