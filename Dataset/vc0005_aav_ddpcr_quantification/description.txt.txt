PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Viral Titer Quantification by Droplet Digital PCR (ddPCR)
Source Link  : https://www.protocols.io/view/viral-titer-quantification-by-digital-droplet-pcr-3byl498p2go5/v1
DOI Link     : https://dx.doi.org/10.17504/protocols.io.3byl498p2go5/v1
Platform     : Droplet Digital PCR (Bio-Rad QX200 system)
Vector       : Recombinant adeno-associated virus (rAAV)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes absolute quantification of rAAV viral genome (vg) copies per mL
using Droplet Digital PCR (ddPCR) with a TaqMan FAM probe targeting the AAV Inverted
Terminal Repeat (ITR) region. The assay partitions each PCR reaction into approximately
20,000 nanoliter-scale droplets, amplifies target sequences within individual droplets,
and reads FAM fluorescence amplitude to count positive (template-containing) and negative
droplets. Absolute concentration is calculated directly by Poisson statistics without
reference standards, making it highly precise for viral vector titer determination.

The workflow consists of five major phases:
  1. Master mix preparation (TaqMan 2x ddPCR Supermix + primers + FAM probe)
  2. Seven-step serial dilution of AAV sample (1:40 then 1:10 x6; D1-D7)
  3. PCR reaction assembly and droplet generation (Bio-Rad DG8 cartridge + QX200)
  4. PCR thermocycling (95C activation / 94C-60C x40 cycles / 98C stabilisation)
  5. Droplet reading (Bio-Rad QX200 reader + QuantaSoft), QC gating, and titer calculation

A positive control (iDreV plasmid DNA, 103 ng/uL, 7453 bp) is run in parallel to validate
master mix composition and cycling conditions before sample titers are reported. Dilutions
D4-D7 (2.5e-5 through 2.5e-8) are loaded into reactions; the linear detection window is
100-10,000 copies/uL. Final titer is calculated by averaging concentrations from dilutions
within the linear range and back-calculating genome copies/mL.


ASSAY DESIGN
------------
Target region  : AAV Inverted Terminal Repeat (ITR)
Chemistry      : TaqMan (FAM/ZEN/IABkFQ dual-quenched probe)
Detection      : Absolute quantification (no standard curve required)
Instrument     : Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Digital PCR system

Primer Y87 (forward) : GGAACCCCTAGTGATGGAGTT  (100 uM, IDT)
Primer Y88 (reverse) : CGGCCTCAGTGAGCGA       (100 uM, IDT)
Probe (FAM)          : /56FAM/CGCGCAGAG/ZEN/AGGGAGTGG/3IABkFQ/  (100 uM, IDT)

MASTER MIX COMPOSITION (per reaction)
--------------------------------------
  1 unit  2x ddPCR Supermix for Probes (no dUTP)
  1 unit  Nuclease-Free H2O
  1 unit  Primer Y87 (100 uM stock)
  1 unit  Primer Y88 (100 uM stock)
  1 unit  FAM Probe (100 uM stock)
  Total master mix volume per reaction : 31.5 uL
  Sample volume per reaction           : 1.5 uL
  Total reaction volume                : 33 uL
  Volume loaded to DG8 cartridge      : 22.2 uL

Per full-plate composition reference:
  2112 uL   2x ddPCR Supermix
  1833.4 uL Nuclease-Free H2O
  38 uL     Primer Y87
  38 uL     Primer Y88
  10.6 uL   FAM Probe

Five independent master mix builds are performed in this BioScript protocol, one per
reaction (D4, D5, D6, D7, positive control). This avoids the split keyword and satisfies
single-static-assignment (SSA) constraints of the lilott8/BioScript compiler.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Master Mix Preparation
  For each of the five reactions, independently combine in a 5 mL Eppendorf tube:
    ddPCR Supermix + Nuclease-Free H2O + Primer Y87 + Primer Y88 + FAM Probe
  Mix each build sequentially (mix for 5 s per addition, 4 additions = 20 s total).
  Vortex briefly; spin down before use. Keep on ice until needed.

Step 2 | Serial Dilution (7 steps, 96-well non-skirted PCR plate)
  Use fresh siliconised tubes or low-binding PCR plate. Change tips between every step.

  Dilution 1 (D1): Add 39 uL nuclease-free H2O to row 1. Add 1 uL of AAV sample.
                   Mix 20 uL by pipetting up and down 20 times. Dilution = 1:40 (2.5e-2).
  Dilution 2 (D2): Transfer 10 uL from D1 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-3).
  Dilution 3 (D3): Transfer 10 uL from D2 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-4).
  Dilution 4 (D4): Transfer 10 uL from D3 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-5).
  Dilution 5 (D5): Transfer 10 uL from D4 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-6).
  Dilution 6 (D6): Transfer 10 uL from D5 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-7).
  Dilution 7 (D7): Transfer 10 uL from D6 into 90 uL H2O. Mix 10x. Factor 1:10 (2.5e-8).

  Seal plate with Microseal B. Store at 4 C until reaction assembly.
  Reactions are loaded with D4, D5, D6, D7 (linear detection range: 100-10,000 copies/uL).

Step 3 | PCR Reaction Assembly
  Using multichannel pipette, dispense 31.5 uL master mix into each 8-strip PCR tube.
  Add 1.5 uL of the appropriate dilution (D4, D5, D6, D7) to each tube.
  For the positive control tube: add 1.5 uL of iDreV DNA at 2.5e-6 dilution.
  Vortex and spin down each tube twice before loading to DG8 cartridge.
  Bench temperature vortex/spin step modelled as heat at 20 C for 10 s in BioScript.

Step 4 | Droplet Generation (Bio-Rad QX200 Droplet Generator)
  Pour Droplet Generation Oil (Bio-Rad 1863005) into the oil reservoir.
  Load 22.2 uL of each sample reaction into the middle row of a DG8 cartridge.
    IMPORTANT: Pipette slowly. Avoid introducing bubbles. Use reverse-pipetting technique.
  Load 70 uL Droplet Generation Oil slowly into the bottom row of each DG8 well.
  Cover cartridge with the DG8 Gasket (Bio-Rad 1863009).
  Insert cartridge into QX200 Droplet Generator. Run droplet generation.
  Target: approximately 20,000 droplets per well (~0.85 nL each).
  Using a multichannel pipette set to 40 uL, transfer droplets from the top well of
  the DG8 cartridge to the appropriate well of the ddPCR 96-Well Plate (Bio-Rad 12001925).
    IMPORTANT: Pipette very slowly (1 uL/s). Do not tilt or agitate the plate after transfer.
  Seal plate with Pierceable Foil Heat Seal (Bio-Rad 1814040) using the PX1 Plate Sealer
  at 95 C for 5 s.

Step 5 | PCR Thermocycling
  Run the sealed ddPCR plate on a Bio-Rad C1000 Touch Thermocycler (or equivalent).
  Ramp rate: 2 C/s. Lid temperature: 105 C.

  Programme:
    Stage 1: 95 C  for 10 min  (600 s)   -- Hot-start enzyme activation
    Stage 2: 94 C  for 30 s  \
    Stage 3: 60 C  for 60 s   > 40 cycles -- Amplification
    Stage 4: 98 C  for 10 min  (600 s)   -- Droplet signal stabilisation
    Stage 5:  4 C  hold                   -- Until droplet reader transfer

  BioScript IR representation (no loop construct; cycles unrolled as accumulated duration):
    heat at 95 C for 600 s   (stage 1)
    heat at 94 C for 1200 s  (stage 2: 40 x 30 s)
    heat at 60 C for 2400 s  (stage 3: 40 x 60 s)
    heat at 98 C for 600 s   (stage 4)
    heat at  4 C for 60 s    (stage 5 hold abstraction)

Step 6 | Droplet Reader Run (QC Gate 1: Reader Validity)
  Open QuantaSoft software. Load template: ITR-Shenqin-24 (24-sample configuration).
  Per-well template settings:
    Sample name: [sample ID]  |  ABS  |  ddPCR mMix no dUTP  |  FAM  |  Ch1 Unknown
  Load Droplet Reader Oil (Bio-Rad 1863004) into the reader reservoir. Prime reader.
  Place ddPCR plate in reader with black clasps facing down.
  Run experiment in FAM/VIC mode, reading by row.
  Set amplitude threshold to 1000 in QuantaSoft.

  QC Gate (BB2/BB3) -- keyed on D4 (highest expected signal):
    PASS (readerSignal4 > 0): >10,000 droplets per well; clear FAM+/FAM- separation.
                              Proceed to positive control QC.
    FAIL (readerSignal4 <= 0): Insufficient droplets or no FAM signal detected.
                               Repeat droplet generation from Step 4.

Step 7 | Positive Control QC Gate (QC Gate 2: Assay Validation)
  Apply dropletReaderDetection to the positive control well (iDreV at 2.5e-6 dilution).
  Expected result: 300-700 copies/uL.

  QC Gate (BB5/BB6):
    PASS (controlSignal > 0): iDreV within 300-700 copies/uL range.
                              Master mix composition and cycling validated.
                              Proceed to titer calculation.
    FAIL (controlSignal <= 0): iDreV out of expected range.
                               Investigate master mix assembly and cycling programme.
                               Do not report sample titers until positive control passes.

Step 8 | Titer Calculation (off-chip, QuantaSoft)
  In QuantaSoft, identify dilutions D4-D7 within the linear detection range
  (100-10,000 copies/uL). Dilutions outside this range (oversaturated or under-detected)
  are excluded.
  Average the concentrations from dilutions within the linear range.
  Back-calculate viral titer:
    Genome copies/mL = (avg copies/uL) x 1000 / (dilution_factor x (1.5 uL / 33 uL))
  Report titer as vg/mL.

Step 9 | Cleanup
  Discard used DG8 cartridges and gaskets as biohazard waste.
  Wipe down hood and bench surfaces with 70% ethanol.
  Dispose of AAV-containing materials per institutional biosafety guidelines.


CFG STRUCTURE (BioScript)
--------------------------
7 basic blocks, 2 if/else gates, 8 CFG edges:

  BB1  main          (entry) -- Steps 1-6 (BB1 is the sole entry node)
  BB2  bsbbif_2_t    (pass)  -- readerSignal4 > 0:  dispose plateWell4-7
  BB3  bsbbif_3_f    (fail)  -- readerSignal4 <= 0: dispose plateWell4-7
  BB4  bsbbif_4_j    (join)  -- dispose readerSignal4-7; detect controlSignal
  BB5  bsbbif_5_t    (pass)  -- controlSignal > 0:  dispose plateWellCtrl
  BB6  bsbbif_6_f    (fail)  -- controlSignal <= 0: dispose plateWellCtrl
  BB7  bsbbif_7_j    (term.) -- dispose controlSignal; end

  Edges: BB1->BB2, BB1->BB3, BB2->BB4, BB3->BB4,
         BB4->BB5, BB4->BB6, BB5->BB7, BB6->BB7

SSA strategy:
  lilott8/BioScript enforces strict single-assignment. Every variable may be consumed
  (appear as argument to mix/detect/heat/dispose) exactly once. The split keyword
  (syntax: result = split source into N) was avoided because: (a) all N output elements
  must be consumed or the compiler flags stranded droplets, and (b) correct syntax
  requires an integer literal (result = split source into N) which was missing in
  earlier versions causing ValueError in visitSplit. Instead, each manifest is used as
  a reservoir tapped by independent dispense calls; each tap produces a unique SSA token
  consumed exactly once downstream.

drain keyword:
  lilott8/BioScript has no visitDrain method (confirmed from ir_visitor.py). The drain
  keyword in .bs source compiles to dispose() in IR. Both pass and fail branch arms
  emit dispose() in the IR; only one arm executes at runtime (exclusive branches).

heat() in IR:
  heat() is an in-place mutation. The same variable token appears in multiple heat()
  calls (e.g. droplets4 through all 5 PCR stages) then is consumed once by a subsequent
  mix() or detect(). This is correct BioScript IR semantics, not an SSA violation.
  The IR counter showing N>2 appearances for heat-processed variables is expected.

ASCII requirement:
  The ANTLR4 FileStream used by this compiler calls codecs.decode(..., 'ascii') on the
  raw file bytes. Every character in a .bs file, including inside comments, must be
  7-bit ASCII (0x00-0x7F). Non-ASCII characters (em dash U+2014, degree symbol U+00B0,
  Greek mu U+03BC, arrow U+2192, multiplication sign U+00D7) in comments cause a
  UnicodeDecodeError at compile time. All such characters have been replaced with ASCII
  equivalents (hyphen, C, u, ->, x).

Duration units:
  BioScript supports s (seconds) and m (minutes) only. Hours are not supported.
  All durations in this file use seconds.

Biological scope note:
  This .bs file encodes one rAAV sample with 4 serial dilution reactions (D4-D7)
  plus one positive control. For a full 24-sample plate, 24 independent .bs executions
  would be required, or the file can be extended by repeating Steps S2-S6 with
  additional unique variable names per sample.
