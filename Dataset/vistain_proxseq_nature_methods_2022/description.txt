PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Quantification of Extracellular Proteins, Protein Complexes and mRNAs in Single Cells by Proximity Sequencing
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-022-01684-z
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-022-01684-z
Journal      : Nature Methods, Vol. 19, December 2022, pp. 1578-1589
Platform     : Proximity Ligation Assay (PLA) combined with single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq);
               plate-based (Smart-seq2 / 96-well) and droplet-based (Drop-seq / 10x Genomics Chromium) pipelines


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes Prox-seq (proximity sequencing), a method for simultaneous single-cell
measurement of extracellular proteins, pairwise protein complexes (up to n^2 per cell), and
whole-transcriptome mRNAs, using pairs of DNA-conjugated antibodies called Prox-seq probes. When
two probes bind proteins that are in physical proximity on the cell surface, their oligonucleotide
tails are ligated by T4 ligase via a universal connector region, producing a PLA product that
uniquely encodes the identities of both proteins. Only ligated products are captured and amplified
during downstream library preparation; unligated probes are automatically discarded. PLA products
are read out by next-generation sequencing alongside cDNA, enabling multimodal single-cell analysis.

The protocol is organised into three experimental arms that share probe design, ligation chemistry,
and sequencing infrastructure but differ in cell type, panel size, and library preparation platform:

  ARM 1 | Jurkat / Raji T cell / B cell validation (Drop-seq, 11-protein panel)
           Validates Prox-seq accuracy; confirms cell-type-specific PLA products; measures
           protein expression correlation with flow cytometry (Spearman rho = 0.88).

  ARM 2 | Human PBMC 38-marker panel (plate-based Smart-seq2 + 10x Genomics)
           Scales Prox-seq to 741 potential protein complexes; discovers the CD9-CD8 protein
           complex on naive CD8+ T cells as a novel interaction not previously described.

  ARM 3 | Primary human macrophage TLR signalling panel (plate-based, PFA-fixed cells)
           15 NF-kB pathway surface proteins (225 PLA products); LPS, PAM, or both stimulation
           at 5 min, 2 h, and 12 h; demonstrates additive signal integration, CD36 co-receptor
           activity, and identification of signalling inputs from receptor complex dynamics.

The complete Prox-seq workflow (probe incubation through sequencing library) takes approximately
1.5-3 hours of hands-on processing per sample batch depending on the arm.


PROBE DESIGN AND CHEMISTRY
---------------------------
Each protein target requires two Prox-seq probes: Probe A and Probe B, each consisting of a
monoclonal antibody conjugated to a single-stranded DNA oligomer.

Conjugation method:
  - Antibodies concentrated and buffer-exchanged into PBS using 50,000 MWCO concentrator (EMD Millipore)
  - Reacted with DBCO-PEG4-NHS ester (Sigma, 764019) in DMSO at 10:1 v/v DBCO:antibody solution
  - Incubated on ice for 1-2 h; purified through 50,000 MWCO concentrator
  - Antibody-to-DBCO ratio measured by UV-Vis (Nanodrop)
  - 1-2 ug DBCO-conjugated antibody (3-13 uM) combined with 80 uM azide-functionalized PLA
    oligomer (IDT) in PBS; allowed to react overnight at 4 C (click chemistry)
  - Long-term storage: reaction brought to 50% glycerol/PBS (Sigma Aldrich) at 4 C

PLA product structure (119 bp total):
  5' [PCR handle] -- [UMI] -- [Probe A barcode] -- [1st half connector] --
     [2nd half connector] -- [Probe B barcode] -- [Poly-A tail] 3'
  - 20 bases hybridise to connector oligomer to enable proximity ligation
  - UMI region corrects for PCR bias
  - Two barcode regions identify both proteins in each PLA product
  - 3' poly-A tail enables capture in poly-A-based scRNA-seq workflows
  - Universal connector region allows any Probe A to ligate with any Probe B

Physical range: 53.8-73.7 nm (estimated from ssDNA/dsDNA persistence length; spans typical
protein complex dimensions, ~10-50 nm)

Ligation solution (per reaction):
  - 50 mM HEPES pH 7.5
  - 10 mM MgCl2
  - 1 mM rATP (New England Biolabs)
  - 9.5 nM connector oligomer (IDT; sequence: TTTCACGACACGACACGATTTAGGTC)
  - 130 U/mL T4 ligase (NEB)
  - Volume: 100 uL (PBMC/macrophage arms); 300 uL (Jurkat/Raji arm)

Ligation efficiency: >90% of Prox-seq probes found ligated with other probes
(Extended Data Fig. 4b), explaining high concordance with flow cytometry.


CELL LINES AND PRIMARY CELLS
-----------------------------
- Jurkat (human T cell leukemia): model T cell line for ARM 1 validation.
  Cultured in RPMI + 10% FBS (Hyclone, Fisher Scientific), no antibiotics, 37 C, 5% CO2.
  Gift from J. Huang (Pritzker School of Molecular Imaging, University of Chicago).

- Raji (human B cell lymphoma): model B cell line for ARM 1 validation.
  Cultured in RPMI + 10% FBS, no antibiotics, 37 C, 5% CO2.
  Gift from J. Huang (Pritzker School of Molecular Imaging, University of Chicago).

- Human PBMCs (peripheral blood mononuclear cells): primary cells for ARM 2.
  Purchased from STEMCELL Technologies. Thawed at 37 C; washed 3x in 10 mL RPMI + 10% FBS
  (300g, 3 min); rested overnight at 37 C, 5% CO2 in RPMI + 10% FBS before use.

- Primary human macrophages: primary cells for ARM 3.
  Purchased from STEMCELL Technologies. Thawed, washed, and rested overnight in RPMI + 10% FBS.
  Plated in 10 wells of a non-tissue-culture-treated 24-well plate before stimulation.

PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

ARM 1 | Jurkat / Raji Drop-seq Validation
------------------------------------------

Step 1 | Jurkat / Raji Cell Mixture Preparation and DSP Fixation
  Cell counts    : 150,000 Jurkat + 150,000 Raji
  Centrifugation : 500g, 3 min; washed once with 1% BSA/PBS; spun again
  Plate format   : 96-well U-bottom plates
  Fixation       : 4 mM DSP (3,3-dithiobis-sulfosuccinimidyl propionate, Life Technologies)
                   in PBS; 37 C for 30 min (= 30m; IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Purpose        : Preserves surface protein architecture prior to probe incubation.
                   DSP is reversed later with 30 mM DTT (37 C, 30 min) before Drop-seq.
  Post-fixation  : Washed once with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS.

Step 2 | Prox-seq Probe Incubation (11-protein panel)
  Volume         : 90 uL probe binding buffer
  Concentration  : 5 nM per pair (2.5 nM Probe A + 2.5 nM Probe B); 6.7 nM each isotype
  Buffer         : PBS + 0.1% BSA (Thermo Scientific) + 0.1 mg/mL sonicated salmon sperm
                   DNA (Invitrogen) + 6.7 nM each isotype control
  Temperature    : 37 C for 60 min (= 60m; IR: 3600.0 SECOND)
  Nonspecific binding: Minimal; consistent with REAP-seq and CITE-seq levels
                       (Extended Data Fig. 5d); no increase with larger panel sizes.

Step 3 | Wash and PLA Ligation (3h rotation)
  Washes         : 2x with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS; 500g, 5 min each
  Ligation vol.  : 300 uL ligation solution (see Probe Design section above)
  Temperature    : 37 C for 3 h (= 180m; IR: 10800.0 SECOND); rotation throughout
  PI addition    : Propidium iodide (1 ug/mL final) added 30 min before ligation end
                   (IR: piMix at 1800.0 SECOND mix duration) to identify dead cells.
  Post-ligation  : Cells centrifuged at 500g, 3 min; resuspended in 1% BSA/PBS + 1/500 PI.
                   Live (PI-negative) cells sorted by FACS (one cell line per 96-well plate).
  DSP reversal   : 30 mM DTT, 37 C, 30 min; centrifuged at 300g, 3 min; resuspended
                   in 0.1% BSA/PBS before Drop-seq.

Step 4 | Drop-seq Encapsulation and Library Preparation
  Beads          : ChemGenes Macosko-2011-10(V+); barcoded beads
  Oil            : HFE7500 + 2% fluorosurfactant
  Droplet size   : ~1 nL
  Processing     : Droplets broken; beads subjected to reverse transcription, exonuclease
                   digestion, whole-transcriptome amplification. PLA products (poly-A tail)
                   and cDNA captured together; processed into separate sequencing libraries.
  Sequencing     : NextSeq Mid-output kit v2.5
                   Read 1: 20 bp | Read 2: 85 bp | i7 index: 8 bp
                   PhiX spike: 40% (low diversity of PLA libraries)
                   Custom primers: Read1CustomSeqB, DropPLA_Read2, DropPLA_i7Read
                   Library fraction: cDNA and PLA each receive 20% of total reads
  Alignment mRNA : Drop-seq tools v2.3.0; human reference genome GRCh38 2020-A
  Alignment PLA  : Custom Java program (github.com/tay-lab/Prox-seq)

Step 5 | Cell-Type PLA QC Detection  [QC Gate 1: BB2/BB3]
  Module         : cellTypeDetection
  Method         : t-SNE and PCA clustering on PLA product space; Wilcoxon rank-sum test
                   with Benjamini-Hochberg correction (P < 10^-40 threshold for markers)
  Expected result: Jurkat cluster enriched for PD1-CD3 (adj. P = 2.2e-55) and
                   CD3-CD3 (adj. P = 5.1e-53); Raji cluster enriched for ICAM1-HLADR
                   (adj. P = 2.7e-54) and HLADR-HLADR (adj. P = 2.4e-46).
  Pass (BB2)     : cellTypeSignal > 0 -> cell types separated by PLA products; dispose.
  Fail (BB3)     : cellTypeSignal <= 0 -> no cell-type PLA signal detected; drain.
  Both -> BB4    : Control passes to PBMC arm.


ARM 2 | PBMC 38-Marker Panel (Plate-Based + 10x Genomics)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Step 6 | PBMC Preparation: Fc Block and RNase Inhibitor  [BB4 join]
  Cell count     : 1 million rested PBMCs; all centrifugation at 300g, 3 min
  Wash           : 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS; pellet
  Fc block       : 95 uL 1% BSA/PBS + 5 uL TruStain FcX (BioLegend); 5 min RT
                   (IR: blockedPBMCs mix at 300.0 SECOND)
  RNase inhibitor: NEB; 1:1000 added to all subsequent buffers to protect mRNA.

Step 7 | 38-Marker Probe Incubation (3-Portion Serial Delivery)
  Volume total   : 300 uL (divided into 3 x 100 uL portions)
  Concentration  : 5 nM per pair (2.5 nM Probe A + 2.5 nM Probe B)
  Serial delivery: Cocktail divided into 3 portions due to volume constraints.
                   Each portion: incubate at 37 C for 15 min (= 15m; IR: 900.0 SECOND);
                   pellet; resuspend in next portion. Total probe exposure ~45 min.
                   (IR variables: pbmcPortion11[0], pbmcPortion21[0], pbmcPortion31[0])
  Post-incubation: Cells spun and washed 3x with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS (300g, 3 min each).

Step 8 | Wash (x3) and Ligation (30 min)
  Washes         : 3x with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS; 300g, 3 min each
                   (IR variables: pbmcWash11[0], pbmcWash21[0], pbmcWash31[0])
  Ligation vol.  : 100 uL ligation solution
  Temperature    : 37 C for 30 min (= 30m; IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Downstream     : For 10x experiment: cells transferred to 10x workflow at this stage
                   (dead cells removed with Miltenyi Dead Cell Removal Kit 130-090-101;
                   diluted to 1,000 cells/uL; processed using standard 10x protocol with
                   modified library prep; aligned with Cell Ranger v6.1.1 + GRCh38 2020-A).

Step 9 | Plate-Based Library Preparation (Smart-seq2) and 72C Pre-Step
  PI stain       : 1 ug/mL final; 10 min incubation
  Sorting        : Live (PI-negative) single cells sorted into 96-well plates; 1 cell/well
  Lysis buffer   : 4 uL Smart-seq2 lysis buffer per well (see Reagents section)
  Non-PFA cells  : Plates frozen at -80 C for storage.
  Pre-RT step    : Thaw on ice; incubate 72 C for 3 min (= 3m; IR: 180.0 SECOND)
  Library prep   : 2-4 uL per well used for PLA product pre-amplification; second PCR
                   attaches single-cell indexes and sequencing adaptor.
  Sequencing     : NextSeq Mid-output kit v2.5
                   Read 1: 75 bp | i5 index: 8 bp | i7 index: 8 bp
                   PhiX spike: 40%
                   Custom primers: SmartPLA_Read1, SmartPLA_i5Read, SmartPLA_i7Read
                   Minimum recommended reads: 10,000 reads/cell for PLA products
                   (diminishing returns above this threshold; Extended Data Fig. 9f-g)

Step 10 | Protein Complex QC Detection (Protein Complex Estimation Algorithm)  [QC Gate 2: BB5/BB6]
  Module         : proteinComplexDetection
  Algorithm      : Protein Complex Estimation Algorithm (iterative quadratic solver; Fig. 2a)
    Input        : Raw PLA product count matrix X (cells x PLA products)
    Step 1       : Calculate expected random PLA count f(X) from probe abundance
                   (maximum background = all proteins monomeric)
    Step 2       : Compute difference delta = observed minus expected random
    Step 3       : One-sided t-test + Benjamini-Hochberg correction (adjusted P < 0.05)
    Step 4       : Statistically significant delta -> update complex count; adjust X; iterate
    Step 5       : Non-significant delta -> complex count unchanged from previous iteration
    Convergence  : Absolute change in complex count < convergence threshold between iterations
  Key findings   : CD9-CD8 protein complex identified on naive CD8+ T cells (novel);
                   37 total complexes detected; 21 supported by literature/IntAct database;
                   CD9-CD8 subpopulation upregulates CCR7 and SELL (naive T cell markers).
  Pass (BB5)     : complexSignal > 0 -> protein complexes detected; dispose.
  Fail (BB6)     : complexSignal <= 0 -> no significant complexes above background; drain.
  Both -> BB7    : Control passes to macrophage TLR signalling arm.


ARM 3 | Primary Human Macrophage TLR Signalling Panel (Plate-Based, PFA-Fixed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 11 | Macrophage Stimulation (LPS + PAM, 5-Min Arm) and PFA Fixation  [BB7 join]
  Cell prep      : Macrophages rested overnight; centrifuged 300g, 3 min before stimulation
  Stimulation    : 100 ng/mL LPS (TLR4 agonist) + 40 ng/mL Pam2CSK4 (TLR2 agonist)
                   in RPMI + 10% FBS; 37 C for 5 min (= 5m; IR: 300.0 SECOND)
                   Note: This .bs file encodes the combined LPS+PAM arm at 5 min.
                   Remaining 9 conditions (LPS only, PAM only; 2h, 12h time points;
                   untreated control) share identical downstream fixation and staining.
  Dissociation   : TrypLE (Gibco); pellet; resuspend in 4% PFA (ChemCruz)
  Fixation       : 4% PFA; room temperature 25 C for 15 min (= 15m; IR: 900.0 SECOND)
                   Purpose: Locks receptor interactions at the stimulation endpoint;
                   prevents antibody cocktail from inducing artifactual signalling.
  Post-fixation  : Spun; washed once with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS.

Step 12 | Fc Block and 15-Marker TLR Probe Incubation
  Fc block       : 95 uL 1% BSA/PBS + 5 uL TruStain FcX; 5 min RT
                   (IR: macroBlocked mix at 300.0 SECOND)
  Probe volume   : 54 uL probe binding buffer; 5 nM per pair
  Temperature    : 37 C for 30 min (= 30m; IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Post-incubation: Pellet; wash 3x with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS (300g, 3 min each).

Step 13 | Wash (x3), Ligation (30 min), and Fixed-Cell Plate Sorting
  Washes         : 3x with 1 mL 1% BSA/PBS; 300g, 3 min each
  Ligation vol.  : 100 uL ligation solution; 37 C for 30 min (= 30m; IR: 1800.0 SECOND)
  Cell sorting   : Pellet; resuspend in 500 uL 1% BSA/PBS + 1/500 PI; live cells
                   sorted into 96-well plates containing modified Smart-seq2 lysis buffer
                   (6 uL/well with proteinase K; see Reagents section)
  Fixed-cell protocol:
    Step 1       : 56 C for 60 min (= 60m; IR: 3600.0 SECOND) -- proteinase K digestion
    Step 2       : 95 C for 10 min (= 10m; IR: 600.0 SECOND) -- proteinase K inactivation
    Storage      : -80 C; plates can be thawed on ice and proceed directly to library prep
  Library prep   : Same as ARM 2 plate-based protocol (SmartPLA primers, NextSeq)

Step 14 | Macrophage TLR Signalling QC Detection  [QC Gate 3: BB8/BB9]
  Module         : macrophageSignalDetection
  Method         : Logistic regression classifier trained on PLA product levels (Fig. 6a/b)
  Best time point: 2h (AUC = 0.82 +/- 0.08; 5-fold cross-validation)
  Top feature    : TLR2-TLR2 PLA product (highest logistic regression coefficient for LPS
                   vs PAM classification; Fig. 6b)
  CD36 activity  : Low-variance PLA products all contain CD36 barcode post-stimulation;
                   new CD36 complexes appear at 5 min post-LPS; CD36 co-receptor for
                   both TLR2 and TLR4 confirmed (Fig. 6c-f)
  Additivity     : LPS+PAM combined stimulation shows additive (non-synergistic) response
                   in both PLA products and protein levels; consistent with live-cell
                   NF-kB microscopy studies (ref. 34: Kellogg et al., Cell Rep. 2017)
  Pass (BB8)     : macrophageSignal > 0 -> TLR complexes and classifier signal detected;
                   dispose macroPlated.
  Fail (BB9)     : macrophageSignal <= 0 -> no receptor complex signal above baseline; drain.
  Both -> BB10   : Terminal node; dispose macrophageSignal; NOP.


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file  : vistain_proxseq_nature_methods_2022.bs
Compiler        : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based; github.com/lilott8/BioScript)
IR output       : vistain_proxseq_nature_methods_2022.ir
DAG output      : vistain_proxseq_nature_methods_2022_main_dag.dot
Basic blocks    : 10 (BB1-BB10)
If/else gates   : 3 (cell-type QC / protein complex QC / macrophage signalling QC)
CFG edges       : 12

Duration token conversions (BioScript supports s and m only; no h token):
  DSP fixation 30 min      -> 30m  -> IR: 1800.0 SECOND
  Probe incubation 60 min  -> 60m  -> IR: 3600.0 SECOND
  Ligation 3h (Jurkat/Raji)-> 180m -> IR: 10800.0 SECOND
  PI addition 30 min       -> 30m  -> IR: 1800.0 SECOND (mix duration)
  Fc block 5 min           -> 5m   -> IR: 300.0 SECOND  (mix duration)
  Portion incubation 15 min-> 15m  -> IR: 900.0 SECOND
  Ligation 30 min (PBMC)   -> 30m  -> IR: 1800.0 SECOND
  Pre-RT step 3 min        -> 3m   -> IR: 180.0 SECOND
  Stimulation 5 min        -> 5m   -> IR: 300.0 SECOND  (heat duration)
  PFA fixation 15 min      -> 15m  -> IR: 900.0 SECOND
  Probe incubation 30 min  -> 30m  -> IR: 1800.0 SECOND (macrophage arm)
  Ligation 30 min (macro)  -> 30m  -> IR: 1800.0 SECOND
  Proteinase K 60 min      -> 60m  -> IR: 3600.0 SECOND
  PK inactivation 10 min   -> 10m  -> IR: 600.0 SECOND

Verified IR behaviours:
  - All heat() durations compiled correctly from m tokens to SECOND (confirmed above)
  - pbmcProbesMix1[0] correctly reused across all three portion mix steps (IR lines 16, 18, 20)
    representing the shared probe reagent in the serial delivery design
  - Both pass and fail branches in IR emit dispose() for all three QC gates; drain in .bs
    source is resolved to dispose() in IR by the lilott8/BioScript compiler -- known
    compiler behaviour; does not affect CFG topology or correctness
  - NOP at BB10 (IR line 119) is expected compiler output for the empty terminal block
  - probeBuf1[0] (BSA_PBS) is dispensed but not consumed in a named downstream variable
    in ARM 1; compiler correctly handles this as a preparatory dispense before probesMixed

Modules (4 detection abstractions):
  proximalLigationDetection  -- PLA product sequencing alignment (custom Java; tay-lab/Prox-seq)
  cellTypeDetection          -- t-SNE/PCA clustering + Wilcoxon rank-sum test on PLA space
                                for Jurkat/Raji cell-type separation (Fig. 1e-j)
  proteinComplexDetection    -- Protein Complex Estimation Algorithm; iterative quadratic solver
                                with BH-corrected t-test; discovers CD9-CD8 complex (Fig. 2a, 3f)
  macrophageSignalDetection  -- Logistic regression on PLA products at 2h (AUC 0.82);
                                TLR2-TLR2 top feature; CD36 co-receptor detection (Fig. 6)

Biological scope note:
  ARM 3 encodes the combined LPS+PAM stimulation at the 5-minute time point as the
  representative macrophage condition. The remaining 9 stimulation conditions (LPS only,
  PAM only, and LPS+PAM at 2h and 12h time points; untreated control) share identical
  downstream fixation, staining, ligation, and library preparation steps and would
  each require a separate .bs file or an extended multi-branch CFG structure.
  The RAF1 KO parallel condition referenced in the isoclone protocol is not applicable
  here; all three arms target surface protein complexes, not genomic editing outcomes.
