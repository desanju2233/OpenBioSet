OVERVIEW
--------
Sprox-seq (Spatial Proximity Sequencing) is a multi-omic method that simultaneously profiles
three molecular modalities from intact tissue sections on a single 10x Visium slide:
  (1) mRNA transcriptome  -- poly(A) capture on spatial barcodes; standard Visium readout
  (2) Surface protein abundance -- PLA UMI counts from Probe A + Probe B per target protein
  (3) Protein complexes (interactome) -- proximity ligation products between co-localised
      protein pairs within 50-70 nm; quantified by Fisher's Exact Test + fractional overlap

The method combines a 32-plex antibody-oligonucleotide conjugate (AOC) cocktail with the
standard 10x Visium library preparation workflow, modified at two key steps: (i) Probe B
carries 6 terminal UTP residues cleaved by USER enzyme post-ligation to expose a poly(A)
tail, enabling co-capture of PLA products alongside mRNA; (ii) a U-Fwd primer is added
during reverse transcription and second-strand synthesis to enhance PLA amplification.

The method was applied to fresh-frozen human palatine tonsil tissue sections from two donors
(three biological replicates: samples A1, B1 from donor 1; D1 from donor 2), revealing:
  - Spatial maps of 32 proteins, 528 pairwise interactions, and thousands of mRNAs per section
  - Protein interaction network complexity positively associated with B cell maturation state
  - A protein-interaction-based B cell developmental trajectory (Non-GC follicles -> Dark zone
    -> Light zone) distinct from that inferred by RNA expression alone
  - Direct in situ capture of VLA-4 (ITGA4-CD29) -- VCAM1 mediated B cell-FDC interactions
    enriched in the Light zone of Germinal Centers across all three biological replicates

The BioScript file encodes sample A1 (representative replicate). Samples B1 and D1
follow an identical workflow and should be encoded as separate .bs files.

Median per-spot output across three replicates:
  RNA:     14,930 UMIs spanning 5,403 genes
  PLA:      4,799 UMIs corresponding to 425 unique PLA pairs
  Protein:  9,713 UMIs corresponding to 32 proteins


AOC PANEL
---------
32 antibody-oligonucleotide conjugate pairs:
  29 protein targets: CD19, CD20, CD21, CD35, CD23, IgM, IgD, IgG, CD32, CD38, CD24,
                      CD3, CD4, CD8, CD28, CD40, CD147, CD279 (PD-1), CD27, CD86, PDL2,
                      CD9, CD81, ICAM1 (CD54), VCAM1 (CD106), CD29 (integrin beta-1),
                      ITGA4 (integrin alpha-4), LFA-1 (CD11a/CD18), CD11a, HLADPRQ
   3 isotype controls: mouse IgG1 (mIgG1), mouse IgG2a (mIgG2a), mouse IgG2b (mIgG2b)

AOC preparation:
  - Antibodies buffer-exchanged to PBS using 50K MWCO concentrators; diluted to ~2 mg/mL
  - Incubated with 2 mM DBCO-PEG4-NHS ester (Sigma 764019) on ice 1 h (9:1 v/v)
  - Purified with 50K MWCO concentrators; diluted to ~8 uM
  - Mixed with 80 uM azide-functionalized PLA oligomers at 1:3 v/v; 4C overnight
  - Purified again with 50K MWCO; stored in 50% glycerol at -20C

Probe design:
  - Probe A: unique DNA barcode oligomer; azide-modified for DBCO conjugation
  - Probe B: unique DNA barcode oligomer + 6x terminal UTP residues at 3-prime end
  - Connector oligomer (IDT) bridges Probe A and Probe B during ligation
  - Both probes target the same antibody (same antibody clone, two conjugation products)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Tissue Section Mounting and Pre-warming
  Reagents    : TonsilTissueSection, SSC_3x (as pad)
  Operation   : mix (mount) + heat
  Duration    : 1 min at 37C on thermocycler adapter
  Purpose     : Pre-warms section before fixation to improve tissue adherence
  Source      : Methods: "slides were placed on a 37C heat block for 1 minute"

Step 2 | Tissue Fixation
  Reagents    : PFA_1pct (1% paraformaldehyde in PBS)
  Operation   : mix
  Duration    : 10 min at room temperature (25C)
  Purpose     : Cross-links proteins and nucleic acids; preserves tissue morphology
                and protein epitopes for downstream AOC staining
  Source      : Methods: "fixed with 1% paraformaldehyde (PFA) for 10 minutes"

Step 3 | Post-Fixation Wash with 3x SSC (x3)
  Reagents    : SSC_3x (x3 separate dispenses)
  Operation   : mix x3 (sequential wash cycles)
  Duration    : 30 s per cycle
  Purpose     : Removes residual PFA before blocking and AOC incubation
  Source      : Methods: "washed three times with 3x SSC buffer"

Step 4 | Blocking with 1% BSA
  Reagents    : BSA_BlockingBuffer (1% BSA in PBS)
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 10 min at room temperature (25C)
  Purpose     : Reduces non-specific binding of AOCs to tissue; performed after
                cassette mounting
  Source      : Methods: "blocked with blocking buffer at room temperature for 10 minutes"

Step 5 | AOC Cocktail Incubation
  Reagents    : AOC_CocktailPairs, ProbeBindingBuffer
  Volume      : 90 uL Probe Binding Buffer + AOCs at 2.5 nM each
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 90 min (5400 s) at 4C
  Purpose     : Probe A and Probe B pairs bind target proteins in situ on tissue section;
                spatial proximity of co-bound probes (<50-70 nm) enables ligation in Step 7
  Source      : Methods: "incubated with 90 uL of Probe Binding Buffer containing AOCs
                (each at 2.5 nM) at 4C for 1.5 hours"

Step 6 | Post-AOC Wash with Wash Buffer 1 (x4)
  Reagents    : WashBuffer1 (x4 separate dispenses)
  Operation   : mix x4 (sequential wash cycles)
  Duration    : 30 s per cycle
  Purpose     : Removes unbound AOCs to reduce background ligation noise
  Source      : Methods: "washed four times with Wash Buffer 1"

Step 7 | Proximity Ligation Assay
  Reagents    : LigationBuffer (T4 DNA ligase NEB M0202L + 10 mM ATP NEB P0756L
                + RNase inhibitor NEB M0314L)
  Volume      : 90 uL
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s) at 37C
  Mechanism   : Connector oligomer bridges Probe A and Probe B of proteins within 50-70 nm;
                T4 ligase seals the nick to generate a PLA ligation product encoding the
                identity of both probes (i.e., the protein pair)
  Source      : Methods: "incubated with 90 uL of Ligation Buffer at 37C for 30 minutes"

Step 8 | Post-Ligation Wash with Wash Buffer 1 (x4)
  Reagents    : WashBuffer1 (x4 separate dispenses)
  Operation   : mix x4
  Duration    : 30 s per cycle
  Purpose     : Removes unligated probes and ligation byproducts
  Source      : Methods: "washed four times with Wash Buffer 1"

Step 9 | USER Enzyme Digestion
  Reagents    : USER_EnzymeBuffer (USER enzyme NEB M5505L)
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s) at 37C
  Mechanism   : USER enzyme cleaves at each of the 6 UTP residues at the 3-prime end of
                the ligated Probe B strand, releasing a poly(A) tail on the PLA product.
                This poly(A) tail enables co-capture of PLA products alongside mRNA
                during permeabilization (Step 15) via poly(dT) spatial barcodes on the
                Visium slide surface.
  Source      : Methods: "USER Enzyme Buffer was then added and incubated at 37C for
                30 minutes"

Step 10 | Post-USER Wash with Wash Buffer 1 (x3) and 3x SSC Dip
  Reagents    : WashBuffer1 (x3), SSC_3x (SSC dip)
  Operation   : mix x3 + mix (SSC dip, abstracted as 20-count dip)
  Duration    : 30 s per WB1 cycle; 20 s for SSC dip
  Purpose     : Removes USER enzyme; prepares slide for brightfield imaging
  Source      : Methods: "additional washes with Wash Buffer 1" then "dipped into 3x SSC
                buffer 20 times prior to imaging"

Step 11 | Brightfield Imaging
  Reagents    : None (heat step abstracts imaging event)
  Operation   : heat
  Duration    : 5 min at 25C (RT)
  Purpose     : Slide removed from cassette; brightfield image and H&E morphology recorded
                on adjacent section for tissue cluster annotation and UMAP visualization;
                slide remounted after imaging
  Source      : Methods: "After imaging, slides were remounted"

Step 12 | Decrosslinking
  Reagents    : SSC_1x_DecrosslinkBuffer (1x SSC)
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 15 min (900 s) at 70C
  Purpose     : Reverses PFA crosslinks; restores mRNA mobility and PLA product
                accessibility for spatial barcode capture
  Source      : Methods: "treated with Decrosslinking Buffer (1x SSC) at 70C for
                15 minutes"

Step 13 | Room Temperature Equilibration
  Reagents    : None (heat step)
  Operation   : heat
  Duration    : 10 min (600 s) at 25C
  Source      : Methods: "equilibrated at room temperature for 10 minutes"

Step 14 | Wash Buffer 2 Rinse
  Reagents    : WashBuffer2
  Volume      : 100 uL
  Operation   : mix
  Duration    : 1 min (60 s) at 25C
  Source      : Methods: "100 uL of Wash Buffer 2 was added and incubated at room
                temperature for 1 minute"

Step 15 | Tissue Permeabilization
  Reagents    : PermeabilizationBuffer (10x Visium Permeabilization Buffer)
  Volume      : 70 uL applied dropwise
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 25 min (1500 s) at 37C
  Mechanism   : Permeabilization releases both poly(A)-tailed mRNAs and USER-digested
                PLA products (which now carry poly(A) tails) from the tissue; both are
                captured by poly(dT) spatial barcoded oligonucleotides on the Visium
                slide surface directly below the tissue section
  Source      : Methods: "70 uL of Permeabilization Buffer was applied dropwise and
                incubated at 37C for 25 minutes"

Step 16 | Post-Permeabilization Wash with 0.1x SSC (x2)
  Reagents    : SSC_01x (x2 separate dispenses)
  Volume      : 100 uL per wash
  Operation   : mix x2
  Duration    : 30 s per cycle
  Purpose     : Gentle wash; captured mRNAs and PLA products remain bound to spatial barcodes
  Source      : Methods: "wells were gently washed twice with 100 uL of 0.1x SSC"

Step 17 | Reverse Transcription (cDNA Synthesis)
  Reagents    : ReverseTranscriptaseReagents (10x Visium RT mix + U-Fwd primer modification)
  Operation   : mix + heat
  Duration    : 45 min (2700 s) at 53C
  Modification: U-Fwd primer added to RT reagent mix to prime PLA product amplification
                in subsequent PCR; not present in standard Visium protocol
  Output      : Spatially barcoded cDNA from both mRNA templates and PLA product templates
  Source      : Methods: "starting from reverse transcription (Step 1.2) through cDNA
                amplification, with two modifications: during second-strand synthesis and
                cDNA amplification, U-Fwd primer was added"

Step 18 | cDNA Amplification and SPRI Library Separation
  Reagents    : SPRIselect_06x (0.6x SPRIselect beads)
  PCR program : 98C 3 min; 12 cycles of (98C 20 s, 63C 30 s, 72C 1 min); 72C 5 min
  Operation   : mix (PCR abstracted as mix + heat) + 0.6x SPRI bead separation
  Key step    : 0.6x SPRIselect ratio separates amplified material by size:
                  Bead fraction (>600 bp) : mRNA-derived cDNA library (cDNA_mRNA_Library)
                  Supernatant (<600 bp)   : PLA ligation products (pla_Supernatant)
  Source      : Methods: "PLA products and mRNA-derived cDNAs were separated using 0.6x
                SPRIselect reagent, with the bead fraction containing mRNA-derived cDNAs
                and the supernatant containing PLA products"

Step 19 | PLA Library Cleanup, Indexing PCR, and Quantification
  Reagents    : SPRIselect_21x, IndexingPrimers, KAPA_HiFi_Mix, SPRIselect_12x, BufferEB
  Cleanup 1   : 2.1x SPRIselect (70 uL beads + 75 uL PLA supernatant); removes small
                fragments and salt from PLA supernatant
  PCR program : 98C 2 min; 13 cycles of (98C 20 s, 63C 30 s, 72C 5 s); 72C 5 min
  Cleanup 2   : 1.2x SPRIselect; elute in 25 uL Buffer EB
  QC          : TapeStation analysis; expected fragment size 150-200 bp
  Note        : 40% PhiX spike-in required during PLA library sequencing due to low sequence
                complexity of PLA barcode sequences
  Source      : Methods: "70 uL of SPRIselect reagent (2.1x) was added to 75 uL of the
                supernatant to complete PLA product cleanup. Purified PLA products were
                indexed and amplified. ... product concentration was measured using
                TapeStation analysis"

Step 20 | PLA Library QC Gate (BB2/BB3)
  Detection   : plaDataQC module on pla_Library for 30 s
  Branch pass : plaQCSignal > 0 -> dispose pla_Library -> proceed to BB4 (cDNA QC)
  Branch fail : plaQCSignal <= 0 -> drain pla_Library (library re-prep required)
  Compiler note: PATCH 1 applied -- plaQCPassFlag dummy dispense/dispose in BB2 body
                forces compiler to emit explicit jump instruction to BB4; without it
                bsbbif_2_t fell through without a jump in the IR

Step 21 | cDNA Library PhiX Spike and QC Gate (BB4/BB5/BB6)
  Reagents    : PhiX_Spike (1% vol/vol)
  Detection   : rnaExpressionDetection module on cDNA_FinalLib for 30 s
  Sequencing  : Illumina NextSeq 2000, 200-cycle P3 kit
                Read 1: 28 cycles (16 bp spatial barcode + 12 bp UMI)
                i7 index: 10 cycles; i5 index: 10 cycles; Read 2: 90 cycles (cDNA insert)
  Branch pass : rnaQCSignal > 0 -> nested VLA-4-VCAM1 detection gate (BB5)
  Branch fail : rnaQCSignal <= 0 -> drain cDNA_FinalLib (BB6)

Step 22 | VLA-4-VCAM1 Protein Complex Detection Gate (BB5/BB8/BB9/BB10)
  Detection   : vlaVcamInteractionDetection module on pla_SequencingLib for 60 s
  Basis       : ITGA4-VCAM1 and CD29-VCAM1 PLA pairs pass Fisher's Exact Test (adj. p < 0.05)
                and show highest enrichment in Light zone of Germinal Centers across all 3
                biological replicates; confirms direct in trans B cell-FDC interaction
  Branch pass : complexSignal > 0 -> dispose pla_SequencingLib + dispose cDNA_FinalLib (BB8)
  Branch fail : complexSignal <= 0 -> drain pla_SequencingLib + drain cDNA_FinalLib (BB9)
  Both join at BB10 -> dispose complexSignal -> BB7 (terminal)
  Compiler note: PATCH 2 applied -- pla_SequencingLib uses cDNA_FinalLib (defined in BB4,
                safely in scope) instead of pla_Library, which caused undefined SSA slot
                pla_Library2[0] in the original IR


DOWNSTREAM COMPUTATIONAL ANALYSIS
-----------------------------------
RNA alignment   : Space Ranger 2.0.1 (10x Genomics)
PLA alignment   : Custom Java aligner (github: tay-lab/Prox-seq)
Analysis code   : Python (github: jjxia955/Spatial-Prox-seq)
Data accession  : NCBI GEO GSE304749

Protein complex identification:
  Fisher's Exact Test (one-sided, 2x2 contingency table per PLA pair per spot)
  Multiple testing correction: Benjamini-Hochberg; adjusted p < 0.05 threshold
  Metric 1 -- Ratio of positive spots: proportion of spots where complex is significant
  Metric 2 -- Protein fractional overlap score:
              F_ij = (F_i + F_j) / 2
              F_i  = (O_ij + O_ji) / (sum_l(O_il) + sum_k(O_ki))
              Normalized, abundance-aware interaction strength per spot

Key protein complexes identified:
  Canonical heterodimers (high ratio of positive spots):
    CD19-CD21  -- B cell coreceptor complex; enriched in Non-GC follicles
    CD21-CD35  -- Complement receptor complex; enriched in Light/Dark zone (Germinal Centers)
    CD81-CD9   -- Tetraspanin complex; widely expressed
    CD29-ITGA4 -- VLA-4 integrin heterodimer; expressed broadly outside GC
  Non-canonical heterodimers:
    CD32-CD38  -- Detected in Non-GC follicles
    CD147-CD38 -- Detected in Non-GC follicles
    CD20-CD32  -- Enriched in Non-GC follicles; associated with complement activation
                  and interferon-gamma response pathways (pathway enrichment analysis)
  Cell-cell interaction (in trans):
    ITGA4-VCAM1 and CD29-VCAM1 (VLA-4 on B cells -- VCAM1 on FDCs)
    Highest enrichment in Light zone across all 3 biological replicates
    Captures direct B cell-Follicular Dendritic Cell synaptic contact without dissociation

Pseudotime trajectory (protein-interaction based):
  Method: Diffusion pseudotime (DPT); batch correction with Harmony
  Root: Non-GC follicles
  Trajectory: Non-GC follicles -> Dark zone -> Light zone
  Contrast: RNA-based pseudotime places Dark zone and Light zone at similar maturation
            state; protein-interaction trajectory uniquely resolves Light zone as most
            mature, consistent with its role in B cell selection
