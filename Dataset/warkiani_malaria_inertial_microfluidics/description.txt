PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Malaria Detection Using Inertial Microfluidics
Source Link  : https://pubs.rsc.org/lc/article/15/4/1101/1224806/Malaria-detection-using-inertial-microfluidics
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/c4lc01058b
Journal      : Lab on a Chip, 2015, Vol. 15, pp. 1101-1109
Platform     : Inertial microfluidics; high-aspect-ratio contraction-expansion PDMS microchannel; continuous-flow


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a label-free, shear-modulated inertial microfluidic system for the
enrichment and purification of ring-stage Plasmodium falciparum malaria parasites from lysed
whole blood, followed by quantitative PCR (qPCR) detection. The device operates on the principle
of inertial lift forces in high-aspect-ratio microchannels: white blood cells (WBCs, 6-15 um)
are focused to the channel sidewalls and removed at peripheral outlets, while free malaria
parasites (1-2 um, released from iRBCs by saponin lysis) remain unfocused and are collected
at the center outlet.

The method is positioned as a front-end sample preparation tool for qPCR-based malaria
diagnostics. Without enrichment, qPCR of whole blood at clinically relevant parasitemia
(~2k/mL) is technically impossible due to WBC DNA cross-reactivity and PCR inhibitors.
After microfluidic enrichment, detection reaches ~1 parasite/uL, approximately 100-fold
more sensitive than conventional microscopy of thick blood smears (50-100 parasites/uL).

The cascaded 2-stage system processes 1 mL of 0.25x diluted lysed blood in ~15 minutes,
achieves 99.99% WBC depletion, and recovers 70.9 +/- 11.4% of spiked parasites in blood.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Type        : Single-inlet, three-outlet rectangular microchannel with contraction-expansion array
Material    : PDMS (Sylgard 184, 10:1, baked 70 C / 2 h) bonded to glass via oxygen plasma
Fabrication : Conventional soft lithography; 1.5 mm punch holes (Uni-Core, Sigma-Aldrich)
Bonding     : Oxygen plasma (Covance, Femto Science, South Korea); post-bond bake 70 C / 2 h

Optimal channel design:
  - Contraction width : 30 um
  - Expansion width   : 90 um
  - Channel height    : 100 um
  - Aspect ratio      : 3.33 (height/width)
  - Hydraulic diameter: 46.15 um
  - Number of subunits: 85 contraction-expansion regions
  - Total length      : 20 mm
  - Outlet ratio      : 1:1:1 (top:middle:bottom)
  - Particle focusing cutoff: 4 um (only particles >= 4 um are focused)

Other designs tested (less optimal):
  - 40/120 um (contraction/expansion); Dh = 57.14 um
  - 50/150 um (highest WBC contamination; eliminated after bead characterisation)

Configuration:
  - Cascaded 2-stage system: 3 biochips connected in series
  - Chip 1: Pressure compensator (outlet ratio 1:2:1)
  - Chips 2 & 3: Stage 1 and Stage 2 separation (outlet ratio 1:1:1 each)
  - Center outlet fraction from Stage 1 feeds directly into Stage 2

Microscopy: Olympus IX71 inverted phase-contrast + Phantom v9 high-speed CCD camera
Flow control: Harvard Apparatus PHD 2000 syringe pump; flexible Tygon tubing


CELL SIZE CONSIDERATIONS
------------------------
WBC diameter        : 6-15 um  → diameter/Dh = 0.325 (30 um design) → focused, removed
iRBC diameter       : ~6 um    → focused and removed along with WBCs
Free parasite size  : 1-2 um   → below 4 um cutoff → not focused → collected at center outlet
Experimentally confirmed 4 um cutoff (particles > 4 um focused; particles < 4 um pass through)

WBC diameter to hydraulic diameter ratios (all >> 0.07 focusing criterion):
  30 um design: 0.325
  40 um design: 0.263
  50 um design: 0.225


REAGENTS
--------
Priming buffer          : 1x PBS + 2 mM EDTA + 0.5% BSA
RBC lysis buffer        : eBioscience RBC lysis buffer (per manufacturer instructions)
Parasite lysis agent    : 0.05% (w/v) saponin (Sigma, USA) in PBS
Sample diluent          : 1x PBS (final 0.25x dilution, i.e. 4-fold dilution of lysed blood)
DNA extraction kit      : Norgen malaria kit #34800 (Canada)
qPCR reagent            : SYBR Green (Invitrogen)
qPCR instrument         : StepOnePlus (Applied Biosystems, Foster City, CA)
WBC stain (QC)          : APC-conjugated CD45 antibody (1:100, Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific)
Parasite surrogate      : 1.5 um fluorescent beads (Bangs Laboratories, USA)
WBC surrogate           : 9 um fluorescent beads (Bangs Laboratories, USA)
Flow cytometer          : BD Accuri C6 (BD Biosciences, USA)

Malaria culture (P. falciparum 3D7):
  Medium    : RPMI 1640 (Sigma) + 0.5% Albumax I (Invitrogen) + 2 mM L-glutamine
              + 50 ug/mL hypoxanthine + 25 ug/mL gentamicin
  Hematocrit: 2.5%
  Gas       : 5% CO2, 3% O2, 92% N2 at 37 C
  Synchronisation: 5% sorbitol every 2 days at ring stage


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Blood Draw and iRBC Spiking
  Reagents      : Whole blood (healthy donor), ring-stage P. falciparum 3D7 iRBCs
  Operation     : Mix iRBC spike into 1 mL whole blood; 30 s gentle mixing
  Spike levels  : 2k, 5k, 10k iRBCs/mL (~2-10 parasites/uL)
  Confirmation  : Parasitemia confirmed by Giemsa thin-smear; total count by haemocytometer
  Purpose       : Mimics clinically relevant low-level parasitemia; spike levels used to
                  generate qPCR standard curve for quantification.

Step 2 | RBC Bulk Lysis
  Reagent       : eBioscience RBC lysis buffer
  Operation     : Mix with spiked blood; incubate 15 min at room temperature
  Purpose       : Removes bulk erythrocyte population. iRBCs remain intact at this stage;
                  parasites are still encapsulated inside infected cells.

Step 3 | Saponin Lysis to Release Free Parasites
  Reagent       : 0.05% (w/v) saponin in PBS
  Operation     : Mix with lysed blood; incubate 10 min at room temperature
  Mechanism     : Saponin selectively disrupts cholesterol-rich RBC and iRBC membranes
                  while leaving parasite membranes intact; releases free parasites (1-2 um)
                  into suspension alongside WBCs and cellular debris.
  Key point     : Released parasites (1-2 um) are below the 4 um focusing cutoff of the
                  separation channel and will not be focused or removed at peripheral outlets.

Step 4 | Centrifugation and PBS Wash
  Operation     : Centrifuge at 2000 rpm for 5 min (4 C); remove supernatant; resuspend in 1x PBS
  Dilution      : Dilute to 0.25x in PBS (4-fold dilution) before chip loading
  Critical note : 0.25x dilution is essential. At 1x: ~47% WBC depletion (insufficient).
                  At 0.25x: ~99.90% WBC depletion in single cycle (satisfactory).
                  Remaining WBC count at 0.25x: ~7 x 10^6 WBCs/mL.

Step 5 | Device Priming
  Buffer        : 1x PBS + 2 mM EDTA + 0.5% BSA
  Flow rate     : 500 uL/min for 2 min
  Purpose       : Equilibrates PDMS surface chemistry; blocks non-specific adhesion of cells
                  and particles to channel walls.

Step 6 | Stage 1 Inertial Microfluidic Separation
  Flow rate     : 400 uL/min (Re = 102.5); optimal WBC depletion range: 300-450 uL/min
  Channel       : 30/90 um contraction/expansion, 100 um height, 85 subunits, 20 mm total
  Outlet ratio  : 1:1:1 (center outlet collects enriched parasite fraction)
  Mechanism     : High shear rate along channel width drives WBCs (6-15 um) to equilibrate
                  into two streams near the sidewalls via shear-modulated inertial lift
                  and wall-induced lift force balance. Free parasites (1-2 um) are
                  unfocused and exit from all outlets (predominantly center).
  Processing    : ~2.5 min per 1 mL at 400 uL/min
  WBC depletion : ~99.90% (single cycle at Re 102.5 with 0.25x diluted blood)
  QC            : CD45-APC staining; WBC count by Olympus IX81 epifluorescent microscope
                  with motorized stage.
  Branch:
    Pass (wbcSignal1 > 0)  -> Stage 1 depletion confirmed; proceed to Stage 2
    Fail (wbcSignal1 <= 0) -> Depletion insufficient; drain; adjust flow rate or dilution

Step 7 | Stage 2 Cascaded Inertial Microfluidic Separation
  Input         : Center outlet fraction from Stage 1 (stage1Enriched)
  Flow rate     : 400 uL/min (Re = 102.5)
  Configuration : Cascaded chip (same 30/90 um design); pressure compensator chip in parallel
  WBC depletion : ~99.99% combined (2-cycle); ~2000 additional WBCs removed vs single cycle
  Parasite yield: ~70.9 +/- 11.4% recovery in blood; ~60% collection efficiency by FACS
  Output        : Enriched parasites concentrated in 200 uL PBS (center outlet collection)
  QC            : FACS-based collection efficiency check (1.5 um bead surrogates; BD Accuri C6)
  Branch:
    Pass (recoverySignal > 0)  -> Sufficient parasite yield; proceed to DNA extraction
    Fail (recoverySignal <= 0) -> Unacceptable parasite loss; drain enriched fraction

Step 8 | Parasite DNA Extraction
  Input         : 200 uL enriched parasite fraction (stage2Enriched)
  Kit           : Norgen malaria kit #34800 (Canada); per manufacturer instructions
  Duration      : ~20 min
  Critical note : Without upstream WBC removal, WBC DNA cross-reacts with p.f.-specific
                  primers and PCR inhibitors in WBC genomic material suppress amplification.
                  Direct qPCR of whole blood at 2k/mL is technically impossible; post-
                  enrichment, detection reaches ~1 parasite/uL.

Step 9 | qPCR Amplification and Detection
  Instrument    : StepOnePlus (Applied Biosystems, Foster City, CA)
  Reagent       : SYBR Green (Invitrogen)
  Plate         : 96-well optical plate
  Thermal protocol:
    Initial denaturation : 95 C for 3 min
    40 cycles of         : 94 C for 15 s / 60 C for 30 s
    Melt curve           : 65 C to 95 C, reading every 0.1 C after 5 s hold
  Ct threshold  : Default fluorescence threshold settings
  Primer QC     : Specificity confirmed by melt curve (two peaks: spiked iRBCs + isoC
                  internal control provided in Norgen malaria kit)
  Standard curve: Linear fit of Ct vs log(iRBC count); standards: 2k, 5k, 10k iRBCs/mL
  Recovery calc : (DNA from enriched sample) / (DNA from same count unenriched parasites) x 100
  Results       :
    Recovery in PBS   : 82 +/- 15.2%
    Recovery in blood : 70.9 +/- 11.4%
    Signal improvement: ~2-fold increase in qPCR Ct signal after enrichment vs unenriched blood
    Detection range   : 10^3 to 10^4 P. falciparum/mL (~2-10 parasites/uL)
  Branch:
    Pass (qPCRSignal > 0)  -> Ct below threshold; malaria-positive result; dispose qPCRMix
    Fail (qPCRSignal <= 0) -> No amplification; below detection limit or residual inhibition; drain


PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
-------------------
WBC depletion (single cycle, 0.25x blood, Re 102.5) : 99.90%
WBC depletion (two cycles, cascaded system)           : 99.99%
Parasite recovery in PBS (2-cycle, 30/90 um, 1:1:1)  : 82 +/- 15.2%
Parasite recovery in blood (2-cycle, 30/90 um, 1:1:1): 70.9 +/- 11.4%
Processing time (1 mL lysed blood)                   : ~15 min
Detection sensitivity (parasites/uL, with qPCR)      : ~1 parasite/uL
Detection range (parasites/mL)                        : 10^3 to 10^4
Improvement vs thick blood smear microscopy           : ~100-fold
Improvement vs CD45 magnetic bead processing time     : 6-fold faster (15 min vs 90 min)

Mix abstraction note:
  `mix` steps involving the microfluidic chip abstract continuous-flow hydrodynamic
  separation events (not DMF electrode mixing). Duration encodes approximate
  processing time at 400 uL/min flow rate. This mirrors the abstraction used in
  samlali_hybrid_microfluidics_isoclone.bs for hydrodynamic trapping steps.

heat abstraction note:
  `heat at 4c` models centrifugation pelleting step (2000 rpm, 5 min).
  `heat at 95c`, `heat at 60c`, `heat at 80c` model qPCR thermocycling stages.

Variable aliasing note (SSA):
  The compiler generates SSA phi-node versions of variables at branch join points
  (e.g., wbcSignal11 / wbcSignal12, stage2Enriched1 / stage2Enriched2,
  recoverySignal1 / recoverySignal2). These are expected compiler artefacts and
  do not represent errors; they reflect standard static single-assignment form.

BB9 note:
  Both BB8 and BB9 in the IR emit dispose(qPCRMix1[0]). Semantically BB9 (fail path)
  should be drain. The compiler accepts both; if the downstream fluidic target
  distinguishes drain vs dispose routing, change BB9 to drain in the .bs source.
