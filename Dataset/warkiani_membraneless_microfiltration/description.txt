PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Membrane-less Microfiltration Using Inertial Microfluidics
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/srep11018
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1038/srep11018
Journal      : Scientific Reports, 2015, Vol. 5, Article 11018
Platform     : Multiplexed inertial microfluidics (spiral PDMS microchannels, trapezoidal
               cross-section, passive pressure-driven flow; no external force fields)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a membrane-less microfiltration system based on massively parallelised
inertial microfluidics, capable of processing macroscopic sample volumes at ~500 mL/min. The
device consists of multiple PDMS layers with embossed spiral microchannels of trapezoidal
cross-section (~200 individual spirals in a full multiplexed unit). Individual separation
channels share a common inlet and exit through two outlets; cells subjected to hydrodynamic
inertial lift and Dean drag forces migrate to size-dependent equilibrium positions and are
collected at either the inner or outer outlet.

The protocol is divided into three independent applications demonstrated in the paper:
  1. CHO / Hybridoma cell retention from perfusion bioreactors (filtration mode, ~500 mL/min)
  2. Yeast (S. cerevisiae) microfiltration for broth fermentation clarification (~320 mL/min)
  3. CHO-K1 cell cycle synchronisation by size-based fractionation (150-300 mL/min)

As cells are separated solely by hydrodynamic forces from externally-driven flow, the system
runs continuously without membrane replacement, eliminating the major operating cost driver of
conventional microfiltration systems.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Material     : Polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS), Sylgard 184 (Dow Corning), 10:1 base:curing agent.
               Baked at 70 C for 2 h; final bonded assembly baked at 70 C for 2 days.
Fabrication  : Soft lithography; SolidWorks master mold design; precision micro-milling on
               aluminium (Whits Technologies, Singapore); PDMS layers bonded by O2 plasma
               (Harrick Plasma, USA) with manual alignment.
Fluid delivery: 3D-printed SLA guide layer with internal fluidic channels and conical pins;
               distributes flow equally across all microchannels.

CHO / Hybridoma filtration and fractionation device:
  - Cross-section : Trapezoidal, 600 um wide x 80x130 um
  - Loops         : 8; radius 8-24 mm
  - Spirals/layer : 4 (connected internally)
  - Multiplexed   : 84 spirals → 500 mL/min combined (~350 L/Day single unit)

Yeast filtration device:
  - Cross-section : Trapezoidal, 450 um wide x 30x70 um
  - Spirals/layer : 8
  - Multiplexed   : 180 spirals → ~320 mL/min combined

Inlets  : 1 shared inlet per multiplexed unit (sample loaded via peristaltic pump)
Outlets : 2 per unit
  - Outer outlet : Cell concentrate / recycle (filtration) or small-cell fraction (fractionation)
  - Inner outlet : Clarified filtrate / product harvest (filtration) or large-cell fraction


WORKING PRINCIPLE
-----------------
Inertial lift force (F_L proportional to a_p^4): arises from the balance of shear-induced
and wall-induced lift forces in straight channels; drives particles toward channel wall
equilibrium positions.

Dean drag force (F_D proportional to a_p^2): generated by two counter-rotating Dean vortices
in the top and bottom halves of curvilinear channels; applies drag transverse to the main
flow direction.

The trapezoidal cross-section creates stronger Dean vortex cores near the outer wall,
enhancing separation throughput and efficiency compared to rectangular channels.

Filtration mode  (~6 mL/min per spiral): inertial lift forces dominate; all particles focus
near the outer wall and exit via the outer outlet (cell concentrate). Cell-free medium exits
via inner outlet.

Fractionation mode (~1.5-2 mL/min per spiral): size-differential equilibrium positions emerge;
small cells (<14 um, G0/G1) remain trapped in Dean vortices near outer wall; large cells
(>14 um, G2/M) focus near inner wall. Each population exits a different outlet.


CELL LINES
----------
- AE-1 Hybridoma (ATCC HB-72): IgG1-secreting; model for perfusion bioreactor CHO retention
  and productivity monitoring. Cultured in Hybridoma-SFM (Gibco) at 100 rpm, 37 C, 5% CO2.
- CHO-K1 (ATCC CCL-61): adherent Chinese Hamster Ovary cell line; used for cell cycle
  synchronisation. Cultured in low-glucose DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep, 37 C, 5% CO2.
- Saccharomyces cerevisiae (Sigma-Aldrich, dry strain): model small microorganism (3-5 um);
  used to demonstrate yeast broth clarification and benchmark vs commercial membrane filters.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

APPLICATION 1: CHO / Hybridoma Cell Retention from Perfusion Bioreactor

Step 1 | Device Priming                                                [BB1 - main]
  Reagents  : BSA_EDTA_Buffer, PBS
  Operation : mix(primingBuffer, primingPBS) @ 20 s
  Purpose   : Wets PDMS microchannels; eliminates air bubbles. BSA (0.5%) and EDTA (2 mM)
              in 1x PBS prevent non-specific cell adsorption to PDMS channel walls.

Step 2 | CHO Cell Suspension Preparation                               [BB1 - main]
  Reagents  : CHO_Cells, BSA_EDTA_Buffer
  Operation : mix(choCells, bsaBuffer) @ 10 s
  Concentration: 1e4-1e7 cells/mL; separation efficiency >95% up to 1e7 cells/mL;
              declines above this threshold (Fig. 2b, paper).

Step 3 | Inertial Filtration — CHO Cell Separation                    [BB1 - main]
  Reagents  : choSuspension, primingMix
  Operation : mix(choSuspension, primingMix) @ 5 s
              [abstracts pressure-driven inertial focusing through 8-loop spiral at 6 mL/min]
  Detection : separationEfficiencyDetection @ 30 s
              [flow cytometry SSC-A vs FSC-A scatter gating at inner and outer outlets]
  Flow rate : 6 mL/min per single spiral (optimised from 1-10 mL/min range)
  Multiplexed: 84 spirals → 500 mL/min combined
  Efficiency: >95% separation at 6 mL/min up to 1e7 cells/mL
  Gate      : choSignal > 0 → BB2 (pass: recycle cells); choSignal <= 0 → BB3 (fail: dispose)

Step 4 | Cell Recycle and Perfusion Culture Continuation              [BB2 - pass only]
  Reagents  : choFiltered, CompleteDMEM
  Operation : mix(choFiltered, freshMedia) @ 60 s; heat @ 37 C @ 86400 s (1440 min = 1 day)
  Notes     : Enriched cells returned to spinner flask bioreactor; fresh media replenished.
              Microfiltration tests conducted daily over 10-day perfusion run.
              Cell density, viability, glucose, antibody titer, pH monitored daily (Fig. 2c).

Step 5 | Cell Viability Assessment — PI/FDA Dual Staining             [BB4 - join]
  Reagents  : Hybridoma_Cells, TrypanBlue, PropidiumIodide, FDAStain
  Operation : mix(hybridoma, trypan) @ 120 s → mix(trypanMix, piStain) @ 300 s
              → mix(piMix, fdaStain) @ 600 s
  Detection : cellViabilityDetection @ 30 s
  Stains    : Propidium Iodide (ex 488 nm / em 617 nm, dead cells)
              FDA — fluorescein diacetate (ex 490 nm / em 526 nm, live cells)
  Expected  : >90% viability post-separation (Fig. 2c)
  c-FOS     : No upregulation of c-FOS shear-stress marker vs negative controls
              (residence time <0.1 s prevents AP-1 pathway activation)
  Gate      : viabilitySignal > 0 → BB5 (pass: dispose); viabilitySignal <= 0 → BB6 (fail)

Step 6 | IgG Antibody Titer Monitoring — ELISA                       [BB7 - join]
  Reagents  : ConditionedMedia, IgG_ELISA_Reagent
  Operation : mix(mediaSample, elisaReagent) @ 1800 s; heat @ 37 C @ 1800 s
  Detection : antibodyTiterDetection @ 30 s
  Kit       : Abcam IgG1 ELISA, Cat. ab100548
  Expected  : Steady titer increase 0-50 ug/mL over 10-day run (Fig. 2d)
              Both sorted and unsorted hybridoma cells show similar proliferation rate
              and IgG production; confirms inertial filtration does not perturb cell
              productivity.
  Gate      : titerSignal > 0 → BB8 (pass: dispose); titerSignal <= 0 → BB9 (fail)


APPLICATION 2: Yeast (S. cerevisiae) Microfiltration

Step 7 | Yeast Suspension Preparation                                 [BB10 - join]
  Reagents  : Yeast_S_cerevisiae, BSA_EDTA_Buffer
  Operation : mix(yeastCells, bsaBuffer) @ 10 s
  Preparation: Dry S. cerevisiae rehydrated at 35 C for 10 min; centrifuged at 2000 g for
               15 min; washed twice with distilled water to remove debris; pellet
               resuspended in BSA-EDTA running buffer.
  Cell size : 3-5 um average (mandates small-channel 30x70 um trapezoidal geometry)
  Concentration: 0.05-1 g/L (1e5 cells/mL gives >90% efficiency; 1 g/L reasonable
               single-cycle performance; Fig. 3b)

Step 8 | Inertial Filtration — Yeast Separation                      [BB10 - join]
  Reagents  : yeastSuspension, PBS
  Operation : mix(yeastSuspension, yeastPriming) @ 5 s
              [abstracts inertial focusing through 30x70 um trapezoidal spiral at 2 mL/min]
  Detection : separationEfficiencyDetection @ 30 s
  Flow rate : 2 mL/min per single spiral (optimised)
  Multiplexed: 180 spirals → ~320 mL/min combined
  Efficiency: >90% at 0.05 g/L (single cycle); Fig. 3a flow cytometry scatter plots
  Benchmark : Compared against Cellulose Acetate and Teflon membrane filters in cross-flow
              mode (Fig. 3c); membrane flux declines progressively due to yeast deposition
              and fouling; inertial system maintains constant throughput throughout.
              Inertial unit: 180 spiral channels at ~320 mL/min.
  Gate      : yeastSignal > 0 → BB11 (pass: dispose); yeastSignal <= 0 → BB12 (fail)


APPLICATION 3: CHO-K1 Cell Cycle Synchronisation (Fractionation Mode)

Step 9 | CHO-K1 Monolayer Dissociation and Resuspension              [BB13 - join]
  Reagents  : CHO_Cells, TrypsinEDTA, CompleteDMEM, BSA_EDTA_Buffer
  Operation : mix(choSyncCells, trypsin) @ 180 s
              → mix(detachedCells, quenchMedia) @ 120 s
              → mix(quenchedCells, bsaBuffer) @ 5 s
  Trypsin   : 0.01% trypsin + 5.3 mM EDTA (Lonza); dissociates CHO-K1 sub-confluent monolayer
  Buffer    : 1x PBS, 2 mM EDTA, 1% BSA (MiltenyiBiotec); prevents agglomeration
  Concentration: 1e6 cells/mL in BSA-EDTA buffer

Step 10 | Inertial Fractionation — Cell Cycle Sorting                [BB13 - join]
  Reagents  : syncSuspension, PBS
  Operation : mix(syncSuspension, syncPriming) @ 8 s
              [abstracts size-differential inertial focusing at low flow rate fractionation]
  Flow rate : 1.5-2 mL/min per spiral (fractionation mode)
  Throughput: 150-300 mL/min multiplexed (1-5 L bioreactor processable within minutes)
  Size basis:
    G0/G1 cells: 11 +/- 2 um → exit outer outlet (smaller; trapped in Dean vortex)
    G2/M cells : 18 +/- 2 um → exit inner outlet (larger; focused near inner wall)
  Results   : Initial G0/G1:G2/M ratio 1.82:1 → 5.02:1 at outer outlet after 1 sort round
              G0/G1 purity ~80% outer outlet (from ~60% in unsorted input; Fig. 4d)
              Multiple sorting rounds further improve enrichment ratio.

Step 11 | Hoechst DNA Staining and FACS Cell Cycle Analysis          [BB13 - join]
  Reagents  : HoechstStain, BSA_EDTA_Buffer
  Fixation  : heat(fractionatedCells) @ 4 C @ 900 s (15 min; 4% PFA in 1x PBS)
  Operation : mix(fractionatedCells, bsaBuffer) @ 5 s
              → mix(stainBuffer, hoechstStain) @ 1800 s (30 min staining)
  Stain mix : 10 ug/mL RNase (i-DNA Biotechnology) + 3.8 mM sodium citrate
              + Hoechst NucBlue LiveReadyProbes (Invitrogen) in 1x PBS
  Detection : cellCycleDetection @ 30 s
              [BD LSR II flow cytometer; FlowJo analysis software]
              [G0/G1 = 2N DNA low Hoechst; G2/M = 4N DNA high Hoechst; Fig. 4d]
  Gate      : syncSignal > 0 → BB14 (pass: recover); syncSignal <= 0 → BB15 (fail: dispose)

Step 12 | Synchronised Cell Recovery for Downstream Use              [BB14 - pass only]
  Reagents  : stainedSync, ConditionedMedia
  Operation : mix(stainedSync, conditionedMedia2) @ 60 s; heat @ 37 C @ 3600 s (60 min)
  Purpose   : G0/G1-enriched outer-outlet fraction transferred to culture vessel or assay.
  Applications:
    - Anti-cancer drug screening (drugs target specific cell cycle phases)
    - Recombinant protein production (G1-phase CHO-derived cells grow faster; higher yield)
    - CHO re-inoculation for synchronised bioreactor production runs

Abstraction notes:
  `mix`  — abstracts pressure-driven inertial focusing through spiral microchannels;
            not DMF electrode mixing. Duration encodes transit/processing time at stated
            flow rate. No reagent mixing in the DMF sense occurs at these steps.
  `heat` — abstracts incubator temperature control (37 C culture, 4 C fixation).
  drain vs dispose — compiler emits dispose() for both in IR; distinction is source-level
            only for human readability of pass/fail branch intent.

Platform note:
  This protocol encodes passive inertial microfluidics (no DMF electrodes, no EWOD actuation,
  no droplet-in-channel operations). Device operates at macroscopic bulk flow rates
  (mL/min to L/min range) using an external peristaltic pump (Masterflex, Cole-Parmer).
  BioScript mix abstractions reflect bulk hydrodynamic channel flow events, not droplet
  manipulation. All three applications share a single function (main) in the compiled CFG.
