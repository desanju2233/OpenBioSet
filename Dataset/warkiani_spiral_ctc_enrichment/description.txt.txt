PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : An Ultra-High-Throughput Spiral Microfluidic Biochip for the Enrichment of
               Circulating Tumor Cells
Source Link  : https://www.rsc.org/publishing/journals/analyst
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1039/c4an00355a
Journal      : Analyst, 2014, Vol. 139, pp. 3245-3255
Platform     : Multiplexed spiral microfluidic biochip (continuous-flow, label-free,
               size-based inertial focusing)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes an ultra-high-throughput, label-free method for the enrichment of
circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from peripheral blood of cancer patients using a multiplexed
spiral microfluidic biochip. The device exploits inertial focusing and Dean drag forces within
rectangular-cross-section spiral microchannels to achieve size-based separation of CTCs from
hematological cells without the need for antibody labelling or immunomagnetic steps.

The protocol is divided into three independent functional experiments:
  1. MCF-7 spiked recovery efficiency experiment -- validates device recovery and purity
     using a breast adenocarcinoma cell line (MCF-7) spiked into healthy donor blood.
  2. T24 cell viability experiment -- independently validates that neither the RBC lysis
     buffer nor the spiral shear forces compromise cell viability, using a bladder
     carcinoma cell line (T24) spiked into a separate healthy donor blood sample.
  3. Clinical CTC enrichment from patient blood -- isolates and identifies CTCs from
     7.5 mL blood samples of Stage IV metastatic breast cancer and NSCLC patients, with
     downstream immunofluorescence (IF) identification and HER2 FISH analysis.

The complete sample-to-CTC workflow (lysis to collection) takes approximately 10 minutes
for 7.5 mL of blood, compared to 30 minutes for the previous integrated biochip design.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Type         : Multiplexed spiral microfluidic biochip
Configuration: 3 stacked single-layer PDMS spiral chips bonded together; 2 inlets, 2 outlets
Fabrication  : SU-8 silicon molds by standard photolithography; silanized with trichloro
               (1H,1H,2H,2H-perfluorooctyl) silane (Sigma Aldrich, USA) to render hydrophobic.
               PDMS prepolymer mixed 1:10 (Sylgard 184, Dow Corning, USA), degassed, poured
               onto SU-8 mold, cured at 80 C for 1-2 h. Four fluidic access holes punched.
               Three spiral chips bonded using oxygen plasma and manual alignment.
               Whole assembly bonded to microscopic glass slide by air plasma machine.

Channel      : Rectangular cross-section spiral microchannels
Separation   : Inertial focusing + Dean drag forces
               - Centrifugal forces in radial direction form two counter-rotating Dean vortices
               - CTCs (>15 um): equilibrate near inner microchannel wall (CTC outlet)
               - WBCs, RBCs, platelets (<20 um): migrate to outer wall (waste outlet)
Enrichment   : ~10^4-fold from RBC-depleted nucleated cell fraction

Inlets       : I1 (sample: nucleated cell fraction), I2 (sheath: PBS at 750 uL/min)
Outlets      : Inner wall (CTC collection), Outer wall (WBC/waste)


CELL LINES
----------
- MCF-7 (human breast adenocarcinoma, HTB-22, ATCC, ~20 um diameter):
  Used for recovery efficiency characterisation (Protocol 1).
  Cultured in high-glucose DMEM (Invitrogen) + 10% FBS (Invitrogen) + 1% penicillin-
  streptomycin (Invitrogen), 37 C, 5% CO2, humidified atmosphere, until 80% confluence.
  Sub-cultivated 1:3 twice weekly (BD Bioscience 6-well plates); media replaced every 48 h.
  Dissociated with 0.01% trypsin + 5.3 mM EDTA (Lonza, Switzerland).
  Resuspended in PBS + 2 mM EDTA + 0.5% BSA (Miltenyi Biotec, Germany).

- T24 (human bladder carcinoma, HTB-4, ATCC, ~30 um diameter):
  Used for cell viability characterisation (Protocol 2). Independent experiment from MCF-7.
  Same culture conditions as MCF-7.


CLINICAL SAMPLES
----------------
- 10 patients: 5 metastatic breast cancer (Stage IV), 5 non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC, Stage IV)
- 5 healthy donor controls
- 7.5 mL whole blood per sample, collected in Vacutainer EDTA tubes (Becton-Dickinson, NJ, USA)
- Processed within 2-4 h to prevent blood coagulation
- IRB-approved protocol (Institutional Review Board, Singapore)
- Collected in collaboration with National Cancer Centre Singapore and
  National University Hospital, Singapore


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

===========================================================
PROTOCOL 1: MCF-7 Spiked Recovery Efficiency Experiment
===========================================================

Step 1 | MCF-7 Cell Culture and Preparation
  Cell line    : MCF-7 (breast adenocarcinoma, ~20 um)
  Media        : High-glucose DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen-strep, 37 C, 5% CO2
  Duration     : 48 h representative culture period (172800 s; BioScript: heat at 37c for 2880m)
  Dissociation : 0.01% trypsin + 5.3 mM EDTA
  Resuspension : PBS + 2 mM EDTA + 0.5% BSA (EDTA_BSA_Buffer)
  Note         : MCF-7 and T24 are spiked in separate, fully independent experiments.

Step 2 | Healthy Donor Blood Collection and RBC Lysis (MCF-7 arm)
  Volume       : 7.5 mL whole blood, Vacutainer EDTA tubes
  Lysis        : Ammonium chloride RBC lysis buffer (Bioscience, USA), 5 min at RT,
                 continuous gentle mixing
  Centrifugation: 1000xg, 5 min (BioScript: heat at 25c for 5m)
  Resuspension : PBS; concentrated to 3.75 mL (2x concentration = ~14e6 WBCs)
  Effect       : RBC lysis removes contaminants; no significant cancer cell loss
                 (98% T24 recovery vs whole blood, p=0.82; no morphology change, p=0.2)

Step 3 | MCF-7 Spike into Nucleated Cell Fraction
  Concentration: 500 MCF-7 cells per 7.5 mL whole blood (clinically relevant level)
  Buffer       : PBS + 2 mM EDTA + 0.5% BSA
  Operation    : mix, 1 min

Step 4 | On-Chip CTC Separation -- MCF-7 Spiked Sample
  Device       : Multiplexed spiral biochip (3 stacked chips)
  Sample inlet : 350 uL/min; Sheath inlet: 750 uL/min PBS
  Duration     : ~10 min for 7.5 mL blood (BioScript: mix for 10m)
  Physics      : Dean vortex + inertial lift; CTCs to inner wall, WBCs to outer wall
  Detection    : ctcDetection module, 30 s
  Results      : 87.6% MCF-7 recovery efficiency; 76.4% T24 recovery efficiency
                 (500 cells/7.5 mL, n=3, Anova single factor, P < 0.05)
  QC Branch    : ctcSignal > 0  -> dispose (pass, BB2)
                 ctcSignal <= 0 -> drain   (fail, BB3)
                 Both converge at BB4 (mcf7Join)

===========================================================
PROTOCOL 2: T24 Cell Viability Experiment (Independent)
===========================================================

Step 5 | T24 Cell Culture and Preparation
  Cell line    : T24 (bladder carcinoma, ~30 um)
  Media        : High-glucose DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen-strep, 37 C, 5% CO2
  Duration     : 48 h (172800 s; BioScript: heat at 37c for 2880m)
  Note         : Completely independent from Protocol 1; own blood sample prepared separately.

Step 6 | Healthy Donor Blood Collection and RBC Lysis (T24 arm)
  Volume       : 7.5 mL whole blood (independent from Protocol 1 blood sample)
  Lysis        : Same ammonium chloride lysis protocol; 5 min RT
  Centrifugation: 1000xg, 5 min (heat at 25c for 5m)
  Note         : T24 cells spiked before lysis; before/after counts compared (Fig. 3A)

Step 7 | T24 Spike and On-Chip Separation
  Spike        : 500 T24 cells per 7.5 mL; resuspended in EDTA_BSA_Buffer
  Separation   : 350 uL/min sample, 750 uL/min sheath, 10 min
  Operation    : mix (BioScript abstraction of spiral chip separation)

Step 8 | PFA Fixation (Viability Arm)
  Reagent      : 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) (Sigma, USA)
  Duration     : 10 min at RT (25 C)
  Operation    : mix + heat at 25c for 10m

Step 9 | Triton X-100 Permeabilization (Viability Arm)
  Reagent      : 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS (Sigma, USA)
  Duration     : 5 min
  Operation    : mix, 5 min

Step 10 | Cell Viability Staining -- PI + Pan-CK/CD45 Panel
  Panel        : pan-CK FITC (1:100) + APC-CD45 (1:100) + Hoechst + Propidium Iodide
                 Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific, Singapore; Hoechst dye in PBS buffer
  PI           : ex 488 nm / em 617 nm; labels dead cells
  Pan-CK       : confirms recovered cells are cancer cells (not WBC contamination)
  Incubation   : 30 min on ice (4 C) (BioScript: heat at 4c for 30m)
  Quantification: BD Accuri C6 flow cytometer; gated on FSC/SSC and fluorescence
  Detection    : viabilityDetection module, 30 s
  Results      : <10% PI+ cells (>90% viable); high shear in spiral microchannels
                 has no adverse effect on cell viability (Fig. 4B)
  QC Branch    : viabilitySignal > 0  -> dispose (pass, BB6)
                 viabilitySignal <= 0 -> drain   (fail, BB7)
                 Both converge at BB8 (viabJoin)

===========================================================
PROTOCOL 3: Clinical CTC Enrichment from Patient Blood
===========================================================

Step 11 | Clinical Blood Collection and RBC Lysis
  Samples      : 10 patient samples (5 breast, 5 NSCLC; Stage IV); 5 healthy controls
  Volume       : 7.5 mL per sample, Vacutainer EDTA tubes, within 2-4 h
  Lysis        : Ammonium chloride, 5 min RT; centrifuge 1000xg 5 min; resuspend in PBS
  Concentration: 3.75 mL (2x); ~14e6 WBCs (optimal for purity per Fig. 2B)
  Input velocity: 350 uL/min

Step 12 | On-Chip Separation of Clinical Sample
  Device       : Multiplexed spiral biochip, same parameters as Protocol 1
  Duration     : ~10 min for 7.5 mL blood
  WBC depletion: ~99.99% (from ~7e6 input nucleated cells)
  Robustness   : 900-1200 WBCs / 5 min uniformly across 0-30 min window (Fig. 2C)

Step 13 | PFA Fixation (Clinical IF Arm)
  Reagent      : 4% paraformaldehyde (PFA) (Sigma, USA)
  Duration     : 10 min at RT (25 C)
  Operation    : mix + heat at 25c for 10m
  Note         : Required for intracellular pan-cytokeratin antibody staining

Step 14 | Triton X-100 Permeabilization (Clinical IF Arm)
  Reagent      : 0.1% Triton X-100 in PBS (Sigma, USA)
  Duration     : 5 min
  Operation    : mix, 5 min

Step 15 | Immunofluorescence (IF) Staining for CTC Identification
  Panel        : pan-CK FITC (1:100) + APC-CD45 (1:100) + Hoechst
                 Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific, Singapore
  CTC criteria : Hoechst+ / Pan-CK+ / CD45-
  Incubation   : 30 min on ice (4 C)
  Imaging      : Epi-fluorescence microscopy + BD Accuri C6 flow cytometry
  Detection    : ctcDetection module, 30 s
  Results      :
    Breast cancer   : 20-67 CTCs/mL (n=5; 100% detection)
    NSCLC           : 33-135 CTCs/mL (n=5; 100% detection)
    Healthy control : 1-4 CK+ cells/mL (background threshold)
    CTC diameter    : 15-25 um (lung); 20-35 um (breast)
    CTC morphology  : Intrapatient pleomorphism observed; eccentric, bi-lobed,
                      kidney-shaped nuclei; microemboli (clusters) in some samples
    Stem cell markers: CD166+/CD133+ sub-population identified (~1 in 135 cells)
  QC Branch    : clinicalSignal > 0  -> HER2 FISH (BB10)
                 clinicalSignal <= 0 -> drain(stainedClinical) (BB11)
                 Both converge at BB12 (finalCleanup)

Step 16 | HER2 FISH Analysis on Isolated CTCs (pass branch only)
  Patient subset: HER2+ breast cancer patients (n=5)
  HER2 criterion: HER2/CEP17 ratio > 2 in single nuclei = amplified

  (a) Cytospin        : Cells spun onto slides (Cytospin, Thermo Scientific, USA);
                        600 rpm, 6 min.
  (b) PFA fixation    : 4% PFA, 10 min RT.
  (c) Dehydration     : Ethanol series 80%, 90%, 100%.
                        BioScript: combined with fixation as heat at 25c for 10m.
  (d) RNase treatment : 4 mg/mL RNase (Sigma, USA) in PBS/0.2% Tween 20;
                        40 min at 37 C. Removes RNA for clean probe hybridization.
                        BioScript: mix + heat at 37c for 40m (2400s).
  (e) Denaturation    : 70% formamide/2xSSC, 80 C, 10 min.
                        Opens dsDNA for probe access.
                        BioScript: mix + heat at 80c for 10m.
  (f) Probe hybridization: HER-2/neu gene locus (red) + CEP17 (green);
                        applied at 42 C, overnight, dark and humid conditions.
                        BioScript: heat at 42c for 600m (10h approximation; s/m limit).
  (g) Post-hyb washes : Wash 1: 50% formamide/2xSSC, 45 C, shaking, 5 min.
                        Wash 2: 2xSSC, 45 C, shaking, 5 min.
                        BioScript: two sequential heat at 45c for 5m steps.
  (h) DAPI counterstain: DAPI (4',6-diamidino-2-phenylindole); coverslipped (50x50 mm,
                        Fisher Scientific, USA).

  Detection    : her2FISHDetection module, 30 s
  Results      : 3 of 5 HER2+ breast cancer patients showed HER2-negative CTCs
                 (HER2/CEP17 < 2), indicating heterogeneity between primary tumor
                 and disseminated circulating cells (Fig. 5C).

Key abstractions:
  - Continuous-flow spiral chip separation abstracted as `mix` (same convention as
    samlali_hybrid_microfluidics_isoclone.bs for channel-based trapping events).
  - Centrifugation at 1000xg abstracted as `heat at 25c` (no centrifuge primitive).
  - Cell culture pre-incubation abstracted as `heat at 37c for 2880m` (samlali convention).
  - Overnight FISH hybridization at 42C approximated as 600m (BioScript s/m-only limit).
  - Three independent protocols encoded in sequence gated by three if/else QC branches.
  - T24 viability arm uses its own independent WholeBlood dispense (FIX #1, #2).

  FIX #4 : PFA fixation (Paraformaldehyde) and Triton X-100 permeabilization added
