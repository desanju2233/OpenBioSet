PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Ultra-fast, label-free isolation of circulating tumor cells from blood using spiral microfluidics
Source Link  : https://www.nature.com/articles/nprot.2016.003
Paper DOI    : http://dx.doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2016.003
Journal      : Nature Protocols, Vol. 11, No. 1, pp. 134-148 (2016)
Platform     : Spiral inertial microfluidic biochip (PDMS/glass, continuous-flow, label-free)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a label-free, size-based method for isolating viable circulating tumor cells
(CTCs) from peripheral blood using a spiral inertial microfluidic biochip. CTCs -- rare cancer cells
shed from primary or metastatic tumors into the bloodstream -- occur at frequencies as low as one cell
per 10^7-10^9 hematological cells in metastatic cancer patients, making reliable isolation a major
technical challenge.

The spiral biochip exploits the combined effect of inertial lift force (FL) and Dean drag force (FD)
in a curvilinear microchannel. Larger CTCs (typically 15-20 um) focus near the inner wall at the
outlet, while smaller blood cells (RBCs 3-8 um, WBCs 10-15 um, platelets 3-8 um) migrate along
Dean vortex streamlines toward the outer wall. This passive, continuous-flow separation requires no
antibodies, magnetic beads, or external force fields.

Key performance metrics:
  - >=85% CTC recovery across multiple cancer cell lines
  - 99.99% WBC depletion (4-log) from healthy samples
  - 7.5 mL lysed blood processed in 37.5 min (single chip) or 12.5 min (3x multiplexed chip)
  - CTCs collected in suspension (viable), compatible with culture, FISH, and sequencing

The protocol has been validated in clinical samples from patients with metastatic non-small-cell lung
cancer (n=15; 10-1535 CK+ CTCs isolated) and breast cancer (n=15; 12-1275 CK+ CTCs isolated),
with a negative-control threshold of >7 CK+ cells per sample defining a positive patient.


SEPARATION PHYSICS
------------------
The spiral microchannel has a rectangular cross-section (500 um wide x 170 um deep) in a two-loop
configuration with radius of curvature ~10 mm. Flow in the curved channel generates secondary
cross-sectional Dean vortices perpendicular to the primary flow direction. The Dean number is:

    De = Re * sqrt(DH / 2*RC)

where Re is the Reynolds number, DH is the hydraulic diameter, and RC is the radius of curvature.
The Dean migration velocity is:

    U_Dean = 1.8e-4 * De^1.63

For particles with diameter comparable to channel dimensions (ac/h > 0.07), inertial lift forces
(shear-induced FL_shear and wall-induced FL_wall) direct cells to equilibrium focusing positions.
The balance of FL and FD results in two focusing positions near the inner channel wall for larger
CTCs, while smaller cells disperse and follow Dean streamlines toward the outer wall.

Design parameters: 75 um sample inlet (outer) / 425 um sheath inlet (inner); 150 um CTC collection
outlet (inner) / 350 um waste outlet (outer). Size cutoff: ~12 um (CTCs >=12 um recoverable).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Material       : PDMS (Sylgard 184, 10:1 base:curing agent) bonded to glass slide (76 x 52 mm)
Channel        : 500 um wide x 170 um deep (±5 um); two-loop spiral; radius ~10 mm
Fabrication    : Standard photolithography (AZ9260 photoresist, DRIE ~165 um etch depth on 6-inch
                 silicon wafer) + soft lithography; silanization with trichloro(1H,2H,2H-perfluorooctyl)
                 silane; plasma bonding (O2 plasma, 300 W, 2 min 30 s)
Configurations :
  Single chip  : 1 spiral layer; 100 uL/min sample + 750 uL/min sheath; 7.5 mL in ~37.5 min
  Multiplexed  : 3 PDMS layers stacked via plasma bonding + manual alignment;
                 2100 uL/min sheath; 7.5 mL in <12.5 min (3 sorters in parallel)
Inlets         : Outer (sample, 75 um split); inner (sheath buffer, 425 um split)
Outlets        : Inner CTC collection (150 um); outer waste (350 um)
Punching       : 1.5 mm single layer; 4 mm bottom layers + 1.5 mm top layer (multiplexed)
Critical       : Channel depth must be 160-170 um; depth outside tolerance causes particle focusing
                 failure and substantially compromises enriched sample purity


CELL LINES USED
---------------
- MCF-7 (human breast cancer, ATCC HTB-22)
- MDA-MB-231 (human breast cancer, ATCC CRM-HTB-26)
- A549 (human lung adenocarcinoma, ATCC CCL-185)
- SW480 (human colorectal adenocarcinoma; used for KRAS codon 12 mutation sequencing validation)
All cultured in complete DMEM (DMEM + 10% FBS + 1% pen/strep), 37 C, 5% CO2.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Device Fabrication (Off-chip, Steps 1-19)
  Duration  : 5-7 d (mask printing) + 7-10 h (photolithography) + 1-2 d (soft lithography)
  Details   : AutoCAD design -> mask printing -> UV lithography on AZ9260-coated silicon wafer
              -> DRIE etching ~165 um -> silanization -> PDMS casting 10:1 w/w, 77 g, 70-80 C 2 h
              -> peel, punch holes, O2 plasma bond to glass -> 85 C 30 min post-bond bake.
              Multiplexed device: three PDMS molds stacked and plasma-bonded with manual alignment.
  Note      : Not encoded in BioScript (off-chip physical fabrication).

Step 2 | Chip Priming - Ethanol Sterilization (Paper Steps 20-24)
  Reagents  : Ethanol 70% v/v in sterile deionized water
  Operation : Flow through both inlets at 750 uL/min for 5 min
  Purpose   : Sterilize channel interior; remove air bubbles
  Note      : Required for downstream CTC culture. Omit Steps 22-24 for non-sterile experiments.

Step 3 | Chip Priming - Sheath Buffer Coating (Paper Step 25)
  Reagents  : 1x sterile PBS + 0.5% w/v BSA (sheath buffer)
  Operation : Flow at 750 uL/min for 5 min
  Purpose   : Coat PDMS microchannel walls with BSA; minimize non-specific cell adhesion
  Note      : Wash residual ethanol from system before sample loading.

Step 4 | Bubble Removal Inspection (Paper Step 26)
  Reagents  : Sheath buffer
  Operation : Bright-field inverted microscope inspection; increase flow rate to dislodge bubbles
  Duration  : ~2 min
  Critical  : If bubbles cannot be removed, discard chip and use a new one.

Step 5 | QC Bead Check (Paper Step 27)
  Reagents  : 3-6 um polystyrene bead suspension (0.1% v/v); sheath buffer
  Flow rates: Sample 100 uL/min (outer inlet); sheath 750 uL/min (inner inlet)
  Duration  : ~5 min run + 30 s detection
  Imaging   : Phase-contrast microscope + high-speed camera (VisionResearch Phantom v9)
  Pass      : 6 um beads complete Dean cycle (inner to outer wall visible); 15 um beads focus
              tightly at inner wall at outlet region
  Fail      : Incorrect focusing -> channel depth out of tolerance (likely DRIE error) ->
              fabricate new master mold (new silicon wafer)
  BioScript : beadFocusingQC detect module; branch BB2 (pass/dispose) vs BB3 (fail/drain)

Step 6 | Blood Preparation - RBC Lysis (Paper Steps 28-31)
  Reagents  : Whole blood (EDTA Vacutainer, processed within 2-4 h of collection);
              3x ammonium chloride RBC lysis buffer (G-Bioscience, cat. no. 786650)
  Ratio     : 1:3 v/v blood:lysis buffer
  Temperature: 24-26 C (room temperature)
  Duration  : 10 min on shaking platform; invert tube to mix
  Endpoint  : Sample color changes to dark red (complete lysis)
  Note      : If spiking with cancer cell lines, add cells before lysis step.
              Removal of RBCs reduces cellular interactions that impair inertial focusing.
              Cell loss from lysis + centrifugation <8%.

Step 7 | Cell Pelleting and Resuspension (Paper Steps 29-31)
  Reagents  : 1x PBS
  Operation : Centrifuge 1000g, 5 min, room temperature; discard supernatant
  Resuspend : In 1x PBS to 2x concentration (~14e6 nucleated cells/mL; 7.5 mL blood -> 3.75 mL PBS)
  Storage   : On ice; process within 2-4 h at 4 C

Step 8 | Spiral Biochip Processing (Paper Steps 32-37)
  Reagents  : Lysed blood sample; sheath buffer (PBS + 0.5% BSA)
  Flow rates: Sample 100 uL/min (outer inlet); sheath 750 uL/min (inner inlet, single chip)
              Multiplexed: sheath 2100 uL/min
  Duration  : ~37 min (7.5 mL, single chip); ~12 min (7.5 mL, 3x multiplexed)
  Collection: CTCs from inner outlet into 15 mL conical tube; waste to 50 mL conical tube
  Microscope: 10x magnification, monitor spiral outlet during run
  Protocol  : Run sheath 2 min first to stabilize; start sample pump; allow uninterrupted run.
  Separation: CTCs (>15 um) focus near inner wall via inertial lift; blood cells (<15 um) exit
              outer waste outlet following Dean vortex streamlines.

Step 9 | Cell Concentration (Paper Step 38-39)
  Operation : Centrifuge collected CTC fraction at 300g, 3 min, room temperature
  Resuspend : In ~250 uL PBS; transfer to microcentrifuge tube

Step 10 | PFA Fixation (Paper Step 40)
  Reagents  : 4% w/v paraformaldehyde in PBS
  Duration  : 10 min at room temperature
  Caution   : PFA is a suspected carcinogen; handle with nitrile gloves, lab coat, goggles
  Storage   : Fixed cells can be stored overnight at 4 C in PBS

Step 11 | Post-Fixation Wash (Paper Step 41)
  Reagents  : 1-2 mL PBS + 0.5% w/v BSA
  Operation : Centrifuge 300g, 3 min, room temperature; discard supernatant
  Resuspend : In ~250 uL PBS

Step 12 | Permeabilization (Paper Step 42)
  Reagents  : 0.1% v/v Triton X-100 in PBS
  Duration  : 1-5 min at room temperature (encoded as 3 min)
  Purpose   : Solubilize plasma membrane for intracellular pan-cytokeratin antibody access

Step 13 | Post-Permeabilization Wash (Paper Step 43)
  Reagents  : 1-2 mL PBS + 0.5% w/v BSA
  Operation : Centrifuge 300g, 3 min, room temperature; discard supernatant

Step 14 | Antibody and Hoechst Staining (Paper Step 44)
  Reagents  : FITC pan-cytokeratin 1:100 (Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific)
              APC CD45 1:100 (Miltenyi Biotec Asia Pacific)
              Hoechst 33342 1:1000 from 1 M stock (Life Technologies)
  Incubation: 30 min on ice (4 C) in dark
  CTC phenotype: Hoechst+, pan-CK+, CD45-, round nuclei, high nuclear-to-cytoplasmic ratio
  Storage   : Stained cells stable overnight at 4 C in PBS under dark conditions

Step 15 | Post-Staining Wash and Transfer (Paper Steps 45-46)
  Reagents  : 1-2 mL PBS + 0.5% w/v BSA
  Operation : Centrifuge 300g, 3 min, room temperature; discard supernatant
  Resuspend : In ~250 uL PBS; transfer to single well of 96-well plate

Step 16 | CTC Fluorescence Detection and Enumeration (Paper Step 47)
  Platform  : Olympus IX71/IX81 inverted microscope with automated stage
  Filters   : ET460/50m (Hoechst), ET535/50m (pan-CK FITC), ET605/70m (CD45 APC)
  Camera    : 12-bit EM-CCD (Andor Technology iXon+885)
  Software  : MetaMorph (1 mm x 1 mm grid well scan); ImageJ (manual threshold)
  Manual    : 40x magnification; Hoechst+/pan-CK+/CD45- cells with round nuclei and high N/C ratio
  Threshold : >7 CK+ cells per sample = positive patient (derived from 10 healthy volunteer controls)
  Results   : 12-1275 CK+ breast CTCs; 10-1535 CK+ lung CTCs from clinical samples
  BioScript : ctcDetection detect module (45 min); branch BB5 (pass/dispose) vs BB6 (fail/drain)

Alternative - DNA-FISH (Box 1, Paper)
  Duration  : 2 d
  Probes    : Vysis ALK break-apart probe; HER2/neu probe (Abbott)
  Fixative  : Acetic acid:methanol 1:3
  Protocol  : Dropwise cell fixation onto slides; RNase 40 min 37 C; denature 70% formamide/2x SSC
              80 C 10 min; dehydrate ethanol series; hybridize 42 C dark humid 16 h; wash; DAPI
