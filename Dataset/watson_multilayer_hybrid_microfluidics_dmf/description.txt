PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Multilayer Hybrid Microfluidics: A Digital-to-Channel Interface for Sample Processing and Separations
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/vertical_interface.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac101379g
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2010, Vol. 82, pp. 6680-6686
Authors      : Michael W. L. Watson, Mais J. Jebrail, Aaron R. Wheeler
Platform     : Multilayer hybrid DMF (two-plate digital microfluidics + glass microchannel CE)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol introduces a second-generation "hybrid microfluidics" device architecture that
integrates two-plate digital microfluidics (DMF) for sample processing with a glass microchannel
network for capillary electrophoresis (CE) separation and LIF detection. The key advance over
the prior single-plate side-on design (Abdelgawad et al. Lab Chip 2009) is the multilayer
configuration: a DMF electrode array patterned on the top of a commercial glass microchannel
device, enabling dispensing from reservoirs and splitting of droplets on-chip -- operations
incompatible with single-plate actuation.

Droplets are manipulated in two-plate format on the top layer, then transferred through a
vertical digital-channel interface (a drilled hole, 2 mm dia.) into the microchannel network
below, where analytes are separated by electroosmotic flow and detected by LIF. The two fluid
manipulation paradigms (AC electrostatic droplet actuation above, DC electroosmotic flow below)
are orthogonal and independent.

Two demonstrations are encoded in this BioScript file:
  Protocol 1: On-chip serial dilution calibration curve (fluorescein + rhodamine 123 internal
              standard; 3-point calibration, n=5 replicates, R^2=0.9937, RSD 2.9-7.4%)
  Protocol 2: Multistep enzymatic protein digestion (AF-BSA with pepsin then trypsin;
              sequential 30 min incubations; CE comparison of undigested, pepsin-only,
              and pepsin+trypsin digest states; n=3 replicates; total ~2 h)


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Substrate 1 : ITO-coated glass cover with Teflon-AF (top plate; ground electrode)
Substrate 2 : Chromium electrode array on glass, coated with Parylene-C + Teflon-AF
              (bottom DMF plate / microchannel cover)
Substrate 3 : Glass slide with etched cross-pattern microchannel network
              (Caliper Life Science, Hopkinton MA; 100 um wide x 20 um deep)

Substrates 2+3 are thermally bonded; DMF electrodes are patterned on the top of substrate 2.
Substrates 1+2 define the DMF two-plate gap (280 um, set by 4x double-sided tape spacers).

DMF electrode layout:
  - 13 driving electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm)
  - 3 reservoir electrodes (3x3 mm or 5x5 mm depending on reagent volume needs)
  - Actuation: 20 kHz AC, 100-200 Vrms (function generator + high-voltage amplifier)
  - Interface electrode overlaps the drilled inlet hole by ~1 mm for reliable droplet transfer

Microchannel layout (cross injector):
  - S (sample inlet): connects to digital-channel interface
  - SW (sample waste): 15 uL reservoir volume
  - B (buffer inlet): 15 uL reservoir volume
  - BW (buffer waste): 15 uL reservoir volume
  - Separation column: 3 cm from injection cross to LIF detection point
  - Unit sample volume: 3 uL droplet (approximates 2 mm dia. x 1 mm deep hole volume)

Device footprint: 35 x 20 mm (commercial microchannel device; limits DMF array to ~13 electrodes)


REAGENTS
--------
Protocol 1 -- Serial Dilution Calibration Curve:
  AnalyteFluorescein   : 1 uM fluorescein + 1 uM rhodamine 123 in 20 mM borate pH 9.0
                         (combined stock droplet containing both analyte and internal standard)
  Diluent              : 1 uM rhodamine 123 in 20 mM borate pH 9.0
                         (maintains constant IS concentration across all dilution steps)
  Separation buffer    : 20 mM sodium borate pH 9.0, filtered through 0.2 um nylon,
                         degassed by ultrasonication 5 min; fills channel network prior to actuation

Protocol 2 -- Multistep Enzymatic Protein Digestion:
  AF_BSA               : 20 ug/mL Alexa Fluor 488-labeled BSA in DI water + 0.1% w/v Pluronic F-68
                         (Invitrogen-Molecular Probes, Eugene OR)
  PepsinSolution       : 1 mg/mL pepsin in 1 mM acetate buffer + 0.1% w/v Pluronic F-68, pH 4.5
                         Cleavage: aromatic residues (Phe, Tyr, Trp)
  TrypsinSolution      : 1 mg/mL trypsin in 50 mM borate buffer + 0.1% w/v Pluronic F-68, pH 9.0
                         Cleavage: basic residues (Lys, Arg)
  Separation buffer    : 100 mM sodium borate pH 9.0 (higher ionic strength for protein separations)
  PluronicF68          : 0.1% w/v additive in all protein-containing droplets; minimises surface
                         adsorption to Parylene-C / Teflon-AF device surfaces

All reagents purchased from Sigma Aldrich (Oakville ON) unless otherwise specified.
Buffers dissolved in 18 MOhm cm deionised water.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

--- PROTOCOL 1: On-Chip Serial Dilution Calibration Curve ---

Step P1-S1 | Dispense Analyte Stock Droplet
  Source    : AnalyteFluorescein reservoir (5x5 mm electrode)
  Volume    : ~3 uL unit droplet
  Content   : 1 uM fluorescein + 1 uM rhodamine 123 in 20 mM borate pH 9.0
  Purpose   : Starting point for serial 2x dilution; rhodamine 123 serves as internal standard
              throughout (peak area ratio AA/AIS used as calibration metric)

Step P1-S2 | Dispense First Diluent Droplet
  Source    : Diluent reservoir
  Volume    : ~3 uL unit droplet
  Content   : 1 uM rhodamine 123 only (no analyte)

Step P1-S3 | Merge and Mix (Dilution 1 -- 2x dilution)
  Inputs    : stockDroplet + diluent1
  Duration  : 10 s (circular mixing across several electrodes)
  Result    : ~0.5 uM fluorescein, ~1 uM rhodamine 123
  Note      : Circular mixing pattern (paper: "several seconds") ensures thorough homogenisation
              before the droplet is split.

Step P1-S4 | Split: Daughter A to Digital-Channel Interface
  Operation : Physical split by reversing electrode actuation while droplet straddles two
              driving electrodes; two equal half-droplets (~1.5 uL each) result.
  Daughter A: driven to digital-channel interface for CE-LIF injection
  BioScript : Encoded as mix(mergedDrop1, diluent1) for 2s (split abstraction; no native
              split statement in BioScript)

Step P1-S5 | Store Daughter B (array)
  Daughter B: retained on electrode array
  BioScript : Represented as dispense(Diluent) placeholder variable (storedDrop1)

Step P1-S6 | CE-LIF Analysis: Dilution 1 (undiluted stock daughter)
  Transfer  : Daughter A driven to interface electrode (overlaps 2 mm drilled hole by ~1 mm);
              droplet spontaneously wets glass and settles into hole
  Loading   : S=900V, SW=0V, B=650V, BW=850V (LabSmith high-voltage sequencer)
  Injection : S=500V, SW=500V, B=1000V, BW=0V
  Detection : 488 nm argon ion laser (20 mW), 60x objective, 536/40 nm BP filter, PMT
              Detection point: 3 cm from injection cross
  Module    : serialDilutionDetection
  Duration  : 30 s

Step P1-S7 | Dispense Second Diluent; Merge with Stored Daughter B (Dilution 2 -- 4x dilution)
  Inputs    : storedDrop1 + diluent2
  Duration  : 10 s mixing
  Result    : ~0.25 uM fluorescein, ~1 uM rhodamine 123
  Prep      : Previous analysed sample removed from channel by suction (fine needle + vacuum
              flask) without removing the DMF top-plate between injections.

Step P1-S8 | Split: Daughter C to Digital-Channel Interface
  Daughter C: driven to interface for second CE-LIF injection
  BioScript : Encoded as mix(mergedDrop2, diluent2) for 2s (split abstraction)

Step P1-S9 | CE-LIF Analysis: Dilution 2
  Module    : serialDilutionDetection
  Duration  : 30 s
  Completes : 3-point calibration curve (stock daughter, 2x dilution, 4x dilution)

QC Gate | Serial Dilution (dilutionSignal1 > 0)
  Pass (BB2): valid CE electropherogram peaks detected; dispose analysed droplets
  Fail (BB3): no signal (interface transfer failure or column blockage); dispose and abort
  Join (BB4): dispose QC signal droplets; enter protein digestion protocol

Calibration results (paper, n=5):
  RSD: 2.9% (stock), 3.7% (1st dilution), 7.4% (2nd dilution)
  R^2 = 0.9937
  Separation efficiency: 1.1x10^6 plates/m (rhodamine 123)
  Improvement over side-on design: 6.7% RSD -> 2.9%; 4.7x10^5 -> 1.1x10^6 plates/m
  (attributed to glass vs PDMS channel material; glass reduces band broadening)

--- PROTOCOL 2: Multistep Enzymatic Protein Digestion ---

Step P2-S1 | Dispense AF-BSA Droplet
  Source    : AF_BSA reservoir
  Volume    : ~3 uL unit droplet
  Content   : 20 ug/mL AF-BSA in DI water + 0.1% F-68

Step P2-S2 | Dispense Pepsin Droplet
  Source    : PepsinSolution reservoir
  Volume    : ~3 uL unit droplet
  Content   : 1 mg/mL pepsin in 1 mM acetate pH 4.5 + 0.1% F-68

Step P2-S3 | Merge AF-BSA with Pepsin
  Duration  : 5 s
  Result    : Equal volume mixture of AF-BSA + pepsin in combined buffer conditions

Step P2-S4 | Pepsin Digestion Incubation
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature)
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s)
  Humidified  : Device stored in Petri dish with water-saturated wipe during incubation
                to prevent evaporation from open two-plate gap edges
  Endpoint    : Loss of intact AF-BSA peak at t=58 s in CE electropherogram confirms complete
                digestion by pepsin

Step P2-S5 | Split: Daughter D (Pepsin Digest) to Interface
  Daughter D: driven to digital-channel interface for CE-LIF analysis (pepsin-only digest)
  BioScript : mix(pepsinReaction, pepsinDroplet) for 2s (split abstraction; pepsinDroplet
              variable reused as placeholder -- compiler-accepted workaround, not a biological
              dispense; represents retained half-droplet on array)

Step P2-S6 | Store Daughter E (Pepsin Digest Half)
  BioScript : dispense(AF_BSA) placeholder variable (storedPepsinDrop)
  Note      : Logical placeholder; models retained pepsin digest subdroplet awaiting trypsin

Step P2-S7 | CE-LIF Analysis: Pepsin-Only Digest
  Module    : proteinDigestionDetection
  Duration  : 60 s
  Expected  : Multiple peaks at range of elution times (multiply charged aromatic peptides);
              intact AF-BSA peak at t=58 s absent (complete digestion confirmed)
  Prep      : Previous sample removed by suction before injection

Step P2-S8 | Dispense Trypsin Droplet
  Source    : TrypsinSolution reservoir
  Volume    : ~3 uL unit droplet
  Content   : 1 mg/mL trypsin in 50 mM borate pH 9.0 + 0.1% F-68

Step P2-S9 | Merge Stored Pepsin Digest with Trypsin
  Inputs    : storedPepsinDrop + trypsinDroplet
  Duration  : 5 s
  Note      : Stored Daughter E (pepsin digest) merged with trypsin for second-stage digestion

Step P2-S10 | Trypsin Digestion Incubation
  Temperature : 25 C (room temperature)
  Duration    : 30 min (1800 s)
  Humidified  : Yes (same humidified chamber setup as pepsin step)
  Note        : Trypsin cleaves at Lys and Arg, reducing multiply charged aromatic peptides
                from pepsin into smaller singly charged fragments. Total workflow ~2 h.

Step P2-S11 | Split: Daughter F (Sequential Digest) to Interface
  Daughter F: driven to interface for CE-LIF analysis of pepsin+trypsin sequential digest
  BioScript : mix(trypsinReaction, trypsinDroplet) for 2s (same split abstraction pattern;
              trypsinDroplet reused as placeholder)

Step P2-S12 | CE-LIF Analysis: Sequential Pepsin + Trypsin Digest
  Module    : proteinDigestionDetection
  Duration  : 60 s
  Expected  : New series of peaks eluting closer together vs pepsin-only trace;
              reduction in multiply charged species; singly charged tryptic peptides dominant
  Completes : Three-state AF-BSA comparison (undigested, pepsin-only, pepsin+trypsin)

QC Gate | Protein Digestion (digestionSignalPepsin > 0)
  Pass (BB5): valid pepsin digest CE peaks detected; dispose all digestion droplets
  Fail (BB6): no signal (enzyme inactive, transfer failed, column blocked); dispose and abort
  Terminal (BB7): dispose both QC signal droplets; NOP exit node; experiment ends

Digestion results (paper, n=3):
  Pepsin-only: complete digestion; multiply charged peptides from Phe/Tyr/Trp cleavage
  Sequential : reduction to singly charged peptides; peaks elute closer together
  Conditions tested: undigested, pepsin-only (30 min), pepsin+trypsin (30 min each)

                      In both cases the second argument is a logical placeholder for the
                      retained half-droplet on the electrode array, not a biological reagent
                      addition. Compiler accepts without error. Document when curating library.
