PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : High-throughput Isolation and Sorting of Gut Microbes Reduce Biases of Traditional Cultivation Strategies
Source Link  : https://www.biorxiv.org/content/biorxiv/early/2019/09/06/759969.1.full.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1101/759969
Journal      : bioRxiv preprint, September 6, 2019
Platform     : Anaerobic droplet microfluidics (flow-focusing droplet generation + image-based
               wavelet OD droplet sorting, operated inside vinyl anaerobic chamber)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a high-throughput end-to-end microfluidic platform for anaerobic
isolation, cultivation, and sorting of gut-associated bacteria in picoliter droplets. The
system enables stochastic single-cell encapsulation in 65-115 pL aqueous droplets suspended
in oil, clonal growth without interspecies competition, and automated image-based sorting of
slow-growing colonies based on an optical density proxy (Wavelet OD). The platform is operated
entirely inside a vinyl anaerobic chamber (Coy Laboratory Products) under strictly anaerobic
conditions (O2 < 1 ppm, H2 1.5-2.5%) to support cultivation of extremely oxygen-sensitive gut
anaerobes.

The protocol is divided into four functional phases:
  1. ASF model community validation -- 8-species Altered Schaedler's Flora (mouse gut) used
     to confirm all species grow in droplets, including extremely oxygen-sensitive strains,
     and that droplets prevent competitive overgrowth of fast-growing over slow-growing species.
  2. Human stool cultivation across 3 rich media -- BHIS, GMM, and YCFA droplets cultured in
     parallel and compared to conventional agar plates using 16S rRNA amplicon profiling.
     Community richness increases 37% (BHIS) to 410% (YCFA) in droplets over plates.
  3. Wavelet OD-based slow-grower sorting -- image-based sorting at ~30 Hz retains droplets
     with sparse colonies (Wavelet OD 0.3-0.7), further amplifying rare taxa from <1% to >1%
     relative abundance (avg. 6.5 ASVs unsorted vs. 12.5 ASVs sorted, out of 158 total).
  4. Viability confirmation -- sorted droplets streaked onto BHIS agar plates; Sanger
     sequencing of 24 randomly picked colonies confirms viability and taxa identity
     (Hafnia 12/24, Enterococcus 8/24, Bacteroides 4/24).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Housing      : Vinyl anaerobic chamber (Coy Laboratory Products)
               Gas mixture: 86% N2 / 10% O2 / 4% H2
               O2 concentration: <1 ppm (monitored by Coy CAM-12 anaerobic monitor)
               H2 concentration: 1.5-2.5% maintained
               H2S reducing column placed inside chamber to prevent electronic corrosion

Fabrication  : Soft lithography (SU-8 3050 negative photoresist molds on 4-inch silicon wafers)
               Channel height: 50 um
               PDMS (RTV 615) parts A:B ratio = 10:1; degassed; cured at 80 C for >= 1 hr
               Channels hydrophobized: Aquapel (Pittsburgh Glass Works) then Fluorinert FC-40

Droplet generation device:
  - PDMS bonded to plain glass slide
  - Flow-focusing junction: bacteria suspension + oil meet at junction
  - Flow rates set by Harvard Apparatus Pump 11 Pico Plus Elite syringe pumps
  - Droplet volumes: 65-115 pL
  - Typical run: 0.5-1 mL emulsion generated in 20 min to 1 hr

Droplet sorting device:
  - PDMS bonded to ITO-coated glass slide (Delta Technologies, CG-811N-S207)
  - Electrodes: low melting-point solder (51% In / 32.5% Bi / 16.5% Sn, Indium Corporation)
                flowed in at 90 C hotplate, connected to standard wires
  - Mechanism: dielectrophoresis (DEP) at sorting junction
  - Actuation: 10 kHz, 800 V p-p, 30% duty cycle square wave; activated for 10 ms per event
  - Sorting rate: ~30 Hz
  - Reinjection flow: 20 uL/hr (droplets); 180 uL/hr (oil spacing)

Imaging system:
  - Inverted microscope: Nikon Ts2R; 4x and 10x magnification
  - Camera: Basler acA640-750um; 925 Hz frame rate; 672 x 360 px; 59 us exposure
  - Control: custom LabView code (external computer connected via sealed rubber ports)


SAMPLE TYPES
------------
Phase 1 | Mouse gut model community:
  Altered Schaedler's Flora (ASF) -- 8-species defined community
  Facultative anaerobes : ASF 360 (L. intestinalis), ASF 361 (L. murinus)
  Anaerobes             : ASF 457 (M. schaedleri), ASF 519 (P. goldsteinii)
  Extremely O2-sensitive: ASF 356 (Clostridium sp.), ASF 492 (E. plexicaudatum),
                          ASF 500 (Pseudoflavonifactor sp.), ASF 502 (Clostridium sp.)
  Source: Taconic Biosciences (fecal pellets); pure strains from Dr. Michael Wannemuehler
  Note: ASF 360 not detected in fecal pellets; 7 of 8 strains confirmed by species-specific PCR

Phase 2-4 | Human fecal microbiota:
  Single stool sample from FMT donor
  Ethics: IRB 132-0212, University of Chicago; written informed consent obtained
  Live cell density: 1e9/mL; Dead cell density: 3e9/mL (BacLight Live/Dead assay, ThermoFisher)
  Dilution for droplets: 200x (to achieve ~20-30% single-cell occupancy per Poisson statistics)


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | Anaerobic Chamber and Device Priming
  Phase    : Phase 1 - ASF model community validation
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : FC40_Oil, HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 300.0 SECOND (IR: devicePrime1[0])
  Purpose  : Flush FC-40 oil and HFE7500 surfactant through channels to establish anaerobic
             two-phase flow conditions. Channels pre-coated with Aquapel then Fluorinert FC-40
             for hydrophobicity. Equipment power and control wires introduced through sealed
             rubber ports to maintain O2 < 1 ppm inside chamber.

Step 2 | ASF Fecal Pellet Preparation and Dilution
  Phase    : Phase 1 - ASF model community validation
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : ASF_FecalPellet, BHIS_ASF_Medium
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (IR: asfDiluted1[0])
  Prep     : Dissociate 3 ASF fecal pellets in 5 mL BHIS-ASF; sediment solids; collect
             supernatant; store at -80 C in 25% glycerol / 25% BHIS / 50% cell suspension
  Dilution : 50x from fecal cell stock suspension
  Media    : BHIS-ASF = BHIS + 5% newborn calf serum + 5% sheep serum + 5% horse serum

Step 3 | ASF Droplet Generation
  Phase    : Phase 1 - ASF model community validation
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : asfDiluted1[0], HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 3.0 SECOND (IR: asfDroplets1[0])
  Details  : Stochastic encapsulation; ~20-30% of droplets contain exactly 1 live cell
             (Poisson statistics). Droplet volume: 65-115 pL. Oil phase: HFE7500.
             All operations performed inside anaerobic chamber.

Step 4 | ASF Droplet Incubation (24 h)
  Phase    : Phase 1 - ASF model community validation
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Operation: heat @ 37.0 C @ 86400.0 SECOND (= 1440 min = 24 h)
  Details  : Droplet emulsion incubated at 37 C inside anaerobic chamber incubator.
             Isolated cells grow clonally inside droplets. Droplets prevent ASF 361
             (parasitic towards ASF 519 in bulk broth) from dominating culture.
             All 8 ASF strains confirmed to grow in pure-strain droplet cultures.

Step 5 | ASF Emulsion Break, DNA Extraction, and ddPCR Quantification
  Phase    : Phase 1 - ASF model community validation
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : PFO_BreakingAgent, DNAExtractionKit
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (break); mix @ 1800.0 SECOND (extract); detect @ 30.0 SECOND
  IR vars  : asfBreakingAgent1[0], asfKit1[0], asfBroken1[0], asfDNA1[0], asfComposition1[0]
  Break    : Add equal part PFO to emulsion; vortex and centrifuge; remove oil+PFO phase
  Extract  : Qiagen DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit; elution volume 100 uL
  Quantify : BioRad QX200 droplet digital PCR system; QX200 ddPCR EvaGreen Supermix
             Species-specific primers (Sarma-Rupavtarm et al. 2004)
             Thermocycle: 94 C 10 min; 35 cycles (94 C 1 min, 60 C 1 min, 72 C 2 min)
  Expected : Bulk broth: ASF 361 dominates; ASF 519 outcompeted (parasitism)
             Droplets  : ASF 457 reaches 34%; ASF 519 reaches 0.96%; richer community
             N = 3 independent experiments; results averaged
  Module   : sixteenSrRNASequencing (detect @ 30.0 SECOND)

Step 6 | Human Stool Aliquot Preparation (3 Arms)
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : FetalCellSuspension (x3 independent aliquots)
  IR vars  : humanStoolBhis1[0], humanStoolGmm1[0], humanStoolYcfa1[0]
  Details  : Three separate aliquots dispensed independently -- one per media arm (BHIS,
             GMM, YCFA) -- to avoid reuse of a consumed variable across parallel branches.
             Each aliquot: 200x diluted from -80 C glycerol stock supernatant.

Step 7 | BHIS Droplet Generation and Incubation (48 h)
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : humanStoolBhis1[0], BHIS_Medium, HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (suspend); mix @ 3.0 SECOND (encapsulate);
             heat @ 37.0 C @ 172800.0 SECOND (= 2880 min = 48 h)
  IR vars  : bhisMedium1[0], bhisOil1[0], bhisSuspension1[0], bhisDroplets1[0]
  Emulsion : 0.5-1 mL; 4-15 million droplets; 65-115 pL each
  Media    : BHIS = 500 mL water, 18.5 g BHI broth, 2.5 g yeast extract, 0.25 g L-cysteine,
             0.5 mL 0.1% vitamin K (95% ethanol), 0.5 mL hemin solution (5 mg/mL, 0.1 M NaOH)
  Control  : 100 uL stool spread onto BHIS agar plates; cultured 7 days

Step 8 | GMM Droplet Generation and Incubation (48 h)
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : humanStoolGmm1[0], GMM_Medium, HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND; mix @ 3.0 SECOND;
             heat @ 37.0 C @ 172800.0 SECOND (= 2880 min = 48 h)
  IR vars  : gmmMedium1[0], gmmOil1[0], gmmSuspension1[0], gmmDroplets1[0]
  Media    : Gut Microbiota Medium (GMM) per Goodman et al. (2011) PNAS 108:6252

Step 9 | YCFA Droplet Generation and Incubation (48 h)
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : humanStoolYcfa1[0], YCFA_Medium, HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND; mix @ 3.0 SECOND;
             heat @ 37.0 C @ 172800.0 SECOND (= 2880 min = 48 h)
  IR vars  : ycfaMedium1[0], ycfaOil1[0], ycfaSuspension1[0], ycfaDroplets1[0]
  Media    : YCFA (modified DSMZ 1611): 10 g casitone, 2.5 g yeast extract, 5 g dextrose,
             short-chain fatty acids (acetic 1.9 mL, propionic 0.7 mL, isobutyric 90 uL,
             n-valeric 100 uL, iso-valeric 100 uL), NaHCO3 4 g, L-cysteine-HCl 0.5 g,
             hemin 0.01 g, mucin 0.0025 g; pH 6.7-6.8; autoclave; add 10 mL vitamin solution

Step 10 | Emulsion Break and DNA Extraction (All Three Arms)
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main)
  Reagents : PFO_BreakingAgent (x3), DNAExtractionKit (x3)
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (break per arm); mix @ 1800.0 SECOND (extract per arm)
  IR vars  : bhisBreaker1, gmmBreaker1, ycfaBreaker1 (break);
             bhisKit1, gmmKit1, ycfaKit1 (kits);
             bhisBroken1, gmmBroken1, ycfaBroken1 (broken);
             bhisDNA1, gmmDNA1, ycfaDNA1 (extracted DNA)
  Method   : Add equal part PFO to emulsion; vortex; centrifuge; remove oil+PFO
             Qiagen DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit; elution volume 100 uL per sample

Step 11 | 16S rRNA Amplicon Sequencing and Community Richness QC Gate
  Phase    : Phase 2 - Human stool cultivation across 3 media
  BB       : BB1 (main) -> BB2 (pass) / BB3 (fail)
  Operation: detect @ 30.0 SECOND (profiles x3); mix @ 5.0 SECOND (x2, combine);
             detect @ 30.0 SECOND (richness gate)
  IR vars  : bhisProfile1, gmmProfile1, ycfaProfile1, combinedProfiles1,
             richnessMeasure1, richnessSignal1
  Modules  : sixteenSrRNASequencing (profiles); colonyDensityDetection (richness gate)
  Sequencing:
    Facility  : Environmental Sample Preparation and Sequencing Facility, Argonne National Lab
    Target    : 16S rRNA V4 region; primers from Caporaso et al. (2012) ISME J 6:1621
    PCR       : 35 cycles
    Platform  : Illumina MiSeq paired-end 2 x 300
    QC        : illumina-utils; demultiplex; join pairs (min overlap 45 nt, <2 mismatches)
    ASV       : Minimum Entropy Decomposition (MED) / oligotyping pipeline v2.1
    Taxonomy  : Global Alignment for Sequence Taxonomy (GAST)
  Metrics   : Richness (R, total ASVs); Shannon index (H'); low-abundance count (Rlow, <1% stool)
  Statistics: Mann-Whitney U test (wilcox.test, R); null = difference of means is zero
  Branch:
    Pass (BB2): richnessSignal1[0] > 0  -> droplet richness > plate richness confirmed;
                dispose(bhisDNA1, gmmDNA1, ycfaDNA1); proceed to sorting arm (BB4)
    Fail (BB3): richnessSignal1[0] <= 0 -> no richness gain;
                dispose(bhisDNA1, gmmDNA1, ycfaDNA1); fall through to BB4
  Results:
    Richness increase over plates: 37% (BHIS) to 410% (YCFA); p < 0.005
    Low-abundance ASVs: 112 (droplets) vs. 58 (plates) avg., p < 0.005
    Shannon diversity increased in BHIS and YCFA droplets; not in GMM
    Bacteroides oligotypes cluster closer to raw stool in droplets (p < 0.005, multiscale bootstrap)

Step 12 | Slow-Growing Colony Sorting: Droplet Preparation and Incubation (24 h)
  Phase    : Phase 3 - Wavelet OD-based slow-grower sorting
  BB       : BB4 (bsbbif_4_j)
  Reagents : FetalCellSuspension, BHIS_Medium, HFE7500_Surfactant
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND; mix @ 3.0 SECOND;
             heat @ 37.0 C @ 86400.0 SECOND (= 1440 min = 24 h)
  IR vars  : sortingStool1, bhisSortMedium1, bhisSortOil1, sortSuspension1, sortDroplets1
  Details  : Fresh BHIS droplet emulsion from human stool. Two independent sorting experiments.
             Droplets reinjected at 20 uL/hr; oil spacing phase at 180 uL/hr.

Step 13 | Image-Based Wavelet OD Sorting and Sorting QC Gate
  Phase    : Phase 3 - Wavelet OD-based slow-grower sorting
  BB       : BB4 (bsbbif_4_j) -> BB5 (pass) / BB6 (fail)
  Operation: detect @ 30.0 SECOND (IR: sortingSignal1[0])
  Module   : colonyDensityDetection
  Imaging  : Basler acA640-750um; 925 Hz; 672 x 360 px; 59 us exposure
  Algorithm: Discrete wavelet frame decomposition (LabView IMAQ Extract Texture Feature VI)
             Wavelet type: Biorthogonal 3.1; Subband: Low Low High (LLH)
             Window: 15 x 15 px; Step: 5 px; Gray levels: 15; Displacement: 3 x 3 px
             Wavelet OD normalized 0 (empty) to 1 (dense colony)
  Thresholds:
    Empty droplets (waste)       : Wavelet OD < 0.3
    Slow-growing colonies (keep) : 0.3 <= Wavelet OD <= 0.7
    Dense colonies (waste)       : Wavelet OD > 0.7
  Electrode actuation (keep path): 10 kHz, 800 V p-p, 30% duty cycle, 10 ms per event
  Sorting rate                   : ~30 Hz
  False-positive rate (upper bound): <= 8% (epsilon formula from paper Methods)
  Branch:
    Pass (BB5): sortingSignal1[0] > 0  -> slow-growing colonies detected in keep path;
                proceed to DNA extraction and sequencing
    Fail (BB6): sortingSignal1[0] <= 0 -> no qualifying slow-growing droplets;
                dispose(sortDroplets1[0])

Step 14 | Sorted Droplet DNA Extraction and 16S Sequencing
  Phase    : Phase 3 - Wavelet OD-based slow-grower sorting
  BB       : BB5 (bsbbif_5_t) [pass path only]
  Reagents : PFO_BreakingAgent, DNAExtractionKit
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (break); mix @ 1800.0 SECOND (extract);
             detect @ 30.0 SECOND (IR: sortedProfile1[0])
  IR vars  : sortedBreaker1, sortedKit1, sortedBroken1, sortedDNA1, sortedProfile1
  Sorted volume: ~1-10 uL (thin layer on oil, estimated by pipetting)
  Break method : Add 150 uL DNA-free water + 150 uL PFO to sorted droplets; vortex; centrifuge
  Extract      : Qiagen DNeasy Blood & Tissue Kit
  Results:
    Sorted vs unsorted: 12.5 vs. 6.5 ASVs amplified from <1% to >1% (out of 158 total)
    Phylogenetic range: sorted samples amplify taxa across wider range of phylogenetic tree
    Rank-abundance shift: p < 0.005 (Kolmogorov-Smirnov test)

Step 15 | Viability Confirmation: Agar Plate Streaking and Colony Picking
  Phase    : Phase 4 - Viability confirmation
  BB       : BB7 (bsbbif_7_j) -> BB8 (pass) / BB9 (fail)
  Reagents : FetalCellSuspension, BHIS_Medium, FDA_PI_Stain
  Operation: mix @ 2.0 SECOND (plate); heat @ 37.0 C @ 172800.0 SECOND (= 48 h);
             mix @ 600.0 SECOND (stain); detect @ 30.0 SECOND (viability gate)
  IR vars  : sortedForViability1, bhisAgarMedium1, platedSorted1,
             fdaPI_Stain1, stainedPlated1, viabilitySignal1
  Module   : dropletViabilityDetection
  Streak   : ~10 uL sorted droplet emulsion + oil onto BHIS agar plate; 37 C for 2 days
  Picking  : 24 colonies randomly picked; each into 2 mL BHIS broth
  Sequencing: Sanger sequencing (27F/1492R primers); GoTaq Green Master Mix (Promega)
              PCR: 95 C 3 min; 30 cycles (95 C 30 s, 54 C 30 s, 72 C 1 min); 72 C 10 min
              Gel: 2% agarose; band excised; QIAquick Gel Extraction Kit (Qiagen)
              BLAST taxonomy: Hafnia 12/24, Enterococcus 8/24, Bacteroides 4/24
  Viability stain: BacLight FDA/PI (ThermoFisher)
  Branch:
    Pass (BB8): viabilitySignal1[0] > 0  -> colonies viable; dispose
    Fail (BB9): viabilitySignal1[0] <= 0 -> no viable growth; dispose
