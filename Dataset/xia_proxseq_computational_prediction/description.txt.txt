PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Computational Prediction of Protein Interactions in Single Cells by Proximity Sequencing
Source Link  : https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011915
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011915
Journal      : PLoS Computational Biology, 2024, Vol. 20(3), Article e1011915
Authors      : Junjie Xia, Hoang Van Phan, Luke Vistain, Mengjie Chen, Aly A. Khan, Savaş Tay
Platform     : Proximity Sequencing (Prox-seq) combined with single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq)


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes Proximity Sequencing (Prox-seq), a method for simultaneous quantification
of mRNA, surface protein expression, and protein complexes (dimers) at the single-cell level.
Prox-seq uses a proximity ligation assay (PLA) with DNA-conjugated antibody probes (Probe A and
Probe B) targeting surface proteins of interest. Ligation of the oligos on Probe A and Probe B
occurs only when the two probes are within 50-70 nm of each other — i.e., when their respective
target protein molecules are co-localised or physically interacting. The resulting ligated PLA
products are barcoded, counted by unique molecular identifiers (UMIs) after sequencing, and
provide direct single-cell readout of protein complex formation.

By combining Prox-seq with scRNA-seq, PLA product libraries are sequenced alongside complementary
DNA (cDNA) libraries, yielding multi-omic single-cell data: gene expression, protein abundance,
and protein complex expression simultaneously from the same cell.

The paper introduces a physical simulation model of Prox-seq data and computationally benchmarks
three methods for removing proximity noise (random ligation background) and quantifying true
protein complexes: the iterative method, the linear regression (LR) method, and a new ensemble
method combining both. The ensemble method is demonstrated to be the most accurate and robust
across diverse biological scenarios.

Two cell line arms are encoded:
  ARM 1: Jurkat T cells — known complexes: CD3:CD3 and CD28:CD28 (homodimers)
  ARM 2: Raji B cells  — known complex: ICAM1:ICAM1 (homodimer on cell surface)

Code repository : https://github.com/tay-lab/Prox-seq_computation
Data repository : NCBI GEO accession GSE196130


METHOD DESIGN
-------------
Probe Design:
  Each target protein is stained with TWO DNA-conjugated antibodies:
    - Probe A: antibody conjugated to oligo A (contains barcode for protein i)
    - Probe B: antibody conjugated to oligo B (contains barcode for protein j)
  Ligation of oligo A to oligo B occurs only if Euclidean distance between
  the two probes <= ligation distance (50-70 nm; set to 50 nm in simulation model).
  Result: a PLA product barcode encoding the protein pair i:j.

  Importantly, unligated probes do NOT contribute to PLA signal because both library
  preparation and sequence alignment require barcodes from both the A and B probe.
  UMI counting of PLA products after sequencing gives the number of protein complexes
  per cell per protein pair.

Free Oligo Modification:
  An experimentally modified Prox-seq procedure adds FreeOligoA and FreeOligoB
  (non-antibody-conjugated oligos) alongside the probe panel. These generate PLA products:
    - Protein_X : free_oligo_B  ->  measures non-proximal (unligated) Probe A count
    - free_oligo_A : Protein_Y  ->  measures non-proximal (unligated) Probe B count
  These non-proximal counts serve as explanatory variables in the linear regression (LR)
  method and ensemble method for per-cell proximity noise estimation.

Proximity Noise:
  Random co-localisation of non-interacting proteins produces "proximity noise": ligation
  events between Probe A and Probe B from unrelated proteins that happen to be nearby.
  Theoretical expected proximity noise:
    E(X_ij) = (d_ligation^2 / 4R^2) * A_i * B_j
  where d_ligation = 50 nm, R = cell radius = 5 um, A_i = Probe A count for protein i,
  B_j = Probe B count for protein j.
  Proximity noise scales quadratically with both protein abundance and ligation distance
  (Fig 2e). Identifying and removing proximity noise is essential for accurate complex
  quantification, especially for highly expressed proteins.

Isotype Control:
  Isotype control antibodies with oligonucleotide conjugation are included in both Probe A
  and Probe B panels. This allows direct estimation of nonspecific antibody binding
  probability from observed PLA data. For extracellular Prox-seq assays, nonspecific
  binding constitutes <1% of both-specific PLA counts (e.g. CD147:CD147 in Jurkat cells,
  S4 Fig), and is much less important than proximity noise.

COMPUTATIONAL METHODS
---------------------

Iterative Method:
  Mechanism   : Solve quadratic equations iteratively for all possible protein dimers.
                Protein complex count = observed PLA count minus expected random PLA count.
                Expected count calculated from protein abundance estimates derived from
                summing PLA product counts per protein.
  Equation    : Y_ij^(m+1) = X_ij - [(sum_A_i - sum_Y_i^m) * (sum_B_j - sum_Y_j^m)] /
                              (total_PLA_sum - sum_Y^m)
  Strengths   : Performs well at high signal-to-noise; no additional experiment required.
  Limitations : Unstable with multiple protein dimers or overabundant proteins;
                sensitive to initialisation (default: all-zeroes matrix).
                Consistently underestimates true complex count (Fig 3c, 3e).
  Best for    : Simpler scenarios with only homodimers or only heterodimers.

Linear Regression (LR) Method:
  Mechanism   : Weighted least squares regression of observed PLA count (X_ij) onto
                product of non-proximal Probe A count (A_i') and Probe B count (B_j').
                Slope beta_1 estimates proximity noise; intercept beta_0 detects complex.
                Protein complex count Y_ij = X_ij - beta_1 * A_i' * B_j'.
  Equation    : X_ij ~ beta_0 + beta_1 * A_i' * B_j'  (weighted by 1 / (A_i' * B_j'))
  Requirement : Free oligo modification experiment required to measure non-proximal counts.
  Strengths   : More accurate than iterative method; performs well in low signal-to-noise
                and multi-dimer scenarios; greater robustness for complex biological cases.
  Limitations : Unstable when non-proximal probe counts are near zero;
                not optimal for high signal-to-noise homodimer/heterodimer-only scenarios.
                Suffers false positives when noise is very low (Fig 4c).
  Best for    : Low signal-to-noise and scenarios with multiple protein dimers or
                overabundant proteins.

Ensemble Method (recommended):
  Mechanism   : Apply LR method first; use LR output (protein complex matrix) as
                initialisation for iterative method instead of all-zeroes matrix.
                Bridges weaknesses of both methods.
  Requirement : Free oligo modification experimental procedure required.
  Strengths   : Robust and consistent across all 8 biological scenario types and both
                high and low signal-to-noise cases (800 randomised tests).
                Highest average prediction score; lowest variance; fewest false positives.
                Most accurate quantification of true-positive complex abundance in single
                cells (Fig 4d).
  Best for    : Recommended default when true biological conditions are uncertain.

Fisher's Exact Test:
  Mechanism   : One-sided Fisher's exact test on 2x2 PLA product contingency table per cell.
                Benjamini-Hochberg correction applied. Identifies enriched protein complexes.
  Use         : Statistical validation of complex enrichment; complementary to quantification.

Prediction Score:
  Formula     : Score = 0.5 * SumPearson - 0.4 * SumMeanDeviation - 0.1 * SumFPrate
  Result      : Ensemble method scores highest and shows lowest variance across all scenarios
                (S10d Fig, S2 Table).


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

--- ARM 1: JURKAT T CELLS ---

Step 1 | Cell Suspension Preparation (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : JurkatCells, PBS
  Operation   : Resuspend Jurkat T cells in PBS at appropriate concentration for staining
  Duration    : 5 min (mix)
  BioScript   : jurkatSuspension = mix jurkatCells with cellPBS for 5m

Step 2 | Nonspecific Binding Block (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : jurkatSuspension, BlockingBuffer
  Operation   : Mix with blocking buffer; incubate 10 min at 4 C
  Duration    : 10 min at 4 C
  Purpose     : Reduces nonspecific antibody binding background
  Notes       : Nonspecific binding <1% of both-specific PLA counts for extracellular
                assays (CD147:CD147 in Jurkat cells, S4 Fig)
  BioScript   : blockedCells = mix jurkatSuspension with blockingBuf for 10m
                heat blockedCells at 4c for 10m

Step 3 | Probe A Staining with Isotype Control (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : blockedCells, ProxSeqProbeA, IsotypeControlAntibody
  Operation   : Mix isotype control into Probe A panel; stain cells 30 min at 4 C
  Duration    : 30 min at 4 C
  Probe A panel: CD3, CD28, PD1, CD147, ICAM1, PDL1, HLADR, B7 + isotype control
  BioScript   : probeAWithControl = mix probeAStain with isotypeControl for 5s
                probeAStained = mix blockedCells with probeAWithControl for 30m
                heat probeAStained at 4c for 30m

Step 4 | Wash Unbound Probe A (Jurkat, 3x)
  Reagents    : probeAStained, WashBuffer (x3)
  Operation   : 3 sequential PBS washes, 2 min each
  Duration    : 3 x 2 min
  Notes       : Unligated probes do not contribute to PLA signal (dual-barcode requirement)
  BioScript   : washedA1 = mix probeAStained with wash1A for 2m
                washedA2 = mix washedA1 with wash2A for 2m
                jurkatProbeAReady = mix washedA2 with wash3A for 2m

Step 5 | Probe B Staining (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : jurkatProbeAReady, ProxSeqProbeB
  Operation   : Stain cells with Probe B panel 30 min at 4 C
  Duration    : 30 min at 4 C
  Notes       : After this step, cells carry both Probe A and Probe B on surface proteins
  BioScript   : probeBStained = mix jurkatProbeAReady with probeBStain for 30m
                heat probeBStained at 4c for 30m

Step 6 | Wash Unbound Probe B (Jurkat, 3x)
  Reagents    : probeBStained, WashBuffer (x3)
  Operation   : 3 sequential PBS washes, 2 min each
  BioScript   : washedB1 = mix probeBStained with wash1B for 2m
                washedB2 = mix washedB1 with wash2B for 2m
                jurkatDualStained = mix washedB2 with wash3B for 2m

Step 7 | Free Oligo Addition (Jurkat, non-proximal probe measurement)
  Reagents    : jurkatDualStained, FreeOligoA, FreeOligoB
  Operation   : Mix free oligos together; add to dual-stained cells; incubate 10 min at 4 C
  Duration    : 10 min at 4 C
  Purpose     : FreeOligoA generates PLA products Protein_X:free_oligo_B (non-proximal Probe A)
                FreeOligoB generates PLA products free_oligo_A:Protein_Y (non-proximal Probe B)
                Non-proximal counts required for LR method: Y_ij = X_ij - beta_1 * A_i' * B_j'
                (Methods: LR method; Ref [4] Vistain et al. Nat Methods 2022)
  BioScript   : freeOligoMix = mix freeOligoADrop with freeOligoBDrop for 5s
                jurkatWithFreeOligos = mix jurkatDualStained with freeOligoMix for 10m
                heat jurkatWithFreeOligos at 4c for 10m

Step 8 | Proximity Ligation Reaction (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : jurkatWithFreeOligos, LigationBuffer, Ligase (T4 DNA Ligase)
  Operation   : Mix ligation buffer with ligase; add to cells; incubate 30 min at 25 C
  Duration    : 30 min at 25 C
  Ligation distance: 50-70 nm (50 nm threshold in simulation model)
  Proximity noise: E(X_ij) = (d_ligation^2 / 4R^2) * A_i * B_j
                   (R = 5 um, d_ligation = 50 nm; Methods: Theoretical calculation)
  BioScript   : ligationReagents = mix ligBuf with ligase for 5s
                jurkatLigated = mix jurkatWithFreeOligos with ligationReagents for 30m
                heat jurkatLigated at 25c for 30m

Step 9 | Ligation Stop and Wash (Jurkat, 3x)
  Reagents    : jurkatLigated, StopSolution, WashBuffer (x3)
  Operation   : Add stop solution (5 min mix); 3x PBS wash, 2 min each
  Duration    : 5 min (stop) + 3 x 2 min (washes)
  BioScript   : jurkatStopped = mix jurkatLigated with stopSol for 5m
                jurkatLigatedReady = (3x wash chain, 2m each)

Step 10 | Single-Cell Encapsulation and Library Preparation (Jurkat)
  Reagents    : jurkatLigatedReady, DNALibraryBuffer, SequencingReagent
  Operation   : Encapsulate cells in individual droplets; lyse; capture mRNA (poly-dT)
                and PLA products (barcoded sequences); prepare combined cDNA + PLA library
  Duration    : 30 min at 37 C (library prep) + 10 min (sequencing reagent addition)
  Output      : Combined scRNA-seq + Prox-seq sequencing library
  BioScript   : jurkatLibPrep = mix jurkatLigatedReady with dnaLibBuf for 30m
                heat jurkatLibPrep at 37c for 30m
                jurkatSeqReady = mix jurkatLibPrep with seqReagentJ for 10m

Step 11 | Jurkat Library QC and Branching Gate
  Reagents    : jurkatSeqReady
  Operation   : detect seqLibraryQC on jurkatSeqReady for 30s
  Detection   : Abstracts fluorometric/electrophoretic QC (Bioanalyzer, Qubit)
                Checks fragment size, library complexity, UMI diversity
  Branch Pass : jurkatLibSignal > 0  ->  dispose(jurkatSeqReady)  ->  proceed to Raji arm
  Branch Fail : jurkatLibSignal <= 0 ->  drain(jurkatSeqReady)    ->  proceed to Raji arm

--- ARM 2: RAJI B CELLS ---
  Steps 12-22 are structurally identical to Jurkat steps 1-11.
  Key differences:
    - RajiCells instead of JurkatCells
    - Raji cells express ICAM1:ICAM1 homodimer (confirmed by all 3 methods, Fig 5d-f)
    - Raji cells do not express CD3 or PD1 (near-zero PLA counts for those pairs)
    - Non-proximal Raji probe counts used to calibrate proximity noise for Raji arm
    - Final QC gate on rajiLibSignal; terminal block disposes rajiLibSignal

Step 12 | Cell Suspension Preparation (Raji)        -- same as Step 1 for Raji
Step 13 | Nonspecific Binding Block (Raji)           -- same as Step 2 for Raji
Step 14 | Probe A Staining with Isotype Control (Raji) -- same as Step 3 for Raji
Step 15 | Wash Unbound Probe A (Raji, 3x)            -- same as Step 4 for Raji
Step 16 | Probe B Staining (Raji)                    -- same as Step 5 for Raji
Step 17 | Wash Unbound Probe B (Raji, 3x)            -- same as Step 6 for Raji
Step 18 | Free Oligo Addition (Raji)                 -- same as Step 7 for Raji
Step 19 | Proximity Ligation Reaction (Raji)         -- same as Step 8 for Raji
Step 20 | Ligation Stop and Wash (Raji, 3x)          -- same as Step 9 for Raji
Step 21 | Single-Cell Encapsulation and Library Preparation (Raji) -- same as Step 10 for Raji
Step 22 | Raji Library QC and Branching Gate
  Branch Pass : rajiLibSignal > 0  ->  dispose(rajiSeqReady)  ->  terminal (BB7)
  Branch Fail : rajiLibSignal <= 0 ->  drain(rajiSeqReady)    ->  terminal (BB7)

Terminal     : dispose(rajiLibSignal)
               Experiment ends. Sequencing data submitted to computational pipeline.
               Code: https://github.com/tay-lab/Prox-seq_computation
               Data: GEO GSE196130


SIMULATION MODEL
----------------
Physical model of PLA product formation per single cell:
  - Non-interacting Probe A points generated as random points on sphere (radius R = 5 um)
  - Non-interacting Probe B points generated as random points on sphere
  - Protein complex points generated as random points; added to both Probe A and Probe B sets
  - Pairwise Euclidean distances computed between all Probe A and Probe B points
  - Pairs within ligation distance threshold (50 nm) are ligated; each probe ligated at most once
  - Non-ligated points returned as non-proximal probe count (measured by free oligo modification)

Variance model:
  - Simulated counts without variance follow Poisson distribution
  - Real data shows overdispersion; negative binomial (NB) variance added: n_NB = 1.5
  - NB formulation: A_i^(t) ~ NegativeBinomial(n_NB, p_NB); p_NB = (1 + A_i/n_NB)^-1
  - NB variance captures positive correlation between PLA product count and non-proximal
    probe count in real data (S1b-S1g Fig)

Calibration:
  - Posterior predictive checks (PPCs): Mann-Whitney U statistic and coefficient of variation
    compared between replicated simulated data and real Jurkat/Raji data (S3a, S3b Fig)
  - n_NB = 1.5 chosen as mean of best-fit n_NB values across all PLA products (S3c Fig)
  - Simulation faithfully captures key aspects of real Prox-seq data
