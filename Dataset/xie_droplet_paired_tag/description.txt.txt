PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Droplet-based Single-cell Joint Profiling of Histone Modification and Transcriptome (Droplet Paired-Tag)
Source Link  : https://www.protocols.io/view/droplet-based-single-cell-joint-profiling-of-histo-eq2ly6kergx9/v1
Paper DOI    : https://dx.doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.pex-2310/v1
Journal      : Research Square (Method Article preprint), 2023
Authors      : Yang Xie, Chenxu Zhu, Zhaoning Wang, Melodi Tastemel, Lei Chang, Yang Eric Li, Bing Ren
Affiliation  : Department of Cellular and Molecular Medicine, University of California San Diego;
               Ludwig Institute for Cancer Research, La Jolla; Center for Epigenomics, UCSD
Platform     : 10x Chromium Next GEM Single Cell Multiome (ATAC + Gene Expression) with pA-Tn5 tagmentation


OVERVIEW
--------
Droplet Paired-Tag is a rapid multiomic technique for joint single-cell profiling of histone
modifications and gene expression simultaneously at scale. The method adapts the original
combinatorial indexing-based Paired-Tag protocol by incorporating nuclei barcoding on the
commercially available 10x Chromium Single Cell Multiome platform and simplifying the library
preparation process.

Compared to conventional Paired-Tag, Droplet Paired-Tag delivers a higher signal-to-noise ratio
for histone modification data and generates joint modality data with the same efficiency as
single-modality methods such as scRNA-seq and scCUT&Tag. The protocol has been validated on
both cultured mammalian cells (mouse embryonic stem cells, mESC) and primary mouse brain tissue,
where it accurately reconstructs cell type-specific active (H3K27ac) and repressive (H3K27me3)
gene regulatory networks.

The core innovation is a bridged Protein A-Tn5 (pA-Tn5) transposome that carries 10x Genomics-
compatible adapter sequences. Nuclei are first incubated overnight with an anti-histone antibody
bound to pA-Tn5. Tagmentation is then activated by Mg2+, inserting sequencing adapters at histone-
marked genomic loci. Tagmented nuclei are loaded onto the Chromium chip, where each nucleus is
co-encapsulated with a gel bead (GEM) barcoding both the CUT&Tag DNA fragments and the nuclear
mRNA in the same droplet, enabling paired epigenomic and transcriptomic readout per cell.

The three-day protocol produces two independently sequenced libraries per sample:
  - DNA library: histone modification CUT&Tag (200-1000 bp; nucleosome ladder pattern)
  - RNA library: single-nucleus gene expression cDNA (400-500 bp peak, narrow)


DEVICE / PLATFORM
-----------------
Microfluidics : 10x Chromium Next GEM platform (ChromiumX controller)
Chip          : Chromium Next GEM Chip J Single Cell Kit (Cat#1000234)
Encapsulation : Gel-bead-in-emulsion (GEM) droplet generation; each GEM contains one nucleus
                + one gel bead; barcoded RT primer on bead captures both cDNA and tagmented DNA
Sequencing    : Illumina NextSeq 2000
  DNA library : 200-cycle kit (#20046812); cycle config 100+8+24+100
                (Read1 + Index1 + Index2 [cellular barcodes] + Read2)
  RNA library : 100-cycle kit (#20046811); cycle config 28+10+10+72
                (Read1 [cellular barcodes] + Index1 + Index2 + Read2)
Demultiplexing: cellranger-arc mkfastq (10X Genomics); downstream analysis with in-house pipeline

PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

DAY 1

Step 1 | OMNI Nuclei Lysis Buffer Preparation
  Combine all components (see Reagents above) in order on ice; mix gently.
  Use immediately; do not pre-make. Keep on ice throughout.

Step 2 | Antibody + pA-Tn5 Conjugation
  Mix  : 1 ug rabbit polyclonal anti-histone antibody + 1 uL assembled pA-Tn5 (0.4 mg/mL)
         in 50 uL MED1 buffer per PCR tube.
  Incubation : Rotate 60 min at room temperature (25 C).
  IR duration: 3600.0 SECOND
  Storage : Conjugate can be stored at -20 C if not used immediately.

Step 3 | Nuclei Preparation (Cultured Cells)
  Input    : 250,000 - 1,000,000 cells
  a) Disassociate cells per culture protocol. Spin at 300 RCF / 5 min.
     Wash once with PBS; spin at 300 RCF / 5 min (4 C). [IR: 300.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  b) Resuspend in 750 uL OMNI buffer (<5 million cells/mL); sit on ice 5 min.
     [IR: mix @ 300.0 SECOND]
  c) Spin at 1,000 RCF / 10 min at 4 C. [IR: 600.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  d) Resuspend nuclei in 100 uL MED1 buffer. Count using cell counter.

Step 4 | Epitope Targeting (Overnight)
  Input    : 250,000 - 500,000 nuclei
  a) Aliquot nuclei into Maximum Recovery tubes. Spin at 1,000 RCF / 10 min at 4 C.
     [IR: 600.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  b) Resuspend in 25 uL MED1. Add conjugated antibody-pA-Tn5 (in MED1).
  c) Incubate overnight at 4 C with rotation (~12 h).
     [IR: 43200.0 SECOND @ 4 C; BioScript: 720m]


DAY 2

Step 5 | MED Buffer 2 Preparation (prepare 3 fresh working volumes)
  Prepare three independent 1 mL batches of MED2 (composition in Reagents section above).
  Instance 1 (med2BufferWash1): first MED2 wash
  Instance 2 (med2BufferWash2): second MED2 wash
  Instance 3 (med2BufferTag)  : tagmentation resuspension
  Note: Three fresh builds required; BioScript cannot re-dispense from computed variables.

Step 6 | PBS + RI Wash Buffer and 1X Nuclei Buffer Preparation
  Prepare PBS + RNase Inhibitor wash buffer and 1X Nuclei Buffer (see Reagents above).
  Use within 1 h; do not pre-make the day before.

Step 7 | Nuclei Washing (x2) and Tn5 Tagmentation
  a) Spin nuclei at 300 RCF / 10 min at 4 C. Wash with 50 uL MED2. [IR: 600.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  b) Repeat wash: add 50 uL MED2; spin 300 RCF / 10 min at 4 C. [IR: 600.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  c) Resuspend in 50 uL MED2. Add 2 uL MgCl2 (250 mM) to activate Tn5.
  d) Rotate in ThermoMixer at 550 rpm, 37 C, 60 min. [IR: 3600.0 SECOND @ 37 C]
     Note: Thaw 10X Multiome gel beads and barcoding reagents at room temperature during this step.

Step 8 | Stop Tagmentation and Post-Tagmentation Wash
  a) Add 50 uL 2X Stop Solution; mix well.
  b) Spin at 500 RCF / 6 min at 4 C. [IR: 360.0 SECOND @ 4 C]
  c) Wash with PBS + RI buffer.
  d) Spin at 500 RCF / 6 min at 4 C. [IR: 360.0 SECOND @ 4 C]

Step 9 | Nuclei Resuspension and Chromium Chip Loading Preparation
  a) Resuspend in 20 uL 1X Nuclei Buffer. Count nuclei using cell counter.
  b) Aliquot 10,000-16,000 nuclei in 8 uL into PCR tube.
  c) Add 7 uL ATAC Buffer B (PN-2000193). Total volume: 15 uL.
  d) Proceed immediately to droplet generation.

Step 10 | RT Master Mix Preparation and GEM Droplet Generation
  Prepare RT master mix:
    Barcoding Reagent Mix (PN-2000267) : 49.5 uL
    Template Switch Oligo (PN-3000228) :  1.1 uL
    Reducing Agent B (PN-2000087)      :  1.9 uL
    Barcoding Enzyme Mix (PN-2000266)  :  7.5 uL
    Total                              : 60.0 uL
  Add 60 uL master mix to 15 uL nuclei mixture. Load onto Chip J.
  Proceed to droplet generation on ChromiumX per 10X Multiome user guide CG000338.
  Aspirate 100 uL emulsion into new PCR tube after generation.
  Note: `mix` in BioScript abstracts the microfluidic GEM encapsulation event.

Step 11 | Reverse Transcription and Cell Barcoding in Droplets
  Incubate emulsion in Bio-Rad T100 thermal cycler (lid at 50 C):
    Stage 1 : 37 C for 45 min  -- reverse transcription (cDNA synthesis)
              [IR: 2700.0 SECOND @ 37 C]
    Stage 2 : 25 C for 30 min  -- template switching and cell barcoding
              [IR: 1800.0 SECOND @ 25 C]
    Hold    : 4 C
  Note: Both cell barcoding and cDNA synthesis complete during this incubation.

Step 12 | Quench and Products Cleanup
  a) Add 5 uL Quenching Agent (PN-2000269). Pipette mix gently.
  b) Proceed to Dynabeads + SPRI bead cleanup per 10X Multiome user guide CG000338.
     Dynabeads cleanup: 5 min mix [IR: 300.0 SECOND]
     SPRI bead cleanup: 5 min mix [IR: 300.0 SECOND]


DAY 3

Step 13 | Pre-amplification
  Protocol : Per 10X Multiome user guide CG000338 with one modification:
             Elute in 80 uL after SPRI cleanup (instead of standard 160 uL).
  Rationale: Concentrates tagmented fragments; scCUT&Tag library complexity is lower than ATAC;
             higher input increases DNA library complexity.
  Extension: 72 C / 5 min [IR: 300.0 SECOND @ 72 C]
  PCR cycles:
    H3K27ac  : 13 cycles (recommended)
    H3K27me3 : 12 cycles (recommended)
    Adjust based on sample type and estimated recovered nuclei.
  Storage  : Pre-amplified product stable at -20 C for up to 6 months.

Step 14 | DNA and RNA Library Preparation
  Split pre-amplified product:
    DNA library : use 40 uL purified product
    RNA library : use 17.5 uL purified product + 17.5 uL H2O
                  (maintains standard 10X Multiome input volume)
  Both library preps per 10X Multiome user guide CG000338.
  Final SPRI cleanup: 5 min [IR: 300.0 SECOND]

Step 15 | Library QC (Fragment Size Check)
  Instrument : Fragment Analyzer or Agilent TapeStation
  Expected   :
    DNA library (histone CUT&Tag) : 200-1,000 bp; nucleosome ladder pattern
    RNA library (cDNA)            : 400-500 bp peak; narrow distribution
  QC gate    :
    PASS (libraryQCSignal > 0)  -> proceed to sequencing [BB: bsbbif_2_t; dispose]
    FAIL (libraryQCSignal <= 0) -> re-evaluate PCR cycles or nuclei input [BB: bsbbif_3_f; drain]
  Detection duration: 30 s [IR: 30.0 SECOND]

Step 16 | Sequencing
  DNA library : Illumina NextSeq 2000; 200-cycle kit (#20046812)
                Cycle config: 100 (R1) + 8 (I1) + 24 (I2, cellular barcodes) + 100 (R2)
  RNA library : Illumina NextSeq 2000; 100-cycle kit (#20046811)
                Cycle config: 28 (R1, cellular barcodes) + 10 (I1) + 10 (I2) + 72 (R2)
  Demux       : cellranger-arc mkfastq; analysis with in-house pipeline


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Low nuclei yield before chip loading:
  - Increase input cells to >250,000.
  - Check for nuclei clumping during CUT&Tag; fragile nuclei may lyse and aggregate in high-salt buffer.
  - If clumping observed: crosslink nuclei with 0.1% formaldehyde 5 min at room temperature before lysis.
  - Typical yield: 30-60% of input nuclei recovered before loading.

Low library complexity (DNA):
  - scCUT&Tag library complexity is overall lower than snATAC-seq.
  - Increase indexing PCR cycles (13 for H3K27ac, 12 for H3K27me3 as baseline).
  - Confirm elution volume at pre-amplification step is 80 uL (not 160 uL).

Unexpected fragment size distribution:
  - Verify Tn5 activation: ensure MgCl2 was added and tagmentation ran at 37 C / 60 min.
  - Check stop solution was added immediately after tagmentation.
  - Verify antibody specificity and pA-Tn5 assembly quality.


TIME ESTIMATION
---------------
Day 1:
  Antibody + pA-Tn5 conjugation (Step 2)       :  1.0 - 1.5 h
  Nuclei preparation (Step 3)                   :  1.0 - 1.5 h
  Epitope targeting (Step 4)                    :  overnight (~12 h)

Day 2:
  Buffer preparation (Steps 5-6)                :  0.5 h
  Nuclei washing and tagmentation (Step 7)      :  2.0 - 2.5 h
  Stop, wash, and nuclei resuspension (Steps 8-9):  0.5 h
  Droplet generation (Step 10)                  :  0.5 h
  Reverse transcription and barcoding (Step 11) :  2.0 h
  Products cleanup (Step 12)                    :  1.0 h

Day 3:
  Library pre-amplification (Step 13)           :  1.0 - 2.0 h
  DNA and RNA library preparation (Step 14)     :  3.0 - 4.0 h
  Library QC (Step 15)                          :  0.5 h

Duration unit convention:
  BioScript supports `s` and `m` tokens only (no `h`).
  All hour and overnight values converted to minutes.
  Key conversions (BioScript -> IR):
    60 min conjugation          ->  60m  -> 3600.0 SECOND
    overnight epitope incubation -> 720m -> 43200.0 SECOND
    60 min tagmentation         ->  60m  -> 3600.0 SECOND
    45 min RT stage 1           ->  45m  -> 2700.0 SECOND
    30 min barcoding stage 2    ->  30m  -> 1800.0 SECOND

Buffer rebuild note:
  BioScript `dispense` is restricted to declared manifests and cannot draw from computed mix
  variables. Buffers needed at multiple protocol sites (MED1 x3 instances for conjugation,
  nuclei resuspension, and epitope prep; MED2 x3 instances for two washes and tagmentation
  resuspend) are each rebuilt independently from their component manifests using distinct
  variable name prefixes (m1a_, m1b_, m1c_ for MED1; m2a_, m2b_, m2c_ for MED2).

drain normalisation:
  `drain` in the .bs fail branch normalises to `dispose` in the IR output.
  This is standard lilott8/BioScript compiler behaviour; consistent with samlali and moazami
  reference protocol IR outputs.
