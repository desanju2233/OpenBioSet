PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Digital Microfluidic Method for in Situ Formation of Porous Polymer Monoliths with Application to Solid-Phase Extraction
Source Link  : https://microfluidics.utoronto.ca/papers/Yang_2011_A%20Digital%20Microfluidic%20Method%20for%20in%20Situ%20Formation%20of%20Porous%20Polymer%20Monoliths%20with%20Application%20to%20Solid-Phase%20Extraction_Small.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/ac2002388
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2011, Vol. 83, pp. 3824-3830
Platform     : Digital microfluidics (DMF) with in situ UV-polymerized porous polymer monolith (PPM) discs on EWOD electrode array


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes the first combination of digital microfluidics (DMF) and porous polymer
monoliths (PPMs) for on-chip solid-phase extraction (SPE). Circular PPM discs (radius ~1 mm,
height 270 um) are formed in situ on a DMF device by dispensing 1 uL droplets of a C12 monomer
casting solution onto the actuation electrode array, translating them to a designated position,
and polymerizing by UV irradiation (100 W, 365 nm, 5 min). The resulting DMF-SPE method performs
all fluid handling steps -- activation, equilibration, sample loading, washing, and elution --
using microliter droplets manipulated by electrostatic forces on the electrode array.

The protocol is divided into three functional phases encoded in BioScript:
  1. In situ PPM disc formation by UV-initiated photopolymerization (Protocol 1)
  2. Standard four-step DMF-SPE validated against commercial C18 ZipTips (Protocol 2)
  3. Preparative sample cleanup: desalting (Protocol 3a) and surfactant removal (Protocol 3b),
     both validated by off-chip nanoESI-MS detection of angiotensin II at m/z 1047


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Design 1 (Standard SPE device -- Protocols 2 and 3):
  Bottom plate : Patterned chromium electrodes on glass; 88 actuation electrodes (2.2 x 2.2 mm
                 each) + 10 reservoir electrodes (5 x 5 mm each); interelectrode gap 40 um.
                 Coated with 7 um Parylene-C + 50 nm Teflon-AF.
  Top plate    : Unpatterned ITO-glass; Teflon-AF 50 nm coating.
  Spacer       : Three pieces double-sided tape; total thickness 270 um.
  Unit droplet : 1 uL (one 2.2 x 2.2 mm electrode footprint x 270 um height).
  Reservoirs   : Elution solvent, Sample, Monomer (C12), Wash solvent, Activation solvent.

Design 2 (Concentration device -- Protocol 4; preliminary):
  Large electrodes  : 12 x (7.5 x 7.5 mm); unit droplet 12 uL; for sample, activation, wash.
  Small electrodes  : 19 x (2.2 x 2.2 mm); unit droplet 1 uL; for elution solvent and monomer.
  Purpose           : Concentrate large sample volume into a small elution droplet (~9-fold).

Actuation signal : 220-300 V_pp, 18 kHz; amplified output of Agilent function generator.
Fabrication      : University of Toronto ECTI cleanroom; photolithography + chromium etching;
                   Parylene-C by vapour deposition (Specialty Coating Systems);
                   Teflon-AF spin-coated at 1000 rpm 30 s, post-baked 160 C 10 min.


PPM DISC CHARACTERIZATION
--------------------------
Formation     : 1 uL C12 casting solution dispensed, driven to centre electrode position,
                UV-polymerized (100 W, 365 nm, 5 min).
Disc geometry : Radius ~1 mm, height 270 um (equal to device spacer thickness).
Morphology    : Skin-like outer layer; popcorn-like porous interior (SEM, Hitachi S-5200).
Surface area  : 3.7 +/- 0.2 m2/g by BET N2 adsorption (comparable to capillary-formed PPMs).
Reproducibility: Coefficient of variation in disc diameter = 10% (n=5 devices).
Passive dispensing: <10% of droplet volume remains on monolith during actuation;
                >90% of droplet continues moving. PPM not covalently bound to device surface;
                removable/replaceable with tweezers when device is disassembled.


REAGENTS
--------
C12 casting solution (prepared within 3 days of use):
  Butyl acrylate              279 uL
  1,3-Butanediol diacrylate   150 uL
  Lauryl acrylate              69 uL
  Photoinitiator               2.5 mg 2,2-dimethoxy-2-phenylacetophenone
  Porogen                      1 mL (4:1:1 acetonitrile / 95% EtOH / 5 mM phosphate pH 6.8)

SPE solvents:
  Activation                  0.1% v/v formic acid in acetonitrile
  Equilibration               0.5% v/v formic acid in deionized water
  Wash                        0.5% v/v formic acid in deionized water
  Elution (peptides)          0.1% v/v formic acid in acetonitrile
  Elution (fluorescein QC)    500 mM sodium borate in deionized water, pH 9.0

Model analytes:
  Fluorescein                 5 uM in 0.5% v/v aqueous FA (loading time / elution volume QC)
  Angiotensin IV (labeled)    10 uM fluorescamine-labeled angiotensin IV in 0.1% FA (efficiency)
  Angiotensin II + NaCl       1 uM Ang II in 100 mM NaCl (desalting validation)
  Angiotensin II + Pluronic   1 uM Ang II in 0.05% w/v Pluronic F68 (surfactant removal)

Fluorescamine labeling: 100 uL fluorescamine (3 mg/mL in acetone) + 10 uL Ang IV
  (10 mM in 10 mM borate pH 9) + 890 uL acetone; incubate >=2 h; dilute 10x with 0.1% FA.
Deionized water: 18 MOhm cm at 25 C; filtered 0.2 um nylon; sonicated 5 min.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | In Situ PPM Disc Formation  [Protocol 1]
  Reagent    : C12 casting solution (1 uL unit droplet)
  Operation  : Dispense from monomer reservoir -> translate to centre electrode position
               (electrode actuation, 220-300 V_pp, 18 kHz) -> UV polymerize.
  UV params  : 100 W lamp, 365 nm, 5 min exposure.
  Result     : Circular PPM disc; radius ~1 mm, height 270 um, SSA 3.7 +/- 0.2 m2/g.
  BioScript  : Second MonomersC12 dispense used as required second operand for mix (translation);
               heat at 25c for 5m abstracts UV exposure (no UV primitive in compiler).
  Repeated   : Three independent discs formed (disc1, disc2, disc3) for Protocols 2, 3a, 3b.

Step 2 | PPM Activation  [Protocol 2, Step 1a]
  Reagent    : Activation solvent (0.1% v/v FA in ACN); 5 uL.
  Operation  : Dispense -> actuate 5x across PPM disc -> incubate 1 min -> move to waste.
  BioScript  : mix for 1m; dispose activationSolvent.
  Purpose    : Conditions C12 stationary phase for reversed-phase SPE.

Step 3 | PPM Equilibration  [Protocol 2, Step 1b]
  Reagent    : Equilibration solvent (0.5% v/v FA in H2O); 5 uL.
  Operation  : Dispense -> actuate 5x across PPM disc -> incubate 1 min -> move to waste.
  BioScript  : mix for 1m; dispose equilibrationSolvent.

Step 4 | Sample Loading  [Protocol 2, Step 2]
  Reagent    : Fluorescamine-labeled angiotensin IV (10 uM in 0.1% FA); 1 uL.
  Operation  : Dispense -> actuate 5x across PPM disc -> incubate -> move to waste.
  Incubation : 2 min (optimized; Fig. 3a: <20% fluorescence remains in droplet;
               p = 0.30 for 2 vs 4 min -- not significant; 2 min selected for all experiments).
  BioScript  : mix for 2m; dispose peptideSample.

Step 5 | PPM Washing  [Protocol 2, Step 3]
  Reagent    : Wash solvent (0.5% v/v FA in H2O); 2 x 5 uL.
  Operation  : Each droplet dispensed, actuated 5x across PPM disc, moved to waste.
  BioScript  : 2 x (dispense; mix for 30s; dispose washSolvent).
  Purpose    : Removes unbound matrix while retaining analyte on C12 stationary phase.

Step 6 | Analyte Elution  [Protocol 2, Step 4]
  Reagent    : Elution solvent (0.1% v/v FA in ACN for peptides; borate pH 9.0 for fluorescein);
               2 x 5 uL; pooled at sample collection reservoir.
  Operation  : Each droplet dispensed, actuated 5x across PPM disc, moved to collector.
  Incubation : Optimized to 2 elution droplets (Fig. 3b: >90% extracted; p = 0.25 for 2 vs 3
               droplets -- not significant; 2 droplets selected for all experiments).
  Detection  : Plate reader fluorescence (ex 390/em 460 nm peptide; ex 480/em 520 nm fluorescein).
  BioScript  : 2 x (dispense; mix for 30s); detect speExtractionDetection for 30s.
  Branch     : speSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB2); speSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB3).

Step 7 | Preparative DMF-SPE: Desalting  [Protocol 3a]
  Fresh disc : disc2 formed independently (FIX #2: disc1 consumed by Protocol 2).
  Sample     : Ang II 1 uM in 100 mM NaCl; 1 uL.
  Procedure  : Same 4-step DMF-SPE as Protocol 2 (Steps 2-6 above); 2 min loading; 2 elutions.
  Problem    : 100 mM NaCl suppresses nanoESI signal; [M+H]+ at m/z 1047 absent without SPE.
  Result     : Strong [M+H]+ signal at m/z 1047 after DMF-SPE (Fig. 4a).
  Analysis   : Off-chip nanoESI-MS (LTQ Finnigan); spray 1.7-2.0 kV; flow 0.5 uL/min;
               capillary temp 200 C. Eluate resolubilized in 50 uL 50% ACN / 0.1% FA.
  Branch     : desaltSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB5); desaltSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB6).

Step 8 | Preparative DMF-SPE: Surfactant Removal  [Protocol 3b]
  Fresh disc : disc3 formed independently (FIX #2: disc2 consumed by Protocol 3a).
  Sample     : Ang II 1 uM in 0.05% w/v Pluronic F68; 1 uL.
  Context    : Pluronic F68 is standard DMF additive to prevent protein/peptide adsorption to
               Teflon-AF device surfaces (used as 0.05% w/v), but is a strong nanoESI ion
               suppressor; removal prior to MS analysis is critical.
  Procedure  : Same 4-step DMF-SPE as Protocol 2; 2 min loading; 2 elutions.
  Problem    : 0.05% Pluronic F68 suppresses [M+H]+ at m/z 1047 without SPE.
  Result     : Strong [M+H]+ signal at m/z 1047 after DMF-SPE (Fig. 4b).
  Analysis   : Off-chip nanoESI-MS (same conditions as desalting).
  Branch     : surfactantSignal > 0 -> dispose (BB8); surfactantSignal <= 0 -> drain (BB9).


SPE PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
-----------------------
Extraction efficiency (fluorescamine-labeled Ang IV):
  DMF-SPE (C12 PPM)  : 93 +/- 14%  (n=3)
  ZipTip (C18)       : 92 +/- 5%   (n=3)
  Conclusion         : Comparable; no significant difference.

Concentration factor (fluorescein; preliminary; Design 2 device):
  Expected : ~12x (12 uL sample / 1 uL elution)
  Achieved : ~9x
  Range    : Within ZipTip commercial range of 2.5-10x.


BIOSCRIPT COMPILER NOTES
-------------------------
BioScript file : yang_2011_dmf_spe_ppm.bs
Compiler       : lilott8/BioScript (ANTLR4-based)
IR output      : 10 basic blocks (BB1-BB10), 3 if/else gates, 12 CFG edges
DAG file       : yang_2011_dmf_spe_ppm_main_dag.dot
Duration units : BioScript supports s and m only; h token not supported.

Duration conversions:
  UV polymerization        5 min   -> 5m   (IR: 300.0 SECOND)
  Activation/equilibration 1 min   -> 1m   (IR: 60.0 SECOND)
  Sample loading           2 min   -> 2m   (IR: 120.0 SECOND)
  Wash / elution           30 s    -> 30s  (IR: 30.0 SECOND)
  Translation mix          10 s    -> 10s  (IR: 10.0 SECOND)

FIX #1 -- heat is void in lilott8/BioScript:
  `heat var at Tc for Td` mutates var in place and returns nothing.
  Original error: UndefinedVariable -- main.ppmDisc is not defined.
  Original code:  heat centredDroplet at 25c for 5m
                  ppmDisc = centredDroplet   <- INVALID
  Fix: centredDroplet / disc2 / disc3 used directly after heat; no alias assigned.

FIX #2 -- each SPE protocol consumes its PPM disc:
  Protocol 2 drives solvents and sample through centredDroplet via sequential mix ops,
  leaving it consumed. Protocols 3a and 3b each form their own independent fresh disc:
    Protocol 2  : centredDroplet  (monomer1a + monomer1b)
    Protocol 3a : disc2           (monomer2a + monomer2b)
    Protocol 3b : disc3           (monomer3a + monomer3b)

mix two-operand constraint:
  BioScript mix requires exactly two operands. The PPM translation step (physical droplet
  movement with no second reagent) uses a second dispense of MonomersC12 as the required
  second operand; same reagent composition, no chemical change introduced.

            MonomersC12, FluoresceinSample, PeptideSample,
