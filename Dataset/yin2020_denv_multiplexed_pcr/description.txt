PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : A Non-Optical Multiplexed PCR Diagnostic Platform for Serotype-Specific Detection of Dengue Virus
Source Link  : https://academicweb.nd.edu/~changlab/documents/Yin2020.pdf
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1016/j.snb.2020.127854
Journal      : Sensors & Actuators B: Chemical, 2020, Vol. 310, Article 127854
Platform     : Integrated on-chip RT-PCR + ion-exchange membrane (IEM) sensor array on polycarbonate microfluidic chip


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes a non-optical, multiplexed, point-of-care (POC) microfluidic platform for
simultaneous serotype-specific detection of all four dengue virus (DENV) serotypes from 1 mL of
human blood plasma in a single on-chip PCR reactor. The platform integrates three functional units:

  1. Off-chip RNA extraction using a chitosan-modified Fusion 5 filter paper dip-and-rinse technique
  2. On-chip multiplex RT-PCR amplification of all four DENV serotypes in a single 25 uL reactor
  3. Ion-exchange membrane (IEM) sensor array detection via current-voltage curve (CVC) measurement

By replacing optical (fluorescence/RT-qPCR) readout with ion-selective nanomembrane sensors, the
platform eliminates spectral overlap constraints and can be scaled to detect arbitrarily large numbers
of targets from a single sample. The IEM sensor's ion-depletion action increases the Debye length in
the detection zone (>30 nm), making it insensitive to pH variation, ionic strength, duplex conformation,
and non-specific amplification products -- the key failure modes of optical and electrochemical sensors.

Total assay time: ~90 min. Limit of detection: 100 copies/mL RNA in spiked human plasma (3x better
than any prior PCR-based DENV technology, Table 1).


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Chip material  : Polycarbonate thermosoftening plastic (3 layers, each 0.3 mm thick)
Chip dimensions: 10 x 4 x 0.1 cm3 (l/w/t)
Fabrication    : Graphtec Pro FC 7000MK2-60 cutting plotter; thermal/pressure bonding

Sections:
  - On-chip PCR chip       : 25 uL reaction chamber; thermocouple plate for thermal cycling
  - AEM sensing chip       : Anion-exchange nanomembrane (AME) sensor array with input/output channels;
                             4 isolated reservoirs, one per DENV serotype sensor

AEM sensor composition:
  Material     : Polystyrene-divinylbenzene with strong basic quaternary ammonium groups;
                 polyethylene binder; polyamide/polyester textile fiber support (Mega a.s., Czech Republic)
  Dimensions   : 0.9 x 0.3 x 0.3 mm3 (l/w/t) per sensor piece; 1 or 4 pieces per sensor
  Functionalization: Serotype-specific oligoprobe attached via EDC coupling on the depletion side

Electrodes:
  Reference    : 2x Ag-AgCl (measure potential across nanomembrane sensor)
  Current      : 2x Platinum (apply current load 0-40 uA at 1 uA/s step rate)
  Potentiostat : Gamry 500 (Gamry Instruments, Warminster, PA); Gamry Framework software

Fluidics box:
  Dimensions   : 102 x 240 x 305 mm3 (l/w/t); contains fluidic pump and thermocouple heater


PATHOGENS AND SAMPLES
---------------------
- DENV-1  : WP-74 strain
- DENV-2  : OBS8041 strain
- DENV-3  : CH53489 strain
- DENV-4  : H241 strain
  (All DENV strains from Naval Medical Research Center, NMRC)
- ZIKV    : PRVABC59 strain (Emory University; used as non-specific negative control)
- Matrix  : Human blood plasma (ZenBio Inc., NC, USA); 1 mL per assay


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | TRIzol-Chloroform Lysis and Phase Separation
  Reagents     : HumanPlasma_DENV_RNA (1 mL), TRIzol_Reagent, Chloroform (0.2 mL)
  Operation    : Vortex with TRIzol 30 s; add chloroform, mix 60 s; centrifuge to separate phases
  Temperature  : 25 C (room temperature phase separation; 120 s)
  Output       : Aqueous supernatant containing DENV RNA (also contains PCR inhibitor
                 Guanidinium thiocyanate from TRIzol)
  BioScript    : Abstracted as mix(TRIzol, 30s) -> mix(Chloroform, 60s) -> heat(25C, 120s)
  Notes        : DENV RNA in plasma is predominantly encapsulated within virions; lysis step
                 is required to release internal RNA. TRIzol-chloroform emulsion aqueous phase
                 collected as supernatant for downstream filter paper extraction.

Step 2 | Chitosan Filter Paper Dip-and-Rinse RNA Extraction
  Reagents     : ChitosanFilterDisc (1 mm diameter), SDS_Wash (1% SDS), DI_Water
  Filter prep  : 5 cm Fusion 5 paper (Whatman); O2 plasma 2 min; overnight chitosan soak
                 (0.05% w/v in 0.1% acetic acid, pH 6.0); wash 3x DI water; vacuum dry;
                 cut to 1 mm discs; stored at room temperature
  Immersion    : Disc immersed in aqueous supernatant; incubate 5 min
  Wash         : 1% SDS (30 s) then DI water (30 s) to remove PCR inhibitors
  Mechanism    : Positively charged chitosan matrix electrostatically captures negatively
                 charged RNA molecules via physical entanglement and electrostatic adsorption
  Recovery     : Down to 50 copies/mL RNA; ~98% extraction efficiency (Liu et al. 2017)
  Duration     : ~10 min total (5 min incubation + 5 min wash)
  BioScript    : mix(disc, 5m) -> mix(SDS, 30s) -> mix(DI water, 30s)
  Notes        : No elution step; filter disc introduced directly into PCR mixture.
                 Chitosan also removes Guanidinium thiocyanate PCR inhibitor from TRIzol phase.

Step 3 | Multiplex RT-PCR Master Mix Assembly and On-Chip Loading
  Reagents     : RT_PCR_MasterMix (12.5 uL), DENV1_Primers (0.5 uL fwd+rev),
                 DENV2_Primers (0.25 uL fwd+rev), DENV3_Primers (0.5 uL fwd+rev),
                 DENV4_Primers (0.25 uL fwd+rev), iScript_RT (0.5 uL), NucleaseFree_H2O (5.0 uL)
  Total volume : 20 uL PCR reaction mixture
  Kit          : iTaq Universal SYBR Green One-Step Kit (Bio-Rad, cat. no. 1725150)
  Primer ref   : Santiago et al. 2013 (CDC real-time RT-PCR assay for DENV 1-4)
  Primer source: Integrated DNA Technologies, Inc.
  Loading      : RNA-loaded filter disc added to assembled PCR mixture; loaded into chip;
                 inlets and outlets sealed; placed on thermocouple plate
  BioScript    : Sequential mix of all primer sets into master mix; mix(rxn_mix, washed_disc, 10s)
  Notes        : Four primer sets in one 25 uL reactor. Spurious bands (primer dimers, mispriming)
                 visible by gel electrophoresis with four primer sets; these are non-issues for
                 IEM sensor detection because probe hybridisation is serotype-specific.

Step 4 | Reverse Transcription (50 C, 30 min)
  Operation    : heat
  Temperature  : 50 C
  Duration     : 1800 s (30 min)
  Purpose      : DENV RNA reverse transcribed to cDNA by iScript reverse transcriptase

Step 5 | RT Inactivation and Initial Denaturation (95 C, 2 min)
  Operation    : heat
  Temperature  : 95 C
  Duration     : 120 s (2 min)
  Purpose      : Inactivates reverse transcriptase; denatures RNA:cDNA hybrid; activates polymerase

Step 6 | PCR Cycling - Denaturation Phase (95 C, 35 cycles x 15 s)
  Operation    : heat
  Temperature  : 95 C
  Duration     : 525 s (35 cycles x 15 s; collapsed to single heat statement)
  BioScript    : No loop construct in lilott8/BioScript; 35 x 15 s = 525 s total

Step 7 | PCR Cycling - Annealing and Extension Phase (55 C, 35 cycles x 60 s)
  Operation    : heat
  Temperature  : 55 C
  Duration     : 2100 s (35 cycles x 60 s; collapsed to single heat statement)
  Amplicons    : DENV-1: 110 bp | DENV-2: 98 bp | DENV-3: 78 bp | DENV-4: 83 bp
  BioScript    : 35 x 60 s = 2100 s total

Step 8 | Post-PCR ssDNA Generation (95 C, 5 min)
  Operation    : heat
  Temperature  : 95 C
  Duration     : 300 s (5 min)
  Purpose      : Denatures dsDNA amplicons to ssDNA in same PCR chamber for IEM hybridisation
  Paper ref    : Section 2.4: "heated at 95 C for 5 min in the same chamber to get ssDNA
                 for the downstream detection"

Step 9 | IEM Sensor 1 - DENV-1 Detection
  Reagents     : ssdna_amplicons, DENV1_Oligoprobe_AEM, PBS_4x, PBS_0_1x
  Hybridisation: Incubate ssDNA amplicons on AEM sensor surface for 15 min
  Wash         : 4x PBS, 5 min (hydrodynamic shear removes non-specifically bound targets)
  Measurement  : Transfer to 0.1x PBS; CVC acquired 0-40 uA at 1 uA/s via Gamry 500
  Detection    : denv1Detection module; 30 s
  Expected CVC shift (target)   : ~0.55 V
  Detection cutoff              : 0.2 V (established from Fig. 4 reproducibility data)
  Branch       : denv1Signal > 0  -> dispose (BB2, DENV-1 detected)
                 denv1Signal <= 0 -> drain   (BB3, not detected)

Step 10 | IEM Sensor 2 - DENV-2 Detection
  Reagents     : ssdna_amplicons, DENV2_Oligoprobe_AEM, PBS_4x, PBS_0_1x
  Protocol     : Identical to Step 9
  Expected CVC shift (target)   : ~0.61 V
  Detection cutoff              : 0.2 V
  Branch       : denv2Signal > 0  -> dispose (BB5, DENV-2 detected)
                 denv2Signal <= 0 -> drain   (BB6, not detected)

Step 11 | IEM Sensor 3 - DENV-3 Detection
  Reagents     : ssdna_amplicons, DENV3_Oligoprobe_AEM, PBS_4x, PBS_0_1x
  Protocol     : Identical to Step 9
  Expected CVC shift (target)   : ~0.67 V
  Detection cutoff              : 0.2 V
  Branch       : denv3Signal > 0  -> dispose (BB8, DENV-3 detected)
                 denv3Signal <= 0 -> drain   (BB9, not detected)

Step 12 | IEM Sensor 4 - DENV-4 Detection
  Reagents     : ssdna_amplicons, DENV4_Oligoprobe_AEM, PBS_4x, PBS_0_1x
  Protocol     : Identical to Step 9
  Expected CVC shift (target)   : ~0.53 V
  Detection cutoff              : 0.2 V
  Branch       : denv4Signal > 0  -> dispose (BB11, DENV-4 detected)
                 denv4Signal <= 0 -> drain   (BB12, not detected)


IEM SENSOR PHYSICS
------------------
Mechanism    : External concentration polarization across anion-exchange membrane (AEM)
               under applied electric field. Asymmetric ion migration creates a depletion
               zone (ion-free, high resistance) and enrichment zone on opposite sides.
Depletion zone: Forms at second critical voltage; Debye length >30 nm (vs ~1 nm at normal
               PCR buffer ionic strength). This large Debye length makes CVC shift insensitive
               to duplex conformation, pairing sequence location, and dangling tail lengths.
Limiting current: Differential resistance in depletion regime orders of magnitude higher than
               membrane resistance alone. Small number of charged targets produces large CVC shift.
Overlimiting : Electroconvective instability (vortices) above limiting voltage shears off
               non-specifically bound targets; further enhances selectivity.
Detection    : Probe-functionalized AEM depletion side. Hybridised target-probe duplexes
               suppress depletion action and shift CVC in overlimiting regime by several volts.
               Shift is 10-100x larger than electrochemical sensor signals.
Selectivity  : Hydrodynamic shear (4x PBS wash + flow) + electroconvective vortices remove
               non-specific PCR products, primer dimers, and cross-reactive ZIKV/DENV serotypes.


PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
-------------------
Total assay time              : ~90 min
Limit of detection            : 100 copies/mL (DENV RNA in spiked human plasma)
Sample volume                 : 1 mL human blood plasma
Serotypes detected            : 4 (DENV-1, DENV-2, DENV-3, DENV-4) simultaneously
Detection cutoff (CVC shift)  : >0.2 V (target group average ~0.4 V; no overlap with control)
Validated against             : ZIKV, all three non-target DENV serotypes, primer dimers,
                                 misprimed non-target products, heterogeneous plasma matrix

Comparison (Table 1, paper):
  PCR                         : 350 copies/mL
  CDC RT-PCR                  : 1 x 10^3 GCE/mL
  ELISA                       : 600 copies/mL
  Optical Surface Plasmon Res.: 2 x 10^4 particles/mL
  FCCS                        : 9 x 10^9 particles/mL
  Nanoporous Alumina Membrane : 16 x 10^8 particles/mL
  IEM Sensor (this work)      : 100 copies/mL  <-- 3x better than nearest PCR-based method
