PROTOCOL DESCRIPTION
====================

Title        : Characterizing Deformability and Electrical Properties of Breast Cancer Cells
               Using a Microfluidic Device Combining Electrical Impedance Sensing and
               Mechanical Constriction
Source Link  : https://pubs.acs.org/ancham/article/90/1/912/818332/Characterizing-Deformability-and-Electrical
Paper DOI    : https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.analchem.7b03859
Journal      : Analytical Chemistry, 2018, Vol. 90 (1), pp. 912–919
Authors      : Ying Zhou, Dahou Yang, Yinning Zhou, Bee Luan Khoo, Jongyoon Han, Ye Ai
Affiliations : BioSyM IRG, SMART Centre, Singapore; SUTD Singapore; MIT Cambridge MA
Platform     : Pressure-driven PDMS microfluidic device with integrated coplanar gold electrodes
               in differential impedance configuration; Fluigent MFCS-EZ pressure controller


OVERVIEW
--------
This protocol describes simultaneous characterisation of single-cell mechanical deformability
and electrical properties as cells transit a narrow PDMS constriction under applied pressure.
Four cell populations are compared: untreated MCF-7 (live breast cancer cells), PMA-modified
MCF-7 (modMCF-7, pharmacologically softened), formaldehyde-fixed MCF-7 (stiffened reference),
and red blood cells (RBCs, highly deformable positive reference).

The key innovation is a differential four-electrode-pair impedance configuration that
simultaneously measures:
  (1) Z_undeformed: electrical impedance of the intact cell before entering the constriction,
      captured by two outer electrode pairs positioned upstream/downstream of the constriction.
      This reflects cell size and membrane capacitance.
  (2) Z_deformed: electrical impedance of the fully deformed cell inside the constriction,
      captured by two inner electrode pairs inside the narrow channel. This is dominated by
      cell membrane electrical properties.

This is the first reported device to measure both Z_undeformed and Z_deformed in a single
transit event. Three mechanical timing parameters are also extracted per cell:
  - Passage time : total time from first contact with constriction to full exit
  - Entry time   : time for cell to enter and fully deform into the constriction
  - Transit time : time spent transiting the interior of the constriction

The differential configuration automatically rejects multi-cell transit events (identified
by successive same-sign impedance peaks) and is insensitive to environmental drift, unlike
conventional non-differential two-electrode approaches.


DEVICE ARCHITECTURE
-------------------
Channel fabrication:
  Photoresist : SU-8 2015 (MicroChem); spin-coated at 2000 rpm; thickness ~20 um
  Soft bake   : 65 C 1 min + 95 C 3 min
  UV exposure : 155 mJ/cm2
  Post bake   : 65 C 1 min + 95 C 3 min
  Developer   : SU-8 developer solution, room temperature
  Mold treat  : trichloro(1H,1H,2H,2H-perfluorooctyl)silane, vapor deposition (hydrophobic)
  PDMS        : Sylgard 184 (Dow Corning); base:curing agent 10:1; degas; pour on mold;
                cure 60 C ≥3 h; peel; punch inlets/outlets with 1.5 mm biopsy punch
  Channel height : ~20 um
  Constriction width : ~8 um (narrower than MCF-7 diameter ~22 um; forces deformation)

Electrode fabrication:
  Substrate   : 4 inch glass wafer
  Material    : Gold, patterned by lift-off process
  Width       : 20 um; spacing within pair: 30 um
  Configuration: Coplanar, patterned on bottom glass substrate (4 pairs total)
  Outer pairs : 2 pairs flanking constriction (sense undeformed cells)
  Inner pairs : 2 pairs inside constriction (sense fully deformed cells)

Bonding:
  Oxygen plasma treatment of PDMS and glass surfaces; align using XY positioner;
  bond permanently; bake 120 C 1 h to strengthen bond.

Impedance measurement:
  Analyser    : HF2IS Impedance Spectroscope (Zurich Instruments)
  Input V     : 500 mV amplitude
  Frequencies : 1 MHz and 2 MHz (simultaneous multi-frequency acquisition)
  Method      : Differential: ΔZ = (Z_medium_only − Z_cell+medium) / (Z_cell+medium × Z_medium_only)
  Advantage   : Eliminates baseline drift; rejects multi-cell events; does not require short/straight
                constriction geometry for detectable signal; measures both deformed and undeformed states

Electrical circuit model:
  Type        : Single-shell Foster-Schwan model
  Components  : Ri (cell interior resistance), Cmem (cell membrane capacitance),
                Rmed (medium resistance), Cmed (medium capacitance), CDL (double-layer capacitance)
  Assumptions : Cell interior capacitance and membrane resistance neglected (simplified analysis)
  Validated against impedance measurements from single cells (Sun & Morgan 2010; Morgan et al. 2006)

Pressure control:
  System      : Fluigent MFCS-EZ
  Application : Positive pressure at channel inlet drives cell flow
  Pressures   : 200 mbar (Figure S4) and 500 mbar (Figure 3 / Figure S3) tested


CELL LINES
----------
- MCF-7 (human breast adenocarcinoma): standard cancer cell deformability model.
  Cultured in standard conditions. Trypsinized and resuspended in PBS for live experiments.
  Average cell size: 22.01 um.

- modMCF-7 (PMA-modified MCF-7): MCF-7 treated with 500 nM PMA (Sigma-Aldrich) for 18 h at
  37 C. PMA activates protein kinase C (PKC), inducing cortical actin remodelling and
  increasing cell deformability. After treatment, cells resuspended in PBS for measurement.
  Average cell size: 22.42 um. Dose-response tested at 100, 300, 500 nM (Figure S6).

- fixedMCF-7 (formaldehyde-fixed MCF-7): MCF-7 fixed in 4% methanol-free formaldehyde
  (ThermoScientific) for 8 min; quenched with 0.127 M glycine for 5 min at room temperature;
  resuspended in PBS. Crosslinking maximally stiffens cytoskeleton. Average cell size: 23.37 um.

- RBC (human red blood cells): highly deformable positive reference. Biconcave disc ~8 um
  diameter. Readily transits 8 um constriction with minimal entry resistance. Resuspended in PBS.

Size note: All three MCF-7 populations show similar size distributions (avg 22–23 um),
confirming that observed differences in passage time and impedance reflect mechanical and
electrical properties, not size effects.


PROTOCOL STEPS
--------------

Step 1 | PMA Modification of MCF-7 Cells (modMCF-7 Preparation)
  Reagents    : MCF7_Cells, PMA_500nM (500 nM in PBS)
  Mix         : 5 s (cells + PMA combined)
  Heat        : 37 C for 18 h (1080 min = 64800 s)
  Wash        : Resuspend in PBS after treatment
  BioScript   : mix mcf7_for_mod with pmaReagent for 5s
                heat modTreatmentMix at 37c for 1080m
                mix modTreatmentMix with modWashPBS for 1m
  IR check    : heat(modTreatmentMix1[0]) @ 37.0CELSIUS @ 64800.0SECOND ✓

Step 2 | Formaldehyde Fixation of MCF-7 Cells (fixedMCF-7 Preparation)
  Reagents    : MCF7_Cells, Formaldehyde_4pct (4% methanol-free, ThermoScientific),
                Glycine_127mM (0.127 M glycine quench solution)
  Fixation    : Mix cells with formaldehyde; incubate 8 min (480 s) at room temperature
  Quench      : Add glycine; incubate 5 min (300 s) at room temperature
  Wash        : Resuspend in PBS
  BioScript   : mix mcf7_for_fix with formaldehydeSoln for 8m
                mix fixationMix with glycineQuench for 5m
                mix quenchedFixation with fixWashPBS for 1m
  IR check    : fixationMix @ 480.0SECOND; quenchedFixation @ 300.0SECOND ✓

Step 3 | Channel Priming with 1% BSA in PBS
  Reagents    : BSA_PBS (1% BSA in PBS), PBS
  Duration    : 20 min (1200 s)
  Purpose     : Blocks hydrophobic PDMS surfaces; prevents non-specific MCF-7 cell adhesion
  BioScript   : mix primingBSA with primingPBS for 20m
  IR check    : primingMix @ 1200.0SECOND ✓

Step 4 | Untreated MCF-7 Cell Measurement at 500 mbar
  Reagents    : MCF7_Cells (live, trypsinized, in PBS), primed channel
  Pressure    : 500 mbar (Fluigent MFCS-EZ, positive pressure at inlet)
  Loading     : Cells flow continuously into device; `mix` abstracts pressure-driven
                channel loading (not a DMF droplet merge step)
  Detection   : impedanceDetection module, 30 s
                Outer electrode pairs: Z_undeformed per cell
                Inner electrode pairs: Z_deformed per cell
                Timing: passage time, entry time, transit time extracted from peak shape
  QC          : Differential sign-reversal check confirms single-cell events
                Multi-cell events (successive same-sign peaks) automatically rejected
  BioScript   : mcf7FlowMix = mix mcf7Live with primingMix for 2s
                impedanceSignal = detect impedanceDetection on mcf7FlowMix for 30s
  Results (500 mbar, Figure S3):
    Passage time  : MCF-7 mean 6.97 ms, modMCF-7 mean 6.0 ms  (N=885, 342)
    Entry time    : MCF-7 mean 1.68 ms, modMCF-7 mean 1.58 ms  (N=666, 460)
    Transit time  : MCF-7 mean 3.39 ms, modMCF-7 mean 3.17 ms  (N=747, 302)
  Branch:
    Pass (impedanceSignal > 0) -> BB2: dispose mcf7FlowMix
    Fail (impedanceSignal <= 0) -> BB3: drain mcf7FlowMix
    Join -> BB4

Step 5 | PMA-Modified MCF-7 (modMCF-7) Measurement at 500 mbar
  Reagents    : modMCF7_Ready (from Step 1), BSA_PBS (fresh priming)
  Pressure    : 500 mbar
  Detection   : impedanceDetection module, 30 s
  Expected    : Shorter passage/entry/transit times vs untreated MCF-7;
                lower Z_undeformed reflecting less resistive cell interior due to cortical actin loss
  BioScript   : modFlowMix = mix modMCF7_Ready with primingMix2 for 2s
                modSignal = detect impedanceDetection on modFlowMix for 30s
  Branch:
    Pass (modSignal > 0) -> BB5: dispose modFlowMix
    Fail (modSignal <= 0) -> BB6: drain modFlowMix
    Join -> BB7

Step 6 | Formaldehyde-Fixed MCF-7 Measurement at 500 mbar (and 200 mbar)
  Reagents    : fixedMCF7_Ready (from Step 2), BSA_PBS (fresh priming)
  Pressures   : 500 mbar (primary run) and 200 mbar (for Figure S4 four-population comparison)
  Detection   : impedanceDetection module, 30 s
  Expected    : Longest passage/entry/transit times of all four populations;
                maximum stiffness from cytoskeletal crosslinking; impedance dominated by
                membrane properties as cell cannot fully conform to constriction geometry
  BioScript   : fixedFlowMix = mix fixedMCF7_Ready with primingMix3 for 2s
                fixedSignal = detect impedanceDetection on fixedFlowMix for 30s
  Results (200 mbar, Figure S4):
    Population  | Passage (ms) | Entry (ms) | Transit (ms) | N (pass/entry/transit)
    RBC         |  9.21        |  2.32      |  4.21        | 264 / 481 / 477
    modMCF-7    | 16.7         |  4.88      |  8.75        | 277 / 580 / 260
    MCF-7       | 20.9         |  5.77      | 10.5         | 313 / 370 / 370
    fixedMCF-7  | 63.0         | 20.3       | 21.3         |  72 / 134 / 119
  Statistical test: Mann-Whitney; *** p < 0.001 for all pairwise comparisons
  Branch:
    Pass (fixedSignal > 0) -> BB8: dispose fixedFlowMix
    Fail (fixedSignal <= 0) -> BB9: drain fixedFlowMix
    Join -> BB10

Step 7 | RBC Reference Measurement with Optical Validation (200 mbar and 500 mbar)
  Reagents    : RBC_Cells (in PBS), BSA_PBS (fresh priming)
  Pressures   : 200 mbar and 500 mbar
  Detection   : impedanceDetection module (30 s) + passageTimeDetection module (30 s)
                passageTimeDetection abstracts high-speed optical camera validation:
                Phantom v9.1 (Vision Research), 1000 fps; timing cross-validated with
                impedance-based passage time measurements
  Expected    : Shortest passage/entry/transit times; minimal impedance signal due to
                small cell size (~8 um), low cytoplasmic conductivity, absence of nucleus
  BioScript   : rbcFlowMix  = mix rbcSample with primingMix4 for 2s
                rbcImpedance = detect impedanceDetection on rbcFlowMix for 30s
                rbcSignal    = detect passageTimeDetection on rbcFlowMix for 30s
  Branch:
    Pass (rbcSignal > 0) -> BB11: dispose rbcFlowMix
    Fail (rbcSignal <= 0) -> BB12: drain rbcFlowMix
    Join -> BB13: dispose rbcImpedance; dispose rbcSignal; NOP (terminal)

Flow abstraction note:
  `mix` statements for cell loading into the microfluidic channel abstract pressure-driven
  flow-through events (cells driven by Fluigent MFCS-EZ at 200/500 mbar), not DMF electrode
  droplet-merging steps. This follows the same convention used in the isoclone reference file
  (samlali_hybrid_microfluidics_isoclone.bs, Steps 2 and 7).

drain vs dispose:
  The lilott8 compiler collapses `drain` into `dispose` in the IR output (observed in
  bsbbif_3_f, bsbbif_6_f, etc.). This is expected compiler behaviour and does not affect
  protocol correctness or CFG structure.

Dual detect on same fluid:
  Step 7 issues two detect calls on rbcFlowMix (impedanceDetection and passageTimeDetection).
  The compiler processes both as sequential detect nodes on the same operand. The QC branch
  gates on rbcSignal (passageTimeDetection) only; rbcImpedance is disposed at BB13.
